Chapter 613: Chapter 260: Medicine Workshop Upgrade, Population Surge, and Resource Easing

The Sun Resistance Pill is not simple either; it can allow the Earth-digging Realm to appear under the sun.

On a smaller scale, for individuals, this is a life-saving item at critical moments;

On a larger scale, for the camp, as long as there are enough Sun Resistance Pills, it can instantly change the battlefield situation.

The only consideration now is the issue of medicinal material sources!

These eleven kinds of medicinal materials all come from Longyou, and currently, none are acquired by Great Xia itself.

Xia Hong’s brows slightly furrowed, then he spoke to Cheng Feng to give an order.

"Inform the people in the Alchemy Workshop to halt their current tasks."

Cheng Feng was puzzled upon hearing this, but he immediately ordered everyone to stop what they were doing.

There are quite a few pharmacists in Great Xia, as many as over sixty.

Everyone stopped and looked back at Xia Hong with faces full of confusion.

Xia Hong’s mind slightly moved, and the system’s resource panel instantaneously reduced by 200,000 wood, 200,000 coal, and 400,000 iron.

Meanwhile, within the Alchemy Workshop, twenty furnaces and that dark green circular cauldron in the center erupted with a burst of strong light.

Seeing this scene, Cheng Feng and the others clearly realized something, and their expressions became excited.

Over ten breaths later, the strong light dissipated.

An incredibly astonishing change had already taken place within the Alchemy Workshop.

Firstly, the Alchemy Workshop’s area had expanded to twice its original size, with a small square ten-meter space added at the back, the purpose of which was unknown;

Secondly, the twenty alchemy furnaces had also grown noticeably larger, and the space for storing fuel beneath them had doubled in size;

Lastly, the green cauldron in the center hadn’t changed in size, but it had acquired a circle of ancient cloud patterns and emitted fluorescence, with occasional whiffs of peculiar medicinal scent wafting out.

Alchemy Workshop (Level 3): Alchemy equipment, any item can be input, attributes can be identified, items with pharmaceutical properties will be automatically recorded.

Special function 1: Can automatically generate formulas based on recorded varieties and input materials into the cauldron according to the formula, add water and burn, resulting in automatic pill formation.

Special function 2: The medicine furnace can be used to determine medicinal properties and organize pill formulas.

Special function 3: Using the medicine furnace for alchemy can increase the pill-making rate for pharmacists by thirty percent.

Special function 4: Comes with a ten-meter medicine field, which can be cultivated into spiritual medicine using coal.

Consumption: Burning per hour/coal 1000; identification feature will consume varying amounts of coal stone depending on the item’s preciousness; the medicine field consumes 200/day.

Upgrade conditions: Wood 1 million coal 1 million iron 5 million silver 10,000 synthesis formulas 100 types

......

Three more special functions have been added, the second and third plainly heading towards the path of nurturing pharmacists; with these medicine furnaces, Great Xia should soon develop its own pharmacists.

The fourth is even more exciting, a medicine field!

Great Xia can cultivate spiritual medicine itself, with a daily consumption of 200 points, which is 4000 pounds of coal a day. Although this consumption is terrifying, if it can continuously generate spiritual medicine for the camp, it is worth it.

In fact, the camp’s supply of coal has been consistently overflowed!

Seeing the first four special functions, Xia Hong was initially very excited, but upon seeing the final upgrade conditions, his face instantly fell.

"These upgrade conditions are becoming more and more stringent!"

Wood, coal, and iron are actually manageable, mainly the latter two.

It’s hard to come up with 100 synthesis formulas; even with the newly generated 13 formulas, Great Xia only possesses a total of 44 types, still short by 56 types; the later these formulas get, the harder they are to combine;

Silver goes without saying, 10,000 points, equivalent to 50,000 pounds of silver, this exceeds the silver resources required for other building upgrades combined.

Before finding the silver mine, apart from the Black Soil Wall and Yellow Earth, the other seven buildings were out of the question, now it truly dashed Xia Hong’s thoughts of upgrading buildings.

After informing Cheng Feng of the upgraded Alchemy Workshop functions, Xia Hong pondered for a moment before continuing to instruct:

"I’ll have someone send over a batch of medicinal materials and Jade Bone later, you prioritize refining Yangyuan Pill, then with remaining materials refine Blood Essence Pill and Beast Spirit Pill, refine as many as you can, also, research the use of the medicine field."

"As you command!"

Cheng Feng’s expression was full of excitement, he soon led others to examine the medicine field.

Speaking of which, the four workshops for alchemy, forging, summer wear, and woodworking all fall under the jurisdiction of the Craftsmen Department, but Cheng Feng’s Harvesting Department frequently uses the Alchemy Workshop, which is why he took over its management, alleviating Mu Dong, the Craftsmen Department Director, to some extent.

Xia Hong returned to the main building, quickly sorting out a large batch of Jade Bone, Cold Apple Jade Dew, and other ten kinds of precious medicinal materials, then called for them to be sent to the Alchemy Workshop.

"Fortunately, Xia Chuan makes ambitious requests, the batch of medicinal materials sent by Long Mountain and Zhaoyang is enough to produce 180 pounds of Jade Bone Medicine, and estimate around 60 furnaces of Yangyuan Pill can be refined, it should yield 540 pills, definitely enough for me to cultivate to the peak of Cold Resistance Level, even breakthrough to Xianyang Level, this resolves the cultivation issue at Cold Resistance Level!"

Xia Hong’s eyes slightly moved; Cold Resistance Level cultivation is of prime importance to himself and others in the camp, and having this matter resolved successfully now allows him to focus on the camp’s development.

.........

Screening work continues, simultaneously ongoing is the Great Migration of Longyou.

Each night, around five thousand newcomers arrive from Mirror Valley; two or three accidental Cold Beast invasions occurred in the middle, but fortunately, their scale wasn’t large, and they all passed without any dangerous incidents, with minimal casualty.
