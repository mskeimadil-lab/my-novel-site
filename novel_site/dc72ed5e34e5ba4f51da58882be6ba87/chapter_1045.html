Chapter 1045: Chapter 370: Yuzong’s Birthday, Jiangxia Falls, Fishing-Hunting Mode and Turning Point_5

Hearing the words "Chen and Cai," Huang Tianxing’s pupils slightly contracted, and a hint of excitement appeared on his face as he nodded heavily.

Landgrave leads the territory, deceitful object’s forbidden land; Chen and Cai must have a mysterious method to counter it.

In this way, Dajue Temple really poses no threat.

The Northern Three Towns only need to let Dajue Temple continue to deplete the strength of the Six Towns in the Central South, and when the time is right, then...

.........

The eighth year of Da Xia, on the last day of January, daytime.

Xing River Five Origin Basin, a twenty-meter-long iron ship is drifting in the waters more than a hundred meters from the shore.

"Spread the bait!"

Snap......

With a loud shout from the leader, more than thirty people standing on the ship immediately scattered a large amount of unknown yellow-white powder into the water. These powder clusters were not heavy, and they floated immediately upon hitting the water.

Five people holding silver fish spears about one meter long were constantly changing positions around the ship. Judging by their actions, they were clearly searching for a target underwater.

Although the spear handles are silver, a close examination reveals it’s due to an abundance of forging patterns, numbering ten thousand, giving it a silver color rather than being silver-made.

In contrast, the twelve sharp hooks with barbs at the spear’s head shine brilliantly, and they don’t show any forging patterns unless closely looked at. Those experienced can see it’s genuine silver material.

The spear’s tail is also equipped with a thick chain, made of Ten Thousand Forged material, connected to the ship’s core.

The entire ship was silent, even the five who held silver fish spears were treading carefully, trying to remain quiet.

At the front of the ship, a young man in black, about thirty years old, seemed to have discovered something. He lowered his head, looking into the water with shining eyes.

A person holding a Strong Bow next to him noticed his anomaly, turned to look at the spot on the water surface he was focused on, and immediately held his breath.

Indeed, underwater appeared two fist-sized bright lights, and behind the lights was a large shadow, rapidly approaching the water’s surface.

As the shadow grew larger, about a quarter of the ship’s size, the young man in black swiftly acted, throwing the fish spear fiercely underwater.

Whizz, whizz, whizz...... Boom, crash...

The spear plunged into the water, instantly pulling the thick chain at its tail, plunging underwater for about ten meters until it was halted by its length, the chain strained straight, fully showcasing the strength the young man in black exerted when throwing the spear.

"Gugugu......"

Simultaneously, an eerie sound emerged from underwater, and the chain began swaying left and right, causing the ship to sink sharply.

However, the ship was clearly designed by weight and size, no matter how it was tugged, it wouldn’t sink.

The creature underwater soon realized it couldn’t pull it down, so it changed tactics and started swimming south, attempting to drag the entire ship to the middle of the waters.

Boom, crash......

However, barely pulled forward by ten meters, on the north side of the ship, a giant Rough Iron Chain connected to the shore, about half a meter thick, floated above the water and strained tight, holding the ship firmly in place in the waters a hundred meters from the shore, immovable by the creature’s force.

"We’ve got something here, come help!"

The young man in black who had thrown the fish spear shouted, grasping the chain connected to the fish spear with both hands, his face reddening as he fiercely pulled it back.

Splash......

A black monstrous fish, five to six meters long, was forcefully pulled half out of the water by the young man. The fish was covered in scales black as ink, which gleamed under the cold light, resembling pieces of ink jade with a unique beauty.

"Hahaha, a new species, a new species, hurry over, quick!"

Seeing the creature’s appearance, the young man in black was delighted, immediately urging everyone on the ship to speed up.

Whiz, whiz......

The other thirty-plus people on the ship, upon hearing the words "new species," were instantly excited, but didn’t forget their task, quickly took out Longbows, nocked their Silver Sharp Arrows, and madly fired at the black monstrous fish.

Thud, thud......

At the same time, four more fish spears swiftly flew at the black monstrous fish, piercing straight through its body. The four who threw the spears also quickly pulled up their chains, joining the young man in black in tightly holding the monstrous fish, allowing others to shoot at it.

The blood from the fish pierced by the arrows was unexpectedly green jade color, identical to the bone color of Advanced Cold Beasts.

Thud, thud......

With over a hundred sharp arrows embedding into its body, the thrashing of the black monstrous fish in the water grew smaller, and the pressure on the five who pulled the chains noticeably reduced.

"Drag it back!"

The young man in black directly released the chain, then ordered the other four to drag the dying black fish to the ship’s edge.

The black monstrous fish was completely exhausted, dragged underwater, unable to move its body.

"A new species, we have the naming rights, everyone, what should it be called?"

"How about Black Xuan?"

"Qiu Sizheng caught it, let’s use the Director’s name, call it Xuanpeng!"

"Hahaha, you can’t be serious."

"This monstrous fish has great strength; Xuan Sha sounds better."

.........

Splash!

Amidst everyone’s merry discussion, the underwater monstrous fish suddenly opened its eyes, fiercely thumped its tail in the water, flipping its body as it sprang out of the surface, opening its huge mouth at the ship, spraying a mass of black, oil-like substance.

Thump......

"Dodge!"

Qiu Peng was clearly caught off guard but still yelled for everyone to retreat. However, the monstrous fish had planned and acted without warning, and the black substance it sprayed was far too much, nearly covering the entire ship. This iron ship, without cabins and only a deck, made it impossible for all thirty-plus people, no matter how quick, to evade in time.
