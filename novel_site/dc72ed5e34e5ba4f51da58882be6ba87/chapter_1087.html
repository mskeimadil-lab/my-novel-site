Chapter 1087 - 379: Awakening, Promotion, and Military Expansion; Jiangxia’s Restoration and Choices (Part 2)

Chapter 1087: Chapter 379: Awakening, Promotion, and Military Expansion; Jiangxia’s Restoration and Choices (Part 2)

Last October, Xia Hong developed the Wuyuan Waters. Although the Great Xia had already produced the Dragon Carp Pill and Green Cod Pill, Xu Ning was completely unaware.

"I can feel that I am still at the Late-stage Cold Resistance Cultivation, which means I now have... a Top-grade Battle Body Qualification!"

Suppressing the wild joy in his eyes, Xu Ning walked up to Xia Hong and bowed solemnly: "I am forever grateful for the lord’s grace that remade me!"

"No need for such formality, rise."

Signaling Xu Ning to rise, Xia Hong also revealed a trace of excitement in his eyes.

"The organs of the Five Great Masters can actually enhance the Battle Body Talent."

Xu Ning used Master Wusheng’s liver, and he still had two pieces in hand, respectively Yan Long Master’s heart and Vajra Master’s lungs.

In this way, the Great Xia can produce two more top-grade war bodies!

Top-grade Battle Body Qualification is certainly very rare!

The Great Xia’s population is nearing a million, and currently, those confirmed to have Top-grade Battle Body Qualification are himself, Li Xuanling, Xia Chuan, and Yuwen Dao.

The key is that Xia Hong has already broken through to the Xianyang Level, and he is well aware of the vast strength differences that arise once different Battle Body Talents reach the Xianyang Level.

Each additional one is a tremendous boost to the camp’s future.

Although Hong Qiaoru is a woman, she is not ignorant. With her husband’s right eye restored, she was already very happy; upon hearing that he now had Top-grade Battle Body Qualification, her expression was even more excited. Just now, as Xu Ning bowed in gratitude to Xia Hong, she followed suit with their little daughter in her arms.

The two young children, Xu Cheng and Xu Ping, didn’t understand much but seeing their father’s joy, they were happy as well.

Xia An and Qiu Peng, standing nearby, showed some envy on their faces while also being happy for Xu Ning, being well aware of the significance of Top-grade Battle Body Qualification despite having a Superior Battle Body themselves.

"Master Wusheng’s liver should offer more benefits than just enhancing the Battle Body Talent. Study it well; if there are any special changes after your Late Stage Cultivation breakthrough, you can tell me."

"As you command!"

After giving his final instructions, Xia Hong looked at Xu Ning and said with a smile: "After resting for half a year, it should be enough. Your Five Originals Guardian position has been assigned to Shi Ping, and I need to arrange a new position for you..."

Learning that his Five Originals Guardian position had been reassigned to Shi Ping, Xu Ning wasn’t surprised. After all, with him comatose for half a year, Wuyuan couldn’t go without leadership.

However, hearing that Xia Hong was going to assign him a new position, his face revealed a trace of nervousness and anticipation.

"Currently, there are five Deputy Director vacancies: Military, Garrison, Prison, Civil Affairs, and Craftsmen. You’re definitely not interested in Craftsmen or Civil Affairs; since you haven’t led troops, the Military Department isn’t suitable either; the Prison Department requires staying in Xia City, which you probably dislike. So, why not join the Garrison Department with Yuan Cheng, how does that sound?"

"Thank you, my lord, for the promotion. I will definitely overcome all obstacles, expand the territories of Great Xia, and never hesitate to face fire and water!"

From Five Originals Guardian to Deputy Director, and from Fourth Rank to Third Rank, it was undoubtedly a promotion. The key was that Xia Hong was so attentive to his position, which deeply moved Xu Ning, almost bringing him to tears with excitement.

"How much Contribution Value do you have?"

Upon hearing Xia Hong inquire about his Contribution Value, Xu Ning was suddenly puzzled but quickly turned subconsciously to look at Qiu Peng.

Qiu Peng obviously understood why Xia Hong asked this and replied with a smile: "Xu Ning’s assets are quite substantial; even after half a year at home, there’s still over five million Contribution Value remaining!"

Xu Ning nodded immediately. Before he fell into a coma last year, he indeed had over five million in Contribution Value, enough for his wife to use not just for half a year, but perhaps for a lifetime.

"Master Wusheng’s liver originally costs 10 million Contribution Value. I’ll give you a discount, making it 8 million. Whenever you accumulate it, you can settle the account with Qiu Peng at the Camp Supply Department!"

"Rest assured, my lord, I will repay these eight million as soon as possible."

Initially stunned, Xu Ning then turned to see Qiu Peng and Xia An, who were secretly laughing, and replied to Xia Hong with a wry smile, as warmth rushed through his heart once more.

Gaining Top-grade Battle Body Qualification, even if it’s worth ten million, a hundred million, or a billion Contribution Points, Xia City would be eager to snatch it up.

Clearly, Xia Hong was giving him a way. It meant he wasn’t taking the liver for free, and even if others knew in the future, there would be an explanation.

Moreover, for someone in a high position like himself in Great Xia, although ten million is a significant sum, it’s not particularly difficult; in a few years, he could accumulate it.

"Alright then, since your body has recovered, don’t idle around. After capturing the North Shore of Xing River, quite a few new posts have appeared in the camp. Yuan Cheng has stationed with Xia Chuan at the Hongguan Camp, so he definitely doesn’t have time to manage matters here. You, as the Deputy Director, should start making a significant impact. Organize those posts, see how to allocate personnel, and additionally..."

At this point, Xia Hong paused, turned to Qiu Peng, and asked, "Currently in the camp, how many are at the Cold Resistance Level and at the Earth-digging Realm Limit?"

"6402 in Cold Resistance Level, 18628 at the Earth-digging Realm Limit."

Xia Hong’s brows furrowed slightly; he remembered well that last year, when they came out of retreat, these numbers were 5054 and 14547, respectively.

Starting from the Earth-digging Realm Limit, cultivation speed significantly slows down.

In six months, gaining over 1300 Cold Resistance Levels is somewhat unusual, for the reason that starting from last October, all 1000 slots in the Martial Arts Pavilion were allocated to the Cloud Jiao and Long Wu armies. Of the newly reached over 1300 Cold Resistance Levels, nearly a thousand are soldiers from these two armies.
