Chapter 250: Chapter 150: Green Bamboo Fence and Marking Stone, Expanding Territory_4

In the past five days, the leader has used nearly six hundred pounds of Beast Blood, surely becoming even stronger. Killing Yuwen Hu is probably not a difficult task anymore.

The only worry now is that the Mirror Immortal Camp might, relying on its numbers, launch a full-scale attack.

If that unseen He Yuanhun joins forces with Yuwen Hu and hundreds from the Earth-digging Realm, even with Xia Hong, the current Great Xia might not stand a chance.

"Do you think the Great Xia Station is hard to find?"

Xia Hong knew exactly what everyone was worried about, but he said nothing more, instead, he asked a question.

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan and others were initially stunned, then, after some thought, they quickly understood Xia Hong’s implication, a trace of delight rising in their eyes.

The Great Xia Station isn’t too hard to find, especially for humans.

To the east of Redwood Ridge is Twin Dragon Mountain. The mountain wall area runs from south to north, only spanning about ten kilometers, and the Great Xia Station is the only valley in the midst of it.

Not to mention deliberately searching, considering the traces left by the Great Xia’s outings, a casual search would likely lead one here.

"The Lu Shang Camp has already merged with Mirror Immortal. If Lu He is even a bit smart, he surely knows that we’ve inherited the Lu Shang Camp’s territory. Unless something unexpected happens, the Mirror Immortal Camp probably already knows the location of our Station."

What Xia Chuan said was like a calming pill for everyone.

Everyone’s expressions immediately relaxed considerably, and the joy was increasingly evident.

The reason is simple.

Knowing the location of the Great Xia Camp, yet they haven’t come looking in these five days.

This proves that regarding the incident five days ago, the Mirror Immortal Camp, most likely, chose to endure it.

"It’s probably because that Yuwen Hu is afraid, after all, he almost got killed by the leader. Coming again to cause trouble, isn’t he afraid of death!"

"We in Great Xia were not at fault in this matter. It was they who trespassed first. He Meng’s killing to repay with life is justified, so on what grounds would they cause trouble?"

"Perhaps, when Yuwen Hu took the leader’s words back, that He Yuanhun got scared immediately and didn’t dare to cause trouble?"

"Hahaha!"

......

Xia Hong certainly wouldn’t take everyone’s jokes to heart.

But it was indeed unexpected for him that the Mirror Immortal Camp hasn’t come to cause trouble.

Though he couldn’t figure out the exact reason, it definitely couldn’t be as Xia Chuan and others speculated. Judging from how He Meng’s group is fearless, that He Yuanhun is definitely not an easy person to deal with, how could he be afraid!

But be that as it may, Xia Hong wouldn’t waste time on this matter.

If soldiers come, use a general to block; if water comes, use soil to cover. Previously he was at most a bit worried, yet now he had two additional constructions in hand, so even if the Mirror Immortal Camp really came, he wouldn’t be afraid at all.

"Let’s not talk about the Mirror Immortal Camp for now, wait until tomorrow night when I take you guys north to have a look. There’s something else I want to talk to you about..."

Xia Hong paused, then took out a Beast Skin Map from the desk, first handing it to Xia Chuan on the left, letting him pass it along once he finished looking.

As everyone examined the map, Xia Hong’s voice followed suit.

"The camp population is still too small. We must find a way to expand the range of activities of the camp personnel, explore more unfamiliar areas, and see if we can discover other camps to quickly increase the population..."

Seeing the already marked southern area on the map, everyone immediately understood Xia Hong’s intent and all nodded in agreement.

"Of course, exploring unfamiliar areas isn’t a simple task, so the rewards will follow accordingly. For exploring new areas, discovering new camps, or even directly increasing the camp’s population, you can earn Contribution Points, and Xia Chuan, you will be responsible for setting the specifics."

"Yes, big brother!"

Currently, Xia Hong wouldn’t personally handle these matters. After delegating them to Xia Chuan and others, he also took out from behind him a piece of Green Bamboo Fence and five Mother-child Marking Stones.

"Also, these two things..."

As Xia Hong described the functions of the Green Bamboo Fence and the Marking Stones, everyone’s expressions became more and more excited, some even couldn’t help but stand up directly.

Accompanied by each piece of Green Bamboo Fence being placed at the western entrance of the valley.

Xia Hong announced the news of the camp expansion and exploration southward, and it instantly spread throughout the camp.

Of course, most importantly was Xia Chuan’s subsequent announcement regarding the rewards for Contribution Points.

Exploring unfamiliar areas, mapping out territory, and having it confirmed accurately, earns 1 Contribution Point for every ten meters;

Discovering a new camp, reporting back to the leader, and upon confirmation, a small camp earns 500 Contribution Points; a medium camp earns 2000; a large camp directly earns 5000; and a gigantic camp earns 10000.

For bringing outsiders into Great Xia, regardless of their cultivation, each person earns 50 points with no upper limit; as long as you have the capability, all Contribution Points will be yours.

The camp’s expansion, along with the Contribution Points reward mechanism, as soon as these two pieces of news were released, everyone in the Great Xia Camp immediately plunged into fervent discussion.
