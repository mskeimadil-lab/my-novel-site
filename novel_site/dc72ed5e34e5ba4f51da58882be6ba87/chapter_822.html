Chapter 822: Chapter 322: The Cold Beast Clans Joining Forces for Treasure, White Dew Gu’s Panic (Part 3)

Xia Chuan’s expression immediately changed.

Ten groups, each with more than twenty heads, that adds up to more than two hundred high-level Cold Beasts, not to mention the more troublesome Snow Mane and Frost Wolf clans.

"Does this mean we have no chance of getting close to Yangyuan Peak?"

Their expressions turned even more grave upon hearing his words.

However, Xia Hong’s face showed a trace of indifference as he smiled lightly and said:

"If all these Cold Beasts unite, there truly is no hope, but after tonight, things will be different. As long as our strength is enough and we are well-prepared beforehand, taking them down one by one is still possible."

Upon hearing this, everyone’s expressions brightened immediately.

Even Xia Chuan glanced at the ten Cold Beast carcasses on the ground and immediately understood Xia Hong’s intention, nodding lightly.

There are less than seven kilometers left to Yangyuan Peak, with such a big commotion just now in the battle, how could those high-level Cold Beasts not hear it?

Their failure to appear proves that these high-level Cold Beast groups are wary of each other, considering they’re here at Yangyuan Peak to compete for treasure, they’re likely mutually hostile.

With that in mind, using torches to take them down one by one indeed holds hope!

"These Cold Beasts aren’t controlled by the White Dew Gu, and haven’t shown any signs of being infected by the Dewdrop Plague, which proves that at most, it can only lure the Cold Beasts here; apart from that, it can’t do anything else, which makes dealing with it easier for us. Once we gradually clear out the surrounding Cold Beasts and disperse the white mist in the area, approaching Yangyuan Peak will naturally follow."

Li Xuanling, who had just finished inspecting the Striped Tiger carcass, approached.

That White Dew Gu was the cause of her second brother Li Xuantiang’s death, clearly, she also harbors resentment and is contemplating how to approach Yangyuan Peak to deal with it.

Xia Hong smiled slightly and said, "Simple to say, difficult to do! Two months have passed, and we’ve just cleaned up this area, tonight’s group of eleven wasn’t too strong, but taking them down was also very strenuous. Next comes Cold Beast groups with over twenty heads each, and if they’ve reached some cooperation among themselves, it’ll be even more troublesome for us."

True!

Since different high-level Cold Beasts can communicate with each other, who can guarantee that the remaining ten groups won’t continue working together?

After all, with two clans in the center, to successfully seize the treasure on Yangyuan Peak, they might join forces again, which isn’t impossible.

Upon realizing this, everyone’s expressions turned more serious.

Xia Hong turned to look at the western side of Yangyuan Peak, his expression slightly tensed.

In a six-kilometer radius, attracting nearly three hundred high-level Cold Beasts, this White Dew Gu certainly went to great lengths.

Xia Hong wasn’t aware of the specific distinction in the strength of high-level Cold Beasts, but he ventured in three days ago to spy on them once; near Yangyuan Peak, Thousand-eye Demon Sheep, Snow Mane breathing ice, Frost Wolf moving swiftly, and Teng Jiao with a thumb-thick emerald jade color... all of these prove that the strength of the inner group of Cold Beasts is increasingly terrifying.

"Being able to draw so many Cold Beasts, it should be something important enough to promote a Beast King, right? I wonder if it has any effect on humans?"

The Cold Beasts are greedy, and naturally, Xia Hong is greedy too; with so many Cold Beasts gathered here, what the White Dew Gu produced must be extremely precious.

Even without mentioning this, the White Dew Gu itself is a great opportunity!

Xia Hong suppressed the desire in his heart, turned to look at Xia Chuan, and subtly asked:

"Why are you here? Is the number of Dragon Martial Army sufficient?"

Before Xia Chuan could answer, Yuwen Dao on the other side eagerly turned his head, looking at him full of expectation.

Xia Chuan naturally noticed this too, smiled and nodded in response: "Many breakthroughs occurred in November, and now the Earth-digging Realm limit has exceeded 1200 people, so the Dragon Martial Army can indeed start forming."

"That’s great!"

Yuwen Dao couldn’t contain his joy and exclaimed excitedly.

Since last month, when the Dragon Wu Team was elevated to an Advanced Hunting Team, he has been waiting for this day, hearing Xia Chuan confirm the number of soldiers is sufficient, he couldn’t suppress the excitement in his heart.

"That’s right, after we return to Beehive to rest, Yuwen Dao should head back to Xia City to form the army; once formed, waste no time and bring the Dragon Martial Army to Beehive, conduct drills directly in Yanglu Realm."

Upon hearing Xia Hong’s words, Yuwen Dao immediately cupped his hands and replied:

"By your command!"

There are still ten kilometers of thick fog area in Yanglu Realm, the outskirts need to continue being cleared, and if the Dragon Martial Army joins, efficiency will certainly improve greatly.

However, two armies alone are far from enough!

Xia Hong continued to ask Xia Chuan: "Is everything in Xia City settled?"

For the past two months, Xia Chuan hadn’t left Xia City due to the reformation, certainly not running this trip just to handle the Dragon Martial Army.

Sure enough, hearing Xia Hong’s inquiry, Xia Chuan’s face immediately showed excitement as he nodded and replied: "Everything’s ready; the Outer City is divided into East, South, West, and North zones, with each area having over ninety thousand people and a complete Six Departments structure established. The heads of the Six Departments are all arranged and are of Cold Resistance Level."

"All of Cold Resistance Level, are there enough people?"

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong’s face revealed a trace of surprise as he asked.

With Six Departments heads in all four zones being Cold Resistance Level, that totals 24 people. Certainly, Great Xia can present 24 Cold Resistance Levels, but as he knows, many Cold Resistance Levels are already handling duties, and the new batch of Cold Resistance Levels integrated into Longyou are busy leading their respective Hunting Teams, likely without time to take positions in the Six Departments.
