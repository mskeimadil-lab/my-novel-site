Chapter 849: Chapter 330: Longshan System Gathering, Simplified Sect System_2

Next, there is the Mirror Valley faction.

The Mirror Valley faction, represented by Yuwen Dao, includes more than thirty Cold Resistance Level members such as the Hong Guang brothers, Zhu Yuan, and Meng Yi. Not only does it have Yuwen Dao as the Hunting Department Director, but also a Civil Affairs Department Director Zhu Yuan, controlling two of the Seven Departments. Although it cannot compare to the entire Redwood Ridge faction, it is currently the second largest force within Xia City.

Then there is the Wuyuan faction represented by Xiao Kangcheng; the Beehive faction represented by Chen Yingyuan and Peng Bo; the Longbei faction represented by Hou Jing...

Of course, no one would admit to having formed their own faction, but in daily camp decisions and the distribution of important resources, people from the same system will inevitably hold the same position, which is undeniable.

This is not partisan maneuvering, but rather human nature.

At the peak period of Longshan Camp, the population exceeded 100,000. So Li Tiancheng sees through these matters very clearly. He understands very well that this has nothing to do with the governance system of Great Xia. Whenever any camp grows large, these issues are bound to appear.

Given this, it is natural for these old members of Longshan to band together, not to confront other groups, but mainly to ensure their group’s interests are not harmed by others.

Their only previous reason for banding together was Lord Li Xuanling; it wasn’t a particularly strong bond.

In the Ice Abyss environment, a powerful lord like Xia Hong could not have just one wife. As Great Xia expands, he is bound to have other women in the future. Whether Li Xuanling can always maintain her current position is actually uncertain.

But with heirs, and two of them at that, the situation is entirely different!

Li Tiancheng shook off the thoughts in his mind, turned around to survey everyone in the hall, and spoke in a deep voice, "It seems you all have your own thoughts. Great Xia does not have our Longshan Succession System. The Xia Code only specifies that heirs have inheritance rights, without discussing the hierarchy. I will advise the lord on a chosen day, and I hope you will also say a few words, how about that?"

Upon hearing Li Tiancheng’s final request, everyone started to respond:

"Elder Li, don’t worry. Heir succession is a natural course of events, and we will definitely advise the lord with you."

"The Longshan Succession System was established by the old leader Tianhua. It not only prevents fratricide but stabilizes people’s minds. It is beneficial both for individuals and the camp. Advising the lord is only right."

"Indeed, we have no ulterior motives. Great Xia now has over 400,000 people, with countless families with many children. Establishing the Longshan Succession System would be a great thing."

......

Upon hearing everyone’s words, a smile appeared on Li Tiancheng’s face.

He didn’t continue talking, only turned around to look toward the Star-picking Hall behind.

......

"I’m just in my twenties, and your second uncle is already considering the successor for the new lord with these people. Isn’t that a bit hasty?"

In the residence directly behind the Star-picking Hall, Li Xuanling sat next to the Cold Ruins Tripod. Hearing Xia Hong’s jest beside her, her cheeks immediately turned slightly rosy.

But she quickly regained her composure and defended, "Second Uncle and the others are just worried about me. If they were truly conspiring, they wouldn’t gather in a place like the second floor of the main building."

"Don’t worry, I won’t blame them!"

Seeing Li Xuanling a bit tense, Xia Hong first reassured her.

Indeed, the main building is only about thirty meters from the Star-picking Hall. This gathering on the second floor of the main building by the Longshan group can’t be deemed conspiratorial, and the fact that they did not take any precautionary measures during their entire conversation clearly shows they are not afraid of being overheard. Given that, it’s not proper to blame them.

"What do you think about the Longshan Succession System? Do you think Great Xia should also emulate your Longshan and establish this system?"

Hearing Xia Hong’s question, Li Xuanling’s expression instantly became subtle.

Her immediate reaction was clearly to agree, but she probably felt that would reveal strong personal interests, so she held back her words.

Then she pondered for a moment and continued, "Meng Ying and the others aren’t wrong. The Longshan Succession System indeed greatly aids in stabilizing people’s minds. Especially for those in higher positions, with more resources at their disposal, often their offspring are more than one. Without the Longshan Succession System, there would be many tragic fratricides, something that Long Mountain experienced before."

"So your elder brother, with neither the strength nor the intellect, still inherited your father’s position as leader?"

Hearing Xia Hong’s jest, Li Xuanling’s expression suddenly froze.

The matter with Li Xuanyan is a typical negative example brought on by the Longshan Succession System.

Anyone with a discerning eye could easily see that, not to mention compared to Li Xuanling, even Li Xuantiang and Li Xuandu were clearly superior to him. But because of the title of the eldest son, Li Xuanyan eventually took the leader’s position.

The subsequent events played out as expected. During the April Alliance, Li Xuanyan’s indecisiveness was evidently a display of incompetence. At that time, if it had been someone else, the outcome would likely have been entirely different from today.

Li Xuanling was rendered speechless at this moment because her actions openly opposed the Longshan Succession System, as she herself had personally pulled Li Xuanyan down from the leader’s position.

So, how could she have the audacity to say she agrees with this system now?

But after hesitating for a moment, Li Xuanling still spoke to Xia Hong:

"My elder brother’s case is just an exception..."

At this point, she paused her speech, then looked directly into Xia Hong’s eyes and continued, "Also, you should trust me and trust yourself. The children we have together are destined not to be mediocre."

As a mother, Li Xuanling clearly doesn’t care much about her image for the sake of her son’s future.

Hearing this, Xia Hong raised his eyebrows but didn’t speak.

The Longshan Succession System is actually a simplified Sect System.

Since Long Mountain had no distinction between legitimate and illegitimate, it resulted in the emergence of the Longshan Succession System. The core content was that the eldest child had the right to allocate all of the father’s heritage, essentially meaning the eldest son inherited everything.

Those with a conscience or those close to their younger siblings would naturally allocate part of the heritage to them; those without a conscience or who were hostile toward their siblings had no such obligation.

It’s hard to say if the Sect System is or isn’t necessary at this point.

The population of Great Xia is growing rapidly, making the emergence of nobility unavoidable.

This Nine-rank title system set by Xia Hong is essentially a recognition of the nobility class and also provides a path for everyone to rise into nobility.

In the future, such noble status will rise layer by layer, and inevitably a day will come when there will be no more titles to confer. At that time, setting hereditary titles will also be unavoidable.

In that case, the Sect System seems necessary to establish!

Xia Hong turned to look in the direction of the main building, a curious look suddenly appearing on his face.

Li Tiancheng and his people are now targeting me.

What they’re trying to do with this Longshan Succession System is, frankly, all for Li Xuanling’s unborn child, a problem Xia Hong doesn’t intend to solve anytime soon.

He is only twenty-two years old now, and his lifespan at Cold Resistance Level is over a hundred. Not to mention, he will break through to Xianyang Level, and with the existence of the camp system, the upper limit of his cultivation will definitely be high, and his lifespan will continue to increase.

Does he need to consider the issue of a successor?

Xia Hong shook his head and said to Li Xuanling, "Tell your second uncle and them to stop having such crooked thoughts. I acknowledge the usefulness of the Longshan Succession System, but Great Xia doesn’t need it at the current stage. Tell your second uncle not to bring it to me. When the time is right for this system to emerge, it will naturally do so."

Upon hearing this, Li Xuanling’s expression instantly froze. She was clearly associating it with something unpleasant and looked increasingly angry at Xia Hong.

"Don’t overthink it, weren’t you confident just now, saying your child is destined not to be mediocre? With you and your second uncle and that big group of people backing him, I shouldn’t really have to establish this system, right?"

Li Xuanling gritted her teeth and remained silent, evidently disagreeing with Xia Hong’s words.

"Then you better not flirt outside..........."

Thud......

A huge drumbeat suddenly resounded from above the Star-picking Hall, cutting off Li Xuanling’s words,

The drum sound was deafening, not only was the Star-picking Hall affected, but the entire valley where Xia City was located seemed to tremble with it.

Thud... Thud... Thud... Thud...

Then a series of eight drumbeats continued.

"The Cold Element Festival is here. Let’s go to the Political Hall!"

Li Xuanling was already dressed up. Her words just now were unfinished, seemingly somewhat indignant, but seeing Xia Hong already leading the way out, she could only grit her teeth and follow.
