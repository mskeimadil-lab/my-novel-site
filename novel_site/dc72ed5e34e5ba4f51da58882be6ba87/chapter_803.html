Chapter 803: Chapter 316: Blood Jade Fox and Teasing, Boiling Xia City, Migration Begins (Part 6)

Xu Ning clearly didn’t want to say more on this topic and suddenly asked Yuan Cheng:

"By the way, has Qiu Peng come to record your military merits?"

Hearing the words military merit, Yuan Cheng immediately became excited, chuckled, and nodded in response: "He asked me two days ago, haha, I single-handedly held off three Cold Resistance Levels, including Li Xuanping. Qiu Peng said that my military merits in the Hive Guard Battle were definitely among the top, probably just slightly inferior to the Minister, even better than Chen Yingyuan."

Upon hearing this, envy first appeared on Xu Ning’s face, but he soon calmed down upon seeing Yuan Cheng’s injuries.

On the night of the Hive Guard Battle, he was present as well, and he saw Yuan Cheng single-handedly confront three major Cold Resistance Levels.

To be honest, if it wasn’t for the timely arrival of the recently broken-through Shi Ping saving Yuan Cheng, Xu Ning could almost be sure that Yuan Cheng would have been the fourth Cold Resistance Level sacrificed by Great Xia that night.

This is indeed military merit earned at the cost of life.

It is indeed enviable, but also difficult to replicate.

"My military merits should be slightly lower than yours, as I severely injured a Cold Resistance Level from Long Mountain. Qiu Peng went to Long Mountain to confirm the identity, and it seems he is called Lu Yushan."

"So rigorous, even going to confirm the identity?"

Hearing that Qiu Peng went to such lengths to confirm identity, Yuan Cheng immediately became curious and then asked: "I heard there would be a major event during the October merit evaluation and reward conference, and it seems the Camp is going to reorganize again, is it true or false?"

Clearly, everyone had heard about the reorganization.

Xu Ning nodded and said: "I learned a bit from Qiu Peng. The reason for such a rigorous review of military merits is because they plan to confer titles based on military merits. It seems that in the future, besides positions in the Six Ministries, titles will be the most important in the Camp, but I don’t really know what that means."

Title?

Yuan Cheng was briefly stunned upon hearing this, but then he recalled something and said: "Shi Lu went to Xia Clothing Workshop yesterday to make a set of clothes for my kid, and she said the clothing makers at the Xia Clothing Workshop were all working on a very beautiful dress. It was said to be woven one strand at a time using Bamboo Silk and dyed with golden powder. She wouldn’t stop talking about it upon return and insisted I go to Xia Clothing Workshop to inquire and see if I could exchange for a set.

The people at the Craftsmen Workshop said that the batch of clothes was custom ordered by the Minister and would be used only in October. Could this be related to the title?"

Xu Ning shook his head, clearly unaware.

"Alright, I need to return to Wuyuan first. I haven’t been there for almost a month, and I don’t know how things are going there."

"Alright, go ahead, I’ll go and ask Qiu Peng for more information."

.........

The excitement in Xia City continued unabated, with more and more discussions about military achievements and titles, though the most important was the curiosity about the over 200,000 people about to migrate from Longyou.

In the Outer City, one temporary large wooden house after another rose from the ground, and the land began to be planned, clearly preparing plots for the newcomers.

Those already residing in the Outer City, aware that more people would soon be arriving, undoubtedly felt even more urgency, especially those who arrived in May from the Beehive, Wushuang, and Hanqiong, who hadn’t had time to buy land and build houses.

In September, the migration would begin, leaving them with not much time.

Time passed slowly like this, and August soon ended.

On the tenth of September, the five Direct Roads leading from Long Mountain, Zhaoyang, White Abyss, Xiling, and Dongkang to Wushuang were officially completed.

A grand migration finally began...
