Chapter 863: Chapter 333: Silver Mine from the Heavens, Yu Yao and Yu Sheng, No Need for an Ox Knife to Kill a Chicken [10,000-Word Mega Chapter Continued]

This valley is located inside the mountain of Twin Dragon Mountain, and the cave where he stands is indeed in the middle of the rock face, about four hundred meters above the valley floor, with the upper rock face estimated to be over two thousand meters above, too far to see the exact size of the opening above, only a faint trace of the night sky can be seen.

"Climbing up the rock wall will take you to the deepest part of the west side of Twin Dragon Mountain!"

Xia Hong looked up at the night sky above, a slight sense of motion rising in his eyes.

To date, for him and for many in the Great Xia, climbing up Twin Dragon Mountain along the rock wall is no longer considered a difficult task.

But for now, he still strictly ordered the camp’s people not to go up Twin Dragon Mountain.

The reason is simple, firstly safety, and secondly necessity.

Because the area above the rock wall where they are located is theoretically the deepest part of the west side of Twin Dragon Mountain, the cliff location is undoubtedly devoid of human activity, and considering the pattern of the three resource areas of Redwood Ridge, Arrow Bamboo Forest, and Long Mountain, the deeper it is, the more dangerous it becomes, so similarly, the environment above the rock wall must be extremely dangerous, as evidenced by the occasional massive tremors felt by Xia City from above.

The demand is even more understandable!

The camp currently controls three resource areas, all of which have not been fully explored, and currently, the resources in Xia City are overflowing, so the camp has no urgent need to explore new areas.

"That’s not wrong, but if a new area can be explored, it will allow for the acquisition of more new resources, which is also quite beneficial for the camp!"

Xia Hong took a deep breath and turned to look north.

"However, the tunnel inside the mountain is not the only one, there is also a secret passage from Hanqiong Inner Valley Main Building straight into Twin Dragon Mountain, but it’s unclear where the final exit is, Lu Yang should have investigated mostly by now!"

Last year when Hanqiong fell, Han Jiuli’s son, Han Feng, rejected his solicitation and escaped with over a thousand people through the secret passage, which Xia Hong hasn’t forgotten.

Lu Yang is the current Hanqiong Guardian, and at the beginning of the year when he took office at Hanqiong, Xia Hong had already instructed him to explore that secret passage to see if he could find any trace of Han Feng and his over a thousand followers.

"Lord, this silver mine is a gift from heaven to our Great Xia, in such a closed valley, with only a small rodent tribe, there is basically no mining difficulty, congratulations to the lord for finally realizing his wish!"

Xia Hong’s thoughts were numerous, but only for a moment.

Chen Ying saw his face full of excitement and, recalling how Xia Hong had been running all over for the silver mine since the beginning of the year, immediately offered his congratulations.

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong’s smile grew even wider, and he nodded, "Finding this silver mine is a great service of yours, rest assured, what I just said still stands, Xia Chuan, when we return, immediately credit Chen Ying with 500 military merits, and all other promised rewards are not to be missed."

Why is the Minister not here, why is Xia Hong calling him?

Chen Ying was a bit puzzled, but within three or four breaths, he understood why Xia Hong called Xia Chuan, and immediately turned to look towards the back tunnel.

"Yes, my lord, there is a great joy, a great joy!"

In the tunnel, Xia Chuan’s voice arrived before he did.

Four or five breaths later, Xia Chuan and Yuan Cheng came into Chen Ying’s line of sight.

The two walked quickly, faces full of joy, rushing over, exclaiming continuously about the great joy.

"Chen Ying greets the Minister, greets Yuan Director!"

Xia Chuan and Yuan Cheng waved their hands, signaling Chen Ying to rise, then directly rushed in front of the perplexed Xia Hong.

"What’s the great joy?"

"Madam is in labor, the two princes are about to be born!"

On hearing this, Xia Hong’s face changed instantaneously, subconsciously walking towards the cave exit, but after two steps he stopped, his brows furrowing slightly, "You two first look at the valley below."

Although they had been mentally prepared before coming, when Xia Chuan and Yuan Cheng saw the exposed silver in the valley below, their faces still showed ecstatic expressions.

"Over two thousand rodents, eighteen of advanced level, over a hundred of intermediate level, this silver mine is simply a gift to us!"

"The valley space is completely enclosed, the rodents have no way to escape apart from digging and climbing, as long as a tunnel is excavated to connect to the valley floor, Teng Jiao Poison Sacks are ignited inside the valley, and part of the army shoots arrows from the cave mouth on the rock wall, a large number of them can be quickly dealt with, then let the army enter the underground to clear out, it won’t even take an hour to be completely cleaned up, this silver mine is indeed like a gift."

Compared to Yuan Cheng, Xia Chuan’s thoughts were much clearer, directly explaining the strategy for dealing with the rodent tribe below.

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong immediately showed approval, asking, "Have people been sent to notify Yuwen Dao? There are only two hours left until dawn, is the Dragon Martial Army coming back from Long Mountain via Direct Road going to be too late?"

Xia Chuan nodded, "People have been sent to notify, I arranged for the Dragon Martial Army to rest overnight in Mirror Valley, and tomorrow night I will lead the Cloud Dragon Army, while Yuan Cheng leads the Dragon Slayer Army to depart from Xia City, the three armies will converge here.

Qiu Peng has already ordered the Camp Supply Department to start preparing intermediate and advanced level Teng Jiao Poison Sacks and Detoxification Pills, and they will be distributed before the army sets out tomorrow night."

Seeing Xia Chuan’s arrangements were so proper, Xia Hong said no more, directly calling everyone, "Then let’s all head back first, this place is less than two kilometers from Xia City, no need to leave anyone here, we’ll come again tomorrow night!"
