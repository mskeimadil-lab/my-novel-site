Chapter 1328: Chapter 433: End of the Martial Arts Competition, Cat Demon and Bearing, the Defeated East Ridge Group_2

This Lord hopes that you will not squander your talents. May your path of cultivation be steady and prosperous, advancing step by step. Strive to become the backbone of the Camp and establish a more solid foundation for the Great Xia in the Ice Abyss, and add more momentum to our Human Clan’s conquest of winter!"

Having been a Lord for over a decade, Xia Hong effortlessly delivered these words meant to stir emotions, immediately igniting the spirits of Lu Dong, Luo An, and others below him.

Luo An couldn’t help but bow and say, "I will remember the Lord’s teachings for life. Long live the Lord, long live the Great Xia!"

The others immediately followed suit, bowing and saying, "We will remember the Lord’s teachings for life. Long live the Lord, long live the Great Xia!"

"Rise! Today, besides the 16 disciples of the Great Xia, there are also four people from other towns. Please come forward and introduce yourselves, so this Lord can get to know you."

Xia Hong waved his hand, signaling Lu Dong and the others to rise, and then turned his gaze towards the four unfamiliar faces in the crowd.

Murong Yan and the other three stepped forward as soon as they heard Xia Hong’s words.

"Murong Yan from Muyin Town, my father is Murong Yuan, greetings to Lord Xia Hong!"

"I have long heard of the name of the Pearl of Muyin. The little girl’s swordsmanship is quite good. The number one in swordsmanship in the Great Xia belongs to Director Yuwen Dao of the Military Department. The little girl might want to seek his guidance more often."

"Hahahaha..."

With this remark, Xia Hong made everyone present burst into laughter.

Murong Yan blushed instantly, but despite her blush, she showed no sign of awkwardness. She turned her head and stared straight at Yuwen Dao, mustering up her courage, "I would be honored. I just wonder if Director Yuwen is willing to guide me."

"Hahahahaha, he will certainly guide you after this is over."

Even Xia Hong couldn’t help but laugh. After laughing, he gently waved his hand to signal Murong Yan to step back, and then turned his gaze to the last three people.

"Jiang Yichen from Jiangxia, a fifth-generation descendant of the Jiang Family, with Jiang Xinfan as the family patriarch."

"Xiahou Qingqing from Jiangxia, a fifth-generation descendant of the Xiahou Family, with Xiahou Zhang as the family patriarch."

Direct descendants of the Jiang Family and the Xiahou Family, no wonder they possess such high talent!

The young Jiang Yichen possesses a top-grade Battle Body talent with a current strength of Five Manes; the young Xiahou Qingqing’s talent is slightly inferior, being only a top-grade War Body, but her strength is already above Nine Manes, clearly taking advantage of her age.

"Descendants of noble families, indeed remarkable!"

Xia Hong only gave an eight-character evaluation before focusing his attention on the last person.

Interestingly, as soon as he spoke, Xia Chuan, Yuwen Dao, Yuan Cheng, Qiu Peng, and others all turned their attention to the last person.

"It’s over, they have found out!"

With so many eyes on him, how could Fu Tiandu not notice?

As the previous three people introduced themselves, he noticed clearly that Xia Chuan and the others hardly turned their heads. But when it was his turn, everyone turned to look at him.

"Probably, when I entered the hall just now, my shocked reaction was half a beat slower than the others. These people are really too keen..."

Fu Tiandu was exceedingly nervous at this moment, but he could only grit his teeth and cup his hands, "Fu Tiandu from Outer Domain Xiahe Village, greetings to the Great Xia Lord!"

[Fu Tiandu, 19 years old, Cold Resistance Level Cultivation, strength of 10 Manes; native of Jinshan Town Yinhe Valley, Extreme East Outer Domain Xiahe Village; entered Hongmen City on November 26th in the eighth year of Da Xia, currently residing at No. 182, West District, Hongmen South City; a reclusive lifestyle, likes to keep to himself, rarely interacts with outsiders, financially well-off.]

In fact, there was a dossier on Fu Tiandu placed on Xia Hong’s desk.

Seeing this dossier, Xia Hong lowered his head, examining Fu Tiandu. He raised his eyebrows first and then thought of the deliberate shocked expression the lad had put on when he entered the hall earlier. His heart stirred slightly, and a flicker of light flashed across his left eye.

"What’s going on with that Cat Demon on your chest?"

Fu Tiandu was in a state of high alert at this point. Hearing Xia Hong’s question, he was initially dumbfounded, and then he trembled violently and fell to his knees.

"Lord, save me, Lord, save me. Fu Tiandu didn’t mean to deceive, it’s just that this cat monster is too powerful, ah......"

Halfway through his words, Fu Tiandu seemed to be interrupted by something, letting out a miserable howl that startled Xiahou Qingqing, Murong Yan, and the other young people, who all stepped back.

"What the hell!"

"This kid is carrying a deceitful object!"

"What a wicked beast, even dared to sneak in here."

......

The response from Xia Chuan and others was rather intense. They swiftly drew their weapons and rushed forward to encircle Fu Tiandu.

"No need to panic, step back!"

But Xia Hong showed little reaction, first motioning for everyone to step back, then continued watching Fu Tiandu, who was undergoing beast transformation, with a strong sense of curiosity arising in his eyes.

This is, after all, the Great Xia Main Hall; not to mention Yangxu Tripod; nearly every high-ranking member present, including Xia Chuan, wore the Suppression Talisman; let alone the hall full of silver.

That deceitful monster, being able to sneak in with Fu Tiandu is astonishing enough, yet it could still act in front of them, making Fu Tiandu transform?

"Huh...huh..."

Seeing Fu Tiandu’s increasingly severe beast transformation, with silver-white fur growing all over his body, Xia Hong could remain seated no longer. He disappeared from the main seat and reappeared beside Fu Tiandu the next second, grasping his arm.
