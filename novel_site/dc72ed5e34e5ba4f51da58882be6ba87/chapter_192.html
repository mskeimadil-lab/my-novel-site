Chapter 192: Chapter 135: The Fearless Mirror Immortal [Three in One]_4

Zhao Long, along with the other eight people, dispersed and climbed up the trees, setting up their longbows in the shadows and aiming at several people in the Mirror Immortal Camp whose positions had been exposed. It was obvious they all understood Xia Hong’s intentions.

Meanwhile, Xia Hong led Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan to directly approach.

As they drew closer, the three clearly saw the true form of the enormous creature.

It was a Golden-eyed Demon Sheep, something all three were quite familiar with. However, this one stretched nearly nine meters in length and stood five to six meters high at the shoulder, its stature comparable to a building. Its back was covered with white mane that hung down four or five meters on both sides, resembling two massive grey-white curtains.

Most terrifying was its back, which bore sixteen pairs of golden eyes as large as lanterns.

Five of these eyes had already been hit by iron arrows, and blood was gushing out;

The remaining eleven eyes intermittently emitted blinding golden rays, like sharp arrows, targeting the person wielding the broadsword and the other archers hiding in the surrounding trees.

The Golden-eyed Demon Sheep’s massive body made the ground tremble with its charge, with a force even greater than the Snow Mane from earlier, smashing the surrounding trees in its path.

In just a short while, more than a dozen trees around had been smashed and knocked down, leaving no intact tree within nearly a hundred meters.

As they took a clear look at the Golden-eyed Demon Sheep, Xia Hong and the other two also saw the person battling it clearly.

Being too far away, they couldn’t see his face, only that he was burly, dressed in black, gripping a giant broadsword about 1.5 meters long and over twenty centimeters wide with both hands.

The broadsword looked exaggerated even in shape, despite its lack of an edge.

Ordinary people probably wouldn’t even be able to lift it.

Yet, in the hands of this person, it was as light as if it weighed nothing.

He swung, blocked, chopped, smashed, and maneuvered fluidly.

What’s more, he could adjust his body using the momentum of the broadsword, continuously circling with the Demon Sheep, and even occasionally managed to inflict deep cuts on the Demon Sheep’s body with the sword.

Iron arrows from the trees around them continuously aimed at the Demon Sheep, targeting its golden eyes on the back, with one person’s arrows being incredibly precise, always catching the Golden Sheep just as it tried to emit a blinding light to suppress the man in black, preventing the Golden Sheep from casting its strong light.

Bam...

The Demon Sheep seized an opportunity to lower its head and charge, catching the black-clad man off guard. With no choice, he raised the broadsword to shield himself, unwillingly being knocked into the air with the sword.

Taking this opportunity, the Golden Sheep on the Demon Sheep’s back simultaneously opened its eyes, ignoring the iron arrows shooting from the surrounding trees, and directly unleashed eleven beams of golden light at the man in black mid-air.

Thud... thud......

"Roar..."

The sounds of seven or eight iron arrows piercing eyeballs echoed across the entire field, followed closely by the painful roars of the Demon Sheep.

However, Xia Hong’s gaze was fixed on the man in black, noticing a beam of light penetrating his abdomen, causing his eyebrows to twitch slightly.

"Brother, was he hit?"

"Yes, he was hit. The man in black should be injured, and quite seriously."

Although Xia Hong’s eyebrows only twitched slightly, Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan beside him couldn’t contain their joy, their faces lighting up as they spoke to Xia Hong.

"Hold on, don’t speak too soon. They have considerable strength. This Demon Sheep is doomed!"

Though the Demon Sheep managed to injure the man in black, all eleven golden eyes on its back were taken out by a round of iron arrows.

"Roar... Roar... Roar..."

With all sixteen golden eyes on its back blinded, the Demon Sheep seemed to have its last bit of ferocity ignited, its body leaping forward as it roared angrily.

The black-clad man landed from afar and had just torn a strip of cloth to bind his abdominal wound when the Demon Sheep was already charging fiercely towards him.

"Boss!"

"Brothers, we can’t let this beast hurt the boss."

"This accursed beast’s eyes are all blind, there’s nothing to fear about it!"

"Charge!"

...

Several roars were heard from the surrounding trees, and over twenty people leapt down, immediately charging towards the Demon Sheep.

These people had bows on their backs, obviously the ones who had been shooting arrows moments earlier. They each held a large knife, exuding a fearless and robust aura as they charged with their battle cries.

Of course, they were fast.

But the Demon Sheep was faster.

The man in black, whom they called ’boss,’ clearly didn’t rely on these twenty-plus individuals, and having bound his wound, he raised his broadsword to dodge sideways.

This Intermediate Demon Sheep had six sharp horns, each two to three meters long, and when it charged, its horizontal attack range was astonishingly large.

Though the man in black was quick, humans inherently had size disadvantages compared to the Demon Sheep, not to mention being slower than it.

Thud...

Just as Xia Hong anticipated, the man in black’s left shoulder was directly pierced by one of the Demon Sheep’s horns, yet the following scene completely exceeded Xia Hong’s expectations.

The man in black, with his left shoulder pierced, didn’t hesitate for a moment, grabbing the broadsword with both hands, he swung it downward, circumventing the Demon Sheep’s horns, and went for its neck.

Thud...

"This strength surpasses mine..."

Watching the descending broadsword being thrust more than half a meter into the Demon Sheep’s neck, Xia Hong’s pupils contracted, his mind slightly shaken.

He had just experienced how the skeleton and surface of an Intermediate Cold Beast were back then. Seeking to pierce the throat at that close range required an explosive force of no less than forty thousand pounds, by his estimates.

Even with his long fist technique, he could only exert a force of thirty-six thousand pounds at most.

This man in black clearly had greater basic strength than himself.

With the broadsword embedded in its neck, the intense pain caused the Demon Sheep to stagger.

It tried to stabilize its body by forcing its limbs, but the twenty-some people coming down from the trees didn’t give it the chance.

The twenty individuals divided their tasks clearly, with ten charging at its head, and the remaining ten-plus dividing into five groups, attacking its limbs and tail.

Due to the Demon Sheep’s massive size, they had barely made a few slashes before its casually bouncing limbs kicked them away.

Broken bones, spitting blood, severed hands, severed feet, knives kicked away, bodies thrown over ten meters away, some even severely injured or unconscious, unclear if alive or dead.

The fate of those twenty or so people was none other than these outcomes.

What shocked Xia Hong, Xia Chuan, and Luo Yuan was that, aside from those severely injured or unconscious, everyone else who could move, without exception, picked up their knives and charged back into the fray.

They seemed entirely unafraid of death.

With the continuous assault of twenty-plus people and the relentless broadsword strikes to the Demon Sheep’s head by the leading black-clad man, over a hundred breaths later, the Demon Sheep’s limbs finally began to tremble, and its body started to sway with exhaustion.

"Hold on just a bit longer, brothers, quickly, this Demon Sheep is about to fall."

"The more I hit it, the blunter it’s become, damn it!"

"No worries, we can sharpen it when we get back, Iron is nothing!"

"Boss, how are you?"

Seeing the Demon Sheep teetering, those still swinging knives around excitedly cried out, with one person even showing concern for the man in black, asking with a hint of worry.

"I’m fine, all of you withdraw for now, this Demon Sheep no longer has the strength to escape... "

Having been on the sidelines for a while, Xia Hong, Xia Chuan, and Luo Yuan heard the black-clad man speak for the first time, and they were all taken aback. They didn’t even realize the man in black paused in the middle of speaking.

This man’s voice was surprisingly young.

No, it was excessively young.

Judging by his tone, he seemed to be as young as Xia Chuan, just sixteen or seventeen years old.

"Let’s welcome the friends from Great Xia together!"

The three up in the trees were a bit stupefied but upon hearing the latter part of the black-clad man’s words, their expressions changed slightly.

He actually noticed them.

Since they were discovered, they given up hiding, and jumped directly down from the trees.

With his gaze cast down, Xia Hong led the other two to walk slowly forward, stopping only when they were just over ten meters away from the people.

"You’ve brought so many people trespassing into Great Xia Territory and hunting without permission, this term ’friend,’ Xia Hong, cannot accept!"
