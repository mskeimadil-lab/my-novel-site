Chapter 1020: Chapter 366: Reinforcements Gather, Rabble, Unexpected Delight, War Breaks Out (Part 4)

Han Feng!

Inside the room, Wang Tao, Lin Fan, Zhu Shun, and everyone else slightly changed their expressions, and suddenly stood up.

Lu Yang had seen Han Feng while traveling last night, but he hadn’t mentioned it to the people in the room. So their big reaction was purely because they heard Han Feng’s name.

It’s understandable—Han Feng is the son of Hanqiong’s leader Han Jiuli, and his talent is not low. He is quite reputable in Longyou, and everyone from Cold Resistance Level from there, including Wang Tao, would naturally have heard of him.

The key point is, Lu Yang has hardly stopped searching for Han Feng’s whereabouts with the East Ridge people for over three years, so everyone in the room knew what was going on, which is why the reaction was so big.

"Han Feng, he went to Jin Mountain!"

"We must get rid of him."

"Sir."

.........

"Calm down a bit!"

Lu Yang was also slightly shaken at the moment, but he still first calmed everyone down, then slowly walked to the door and opened it with his hand.

Five years ago, when Han Feng escaped through the secret passage, he was not yet thirty; now he should just be a little over thirty, which is quite young compared to his cultivation level.

"Seventeen-maned, the strength is almost like mine."

As soon as Lu Yang opened the door, he immediately judged Han Feng’s strength, and then directly invited him inside.

When Han Feng saw Lu Yang, his expression was obviously complicated, and then when he saw the group inside the room and recognized several familiar faces from Longyou, who were glaring at him, he took a small breath and walked straight in.

"Brother Han, you really made us search for you these years!"

Lu Yang casually closed the door while Li Hu and several of the Cold Resistance Level quickly blocked the entrance. Wang Tao, Lin Fan, and Zhu Shun also stood up and discreetly surrounded Han Feng.

"Brother Lu, sirs, there’s no need for this. Leader Xia showed me mercy back then, and I’m visiting today to avoid misunderstandings. I won’t tell Jinshan Town about East Ridge, nor will I reveal your identities."

Upon hearing Han Feng’s words, Lu Yang’s expression didn’t change much; he just stared at him for a moment before speaking in a deep voice: "Brother Han not only knows East Ridge but has even found out my name. Seems you know quite a lot!"

Han Feng had just knocked and asked if it was East Ridge Camp, and then immediately called out his own name, evidently having researched a lot before coming.

Although it was known that Xia Hong had let Han Feng go, and the surviving members of the Hanqiong Camp were mostly personally saved by Xia Hong, Han Feng indeed owed him a great favor.

But the passage in East Ridge is far too important!

Also, what does it mean that Han Feng took the initiative to visit today?

Is he intending to blackmail me with this?

Lu Yang dared not gamble; he watched Han Feng still weighing whether to risk at Hongguan Camp to finally shut Han Feng up.

Han Feng clearly saw through Lu Yang’s thoughts and showed a faint bitter smile, quickly saying: "Brother Lu, rest assured; I came to you today because I just want to inquire about the whereabouts of my two sisters."

......

Hearing Han Feng’s words, Lu Yang was taken aback.

The others also looked serious; Wang Tao recalled something and said with slightly furrowed brows: "Are your two sisters called Han Yue and Han Shuang?"

Han Feng’s face immediately showed a joyful expression, nodding heavily at Wang Tao.

Han Yue, Han Shuang...

Lu Yang’s expression instantly relaxed, along with Zhu Shun, Lin Fan, and all Cold Resistance Level in the room, their demeanor suddenly more relaxed.

Seeing the people in the room change their expressions, Han Feng realized they were obviously familiar with his sisters and quickly looked up to ask Lu Yang: "Brother Lu, do you all know my two sisters?"

"More than that—your two sisters are now quite popular in the Great Xia..."

There are very few with the surname Han; even among eighty thousand people in the Great Xia, there aren’t many. The most famous, and whose age matches Han Feng’s sister, is the maid serving in the Star-picking Hall for Lord Xia Hong’s family, the sisters Han Yue and Han Shuang.

These two, unexpectedly, are Han Feng’s sisters!

Lu Yang spoke with Han Feng about Han Yue and Han Shuang while his thoughts began to engage as well.

Han Feng on the other side, after hearing that his two sisters became maids to Lord Xia Hong and broke through to Cold Resistance Level, had a face full of joy.

Five years ago, when he left Hanqiong Station with a thousand people, his biggest regret was not finding his two own sisters. It wasn’t that he didn’t want to take them, but the chaos caused by the White Dew Monster-confined Wu Xiong in Hanqiong Gourd Valley was disastrous, with many dead or on the run; he simply couldn’t find his sisters.

For five years, he lived with regret every day. This time, summoned by Jinshan Town to aid Jiangxia, he remembered upon seeing Lu Yang because five years ago, when Xia Hong let him leave through a secret passage, Lu Yang was standing right behind him.

Combined with Lu Yang’s reaction at the time, he was more certain that the Great Xia had already cleared out the secret passage and someone had infiltrated Beishuo Town, which is why he bravely came to Lu Yang to inquire.

Now after getting the answer he wanted and knowing his two sisters are living well in the Great Xia, with high cultivation, he felt naturally more at ease.

"Thank you, Brother Lu, for telling me these things. Five years ago, I suspected they weren’t dead, and having left Longyou through the secret passage, I’ve worried every day for how they fared these five years. Now I can finally be at ease."
