Chapter 270: Chapter 156: Mirror Immortal’s 10 Days, Gathering Enough Beast Blood_2

"In the past few days, the brothers have seen it clearly. Let alone hunting, their people only dare to operate within a one-kilometer radius around the valley. They don’t even dare to enter the depths of Redwood Ridge, which shows they are frightened of us to the core. In that case, why wait for ten days? We should set off immediately."

......

Yuwen Hu’s face turned even uglier. After all, he was the Second Leader of the Mirror Immortal Camp, and before he could even finish speaking, he was openly refuted, which showed that the people in front of him had no respect for him at all.

"Could it be that the Second Leader has once again suggested something to the Great Leader?"

"Young Master Dao did it before, and now even the Second Leader is starting to speak for that Xia Hong. Could it be that Yuanzhou Camp had some previous relations with this Great Xia?"

"It makes sense to think about it, Xia Hong hasn’t even broken through to the Cold Resistance Level, yet the Second Leader, with such powerful strength, has failed to deal with him multiple times. Could it be..."

The few people below, as if they hadn’t noticed his expression, continued with their mockery one after the other.

They clearly blamed the matter of waiting ten days on him.

Clap...

"Are you all deaf? It was the Leader’s order!"

Seeing his father being coldly mocked, Yuwen Dao finally couldn’t hold back anymore. He slammed the table in anger and stood up directly.

The five uncles around him also immediately stood up with him, glaring angrily at those who had just spoken.

If it were a different setting or different people, Yuwen Dao would certainly have been able to control the situation with this move. But today, the people present didn’t even respect Yuwen Hu, the Second Leader, let alone him.

"Oh, Young Master Dao..."

The person leading the group was about to mock Yuwen Dao, but just as he uttered three words, a sharp whooshing sound came from the rear of the upper compartment.

Whoosh...

A sharp, white airflow flew straight past that person’s left ear like a sleeve arrow, yet its speed was countless times faster than a sleeve arrow.

Not to mention seeing it clearly, by the time people heard the sound, the airflow was already pinned to the wall of the bamboo house.

The person who had just spoken stood there expressionless, not knowing what had happened.

The others also didn’t know what had happened, just stared at his ear.

One breath, two breaths, three breaths...

Plop...

The person’s left ear suddenly fell off onto the ground.

The ear dropped, revealing a sharp and neat wound, with the blood even taking a second or two before it started flowing.

At this moment, everyone turned their heads to look at the wall to find that the thing that had brought forth the white airflow was merely a small, snow-white bamboo leaf.

A small bamboo leaf, no bigger than a thumb, had directly sliced off the ear of someone already beginning to reshape their skin in the Earth-digging Realm, which was already absurd enough.

After being sliced off, the ear fell after three breaths, and blood only flowed from the wound five breaths later.

The speed and power at which this bamboo leaf was just shot...

Only Yuwen Hu, among the entire crowd, looked at the bamboo leaf with trembling pupils.

Bang...

The person who had just spoken, including the ones who had also mocked Yuwen Hu and his son, turned pale and immediately knelt down.

"Where did you get the courage to be so disrespectful to the Second Leader?"

The voice from the compartment, as gentle as jade with just a slight hint of anger, made everyone in the room afraid to even breathe.

The people kneeling on the ground trembled uncontrollably.

Only Yuwen Hu, among the entire crowd, maintained a somewhat normal expression. But even he slightly bowed after the voice from the compartment sounded.

"Second Brother, continue speaking!"

Upon hearing the voice inside, Yuwen Hu straightened up, turned to the people below, and solemnly continued the unfinished words:

"Within these ten days, no one is allowed to approach Great Xia Valley. After ten days, the Leader will personally lead all 271 Earth-digging Realm people from the camp to attack Great Xia. The rest will stay with me to guard the camp!"

As Yuwen Hu’s words fell, the people kneeling on the ground immediately showed joyful expressions, including the person who had just lost an ear.

Meanwhile, Yuwen Dao and the other five initially appeared puzzled, but after thinking for a moment, their expressions quickly darkened considerably.

The expression on Yuwen Hu’s face, the one who gave the order, became even more unpleasant.

"Alright, everyone go prepare!"

"Yes, Leader."

The voice from the compartment sounded, and everyone bowed and left one after another, soon all departing.

A sharp screech faintly echoed from the empty compartment.

"Jie jie jie jie, watch, see, it’s all mine, it’s all mine, you won’t make it, you won’t make it."

......

The wooden house next to the bamboo building.

As soon as Yuwen Hu returned, his son Yuwen Dao, with a difficult expression, approached.

"Taking 271 Earth-digging Realm individuals past is clearly excluding you, and counting even myself, the 43 who haven’t joined the Mirror Immortal. Father, what exactly does the Leader mean?"

Yuwen Hu didn’t answer his son’s question. He merely sat down on a chair, looking at the silver-edged hooped jade pendant in his hand, frowning in thought.

Seeing the hooped jade pendant, Yuwen Dao’s expression changed subtly, and he leaned closer to Yuwen Hu, asking quietly: "Father, has the jade pendant reacted?"

Yuwen Hu shook his head slightly and said, "No."

"Then it’s purely to exclude us. We’ve been in the Mirror Immortal for more than a year, Father, you, me, and Uncle Hong, every one of us has fought life and death for the camp. Just because we haven’t joined the Mirror Immortal due to such an absurd reason, the Leader is now taking them to exclude us?"
