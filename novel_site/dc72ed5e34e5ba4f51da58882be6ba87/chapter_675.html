Chapter 675: Chapter 281: Yang Li’s Suppressed Anger and Hatred (Part 3)

There was no other way. Li Yuanqing and Yang Gan’s surveillance and suppression of them had practically reached a perverse level.

It could be seen from the number of people in the Hunting Team. If it were an ordinary Hunting Team with two members about to break through the Earth-digging Realm limit, other Earth-digging Realm members would be vying to join.

But this Hunting Team, because of Li Yuanqing and Yang Gan’s suppression, only recruited five people, with Yang Yue being one of them sent by the two.

Thinking about the reason Li Yuanqing suppressed him, Li Hu found it both hateful and laughable.

Li Yuanqing was actually afraid that he would perform too well, overshadowing him, thereby gaining Li Xuantiang’s attention, which might eventually lead to Li Tiancheng’s notice.

Honestly, the person Li Hu hated, from the beginning to the end, was only one—Zhu Rou, who forced his mother to death. Not to mention Li Yuanqing, but even towards Li Xuantiang, his father, he felt nothing, let alone Li Tiancheng and other people from Long Mountain.

Yet Li Yuanqing seemed to be crazed, from the moment he started cultivation, he desperately suppressed him, fearing that his cultivation speed would be slightly faster.

Coincidentally, similar was Yang Gan, his half-brother. He was no different.

The narrow-mindedness of these two was unfathomable to Li Hu and Yang Zhong at times.

But the reality was like this; the two of them had no choice but to find a way to cope with it.

"Fortunately, things are different now. We are already people of Great Xia. With the resources given by Minister, breaking through the Cold Resistance Level is a matter of time, and avenging is no longer an issue. Moreover, if we can help Great Xia defeat Long Mountain in the future, both Yang Zhong and I will have limitless prospects..."

Li Hu pondered silently as he soon walked out of Long Mountain and arrived at the camp.

The resource management in Long Mountain was extremely strict; they had to first submit their prey to the camp warehouse before they could take away their share. If they wanted to exchange for other resources later, they would have to use their own share to trade.

The ratio was seventy to thirty, meaning for a prey yielding a thousand pounds of meat, they could take away three hundred pounds. For a single person, it’s a lot, but when divided among seven people, each would only get about forty pounds.

Tonight’s prey included two animals; both he and Yang Zhong managed to each get over a hundred pounds, which was already considered a good harvest.

Of course, that was before.

Ever since they’ve tasted Great Xia’s salt and Dan Medicine, Yang Zhong was no longer interested in ordinary Beast Meat;

Needless to say for Li Hu, who just broke through the Earth-digging Realm limit tonight, his mind was set on the Beast Blood hidden in Long Mountain, uninterested in the Beast Meat.

"Let’s head back, leave the Beast Meat there; after some time, we’ll trade it for a set of clothes."

"No point, I’ll keep mine too, and when we gather enough, trade them for some Healing Medicine."

After leaving the warehouse, the two headed straight for home.

Both had decent cultivation and each had their own housing in the camp, conveniently located next to each other.

Exiting the central area of the camp, they soon reached the outskirts near their homes, where they usually wore smiling faces seeing their houses.

Today, things were clearly different.

More than a hundred meters away from their homes, both their expressions concurrently darkened.

Their homes had been intruded upon by many people, with their personal belongings scattered at the doorstep, and some were rummaging through their things inside, obviously searching for something...
