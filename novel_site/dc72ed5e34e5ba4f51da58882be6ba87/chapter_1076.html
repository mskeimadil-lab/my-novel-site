Chapter 1076 - 377: Five-Headed Monster Face Maitreya, Five Organs and Five Elements Exorcism Method, Great Xia Advances East (Part 3)

Chapter 1076: Chapter 377: Five-Headed Monster Face Maitreya, Five Organs and Five Elements Exorcism Method, Great Xia Advances East (Part 3)

As long as it absorbs the over one million soul bodies in Bashang City, it will be fully resurrected!

Realizing this, Xia Hong no longer hesitated for a moment and immediately understood that he had to make a move.

This soul-absorbing ability alone was enough to prove that the Five-headed Mysterious-faced Maitreya was beyond his reach at this stage. If it revived, not only would the Nine Towns suffer, but the Great Xia would also face disaster.

To say it’s bad luck is an understatement; the consequences would be far more severe.

Having decided to take action, the next step was to find the right opportunity!

He could see at a glance that among the Five Great Masters, it was the Master Wusheng, with glowing green pupils, who was the dominant force. So he hid in the shadows, constantly observing Master Wusheng’s state.

Coincidentally, it happened at this very moment!

Master Wusheng had been cooperating with the Spiritual Body of the Mysterious-faced Maitreya to absorb the souls in the city. But just now, he seemed to be hit by something, and a trace of golden light appeared in his pupils, and his expression suddenly became dazed.

Xia Hong, who had been watching Master Wusheng closely, could not miss this opportunity!

Having been lurking in the city for a long time, he seized this moment to employ all his means, launching a sudden attack, stabbing with a single strike into the lower right side of Master Wusheng’s chest.

The thick smoke emerging from the wounds on both the front and back of Master Wusheng’s body fully confirmed many of Xia Hong’s suspicions, so at this moment, he was no longer as worried as before.

Even if pursued simultaneously by the soul body of the Mysterious-faced Maitreya and the Four Great Masters, he did not let go of Master Wusheng, directly colliding with him, carrying his body and flying away from above the soul of the Mysterious-faced Maitreya.

"You wretch, let go!"

Of the Four Great Masters behind him, Yan Long Master was the fastest to catch up with Xia Hong. His eyes cold, he raised the long spear in his hand, conjuring a giant flame dragon, thrusting it directly towards Xia Hong’s back.

Whoosh......

The red long spear instantly evacuated the surrounding airflow, forming a fan-shaped spear edge with a radius of two to three miles. The spear tip arrived in an instant, pressing against Xia Hong’s back, piercing through the skin membrane and into the flesh, stopping as it hit his bone.

It stopped just at the bone.

Yan Long Master discovered that his long spear could not penetrate any further, and his expression did not change much. He had fought Xia Hong once last year and was the first to sense and say that Xia Hong was not from the Nine Towns.

Knowing Xia Hong’s strength, he did not continue to try to penetrate the bone, instead, he suddenly swept leftward fiercely, attempting to drive Xia Hong away to rescue Master Wusheng.

His spear force was astonishing. Although it couldn’t break Xia Hong’s bones, breaking through the skin membrane was not a problem at all. Normally, a person would certainly dodge to the side under such a sweep.

But Xia Hong did not!

Xia Hong gritted his teeth, using his back to withstand Yan Long Master’s blow, allowing a large chunk of his back’s flesh to be cut open, then suddenly reached for a silver short dagger from his waist with his left hand, turned half around, and stabbed fiercely towards Yan Long Master’s heart.

Slash......

Xia Hong’s Basic Strength was close to eleven jun. As the ancestor of the Holy Pattern Bloodline, he had a fifty percent strength boost, plus a thirty percent boost from Long Fist. Even using a dagger without the addition of Martial Arts Skills, his strength was over an astonishing nineteen jun.

The dagger pierced downward, and not just the heart area but the entire surrounding region was forcibly smashed through.

The dagger, along with his fist, pierced directly through Yan Long Master’s back.

A normal person’s heart being pierced like this would certainly result in immediate death without question.

But Yan Long Master not only did not die but looked down at his heart area, then raised his head to look at Xia Hong, his pupils filled with shock and astonishment:

"How... how do you... know..."

His heart was now in Xia Hong’s grasp.

Unlike a normal human’s red heart, Yan Long Master’s heart was purely black and had a strange face on it, identical to that of the Five-headed Mysterious-faced Maitreya.

Hiss......

Xia Hong still had his silver dagger inserted in it, and like the right side of Master Wusheng’s chest that was pierced earlier, it was emitting thick black smoke, clearly reacting violently with the silver.

Yan Long Master, like a deflated ball, instantly weakened and became just like the earlier Master Wusheng, unable to resist under Xia Hong’s grip, seemingly lacking even the strength to move.

Master Wusheng had just been successfully ambushed by Xia Hong, and the other Four Great Masters had directly chased after him. Seeing Yan Long Master severely injured and clearly on the brink of death, the remaining Vajra Master, Hei Ming Master, and Ju Yuan Master immediately halted in mid-air, eyeing Xia Hong cautiously, their pupils full of apprehension and doubt.

"Last year, when he didn’t dare to take my chest slash, I realized!"

Xia Hong directly answered Yan Long Master’s question, glancing lightly at Vajra Master, a gleam flashing in his eyes, continuing: "The lungs hide the soul, associated with metal in the five elements, governing the nose. The Six Paths Vajra Mantra just happens to be a method of inhaling Zen Gold Powder through the nose into the lungs, to strengthen the body;

The heart hides the spirit, associated with fire in the five elements, governing the tongue. The Lengyan Divine Spell involves placing Fire Melting Pills under the tongue, concentrating the fiery spirit to resist cold light;

The liver hides the soul, associated with wood in the five elements, governing the eyes. Although your Rebirth Mantra functions differently from the previous two, in many situations, it basically corresponds as well, so naturally, your vulnerable point is the liver area!"

The liver, located on the lower right side of the chest, is exactly where Xia Hong stabbed with the Dragon Sparrow Saber.
