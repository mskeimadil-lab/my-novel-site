I consulted the leaders of eleven camps, and currently, there are only six known Xiang Han Trees, with an estimated annual harvest of less than 2000 jin."

To mix 100 jin, you need to add 2 jin of Intermediate Beast Blood to yield 1 jin of liquor. With a total annual output of 2000 jin, that means at most, only 20 jin can be produced per year.

Xia Hong slightly shook his head, placed the wine jug behind him without a trace, and turned directly to look at Cheng Feng, saying: "What are the four bottles you brought?"

Everyone laughed silently as Xia Hong put aside the wine jug; after all, the Cold Blood Wine was especially made for Xia Hong, and seeing him so fond of it, Mu Dong felt naturally happy.

Cheng Feng stepped forward at this moment and sequentially opened the four bottles he was holding.

The rich aroma of medicine spread out, somewhat diluting the fragrance of the Cold Blood Wine, allowing everyone to regain their clarity.

"Recently, the camp’s resource supply has become abundant, allowing the search range of the collection department to expand. In the Arrow Bamboo Forest and Five Ape Mountain areas, we discovered a lot of resources, including a variety of medicinal ingredients. The Medicine Workshop developed more than a dozen recipes from these."

Among these, I deemed four to be particularly important, so I brought them for the leader to see."

Cheng Feng displayed the pills from each of the four bottles and explained the effects of these four types of Dan Medicine to everyone.

Everyone listened with visible astonishment, unable to suppress the joy on their faces.

The Blood Sore Pill, an upgraded version of the Golden Sore Powder, offers better healing effects, capable of quickly recovering Earth-digging Realm injuries, though its effects on the Cold Resistance Level remain unknown.

The Hot Wind Pill can quickly heal typhoid, regarded as an upgraded version of the typhoid medicine brought from Five Ape Mountain.

The Blood Explosion Pill can temporarily block all physical pain.

The Beast Essence Pill can improve the efficiency of the human body’s absorption of Beast Meat.

"Beast Essence Pill!"

The first three already piqued Xia Hong’s interest, but upon hearing the description of the Beast Essence Pill, Xia Hong immediately reached out, took the bottle, and sniffed it under his nose, his expression becoming instantly excited.

"The medicine is so mild, essentially usable for anyone over the age of six!"

Hearing Xia Hong’s words, Cheng Feng nodded emphatically, excitedly saying: "Leader, you are impressive; you realized at a glance that this Beast Essence Pill is indeed very mild. Children we tested around six years old showed an increase in absorption efficiency by about three to five percent."

Three to five percent!

Not only Xia Hong, but Xia Chuan, Shi Ping, and Mu Dong all had their eyes light up instantly.

Three to five percent may not compare to the effects of the Martial Arts Hall, but the key point is, it can be used by children and adults over six!

The Martial Arts Hall can only accommodate 200 people a day, and it requires Contribution Points to qualify. Considering the camp’s reliance on advanced combat power, currently, only those in the Earth-digging Realm, due to Xia Chuan allocating half the quota to Logging Realm, are allowed in for training.

The reserves over the age of six aren’t eligible to enter, nor do they have the conditions, as they don’t even have Contribution Value yet.

"The ingredients for making Beast Essence Pill consist of more than ten types. The rarest is called Jade Bone Grass, which Lord Minister brought back from Five Ape Mountain last time. I’ve already sent the collection department to search that area these past few days. If the production increases, the Logging Realm in our Daxia Camp will definitely become younger, and maybe one day, even children as young as seven or eight years old might break through to the Logging Realm!"

Everyone, hearing this, was visibly shocked.

A seven- or eight-year-old in the Logging Realm, who would dare to imagine that!

"Have you ever thought that maybe one day, everyone in our Great Xia will be able to cultivate, and even children as young as five or six could have Logging Realm strength?"

Xia Chuan’s expression froze, as a memory of Xia Hong once telling him this came to mind.

His brother had said this just over a year ago.

That day seems not far away!

"These three types of Dan Medicine are also important, but for now, we still prioritize making the Beast Essence Pill. All Dan Medicines from the Medicine Workshop can be placed in the Camp Supply Department. Pricing according to production and demand is open for exchange to everyone. Of course, this applies to several other things as well..."

Xia Hong returned the medicine bottles to Cheng Feng, then glanced at the nine items he saw today, showing slight emotion.

Reflecting upon earlier, hearing about the camp’s population swelling to twenty-three thousand, Xia Hong felt a sense of understanding, he was no longer surprised by the new developments in front of him.

The expansion of activity areas, the broadening range of collection, the surge in population, the improvement in strength, the expansion of demand... All these factors combined will inevitably lead to a leap in productivity development.

These nine items should just be the beginning!

After all, 18,000 people just recently merged, surely after some fermentation, new developments will continuously emerge.

"Mu Dong and Cheng Feng did a great job. Each of these nine items gets a reward of ten thousand Contribution Value, divided among everyone involved. Xia Chuan, make sure the entire camp knows about this!"

"Thank you, Leader!"

Upon hearing this, Mu Dong and Cheng Feng showed expressions of extreme joy.

Ten thousand Contribution Value!

In the current camp, it’s incredibly valuable. Even though it needs to be shared with others, both of them will still receive a considerable amount of Contribution Points!

"Understood, brother!"

Receiving the assignment, Xia Chuan’s eyes also showed a glimmer.
