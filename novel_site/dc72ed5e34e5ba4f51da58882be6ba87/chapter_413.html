Chapter 413: Chapter 198: Unblocking Acupoints, Breakthrough Imminent

"He... Big brother..."

"Don’t talk, focus on cultivation, it’s almost finished!"

Seeing Xia Chuan trying to speak, Xia Hong interrupted him immediately.

Then, he walked slowly to Xia Chuan’s side, his left eye flashing lightly.

Threads of beast blood mist continuously drilled into Xia Chuan’s skin pores. These pores would tear open and expand instantly when filled with the blood mist, and then repair themselves to form a tougher and thicker skin layer.

"This Eye of Truth has such wonderful applications!"

A hint of joy appeared in Xia Hong’s eyes as he continued to observe Xia Chuan’s skin membrane.

Soon he discovered that at some key acupuncture points on the skin membrane, there would be a phenomenon of blockage and stagnation. As these phenomena appeared, the pain on Xia Chuan’s face would also intensify.

"Don’t move, let me try something. Tell me how it feels!"

Xia Hong’s expression slightly tightened, he clasped his right hand’s two fingers together, his arm muscles flexing, using subtle force, aiming for an acupuncture point on Xia Chuan’s right shoulder, gently pressed.

"Pop..."

His basic strength was terrifyingly powerful, even with a gentle touch, Xia Chuan’s shoulder made a pop sound.

However, as the sound was made, the pain on Xia Chuan’s face suddenly lessened significantly. He endured the pain to speak: "It’s very comfortable, big brother!"

Xia Hong looked at the position he had just pressed, his eyes brightened instantly.

It wasn’t because of Xia Chuan’s response, but because at the moment in Xia Hong’s eyes, the point he had touched was smoothly infiltrated by the blood mist.

"I can use external force to destroy skin pores to allow blood mist to pass through smoothly."

Having reached this conclusion, Xia Hong’s expression became increasingly excited, using both hands to tap all blocked acupuncture points on Xia Chuan’s body.

"Hoo..."

Using both hands while controlling his arm with subtle force, he could only tap with two fingers, which was more exhausting than striking with full force. Half an hour passed, Xia Hong was already drenched in sweat, his clothes soaked through.

In contrast, the look of pain on Xia Chuan’s face decreased, even showing a hint of enjoyment towards the end, the blood mist on the skin membrane was completely absorbed, and his skin became more radiant.

"Big brother, are you okay?"

Xia Chuan stood up and saw Xia Hong looking exhausted, not even having time to put on his clothes, asking worriedly.

Xia Hong waved slightly, saying: "I’m fine, the blocked acupuncture parts are not many, just need once to clear them. Next time, your beast blood dosage should be able to increase a lot, let me know then.

By the way, what is your basic strength now?"

A warm feeling passed through Xia Chuan’s heart. Hearing Xia Hong’s question, he immediately clenched his hard fist, his expression suddenly became excited.

"It’s already forty-seven thousand, I can feel that my skin remodeling progress should be only twenty percent, big brother, I should be a superior battle body, right?"

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong’s expression instantly revived.

Xia Chuan didn’t have a system; his perception of the skin remodeling progress was, of course, not as precise as his own, but being able to perceive twenty percent certainly wouldn’t be too far off.

The Earth-digging Realm limit is thirty thousand pounds, the skin remodeling progress just twenty percent, the basic strength has increased by seventeen thousand, for the next eighty percent progress, even at the minimum calculation, Xia Chuan’s final basic strength could reach up to one hundred and fifteen thousand pounds.

The increases of upper, middle, and lower battle bodies are respectively seventy thousand, fifty thousand, and thirty thousand pounds, according to the current increase, Xia Chuan has already exceeded the superior battle body.

The issue is, the increase cannot be calculated directly like this.

The further along, the greater the beast blood dosage, the greater the increase.

My last thirty percent progress made the strength soar directly to three hundred thousand pounds.

Recalling his skin remodeling process, Xia Hong turned to look at Xia Chuan, revealing a hint of anticipation on his face, saying:

"Not just that, maybe you, like me, are also a superior battle body!"

Upon hearing Xia Hong’s words, Xia Chuan’s face immediately became more excited.

The four types of battle bodies, superior, upper, middle, and lower, and the most common, breakthrough cold resistance level—these five distinctions are no longer a secret in Great Xia.

During this period, Xia Chuan had been very anxious.

After all, big brother Xia Hong is a superior battle body, and since Xia Hong could defeat He Yuanhun right after breaking through, it is estimated to be the most top-notch kind within the superior battle bodies.

With such an extraordinary example before him, as his younger brother, even if he were just a middle or lower battle body, it would be inadequate.

He desperately increased the use of beast blood dosage for this reason.

Now hearing from Xia Hong himself that he might be just like him, a superior battle body, the huge weight in Xia Chuan’s heart finally settled down.

Meanwhile, he became more expectant of breaking through the cold resistance level.

"Although Yuwen Dao’s progress is higher than yours, it is only forty percent. Seems like it will still take quite some time for you two to break through to the cold resistance level!"

Xia Hong shook his head while speaking, a trace of concern appearing on his face.

Xia Chuan noticed Xia Hong’s expression and hurriedly asked: "Big brother, is it because of the beast tide? Do you need Yuwen Dao and me to break through to cold resistance level soon?"

At present, the newly encountered trouble at the camp is probably the beast tide to the north in the Arrow Bamboo Forest. Associating with Xia Hong just returning from there, Xia Chuan naturally guessed it was related to the beast tide.

"Yes, but it’s not just the beast tide..."

Although the beast tide was partly the reason, Xia Hong also wanted to explore deep into the Arrow Bamboo Forest to thoroughly understand the cause of the beast tide. But going alone made him uncertain; having a few more helpers would naturally be safer.

On the other hand, it’s his own cultivation issue.

Until now, Xia Hong hadn’t figured out the specific division of the cold resistance level realms, nor did he know what the subsequent Visible Yang Realm was about.
