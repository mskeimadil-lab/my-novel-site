Chapter 1447: Chapter 461: Entering the Plain, Blood Jade Pearl, and the Terrifying Sea of Romantic Flowers (Part 2)

Interestingly, the size of the trees in the Blood Miasma Zone seems much more normal compared to those outside, their lengths unusually uniform, generally ranging from 10 to 20 meters, with the thickest diameter being half a meter.

Aside from the eerie color, simply looking at the scale of the trees in the Blood Miasma Zone gives Xia Hong the illusion of being back in Earth’s forests from his previous life.

"Different environments foster different vegetation, that’s nothing. What’s key is that these trees can grow normally in the Blood Miasma Zone, they are probably not ordinary things. The area Cai Qiu cleared has already spanned over 400 kilometers. Yesterday around Zhenyuan Camp, I didn’t see a single tree like these..."

Xia Hong’s eyes slightly moved when looking at the trees. Though he internally wanted to investigate, seeing Fan Qinghu already gathering people over there, he restrained himself and then moved towards the crowd.

Fan Qinghu had already taken out a Beast Skin Map Scroll from his arms and unfolded it in front of everyone, the upper part of the scroll marked as 21 Zone, clearly their previous exploration results of this area.

From the map situation, their overall exploration progress of Zone 21 is currently only about less than 30%.

"Heading eight kilometers southwest, there’s a small hill covered with Romantic Flowers. Last time Yun Wu and I led the team, we personally saw three Copper Bone Corpse Demons active on that hill. This time, our target is them.

We’ve figured out the route ahead of time. Though this route avoids the three Blood Cold Beast clans along the way, to prevent accidents, everyone still needs to stay vigilant. If we encounter isolated Cold Beasts or small-scale Beast Tide, we can act directly, but once the entire clan is provoked, we’ll retreat immediately without delay."

After Fan Qinghu finished speaking, he clenched the Big Saber at his waist, gestured to everyone, and then directly led the way southwest.

What followed was interesting, fifty Xianyang Level individuals lined up in a line, following behind him, advancing slowly.

Xia Hong’s eyes flashed with oddity, but after pondering for a moment, he quickly understood the reason for this.

Upon entering the Blood Miasma Plain, all Xianyang Levels must release their full power to resist the miasma’s invasion at all times. Consequently, they inevitably became beacons within the Miasma Area. If they scattered freely, it would surely attract powerful beings from unknown areas.

"Cai Yunlan previously told me that Cai Qiu’s exploration of Zone 21 has been ongoing for two months. After two months, they still have to be so cautious, is it truly a step-by-step crisis? Blood Cold Beast, if only I could witness it..."

Hiss...

The Blood Miasma Plain seemed to read Xia Hong’s mind. He hadn’t walked more than two kilometers following Cai Yunlan when his inner voice hadn’t even finished, and a crisp, piercing snake cry descended from the treetop on the left.

Hoo...

As soon as the snake cry rang out, six people flew out from the group, their positions meticulously controlled, surrounding the tree from front, back, left, right, and top. Without hesitation, they drew their Weapons and attacked the treetop where the Blood Python, bright red in color, was located.

"Two meters long, and in such an outer region, there’s an Advanced Cold Beast!"

There were a total of 51 people present. Xia Hong didn’t dare ensure he was the first to spot the Blood Python, but being among the first five was likely. He was momentarily stunned, noticing the Blood Jade Color of the small python and its two-meter length.

Where are they now?

Having walked less than two kilometers, there’s already an Advanced Cold Beast?

Roar...

The next moment, an astonishing scene unfolded.

The Blood Python, sensing its encirclement, roared angrily, twisted its body, and suddenly grew to several dozen meters long, precisely matching the snake-like Advanced Cold Beast size in Xia Hong’s impression.

The Blood Python opened its Blood Basin mouth, spraying black smoke, which instantly formed a long, black sword blade measuring several dozen meters, and swung it fiercely, directly striking at the four Xianyang Levels encircling it.

Chii...

The black sword blade’s edge, cutting through the dense blood-colored miasma, created cracks, slicing the surrounding trees in half, and when it hit the ground, it caused intense reactions, carving rifts into the earth.

"Blood Jade Xuan Snake, its expelled black smoke contains poison, don’t get hit. We’re close to their clan’s territory, Lin Wu, make it quick."

"Yes, Sir!"

Upon hearing Fan Qinghu’s command, the person at the very top responded as their Big Saber fiercely swung down, the powerful blade landing directly on the top of the Xuan Snake’s head.

Simultaneously, the four Xianyang Levels avoiding the sharp blade from the Xuan Snake’s mouth charged at four points on the Xuan Snake’s body, almost simultaneously raising their Weapons to stab, pinning the Xuan Snake’s body firmly into the ground.

"Ha..."

Crack!

Lastly, a person beneath the Xuan Snake chopped fiercely at its tail with a giant axe, and with the collaboration of the four people at the front, the only part the Xuan Snake could move was likely just its head.

Roar...

It suddenly twisted its head, madly spewing black smoke from its mouth, attempting to resist the Big Saber coming down from above, but under the strong cooperation of the five Xianyang Levels, how could Lin Wu, holding the Big Saber above, afford it such a chance?

Ka...

Lin Wu’s foundational strength was roughly at 15 Jun; this full-powered swing, descending from the sky, approached nearly 20 Jun.

The Blood Jade Xuan Snake’s head was directly hit, and its fate was as expected.
