Chapter 622: Chapter 262: Garrison Candidates, Martial Arts Enthusiasm, and the Eve of a Breakthrough Tide

The deeper purpose, of course, is for next month’s martial arts tournament.

Now the event of the martial arts competition is causing quite a stir, with three groups for Cold Resistance Level, Earth-digging Realm, and Logging Realm. Everyone knows about it.

The actions of the Zhao Yuandao siblings are clearly intended to reduce their competition, forcing those intentionally stuck at the Peak of Logging Realm to break through so they are reassigned to the Earth-digging Realm group. Any slightly clever person would quickly figure it out.

However, facing the provocations of the siblings, some people couldn’t resist taking the bait.

In the past five days, at least a dozen people at the Peak of Logging Realm got caught up in it. The matter caused quite a commotion, and it’s heard that some unsatisfied individuals reported the siblings to Lord Qiu Peng, who is in charge of the martial arts tournament.

Xiao Hen recently visited his sister’s house frequently, so he got to know quite a bit of insider information.

It’s said that Lord Qiu Peng, upon learning of this matter, just laughed it off and said that the siblings’ actions did not violate any rules, and those who got caught were simply unable to hold their temper, with no one else to blame but themselves.

Indeed, these people who haven’t even reached the Earth-digging Realm are just like kids in the eyes of Director Qiu Peng, and he naturally wouldn’t interfere with children’s antics.

However, Xiao Hen thought deeper. He believed that Director Qiu Peng’s words were not wrong; the fact is exactly so. The Zhao Yuandao siblings did not force anyone to accept their challenges; those who got caught really couldn’t keep their composure, with no one else to blame.

Recalling how Big Sister and Second Sister gathered him, Xiao Lin, and several other cousins together, solemnly instructing them to wait until after the martial arts tournament before breaking through, Xiao Hen was full of curiosity.

"Why such serious instructions? What exactly is the reward?"

Indeed, five days have passed since Qiu Peng announced the news of the Great Xia martial arts competition, but the specific rules of the tournament have yet to be published.

The rewards are even more elusive.

Everyone knows the rewards are generous, but what exactly they are is still unknown to anyone.

Suddenly, Xiao Lin came running over from another side of the Martial Arts Hall, panting heavily.

"A Hen, A Hen, I’ve found out, I’ve found out..."

His face was full of excitement, and he directly pulled Xiao Hen aside, lowered his voice, and continued, "The Earth-digging Realm competition system has come out. It’s said to be a selection system. Currently, the total number of people in the Great Xia Earth-digging Realm is over 4000. Each person initially has 10 matches, ranked according to the number of victories. If victory counts are the same, it depends on the duration of battles. The top 128 will advance, followed by seven rounds of elimination matches to determine the top ten."

Xiao Hen turned his head to look into the depths of the Martial Arts Academy, noticing those at the Earth-digging Realm limit indeed gathered together discussing, and immediately asked:

"What about the rewards? Have you found out about the rewards?"

Xiao Lin immediately replied, "They’ve already been announced, posted in front of the main building. We can’t go now."

Xiao Hen nodded. It was daylight outside, and they couldn’t leave the Martial Arts Academy, needing to wait until it was dark to check in front of the main building.

"But I just clearly heard the reward for the tenth place in the Earth-digging Realm group!"

"What is it? Quickly, tell me."

Upon hearing Xiao Lin’s follow-up, Xiao Hen immediately urged him with a face full of anticipation.

"Gulp..."

Xiao Lin first swallowed his saliva, then continued:

"For the tenth place, five Blood Essence Pills, 200 pounds of Beast Blood, 5000 Contribution Points, a customized Thousand Forged Weapon, a one-month exclusive training slot at the Martial Arts Academy, 20 pounds of aged wine, 50 pounds of refined salt."

Xiao Hen’s expression froze instantly, followed by rapid breathing, and his eyes became extremely hot.

Xiao Lin couldn’t help but start calculating: "50 pounds of refined salt, at the current Camp Supply Department price of 200 points per pound, this reward alone is worth 10,000 Contribution Points;

Aged wine is more expensive, requiring 400 points for 1 pound, so 20 pounds is 8000 points;

A one-month exclusive training slot at the Martial Arts Academy, currently costing 50 Contribution Points per day. Normally calculated, it’s only worth 1500, but this is for an exclusive one-month slot, meaning you can stay here for a month continuously, and the value could be over ten times that, just calculate it as 10 times;

A customized Thousand Forged Weapon; if I remember correctly, currently only the leader in our camp has one. The Craftsmen Department brought out two last month, but they were priced at 12000, and no one exchanged for them;

Beast Blood is currently 30 Contribution Points per pound, so 200 pounds is 4000 points;

Lastly, the five Blood Essence Pills; I’ve heard from those in the Earth-digging Realm that Blood Essence Pills are a type of Spirit Pill used only when breaking the 30,000 pounds limit in the Earth-digging Realm, and start reshaping skin with Beast Blood, currently priced at 700 points each by the Camp Supply Department, totaling 3500 Contribution Points for 5 pills.

Then add in the 5000 Contribution Points reward.

Which means, just for the tenth place reward in the Earth-digging Realm group, the total value is..."

"57500, no, even more than that, the real value is only higher!"

Xiao Hen calculated it out first, and at this moment, he finally realized why his Big Sister and Second Sister were so solemn earlier.

Nearly 60,000 Contribution Points, quite a sum!

This amount of Contribution Points is significant, even for his father Xiao Kangcheng, the captain of the Xiao Mountain Team, who probably wouldn’t take it lightly.

"The Xiao Mountain Team is already an Intermediate Hunting Team, but on average, my father earns about 2500 Contribution Points a month, and this is enough for him to earn over two years.

And this is just the reward for the tenth place in the Earth-digging Realm group!

How much will be rewarded for the first place?"

Gulp...

Xiao Hen also couldn’t help but swallow, with his face full of shock and agitation.

Of course, what followed was an incredibly passionate fighting spirit.

If the Earth-digging Realm is like this, the rewards for the top hundred in the Logging Realm won’t be far off.

"This is my opportunity. I’m already quite behind the first team of geniuses at camp. If I fall behind, I’ll continue to fall behind in the future. If I can obtain such a huge amount of resources, plus my father’s support, my cultivation progress in the Earth-digging Realm will accelerate significantly, maybe I can catch up to Hong Shaojun, Hong Shaokang, and Li Yuanqing!"

Hong Shaojun, Hong Shaokang, Li Yuanqing...

These are the ones who previously broke the age record for breaking through the Earth-digging Realm. As these names emerged in his mind, Xiao Hen first clenched his fists but then suddenly realized something, and his expression became a bit peculiar.

"They must be regretting it now, huh?"

They’ve all broken through to the Earth-digging Realm shortly before this, so naturally, they will be classified into the Earth-digging Realm group in this martial arts competition.

In the Earth-digging Realm group, those who can compete for the top ten are bound to be the ones who have broken through the Earth-digging Realm limit and started using Beast Blood. The Hong family siblings and Li Yuanqing, who just broke through, basically have no chance of ranking.

"A Hen, I’ve seen you running to your brother-in-law’s house frequently recently. Do you already know some insider information? If there’s something good, don’t forget about your brother!"

Seeing Xiao Lin’s anxious and expectant eyes, Xiao Hen couldn’t help but show a hint of helplessness.

"Isn’t my Big Sister pregnant? My mother cooks meat soup for her every day, and when dawn approaches, she lets me deliver it over. Don’t worry, if I really know any insider information, I certainly won’t forget you."

Hearing this, Xiao Lin finally felt at ease and pulled him along to continue their training.

.........

At the back of the main hall on the top floor of the main building, Xia Hong, who was sitting cross-legged quietly in the room, suddenly opened his eyes, with a hint of a smile on his face, shook his head, and said:

"These kids are quite clever. It seems this wave of breakthroughs will wait until after the martial arts competition. However, Xia Chuan, this kid, didn’t even tell me about such a big event..."

After pondering for a moment, Xia Hong stood up, then turned his head to glance at Xia Chuan’s house next to the main building. After thinking for a moment, he took a few things with him and vanished from the room.
