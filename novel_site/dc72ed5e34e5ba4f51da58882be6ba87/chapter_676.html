Chapter 676: Chapter 282: Humiliation and the Formation of a New Army

"Stop!"

The two hurried towards the house, and when they were still over ten meters away, Yang Zhong, with his hot temper, directly yelled angrily at those people.

"Oh, the young masters have returned from hunting."

"Today’s return is a bit early, there’s still over two hours until dawn!"

"Why is the third young master so irritable? We’re just taking a casual look."

.........

The sarcastic remarks at the door made Yang Zhong’s already high temper flare up even more, his hands gripping the stick trembling non-stop.

Hearing the final address of "third young master," he couldn’t hold back any longer and directly charged at the man, ready to strike.

The man’s strength was obviously not strong; seeing Yang Zhong rushing towards him, he was immediately scared and took a few steps back.

Pa...

Fighting among fellow members within the camp, once verified, regardless of the reason, the minimum punishment is a month’s labor in the mine; causing serious consequences could result in a lifetime of servitude in the mine.

Li Hu, who knew the camp’s rules well, naturally couldn’t allow Yang Zhong to strike, so he quickly reached out to stop him, then gave Yang Zhong a look.

Yang Zhong was not foolish; seeing Li Hu’s look, he instantly calmed down, stood still, took a deep breath, then stared at the man who had retreated a few steps, showing a hint of disdain.

The man obviously couldn’t save face, realizing now that the two didn’t dare to act, he continued to mock Yang Zhong, saying, "Third young master, you’ve indeed improved a lot lately, daring to make a move in the camp now."

"You barged in for no reason..."

"What’s the point of talking to this pack of dogs, just find their master."

Yang Zhong hadn’t finished speaking when he was interrupted by Li Hu.

Hearing Li Hu’s words, the dozen or so people around the house immediately showed anger on their faces, just about to retort, when from Li Hu’s room on the left came a very calm voice.

"Old Four, come in."

"Old Three, you get in here right now too!"

If the first voice was calm, showing no trace of its owner’s emotions;

The next arrogant and domineering shout was completely the opposite, filled with ferocity and brutality, clearly showing a deep disdain for this old three mentioned.

Upon hearing the two voices from inside the house, Li Hu and Yang Zhong’s expressions immediately turned dark, but they quickly regained their composure and walked inside.

Inside the room were thirteen people in total, eight dressed as subordinates, rummaging around; the remaining five were about the same age as Li Hu, and were dressed considerably more luxuriously than the nine, standing in the center of the room, scrutinizing Li Hu and Yang Zhong as they slowly walked in.

The moment Li Hu and Yang Zhong walked in, the five people clearly noticed something, their expressions slightly changed.

The man in the center, staring hard at Li Hu, said in a low voice, "Old Four, I haven’t seen you for half a month, and you’ve already broken through to the Earth-digging Realm Limit. Is there something you’re not telling me?"

"What’s up, Li Wuqing? You’re allowed to cultivate, but I’m not allowed to break through?"

It was no surprise that Li Hu’s cultivation was seen through by Li Wuqing in a glance, after all, the other party had broken through the Earth-digging Realm Limit two years ago and started using Beast Blood.

Li Wuqing was quite surprised, clearly never having seen Li Hu so defiant, stared down at him for a moment, then seemingly thinking of something, chuckled lightly:

"Li Hu, you probably think that breaking through the Earth-digging Realm Limit means you can join the Longyou Army, and when you’re not in the Hunting Team, I won’t be able to manage you, right?"

Li Hu sneered, considering it a response to Li Yuanqing.

The reason Li Yuanqing could recklessly plant people into his Hunting Team was because his grandfather Zhu Xing managed all the ordinary Hunting Teams in Long Mountain.

As Li Yuanqing said, he had just broken through the Earth-digging Realm Limit, and would soon be able to join the Longyou Army, at which point he would no longer be in the Hunting Team; not even Zhu Xing could control him then, let alone Li Yuanqing.

The Longyou Army is the strongest weapon in Long Mountain’s hand, extremely strictly managed, and its affairs can only be meddled in by the leader and a few others like Li Tiancheng, and Zhu Xing has no chance.

He had been risking his life to expedite his breakthrough, just so he could enter the army sooner and rid himself of the surveillance of Li Yuanqing and Yang Gan.

Of course, the more important objective is to infiltrate the Great Xia, to steal...

Huh!

As thoughts swirled in Li Hu’s mind, a fist wind suddenly came crashing towards him.

Li Yuanqing had often laid hands on him before, and Li Hu’s response was quick. A ruthless glint flashed in his eyes as he swiftly drew a broadsword to counter.

Fighting was forbidden within Long Mountain Camp, but since the opponent struck first, he had nothing to fear. He knew his capability was certainly inferior to Li Yuanqing, given that the latter had broken through two years prior, so he directly drew his broadsword to respond.

"Breaking through two years ago, the Basic Strength at most is over ten thousand more than mine, thinking he can just defeat me with fists......"

Bang!

Li Hu’s thoughts were abruptly interrupted by a straight punch from Li Yuanqing.

Crack...

The other’s fist landed squarely on his broadsword, the terrifying force transmitted up his arm, causing bones to crack audibly, and Li Hu’s face changed instantly.

The broadsword slipped from his grip, and he flew backward over ten meters, smashing through a wall behind, embedding his body half a meter into the ground.

Puh-choo......

"Brother Hu!"

Nearby, Yang Zhong was stupefied for a few seconds. Upon regaining his senses, he looked panic-stricken and immediately tried to help Li Hu up, but barely took two steps when Yang Gan, beside Li Yuanqing, kicked him flying.

"Old Three, did Big Brother tell you to move?"

After kicking Yang Zhong away, Yang Gan aggressively rushed forward and stomped fiercely on Yang Zhong’s left face, crushing his head into the ground by more than ten centimeters, leaving only the left eye exposed.
