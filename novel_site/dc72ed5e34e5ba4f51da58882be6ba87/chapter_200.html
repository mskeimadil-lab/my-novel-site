Chapter 200: Chapter 137 Attempt to Breakthrough, Dispute [Trinity]_4

...

After listening to He Gang and He Meng, Yuwen Dao, who had been calm, immediately changed his expression. He turned to look at the two, suppressing his anger, and said in a deep voice:

"I go out hunting, not wanting to take more of the Cold Beast Blood share from the camp, to avoid being criticized by people like you, saying I can only rely on my father;

Whether I have taken any benefits from the Great Xia Camp, you can ask those who went out hunting with me, some of whom are your people.

Oh, right, you are falsely accusing me, so naturally, you wouldn’t ask!"

After sarcastically finishing the last sentence, Yuwen Dao walked to the two brothers, He Meng and He Gang, disregarding the injury on his shoulder, picked up his Broadsword, and directly pointed at them, angrily saying:

"And, I warn you all, from now on, anyone who dares to call me ’Young Master Dao,’ even if the Great Leader is present, I will drag him onto the ring and settle life and death!"

"Young Master Dao" was not a respectful title but a nickname given to Yuwen Dao by He Meng, mocking him for relying on his father, Yuwen Hu.

At first, this nickname was only used privately, and although Yuwen Dao disliked it, he had no choice. But tonight, He Meng and the others openly called him this, which he could not tolerate.

Yuwen Dao’s voice was loud, filled with anger. Whoever dared to call him by that nickname again, he would indeed follow camp rules and take them onto the ring to settle life and death, and no one doubted this.

Normally, in such a tense meeting, even if not intimidated by Yuwen Dao’s anger, He Meng wouldn’t say anything more.

But instead of shutting up, he boldly took on Yuwen Dao’s anger, refusing to back down: "Do you think I, old man, would be afraid to get on the ring with you, Yuwen Dao? Who are you trying to scare, or should we just get on the ring now?"

Though he said he wasn’t afraid, He Meng dared not utter "Young Master Dao" again, but his confrontational attitude made Yuwen Dao instinctively step forward.

Just as he was about to agree to He Meng’s suggestion to get into the ring, Yuwen Dao glanced and saw his father above, gently shaking his head at him. He immediately calmed down, pondered for a moment, and finally stepped back.

"Heh..."

Yuwen Dao’s stance made He Meng and the others believe he had conceded, and he sneered with a cold smile.

"All right, let’s say no more!"

Yuwen Hu from above stopped the quarrel and continued:

"No matter what hidden strength Daxia Camp and Xia Hong have, it’s impossible for them to take Redwood Ridge for no reason.

Since contact has been made, let strength speak.

No matter what dealings Rogue has with Great Xia, and whatever agreements they may have, Lu Shang Camp’s land belongs to our Mirror Immortal Camp, without a doubt.

All hunting activities will continue as usual, and Redwood Ridge will not be neglected.

If you encounter Xia Hong again, deliver this message to him, tell him that if he truly wants to take Redwood Ridge single-handedly, let strength speak!"

Hearing these words, a smile appeared on He Meng’s face.

Letting strength speak was originally in line with his thinking.

He Meng, thinking of something, showed a hint of ruthlessness and stepped forward to ask:

"Second Leader, Great Xia has a large population, as the Great Leader previously mentioned the need to expand the population quickly. Why not just kill Xia Hong and bring all the people from the Great Xia Camp into our Mirror Immortal Camp?"

Upon hearing this, Yuwen Hu did not respond but first turned to look behind the bamboo building, a look of hesitation flashed across his face.

"No need to kill, first try to persuade Xia Hong to willingly bring his people into our Mirror Immortal Camp, that would be the best..."

Before Yuwen Hu could finish, He Meng interrupted him and continued to ask:

"What if persuasion doesn’t work?"

Yuwen Hu looked at He Meng, frowning. He certainly knew what answer He Meng wanted to hear, but it didn’t align with his principles, so he didn’t answer.

Seeing Yuwen Hu not answering for a long time, He Meng grew more impatient and was about to speak.

At this moment, a gentle voice as warm as jade came from behind the bamboo building.

"Dao’er did well tonight, leading a team to hunt two Intermediate Cold Beasts, and despite being seriously injured, he was able to negotiate and successfully bring back the two prey. Brave and wise, Second Brother, you have raised a good son!"

Everyone in the hall, upon hearing this voice, respectfully knelt down.

"Thank you for the praise, Great Leader!"

Facing the accolades from behind the bamboo building, Yuwen Dao quickly bowed in response, with a calm expression and no change in his demeanor.

But He Meng to the side clenched his fists, his face full of jealousy.

Sitting at the top, Yuwen Hu also stood up slightly, facing the direction of the voice with a slight bow.

"Great Leader is too kind!"

The voice behind the bamboo building fell silent for a moment, then spoke again.

"Starting today, the camp will add another Intermediate Hunting Team, led by Yuwen Dao, to hunt Intermediate Cold Beasts."

The people in the hall erupted in discussion upon hearing this.

Mirror Immortal Camp’s hunting teams are divided into Advanced, Intermediate, and Low-level.

The name implies that the level of the hunting team corresponds to the level of Cold Beasts they handle.

The Low-level teams need not be mentioned, there are many Low-level hunting teams in Mirror Immortal Camp.

Currently, there is only one Advanced team, composed of twelve members, personally led by the Great Leader and Second Leader, both of whom are Cold Resistance Level experts, along with ten of Mirror Immortal Camp’s strongest Earth-digging Realm experts.

There were also two Intermediate teams before, with those involved being among the stronger Earth-digging Realm experts in the camp.

He Meng is the captain of one of those hunting teams.

And now, a new team is to be formed, personally led by Yuwen Dao.

It may seem he’s just an Intermediate Hunting Team Captain, but with increased voice, the Leader’s favor, and even improved treatment, these three aspects are what every Earth-digging Realm expert in the camp dreams of.

With the Leader’s words, Yuwen Dao gained it all!

Everyone turned to look at Yuwen Dao, unable to hide their envy.

He Meng, already full of jealousy, now had an even more distorted expression.

And Yuwen Dao, clearly not expecting Great Leader He Yuanhun to value him so highly, was visibly taken aback.

"Whether by killing or persuasion, as long as all members of the Great Xia Camp are brought into our Mirror Immortal Camp, I don’t care what method you use. This matter, Second Brother, I entrust to you!"

He Yuanhun said these final words, and without waiting for Yuwen Hu to respond, a sense of withdrawal seemed to emanate from behind the bamboo building.

"Yes, Great Leader!"

Yuwen Hu responded with clasped hands, and everyone respectfully bowed. After a while, realizing that the Leader was no longer paying attention to the hall, everyone slowly raised their heads.

"Alright, as per the Leader’s instructions, do you two understand?"

Upon hearing Yuwen Hu’s inquiry, both He Meng and Yuwen Dao nodded.

The two turned to face each other, a strong sense of rivalry in their eyes.
