You overestimate those so-called high-level figures you keep talking about.

This is the letter Meng Tian just sent me, take a look!"

Xia Hong suddenly took out a beast skin roll from his chest and threw it onto the bed.

Meng Tian?

Hearing this name, Li Xuanling was first startled, then picked up the beast skin roll. After reading the contents, his face turned ashen.

The beast skin roll recorded all the conversations of Meng Ying, Jiang Ping, and Sun Yan after they returned to the cell, including discussions about connecting with others to force Great Xia to let go of their four siblings, all remembered distinctly.

"Even Meng Tian, you managed to buy off?"

"Is there a need to buy off?"

Xia Hong’s lips curved into a cold smile, disdainfully said: "Since last night after taking Long Mountain, there’s been an endless stream of Long Mountain people pledging allegiance to Great Xia. Meng Tian is merely one of them. Among such high-level outsiders like him, there are at least three or four hundred. As for the ordinary people below, they are even more numerous."

Li Xuanling was evidently deeply shocked, her expression already dazed.

Meng Tian’s identity is indeed too special.

His father Meng Ying is one of the Longshan Eight Great Vajra, whose loyalty to the Li Family is unquestionable. Yet Meng Tian, a high-level member of Long Mountain, quickly turned to Great Xia, showing how unpopular the Li Family is.

And among such high-level members like Meng Tian, there are three or four hundred more.

"Guess, is there anyone in your Li Family clan who also pledged allegiance to my Great Xia to save their lives?"

Xia Hong kept attacking Li Xuanling’s inner feelings and continued speaking before she could answer: "Even Meng Tian, a definite high-level core member, has no loyalty whatsoever to your Li Family, let alone those ordinary people below.

Your so-called high-level governance is already riddled with holes!

Like the thirteen thousand people of Long Mountain, whether they can integrate into Great Xia is not for the Li Family to decide; the seven families totaling eleven thousand people, likewise cannot be decided by Hou Jing and those at the Cold Resistance Level.

The resource allocation model of the Longyou camps predestined that a slight division would lead many to turn towards Great Xia for a simple reason..."

For ordinary people, Longyou is too unfair; all camps practice the same rules as Long Mountain in resource allocation, where all resources unconditionally favor the most core family or families.

Starting from the initially seen elite system of the Beehive to the deeper understanding of Longyou later, Xia Hong had already seen this point.

The Beehive Chen Family, Zhaoyang Hou Family, Longshan Li Family, Wushuang Wu Family, Hanqiong Han Family... the twelve families of Longyou, rather than being twelve giant camps, should be seen as twelve controlling mass populations, independent large families.

"You twelve families are like parasites, constantly drawing blood from the more than four thousand people of Longyou, enjoying their sustenance while maintaining your supreme position. Without external intervention, those below are powerless to resist, and you can certainly continue this game.

But once strong external forces intervene, you twelve families will immediately suffer backlash and be attacked; tonight’s Li Family is the end!"

After these words, Xia Hong paused and continued to sneer: "If Hou Jing’s four families are smart, they’ll know that keeping a low profile is their best way to stay alive. If they dare entertain any improper thoughts, tonight’s Li Family will be their tomorrow."

Upon hearing these words and realizing the Li Family members can’t escape death, Li Xuanling raised her head, staring at Xia Hong with unabashed intense hatred in her eyes.

However, Xia Hong’s face was full of indifference, coldly smiling as he continued:

"I don’t care if you want to kill me all the time; whether you have the ability to kill me is what matters most. Before you have the skill, you’d better behave and not look for trouble!"

Clap... clap... clap...

As Li Xuanling’s expression grew more flustered, the sounds coming from her throat also became increasingly strange.

In the second year of Great Xia, on the seventh day of the seventh month, the night arrived as expected.

After some stern warnings from Xia Chuan, Hou Jing and the others showed a noticeable change in attitude, evident from Yuwen Dao’s assembly of more than ten thousand Earth-digging Realm workers, taking less than an hour.

Once over ten thousand personnel had been reassembled, Yue Feng and ten others immediately led their respective thousand-man teams to drive all people scattered at various places of deployment to gather in the North District’s dungeons.

Twelve thousand people, no matter what action is taken, consider it a major event, and naturally, it didn’t go smoothly; however, with Xia Chuan’s order, Yuwen Dao and Yue Feng showed no mercy.

"Listen up, I’ll say it once; those who fail to gather outside the North District’s dungeon within half an hour will be treated as offenders against the Minister, subject to three years of mandatory labor!"

As Yuwen Dao signaled Yue Feng and the others to convey the orders, the entire deployment area immediately became lively, as a massive stream of people walked out of their houses toward gathering outside the North District’s dungeon.

"What are they planning to do?"

"I don’t know, are we going to be locked up in the North District dungeon?"

"How’s that possible, with tens of thousands of people, even wanting to lock them all up is impossible?"

"Long Mountain has changed hands; at this moment, calling us to the dungeon, what else could it be for, surely is to deal with the Li Family and the Earth-digging Realm people inside the dungeon!"

"My father is currently locked in the dungeon; will Great Xia kill him?"

"Unlikely, there are more than seven thousand Earth-digging Realm members locked inside, Great Xia shouldn’t dare execute them all."
