Chapter 1401: Chapter 451: Blinded by Lust, Overestimating Oneself, Chui Mountain Changes Hands, Cai Shiqi Breaks Down, Intimidation_6

When that white sword light appeared, he thought it was Xia Hong.

"Great Xia has produced a new Xianyang Level, and it’s a woman! At least with a strength of 19 Jun, on par with me... No, no, this can’t be a recent breakthrough. Great Xia has hidden it so well..."

While Yang Zhun’s mind was racing, new changes arose as Duan Hong was killed.

"As the Lord here, I will only remove the main culprit Duan Family. Those who participated in the rebellion today, as long as you lay down your weapons and surrender, I promise no punishment!"

Mu Longhe directly assumed the role of the Lord, and the soldiers of Chui Mountain, who were resisting alongside the Beishuo Army, heard the words and their eyes flickered immediately.

Of course, those whose minds were shaken were not from the Duan Family. This was only a very small portion.

The problem is that battlefield emotions are contagious.

This small group’s loss of will to resist, laying down their weapons and surrendering, quickly caused a chain reaction. Many people’s defenses began to crumble, and they started to flee in droves.

Even the most resolute Beishuo Army began to be affected.

"Beishuo Army, don’t panic, rally at your positions!"

Yang Fa’s expression had long turned ashen. The three thousand Beishuo Army, gradually transferred since the beginning of the year, aimed to capture Chui Mountain Town. Now they haven’t taken the town but have lost nearly a thousand soldiers. The remaining two thousand must not have any more issues.

He first ordered the remaining Beishuo Army to quickly gather, then turned to Mu Longhe and said in a deep voice, "Lord Mu, since Duan Hong is dead, and you’ve retaken Chui Mountain Town, the Beishuo Army will retreat accordingly, let’s cease fighting!"

"Cease fighting?"

Mu Longhe didn’t speak at all, as Li Xuanling sheathed her longsword, letting Duan Hong’s body fall to the ground, then turned her head to look at Yang Fa, with a strange look in her eyes.

"It seems not only is Cai Qiu Prince naive, but you’re not much better!"

Li Xuanling chuckled lightly, deliberately teasing Cai Shiqi, then raised her head and signaled to Yang Fa, "Look behind you!"

Yang Fa was stunned, turned to look at the direction Li Xuanling indicated. As soon as he saw the situation outside the Inner City, his pupils shrank sharply.

Around the Inner City, without knowing when, more than a dozen beams of golden light emerged. Although it was three to four kilometers away, Yang Fa could still see it clearly.

That was not golden light, but a visual effect formed by a large number of soldiers clad in pale golden battle armor walking together.

"Cloud Jiao, Long Wu, Dragon Slayer, Tiger Leopard, Black Arrow, five armies, advance rapidly!"

"Xuanwu, Divine Martial, Longyuan, Ying Wu, Zhaolong, Fei Peng, Mountain River, Falcon Hunting, Xiong Wu, Beizhao, ten armies, spread out and set up checkpoints. Kill anyone who tries to break through, do not let a single one escape."

"At your orders!"

"At your orders!"

.........

The fifteen armies crowded together from the outside, quickly encircling the entire Inner City airtight. Yuan Cheng jumped onto the rooftops from the north side, swiftly issuing several commands, and the leading five armies rushed like a flood towards the Duan Clan Land, together with some soldiers retreating to the Lord’s Hall, were immediately surrounded by them.

"Xia Army, how can there be so many Xia Army?"

"Gulp... Look closely, the Xia Army soldiers seem to all be of Cold Resistance Level cultivation?"

"Fifteen armies, fifteen thousand people, all at Cold Resistance Level?"

"How is this possible!"

"When did they infiltrate the Town?"

"It’s definitely Deputy Army Chief Mu Qinghe. I remember now, didn’t the Duan Family always have the Yellow Armor Three Armies guarding the city gates the past few months? They must have cooperated to let them in."

"I remember, over the past few months, many Cold Resistance Level soldiers have quietly infiltrated the city, wasn’t it said that they were all from Beishuo?"

"Hahaha, true and false, Vice Army Chief Mu used the opportunity to let Beishuo’s Cold Resistance Level soldiers into the city to let the Xia Army in."

.........

The Duan Family below instantly turned ashen-faced.

Yang Zhun, Yang Fa, the two brothers’ pupils became deeply gloomy.

At this point, if they still couldn’t react, it’d be too foolish.

This fifteen thousand army may not have been gradually let into Chui Mountain Town by Great Xia before but at least it showed one thing — Great Xia’s infiltration of Chui Mountain Town was much deeper than they originally imagined.

This rebellion, they all served as a wedding garment for Great Xia!

After Duan Hong’s death, Xiang Fuhai and the other three no longer constrained Yang Zhun and others.

Yang Zhun flew directly to the Duan Clan Land, joining the Beishuo Army;

Chu Yan’er, leading the group from Chen Cang, also came over. Her expression changed repeatedly, quickly understanding the ins and outs of the situation, forced a few smiles, and finally raised her head, looking straight at Li Xuanling in mid-air, bowing and smiling as she spoke:

"Great Xia’s clever calculation, clever calculation, this Commandery Princess concedes defeat this time. May I ask elder sister’s esteemed name and position in Great Xia?"

Li Xuanling above, glanced at Chu Yan’er, then immediately shifted her gaze, evidently not intending to answer her.

Chu Yan’er’s expression froze, feeling quite embarrassed.

Several people behind her reacted even bigger than she did, couldn’t help but stand out, clearly wanting to say something, but were all stopped by Chu Yan’er with a gesture.

"Great Xia, Great Xia, what a Great Xia, you’ve utilized this Princely Heir, how bold, how bold, you truly have guts..."

Cai Shiqi, who had thoroughly become a backdrop today, finally couldn’t hold back.

His mind clearly wasn’t as agile as Chu Yan’er, but only until this moment did he completely understand the whole story of this rebellion.
