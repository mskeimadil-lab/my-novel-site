Chapter 371: Chapter 185: New Residence, Land, Arrow Bamboo Forest_3

Hearing Xia Chuan’s words, everyone immediately stopped talking.

But just then, Xia Chuan suddenly changed his tone and continued: "However, there’s another way to live comfortably."

As he finished speaking, Xia Chuan signaled to Qiu Peng.

Qiu Peng brought a few people to carry out a wooden board and placed it in the center of the main hall.

The wooden board was about one and a half meters long and roughly thirty centimeters wide, with three thick lines running horizontally across it, dividing it into five sections. Around these lines were many small square boxes, each labeled with numbers starting from 1.

In the middle of the board, there was a giant frame.

In the center of the big frame, two characters were marked.

"Main Building"

Seeing the words "Main Building," everyone looked surprised.

Some Earth-digging Realm members, including many from the Mirror Immortal Camp, seemed to realize something and their expressions brightened immediately.

Some of the smarter ones, seeing the words "wooden building" and those numbered square boxes, also had their own guesses.

Feeling it was about time, Xia Chuan pointed at the wooden board and continued:

"From today onwards, anyone in the camp who breaks through to the Earth-digging Realm is eligible to purchase a plot of land from the Camp Supply Department. The small numbered boxes here represent the plots of land planned for you, each plot is 100 square meters. After purchasing the land, you can spend contribution points to have the Craftsmen Department construct a building for you... your own house."

Xia Chuan paused at this point, noticing that everyone’s eyes were starting to shine, and he added more fuel to the fire, continuing:

"This house, along with the land beneath it, will be registered and permanently preserved by the Camp Supply Department. As long as there are no natural disasters or man-made catastrophes, it will be lived in by you and your descendants for generations."

Roar...

With this, everyone was in an uproar.

"For generations, even when I die, I can leave it to my sons and grandsons?"

"As long as I buy it, it’s mine."

"The house is nothing, the land is what’s valuable, it must be built."

"Earth-digging Realm, only those who reach the Earth-digging Realm are eligible."

"I must hurry up and cultivate, the highest number is 4200 on that board, wanting to live in the valley, I have to break through quickly, or it’ll be gone soon."

...

The Logging Realm and some ordinary people were just visibly excited, caught up in the heated discussion. Meanwhile, the nearly five hundred Earth-digging Realm members in the camp, knowing they had the qualification to build houses, their gazes turned fiery, and nearly all eyes were drawn to the wooden board behind Xia Chuan.

This was no longer just about improving their living conditions.

It was about the opportunity to leave a fortune for their descendants.

With the development of the Great Xia as it is today, they could almost foresee the future value of these plots of land.

"Sir, what about the price of the land, could you tell us more about it!"

Yuan Cheng was the first to impatiently speak up, asking the question everyone was concerned about, and all eyes instantly focused on Xia Chuan.

Xia Chuan laughed lightly at this, seeing the eager looks on everyone’s faces, he didn’t keep them in suspense and directly pointed to plot number 11 on the board, saying:

"The price of plots varies depending on location, you can find out from the Camp Supply Department later. I’ll use plot 11 as an example, the price for this plot is 5000 Contribution Points!"

Gasp...

Even putting aside ordinary people and those in the Logging Realm, even Yuan Cheng, who had followed Xia Hong on the initial hunts, couldn’t help but gasp.

points, what does that mean?

He had just over 6000 points on him.

And as far as he knew, Xia Chuan had just over 10,000 points.

Seeing Yuan Cheng’s expression, Xia Chuan secretly laughed in his heart.

This price set by Qiu Peng had been discussed with him, he had a rough idea of who could afford it at this stage.

This price was certainly considered expensive at present.

But not unreasonably so, it wasn’t difficult for those in the Earth-digging Realm to buy.

Just by calculating the bare minimum income, 2500 kilograms of iron, 5000 kilograms of coal, an Earth-digging Realm’s monthly mining income alone would be 500; for a low-level Hunting Team, the minimum quota is fifteen low-level Cold Beasts, divided among ten people, each could roughly earn 450 points.

In this way, it would take about five months to afford.

And that’s calculating the lowest income. If one wants to buy faster, hunt and mine more, it wouldn’t take five months to have it in hand.

Compared to the Ten-stone Iron Bow that costs 2000 Contribution Points to exchange, this land’s price could be considered extremely low and unreasonable.

After all, looking a bit further ahead, as the population increases, the prices of these lands will definitely skyrocket, possibly to an outrageous extent.

"Setting it at 5000 is considered a benefit in advance for you!"

Xia Chuan thought to himself. Seeing that the discussion among the crowd was almost over, he had the board removed and continued:

"Alright, you can find out more about building houses from the Camp Supply Department later. Let’s conclude this for today. Next, I have two more announcements to make..."

The first announcement is to commend Mu Dong and Cheng Feng.

Salt, silk, white paper, cloth, alcohol, Beast Essence Pill, Blood Sore Pill, Hot Wind Pill, Blood Explosion Pill, nine items, each rewarded with 10,000 Contribution Points.

As soon as Xia Chuan announced Xia Hong’s commendation order, the entire camp of over 20,000 people erupted in excitement, even those in the Earth-digging Realm were no exception.

10,000 Contribution Points, and it’s even a one-time reward.

Even upon hearing Xia Chuan say that it would be distributed equally among all participants, everyone still looked very excited.
