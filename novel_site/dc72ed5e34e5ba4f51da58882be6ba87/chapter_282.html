Chapter 282 - 159: Blood and Flesh Wall, The Leader Hasn’t Broken Through Yet?

Chapter 282: Chapter 159: Blood and Flesh Wall, The Leader Hasn’t Broken Through Yet?

"Sir, he has already taken people to the east corridor, and Zhao Hu and Zhao Bao have also taken a few people to the corridors on the north and south sides."

No wonder the Frost Wolves on the north and south sides haven’t hit us yet.

Upon realizing this, Xia Chuan’s expression became even more grim.

Of course, it wasn’t to blame Luo Yuan; he initially called Luo Yuan to guard the east side against the Teng Jiao. Zhao Hu and Zhao Bao took people ahead of time to guard the Frost Wolves on the north and south sides, which was exactly as he intended.

The problem is that all Earth-digging Realm warriors on their side have already been deployed, and to cover all four directions, they’ve practically reached their limits.

The original plan was to split into two groups: his group of thirty-four people and Luo Yuan’s group of thirty-three, only to guard the west side, taking turns in the corridor.

But there are just too many Cold Beasts.

Not only are there many of them, but the four groups seem to be working together.

The Snow Mane Demon Sheep charges the west front; the Frost Wolves split into two groups, detouring along the mountain wall to attack the north and south sides; the Teng Jiao drops directly from above the valley to strike the back of the wooden house on the east side.

With cooperation on all four sides, they simply don’t have enough people.

Luo Yuan, Zhao Hu, and Zhao Bao knew the pressure on the west side was heavy and probably only took eight to nine people each.

There are forty people on his side, the most.

But even with forty people, they can’t hold off the two to three hundred Cold Beasts in front of them.

If that’s their situation, the outcome for the other three directions with only ten people each is predictable.

Worse yet, Xia Chuan felt his eyes, covered by white gauze, beginning to sting, and his body’s surface temperature was dropping rapidly; he probably wouldn’t last much longer.

"The limit is ten minutes; nine have already passed. At most, I can hold on for another minute, then I won’t be able to endure, and staying outside any longer, even if not killed by Cold Beasts, we’d freeze to death!"

With urgency in his heart, Xia Chuan turned to look at Yuan Cheng and the others, finding that frost had started forming on their bodies, their archery posture becoming increasingly distorted, and their accuracy was already much worse than before.

After all, without reaching the Cold Resistance Level, ten minutes is the limit.

If it weren’t for this white silk fabric, they probably couldn’t hold out even for ten minutes.

"Chuan, should we notify the leader? We can’t hold on!"

Yuan Cheng’s voice trembled slightly as he spoke. He was the seventh in Great Xia to breakthrough to the Earth-digging Realm, and given his condition, it’s easy to imagine how it was for the others.

Although the others’ hands didn’t stop the archery action, their eyes were focused on Xia Chuan, clearly agreeing with Yuan Cheng’s suggestion.

Xia Chuan turned his head; directly opposite the corridor was Xia Hong’s room door. He took just one look before quickly turning his head back to look out the wooden house, gritting his teeth and said:

"Eldest brother is still breaking through; we can’t disturb him. We’ll handle this ourselves!"

The Teng Jiao Poison Mist spread out, causing some impact on the Cold Beasts, but not deadly, and mostly only on those in the front.

As the Cold Beasts in the back continued to replace those in front, the mist’s effect visibly weakened, and even later had almost no impact on the Cold Beasts.

Bam... Bam... Bam...

Finally, the first Snow Mane slammed into the main door, followed by the second, third, fourth...

The sound of Cold Beasts’ fierce impacts came one after another.

The Great Xia wooden house spans two hundred meters long on each side, with stakes driven more than two meters deep into the ground, mixed with much iron reinforcement inside.

The original design was indeed to prevent a large number of Cold Beasts from crashing in.

But who could have imagined so many would come all at once?

Crack...

The crashing grew louder, and the wooden house trembled more violently.

Finally, after dozens of hits, there came a cracking sound.

"Everyone inside, head to the second floor, quickly!"

Xia Chuan felt the cold in his body had reached its peak, remaining outside any longer would make them faint. He shouted to everyone.

All people put away their bows and arrows, quickly retreating from the corridor to the interior.

"Zhao Long, take people to notify Luo Yuan, Zhao Hu, Zhao Bao, everyone to the second floor. Staying outside, we’ll die even faster."

"Alright!"

Zhao Long led a few people towards the other corridors, while Xia Chuan took the remaining people directly down to the second floor.

"Help..."

"Cold Beasts have broken in from this side, quick, we need people."

"Cold Beasts are rushing in from the back."

"Everyone, stay in the main hall, don’t run around, I’ll go notify people."

...

Before even reaching the second floor, the sounds of panic and shouting reached his ears, and without hesitation, Xia Chuan rushed down directly.

Seeing the scene downstairs, his pupils constricted instantly.

At the end of the four corridors on the southeast and northwest sides of the second floor, holes three to four meters wide had already been broken through. Up to three to four Frost Wolves on the north and south corridors were already rushing towards the main hall.

On the west side, a Snow Mane head just poked through a hole, its blood-red, ferocious eyes fixed on the Iron Refining Furnace, full of excitement and madness;

On the east side, half of a Teng Jiao had crawled in, its vertical pupils staring intently at the Iron Refining Furnace, filled with desire.

Having seen this look of the Cold Beasts countless times while using Small Torches as bait, Xia Chuan understood immediately.

No, compared to Small Torches, their gazes at the Iron Refining Furnace were even more crazed.

"Luo Yuan, take people to kill the Teng Jiao; Zhao Long, you three bring people to kill the two Frost Wolves on the south; Huang Yong, Hong Gang, take people to drive out the Snow Mane; Yuan Cheng, follow me to kill the Frost Wolves on the north, act quickly, and block up the corridor entrances immediately after killing them."

Though Xia Chuan arranged everything clearly, the despair in his eyes was several times thicker than just now.
