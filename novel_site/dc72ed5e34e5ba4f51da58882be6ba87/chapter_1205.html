Chapter 1205: Chapter 405: Taking the Fall, Battle for Supremacy, Playing Dirty When Outmatched, Burning with Anger (Part 5)

But what is that incredibly clear penetrating sensation?

Hoo...

The hair on Xia Hong’s back stood on end instantly; he didn’t even turn around, knowing Yang Zhun had already appeared behind him.

Moreover, the tip of Yang Zhun’s spear was already pressed against his spine.

"Is it teleportation? No, no, teleportation wouldn’t leave an afterimage. It must be that the speed is too fast. I didn’t even perceive it, and yet Yang Zhun still reserved such a trick!"

Sss...

Countless thoughts flashed through Xia Hong’s mind, but this maneuver by Yang Zhun, left until now, clearly aimed for a kill. He didn’t even have time to turn his head, when the long spear broke through his spine directly.

A point of icy light abruptly gathered at the center of his spine, near twenty jun of colossal strength crushed down from behind like Mount Tai, and Xia Hong’s body instantly flew forward hundreds of meters.

The key point was that during his forward flight, Yang Zhun’s silver spear tip never left his spine.

Yang Zhun’s spear was so powerful that thousands of silver rays burst forth, instantly covering both of them, preventing those below from seeing inside.

"The victory is decided!"

Yang Fa excitedly couldn’t wait to speak up first.

Before the silver rays burst, he clearly saw Xia Hong’s spine break open; inside, the pale bones were clearly visible, plus his body was pinned by the spear, Xia Hong had no chance of turning the tables!

Once the silver rays dissipated, his brother’s spear would have penetrated Xia Hong’s body.

The strongest in Nine Towns could only be...

"Something’s wrong, look at the saber in Xia Hong’s hand!"

Qin Feng was the first to notice something amiss, his eyes slightly narrowing, and without waiting for the silver rays in the sky to completely dissipate, he shouted immediately.

Upon hearing, ten people instantly looked up.

At that moment, the silver rays in the sky completely dispersed too.

The excitement on Yang Fa’s face froze instantly after seeing the real situation within the silver rays.

Yang Zhun’s spear hadn’t penetrated Xia Hong’s body as he thought, but still pressed against Xia Hong’s spine.

Not only did it not penetrate, but even Xia Hong’s bones hadn’t been broken.

On the contrary, the Dragon Sparrow Saber originally in Xia Hong’s hand had penetrated Yang Zhun’s abdomen at this moment, slanting upwards and exiting his back.

Poof!

"Dark red bones, you..."

A mouthful of reverse blood sprayed out from Yang Zhun, his face full of shock and dread.

His shock wasn’t from the Dragon Sparrow Saber now lodged in his abdomen, but the high-strength bones when he attempted to penetrate Xia Hong’s body.

Nearly twenty jun of force couldn’t even crack Xia Hong’s bones?

"Dark red, above the golden bones, there’s an even higher-level bone talent!"

This thought flashed in Yang Zhun’s mind, and an extremely dangerous sensation emerged from his heart; he suddenly looked up to find Xia Hong had rushed to his front, not only pulling the Dragon Sparrow Saber out of his abdomen, but his left eye also exploded with a burst of strong light directed at him.

Bam...

Xia Hong already pulled the Dragon Sparrow Saber from his abdomen, their distance implying Yang Zhun dared not hesitate; he unleashed an identical terrifying speed and disappeared directly.

"Cough..."

Holding the Dragon Sparrow Saber, Xia Hong coughed a mouthful of blood, looked up at the fleeing Yang Zhun, eyes flashing with deep apprehension.

"Almost capsized in the ditch, really can’t underestimate others!"

Despite the apprehension, upon seeing Yang Zhun not only had abdominal wounds but also a bleeding wound in his left chest, Xia Hong’s eyes immediately revealed a conjecture.

Whoosh...

His left eye again burst with a strong light, directly targeting Yang Zhun.

Because of the sufficient distance, Yang Zhun didn’t exhibit teleportation-like speed this time, choosing to dodge physically.

His body did dodge, but Xia Hong stuck close.

Clank...

The Dragon Sparrow Saber swiftly sliced across his neck, Yang Zhun’s spear horizontally blocked in mid-air, and upon their clash, his expression changed instantly.

"Brother Yang, seems your injury is more severe than mine!"

Xia Hong, understanding Yang Zhun’s change of face, smirked coldly.

Post-injury, strength does diminish!

Especially when both injuries involved their bones.

Bones are indeed the power source at the Xianyang level.

Following the recent clash, he can distinctly sense Yang Zhun’s basic strength had dropped to 13 jun, whereas his still explodes at 16 jun.

"Let me guess, the speed you burst out just now must come at a price, isn’t it injured as a price?"

Xia Hong’s words made Yang Zhun’s face increasingly sullen.

One engagement!

One engagement, yet he sees through his reliance.

Xia Hong’s combat talent and instinctual acuity are chillingly high.

Anyone with these two abilities is already troublesome.

Critical that his aptitude is so defiant!

Trouble.

Beishuo will have major trouble ahead.

Hoo...

Xia Hong advanced again, this time not with slashes but fusing human and blade with a sudden surge, the Dragon Sparrow Saber in his hand seemingly transforming into a silver spear, darting towards Yang Zhun’s face instantly.

Rapid Lightning Spear Technique!

During the late April Battle of Bashang, when Xia Hong ambushed the three masters, he utilized this spear technique of his own creation.

Using the Dragon Sparrow Saber as a spear, a common occurrence for him, so gripping this saber, he now resembled a spearman more than Yang Zhun.

The spear struck, Yang Zhun likewise blocked with his spear, yet unlike before, this time he couldn’t precisely seize Xia Hong’s thrust timing, so his body retreated while sweeping ahead, attempting to repel Xia Hong this way.

This method indeed worked!

Bam...

Xia Hong’s Dragon Sparrow Saber was struck by him, halted over two meters away.

Yet as he stopped, Xia Hong suddenly raised his head.

Whoosh...

Yang Zhun hadn’t truly witnessed what strength lay in Xia Hong’s left-eye light, but intuitively understood it was serious trouble if hit.

Thus, gritting his teeth, with no choice, his body vanished directly.

To evade it, he again burst forth with teleportation-like speed!

This time, Xia Hong wasn’t as bewildered as before; he seemed to discern Yang Zhun’s disappearance origin, abruptly turned his gaze to the flank, then sprang over there.

"Release!"

But at this moment, Yuwen Dao’s voice suddenly came from below.

Xia Hong, originally rushing towards Yang Zhun, glanced down below, and his expression instantly darkened.

Taking advantage of everyone’s focus above, Qin Feng and Shangguan Yang quietly headed towards the Ministry Building.

In a flash, they actually captured Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao.

Murong Chui, Xiahou Zhang, Xiang Fuhai, they didn’t even react.

"Lord Xia Hong, even as strong as Great Xia is but one town; neither in troops nor in Xianyang-level strongmen can it compare to our Six Towns. Yang admits your strength is indeed formidable, but you aren’t yet powerful enough to wreak havoc on the Southern Slope of Moao Mountain at will. Since you admitted the Three Towns Chamber’s people were killed under orders, today, you must give us a resolution!"

Yang Zhun’s eerie voice echoed, leaving Xia Hong’s expression furious to the extreme.
