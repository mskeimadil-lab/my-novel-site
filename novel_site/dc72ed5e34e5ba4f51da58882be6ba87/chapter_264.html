Chapter 264: Chapter 154 Inheritance Rights, Strategic Contraction [Sorry for Being Late]_2

This is also the most direct reason for the deaths of Li Yun and Li Pingkai.

In fact, Xia Hong had previously considered the possibility of being ambushed or encountering the other party’s large forces. However, at that time, his skin training progress had improved significantly, his basic strength had officially surpassed the Cold Resistance Level threshold, and he was extremely confident.

Unless the two Cold Resistance Level individuals from the Mirror Immortal Camp acted together, even if faced with three hunting teams like He Meng’s composition, he was confident he could lead everyone to escape unscathed.

But he never expected that the Mirror Immortal Camp would be involved with the Deceitful Monster, and tonight, to encircle him, they almost mobilized their entire forces.

Yuwen Hu led the team of several hundred Earth-digging Realm members, which was practically all of the Mirror Immortal Camp’s strength, except for that He Yuanhun.

Furthermore, the opponent had been constantly dispatching people to monitor the valley base.

Judging from the ambush situation at the time, the most people were on the east, which was the large group brought by Yuwen Hu, evidently all rushed in from the outskirts temporarily.

In other words, as soon as the Hunting Team from Great Xia left the valley, the Mirror Immortal Camp would quickly become aware.

It must be so, otherwise, it wouldn’t be such a coincidence!

Xia Hong looked around the periphery of the valley, a few streaks of cold light flashing in his eyes, then withdrew his gaze, and said to everyone in the camp:

"Let’s not talk about my mistake first!

Li Yun and Li Pingkai both died for the camp, their widows and children will naturally be supported by the camp. Li Yan, Li Ting, Li Feng, and the two mothers, until they reach the Earth-digging Realm, all cultivation expenses will be borne by the camp gratuitously.

Besides, the housing of Li Pingkai and Li Yun will continue to be occupied by their families, and their remaining Contribution Values, weapons, and bows will all be inherited by their children, and the camp will not reclaim any of it."

Hoo...

As Xia Hong finished speaking, everyone in the camp suddenly breathed a bit more heavily.

Even among the Hunting Team, the expressions of some with children changed.

Li Yun and Li Pingkai, both of whom were once members of the Hunting Team, lived on the third floor, and now after their deaths, the rooms still belong to their families;

Contribution Values, bows, weapons, all three things are directly inherited by their children;

The most crucial part is that the three children, including two mothers, will have all their cultivation expenses provided by the camp at no charge until they reach the Earth-digging Realm.

Even in a brutal world like the Ice Abyss, familial bonds and blood relations remain the strongest link between people.

After Great Xia began implementing the Contribution Point system, everyone, regardless of gender, started working towards earning Contribution Points as long as their cultivation level was reached.

Eventually, the Contribution Points everyone worked so hard to earn are essentially used for two things: cultivation or family.

When venturing out, be it hunting Cold Beasts or competing with other camps, even in simple logging or gathering, no one can guarantee absolute safety.

This statement by Xia Hong gives them peace of mind regarding future concerns.

As long as someone dies for the camp or for Great Xia, not only will their widows and children receive the best care from the camp, but their contributions to the camp will also remain intact and be inherited by their children.

The word "inheritance" might sound ordinary, but for the people in the camp, it is something extremely unfamiliar and unimaginable.

It’s essential to know that neither Great Xia, Da Shi, Huang Zhao, Da Chuan, nor even the Rogel Camp had set such a precedent before.

The reason is simple; in the absence of abundant resources, inheritance isn’t even up for discussion, which is normal.

After all, limited resources must be given priority to the most valuable people.

In fact, even in today’s Great Xia, resources can hardly be described as abundant. The previously overflowing Cold Beast Meat will likely become scarce again soon due to the Mirror Immortal Camp’s invasion.

Although the Redwood Ridge is vast, their hunting range is only that little bit on the outskirts. With the recent Hunting Team’s casualties and defeat, the invasion by the Mirror Immortal Camp will certainly accelerate, reducing their hunting area even further.

In such a situation, Xia Hong still made such a decision.

Xia Chuan couldn’t help but want to speak immediately, as did Luo Yuan, Zhao Long, and others, who were formerly members of the Rogel Camp along with Li Yun and Li Pingkai, who hesitated and wanted to persuade him.

But when they glanced at the widows of Li Yun and Li Pingkai and saw the recovered colors on their orphaned wives and children, they thought better and held back.

"All remaining members of the Hunting Team, come with me!"

After announcing his decision, Xia Hong said nothing more and led everyone to the third floor.

He did not reveal to the public the Mirror Immortal Camp’s connection with the Deceitful Monster, fearing it would cause panic. However, the Hunting Team needed to be informed.

Additionally, after this significant defeat, the Mirror Immortal Camp will undoubtedly take further actions, and arrangements for Great Xia’s external resource gathering must change.

...

On the third floor, seeing the fifteen members of the Hunting Team, Xia Hong sighed heavily in his heart.

As soon as he entered the valley, he noticed that all fifteen were injured, with the most severe being Zhao Bao and Ying Xuan, the former unable to walk on his left leg, only able to move using a wooden crutch, and the latter’s cheeks pierced through, the arrows removed, but the holes were still exceptionally clear, preventing him from speaking.

Xia Hong pondered for a moment, then explained the situation with the Mirror Immortal Camp’s Deceitful Monster and shared his speculations.

"Hahaha, serves them right, so they were infiltrated by the Deceitful Monster. Let’s just wait and watch how they meet their end."

"Rising from the dead, yet unaware of being controlled by the Deceitful Monster, wonderful, the Mirror Immortal Camp is doomed."
