Chapter 243: Chapter 148: Mysterious Visitor, Deciphering the Cold Resistance Level [Vote for Monthly Pass]_4

After introducing himself, he showed a hint of helplessness, cupped his fists towards Xia Hong, and shook his head, saying: "Frankly, Xia, you might not believe it, but I truly got lost during the hunt. I stumbled through Twin Dragon Mountain and accidentally intruded on your camp. It was unintentional, and I hope you can forgive me!"

The village seems to be a large-scale human gathering place between a camp and a town.

Shenwu Village, such a grand name it has.

Twin Dragon Mountain should refer to the two high peaks on the east and west sides of the valley.

He crossed Twin Dragon Mountain?

Xia Hong looked up at Gao Yu, and his heart trembled slightly.

It’s laughable to say, although the Great Xia Station is located in the valley, Xia Hong has never once climbed the two mountains on the east and west sides.

Aside from knowing that they are over two thousand meters high, Xia Hong knows nothing about the other information on the mountains, such as tree species, types and distribution of Cold Beasts, geographical features, or whether there is human activity.

The reason isn’t complicated; with Redwood Ridge nearby, there was no need for him to lead people into unknown areas. Moreover, since nothing special ever happened at the mountaintop, there was no need to bother with it.

But ever since the incident with Wang He, taking the opportunity to apply Coagulated Fire Oil on the upper cliff, Xia Hong climbed up once and looked afar toward the east side, which is behind the valley.

The expanse of Twin Dragon Mountain exceeds that of Redwood Ridge, and Xia Hong estimated its east-west span to be at least over two hundred kilometers.

This Gao Yu claims he crossed Twin Dragon Mountain?

So, the Shenwu Village he mentioned is outside Twin Dragon Mountain.

Hunting alone, crossing an area of over two hundred kilometers...

It’s one thing to hunt alone.

But crossing two hundred kilometers alone, what concept is this?

After coming over for a year and leading the Great Xia Camp till now, even counting the two or three kilometers on the outskirts of Redwood Ridge, the activity range would not exceed ten kilometers.

No matter how you think about it, this is completely impossible!

"May I ask Brother Gao, what is..."

Xia Hong subconsciously spoke, but realized it was inappropriate mid-sentence, so he stopped.

"Xia, you don’t believe I crossed Twin Dragon Mountain alone and want to know what my cultivation level is, right?"

This Gao Yu not only courteous but also perceptive!

He already knew what I intended to say before I finished speaking!

Xia Hong praised him in his heart, then raised his head to meet his gaze and sincerely nodded.

Probing into others’ strength is indeed presumptuous.

However, since the other party guessed it, he didn’t need to conceal it.

Gao Yu didn’t answer Xia Hong’s question; instead, he stared at him for a moment and said loudly: "If I’m not mistaken, you are now advancing from Skin Remodeling to the Cold Resistance Level, right?"

Before Xia Hong could answer, Gao Yu continued: "I was lucky to advance a step earlier than you, having reached the Cold Resistance Level years ago!"

Indeed, the Cold Resistance Level!

Although he had guessed it earlier, hearing the answer directly from the other’s mouth, Xia Hong still heaved a heavy sigh of relief in his heart.

Since returning to the camp, despite Gao Yu’s courteous manner, Xia Hong had not fully relaxed.

After all, since the other’s strength surpassed his, whether things are good or bad, ultimately depends on the other party’s intentions. This feeling of not being in control of the situation naturally made Xia Hong unable to completely let down his guard, especially with the other’s true motive unclear, which made him feel all the more uneasy.

He could roughly perceive Gao Yu’s strength through slight changes in Qi Muscle, stronger than Yuwen Hu but probably within the Cold Resistance Level.

Of course, that was a guess. Gao Yu’s admittance now undoubtedly made him feel more at ease.

Xia Hong’s mind wavered slightly, and the five wooden mannequins preparing to come down from the second floor stopped in their tracks.

A moment later, he showed a hint of surprise, hurriedly stood up, cupped his fists, and said: "Then I must address you as Senior!"

Gao Yu quickly waved his hand and shook his head, saying: "That’s not necessary, not necessary at all, merely luck. From what I observe, Xia, your progress in Skin Remodeling may not be high currently, but your breath is long, your physique is robust, showing the potential for a Combat Body. Once your Skin Remodeling reaches the Leakless Realm and you break through to the Cold Resistance Level, your strength will certainly skyrocket."

"I, for one, am not worthy of being called Senior!"

A Combat Body, the Leakless Realm...

Upon hearing two unfamiliar terms consecutively, Xia Hong’s interest was immediately piqued.

Not only him, but Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and other members of the Hunting Team also looked curious.

Gao Yu’s keen insight was self-evident, so there was no need for Xia Hong to ask. Upon seeing the expressions of the people, he immediately smiled and enlightened everyone.

"A normal breakthrough to the Cold Resistance Level..."

As Gao Yu elaborated, learning the many details of breaking through from the Earth-digging Realm to the Cold Resistance Level, everyone showed expressions of sudden realization.

While Xia Hong’s expression went from dazed to fearful, and ultimately to being overwhelmingly excited.
