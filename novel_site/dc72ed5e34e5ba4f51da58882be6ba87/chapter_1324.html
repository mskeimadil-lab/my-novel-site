Chapter 1324: Chapter 432: Shattered Star Sword, Yellow Mustard Bag, Preparations for Departure, Rankings and Rewards (Part 2)

At this point he paused slightly, then his expression turned excited as he said, "That Mystic Light Armor not only increases toughness and weight, it can also greatly enhance the sharpness of the blade. We smelted a ring of Mystic Light Armor around the sword body, and focused on filling the blade with it. As for how sharp it is, I estimate..."

Shhh—

By now, Xia Hong had already slowly drawn the Shattered Star Sword from its scabbard.

The instant he drew the Shattered Star Sword, Xia Hong immediately sensed clearly that the sword itself weighed just over twenty million jin, and the remaining more than one million jin was the weight of the scabbard.

That’s right!

This Shattered Star Sword, even its scabbard was made from Ten Thousand Forging Level iron.

The blade of the Shattered Star Sword was black just like the scabbard, but its surface was densely dotted with countless tiny, brilliant specks of light, like the night sky after the stars had been shattered—truly worthy of the name Shattered Star.

Xia Hong subconsciously lifted his head and glanced at the other nine weapons.

Mu Dong immediately realized what Xia Hong was thinking, and hurriedly tried to dissuade him with a tense look: "Lord, you must not. It won’t be too late to test the sword once you’re out in the wilderness!"

"Hahaha, relax, relax, I’m not that extravagant."

Xia Hong laughed and motioned for Mu Dong not to worry. Every Ten Thousand Forging Level weapon required an enormous investment of manpower and resources to forge. To use them just for testing was pure rich-man behavior, and Great Xia didn’t have that kind of financial strength yet.

"Lord, please come with me!"

Looking at the Shattered Star Sword, the more Xia Hong looked, the more he liked it. In the end he didn’t put it back on the rack, but kept it directly in his hand. Then he looked at the other nine weapons and singled out the Coiling Dragon Stick as well.

Taking the two weapons, Xia Hong followed behind Mu Dong to the inner side of the quiet room, stopping in front of a desk. When he saw what lay on the desk, his expression instantly hardened.

On that desk, there was a square yellow stone slab laid out flat, two meters to a side.

Visually, it was indeed a stone slab, yet as Xia Hong looked at the whole slab, his eyes inexplicably felt a sense of chaos. He couldn’t help reaching out to touch it; the texture was also indeed that of stone.

Where was this sense of chaos coming from?

Mu Dong’s face was full of smiles. After Xia Hong finished touching the slab, he actually stepped forward, lifted the entire slab up, and very quickly folded it into the shape of a bundle.

The stone slab was folded into a bundle...

Seeing this scene, a guess immediately surfaced in Xia Hong’s heart. With a look of delighted surprise, he asked, "So this isn’t a stone slab, but cloth?"

Mu Dong nodded excitedly, then suddenly thought of something. He went to the other side of the quiet room and brought back two Ten Thousand Forged giant axes, and right in front of Xia Hong, stuffed the axes straight into the bundle.

The handles of those two giant axes alone were more than two meters long; counting the axe blades, the total length was at least close to three meters. Yet the round bundle in Mu Dong’s hand had a diameter of at most one meter.

The giant axes were actually completely loaded into the bundle.

A Storage Bag!

Those three words flashed quickly through Xia Hong’s mind, and he couldn’t hide his excitement. He hurriedly reached out to take the bundle from Mu Dong, then, looking incredibly thrilled, reached in to feel for the giant axes inside.

"Among the many Ore the Lord brought back from the Jiao Long Nest, the most precious should be this Yellow Mustard Stone. It actually has the effect of expanding space. Containers made from it have a real capacity ten times that of their physical volume."

The Craftsmen Department had actually managed to make a Storage Bag!

Looking at the Storage Bag in his hand, Xia Hong’s face was mixed with shock and intense joy. He had already probed the space inside the bag; it was about five cubic meters, indeed roughly ten times the bundle’s own volume.

"How much Yellow Mustard Stone does it take to make a bundle like this?"

The Ore brought back from the Jiao Long Nest last year had been too much, and Xia Hong had even forgotten which one the Yellow Mustard Stone was. Thinking of how useful Storage Bags were, he immediately asked the key question.

The helplessness on Mu Dong’s face already told him the answer.

"That piece of Yellow Mustard Stone weighed just over sixty jin in total. This piece of Yellow Mustard Cloth used nearly ten jin. We also forged three wine flasks for the Lord, which used another fifteen jin, and now there are only thirty-six jin left."

Like presenting a treasure, Mu Dong took out three gourd-shaped wine flasks of different colors from the side and put them straight into the bundle.

Xia Hong took out the two giant axes from the bundle, then put the Shattered Star Sword and the Coiling Dragon Stick inside. Only then did he turn his head to look at Mu Dong, his expression a bit odd as he said:

"Pulling all these tricks... you knew I was going out, didn’t you?"

"Heh heh!"

Mu Dong chuckled, then said, "This subordinate knew nothing. It was the Minister who hinted to me that after the Lord left seclusion this time, you’d probably be heading out alone to scout the strength of the Feudal Towns. With the mountains high and the roads long, you ought to have something handy on you. The Yellow Mustard Bag, plus these three wine gourds, so the Lord can still sip summer wine from time to time on the road—wouldn’t that be delightful?"

"I’ll borrow your good omen, then. These two items really are quite good..."

At his words, a smile appeared on Xia Hong’s face. He lightly hefted the Yellow Mustard Bag twice to feel its weight, and a look of understanding flashed in his eyes.

Rather than a Storage Bag, it was more like a holding bag. Once the Shattered Star Sword and Coiling Dragon Stick were put inside, the Yellow Mustard Bag’s weight immediately shot up. This was far from the storage ring–type function he’d known in his previous life.

Even so, it was already quite good.

A normal bundle, even if it could fit two weapons, would be impossible to carry on the back.

This Yellow Mustard Bag perfectly solved his travel problem.

"Alright, as for the remaining eight weapons, aside from the Dragon Sparrow, Cold Blood, and the Heavenly Transformation Sword, the other five can be sent directly into the Camp Supply Department’s armory. Have Qiu Peng bring people to set a price. Later, whenever someone in the Camp breaks through to the Xianyang Level, they can start using them first."
