A typical member of a low-level Hunting Team in the Earth-digging Realm can at most earn a little over 1,000 contribution points per month, so 2,000 contribution points is definitely a huge amount of money for these dozen or so women present, and such a reaction is not surprising.

Zhao Ying, with her rich experience in childbirth, noticed Li Xuanling playing with the children on the bed, remembered something, and hurriedly said to Xia Hong, "Have the two princes not been named yet? The Lord should give them names!"

Upon hearing this, not only were the ten or so maids, including Han Yue, Han Shuang, excited, but Li Xuanling also spirit up immediately, holding the two children, turning her head to gaze at Xia Hong with great interest, eager to see what names he would choose.

"Yu Yao, Yu Sheng!"

Xia Hong was clearly prepared and directly announced the two names.

"Xia Yuyao, Xia Yusheng..."

Li Xuanling, lying on the soft couch, gently recited the names of the two children, her eyes full of brightness, obviously very satisfied with these two names.

"Prince Yu Yao, Prince Yu Sheng, oh my! The Lord has chosen such excellent names, they sound like noble scions. As a common woman, let me first pay respects to the two young princes, and bask in their noble presence, greetings to the young princes!"

Zhao Ying, having experienced many similar situations, spoke fluently, causing smiles to appear on the faces of Xia Hong, Li Xuanling, and the other maids in the room, including Xia Chuan who had just walked in.

"We should also bask in the joy and pay respects to the two young princes!"

Han Yue, Han Shuang, and the other maids ceremoniously bowed towards the bed.

"Han Yue, go to the Political Hall and inform everyone that I will be there shortly."

"Yes, Lord!"

"Xia Chuan, come, hold your little niece and nephew!"

After instructing Han Yue to notify everyone, Xia Hong saw Xia Chuan enter, smiled, and picked up the children from Li Xuanling’s arms, signaling him to come and hold them.

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan hurriedly stepped forward, taking the niece and nephew from Xia Hong’s hands, and noticed the unusual holy patterns on their foreheads, and looked up at Xia Hong with concern.

"It’s alright, it’s caused by the Jade Dew Stone within their Xuanling bodies, these little ones are likely benefiting from a blessing in disguise."

Hearing this, the worry on Xia Chuan’s face instantly disappeared, and just as he was about to speak, his little niece on the left reached out and grabbed his collar.

Xia Chuan was taken aback, then suddenly looked up in shock.

Five thousand pounds, born at the Logging Realm, how... how is this possible!

Xia Chuan found it hard to believe, stretched out his index finger, and touched his little nephew Xia Yusheng’s hand on the right, and, after he grabbed it, finally confirmed his perception was not a mistake.

"Elder Brother..."

"No need to say more, they just have some anomalies at birth, only time will tell how their talents will develop, let’s go, there are many others waiting in the Political Hall, let’s take them out to meet everyone."

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan immediately nodded, refraining from saying anything further, but looked at his niece and nephew in his arms with intense joy in the depths of his eyes.

"Eh, there are water beads, what is this?"

Suddenly, a cool sensation rose from Xia Chuan’s left hand holding his little niece, and as he looked, he noticed a thin layer of transparent mist forming on his arm.

The mist was not completely transparent, with a layer of bluish glow, and under the high temperature of the Cold Ruins Tripod, it was clearly converting into droplets.

"Elder Brother, is this mist turning into Cold Apple Jade Dew? What about Yu Yao?"

"It’s definitely Cold Apple Jade Dew. If I guess right, Yu Sheng absorbed the core blue flame of the Jade Dew Stone, making his constitution more yang, while Yu Yao integrated the transparent outer layer, resulting in a cold-leaning constitution, constantly emitting this unique cold aura that condenses into jade dew when encountering high heat, clearly a remarkable treasure!"

The primary form of Cold Apple Jade Dew, Xia Hong had seen it on the Cold Apple Trees of Long Mountain, and it was exactly like the mist caused by his daughter; he had noticed it just upon contact with his daughter.

"Does the condensation of Cold Apple Jade Dew affect Yu Yao’s body...?"

"It has no significant impact. Yu Yao has a continuous source of this kind of cold aura inside her, mostly overflowing, otherwise it wouldn’t affect the surroundings!"

Upon hearing Xia Hong’s answer, Xia Chuan’s expression became even more excited.

This meant that as long as his niece grew up safely, the Great Xia would have an endless supply of Cold Apple Jade Dew.

His little niece was simply... extraordinary!

No, with such an extraordinary little niece, what about the little nephew?

Just as Xia Chuan was about to continue observing, Xia Hong had already taken the two children from him and was briskly making his way toward the Political Hall.

"Hopefully, we can, didn’t the Lord have Han Yue inform us to wait?"
