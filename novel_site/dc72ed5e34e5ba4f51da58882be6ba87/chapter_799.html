Chapter 799: Chapter 316: Blood Jade Fox and Mischief, Xia City in Turmoil, Migration Begins

This breathing sound is just like what we heard at the first silver mine.

Is that Beast King following us?

Gulp...

At this moment, even Xia Hong couldn’t help but swallow, staying still for three to four breaths before abruptly turning his head to look behind.

Li Xuanling and Li Tiancheng followed suit, looking behind them.

Behind them, a hundred meters to the west of the Mud Slope, stood a gigantic beast over a hundred meters long and ten meters tall at the shoulder.

The beast’s head alone was over ten meters in diameter, covered in moonlit silver fur, with a circle of spikes around its neck over ten meters long, resembling sharp steel spikes in the snowy night, intensely intimidating, with vertical cyan pupils like giant diamond-shaped doors, fixedly staring at the three of them without blinking.

"Wolf King, it’s the Beast King from the first silver mine!"

"It discovered us a long time ago and followed us from behind all the way."

"Run separately, or none of us will survive!"

After exchanging a sentence, Li Xuanling and Li Tiancheng immediately decided to escape separately, but just as they were about to move, Xia Hong stopped them.

"Wait, something’s wrong!"

Xia Hong twitched his nose, a glint of light flashed in his left eye, and after staring at the giant wolf for a moment, the seriousness on his face disappeared instantly.

"What a cursed beast, playing tricks like this..."

Li Xuanling and Li Tiancheng were puzzled, not knowing what Xia Hong meant.

"You stay here, don’t move, wait for me!"

But before they could inquire, Xia Hong left a sentence and drew the Cold Blood Blade from his waist and charged toward the enormous Wolf King.

"Is this guy crazy!"

"No, something seems amiss, with the Wolf King’s size, the Cold Beast on the hill behind us isn’t reacting at all."

Li Xuanling’s expression froze, and she immediately realized something.

Over at the west side, Xia Hong had arrived in front of the Wolf King.

"Roar..."

The Wolf King’s thunderous roar exploded in their ears, Li Xuanling instantly felt the surrounding land for dozens of miles start to tremble violently, and she turned to look at the hill behind them again, seeing there was no movement at all, her expression turned grim.

"Truly trapped, it must be the toxic mist sprayed by the Advanced Blood Jade Fox, we’ve all inhaled it, how did he see it?"

"Did you forget, when White Dew Gu controlled Xuantian’s six men to assassinate him, he saw through it immediately, he must have some means to see through illusions and disguises."

Putting aside their shock, Xia Hong was already smoothly engaged with the Wolf King.

The Wolf King’s size was just too large, Xia Hong, charging from the ground, seemed almost inconsequential, even though he leaped very high, he could only reach the lower jaw of the Wolf King.

Swish...

The Cold Blood is a horizontal blade, so Xia Hong often uses it for slashing, but this time he used it to hack, and his target wasn’t the Wolf King’s jaw, but the very lower part of its right forelimb.

Hiss...

A sharp shredding sound echoed as the Wolf King’s right forelimb suddenly lifted, facing Xia Hong’s Cold Blood Blade, carving out three golden claw marks.

Those golden claw marks seemed to slice through the air, leaving barely any discernible trajectory, appearing only for the moment when the Wolf King raised its claw, then directly clashed with Xia Hong’s Cold Blood Blade.

Clang...

A violent sound of gold and stone clashing erupted, bursting into a sky full of sparks.

"More than thirty bristles, this Blood Jade Fox’s strength is unexpectedly powerful!"

At the instant the Cold Blood Blade and the golden paw clashed, the massive Wolf King body directly dissipated, revealing in front of Xia Hong a snow-white fox-shaped Cold Beast only twenty-something centimeters with a half-meter long fluffy tail.

The sparks hadn’t yet dissipated, the Cold Beast’s originally snow-white fur, under the illumination of the fire, turned into a crystal-clear blood jade color as Li Xuanling said, truly living up to the name of Blood Jade Fox.

"Chirp..."

The Blood Jade Fox’s crimson pupils were filled with panic at that moment, because its paw had been directly severed a small portion by Xia Hong’s Cold Blood Blade.

The strength with over thirty bristles was naturally not qualified to act fierce in front of Xia Hong.

The Blood Jade Fox reacted swiftly, abandoning even its paw to transform into a white blur, fleeing towards the east, the direction of the Mud Slope’s nest.

"Already come knocking, and you still want to run!"

Unfortunately, Xia Hong didn’t give it a chance.

The Blood Jade Fox hadn’t escaped ten meters when Xia Hong suddenly turned around, sprinting rapidly, and plunged the Cold Blood Blade downward into the front end of the white blur.

"Sizzle..."

The Cold Blood Blade pierced directly through the Blood Jade Fox’s neck, pinning it to the ground, though the Blood Jade Fox was unwilling to give up resisting, glaring fiercely at Xia Hong, emitting sizzle sounds from its mouth.

Very quickly, however, it noticed something wrong, its eyes filled with terror, twisting its head frantically, obviously trying to break free from the Cold Blood Blade.

But Xia Hong’s strength was so terrifying, he gripped the handle tightly, the Blood Jade Fox struggled for only three to four breaths before running out of strength.

Xia Hong pulled out the blade, weighed the Blood Jade Fox in his hand for a moment, then looked down at the Cold Blood Blade which became much heavier, shaking his head slightly.

"Your blood-sucking speed is getting faster and faster, just in this short time, you consumed seven to eight pounds, next time I pull the blade faster, or else I will have to feed you with Advanced Beast Blood in the future."

The bleeding volume of an Advanced Cold Beast is only ten to fifteen pounds, but the effect is ten times that of Intermediate Beast Blood and can be used for alchemy, considered extremely precious for Great Xia, feeding it all to the blade leaves Xia Hong a bit heartbroken now.
