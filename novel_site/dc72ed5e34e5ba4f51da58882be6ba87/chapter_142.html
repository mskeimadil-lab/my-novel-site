Chapter 142: Chapter 116: Great Righteousness, Hunting Flood Dragons, Saliva [Five-in-One Mega Chapter]_3

Luo Yuan, Zhao Long, and their two other brothers, along with Yuan Cheng, who just had a breakthrough, were now capable of hunting together. Xia Hong did not plan to lead the hunting team forever.

The hard currency of the camp, Cold Beast Meat, would soon be obtained by them. If they were dissatisfied with the current distribution method,

then Xia Hong would really have to make a new one.

"Leader, how do we divide the contribution points for the prey during joint hunts?"

The five of them remained silent for a short while before Yuan Cheng was the first to speak.

Hearing the question, Xia Hong had a peculiar expression but after thinking it over, he still replied, "Under normal circumstances, it’s naturally based on the role each of you plays. This is something for you to discuss among yourselves during your joint hunts. If I’m out hunting with you, I won’t take any contribution value. You all can split it evenly!"

"The leader is magnanimous!"

Hearing Xia Hong’s words, the five of them suddenly showed joy in their eyes.

Luo Yuan couldn’t help but speak up.

Xia Hong chuckled and shook his head. He was the leader, and what’s more, he had the strength and didn’t really need these things, so it was fine to distribute them to others.

Seeing that the five had no other questions, Xia Hong still wore a peculiar expression. After glancing around at the five, he continued, "Do you have any objections to the contribution point conversion for the five types of prey?"

Hearing Xia Hong’s question, the five looked puzzled for a moment but all shook their heads.

This made Xia Hong’s expression even more peculiar.

Could it be that these five people couldn’t do the math?

Luo Yuan noticed Xia Hong’s expression, evidently realizing something, and his face showed a hint of anger. He said, "Leader, you are underestimating us too much.

Putting everything else aside, we risked our lives before to save us and provided so much Cold Beast Meat for our cultivation without any compensation. Such selflessness ensures our safety. Without the leader’s contribution, we would have no chance of breaking through to the Earth-digging Realm, and the Great Xia wouldn’t be what it is today.

We venture out hunting with the intention of contributing to the Great Xia. If we kept tabs on every little thing and only thought about ourselves, would we even be considered worthy of being part of the Great Xia?"

After Luo Yuan spoke, Zhao Long and the other two, as well as Yuan Cheng, immediately understood. So Xia Hong had remained silent for so long because he thought they would be dissatisfied with the prey contribution point conversion.

"If not for the leader, I would have died long ago and wouldn’t have the good life I have now. Leader, even if it’s two or three hundred points, even if you ask me to hunt for free, I, Yuan Cheng, have no objections whatsoever."

"The leader’s great kindness is etched in our hearts. Even if the three of us brothers do more, it wouldn’t match what you, the leader, have done before. Just like Yuan Cheng, as long as the leader gives the word, the three of us will charge ahead, go through fire and water without any complaints."

...

Their declarations moved Xia Hong slightly.

Xia Hong glanced at the five before finally turning his gaze to Xia Chuan beside him.

Xia Chuan had a helpless smile on his face, he pouted at Xia Hong.

Xia Hong also chuckled and shook his head. Though he made no sound, he immediately understood what Xia Chuan meant.

"See, I knew it would be like this."

The contribution point conversion for the five types of Cold Beast prey was something Xia Chuan and Xia Hong had discussed together,

According to Xia Chuan’s initial plan, the contribution points for the five types of prey were much lower. It was Xia Hong’s fear that Luo Yuan and the others would object that made him insist on increasing them.

Xia Chuan had said at the time that the five would not mind, but Xia Hong did not believe it.

Now it seemed Xia Chuan was right.

Or perhaps Xia Hong did not fully appreciate the exemplary influence he had and his high prestige among the people in the camp.

"Well, I was being petty. Your noble awareness ensures that all my efforts were not in vain. The stronger the Great Xia becomes, the better your lives will be; conversely, the better your lives, the stronger the Great Xia will naturally become.

Just remember one thing: the future of the Great Xia is deeply connected with each and every one in the camp, and the two complement each other."

"Got it, leader!"

The five answered in unison, greatly invigorating Xia Hong’s spirits.

He looked up towards the Cloud Mist Zone and pointed distantly with a large axe.

"Remember the three points I mentioned earlier, prepare to enter."

After speaking, Xia Hong led the way, and the remaining five quickly caught up behind him.

As the toxic mist grew thicker, Xia Hong felt the air becoming extremely unsuitable for breathing. He quickly pulled out the thoroughly soaked leather from his chest and covered his nose and mouth with it.

The other five did the same.

With their noses and mouths covered, the six of them plunged into the Cloud Mist Zone.

...

"Estimated body length is around 35 meters, 65 centimeters thick, similar to the one we encountered earlier. The range of the Cloud Mist Zone is roughly 50 meters in diameter."

After cautiously advancing about ten meters into the Cloud Mist Zone, the group looked up and already spotted their target. Xia Hong spoke in a low voice, muffled by the cloth covering his nose and mouth, so the sound wasn’t very clear.

However, the six who were close by could still hear him well.

The trees in Redwood Ridge were already large, with the Cloud Mist Zone’s 50-meter range encompassing only a few dozen large trees. The Teng Jiao was right above a large tree in the center area.

The trunk of that tree had a diameter of about eight meters, not the thickest, but the main trunk was exceptionally tall with many offshoots extending to the sides, where the body of the Teng Jiao was coiled twistedly, with only its tail hanging to the ground.
