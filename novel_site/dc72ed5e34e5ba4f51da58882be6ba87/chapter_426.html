Chapter 426: Chapter 201: Wuyuan Garrison, Cold Marrow Iron_3

Because as he spoke, he was constantly observing the reaction of that Great Xia Leader and the surrounding eight people.

Just as expected, the one-eyed Great Xia Leader and the eight people on both sides below showed expressions of joy upon hearing his words.

When Xiang Liang heard those words outside the house earlier, he was mentally prepared.

Combining this with the reactions of the nine people in front of him, how could he not realize that the reason Flying Tiger Camp found the location of the Great Xia Station was clearly this group of people doing it on purpose.

The purpose, just as he had overheard outside the cabin, was to deliberately incite the people of Flying Tiger Camp to harbor greed, enticing them to attack Great Xia.

This way, they could justifiably annex Flying Tiger Camp...

"Hahahaha, this Flying Tiger Camp has some guts, thought it’d be just them, didn’t expect they’d bring in the other two families, this really saves us a lot of trouble, hahahaha..."

Lin Kai, sitting at the top on the left, suddenly stood up from his chair, laughing incessantly, and the other seven people also showed smiles on their faces.

Xu Ning, sitting at the top, also couldn’t help but show a smile on his face at this moment.

He looked down at Xiang Liang below, glanced at the wooden box in his hand, pondered for a moment, then said thoughtfully: "Since Wang Lie knows to bring in the other two families, he must have gone to Hefeng Camp as well. Your father didn’t agree, so he sent you to inform us?"

Xiang Liang, at this point, couldn’t care less about the shock in his heart and quickly nodded, "Sir, your insight is remarkable, I admire it. When Wang Lie saw that my father disagreed, he brought the other two families and attacked our Hefeng Camp together last night. It’s likely that my father and the others in our camp have been controlled by Wang Lie and the three families. I only managed to escape by chance amidst the chaos, and luckily, I was saved by Master Xu An, which is the only reason I’m alive."

After Xu Ning heard this, he pondered for a moment and looked at his brother, Xu An.

Xu An nodded and said, "He didn’t lie, when I saved him, he had already been hit by three arrows on his back, almost died, it was with a Hot Wind Pill and Golden Sore Powder that he survived."

Only then did Xu Ning smile and nod, looking at Xiang Liang, "I know your father, Xiang Ping, he’s a decent person, so your words... I believe!"

Upon hearing Xu Ning’s last two words, Xiang Liang’s expression suddenly became excited.

By now, he was no longer worried about the attack on Great Xia.

What he had just seen with his own eyes, heard with his own ears, and the conversation and reactions of the nine people in the hall at this moment, were enough to prove that Wang Lie gathering the three families to attack Great Xia Station was an absolute joke.

The only thing he was worried about now was the aftermath of Great Xia seeking revenge.

With Xu Ning believing him, Hefeng Camp was no longer in trouble.

No wonder, no wonder his father insisted on not agreeing to Wang Lie’s proposal; he must have had some understanding of Great Xia Camp’s strength.

Yes, hadn’t the Great Xia Leader just said he knew his father?

Have they met before?

Xu Ning’s doubts were quickly dispelled by someone’s words in the hall.

"Xiang Ping is indeed a decent person. Among the several transactions with the four camps in Northern Mountain, only he proposed to send someone to see Great Xia, but unfortunately, Wang Lie stopped him on the spot..."

Upon hearing Lu Yang’s words, Xu Ning nodded his head.

The transactions with the four camps in Northern Mountain had always been handled by Lu Yang’s East Sun Team, and he was the only one who had contact with the leaders of the four camps.

Xu Ning said he knew Xiang Ping, also from Lu Yang.

Clap...

Lin Kai, clad in golden armor, stood up directly from his chair, looked back at Xu Ning, and said, "In that case, let’s not wait. I’ll lead a team over and deal with these three camps all at once. What do you think, Xu Zi?"

After pondering for a moment, Xu Ning directly nodded, "Alright, I’ll go with you, just as we’ll be returning to the station tomorrow night for the New Year, these over five thousand people can be our congratulatory gift from Wuyuan for the Leader’s return!"

Upon hearing the two lightly decide to take action against Flying Tiger Camp, Xiang Liang was scared and shocked, but before he could calm down, he noticed Xu Ning’s last words, and he was instantly stunned.

Return to the station?

Isn’t this place the station of Great Xia?

No, this one-eyed sir isn’t even the leader of the Great Xia Camp?

This...

As Xiang Liang’s mind turned into a storm of waves, his brain was rapidly processing.

Very quickly, he realized this was a perfect opportunity, and he immediately addressed Xu Ning at the top, speaking loudly:

"I have a precious treasure to present to the Great Xia Leader, I hope sir can introduce me!"

As Xiang Liang’s voice fell, the whole hall instantly fell into silence.

Xu Ning at the top, as well as everyone below, including Lin Kai who had already donned his helmet and left his seat to gather people for departure, all turned their eyes on Xiang Liang kneeling on the ground, faces full of peculiar expressions.

"You want to meet our leader?"

After a long while, Lin Kai finally couldn’t hold back and spoke. After he finished, he directly walked slowly towards Xiang Liang.

Lin Kai, standing over 190 cm tall, with his pale gold armor and helmet covering half his face, made his tall and strong figure even more imposing.

With his intentional heavy footsteps, he felt even more oppressive.

Kneeling on the ground, Xiang Liang felt like a mountain was walking towards him, his back instantly soaked with sweat, and his body couldn’t help but begin trembling.

"Present a treasure, is it that thing in your hand, let me see."

Despite his trembling body, upon hearing Lin Kai’s words, Xiang Liang still bit his lip and insisted in his tone, "Forgive me, sir, this treasure is of great importance, I must see the leader in person."
