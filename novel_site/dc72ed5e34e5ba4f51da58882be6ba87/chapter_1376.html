Chapter 1376: Chapter 446: Arrangements, Growing Strength, and Departure—the Grand Sight of Hongmen

The Hu Brothers can only be considered a casual move!

Both of them have Intermediate Battle Bodies, the older brother Hu Tiejiang is 38 years old this year, and the younger brother Hu Tiechuan is 36, with a strength of just over 5 mane.

With this aptitude, it’s basically certain that the investment and return are not going to be equivalent, so under normal circumstances, there’s no need to support them.

If Xia Hong were to make an exception for them, the reason might be fate.

Last month, they traveled together, although the Hu Brothers took the initiative to approach him, it was enough to make Xia Hong remember them, so it counts.

Besides fate, it’s also possible that the brothers’ determination to change their social status is a reason.

In simple terms, it’s ambition!

The disaster they suffered was ultimately to remove their own lowly status and their wives’ slave status. Normally, they should feel some regret after enduring so much inhuman torture and suffering.

But according to Xia Hong’s observation, during their twenty days at Hong Mansion, the brothers did not complain even once, not just them, but their wives Fang Jing and Wang Qing as well.

This proves that even after suffering so much, the brothers wholeheartedly acknowledge and persist in their actions to shed their status.

This determination to rise above mediocrity and risk their lives to change their status and destiny is rare not only in Cai Qiu but in Great Xia as well.

Of course, not complaining does not mean there is no hatred.

The brothers harbor deep hatred toward the Zhang Family of Red Tiger Ridge and the Law Enforcement Team of Qinghua City, which has penetrated to their bones.

This is the core reason why Xia Hong supports them.

Last August, after leaving the Bone-Eroding Path, he lived with Su Jing and her four siblings in the depths of Evergreen Valley for over four months to heal. During that time, he hunted countless Advanced Cold Beasts, partly for the siblings’ cultivation resources and to understand the Cold Beast situation in Evergreen Valley.

Before setting off last month, he only took five Beast King Level skeleton sets and stored the rest in that cave. Those supplies naturally meant nothing to him, but to the Hu Brothers, they were a definite fortune.

Of course, whether the brothers can use them properly is another matter.

Xia Hong gave some instructions and then dismissed the two.

"If they can really rise with these supplies, then they’re both promising, they might not stir up Cai Qiu, but Evergreen Valley should see some impact;

If neither of them has the ability, these supplies will only bring them calamity; risks and opportunities coexist, it’s all up to them!"

After the two departed, Xia Hong returned to the meditation room in the main building to continue sitting on the Enlightenment Lotus Platform and checked the remaining Dan Medicine.

"6 Washing Marrow Pills, 13 Top-grade Bone Shedding Pills, in one month about one third was used, similar to my previous Jade Bone situation, still needs two more months to be used up. Cai Yunzhou was also present just now, I reckon he’s warned too, they shouldn’t dare to provoke me randomly anymore, setting off to Lin Chu County in two months would have some assurance!"

Fabricating a false origin and bluffing to ensure my safety is somewhat helpless, but it’s the only choice I have.

Before understanding the specific strength of the Tribulation Body Realm, there’s certainly no courage to go to Cai Qiu Town, but Lin Chu County, which is one level lower, is feasible.

"Furthermore, I need to come up with another false identity, although the identity of a Daqin Prince is intimidating, it also limits my actions, like being unable to buy things freely..."

Actions need to match identity, since the fabricated Great Qin Feudal Town has such strength, naturally it should have better things than Cai Qiu. Last month, in Treasure Pavilion, he wanted to buy many items but to avoid suspicion, he pretended with disdain and barely bought anything.

Even when purchasing Washing Marrow Pills, Bone Burning Pills, and Top-grade Bone Shedding Pills, he used "for research" as a reason.

The Bone Burning Pill is okay, but the Washing Marrow Pill and Bone Shedding Pill are truly useful to him, if possible, he would’ve bought more.

"It’s useless to think now, will figure out something when I arrive at Lin Chu County!"

After a moment of thought but unable to find a solution, Xia Hong simply stopped pondering, as there’s enough Dan Medicine for two months, cultivation comes first.

He closed his eyes and quickly entered the cultivation state.

As Xia Hong was closing himself off for cultivation, the Hu Brothers also started their final preparations before leaving the mansion.

Hu Tiejiang first found the steward Zhou Qing, had him take the entire family to the Subordinate Bureau to remove their slave and lowly status and then obtained civilian status.

The money for their status change was originally taken by the Law Enforcement Team, but after Su Jing and her siblings rescued them last month, it was immediately returned. With Zhou Qing’s appearance and the grand reputation of Hong Mansion, the Subordinate Bureau dared not obstruct them.

After changing their status, Hu Tiejiang did not rush to leave but sent his family back to Hong Mansion, leaving them behind, he took his brother Hu Tiechuan to Qing Valley together.

No one knew what the brothers were doing in Qing Valley, but after just seven days, they returned to Qinghua City with a large cart, pulling straight into Treasure Pavilion without even visiting the Subordinate Bureau.

On February 23 during the day, the brothers finally returned to Hong Mansion.

Back at the side courtyard of Hong Mansion, they didn’t see their own wives or family but went directly to the meditation room, where Hu Tiejiang opened a bundle revealing more than thirty pill bottles of various sizes inside.
