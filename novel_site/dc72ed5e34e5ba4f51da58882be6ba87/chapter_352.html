Chapter 352: Chapter 180: Exchange Meeting, Murder, Great Xia Gospel_3

"Elders, there’s no need to panic. I am Xia Chuan, the Great Xia Camp Director. I come today with no ill intentions, but to bring you all a grand gift!"

Under the flickering light, the moment they saw Xia Chuan, Zhu Xing, Meng Yuan, and people from the other eight camps all had their pupils contract.

It wasn’t just Xia Chuan’s youthful and handsome face that was striking. His black cloak, beast skin boots, and blue beast fur hat were enough to astonish everyone present.

Not to mention, the slender long knife hanging at his waist.

It was evident at a glance that the sheath of that long knife was made of iron.

Coupled with the force that had just sent the two people inside flying out.

Meng Yuan and Zhu Xing exchanged glances, their expressions filled with seriousness.

"Lord Xia Chuan, you have indeed arrived!"

As Xiao Kangcheng spoke, the rest immediately looked at him with anger in their eyes.

"Do not blame Leader Xiao, I have said, I come today with no malice, only to present you with a grand gift. Do you elders recognize these two people on the ground?"

Xia Chuan didn’t intend to harm Xiao Kangcheng, so after helping him with a phrase, he immediately continued, prompting everyone to take a good look at the two people on the ground.

Though they were skeptical, the crowd still turned to look at the two people on the ground.

"Isn’t that Liu Erhu?"

After observing carefully, Meng Yuan was the first to exclaim in surprise.

He seemed somewhat doubtful and immediately looked at the other person. After examining, he exclaimed in shock again, "Liu Sanhu!"

With that, even the ten other leaders, including Xiao Kangcheng who had already suspected, couldn’t help but look down and examine closely.

"It’s really Liu Erhu and Liu Sanhu!"

"Could it be that the Three Tigers Camp was destroyed by him..."

"Was it this Great Xia Camp that wiped them out?"

...

Once they confirmed the identities of the two on the ground, indeed the two leaders of the Three Tigers Camp, everyone’s gaze towards Xia Chuan completely changed, as countless guesses emerged in their minds.

Seeing that everyone recognized the two, Xia Chuan’s eyes lit up, saving him much effort. Smiling, he continued:

"The Three Tigers Camp committed numerous crimes in the vicinity of Five Ape Mountain, causing you much trouble. Since Great Xia has arrived, such acts cannot be tolerated. Today, besides these two main culprits, Yuwen Dao, come in!"

As he finished speaking, Xia Chuan called out to the outside of the cave.

Turning upon hearing his voice, everyone hurriedly looked towards the cave entrance.

From outside the cave, Yuwen Dao led a large group of over a hundred people rushing in, none other than members of Great Xia’s Hunting Team.

Nearly everyone held one or two captives, all tightly bound with ropes, among them men and women, even some elderly. The one thing they shared in common was the thick aura of hostility around them.

Identical to that of Liu Erhu and Liu Sanhu.

Yuwen Dao held two people in his hands, entering and promptly kicking them down, then respectfully saluting Xia Chuan, "Lord!"

The others followed suit, kicking their captives forward before respectfully saluting Xia Chuan and then standing motionless behind the captives.

After Yuwen Dao and the others saluted, a deathly silence fell over the cave.

Zhu Xing, Meng Yuan, the people from the other eight camps, and nearly everyone including Xiao Kangcheng, at this moment, focused their entire attention on Yuwen Dao and his group, their pupils quivering incessantly, faces filled with shock.

As Xia Chuan scanned the room and saw the reactions on their faces, a faint smile appeared on his lips, opting not to speak immediately.

He knew full well why they reacted this way.

Remaining silent was to give them time to mentally process.

"Are they wearing a complete set of beast skin clothing?"

"Longbows, broadswords, everyone has one?"

"Look at that, isn’t the double-edged giant axe on his back just too massive, how much iron ore was spent on that, possibly over two or three thousand pounds?"

"Swords, swords, axes, long sticks, and long spears, so many weapons."

"Not just that, look at their waists, each has a token hanging, that token also seems to be made of iron."

"Is that a beast tooth accessory on that one’s neck?"

"That seems to be small fangs of the Thorny Wolf, a Thorny Wolf has only two, that strand must have over a hundred!"

"Hiss..."

...

Murmurs of discussion had already started among the crowd.

Upon hearing the chatter from their own people behind, Zhu Xing and Meng Yuan felt embarrassed, quickly turning to glare at them, but upon turning back to look at Yuwen Dao and his group, the shock in their eyes betrayed them.

If even the leaders couldn’t contain their astonishment, how could those under them do any better?

Comparing to the people in this area of Five Ape Mountain, those from the Great Xia Camp, whether in attire or weaponry, even casual accessories, were too eye-catching.

So striking that they couldn’t look away.

The entire cave was enveloped in silence for nearly forty to fifty breaths due to the appearance of Yuwen Dao and his group, until someone finally looked away from them, and Xia Chuan felt it was the right time, walked slowly to the rear of Liu Erhu and Liu Sanhu, gripping the knife at his waist, a cold light flashing in his eyes.

"These beasts, using their little power to wreak havoc, not only enslaving others and treating human lives as worthless but also using their own people as bait to hunt Cold Beasts. Such degradation of human ethics and their heinous crimes cannot be tolerated by Great Xia while we are at Five Ape Mountain. The first grand gift I prepared today is to eradicate these villains for the people of Five Ape Mountain."

Crack...

As Xia Chuan’s words faded, a pitch-black knife light flashed from his hand like lightning. Before anyone could even react, the sound of a crack echoed in their ears, and the heads of Liu Erhu and Liu Sanhu were already separated from their bodies on the ground.
