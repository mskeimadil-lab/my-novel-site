Chapter 1005 - 363: Molten Fire Pill and Lengyan Divine Spell, The Battle Begins (Part 3)

Chapter 1005: Chapter 363: Molten Fire Pill and Lengyan Divine Spell, The Battle Begins (Part 3)

Buzzing......

The moment the first round of chanting was completed, it felt as if something above the large tent had awakened, emitting a mysterious humming sound.

Inside the tent, apart from two stewards and a dozen Cold Resistance Levels, all the people with Earth-digging Realm Cultivation started to blush, and their body temperatures began to rise rapidly, making the entire tent noticeably warmer.

"Namo Sadtatha Sugata Ye Arhat Samyak Sambuddha Shay..."

After completing the second round, not only their faces but even their skin turned red, streams of heat waves gushing out from everyone, making the campfire inside the tent blaze more fiercely.

"Namo Sadtatha Sugata Ye Arhat Samyak Sambuddha Shay..."

BOOM......

When the third round was completed, all those with Earth-digging Realm cultivation in the tent started to emit flames from their bodies, yet they remained with their eyes closed, gasping heavily, as if oblivious to their own bodies being set alight.

"No, no, this isn’t real fire, true fire would have burned them to death long ago, what is this? Is it the effect of that Fire Melting Pill just now, or the effect of three rounds of chanting..."

Xiang Liang, who secretly opened his eyes to see the whole transformation inside the tent, felt massive waves of shock rising in his heart. He had vaguely guessed why Dajue Temple wanted everyone to set off at midnight, aiming to reach Jinbi Village by dawn.

"Wang Chuan, take down the tent!"

Wang Yu’an called his father by name directly, and the Cold Resistance Levels from Lower River Village seemed to find nothing improper in it.

"Yes!"

Even Wang Chuan himself did not show any strange expression, and opened his eyes to lead the Cold Resistance Levels to immediately take down the black tent.

BOOM!

The moment the black tent was opened, a wave of scorching heat instantly poured out, directly counteracting the fierce cold rushing from the outside, and even the cold light in the sky seemed to be pushed back by the flames bursting from everyone’s bodies.

It wasn’t just the black tent from Lower River Village being opened; the other nineteen black tents were also being opened in succession. By now, it was already daylight, and everyone, over twenty thousand people with Earth-digging Realm, found themselves exposed under the cold light.

"I, I can’t feel the cold anymore!"

"The cold light shines on the skin, but it’s nothing."

"This is the supreme power of Yan Long Master, hahahaha..."

"I can finally appear under the cold light."

"But I’m just at Earth-digging Realm cultivation!"

"Thank Yan Long Master, thank Yan Long Master, hahahaha..."

......

Everyone opened their eyes, discovering they were completely exposed under the cold light. Initially, they felt a bit panic-stricken, but they soon realized that their bodies showed no change and the cold outside posed no threat to them.

Crowd cheers followed one after another. In the Ice Abyss World, appearing under the cold light was originally the privilege of Cold Resistance Levels, yet at this moment, even these over twenty thousand people with Earth-digging Realm had achieved it. Their excitement can be well imagined.

"Ah......"

"Ah!"

"Ah..."

However, very soon, a series of screams erupted from the crowd, abruptly halting the cheers. Everyone quickly turned to look at those who were screaming beside them.

Xiang Liang was no exception. He found that at least a hundred people in the place screamed. In the military tent he was in, five people screamed. His gaze swept over and he immediately noticed that none of the screaming people had flames on their bodies, and moments after their screams, they were frozen to death by the cold light.

"No need to panic, these are all spies from the Nine Towns mixed into our Qinghepu!"

The high-pitched call of Chief Bhikkhu Yan Xin came, and the originally restless crowd immediately quieted down. As they saw the appearance of those frozen to death, exclamations arose within the ranks.

"It’s them; they just came to our Upper River Village last year."

"Same here, the seven frozen to death came to Qinghe Village this year."

"Same here."

"These three as well!"

.........

All those frozen to death were people who had only come to Qinghepu in the past two years, which almost directly confirmed what Bhikkhu Yan Xin had just said.

These people who were frozen to death were indeed spies from the Nine Towns.

"Luckily... luckily I broke through before coming. If I hadn’t, I would have been among those screaming!"

Xiang Liang watched the frozen corpses, his pupils trembled violently, his back already soaked, and beside him, Bai Shanqiu looked at him, eyes also showing a sense of relief.

"Our Dajue Temple holds no grudge against Nine Towns, yet in recent years, Nine Towns either sends spies into every house in Qinghepu or arbitrarily abducts innocent villagers, or pries into the secrets of our Dajue Temple... truly a catalogue of evil deeds. Our masters of the temple, out of compassion for the human race of the Ice Abyss, have repeatedly tolerated them. Yet, not only have they not stopped, instead, they go further and further, with continued aggressions. On the 28th of last month, they even openly set fire in Qinghe Village, hurting countless people.

They push us too much, provoke the wrath of our masters — how can our Dajue Temple allow Nine Towns to continue their misdeeds?"

The people of Qinghepu were already fervently loyal to Dajue Temple, and upon hearing Chief Bhikkhu Yan Xin’s words, their emotions were immediately incited. Those with more extreme personalities were already full of killing intent, fiercely repeating the words ’Nine Towns’.

"Yan Long Master has bestowed divine power, granting you Cold-Resistant Divine Fire, the chief villain of Nine Towns is Jiangxia. Today we make Jiangxia repay blood with blood!"

When Chief Yan Xin uttered the last four words, his expression was extremely solemn, and his body suddenly burst into divine flames. Then he raised the Zen Stick in his hands high, pointed it towards Jinbi Village on the east side, and his lips lightly parted: "Kill!"

"Kill......"

"Let Jiangxia Town repay blood with blood!"
