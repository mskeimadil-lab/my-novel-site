Chapter 438: Chapter 205: Director Emerges from Seclusion, Treasure Offering Opportunity (2)

"There’s more people coming, fifteen hundred, no, nearly two thousand..."

..........

At this moment, what shocked the five to six thousand people from the four camps, including Flying Tiger, was not the dense arrow rain in front of them, but the massive force of Hunting Team members suddenly flooding in from all directions.

They originally thought that the things that had entered the camp and approached the wooden building were already the entirety of Great Xia’s power.

It was only at this moment that they truly realized that what they had just seen was merely the tip of Great Xia’s iceberg.

They even forgot about the flying beast in the sky, their gazes all focused on the surrounding Great Xia Hunting Team members, their faces blank, their expressions numb.

"More than two thousand, at least more than two thousand, how could there be so many in the Great Xia Earth-digging Realm..."

Xiang Liang looked around at the crowd, seeing that the numbers of Great Xia Earth-digging Realm were almost catching up to half of theirs, he only felt something blocking his throat, unable to utter a word for a long time.

In the crowd, his father Xiang Ping was already stunned, with only terror and numbness left in his heart.

The shock of the five to six thousand people like Xiang Liang can be set aside.

The flying beast in the sky was already almost buried by the arrow rain.

Although the fierce wind stirred by its wings was strong, it couldn’t withstand the iron arrows. There were simply too many, covering nearly its entire body.

Though it managed to swat away most of the iron arrows, many still embedded themselves in its feathers. It wasn’t clear if they penetrated its flesh, but judging from its slowed wing speed, the impact on it was considerable.

At this point, the flying beast should have taken off.

"Screeech..."

However, after letting out a sharper and angrier screech, it suddenly spread its wings and dived towards the main building from mid-air.

"Such audacity!"

"Dares to dive down."

"Spread out, keep firing, don’t let it get near the main building."

......

Luo Yuan and several Intermediate Hunting Team leaders, upon seeing the flying beast diving towards the wooden building, instantly became furious, first instructing everyone to continue shooting arrows, then dropping their longbows, drawing their weapons, and charging toward the wooden building.

After all, it’s a flying beast capable of aerial attacks; the best way to hurt it was still with bows. But since it was recklessly diving down, Luo Yuan and the others wouldn’t miss such an opportunity.

Moreover, how important the wooden building was.

Especially the top floor, which was where the leader was in seclusion cultivating.

They wouldn’t let this beast get close.

"Everyone, step back!"

But just as Luo Yuan and others approached the wooden building.

A sharp voice sounded from atop the building.

Whiz...

Then, an iron arrow suddenly pierced through the rooftop.

The sound of the iron arrow piercing through the air was crisp and ear-piercing, overshadowing the sound of other arrows being shot below.

Thud...

The iron arrow shot out in less than an instant.

The crisp sound of the arrowhead piercing flesh echoed into everyone’s ears below.

"Screech..."

The screech of the flying beast, barely ten meters away from the rooftop, lost all its previous ferocity, instead filled with pain.

It even carried a hint of fear and panic.

Flap, flap...

Its pupils contracted sharply as it looked at the main building. Just a moment later, it seemed to realize something and immediately flapped its wings frantically, hastily changing direction to flee.

But unfortunately, it was too close.

There was no more chance.

Whoosh...

A dark figure suddenly leaped high from the rooftop of the main building, instantly soaring more than ten meters into the air, positioning itself above the flying beast.

That person, dressed in form-fitting black attire, had a youthful face but incredibly sharp eyes, wielding a broad giant sword, slashing down at the flying beast’s head from above.

Whoosh...

The giant sword descended, not with a sword edge, but with an overpowering, crushing momentum like that of thunder, so terrifying that the surrounding snowflakes were suspended, seemingly bringing a momentary standstill to the entire space above the wooden building.

Bam...

The flying beast, eager to turn its body, had its head struck, blood splattering, its massive body staggering in mid-air, almost crashing down.

"Screech..."

The cry of the flying beast no longer had any ferocity, only panic and terror.

It frantically twisted its body, flapping its wings, and bolted towards the sky.

"Think you can escape so easily?!"

Since everyone below had already stopped shooting arrows, the cold laughter of the young man in black was exceptionally clear to everyone’s ears.

As everyone looked closer, they realized that as he swung the broad sword with his right hand, his left hand had already grasped the neck of the flying beast.

"Ah..."

With a shout, that person exerted his left hand, forcibly pulling the flapping flying beast back.

Not only did he yank it back, but he also dragged the flying beast down from the sky together with him.

Since the battleground was right above the main building, the man in black feared crashing onto the rooftop, so he adjusted the direction slightly, bringing the flying beast’s body directly to the front of the main building.

Bam... bam... bam...

Upon landing, the man in black dropped the broadsword, grabbed the flying beast’s legs with both hands, and began a frenzied assault.

Without any fancy swordplay or needing to draw a bow and arrow like Luo Yuan and the others, the young man in black simply held onto the legs, smashing the massive body of the flying beast onto the ground repeatedly, left and right.

"Just how terrifying is this person’s strength..."

"Could that flying beast not fly just now?"

"It wasn’t just unable to fly; it couldn’t even flap its wings."

"Is it already dead?"

"Gulp..."

......

The five thousand-plus people including the four families of Flying Tiger just watched as the flying beast completely lost its life in the hands of the young man in black, without even letting out a single wail of agony.
