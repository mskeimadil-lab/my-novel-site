Chapter 1442: Chapter 459: Prelude in the Hall, Progress Touching, Blood Cold Beast and Copper Bone Corpse Demon (Part 4)

However, as he relaxed, his curiosity grew stronger.

Such formidable military might, especially with the two families joining forces, yet the progress of expansion is still slow—why is that?

Just as Xia Hong’s mind was filled with curiosity, Fan Qinghu began speaking, pointing at the sand table.

"Currently, the Miasma Area we’ve cleared is about 400 square kilometers, starting from the northern end, with a depth of 20 kilometers and a width of 20 kilometers. Zhenyuan Camp is right in the middle of this area.

The Trial Zone for the Feudal Town’s elites this time will be marked by Zhenyuan Camp. The northern 200 square kilometers can be freely hunted; everyone must be warned not to cross the line of Zhenyuan Camp. Although most of the miasma in the southern half has been cleared, there are quite a few Blood Cold Beasts inside, at least a dozen hidden Beast Kings. Among their tribes, there are many Advanced Blood Cold Beasts. If one encounters them in scale, without army reinforcements, those youngsters face only certain death."

"Rest assured, Admiral, I will go back and tell them right away!"

Cai Yunlan immediately nodded solemnly in response.

"There’s no rush; it will be a while before they arrive. Also, although the intensity of the blood-colored miasma has decreased again this year, for safety purposes, we still need to give each of them 30 Heart Cleansing Pills, as the trial will last for two months. Calculating at two pills per day should be sufficient..."

Pausing here, Cai Yunlan continued, "This year’s trial is different from previous ones, with over a thousand elites participating. To prevent any sudden incidents and ensure safety, tomorrow I will dispatch fifty thousand Town Guard troops to the central point of the northern 200 square kilometer area to set up a camp. They are instructed to patrol each district daily. Before the trial officially begins, it’s crucial to inform everyone clearly: if any situation arises that cannot be handled, retreat to the camp if possible; if retreat is not possible, send a Command Arrow for reinforcements."

After all, these thousand people are the elite offspring of the entire Feudal Town, and Fan Qinghu’s arrangement is indeed reasonable. It shows that, while Cai Qiu’s training method for his descendants may be extreme, it’s far from cruel—at least he ensures their safety within his capabilities.

"According to Fan Qinghu, the northern 200 square kilometers area has been largely cleared, and the danger of miasma is not significant. What’s left, at most, are some Advanced Cold Beasts. Whether the 30% mortality rate last year is due to the overwhelming power of the Blood Cold Beasts..."

From the moment Fan Qinghu mentioned Blood Cold Beast, Xia Hong had already perked up his ears. Just on the way flying over with Cai Yunlan, he had witnessed the thin blood-colored miasma in the northern area of the camp, and Cai Yunlan had given him a rough introduction about Cai Qiu’s approach to resolving these miasmas.

The blood-colored miasma is actually not difficult to clear—there’s one method: use coal stone to burn silver, melting it into silver slurry isn’t enough; it must be burned further. The vaporized silver solution can gradually dilute them;

Of course, this is a highly efficient method. Another way is to directly burn silver as fuel, but this has a lower utilization rate, and the resulting silver vapor’s effect is not as strong, making dilution much slower.

"Indeed, not only does the system treat silver as a resource for fuel, the other humans in the Ice Abyss do as well. Vaporizing silver slurry already exceeds normal understanding—only within the Ice Abyss World can this be achieved!"

As Xia Hong sighed, Fan Qinghu had already arranged everything about the trial, starting to speak about the second issue:

"Let Yun Kai lead the team for the trial, and Yun Lan, you fourteen are here this time to collaborate with the Silver Scale Army, advancing south with me into the Blood Miasma Zone to deal with those Copper Bone Corpse Demons..."

At this point, Fan Qinghu glanced sideways at Xia Hong, continuing in a deep voice:

"The number of Copper Bone Corpse Demons on the outskirts is increasing, with various strange methods and growing power. Lord Landgrave should issue orders after the New Year for each county and city to continue deploying more Xianyang Level troops!"
