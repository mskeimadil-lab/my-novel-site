Chapter 224: Chapter 143: Picking Up a Bargain, Acting Maliciously [Three-in-One Mega Chapter]_4

The tacit understanding cultivated over a long time allowed everyone to immediately grasp Xia Hong’s intentions, and the fifteen people quickly scattered in all directions.

"Let’s go, let’s check it out!"

Seeing the others all dispersing, Xia Hong spoke, bringing along Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan, heading directly towards the source of the noise they had just heard.

Crunch... Boom... Whoosh... Whoosh...

"Keep shooting, don’t stop!"

"This beast has entered the Kui Marsh, and it’s out of strength. Quickly, shoot the arrows!"

"Roar..."

As they got closer, the sounds ahead gradually became clearer.

There were sounds of large trees breaking and collapsing, the rapid firing of arrows, commanding voices from humans, and the roaring of Cold Beasts...

Once the distance narrowed to about a hundred meters, everyone could clearly see the hunting scene of the group in front.

Underneath a giant Ice Tree, seven or eight people wielded huge axes, maneuvering around with a Teng Jiao, while arrows continuously fired from more than ten large trees around, aiming at the weak spots on the Teng Jiao’s body.

Behind the large tree, there lay a Golden Armored Thunder Kui, covered in wounds, motionless on the ground. Its body was nearly ten meters long, similar in size to the Golden Armored Thunder Kui they had seen last time.

"Is that... a Teng Jiao?"

"It’s a Teng Jiao, but it looks about sixty to seventy meters long; it should be Intermediate level!"

"No, over there, there’s another one!"

"That’s the Golden Armored Thunder Kui we saw last time; it’s already dead."

"These people, their strength is so strong, they just killed a Thunder Kui, and now they’re taking on a Teng Jiao?"

"Not just that, Thunder Kui, Teng Jiao, counting that Demon Sheep, these people have successively hunted three different types of Intermediate Cold Beasts."...

Amidst everyone’s discussion, Xia Hong did not feel much at first.

Only when he heard Xia Chuan’s last sentence did his expression become much more solemn.

After all, it is a giant camp, its underlying strength is much stronger than that of Great Xia.

Successfully hunting three different types of Intermediate Cold Beasts, this isn’t just about having strength; it also requires enough understanding of the habits, skills, and weaknesses of different Cold Beasts.

These valuable experiences must be obtained by predecessors risking their lives, making it one of the camp’s most precious assets, which can’t be gained just by hiding in the camp for training.

"Watch carefully, it’s a good chance to see where the weaknesses of the Teng Jiao are!"

Upon hearing Xia Hong’s words, Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan immediately realized that it was a rare learning opportunity, their eyes brightened slightly, and they hurriedly kept a close watch.

"Intermediate Teng Jiao doesn’t produce poisonous mist like the Lower-level Teng Jiao?"

"Impossible, this should be considered one of Teng Jiao’s special strengths; there’s no reason for it to disappear after evolution!"

Hearing the doubts from the two, Xia Hong’s brows furrowed slightly. However, after observing the battle scene at Mirror Immortal Camp for a while, he began to make some guesses.

"They don’t dare to touch the Teng Jiao’s body, and they’ve been deliberately or unintentionally directing the Teng Jiao towards that swamp."

After he finished speaking, Xia Hong carefully observed the Teng Jiao’s movements.

Just then, the Teng Jiao’s body happened to fall into the swamp. It opened its fangs, trying to use its tail to support itself, lifting its upper body to attack the few people behind it.

A magical scene unfolded.

The Teng Jiao, applying force with its tail on the swamp ground, began to sink deeper, and the harder it struggled, the faster it sank.

Within moments, more than ten meters of its tail became fully trapped in the swamp.

Moreover, once it was trapped, it couldn’t pull itself out...

"The Thunder Kui’s habitat can actually be used to hunt Teng Jiao!"

Seeing this, how could Xia Hong not understand? He couldn’t help but marvel, sharing the same expression as Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan beside him.

Of course, they were amazed but didn’t forget the reason they came all this way tonight, seeking out the people from Mirror Immortal Camp.

Xia Chuan clearly saw that the group from Mirror Immortal Camp, led by He Meng, was directing everyone.

Seeing the Teng Jiao’s body half-submerged in the swamp, he hastened to turn his head and quietly reminded Xia Hong:

"Big brother, it’s about time. If we don’t take action now, this Teng Jiao will die!"

Xia Hong had already scouted the nearby terrain. Seeing the Teng Jiao’s injuries worsening and realizing time was running out, he quickly turned his head and whispered a few words to Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan.

Upon hearing him, their eyes brightened slightly. They immediately took out several pre-prepared torches and ran separately to the left and right, positioning themselves to the north and south sides of He Meng’s group encircling Teng Jiao.

Xia Hong turned to scan his surroundings, selecting a large tree and climbing to a position about seventy to eighty meters high to look towards the left and right.

Soon, a torch flickered into light about a kilometer to the south.

Shortly after, another torch flickered about two kilometers to the north.

Though they were far away and the torchlight was faint, Xia Hong could clearly see the two torches moving rapidly towards the center.

Of course, the torches were secondary.

What mattered was, as the torches lit up on both sides, Xia Hong clearly saw huge creatures from the north and south rushing towards the torches.

Bang, bang, bang...

As those huge creatures drew nearer, the commotion grew louder and louder.

Without having yet killed the Teng Jiao, He Meng and his party’s expressions turned drastically as they looked hurriedly towards the north and south.

"Help!"

"Help me, help me..."

With the two flames rapidly approaching through the dense forest, frantic screams echoed from both the north and south.

Though He Meng and his party had not stopped their bow and arrow attacks, their faces were already a deathly shade of green.

"Why would someone come at this time!"

"And they’re being chased by Cold Beasts..."

"Disaster, disaster, those two damn bastards!"

"Get lost, all of you, don’t lead the Cold Beasts here, run somewhere else!"

Anxious and at a loss, He Meng even turned his head and roared at the people approaching from the north and south, trying to drive them away.

But his shouts were destined to be in vain.

Though Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan acted panicked, inside they were thrilled. As they closed to within a hundred meters of He Meng and his group, estimating that the Cold Beasts behind them were very near and about to catch up,

they both threw their torches towards them...
