Chapter 1238: Chapter 412: Outer Realm People, Demon Dragon’s Power, Confident Chu Yan’er, Hou Ming’s Skills_2

Bai Shanning immediately nodded and smiled upon hearing this, "From Hongmen City, no wonder you were just looking at Senior Mu, probably got here a bit earlier than us."

Since the decision to integrate into Moao South Slope Territory was made, Xia City naturally cannot remain closed forever. Hongmen City opens in July, and the Outer City of Daxia also opens around that time, simultaneously starting to unfold to the outside world.

Now, anyone who finds the Ministry Building in Hongmen City, states their purpose as usual, and pays a small fee per person, can freely come to Xia City for a visit.

This opening was initially only for Eight Towns people, but later, as Great Xia’s reputation widened, people from various remote camps outside of Eight Towns also gradually started coming over.

After hearing Xiang Liang explain the content of those six people’s travel guide, Bai Shanning smiled, "Extreme North Outer Domain, that’s even farther north than Beishuo Town; at least a thousand kilometers away, it’s really not easy for these folks..."

Seven people, four at Cold Resistance Level, three at Earth-digging Realm; Bai Shanning has a strength of 15 Zhous already recognized that the four middle-aged ones have strengths between 8 to 10 Zhous. With such little strength, traveling a thousand kilometers and crossing Eight Towns’ territory just to see Xia City is truly challenging.

But after pausing, Bai Shanning changed his tone with a smile, "But, it’s good now that they’re in Xia City. These seven people will likely be like the others, not want to leave after visiting, and eventually, choose to join our Great Xia as well."

Xiang Liang and the other three immediately nodded with a touch of pride on their faces.

Given the current splendor of Xia City, not only people from these small Outer Realm places, even those from the Eight Towns with Cold Resistance Level, can’t help but linger. In fact, since July, people have continuously chosen to stay after visiting Xia City.

Based on Xiang Liang’s observation of the attire and temperament of those six, once they enter Xia City for a tour, they’ll surely choose to stay.

"Alright, Brother Mu, it’s about time; let’s continue our journey!"

The seven were only taking a break here; as Xiang Fuhai spoke up, Mu Longhe and Xiang Liang, along with others, immediately got up, ready to head further west.

Meanwhile, at another table, six people watched them leave. After about ten more minutes, their expressions finally completely relaxed.

Chu Dongning exhaled gently, intending to speak, when suddenly footsteps were heard again from behind on the staircase.

The six turned and immediately stood up in unison.

Chu Yan’er, dressed in simple green attire, slowly descended from the stairs with an unusually solemn face. Upon closer inspection, one would find that her long black hair was slightly shimmering with water light.

Chen Yimou immediately whispered, "Miss, did you find anything?"

Chu Yan’er raised her head; a trace of horror flashed in her pupils as she solemnly said, "The message sent back by Chen Yuanbai and the three lords four months ago, it is likely inaccurate!"

Chen Yuanbai, Huang Yingqing, and Li Jiuhe were the three Xianyang Levels sent by Chen Cang this April to support the Northern Three Towns against Dajue Temple.

In May, the three returned to Chen Cang and naturally reported the situation with Dajue Temple in detail to the Feudal Town’s central command. As Commandery Princess Chu Yan’er, she naturally has the right to know this information.

And Chen Yimou happens to know about this as well.

The most important message Chen Yuanbai brought back should be about the Red Blood Demon Dragon at Qinghepu.

The seven of them left Hongmen City last night, and should have arrived in Xia City long ago. The reason they stayed at Qinghepu was because of this matter.

Due to Chu Yan’er’s actual strength being stronger, and having more methods than the other six, they couldn’t argue with her and had to follow her lead.

Chu Yan’er went alone to investigate the situation at Chilong Lake with the dawn light and hasn’t returned yet. When Xiang Liang questioned the six earlier, Chu Yan’er wasn’t upstairs. Thankfully, Chu Dongning acted quickly and dispelled Xiang Liang’s concerns, otherwise, if Xiang Liang truly suspected them, they wouldn’t know what to do.

The message brought back by Chen Yuanbai and the three is inaccurate...

Upon hearing Chu Yan’er’s words, Chen Yimou looked puzzled.

Chen Yuanbai is Chen Yimou’s uncle, so he knows a bit about the Red Blood Demon Dragon. According to his uncle’s message from four months ago, the Red Blood Demon Dragon’s power was terrifying, and fifteen Xianyang Level folks couldn’t gain an upper hand on it, nearly affirming it as a Five Luminaries Realm Cold Beast, or in chen cang’s common terms, an Emperor Level Fierce Beast.

"Miss, you mean that the Demon Dragon surpasses the Five Luminaries Realm?"

"Five Luminaries Realm?"

Chu Yan’er hesitated upon hearing this, then realized that Chen Yimou had only heard part of the news, shook her head solemnly, "It can speak in human tongue; influences heavenly phenomena with just its flesh; fifteen Xianyang Levels can’t harm it collectively; senses danger and immediately breaks free from deceitful control during its slumber, how can that Red Blood Demon Dragon be just at Five Luminaries Realm?"

Chen Yimou’s expression instantly froze upon hearing her, pupils filled with horror.

And naturally, the remaining five including Chu Dongning reacted even more intensely.

Five Luminaries Realm, Beast Emperor Level, is already something beyond their reach.

Going further up...

"I spent three hours under the lake sketching the dragon’s form; when I got to the eyes, the brush wouldn’t make a mark, something that’s never happened before with my brushwork, that Demon Dragon is at least Heavenly Phenomenon Realm, or there’s another possibility..."
