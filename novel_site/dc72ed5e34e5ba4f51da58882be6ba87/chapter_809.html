Chapter 809: Chapter 318: Transformed Li Xuanling, Reform, 9th-Class Viscount (Part 2)

At this moment, tasting it herself, she finally realized just how miraculous this thing was.

It wasn’t just about the taste. After eating, her body felt very comfortable, and it had a very noticeable effect on clearing the blood and qi pathways in her body. This meant that eating it could enhance cultivation speed.

Green Saliva Braised White Bear Paw, Cheng Guo’s Roasted Lamb Ribs, Crystal Branch Stewed Kui Ribs, Fragrant Cold Fruit Tiger Soup—after trying these four dishes, Li Xuanling didn’t have a moment’s rest for her mouth. In the end, it was Han Yue who actively poured a glass of Blood Cold Wine that finally pulled her attention away from the food.

Li Xuanling picked up the glass and drank it all in one go. Her face instantly turned red, and a dizzying sensation surged into her mind. She closed her eyes, savoring it for more than ten breaths before slowly opening them, her face full of excitement and exhilaration.

This wine, it’s not only delicious but also able to enhance cultivation?

"This is Blood Cold Wine. The Lord specifically asked me to prepare five pounds for the Madam. In the future, if the Madam wants to drink, I can fetch it anytime."

The psychological impact Li Xuanling received from this evening meal was not in the least inferior to when she first saw the sights of Xia City.

Until Han Yue and others cleared everything up and prepared to leave, she still hadn’t come back to her senses.

"Madam, Han Yue still has to serve by the leader’s side. Han Shuang, Nong Ying, Qiu Ru, Ming Yue, and Hua Ling, these five will be dedicated to serving you; Han Shuang is the maid deputy. If the Madam has anything, you can ask her to find me."

Han Yue left five maids behind; among them, the maid deputy called Han Shuang, who resembled her by seven points, should be her sister.

Li Xuanling nodded, sent Han Yue off, and then lay down on the bed with the help of the five maids, preparing to rest.

It was actually soon to be dawn. Technically, Cold Resistance Level individuals should sleep at night, but if not going out for hunting, sleeping during the day wasn’t a problem.

She had already eaten a lot and drank a pound of Blood Cold Wine, so she was naturally dazed and fell asleep as soon as she hit the bed.

But soon, she was startled awake by a large hand reaching under the covers.

"Who dares..."

Li Xuanling, awakened, instinctively lashed out, but before she finished speaking, she realized who it was and immediately shut her mouth.

Then came a flurry of rustling sounds from inside the room.

After a long while, the movements inside the room gradually subsided.

"Weren’t you unwilling before, changed your mind?"

"Get out!"

The two maids on duty outside the door were already blushing with embarrassment.

.........

Second Year of Great Xia, the first day of October

As soon as night fell, the inner city became exceptionally lively.

Normally, the nighttime was when the camp was engaged in hunting and production, so liveliness was normal, but tonight’s situation was obviously unusual.

Many Cold Resistance Levels were heading toward the main building, and a large number of Earth-digging Realm seemed to have received some news in advance, gathering beneath the main building in lively groups, chatting animatedly.

"In the Hive Guard Battle, I killed an enemy, which counts as a small merit."

"Killing an enemy is not a small merit! I’ve found out, the ones attacking the hive were the Longyou Army, all with cultivation above the Earth-digging Realm Limit, so being able to kill one is definitely a significant merit!"

"Indeed, most of those able to kill enemies at that time were at the Cold Resistance Level. Among us, not many could achieve a kill."

"I injured two with arrows, and the Camp Supply Department has already confirmed with me."

"Same here, injured one person."

"Tonight’s highlight should be those Cold Resistance Level leaders, their enemy kill counts are high, and the Lord will definitely reward a lot of Contribution Value."

"Contribution Value is nothing; it’s the ennoblement that’s the real highlight."

"Ennoblement, what’s that?"

"I heard from the Camp Supply Department that the Lord has established a Ninth Rank title system specifically to reward those outstanding in military achievements."

"I know, I heard that those with the title in the future will have a different status and special treatment, though it’s unclear what exactly that entails."

"A different status, how different can it be? I say, extra Contribution Value would be more practical!"

"With so many people in the camp now, Contribution Value is increasingly hard to earn. Every night, hundreds of hunting teams go to Redwood Ridge. Hell, if you go late, there aren’t even trees to cut, let alone low-level Cold Beasts which are fought over fiercely."

"I know, last month some teams got into fights over prey, almost to the death, and Yuwen Sizheng personally had to step in."

"Indeed, Contribution Value is hard to earn now, and if the title isn’t useful, more Contribution Value would be better."

"Let’s wait and see what’s going on, maybe it will be useful!"

Tap tap tap......

The chatter underneath the main building was interrupted by the sound of a marching army.

Everyone turned their heads toward the outer city to see a large number of soldiers clad in light gold battle armor slowly walking this way.

"It’s the Cloud Dragon Army, the Cloud Dragon Army is here too."

"No kidding, capturing Longyou was mainly due to the Cloud Dragon Army; they’re bound to receive rewards as deserved."

"Looks like there’s over a thousand. Wasn’t it said that over three hundred Cloud Dragon Army soldiers died in Longyou?"

"With so many new arrivals to the camp lately, they’ve already been replenished."

"Leading them is Captain Liu Yuan; the Minister isn’t here."

"The Minister is definitely already at the Political Hall."

"When they attacked Long Mountain, Captain Liu was there too. His contributions are probably significant."

"Not just Long Mountain; I heard he was at Changbai Gully, Ning Yuan Hill, and when we defended the hive. He was there when the leader led the army to take White Abyss and Xiling. Few could compare to his achievements in capturing Longyou."
