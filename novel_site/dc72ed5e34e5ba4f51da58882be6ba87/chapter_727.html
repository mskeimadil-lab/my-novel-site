Chapter 727: Chapter 298: Hou Jing Defects, White Abyss Falls, Zhaoyang’s Banner Changes (3)

A flagpole seven to eight meters high was positioned right in the center of the iron gate, with a black banner fluttering in the wind. The character "Xia" on the front, together with the golden Cloud Dragon pattern on the back, suddenly caught everyone’s eyes.

"Scout Camp, charge up the stone platform. Mo Blade Camp, keep advancing. Horizontal Blade Camp, last ten breaths to shoot arrows, abandon the bows after ten breaths, and rush into the iron gate to protect the banner!"

The moment Yuwen Dao planted the banner behind the iron gate, Liu Yuan’s shout echoed from the stone platform.

"Kill!"

"Charge..."

"The iron gate has fallen, brothers, charge in."

......

As the shouts fell, the front line of three hundred soldiers wielding Mo Blades seemed as if they had taken a stimulant; they each let out a roar of excitement, raising their Mo Blades high and charging madly towards the iron gate.

The six units of Horizontal Blade Camp soldiers at the back were not to be outdone. Within ten breaths, they quickly emptied their arrow quivers, then immediately discarded their bows and drew their horizontal blades, surging towards the iron gate like a tide.

The first soldier from the Mo Blade Camp finally charged into the iron gate, only to be met by more than a dozen sharp blades.

The people of White Abyss were no fools; given the importance of the iron gate, over a hundred guards were stationed at the back, all with cultivation at the Earth-digging Realm Limit. Their weapons and iron armor were also at the Hundred Forged Level.

Slash......

More than a dozen sharp Hundred-forged Great Blades hacked at his body, tearing several gaps in the soldier’s iron armor. The immense force pressing down on him caused him to collapse to the ground; if not for someone supporting him from behind, he would have knelt down and been unable to get back up.

"Only a few gaps, how is this possible?"

"His weapon didn’t break either, this..."

"How can his blade be so sturdy?"

"Aren’t all of them Hundred Forged Level?"

......

Though they nearly brought the soldier to his knees, not one of White Abyss’s side showed a smile. Instead, their pupils trembled violently, their faces aghast.

With similar cultivation and equally equipped Hundred Forged Level weapons, the iron armor of the Great Xia soldier only had a few breaches after being attacked by over a dozen people.

How is this possible......

"Though all are Hundred Forged Level, the Thunder Kui Iron Armor I wear is not the same as yours!"

The Mo Blade Camp soldier sneered, then suddenly lifted his head, grasped his Mo Blade and swept it horizontally in front of him, immediately shouting and charging into the iron gate.

"Kill!"

"Kill......"

Once the first broke through, the second soon followed. The front line of three hundred soldiers wielded large Mo Blades; once one soldier broke through, he could sweep away a large group. As long as they maintained the position for three or four breaths, more Mo Blades would immediately follow.

Thus, the defeat of the iron gate began!

With the swarming charge of Mo Blade Camp soldiers inside, in less than a few minutes, they had created a space more than a hundred meters wide behind the iron gate.

With no further obstruction, the six units of Horizontal Blade Camp soldiers also surged in like a tide.

Slash......

Liu Yuan, leading over a hundred Scout Camp elites, had already cleared the stone platform above the iron gate, scattering the Earth-digging Realm crowd attempting to close the gate. Seeing that the soldiers of the Nine Camps below had charged in, he had no more reservations and directly led the Scout Camp charging down.

"The main gate is breached, people of White Abyss, listen clearly, surrender and you will not be killed!"

Liu Yuan, after all, had cultivation at the Cold Resistance Level. With a single forward charge, he could sweep away a large number of people; in less than four or five breaths, he reached the side of those retreating with Earth-digging Realm Limit cultivation, wielding a great blade to relentlessly harvest their lives while also shouting at them to surrender.

"Surrender and you will not be killed!"

"Surrender and you will not be killed!"

......

Numerous elite members of the Scout Camp followed him, shouting "Surrender and you will not be killed," continuously attacking the psychological defenses of the White Abyss people.

"Where’s the leader?"

"Where did the second leader go?"

"They’re all in the back, didn’t you see? The main building has already collapsed."

......

Those of White Abyss still resisting, in their despair, pinned their hopes on their leader, Bai Qiucheng. But with a deliberate reminder, they realized the top floor of the main building in the center of the camp had already collapsed.

On the collapsed structure, there were six figures fighting, among which two were being pushed back, none other than the second leader, Bai Qiuping, and the third leader, Wu Ningyuan. Only Big Leader Bai Qiucheng seemed to have a slight advantage.

But the trouble was, the black-clothed man who had just placed the banner on the stone platform was now charging towards the main building where Bai Qiucheng was, with a broad sword in hand.

That meant Great Xia would soon have four against three.

The camp was broken, and Cold Resistance Level was also completely defeated.

White Abyss was finished!

Clang......

An unknown person was the first to drop their weapon, followed soon by a second and a third. Except for a few die-hard loyalists still hoping for a turn of events on the main building battlefield, more and more people began laying down their weapons and kneeling to the Cloud Dragon Army soldiers in surrender.

"Bai Qiucheng, surrender. The leader hasn’t personally taken action, which is giving you a chance. Stubborn refusal will only lead to death in the end."

"White Abyss has no enmity with Great Xia. Why attack my camp?"

"Stop the nonsense, Bai Qiucheng. If you wish to merge with Long Mountain, you have to accept your fate. I ask you again, surrender or not?"

"Peng Bo, you’re dreaming, Long Mountain will eventually destroy Great Xia. Qiuping, Ningyuan, come with me..."

Before Bai Qiucheng’s words settled, and before Yuwen Dao could approach, two figures from the main building seized the chance to quickly flee towards the west, in the direction of Long Mountain.

Only the person fighting Chen Yingba hesitated for a moment before gritting his teeth and putting down his weapon.
