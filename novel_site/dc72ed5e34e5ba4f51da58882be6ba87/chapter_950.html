Chapter 950 - 351: Lu Yang’s Plan, Upheaval in Xia City, Sumeru Nightmare, His Highness’s Golden Words (Part 4)

Chapter 950: Chapter 351: Lu Yang’s Plan, Upheaval in Xia City, Sumeru Nightmare, His Highness’s Golden Words (Part 4)

Roar......

The Beast King Level Demon Sheep is different from the High-level Demon Sheep. Its entire body is covered with tens of thousands of eyeballs, and the golden light shooting out from them leaves almost no blind spots, carrying an indestructible sharpness;

The Beast King Level Snow Mane is similarly formidable. Its hundreds of icy long tongues carve cold edges in the air, trapping Xia Hong outside, preventing him from entering, and constantly looking for a blind spot to attack Xia Hong;

The remaining Frost Electric Ape, Striped Tiger, Blue Moon Elf, Cloud Mist Teng Jiao, Golden Armored Thunder Kui... these Beast King Level Fierce Beasts, all display their special skills, entangling Xia Hong, oppressing him so he cannot get near that Fire Stone Tiger.

"Wah... Wah...... Daddy... Save me..."

Xia Yuyao, who had cried until she was almost unconscious, finally saw her father approaching, and her cries became even more shrill and painful.

Her desperate cries made Xia Hong, in the center of the beast tide, even more heartbroken.

Crack...

"Wah......"

The Fire Stone Tiger opened its mouth and bit off Xia Yuyao’s two legs.

"Ah!!!"

Xia Hong’s expression became almost maniacal, the towering phantom behind him swayed, clearly showing great mental turmoil, but he was helpless as there were too many Beast Kings around him.

He couldn’t move forward, even if he was willing to risk injury.

"Xuanling, Xia Chuan, come and help!"

Seeing that his eldest daughter was about to die by the tiger’s mouth, Xia Hong finally couldn’t help but call out to Li Xuanling and Xia Chuan behind him.

Li Xuanling’s strength is second only to his own, and Xia Chuan is almost the strongest among the remaining people. He had no other solution but to call them.

Hearing his call, the two rushed towards Xia Hong, as the Beast Kings were all focused on Xia Hong, allowing the two to approach quickly.

"Brother, give up, Yu Yao can’t be saved."

"Husband, I..."

Unlike Xia Chuan’s calmness, Li Xuanling was clearly more conflicted. On one hand, she knew her eldest daughter could not be saved, but as a mother, how could she possibly admit to giving up her daughter?

So she could only silently shed tears, looking at Xia Hong, her face full of guilt.

"How did this beast tide come about?"

Xia Chuan was stunned, apparently not expecting Xia Hong to ask about the cause of the beast tide at such a critical moment, but quickly began to answer: "It was..."

Thwack!

Before Xia Chuan could finish a word, Xia Hong’s Dragon Sparrow Saber had already pierced his brow, his face full of shock and disbelief yet to fade when Xia Hong withdrew the saber and decapitated Li Xuanling.

Crack!

"Brother, you..."

"Husband."

Swoosh...

"Still pretending, you’ve been exposed, stop acting!"

Xia Hong’s face was full of hysteria, the madness instantly fading after killing Xia Chuan and Li Xuanling in front of him, accompanied by the sizzling flames at their brow and neck, Xia Hong laughed bitterly, his face full of mockery.

Boom......

"Ah ah... How did you find out, how is it possible?"

As the flames exploded, the bodies of Li Xuanling and Xia Chuan suddenly dispersed into a ball of black clouds, a voice of extreme pain and howling came from the black smoke, followed by incredulous questioning.

Xia Hong sneered twice, taking out a silver-black jade from his bosom, the jade emitting a strong high temperature, its color nearly crimson.

"Discovered long ago, just noticed something peeking at me from the cliffside. With this Suppression Talisman responding so intensely, I guessed you must be hiding in the clouds, waiting to attack as I descended the mountain. Unexpectedly, you acted so excessively."

Swoosh......

When Xia Hong finished speaking, his left eye flashed faintly, and the scene of Xia City, originally filled with rolling smoke, instantly shattered piece by piece like broken glass. The bodies on the ground, the entire city, including all creatures within the city, turned into thick white mist.

To be precise, it should be white clouds.

Within the entire scene, the only things unchanged were the Cold Beasts attacking him and the black cloud emitting the voice from the sky.

Of course, the Fire Stone Tiger at the back and Xia Yuyao clamped in its mouth also disappeared.

"Kekekeke...... Even if you see through it, what can you do? Having fallen into my Sumeru Space, you’ll never escape. So many Cold Beasts, I don’t believe you can kill them all alone, kekekeke..."

Roar......

Hundreds of Beast King Level Cold Beasts, accompanied by the deceitful monster’s sinister laughter from the black cloud, roared terrifyingly as they charged madly towards Xia Hong.

Xia Hong’s complexion changed slightly, although he spoke lightly, the life-and-death battle against the Cold Beasts was not fake. When he saw the billowing smoke over Xia City from the sky, he realized the entire scene was a deceitful monster’s fabrication.

The problem is, even with the Eye of Truth, he couldn’t find the exit to escape this space, so he had no choice but to play along with the deceitful monster.

In his eyes, everyone was just a ball of white mist, only Li Xuanling and Xia Chuan were formed from black smoke, immediately realizing the problem lay with them. So he deliberately acted hysterically, then borrowed the deceitful monster’s transformation of the two to aid him, finally striking abruptly, attempting to use the Dragon Sparrow Saber smeared with Solidified Fire Oil to slay this deceitful monster in one blow.

But clearly, he underestimated this deceitful monster.

Solidified Fire Oil could hurt it, but not kill it!

"I can’t possibly kill all these Cold Beasts alone. Ultimately, it’s either being killed or dying from exhaustion, the Suppression Talisman is ineffective, the Yangxu Tripod is absent, Solidified Fire Oil can only wound and not kill, I can only rely on the Eye of Truth..."
