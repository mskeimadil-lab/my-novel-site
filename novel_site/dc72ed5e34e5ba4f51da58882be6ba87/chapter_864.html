After speaking, Xia Hong hurriedly walked towards the tunnel exit, clearly not as calm inside as he appeared to be.

Behind him, Xia Chuan and the other two were all smiles and quickly hurried after him.

......

"Ah... it hurts, it hurts... it hurts so much..."

"Madam, don’t panic, take deep breaths slowly, it will be out soon."

"Quick, get another basin of hot water."

"I can’t, ah... it hurts so much, so much..."

...

In Xia City’s Star-picking Hall, Li Xuanling’s exceptionally shrill screams had been going on for a long time, audible even from outside the Political Hall more than a hundred meters away.

Outside the Political Hall, nearly three hundred people had gathered by now, almost everyone at the Cold Resistance Level in the camp was there.

Everyone looked up at the Star-picking Hall, faces filled with worry and anticipation.

"How could it be taking so long? It’s been half an hour!"

"It’s obviously not normal! Didn’t you feel that the temperature over at the Star-picking Hall is much lower than usual?"

"I felt it. I just heard from a maid over there that the entire Star-picking Hall is filled with transparent mist, even the Cold Ruins Tripod is useless. Seven midwives have come, taking turns just to withstand that cold!"

"Wasn’t it said that the Jade Dew Stone had already been refined by the madam?"

"The madam is at the Cold Resistance Level in cultivation, and she’s in so much pain, this..."

"The two princes have to be born safely!"

"They will be, let’s wait a bit more."

......

At the front of the crowd, Li Tiancheng, Meng Ying, Zhou Yuan, and a group of Long Mountain people all wore worried expressions. If not for the strict rules of Great Xia, they would have rushed into the Political Hall and across the iron bridge to the Star-picking Hall.

The Ice Abyss people have strong constitutions, women included. Even an ordinary woman giving birth normally takes no more than half an hour, and the higher the cultivation level, like in the Logging Realm or Earth-digging Realm, the shorter and easier the process.

It’s understandable: the stronger the constitution, the stronger the body’s functions, and naturally, they can endure more pain.

For Cold Resistance Level women, not to mention the Earth-digging Realm Limit, the process of reshaping the skin with Beast Blood is hundreds of times more painful than childbirth. This level of pain during childbirth is nothing to them.

Xia Chuan’s two wives, Xiao Ning and Xiao Yu, both had childbirth processes that lasted less than five minutes and made no screams, which is normal.

Li Xuanling’s current situation is clearly abnormal.

Everyone immediately associated it with last year’s Yanglu Realm incident, and it was almost certain that Li Xuanling’s current anomaly was caused by that Jade Dew Stone.

Whoosh......

Full of anxiety, a figure suddenly rushed up from downstairs, barging into the Political Hall, heading straight for the Star-picking Hall.

"The lord is back."

"It’s the lord!"

...

"Everyone be quiet, no noise! Wait for the news in the Political Hall!"

The crowd had just started to murmur, and was quickly silenced by Xia Chuan who came up behind them. He gestured for everyone to quiet down and wait for news in the Political Hall, while he followed Xia Hong toward the Star-picking Hall.

......

"What’s happening?"

From a distance, Xia Hong could hear Li Xuanling’s shrill screams, and his face grew concerned. As he approached the Star-picking Hall and found the temperature of the entire hall exceptionally low, he furrowed his brow and quickly asked Han Yue by the room door about the situation.

Han Yue looked very panicked and spoke quickly, without time for formalities:

"Report to the Lord, half an hour ago, the temperature in Star-picking Hall fluctuated, and everywhere in Madam’s room - the bedside, floor, table, windowsill - condensed with this water-blue liquid. We heard Madam’s screams and immediately went to inform the midwives."

After the room temperature completely dropped, Madam’s condition somewhat stabilized. The midwives only have Earth-digging Realm cultivation, they couldn’t withstand it, so we’ve already rotated seven midwives to help with the delivery."

As Han Yue spoke, she took a small package of water-blue liquid from her bosom and handed it to Xia Hong.

"Is this... Cold Apple Jade Dew?"

Just then, Xia Chuan came from the Political Hall, and upon seeing what Han Yue handed to Xia Hong, his expression froze for a moment, then he reacted, and his body trembled. He asked Han Yue: "You just said that Madam’s room is filled with this liquid, how much have you collected?"

Although Han Yue was only at the Earth-digging Realm cultivation, she had long served Xia Hong and often moved around the Camp Supply Department. Even if she had never seen Cold Apple Jade Dew with her own eyes, she had heard about it, knowing it was an extremely precious alchemy material.

Upon hearing Xia Chuan’s question, she realized something, her expression momentarily dazed, then nodded and softly said, "We’ve collected at least a thousand drops, and Madam’s room is still continuously producing more."

"This..."

Xia Chuan’s expression froze, then he turned to look at Xia Hong, both with eyes of astonishment mixed with deep concern.

"The Jade Dew Stone was certainly refined by Xuanling, she wouldn’t lie, chances are the refined Jade Dew Stone has some impact on the child, dystocia being one part of it? To even counter a Level 4 Cold Ruins Tripod, the impact is too large!"

Xia Hong forced himself to stay calm, then handed the Cold Apple Jade Dew to Xia Chuan, starting to pace to relieve his mood, yet his frequent glances into the room revealed his inner turmoil.

"Brother, rest assured, nothing will go wrong!"

Xia Chuan, unable to help, could only offer comforting words.

"Hm? The temperature is rising!"

After waiting more than a hundred breaths, Li Xuanling’s voice inside the room suddenly softened a bit. At the same time, Xia Hong clearly noticed the room’s temperature rapidly returning to normal.
