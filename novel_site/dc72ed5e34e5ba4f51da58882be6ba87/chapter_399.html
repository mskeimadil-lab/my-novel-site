Chapter 399 - 194: A Tragic Scene, Ice Skin Jade Bone, Xia Hong’s True Strength

Chapter 399: Chapter 194: A Tragic Scene, Ice Skin Jade Bone, Xia Hong’s True Strength

Is this tiny thing in front of me a high-level Cold Beast?

Not to mention the more than four hundred people below, even Yue Feng, standing beside Yuwen Dao, was stunned at the moment.

"Are you all deaf? Run!"

Yet, Yue Feng was the quickest to react.

Realizing that Yuwen Dao had no reason to deceive them, Yue Feng swiftly drew the Iron Core Bow from behind and, while nocking an arrow, shouted loudly at the people below once more.

The more than four hundred people below finally reacted at this moment.

"Run!"

"Run quickly, scatter!"

Breaking through windows, climbing over walls...

The people on the first floor scattered rapidly, but with over four hundred individuals, it wasn’t that simple to all escape the wood cabin at once.

"Huff..."

The Snow Mane, no longer than one meter, snorted again at this moment.

Its crystalline green eyes, as translucent as jade, gently scanned the scattering crowd, a human-like cruel grin appearing on its face.

The next moment, it lightly shook its body.

Rustling...

A rustling sound rang out as the spines, barely dozens of centimeters long on the Snow Mane’s back, shot out from its body surface like steel needles, moving so fast they sparked against the air.

Whoosh whoosh whoosh...

Hundreds of spines burst out like a torrential rain of pear blossoms, illuminating the originally pitch-dark wood cabin with flashes ignited by the spines, creating an eerie yet terrifyingly beautiful scene.

Thunk... Thunk... Whoosh...

After the flashes, countless sounds of spines piercing human flesh echoed.

"Ah..."

"Ah!"

"Run quickly, those steel spines are unavoidable!"

"Ah, run quickly!"

...

Relentless screams eventually echoed throughout the entire wood cabin.

Blood splattered, broken limbs and blood intermixed with the continuous screams, turning what was a refuge into a hell on earth in an instant.

The spines shot by the Snow Mane were incredibly powerful and fast, not stopping at a single hit, but piercing directly through bodies. If the person hit was unlucky enough to have someone in front, they too would be penetrated.

Even after piercing through bodies, the spines’ force didn’t diminish, shooting through the walls and flying outside the wood cabin.

Instantly, the walls of the entire cabin were riddled with holes.

Among the more than four hundred people here tonight, aside from members of the three Hunting Teams and a few Earth-digging Realm miners, the majority were Logging Realm personnel from the gathering department.

Even the Earth-digging Realm couldn’t resist the spines fired by the Snow Mane, let alone these frail Logging Realm individuals.

"Break through the walls and escape, don’t look back!"

Yuwen Dao, his hands never stopping shooting arrows, still managed to remind everyone to break through the walls and escape. But now his voice was filled with panic, even a hint of desperation.

From the Snow Mane shaking its body until now, it had only been about five to six breaths.

In such a short time, at least one hundred people were lying on the ground.

It’s not clear how many are dead for the moment, but the problem is, after the spines shot out from the Snow Mane’s back, they quickly grew back.

This new batch of spines looked even sharper and denser than before.

What’s more deadly is, neither he, Yue Feng, Hong Guang, nor Meng Yi could affect the Snow Mane with their iron arrows.

Clang... Clang...

Keep in mind, among the four, except Yue Feng’s basic strength was slightly weaker, just over twenty thousand pounds, the other three had exceeded the Earth-digging Realm’s limit, with Yuwen Dao reaching over fifty thousand.

Even the iron arrows shot by Yuwen Dao couldn’t scratch its skin, let alone the others from Hong Guang and the rest.

"This Ten-stone Iron Bow made with Bamboo Silk and Teng Jiao Tendon, with a pull force of up to thirty thousand pounds, can’t even pierce its outer skin..."

Yuwen Dao’s voice trembled slightly, and in this short time, the four of them had almost run out of iron arrows.

Yet the Snow Mane hadn’t even moved its body an inch.

Not only did it not move, but the Snow Mane even raised its head, looking in their direction on the second floor where the four stood.

Perhaps it was the bloody scent permeating the wood cabin; the Snow Mane looked at the four, eyes full of violence, with an extremely evident sense of enjoyment and greed.

Rustling...

The Snow Mane shook its body again.

But unlike before, this time it clearly shook upwards.

"Scatter!"

Yuwen Dao shouted sharply, even dropping his bow and arrows to flee quickly backward. Hong Guang, Meng Yi, and Yue Feng reacted to his voice, each running in a different direction, scattering swiftly.

Whoosh whoosh whoosh...

A wider and more numerous round of spines shot abruptly from the Snow Mane’s back in all directions of the wood cabin.

After the first round, less than ten breaths had passed, and everything happened too quickly. By the time the second round of spines shot out, less than a tenth of the more than four hundred people had truly escaped the cabin.

In the first round, over a hundred had already fallen. With the new round of spines attacking, faces of those in the rear filled instantly with despair.

A few even gave up escaping, closing their eyes to await death.

This time, neither Yuwen Dao nor Yue Feng had the mind or energy to remind others, for they were struggling to save themselves.

Bang...

Yuwen Dao broke through a window with his head, jumping straight out. Looking back and seeing Hong Guang and Meng Yi already out on the other side, a happy glint appeared in his eyes, but as he turned to Yue Feng, his pupils tightened.

Yue Feng was too slow.

Not only was he slow, but his movements were evidently slower than the other three, failing to avoid the spines shot by the Snow Mane. Both his shoulder and left calf were pierced, and he had already fallen by the windowsill on the west side of the second floor.
