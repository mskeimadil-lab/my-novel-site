Chapter 551: Chapter 239: Shifting Situation, Great Xia Special Envoy (Part 4)

Is there a young Cold Resistance Level?

Of course there is, Li Xuanling is one.

Li Tianhua has four children, the youngest, Li Xuanling, is only 22, making him the youngest known Cold Resistance Level on the Longyou Land.

The issue is that in the entirety of Longyou, there’s only one Li Xuanling.

And at this moment, standing before them is this young man.

Judging by appearance, he’s also in his early twenties.

"At the very least, he wouldn’t be over twenty-five. He must be a child of some big figure from Great Xia, could he be the leader’s son?"

Li Xuandu exchanged subtle glances with a few people beside him, his expression slightly cold.

The surrounding people clearly understood his thoughts, and the gaze directed at the young man in black also became colder.

"May I ask, are you the young lord of Long Mountain, Li Xuanling in person?"

Qiu Peng didn’t know that the people inside the room had misunderstood his identity, ready to make a move against him, and he just followed the description provided by Chen Yingyuan, guessing out the identity of the beautiful woman at the head, and immediately gave a slight bow and spoke.

Indeed, Great Xia is thoroughly informed about the situation in Longyou.

Seeing that the other party could recognize his identity at a glance, Li Xuanling’s gaze slightly hardened, first staring at Qiu Peng for a while, then laughingly said: "Great Xia Special Envoy? This is the first time I’ve heard such a title."

Seeing that Qiu Peng only possessed mediocre abilities, Li Xuanling was not polite, made a jest about the title "Special Envoy," then suddenly deepened his voice:

"Does Special Envoy Qiu know that unlawfully intruding upon the crucial grounds of our Longyou Army Camp can cost your life?"

Upon hearing, Qiu Peng raised his head, and stared straight at Li Xuanling, without the slightest attempt to hide the provocation in his eyes.

"Qiu Peng’s life isn’t worth much, young lord is free to take it, but before doing so, my master has some words to inform the people of Long Mountain..."

Is this just a show of force?

Trying to scare me!

There wasn’t the slightest hint of fear on Qiu Peng’s face, he paused for a moment, coldly laughed in his heart before loudly saying: "Great Xia is not interested in Longyou Land, nor does it wish to get involved in the current disputes in Longyou. The reason for deploying troops to Beehive and Wushuang this time is because the leaders of the two houses actively sought aid. Therefore, if there is any offense, I hope the people of Long Mountain will forgive. Both Wushuang and Beehive have already formed alliances with our Great Xia, and henceforth the three will be led by Great Xia. I hope the people of Long Mountain are aware of this!"

After these words, Li Xuanling, including the seven others present, had their expressions turned exceedingly grim, their gazes towards Qiu Peng noticeably carrying more murderous intent.

First of all, Qiu Peng’s words already showed that Great Xia indeed occupied Beehive.

Secondly, although they were unclear of the situation with Beehive, just minutes ago, Li Ying was standing in the same place as Qiu Peng, telling them in person how Great Xia captured Wushuang.

And now Qiu Peng says Wushuang actively sought aid, and that’s why Great Xia deployed troops.

This is just brazenly lying with open eyes!

Not only did they come flauntingly to declare sovereignty but also treated them like fools.

As for Qiu Peng’s claim that Great Xia is not interested in Longyou Land and doesn’t wish to involve itself in the current disputes of Longyou, they completely disregarded it.

Only a fool would believe such words.

After all, who gave this person such audacity to dare to boldly come before them and say these things!

Li Xuandu, along with the other six Cold Resistance Level, all stood up from their chairs, the murderous intent in their eyes rising to an extreme, with even two of the more rash ones starting to walk towards Qiu Peng.

Clearly, they were planning to take action!

Qiu Peng’s words did seem to hint at courting death.

Li Xuanling had not spoken from start to finish, as though he hadn’t noticed the actions of the other seven, only his gaze remained calmly fixed on Qiu Peng.

"I forgot to tell everyone that my master sent two special envoys this time; I am only one of them. Judging by the time, the other should be arriving at Ping Valley shortly. Before you act, you should think it through."

Step...

Qiu Peng’s words caused the killing intent on the faces of Li Xuandu and the seven others to instantly freeze.

Li Xuanling’s exquisitely calm face finally showed a slight ripple.
