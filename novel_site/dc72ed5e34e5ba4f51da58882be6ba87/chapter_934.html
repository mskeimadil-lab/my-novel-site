Chapter 934: Chapter 348: Schemes and Counter-Schemes, the Five of East Ridge, Zhu Yang’s Threat, The Dust Settles (Part 4)

Yanggu Li Shaoqing?

Upon hearing that Yanggu had sent someone first, Lu Yang raised his eyebrows slightly, turned to look at the leader of Yanggu, Yang Quan, and his eyes immediately showed a hint of coldness.

"Just said to cooperate with us, East Ridge, and now he’s betraying us, a two-faced scoundrel, hmph!"

Hearing Zhao Hu’s angry reprimand behind him, Lu Yang shook his head and chuckled softly, "Zhu Yang has personally arrived, his betrayal is understandable, I reckon it’s not just him..."

Evidently, Zhu Tong plans to send the remaining four from Zhu Ling to continue the challenge, letting people from other camps go up, using a relay battle to exhaust Luo Cheng and the other two. Yang Quan sending someone up now clearly shows his allegiance towards Zhu Tong.

Yang Quan had specifically approached Lu Yang two days ago, expressing the intention of his and East Ridge to cooperate. Now it’s evident he’s changed his mind after seeing Zhu Yang.

Of course, it’s not just Yang Quan, there’s also Wan Tianhe!

The three who just went up were defeated without any surprise.

"Long River Wang Wu, challenge platform 1."

"Wanlong Chen Lin, challenge platform 2."

"Yanggu Li Zichuan, challenge platform 3."

Sure enough, Wan Tianhe couldn’t resist sending someone up too!

The betrayal of Yanggu and Wanlong did make Zhu Tong feel good, but the subsequent developments on the platform had gone far beyond his expectations, making his expression increasingly difficult, and his body trembled uncontrollably.

The strength of East Ridge’s three is just too strong!

On platform 3, Zhao Yuanshan lasted until the sixth round of challenges before showing signs of exhaustion and finally conceded in the ninth round under Lu Yang’s signal to a contestant from Hongyuan Camp;

On platform 2, Xiao Yan defeated thirteen people in a row and similarly, under Lu Yang’s signal, deliberately lost to someone from Zi Qiang Camp;

On platform 1, Luo Cheng’s strength was indeed the most terrifying, not using the long spear until the fifth round, continuously defeating eighteen people, only showing slight signs of fatigue, yet still standing on the platform.

Lu Yang was obviously manipulating the scores deliberately, ranking the eight camps, which was something Zhu Tong himself used to do, how could he not see through it?

The problem is, he simply has no way to counter it.

Once people from Hongyuan and Zi Qiang went up, he sent his own players to take down their platforms, but before a few rounds passed, East Ridge’s three immediately came up and took them down.

After dozens of rounds of twists and turns, the final score ranking was miserable to behold.

East Ridge 41, Hongyuan 6, Zi Qiang 5, Zhu Ling 4, Wanxing 4, Long River 3, Yanggu 2, Bai Yuan 2.

According to the rules set by Zhu Tong, everyone only has two chances to actively challenge the platform, meaning that under normal circumstances, all participants would use up the opportunities, so the total score should be exactly eighty.

As of now, the total score has reached 67.

At this point in the competition, the champion is actually already decided.

The current situation has completely reversed, Lu Yang and the people from East Ridge are beaming with joy, while Zhu Tong and Zhu Ling’s people seem drained of their souls, appearing somewhat limp.

"It’s about time, there are only eight people left with the opportunity to challenge: Zhu Baixu and Lin Qiang from Zhu Ling each have two chances, Wan Qingyu of Wanxing has two, Long River Wang Wu has one, Hongyuan’s Hong Yang has one, Zi Qiang’s Zi Ning has one, and then there are our two from East Ridge who haven’t participated yet, hurry up!"

At this moment, the three platforms, two have already seen several changes in people, only Luo Cheng on platform 1 has remained unchanged, despite not looking great, the remaining people are simply too afraid to challenge him.

Hearing Lu Yang’s words, everyone suddenly remembered that East Ridge still has two people who haven’t taken action, and instantly turned to look behind Lu Yang.

The two were still covered in black robes, making it hard to see their strength, but with Luo Cheng and the others in front, who would dare underestimate these two?

"Leader Hong, let Hong Yang go to platform 1!"

Hong Fan heard this and his face bloomed with joy, quickly sending his young son up.

"Luo Cheng, that’s enough, come down and rest!"

Despite having quite a few wounds, Luo Cheng’s face remained extremely calm, even in the second before Lu Yang signaled him to descend, he was still adjusting his breath as much as possible, clearly preparing for the next battle.

This mindset alone was enough to earn Lu Yang’s high regard.

"Luo Ming’s son! Even without mentioning his spear technique talent, just his mentality and perseverance surpasses many, no wonder even the Lord praises him!"

Just as Luo Cheng stood at the back, a porcelain bottle was thrown towards him.

He quickly reached out to catch it, realizing it was thrown by Zhao Hu in front.

"It’s just a competition, is it necessary to fight so hard? Start healing!"

Luo Cheng opened the porcelain bottle and saw the Blood Sore Pill inside. A warmth flowed through his heart, and he cupped his hands to Zhao Hu, "Thank you, Uncle Hu."

Zhao Long and his brothers were Luo Cheng’s old subordinates, during the Rogel Camp days, Luo Cheng, though young, was already aware of them, so he was quite familiar with these three uncles. However, as their status differences grew, he was embarrassed to seek them out actively.

Coming to East Ridge on this occasion, seeing Zhao Hu, he was still quite excited, but worried that they still blamed his father for abandoning the camp back then, so he didn’t dare to greet them actively.

Now Zhao Hu actively gave him Healing Pills, he was naturally moved.

Luo Cheng’s thoughts are set aside for now.

Once Hong Yang replaced him as the platform leader, Zhu Ling couldn’t hold back anymore.
