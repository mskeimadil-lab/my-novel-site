Chapter 805: Chapter 317: Wrapping Up the Migration, Great Xia’s Flourishing Scene, Star-picking Hall

This is clearly a multi-beneficial and highly effective policy.

Her niece’s cleverness, Li Tiancheng knows very well.

Li Xuanling said this clearly to deliberately provoke Xia Hong.

Li Tiancheng pondered for a moment and still spoke in a low voice to comfort her:

"Xuanling, Leader Xia is quite kind to you, every month 10 pieces..."

"May I ask, is the visitor Senior Li Tiancheng?"

But before he finished speaking, he was interrupted by a voice coming from the city gate.

The two looked up and found that at the city gate, two golden-armored soldiers, leading more than a dozen young women with delicate features, were jogging towards them.

As these people jogged over, the two quickly examined their attire carefully.

At the city gate, whether it was the soldiers on duty or those checking people entering the city, they wore black hundred-forged iron armor, and their basic strength was mostly less than twenty thousand pounds.

But the two in front were wearing the same golden armor as the Cloud Dragon Army soldiers, and their basic strength was over twenty thousand pounds.

Moreover, the more than a dozen young women with delicate features behind them, although their cultivation had just broken through the Earth-digging Realm, their attire was not simple,

They were wearing cotton clothes like the two, over which was a blue velvet jacket, and judging by the density of the velvet, it was clearly the top layer fur of a Frost Wolf.

The top layer fur of Frost Wolf is not something rare in Long Mountain, but due to its strong warmth and low yield, this kind of fur is usually used for making hats, and not everyone can wear it.

To use it for clothes, and for more than a dozen women who have just broken through the Earth-digging Realm to wear, that’s different.

These are not the only things; the silver rat fur around their necks, water-blue silk sewn at the cuffs, bone jade accessories on their hair, snow white felt skirts on their lower bodies...

These women’s clothing and accessories alone originated from more than a dozen different types of cold beasts, not to mention the exquisite craftsmanship.

Could it be, in Great Xia, even those in the Earth-digging Realm can wear these clothes?

Even Li Tiancheng, who has seen much of the world, couldn’t help but be stunned.

Li Xuanling, even more so, her accessories and clothes were not few, but compared to these women from Great Xia, they could only be described as simple.

Though having Cold Resistance Level cultivation, Li Xuanling was still a woman after all. The love for beauty is innate, and seeing such stunning clothes and accessories, she couldn’t hide her curiosity, constantly looking at those women.

In the spirit of just arriving, Li Tiancheng didn’t dare to be ostentatious, nodded, and cupped his hands towards the two golden-armored soldiers: "Indeed, I am he. May I ask who you are?"

"Great Xia City Gate Commander, Zhu Kang!"

"Great Xia City Gate Commander, Hong Yu!"

The City Gate Commander, apparently a position specifically to guard the city gate.

Li Tiancheng nodded, just about to speak, when among the more than a dozen women, a leader first rushed to Li Xuanling’s front.

"Then this must be Madam. My name is Han Yue, the head maid of the Star-picking Hall, here under the Lord’s command to greet Madam."

A single "Madam," instantly made Li Xuanling’s face flush with shame, instinctively wanting to refute, but before she could speak, the more than a dozen maids behind her bowed in unison to her.

"Greetings, Madam."

Hong Yu and Zhu Kang exchanged a glance, also bowing.

"Greetings, Madam!"

.........

Li Xuanling was instantly frozen in place, not knowing what to say.

"No wonder the Lord said to wait at the city gate; once Madam arrived, we would surely recognize her. Among tens of thousands in Great Xia, I’ve never seen anyone more beautiful than Madam!"

"Indeed, Madam wearing a pure white dress, without any accessories, can still be this beautiful. If she wore summer clothes, it would surely be unparalleled."

"Only someone as beautiful as Madam can match the Lord."

.........

Li Xuanling, with a naturally thin skin, became instantly red-faced after being lavishly praised by the maids, losing the composure she usually had as the leader of Long Mountain.

"Alright, let’s not block here, let Madam enter the city first!"

Han Yue spoke, cutting off the maids, leading Li Xuanling and Li Tiancheng towards the city gate.

The soldiers on duty under the city gate apparently recognized these maids, and seeing Li Xuanling, who came like a moon surrounded by stars, immediately realized something and dropped everything, clearly intending to salute.

Seeing this, Li Xuanling frowned slightly, saying softly, "It’s just entering the city; no need for all this commotion..."

She paused slightly, clearly worried her words might not work and continued to Hong Yu, "I think, in the bitterly cold weather outside, it’s better to let the last group hurry into the city to avoid any accidents."

"As Madam commands!"

What surprised her was that Hong Yu and Zhu Kang showed no hesitation, understanding her intent, and immediately sped up to the city gate.

"Don’t stop, keep to your duties! Continue letting people into the city."

"Everyone continue with your tasks, no wandering eyes."

.........

Seeing Hong Yu and Zhu Kang obediently following her orders so freely, a peculiar feeling arose in Li Xuanling’s eyes.

But Han Yue, the maid leading the way beside her, clearly guessed what she was thinking and whispered to her, "Madam need not be so restrained. Five days ago, when the Lord returned, he mentioned that in Xia City, you are equivalent to him, so we will all follow your orders."

Hearing this, Li Xuanling felt somewhat pleased.

But upon further reflection, she couldn’t help but pout.
