Chapter 193 - 136 He Yuanhun, Yuwen Hu, Yuwen Dao (Three in One)

Chapter 193: Chapter 136 He Yuanhun, Yuwen Hu, Yuwen Dao (Three in One)

From the beginning, He Meng and his group’s arrogant demeanor;

Until tonight, this group before us intruded into Redwood Ridge for hunting without permission;

These two incidents have already determined that the Mirror Immortal Camp could not possibly become a friendly neighbor, and it is highly likely that they will compress the survival space of Great Xia.

Despite the hunting of the Demon Sheep just now, this group showed a fierce fighting spirit that feared no death, which made Xia Hong feel a bit of admiration.

But an enemy is still an enemy.

Any enemy that might threaten the survival of Great Xia, whether it’s a Cold Beast, Deceitful Monster, or human, Xia Hong would mercilessly kill them if necessary and capable.

I, Xia Hong, dare not accept!

The previous words had already made Xia Hong’s tone extremely low, and anyone could hear the surging anger within it.

The last seven words of Xia Hong were even more bone-chilling cold.

Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan both thought he was about to give the order to act, so they drew out the sabers from their bodies and took a step forward in unison.

"Great Xia Territory? Isn’t this the territory of Lu Shang Camp?"

"Lu He already said that this area was given to the Mirror Immortal Camp, when did it become your Great Xia territory?"

"We haven’t even pursued the matter of Great Xia driving the Rodent herd into the Arrow Bamboo Forest last time, and now you turn it around and accuse us of trespassing on your territory?"

"Hahaha, ridiculous, what a bullshit Great Xia, just eyeing our prey and harboring ill intentions."

"Just three people, even if I’m injured, I’m not afraid."

"Boss, no need to talk, let’s show them what’s what!"

...

The people from the Mirror Immortal Camp had a tough time hunting the Demon Sheep just now, with over twenty people down to just over ten, and now only nine were still standing.

And these nine were mostly injured.

Some were even standing only with support.

Yet even so, upon hearing Xia Hong’s slightly icy words, not a single one of them showed fear, instead, they all boldly retorted.

A few with relatively nimble legs seemed to find mere rebuttal insufficient, and like Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan, they also drew their sabers and took two steps forward.

Their reaction was more intense than even Xia Hong’s three, with an air of willingness to fight to the death at the slightest disagreement.

Xia Hong lowered his gaze, and combined with the fierceness shown by this group during hunting, such a reaction now did not surprise him.

At a distance of more than ten meters, Xia Hong could already make out most of their faces, and the He Meng he dealt with during the last Arrow Bamboo Forest altercation was not among them, but from the few loud voices he heard just now, someone from that time must be present.

He Meng brought fourteen people last time.

Xia Hong focused his gaze on the man in black surrounded by the nine.

Matching the voice he just heard, the man in black did indeed look very young.

With thick eyebrows and a broad face, the expression on his face was as calm as still water, and the form-fitting black strong clothes highlighted his sturdy physique. Perhaps to conceal the wound on his shoulder, he held the hilt of a broadsword stuck into the ground, standing straight like a tall pine, motionless.

He looked at most around Xia Chuan’s age, but his steady and seasoned presence was not one of a young man.

While the nine around him were visibly older, some even appearing middle-aged, their demeanor and behavior, and possibly even their strength, made them seem younger than him.

As Xia Hong watched the man in black, naturally, the man in black was also watching him.

Their gazes met in the air for a brief moment. The man in black’s expression gradually relaxed, a slight smile appearing, and he clasped his fists, speaking brightly:

"Last time I heard from He Meng that there’s a Great Xia Camp near Redwood Ridge, and that Leader Xia Hong is unparalleled in bravery, leading a group to slaughter the Rodent herd terrified and fleeing, nearly entering the Arrow Bamboo Forest. I held respect in my heart, intending to visit in a few days, only to unexpectedly meet here tonight ahead of time.

Mirror Immortal Camp, Yuwen Dao, meet Leader Xia!"

...

Xia Hong’s somber face stiffened for a few seconds.

He considered that Yuwen Dao might react more intensely than the other nine.

Even directly taking action was not impossible.

No matter what, he did not expect that Yuwen Dao would actually be so polite.

Of course, after stiffening, Xia Hong became even more vigilant.

Compared to He Meng and others from the Mirror Immortal Camp’s straightforwardness, Yuwen Dao was clearly a more troublesome character, despite his courteous tone.

Though his words were polite, he alluded to the incident of the Rodent herd nearly breaking into the Arrow Bamboo Forest last time, clearly being tough within his mildness.

"Regarding the incident with the Rodent herd, I had negotiated with He Meng last time. Mirror Immortal Camp has occupied Arrow Bamboo Forest for only three months, without notifying Great Xia beforehand, and the whole incident did not really cause any losses to the Mirror Immortal Camp. More crucially, the Rodent herd never entered the Arrow Bamboo Forest in the end.

You think you can brush this off, I’m afraid it’s not that simple!"

Don’t strike at a smiling face, since Yuwen Dao was being so polite, expressing the intention of negotiating properly, Xia Hong decided to follow his lead, softening his tone slightly.

Of course, it was just the tone that was softened.

After saying the last sentence, he raised his right hand and waved slightly backward.

Whoosh...

Three Iron Arrows flew through the air from the trees behind, piercing the ground two to three meters in front of Yuwen Dao and his men.

The Iron Arrows embedded twenty to thirty centimeters into the ground, making the sound of taut strings and resonating tails from the tremendous force very clear.
