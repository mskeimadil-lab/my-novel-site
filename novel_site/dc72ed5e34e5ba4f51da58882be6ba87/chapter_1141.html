Chapter 1141: Chapter 391: Yang Family Disciples Rise Above Beishuo, Shocking News from Cai Qiu, Sudden Upheaval

Hoo......

The gust of wind stirred by the golden palm brought with it a whistling noise from above. Yang Ning and Li Hu instantly became alert as if faced with a great enemy, raising their hands to block while quickly moving to the sides.

The strength of the two was different, so their reactions were also different.

Li Hu’s basic strength was only fourteen zong, so he dared not take any risk and had to use both hands with full force to block;

whereas Yang Ning’s strength was as high as twenty-one zong and he only used his left hand to block. Seeing Yuan Cheng attacking with both fists, attempting to subdue them both in one move, a chill appeared in his eyes, and he unexpectedly drew a silver small dagger from behind and swiftly thrust it towards Yuan Cheng’s chest.

Whoosh......

Seeing Yang Ning pull out the dagger, Yuan Cheng’s eyes showed no surprise.

Although the Treasure Tower explicitly prohibited carrying weapons, most of these people would only leave conspicuous main weapons downstairs. It was inevitable that small, concealable weapons like daggers would be brought in.

So the surrounding spectators showed no surprise. When Yuan Cheng made his move, they had already retreated, and now, seeing Yang Ning take out a dagger to counterattack, a trace of shock appeared on their faces.

Bang... Bang...

Yuan Cheng’s palms eventually landed on the heads of Yang and Li, producing two loud booms.

Crack... Crack......

Then came the crisp sound of bones cracking.

The weaker Li Hu’s arms were directly snapped, and his body was like a nail driven straight into the third floor, leaving only half of his body outside;

the stronger Yang Ning, as he only blocked with his left arm, had only his left arm broken, and due to his quicker reaction, avoided half a body position, thus collapsing diagonally to the left.

But his body did not completely collapse.

Because Yuan Cheng was holding his silver small dagger in his left hand, suspending his whole body in midair, while looking at him with a slightly mocking and sarcastic expression.

"Director Yuan’s strength is so strong?"

"The dagger in that Beishuo man’s hand is silver-made, right? It couldn’t even cut Lord Yuan’s skin membrane?"

"Speed, it’s all about speed. That man’s strength should be above 20 zong, yet after Director Yuan blasted away the other person, he could still catch his dagger in a split second. This proves that their strengths are on completely different levels!"

"Great Xia’s officials of the Director level generally have a strength above 30 zong. I thought it was just a rumor, but it turns out to be true."

.........

Bang!

Yuan Cheng wouldn’t miss such a good opportunity to establish dominance. He tightened his grip on Yang Ning’s dagger and kicked him directly away.

Thunk...

Yang Ning’s body crashed hard into the wall, spewing out a mouthful of blood. Struggling to free himself from the wall for a long time, he looked up to meet Yuan Cheng’s eyes, which were filled with arrogance and sarcasm. He clenched his fists tightly, his heart full of hatred and unwillingness.

Only nine years, just nine short years!

He remembered clearly, nine years ago, he and Li Hu looked at the people of Great Xia with such eyes. In just nine short years, their roles had reversed.

The key point was that Yuan Cheng was merely a subordinate that Xia Hong brought to Rogel Camp nine years ago.

This intense shame resulting from the role reversal drove Yang Ning nearly insane, but realizing that his strength had created a huge chasm between him and Yuan Cheng, all his madness could only turn into powerlessness, and he could only glare angrily at Yuan Cheng.

"No need to look at me like that. Nine years have passed, and I have grown stronger. I thought you might have made some progress too, but it seems you’re still such a waste. It looks like your so-called pride of coming from Beishuo amounts to nothing!"

Yuan Cheng directly lifted the unconscious Li Hu, walked slowly to Yang Ning, and seeing him glaring stubbornly, instead of getting angry, he shook his head with a mocking smile, feeling no fun.

"I, Yang Ning, admit defeat today. No more nonsense, do it!"

Realizing that their identities were exposed, Yang Ning was convinced that today was a day of certain death. Having lost the strength advantage, he could not lose face for Beishuo further.

His attitude of being ready to die indeed elicited quite a bit of admiration.

"Yang Ning, a fourth-generation disciple of the Yang Family from Beishuo, right?"

"Among the fourth-generation descendants of the Yang Family, there’s no one named Yang Ning among the Six Horses, is there?"

"The Six Horses are Yang Bao, Yang Gong, Yang Xuan, Yang An, Yang Shou, and Yang Ding. There is indeed no Yang Ning."

"Not very famous, must be from a branch family."

"Even branch family members are still members of the Yang Family! Why come to Hongmen?"

.........

Hearing the voices around him, Yuan Cheng frowned slightly. His actions today were not meant to give face to Yang and Li. After pondering for a moment, he pulled Yang Ning out of the wall and then, like handling chicks, threw both Li Hu and Yang Ning out the window.

Both fell silently after being thrown out the window.

Yuan Cheng approached the window and looked down, his face revealing a smile, and he jumped directly out of the window.

Qiu Peng and Lin Kai followed closely behind, and the curious ones inside the building also exited, while the others crowded the windows to continue watching the commotion.

"May I ask Director Yuan, what heinous crime did my two grandsons commit that merits you personally injuring them like this?"

First, a questioning voice came from below, and then those by the window saw that thirteen people dressed in white had hurriedly come to the front of the building. It turned out that the elder leading the group had caught Yang Ning and Li Hu, which is why they did not make any sound upon being thrown out by Yuan Cheng.
