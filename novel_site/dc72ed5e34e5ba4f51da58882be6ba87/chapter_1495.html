Chapter 1495: Chapter 470: Meticulous Preparation, Jackal Bureaucrats, the Uprising Begins_5

Swish!

As if to confirm Zhang Yuchuan’s words, before he could finish speaking, a sudden burst of firelight rose into the sky about seven or eight kilometers to the south. The light arched into a fire arc in the air, leaving a trail of white smoke that lingered.

"The south side, Baiying Village has acted. How can it be so early?"

Zhang Yuchuan’s face suddenly changed, he quickly bowed his head in thought, his expression shifting a few times before he turned to the person on his left, cupped his hands, and spoke: "Trouble Guardian Wang to check the outskirts, see how long it will take for the others to arrive."

"Alright, I’ll go now!"

The person on the left, upon hearing, immediately swept towards the outskirts of Su Field.

"It’s only been half an hour, it doesn’t make sense for it to be so early. There’s probably been a leak, Baiying Village’s Su collecting area is just eight kilometers from here. If there really is a leak, someone will soon come to report. Those in the Earth-digging Realm haven’t arrived yet, we need to hurry, we need to be quicker..."

Zhang Yuchuan’s prayers eventually worked.

That Guardian Wang quickly brought good news.

"They’ve arrived, the Earth-digging Realm Limit is entering Su Field. In at most a hundred breaths, the congregation will be able to get the weapons."

"Good!"

Zhang Yuchuan’s expression suddenly brightened, then he took a bone whistle out of his arms, led two protectors, and swiftly moved towards the perimeter.

"Ghosts!"

"Who?"

"There are ghosts in Su Field!"

.........

Interestingly, there’s currently a large group from the Earth-digging Realm walking inside, three people with evil ghost masks rushing outward, suddenly frightening many.

However, Zhang Yuchuan had no time to care. When timing approximately a hundred breaths, he suddenly rushed to the perimeter, slightly gathered his strength, and fiercely blew the bone whistle in his mouth.

Beep... Beep... Beep... Beep... Beep......

Five rapid whistle sounds spread out in all directions, a rustling noise immediately emitted from Su Field, yet its surface remained calm and undisturbed.

"Whistle?"

"Wasn’t that a whistle just now from Su Field?"

"What’s happening!"

......

On the perimeter of Su Field, Zhang Long, along with the Eight Deputy Managers, was looking up at the firelight in the south sky, full of confusion. The urgent whistle sounds from Su Field suddenly drew everyone’s attention back.

"That looks like a Command Arrow! Has something happened in Baiying Village?"

Beside him, Zhang Hu’s casual comment made Zhang Long’s expression suddenly tense; he turned his head to look inside Su Field, his pupils shrinking suddenly.

Swish......

A sharp whizzing sound came from the side, Zhang Long’s heart skipped a beat, his face full of horror, his body frantically retreated to dodge.

His reaction was very fast, yet alas, the attacker was too close, almost within reach.

No matter how quickly he retreated, he could only avoid the vital spot on his neck; the opponent’s dagger still stabbed into his chest.

Thunk......

"Brother."

"Manager Zhang!"

"Liu Zhang, what are you doing?"

"Defying authority, Liu Zhang, you’re courting death!"

.........

The sudden turn of events left everyone present momentarily dumbfounded.

Zhang Hu and the other seven deputy managers, the hundred County Guard Army members, and the over a hundred people from the Subordinate Bureau, even those in the Earth-digging Realm preparing to enter Su Field nearby, all paused and turned to look.

Meanwhile, Liu Zhang took advantage of everyone’s moment of confusion, quickly running towards Su Field, away from Zhang Long and the others.

"With Frost Resilience, burn down Chu Territory, resist harsh taxes, reclaim our necessities, smash household registration, no more hierarchy, brothers, strike!"

Liu Zhang, reaching the outskirts of Su Field, suddenly turned and angrily shouted towards Zhang Long and the others.

Zhang Long and all the Bureau members turned pale upon hearing, their faces full of suspicion and uncertainty.

"With Frost Resilience, burn down Chu Territory, resist harsh taxes, reclaim our necessities, smash household registration, no more hierarchy!"

"With Frost Resilience, burn down Chu Territory, resist harsh taxes, reclaim our necessities, smash household registration, no more hierarchy!"

.........

The next scene made their faces instantly turn white, the original suspicion turning directly into panic and terror.

Countless Earth-digging Realm members began to chant the slogans, over a thousand people suddenly rushed out from the west side of Su Field, right behind Liu Zhang, dozens from the front ranks even wearing war armor.

"Brothers, kill these dog officials, we revolt!"
