Chapter 1451 - 462: Copper Bone Corpse Demon, Heart-Stopping and Death-Defying Escape, Trial Interlude, Treacherous Changes (Part 2)

Chapter 1451: Chapter 462: Copper Bone Corpse Demon, Heart-Stopping and Death-Defying Escape, Trial Interlude, Treacherous Changes (Part 2)

In that instant, the Shattered Star Sword in his hand unleashed at least thousands of Feather Light Sword Edges, simultaneously repelling Fan Qinghu and the other three. Meanwhile, sixty percent of the Feather Light Sword Edges poured like torrents directly onto the fake Xia Hong.

"Die!"

The Feather Light Sword Technique was the top-tier swordsmanship Xia Hong first devised using the Enlightenment Lotus Platform. This sword move was derived from Li Xuanling’s Spiritual Sword Technique, originally tailor-made for Lu Yang. This swordsmanship measures by the number of Feather Light Sword Edges produced—over a hundred is entry-level, over three hundred is minor success, over five hundred is great success, surpassing a thousand reaches Transformation Realm.

As the creator of the Feather Light Sword Technique, Xia Hong’s mastery needs no elaboration. In his combat, the starry sea of Feather Light Sword Edges certainly exceeds five thousand; even with sixty percent, there are three thousand sword edges that instantly poured onto the body of the fake Xia Hong.

The sheer number of sword edges is secondary!

The key is that these sword edges combine like tidal waves, immediately amplifying martial arts power by fifty percent. It’s the terrifying force of nearly a Hundred Jun added to the sword edges that is its true core killing move.

Swish swish swish swish swish......

As thousands of Feather Lights descended upon him, and experiencing Xia Hong’s real power, the fake Xia Hong’s face abruptly changed.

In pastbearing over a thousand sword lights, looking at the countless wounds etched across his body, he finally dared not continue blocking and began swiftly retreating while trying to repel the power of the Feather Light Sword Edges.

However, it wasn’t over yet!

Sizzle......

After the thousands of Feather Lights, a divine light suddenly shot out from Xia Hong’s left eye, heading straight towards the fake Xia Hong’s body.

The fake Xia Hong’s brows furrowed sharply, and upon seeing the divine light, he darted away, fleeing to the left.

"Try to escape!"

Unfortunately, Xia Hong had already predicted his movements, inclining his body forward, quickly turning his head left, and shooting another divine light.

Sizzle......

The second divine light still missed.

Because that fake Xia Hong managed to hide behind the nearest Iron Bone Corpse Demon at the last crucial moment.

The Iron Bone Corpse Demon had been driven here by Fan Qinghu and several Xianyang Level collaborators; through sheer chance, it became the fake Xia Hong’s shield.

Clang......

"Hehe..."

The divine light hit the body, and in an instant, the little remaining flesh of that Iron Bone Corpse Demon melted away, uttering unclear yelps as its black iron skeleton began to crack like a spider web.

Boom......

Within less than five breaths, the skeleton of that Iron Bone Corpse Demon shattered with a boom, scattering into fragments on the ground.

Curiously, amid the fragments, a crystal identical in color to a Blood Jade Pearl flew out. Being the closest to the corpse demon, Xia Hong, noticing the greed in everyone’s eyes around him, naturally caught the blood-colored crystal first.

"This treacherous beast even resorts to such venomous tactics against its kind. Admiral Fan, this must not be let free; otherwise, consequences will be dire!"

Upon hearing the fake Xia Hong’s words, Xia Hong’s brow furrowed sharply. He turned to see Fan Qinghu and the other three he had just swept away return again. His face immediately darkened, especially noticing the caution and murderous intent in their eyes; his expression turned even grimmer.

Previously, before the Copper Bone Corpse Demon transformed truthfully, Fan Qinghu and his group already considered him the most troublesome. Now, having shown such terrifying prowess and mistakenly using the Shattering Divine Light to kill an Iron Bone Corpse Demon, Fan Qinghu and the four, along with the surrounding Cai Qiu’s Xianyang Levels, probably all believe he is fake.

Beyond explanation!

Since coming to Cai Qiu, he never revealed his true strength, originally planning to use it effectively at some key moment. Who could have thought that until now it would be exploited by a deceitful monster.

Fake becomes real, and real becomes fake, what kind of situation is this?

"Admiral Fan, the hundred-thousand strong army outside the miasma should already be facing major issues. I advise you to go and check immediately..."

"Stop dallying with this treacherous beast, kill!"

Fan Qinghu didn’t listen to Xia Hong’s explanations at all, not even needing the fake Xia Hong to instigate. He immediately led Cai Yunsheng and the other three to attack.

Whoosh......

The strongest among Cai Family’s trio wielded power around sixty Jun, together with Fan Qinghu slightly above seventy Jun; just these four already made it difficult for Xia Hong to manage.

Not to mention the fake Xia Hong, lurking like a venomous snake, eagerly around the four. Pretending only sixty-plus Jun’s standard might, yet, with Fan Qinghu’s four cooperating, occasionally landing lethal strikes—within just several score breathes, Xia Hong’s body bore over ten wounds from the longsword.

Worse yet, with the surrounding Iron Bone Corpse Demons gradually cleared, numerous Xianyang Levels started joining the encirclement.

"If I don’t escape soon, I’ll lose my life..."

During his defense, Xia Hong tried explaining countless times, yet Fan Qinghu’s four were set upon identifying him as fake, ignoring him outright. Realizing he no longer had room for explanation, with the situation growing increasingly dire, he harbored escape intentions, first looking northwards, hurriedly abandoning thoughts of fleeing.

He had just warned Fan Qinghu about the hundred-thousand army in trouble outside the miasma, not without cause. Being here so long, he finally understood regardless of when he got trapped. The fact that the "fake Xia Hong" set such a kill trap proves that before entering the miasma area, it’s been targeting him.
