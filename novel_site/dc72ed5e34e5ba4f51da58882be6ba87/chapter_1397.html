This questioning quickly attracted the attention of the woman in white, the old man in black, and the five armored commanders, all turning their heads to look at him with displeasure.

"Isn’t he the Caiqiu person who likes to select concubines everywhere, right?"

Li Xuanling didn’t come to this tavern intentionally. She glanced at Cai Shiqing briefly before shifting her gaze, speaking in a calm tone to the five people beside Yuan Cheng.

Yuan Cheng, Qiu Peng, Xu Ning, Liu Yuan, and Hou Quan had been ambushing in Chui Mountain Town for over two months, but information gathering had always been Qiu Peng’s responsibility, so the four immediately focused their attention on Qiu Peng.

Qiu Peng glanced at Cai Shiqing, realizing that this fool must have offended Li Xuanling, hurriedly nodding and bowing, he said, "Reporting Madam, this person is named Cai Shiqing, of a lascivious nature, and has used force in numerous village-level camps in Jinshan, Wuchuan, and other towns to compel good families to serve his lust, with more than three hundred victims in total.

Reportedly a relative of the Cai Family, though likely from a collateral branch; since last August when Cai Shiqi came over, he has restrained himself a bit, and his status in Caiqiu is far inferior to Cai Shiqi..."

"Lowly dog bastard, how dare you..."

Stripped of his dignity, Cai Shiqing’s face fluctuated between green and white, and upon hearing Qiu Peng’s final words about his status being far inferior to Cai Shiqi’s, he couldn’t suppress the anger surging from his heart and suddenly drew his longsword, interrupting him directly.

Swish......

Unfortunately, before he could finish speaking, a chopstick flew from Li Xuanling’s hand and directly pierced through his throat.

Cai Shiqing was completely bewildered, his Cold Resistance Level life force preventing him from falling immediately, he looked at Li Xuanling who threw the chopstick at him, his face full of incredulity, raising his hand to point at Li Xuanling in disbelief:

"You... how dare you... you..."

Kill me?

In the hinterlands of the Nine Towns Territory, someone dared to kill me?

How is this possible......

Even as his life force was fully extinguished, Cai Shiqing’s pupils remained filled with confusion and bewilderment, he was a Cai Family relative, with a bright future and prospects, how could he die in a place like the Nine Towns?

Standing beside Li Xuanling, Cai Wan and his four companions were already stunned.

They hadn’t expected Li Xuanling to act so directly, killing Cai Shiqing without even asking a question.

"Killing a Cai Family relative, how dare you... how dare you..."

Cai Wan seemed too shaken by the scene in front of him, he dashed to Cai Shiqing’s side in an instant, looking at the lifeless form, he turned to Li Xuanling, repeatedly questioning.

"Killing a Cai Family relative is a grave offense in Caiqiu, no matter where you’re from, you’re in big trouble, you’re in big trouble..."

"Chattering, these four who aid and abet evil aren’t good people either. Old Xiang, kill them directly, prepare the heads of all five, and let the Caiqiu Prince take a good look later!"

Li Xuanling was utterly uninterested in hearing Cai Wan’s nonsense, and she directly ordered Xiang Fuhai.

At the sound, Xiang Fuhai sprang into motion; with four Cold Resistance Level experts barely over 40 Zong, he didn’t even bother using his long staff, moving quickly four times, killing the fleeing Cai Wan and his companions with his palms before the attic window was fully shattered.

Judging by the glance Cai Shiqing had cast upon Li Xuanling when she first appeared at the top floor, she had already sentenced these five to death.

This Cai Shiqing, harboring such lewd desires towards a woman whose strength oversaw his own, effectively confirmed the intel returned by Han Feng, Li Hu, and Yang Zhong from Jinshan Town.

This person was utterly consumed by lust, relying on power to select concubines in village-level camps everywhere, there’s no shortage of instances even when in Jinshan and Wuchuan Towns, where he bullied men and women relying on his birth, and last July during his visit to Muyin Town, he even attempted to forcibly wed Murong Chui’s granddaughter Murong Yan as a second wife, with Murong Chui negotiating for over a month; eventually restrained only after Cai Shiqi arrived, Cai Shiqing temporarily backed off.

Although he backed off temporarily, he left threatening words to Murong Chui, stating his determination to marry Murong Yan, resulting in Murong Chui having no choice but to send his granddaughter and all young women of the clan to live in Xia City.

For someone like this, encountering me, how could he possibly survive!

Seeing Yuan Cheng already had Cai Shiqing and his companions decapitated, Li Xuanling revealed a sly smile under her veil, suddenly sensing something, she quickly turned to look at the military camp outside the window.

"Stop, stop on behalf of this Lord!"

Duan Hong’s voice came from the direction of the Lord’s Hall, and Li Xuanling’s eyes instantly flashed with a chill.

"Here they come, Old Xiang follow me outside, Liu Yuan and Hou Quan, you two take the Cloud Jiao Long Wu armies, enter the camp to clean up, Yuan Cheng, Xu Ning, Qiu Peng, you lead the remaining eight thousand troops, surround the entire Inner City, let no one escape."

"Your subordinates obey!"

Yuan Cheng and his companions were shocked, hurriedly responding with bowed heads, only to realize Li Xuanling and Xiang Fuhai had already disappeared.

Without hesitation, they jumped off the window, dispersing throughout the Inner City, beginning to direct the army’s actions.

.........

When Duan Hong finally broke free from the side hall and flew into the air, witnessing the situation in the military camp, he was instantly stunned!

The original expectation for this coup was that Mu Qinghe could mobilize at most three thousand men; with Yellow Armor First and Second Armies, along with the clan’s progeny and Duan Family direct lineage totaling over three thousand, failure seemed impossible. Then, once the three thousand Beishuo Army moved out from the Duan Clan Land, the coup Mu Family planned would immediately fail.
