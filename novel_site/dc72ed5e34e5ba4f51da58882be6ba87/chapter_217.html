This time, after using the Cold Beast Blood for the second time, Xia Hong, in front of them, mined more than 1,400 pounds of iron ore in one day.

The next day, when everyone followed Xia Hong out of the mine, their faces showed a bit of numbness. After Xia Hong instructed them about going out to hunt tonight and went upstairs, everyone exploded into lively discussions.

"Three times, the leader’s basic strength is almost 40,000 pounds, right?"

"Probably not yet, but it’s close!"

"Three of us are needed to match his mining output!"

"Even though I’ve reached the Earth-digging Realm, I feel like I still have the strength of Logging Realm in front of the leader!"

"No kidding, I feel that too!"

"The leader hasn’t even broken through yet. If he breaks through to the Cold Resistance Level, we probably wouldn’t be able to withstand even one punch from him!"

"Can you withstand it now?"

...

Luo Yuan’s last question immediately silenced everyone.

They recalled the time when Xia Hong jumped down from the tree to hunt that Intermediate Snow Mane, and from the power of that single axe, none of them dared to say they could withstand it.

"We need to put in more effort. Even if we can’t catch up with the leader, at least we should be able to be helpful. If the gap becomes too big, the leader might soon have no use for us!"

After saying that, Luo Yuan also went upstairs.

The others, after pondering for a moment, their expressions gradually turned determined, and they each went upstairs to rest, preparing their energy for the night’s hunting.

...

On February 4th, Xia Hong led the team out for hunting again.

To meet the needs for the next three days of improvement, it took an entire night, and Xia Hong finally managed to kill the third Snow Mane just an hour before dawn.

Hunting three Intermediate Snow Mane in one night left both Xia Hong and the other fourteen members of the Hunting Team utterly exhausted.

Despite this, excitement glimmered more in everyone’s eyes.

First, breaking through limits itself is exciting.

Secondly, they all knew Xia Hong needed a lot of Cold Beast Blood. The more efficiently they hunted Intermediate Cold Beasts, the more Cold Beast Blood they collected, which also meant Xia Hong could break through to the Cold Resistance Level faster.

The three Cold Beasts together produced more than 170 pounds of blood.

Xia Hong began the process of reshaping his skin once more. Due to resource limitations, he didn’t push to the extreme this time but stopped at around thirty pounds. Even with this control, he fainted from pain several times.

During this period, Xia Hong led the team hunting once every three days, bringing back three Intermediate Snow Mane each time, and immediately extracting the blood for use. This continued for five times, and as soon as Zou Yuankai’s injury healed, he immediately rejoined the Hunting Team, acting with everyone.

...

February 19th, First Year of the Great Xia

The Hunting Team brought back three Intermediate Snow Mane once again.

The camp residents were no longer surprised by these behemoths, as similar prey had been brought back almost twenty times during this period.

Xia Hong, as usual, went directly upstairs, while Xia Chuan took the prey to the Camp Supply Station to prepare the bloodletting.

The other Hunting Team members, those injured hurried back to apply medicine and heal, and those without injuries went straight to the mine as it was still early.

[Resources: wood 48212, coal 1020, iron 13210, silver 0, gold 0]

Upstairs, Xia Hong didn’t wait for the Cold Beast Blood as usual but first checked the recently accumulated resources in the system.

From February 4th to now, a total of only fifteen days had passed.

Accumulating so many resources in fifteen days proves how frenzied the camp’s gathering had been in this half month.

The Logging Realm doesn’t need much mention; the numbers were continuously rising, tools were constantly improving, and with over three hundred people, they could gather about three thousand wood resources a day. In half a month, forty-eight thousand is reasonable.

The main focus is the Earth-digging Realm. Over the past half month, Xia Hong led them on five hunting trips. On those days, it was impossible to mine, so strictly speaking, they only had ten days for mining.

Their current mining efficiency is about 7500 pounds of iron ore and 12000 pounds of coal per day, which translates to 750 points and 600 points in the system.

To upgrade the wooden mannequin as soon as possible, Xia Hong arranged for coal mining on only two of those ten days, with the remaining eight days focused on iron.

Note that he had been training during this period, only mining a bit of iron on the first day and not visiting the mine at all thereafter.

In those eight days, even with full efforts, these fifteen people could only mine about 11250 points, and even with fluctuations, it wouldn’t exceed 12000.

Yet, the system showed 13210 points of iron resources.

Clearly, after returning from hunting, some people didn’t rest much and went mining instead, seizing every minute.

After thinking for a moment, Xia Hong understood what was happening. Knowing that everyone wanted to quickly assist him in breaking through, a warm feeling flowed through his heart.

The process of reshaping skin was painful, yet Xia Hong persistently endured, without any slack.

Firstly, he enjoyed the improvement after the pain;

Secondly, breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level was no longer just his affair alone.

During this period, the Logging Realm, Earth-digging Realm, and even the ordinary people left in the camp had all been working tirelessly, day and night, collecting resources for his breakthrough.

The gazes of over a thousand people could be said to be focused on him.
