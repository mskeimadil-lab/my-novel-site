Chapter 1175: Chapter 400: Building Upgrades, Preparations and Plans, Spousal Tacit Understanding

Holy Vein Pill!

As soon as the pill took shape, Xia Hong could already smell the rich, exotic fragrance.

Receiving the yellow porcelain bottle handed over by Cheng Feng, he immediately opened it.

Seeing the six golden, round elixir pills inside, Xia Hong’s expression slightly stirred, looking up at Cheng Feng with a hint of expectation.

Cheng Feng naturally understood why Xia Hong showed such an expression. The excitement in his eyes had not yet dissipated, and he spoke excitedly, "Lord, this Holy Vein Pill has miraculous effects on meridian-related injuries. I am not yet sure of its effects at the Xianyang Level, but for those with Cold Resistance Level cultivation, even if all their meridians are severed, taking this pill will completely restore them in less than a day. You should still remember Shi Qing, right?"

Seeing Xia Hong nod, Cheng Feng continued, "Shi Qing was incapacitated back then because all the joints and meridians in his body were completely destroyed by Wood Puppet Monster’s threads. Four years ago, Minister painstakingly reattached all his joints and primary meridians. This gave him another chance to cultivate, but since the majority of his meridians were still in a broken state, his cultivation progress was much slower than others, and during battles, he could only exert fifty to sixty percent of his power at most."

The meridians are equivalent to the body’s sensors. Whether in battle or cultivation, their role is crucial, and Xia Hong naturally understood this principle. Hearing this, he basically got the whole picture.

"When I refined the first batch the day before yesterday, I tested it on Shi Qing. After taking the pill, the injuries to his meridians immediately healed, and his basic strength jumped from 1 zong to 1.2 zong on the spot."

"So, this Holy Vein Pill is significantly effective for those at the Cold Resistance Level?"

"Calling it a miraculous medicine is not an exaggeration. Even without cases like Shi Qing’s, most people who hunt and fight long-term outside usually sustain some meridian damage. As long as they take the Holy Vein Pill, their meridians can be perfectly restored. If this pill is placed in the Camp Supply Department, it will surely be wildly sought after!"

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong immediately nodded and continued to ask, "What spiritual materials are needed to refine the Holy Vein Pill? Can it be mass-produced?"

Upon hearing this question, Cheng Feng’s face paused, followed by a wry smile, "The Holy Vein Pill requires a total of 28 kinds of spirit plants to refine, most of which are not a problem. However, Bone-breaking Grass and Yellow Jade Milk are the two most difficult to acquire. We only found Bone-breaking Grass last month in the Jiuqu Water Area, and there are only 5 plants. As for Yellow Jade Milk, it was sent back five days ago by people from the Zhaoyang outpost. I inquired about it, and it was accidentally discovered in a stone cave deep in Beimang Mountain.

I have currently transplanted Bone-breaking Grass into the medicine field, but Yellow Jade Milk should be a naturally formed spiritual material, and transplantation is not possible. Unless we can find a large quantity of Yellow Jade Milk, mass production is definitely not possible.

I have yet to rate the Holy Vein Pill, but looking at the difficulty of refining it, it should be classified as a Level 6 Spiritual Pill without a doubt."

Hearing the stringent refinement conditions, Xia Hong constantly shook his head.

Cheng Feng is the Director of the Harvesting Department. Five years ago, he, together with Deputy Director Bai Dongying, categorized all the Dan Medicines of Great Xia into six ranks and established a supporting Level 6 Pill Master examination system.

Level 6 is the highest level of Spiritual Pills. To his knowledge, there are currently twelve types classified as Level 6, among which the most representative ones should be the Bone Condensing Pill, Bone Gathering Pill, Dragon Carp Pill, and Green Cod Pill.

After all, rare things are hard to find. If a good thing like the Holy Vein Pill could be easily made, that would indeed be strange.

Xia Hong sighed slightly, putting away the Holy Vein Pill first. He then raised his head and looked around the Alchemy Pavilion, curiously asking, "How many elixir recipes has the Alchemy Pavilion currently synthesized in total?"

Upon hearing this question, the wry smile on Cheng Feng’s face immediately disappeared. His expression slightly stirred as he said, "Since last October when the Lord of October explored the Wuyuan Waters, new species have been continuously sent over. Thanks to the fact that most of them are spirit plants and spiritual materials, the Alchemy Pavilion’s identified medicinal resources have been increasing, and the synthesis of elixir recipes has been steadily rising.

By this April, the Alchemy Pavilion has identified a total of 4,821 kinds of medicinal resources, and synthesized 128 elixir recipes.

At the end of April, the Lord took control of Bashang Town and Jiuchu Camp, and a large number of new species were sent over; plus, with He Yin Village acquired in July, the Camp’s Hunting Team can freely enter and exit Moao Mountain. The new spirit plants and spiritual materials transported back during this period have been countless. The new medicinal resources identified by the Alchemy Pavilion have reached dozens of kinds every day, and now there are already 5,291 medicinal resources, with almost daily new elixir recipes being synthesized, currently reaching 304.

Moreover, as the exploration progress on the He Yin Village side continuously deepens, more new species will be sent back, and the number of synthesized elixir recipes will keep soaring. I estimate this surge will last for a long time, and the total number of synthesized elixir recipes should reach over 500 in the end."

Hearing that Great Xia currently has as many as 304 types of Dan Medicines, Xia Hong couldn’t help but feel somewhat excited. After pondering for a moment, he immediately agreed with Cheng Feng’s prediction and even felt it might be somewhat conservative.

The primary function of the Medicine Workshop is to identify. It records every type of identified medicinal resource, and then, based on these various medicinal properties, automatically synthesizes various elixir recipes.

It is like a database with an automatic integration function. To maximize its potential, the only way is to continuously input new spirit plants and spiritual materials so that its database keeps expanding. Consequently, the synthesized recipes will naturally become more and more numerous.
