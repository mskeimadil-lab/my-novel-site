Chapter 1394: Chapter 450: Out of Control, Intimidation and Naivety, The Battle Situation Clears, Cai Shiqing the Beautiful

Outside the military camp, several main roads are indeed filled with waves of soldiers clad in Thousand Forged Iron Armor, with white cotton cloth wrapped around their left arms, rapidly charging towards us.

At a glance, there are at least seven or eight thousand of them...

"Defensive Army?"

"That’s the Defensive Army’s War Armor!"

"There must be at least seven or eight thousand people, where did Mu Qinghe get them from?"

"Mu Qinghe originally had three thousand under his command, and the three large camps of Tie Ling, East Qing, and Pingkang were previously managed by Zhu Ziying, Helian Shan, and Qiu Sanming respectively. They must have loyal followers as well, and they’ve secretly recalled all the outside Defensive Army back to the town."

"The Defensive Army, the Defensive Army soldiers, can they come out during the day?"

"They’re all at the Earth-digging Realm cultivation level!"

......

Soon, someone realized the key point, and everyone was stunned as they watched the massive Defensive Army soldiers charging towards the camp.

"No wonder, I was wondering why there were a dozen fire arrows launched in the city earlier, yet only one large troop came from the West District. That old dog, Mu Qinghe, even arranged the Defensive Army so well!"

Inside the camp, Duan Xian slashed through two or three rebels in front of him with his blade, staring angrily at the Defensive Army soldiers attacking from outside.

"Among the Nine Towns, who has the ability to have Earth-digging Realm soldiers appear during the day?"

Duan Xian’s fury stopped abruptly with Duan Mian’s soft inquiry beside him. Several thoughts flashed through his mind, his face filled with shock.

"Great Xia!"

"Great Xia!"

They almost shouted at the same time.

In the year before last, during the Battle of Hongmen, over thirty thousand Great Xia soldiers appeared directly during the day, leaving a deep impression on the towns commanded by Moao.

With the commercial exchange over the past two years, Great Xia’s Sunlight Pill is no longer a secret. It’s available at Hongmen’s Treasure Tower for just three thousand silver, expensive but within reach for many.

The problem is, the Treasure Tower doesn’t allow bulk purchases of Sunlight Pills, and they only permit individual purchases. The town’s merchant guilds don’t have procurement qualifications.

Now, looking at the Defensive Army charging toward them, there are nearly six thousand soldiers, which would cost 1.8 million silver easily. Even if Mu Qinghe had such money, the Treasure Tower wouldn’t just sell them to him.

Thus, there can only be one explanation!

Behind Mu Qinghe’s rebellion, there’s more than just Cai Qiu Prince.

There’s Great Xia!

"Why panic, it’s only six thousand Defensive Army soldiers. Dreaming if they think they can turn the tables with them. Charge forward, eliminate the camp rebels first, then break out!"

Suddenly, a sharp shout erupted from the northern sky above the camp.

The Beishuo Army inside the camp looked up, their expressions immediately becoming incredibly invigorated.

In the northern sky, a middle-aged man with a calm demeanor was floating, wearing golden silk clothes and holding a long silver spear. His expression sharp, he gazed steadily at the Defensive Army charging from the camp outskirts, his lips curled slightly in disdain.

"Yang Fa!"

"Lord Town Chief is here."

"Hahaha, the rebels are doomed."

"Mu Qinghe and his gang, your end is here!"

......

Duan Mian, Duan Xian, and the Duan Family direct line soldiers were all instantly thrilled to the extreme, not just them, even the Beishuo Army hadn’t expected the Town Chief Yang Fa to personally come, and morale soared.

In stark contrast were the Mu Family direct line soldiers.

Fighting to this point almost an hour had passed, Duan Hong still hadn’t appeared, being their biggest reliance in keeping their spirits up. Yang Fa’s sudden appearance, as an Xianyang Level, naturally dealt them a significant psychological blow.

However, their discouraged morale was quickly lifted.

"Duan Family thieves, you dare call my Mu Family rebels? When this Army Chief followed the predecessor Lord in establishing the foundation of Chui Mountain, you bunch of fools weren’t even born yet. And now you dare label me a rebel!"

As the Defensive Army charged the camp outskirts, a voice also resounded in their rear airspace—a familiar, yet venerable voice that everyone in Chui Mountain Town knew well.

The Mu Family direct line soldiers in combat all suddenly shuddered, turning uniformly to look at the sky behind the Defensive Army.

An elder in summer clothes, holding two swords, though aged in appearance yet tall and sturdy in build, stood proudly in mid-air with a glare directed toward the Duan Mian, Duan Xian, and the Duan Family members below. His long beard slightly trembled, hinting at his intense anger.

"Army Chief!"

"Lord Army Chief is back."

"Lord Army Chief!"

"Lord Army Chief has returned to stand up for us!"

"Hahaha, Duan Family goes against justice, Army Chief can’t tolerate it any longer."

"Army Chief is back, Chui Mountain is saved."

"Duan Hong tail-wagged begging Beishuo, unqualified to be Chui Mountain’s master. Brothers, the Army Chief is most qualified."

"Indeed, appointing the Army Chief as Lord gives hope to revive Chui Mountain!"

"Hahaha, we can’t lose, we can’t lose!"

......

It can be said that today, all the soldiers involved in the rebellion, seeing Mu Longhe, finally relaxed their tense nerves.

From this point on, they held no psychological burden over the rebellion.

Overthrowing Duan Hong and welcoming back Mu Longhe instantly became their consensus.

"Brothers, attack!"

Seeing Mu Longhe appear, Mu Qinghe, Zhu Ziying, and the other five showed no surprise, clearly informed beforehand.

This resurgence of high morale was certainly not to be missed by them.
