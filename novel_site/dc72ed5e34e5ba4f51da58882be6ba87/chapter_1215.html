Chapter 1215: Chapter 407: Filial Piety Cannot Surpass the Mu Family, Benevolence Cannot Surpass Xia Hong, Moao South Slope Dominator (Part 4)

Cut off Mu Longhe’s arms, intimidate the Six Towns, and force them to reveal the truth about the deaths of those Three Towns Chamber of Commerce people, arguing that there were hidden motives; then take the opportunity to propose an investigation to uncover the truth, and demand that the Six Towns send personnel. In this way, Great Xia can establish prestige and clear its name before all, winning the trust of the people.

The entire process is meticulously interconnected and progresses layer by layer, not only successfully replacing Yang Zhun as the number one in the Nine Towns, but also elevating Great Xia above the Eight Towns, actually becoming the number one town in Moao. From this moment, Xia Hong has indeed become the dominator of Moao South Slope, a great Xia Hong, a great Great Xia Town!"

In South City Zhao’s Restaurant, Chu Yan’er looked extremely solemn.

After hearing her words, the five middle-aged men including Chen Yiqing were immediately lost in thought.

"Moao South Slope Dominator, really? Twelve Beast King Puppets, today Yang Zhun and others were unprepared, should the Six Towns truly unite, they wouldn’t fear Great Xia?"

Hearing Chen Yu’s retort, Chu Yan’er shook her head without responding.

Chen Yiqing’s brow furrowed slightly as he looked at his eldest son with dissatisfaction: "Yang Zhun and the others were unprepared, and Xia Hong didn’t employ any means either. Even though over thirty thousand troops seemed present, aside from the initial twenty Divine Machine Crossbows, they mostly just watched today, the confrontation was between Xia Hong and twelve Xianyang Level figures."

Chen Yu immediately realized the situation, his expression subtly changing.

Chen Yiqing did not bother with his son further but turned to Chu Yan’er: "What Xia Hong just mentioned as Sunlight Pill should be derived from cracking the formula of Yan Long Master’s Fire Melting Pills. I sent the Fire Melting Pills back at the end of last year, but they haven’t been produced yet. For Great Xia to make it so quickly, Xia Hong must have obtained Yan Long Master’s heart."

Many might not have noticed that of Great Xia’s over thirty thousand troops, more than eighty percent only have the Earth-digging Realm Limit Cultivation. It was daytime during the past two hours when people at this cultivation level supposedly cannot be exposed in sunlight.

When Xia Hong said the words Sunlight Pill, Chen Yiqing recalled the Fire Melting Pills from the beginning of the year at Dajue Temple.

Chu Yan’er nodded, speaking softly: "Not only Yan Long, but both Wu Sheng and Vajra Masters were also killed by Xia Hong, which means Great Xia may control more than just the Fire Melting Pills method; they likely have alternatives for Zen Gold Powder and Primordial Soul Pill as well."

Zen Gold Powder enhances skin membrane defenses, making one impervious to blades and guns; but the Primordial Soul Pill, I seem to remember Master Wu Sheng used it to kill in the end?"

Seeing Chu Yan’er, who just arrived from Chen Cang, being so well-informed about the Five Great Masters, was not strange to Chen Yiqing at all.

Last year he was part of the Seven Towns reinforcements, witnessing the entire process from Dajue Temple’s opening battle to its eventual downfall, he clearly understood the methods of the Five Masters, and as early as May this year, he had sent back the news to Chen Cang through Chen Huang and Li.

Chu Yan’er evidently read the intelligence he sent back before she came.

"I remember the methods of the Five Masters all require reading matched sutras to be effective, while Great Xia’s Sunlight Pill doesn’t need sutra recitation; it seems a bit more advanced."

Chen Yiqing did not answer Chu Yaner’s previous question but suddenly brought up the sutra matter with a somber expression.

Chu Yan’er was momentarily stunned by his words but soon understood, her expression growing increasingly serious as she pondered briefly.

"That means Zen Gold Powder and Primordial Soul Pill have likely been significantly improved too, adding three strategies to Xia Army at once. The crucial point is we still don’t know the specific effects of Wu Sheng Master’s Primordial Soul Pill!"

Having information gaps is sometimes deadly.

The Sunlight Pill is not surprising; Dan Medicine enhancing human defense like Zen Gold Powder isn’t absent in Chen Cang either; the key is what exactly the Primordial Soul Pill does, which must be confirmed!

Having just approached, Chen Yiqing already confirmed that those twelve Beast Kings are not true Cold Beasts but rather puppets with Beast King-level strength.

Beast King Puppets aren’t unfamiliar to Chu Yan’er.

But twelve beasts are enough to grab Chen Cang’s attention.

"This Xia Hong will certainly become a major threat to my Chen Cang!"

Chu Yan’er’s sudden assertion startled Chen Yiqing and the six present, making them look at her in disbelief.

"Little Princess, that’s a bit overdone?"

"Xia Hong is indeed strong among Xianyang Levels, and currently Great Xia can offer some remarkable tactics, but a major threat? Aren’t you overestimating him?"

"If the Blood Miasma Plain weren’t blocking in the middle, regardless of Xia Hong or Nine Towns, Moao Mountain’s South Slope would have long been a Chen Cang dependency."

...

Many immediately retorted, Chen Yiqing did not express disagreement but remained silent, waiting until others finished. Then, he suddenly seemed to remember something and turned to Chu Yan’er, asking: "Before Uncle Chen Yuanbai and two others returned to Chen Cang at the beginning of May, they told me Lord Landgrave intended to ally with Cai Qiu to cleanse the deceitful objects at Blood Miasma Plain. Dare I ask Little Princess, are you aware of this?"

"It’s not Lord Landgrave planning to ally with Cai Qiu, but Cai Qiu’s people seeking Lord Landgrave, wanting to team up with our Chen Cang!"

After being refuted by everyone, though Chu Yan’er knitted her brows slightly, she didn’t argue back. Until she heard Chen Yiqing’s question and promptly corrected his mistake.
