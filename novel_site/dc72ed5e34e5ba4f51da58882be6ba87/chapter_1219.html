Chapter 1219: Chapter 408: The Aging Li Tiancheng, a Powerful Team, Hidden Motives, and the Fusion of Three Towns (Part 3)

At this moment in the great hall, there are not only over six hundred armored Cold Resistance Level warriors, but also three people in casual attire sitting in chairs at the front row, namely Xiang Fuhai, Murong Chui, and Xiahou Zhang.

When Xia Hong’s family of four came out, their expressions clearly froze.

Not to mention Murong Chui and Xiahou Zhang, even Xiang Fuhai, who had stayed in Xia City for over four months, was seeing Xia Hong’s wife and children for the first time.

Like the rest of the six hundred people in the great hall, they immediately noticed Li Xuanling’s cultivation, and then looked at the two children in Xia Hong’s arms.

Thunk...

Since Murong Chui and Xiahou Zhang were sitting in chairs, their bodies shook suddenly, and they immediately made a clanging sound.

Amidst the observation of the crowd, Xia Hong had already taken a seat with Li Xuanling, holding his two children and sitting in the main seat, while Li Xuanling sat in the adjacent seat.

As soon as the four of them sat down, Xia Chuan immediately led the Eight Departments Director and bowed respectfully towards the main seat:

"Subordinates greet the Lord, greet the Lady, greet the two little lords!"

More than two hundred people in the main hall immediately bowed respectfully as well, saying: "We greet the Lord, greet the Lady, greet the two little lords!"

Clang...

Everyone in Great Xia was wearing war armor today, and the sound of friction was quite loud with so many people bowing at once. Moreover, when Xia Chuan and others performed the ritual, they clearly deliberately stimulated their vital energy, and the six hundred at the back followed their example, also stimulating their vital energy.

It’s worth noting, this time to intimidate the Six Towns, Xia Hong had summoned all of Great Xia’s elites to Hongmen City, and among these people, those qualified to come to the top floor of the Ministry Building to see him had highly noteworthy identities.

At this moment, in the great hall of over six hundred people, their positions are arranged according to the official ranks of Great Xia.

The front is Summer Minister Xia Chuan, followed by Yuwen Dao, Qiu Peng, Yuan Cheng, Xu Ning, Luo Yuan, Hou Jing, Li Tiancheng, Mu Dong, Cheng Feng, Bai Dongying, Zhu Yuan—11 Directors or Deputy Directors of the Eight Departments, the 12 currently only third rank or higher officials in Great Xia;

In the middle are Yue Feng, Lin Kai, Lu Yang, Liu Yuan, Li Yuankun, Xu An, Zhao Long, Zhao Hu, Zhao Bao, Shi Ping, Ying Yi, Tong Xinglong, Xu Shicheng, Hong Tian, Hong Gang, Hong Guang, Hou Quan, Hou Tong, Yuwen Yong, Meng Yi, Bai Wuting, Lin Fan, Zhu Shun, Wang Tao, Xiao Kangcheng... etc., over 200 people, are either directors of the Eight Departments in various districts of Xia City or at major stations, or army Commanders or Vice Commanders, all fourth rank and fifth rank officials of Great Xia;

Finally, the largest part with over 400 people, Zhu Xing, Wang Yuan, Hou Ming, Zhang Jiuling, Yuan Shan, Yuan Ning, Lu Yushan, Liu Chang, Tong Xingsheng, Hou Ying, Hou Bing, Hou Ming, Lin Ye, Zhou Yuan, Cheng He, Zhang Qingyuan, Lu Yin... either Deputy Directors of the Eight Departments or Defense Envoys, otherwise Colonels in the army, at minimum occupying important positions in the Eight Departments of Xia City, with a small portion being fifth rank, and the majority being sixth and seventh rank officials;

These over six hundred people are the core elites currently supporting the entire realm of Great Xia in every aspect.

The Ice Abyss’s foundation of respecting power will never change, so in Great Xia’s personnel appointments, capability and strength are evenly balanced, which is why Xia Hong frequently reminds Mu Dong and Cheng Feng to focus on enhancing their personal strength.

This also means that anyone who can secure an official position in Great Xia will certainly not have weak strength. Xia Hong only needed a slight sensation of their aura to understand the strength of over six hundred people present.

The further back, the weaker the strength, but even the weakest has Basic Strength above 15 Zong, with a few surpassing 20 Zong;

In the middle region, over 200 people have relatively large strength ranges, with people like Yue Feng, Lin Kai, the Zhao brothers, who have strengths exceeding or nearing 30 Zong, and others like Xiao Kangcheng, Lin Fan with over 20 Zong, but it’s certain that none have strength below 20 Zong;

Whereas the twelve at the front have polarizing strengths, with strong ones like Xia Chuan at 41 Zong, Yuwen Dao nearing 40 Zong, and Yuan Cheng, Lu Yang, Xu Ning, having received the Master’s Organ, now slowly catching up with, two at 39 Zong and one at 36 Zong, whereas the weakest Mu Dong astonishingly only has 18 Zong strength.

Over six hundred people with strengths of 15 Zong or above, deliberately stirred their vital energy to speak, and not only this main hall, but the entire Ministry Building shook violently.

The commotion was secondary, but the key was that so many people, even just speaking, caused the concentrated vital energy to create an alarming shock force.

It is nighttime, and many coal furnaces are lit on both sides of the hall, as the air stirred, the furnace flames instantly grew stronger, raising the hall’s temperature by several degrees abruptly.

Both Xia Yuyao and Xia Yusheng were experiencing such a scene for the first time, but their reaction, however, slightly surprised Xia Hong.

Both had their little faces tense, Xia Yusheng instinctively tightened his grasp on Xia Hong’s left arm, looking slightly nervous; while Xia Yuyao was also nervous, though visibly more excited, her big eyes bold in examining the people at the front.

Xia Hong patted her little head, slightly raising his brows, he did not signal for those in the hall to rise, but gently turned his head to look at the three people still steadily sitting in the chairs to the left.

A simple gesture did not necessarily need such a big motion.

Xia Chuan deliberately did it, and Xia Hong knew clearly what the purpose was in his heart.
