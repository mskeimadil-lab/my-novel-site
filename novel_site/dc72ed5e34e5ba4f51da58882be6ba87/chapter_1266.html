Chapter 1266: Chapter 418: Seizing the Opportunity, the Shock Brought by the Eldest Young Master, Hongmen Scroll_3

The first floor of the Martial Arts Hall is specifically for children between the ages of 6 and 15. At this age, very few have reached the extreme cultivation of the Earth-digging Realm and started to reshape the skin, with the majority being at the Logging Realm and Earth-digging Realm.

In these phases, improvement largely relies on absorbing and transforming the energy contained in beast meat to enhance one’s basic strength. Therefore, practicing punching to release internal energy is essential for cultivation in the Logging Realm and Earth-digging Realm.

As a result, compared to the second and third floors, the first floor is usually the busiest.

But today’s situation is completely different.

Currently, on the first floor, there’s only one child punching, surrounded by hundreds of children of various ages and teenagers, all forming a circle while watching the child with curiosity and shock in their eyes.

The child in the center practicing punches is wearing a custom-made jade-white summer outfit. From the softness of the fabric and the visual effect of its exquisite texture, it’s not difficult to see that the material is undeniably valuable;

He wears a jade-colored belt at his waist with a blood-colored jade ring hanging from it, complemented by white pants trimmed with silver thread embroidered with cloud patterns, and white bear boots. Clearly, he’s undergone the hair-grooming ceremony, as his hair is all tied up and secured with a pearl-adorned silver hairpin. Due to the intense punching, soft fuzz spreads across his forehead, making his eyebrows appear even more adorable and bright.

He looks at most six or seven years old. Despite his youthful features, being stared at by so many eyes at once, he doesn’t show the slightest sign of timidity, flawlessly executing a series of Long Fist moves from start to finish, smooth and delightful, displaying a level of proficiency that can rival many adults in Xia City.

"It seems... he broke through!"

"Broke through?"

"How is that possible?"

"Look, after finishing this set of punches, he directly broke through the Earth-digging Realm!"

"It’s true, he really broke through. Just now, when the Eldest Young Master came in, I clearly saw his basic strength was over 9800 pounds, and after the punch, he broke through?"

"This..."

Someone at the Cold Resistance Level was the first to notice the change in Xia Yuzong’s basic strength. Once he spoke up, everyone else reacted.

As more and more Cold Resistance Level observers saw the mighty power contained under Xia Yuzong’s tiny body, their pupils contracted sharply, followed by explosive exclamations throughout the first floor.

On his first day of turning six, entering the Martial Arts Pavilion, he broke through the Earth-digging Realm!

"I remember the quickest record for breaking through to the Earth-digging Realm in the Martial Arts Pavilion was eight years and nine months old, and the Eldest Young Master just broke it?"

"In the past three years, this isn’t just breaking records, it’s a dimensional attack!"

"And this is the Eldest Young Master; since he couldn’t cultivate until six, if he could cultivate earlier, wouldn’t he have broken through long ago?"

"This is truly the pride of Great Xia!"

"No wonder it’s the Eldest Young Master; I’m impressed."

"Not surprising, not surprising, a tiger father breeds no loser son. Rumor has it that the minister’s talent ranks just below the lord and lady in Great Xia. It’s normal for the Eldest Young Master’s talent to be high!"

"But this... it’s just too shocking!"

......

In the crowd, Xiang Liang, despite being mentally prepared long ago, couldn’t help but swallow his saliva, his pupils filled with relentless astonishment and excitement.

"I remember clearly, last October in the Inner City, seeing the Eldest Young Master, his basic strength was only over seven thousand pounds, and in just two months he broke through!"

Suddenly, Xiang Liang thought of something, a fiery sparkle lit up in his eyes.

"Spirit Milk Pill, it’s definitely Spirit Milk Pill. Last year the Alchemy Pavilion developed an extremely precious spirit pill that can be taken by children aged three to six, enhancing their basic strength without side effects. Minister does not lack Contribution Value, surely the Eldest Young Master enhanced using Spirit Milk Pill."

The fiery gleam in Xiang Liang’s eyes was naturally for Wang Yuxiu’s belly, his child who was still four months away from being born.

What parent wouldn’t want their child to become more exceptional?

In the Ice Abyss World, the standard for determining whether a child is exceptional weighs heavily on talent.

Thinking of the price of Spirit Milk Pills, Xiang Liang immediately felt a sense of urgency.

As he knew, the production of Spirit Milk Pills is extremely limited. After the Alchemy Pavilion developed it last year, the Camp Supply Department released only 100 pieces in the first batch, with a starting price of 50,000 Contribution Value per piece, and a person can bid for up to 5 pieces.

The news of Xia Chuan spending nearly a million Contribution Value to acquire 5 Spirit Milk Pills has long spread throughout Xia City.

Thinking of this matter, Xiang Liang’s fiery enthusiasm triggered by Spirit Milk Pills instantly cooled down. With his current financial strength, obtaining such a precious cultivation resource as Spirit Milk Pill was no different from a fool’s dream.

"Though the Camp Supply Department is offering more precious resources, obtaining them depends on one’s ability, and the Contribution Values of the minister and various directors were earned from their years of merit in battles for Great Xia. I can only chase after them with admiration!

Two years ago, risking by lurking in Lower River Village with Lord Xu already started changing my fate and allowed the Xiang Family to rise, but it’s still far from reaching Great Xia’s pinnacle. To continue improving, I cannot relax for a moment, every opportunity must be seized, not just for myself, but also for Yuxiu and the child she carries, and the future of the Xiang Family!"

Once calm, thoughts become clear; Xiang Liang soon found the key issue and understood his direction for hard efforts ahead.
