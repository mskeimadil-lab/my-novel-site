Chapter 874: Chapter 335: Songyang Ridge, Seven Houses Surrender, First Batch of Silver Arrives

The name "Songyang Ridge" was not given by Xu Ning; he discovered this place while leading a small team through the Snow Forest in May, and only after making contact with the locals did he learn of it.

It is called Songyang Ridge partly because of its relatively high terrain, and partly because the predominant vegetation on the ridge is a type of tree called Jade Pine.

The Jade Pine grows in a unique way; its trunk twists and turns upwards, reaching a height of no more than twenty meters. Though it appears malnourished, its branches are densely covered with leaves.

Moreover, the leaves are transparent and extremely smooth, resembling glass. Since snow does not accumulate on them, the entire ridge reflects intensely in daytime sunlight, making it blinding to the eye.

Of course, it is night right now, so this special scene cannot be seen.

"Lord, they are here!"

Hearing his brother Xu An’s reminder, Xu Ning nodded slightly. He had already seen a dozen people approaching from a mile away on the west side.

A total of sixteen people came from the west side. Upon discovering over twenty powerful presences and more than three hundred people clad in golden armor carrying strong bows on Xu Ning’s side, their pace noticeably slowed. Yet, led by a middle-aged man at the forefront, they continued to approach slowly.

"Sixteen people, with only two at the Earth-digging Realm. It seems all the Cold Resistance Level members of the Seven Families are here. Tonight will see a result."

"Those two at the Earth-digging Realm are women, and so young! Could they be planning to..."

......

As they observed Xu Ning and his team, Xu Ning naturally observed them as well.

Having discovered Songyang Ridge in May, Xu Ning had secretly sent people to thoroughly investigate the Seven Families of Songyang Ridge in the past three months. He knew well that each of the families had only two Cold Resistance Level members, and today all of them had come.

Yu Bin, wise with age, realized something when he saw the two young girls at the Earth-digging Realm, and a smile spread across his face.

Though he did not finish speaking, the others immediately understood, each showing a slight smile.

Only Xu Shicheng, the Chief of Punishment of the Prison Department, did not smile. Instead, his brows furrowed suddenly, and his gaze toward the sixteen people across the way became much darker.

......

Soon, the sixteen people from the west stood before Xu Ning.

It was evident that fear was apparent in their eyes, especially when their gaze rested on the fully armed three hundred people behind Xu Ning, and several audible gulps could be heard.

"Ji Yang from Wanlong, representing the Six Families of Songyang Ridge, greets Lord Xu!"

Despite having dealt with Xu Ning many times over the past three months, Ji Yang couldn’t help but feel a strong sense of dread as he carefully observed the more than three hundred people Xu Ning had brought tonight.

Twenty-seven at the Cold Resistance Level, while the remaining over two hundred were all at Earth-digging Realm Limit Cultivation. Some were already beginning the Reshaping Skin process, with dozens more appearing unstable, clearly on the verge of breaking through.

Is Great Xia truly this powerful?

Before, Xu Ning had sent him many great things from Great Xia—top-grade cotton, refined salt, Beast Spirit Pills, Blood Essence Pills... From these gifts, Ji Yang had already sensed the might of Great Xia, which is why he advocated for the other six families of Songyang Ridge to join Wanlong Camp in assimilating into Great Xia.

"As Xu Ning said, Wuyuan is merely an external outpost of Great Xia. His position as the Five Originals Guardian is a fourth-rank official status, ranking second to last in Great Xia. Yet he can effortlessly muster over twenty Cold Resistance Level individuals and three hundred at Earth-digging Realm Limit. Then the Third Rank, Second Rank, and First Rank above that must be..."

Recalling the information he had learned about Great Xia, Ji Yang felt numb with disbelief.

Power at this level far exceeded his understanding.

"Brother Ji, aren’t you going to introduce these gentlemen?"

Hearing Xu Ning’s words jolted Ji Yang back to reality, and he quickly addressed the six people beside him, "Lord Xu is the Five Originals Guardian of Great Xia, and it’s the first meeting for all of you, so please introduce yourselves. Brother Ji won’t speak on your behalf!"

The six immediately stepped forward, nodded, and bowed to Xu Ning:

"Wang He from Song Lake, greets Lord Xu."

"Nie Zichuan from Zhaolu, greets Lord Xu."

"He Qing from Songbei, greets Lord Xu."

"Zhu Ziyang from Iron Valley, greets Lord Xu."

"Wei Dong from Binhe, greets Lord Xu."

"Chai Biao from Qingsong, greets Lord Xu."

The strength of Songyang Ridge is indeed not significant. Among the seven Cold Resistance Level leaders, Ji Yang and Nie Zichuan are the strongest, with a Basic Strength barely reaching six manes. Judging by their skin membrane conditions, they are all at the Late Stage of Cold Resistance Level, their talents clearly lacking. The strength of the remaining five leaders is even weaker, between Three-maned and Five-maned levels.

The remaining seven Cold Resistance Level individuals are even weaker, with strength ranging from one to three manes.

Xu Ning glanced over the six and smiled slightly, saying, "You all have yet to join Great Xia, so addressing me as Lord is quite flattering. No need for such courtesy!"

Upon hearing Xu Ning state that they had not yet joined Great Xia, subtle changes flickered across the seven’s faces, and it was clear there were inner tremors.

Xu Ning caught the slight changes in their expressions and with a hint of curiosity in his eyes, said, "Though it is my first time meeting you all, Brother Ji has likely already conveyed the intentions of Great Xia. Since you are here today, I presume you have an answer in mind. Please speak frankly!"

Ji Yang did not expect Xu Ning to be so direct. After exchanging glances with the six leaders beside him, he finally stepped forward with some apprehension and cupped his hands, saying, "With Great Xia’s willingness to assimilate us, we certainly don’t mean to appear ungrateful. However, Lord Xu, please forgive my bluntness. The Seven Families of Songyang Ridge have a population totaling over 114,000, not a small number. If Great Xia offers no conditions, we..."
