Chapter 470 - 215: Teaching Luo Cheng Techniques, News of the Jade Bone (Part 3)

Chapter 470: Chapter 215: Teaching Luo Cheng Techniques, News of the Jade Bone (Part 3)

It’s just that all three Cold Beasts were cut in half, clearly handed over upon entry into the settlement.

"Uncle Yu, did you go hunting?"

Luo Cheng’s pupils dilated, his face full of surprise as he asked.

When his father Luo Ming led the team out hunting, the most they ever brought back was two beasts in a night. Even then, Luo Ming, despite his greatest contribution, only got over a hundred pounds personally.

Under normal circumstances, his father would get at most forty to fifty pounds of beast meat each night he went out.

Yet Xia Hong had three and a half Cold Beasts directly in his yard at this moment.

The key point was, yesterday, Xia Hong hadn’t prepared anything, nor had anyone visited him, and now he showed no signs of having gone out hunting.

What does this mean?

Gulp...

Luo Cheng looked up at Xia Hong, swallowing nervously.

"Use your knife, handle that Snow Mane, cook as much as we can eat!"

Seeing that Xia Hong clearly did not want to speak, Luo Cheng did not fuss, drew his knife, and began handling the Cold Beast.

Even though he just broke through to the Logging Realm, Luo Cheng obviously lacked experience, taking over half an hour and soaking in sweat to cut a few pieces of beast meat and start cooking.

After a while, as the beast meat was nearing readiness, Xia Hong added a handful of refined salt, the aroma spreading, and they started to feast heartily again.

"After eating the beast meat, take one pill, then start cultivation."

Just after finishing the beast meat, Luo Cheng saw a wooden bottle appear in front of him. Without asking Xia Hong, he opened the bottle to see the Dan Medicine inside and swallowed one immediately.

Xia Hong then took out a spear over a meter long from inside the house, walked to the yard, held his breath and mind, and began to practice.

Holding the spear with both hands, his whole demeanor changed instantly. The spear, accompanied by a sudden turn of his body, sharply carved a half-arc into the ground.

Swish...

A half-meter-high plume of dust rose abruptly, and a three to four-meter-wide arc-shaped groove appeared on the stone ground in front of Xia Hong, just by visual estimation, it was several tens of centimeters deep.

This left Luo Cheng momentarily dumbfounded.

"A month for the stick, a year for the knife, a lifetime for the spear..."

Seeing Luo Cheng staring at him intently, Xia Hong’s lips curled into a faint smirk as he continued talking to himself.

"The spear is the commander of a hundred weapons, primarily used for intercepting, holding, and stabbing. In finer terms, it involves seventeen techniques: chopping, crashing, penetrating, pointing, clouding, deflecting, lifting, wrapping, sweeping, blocking, throwing, swinging, withdrawing, bumping, twisting. Normally, mastering spear techniques takes at least ten years of practice, and with a bit more talent, at least three years...

As Xia Hong demonstrated and explained the seventeen spear techniques one by one, he continued, "In the three realms of Logging, Earth-Digging, and Cold Resistance, strength distinguishes power, so weapon use involves no great skill—just proficiency. To master all seventeen spear techniques, there is no shortcut, only daily persistence of ten thousand repetitions. Master one technique in a month, and you’re already considered talented. For the average person, it’s hard to keep up..."

With this final note, Xia Hong tossed the spear aside and headed back into the house.

"Thank you, Uncle Yu!"

Standing to the side, Luo Cheng bowed respectfully to Xia Hong before heading to the yard to pick up the spear.

But as soon as he took hold of it, he was nearly toppled by its weight.

"This spear, it’s so heavy!"

Luo Cheng’s hands trembled, estimating the spear to weigh at least fifteen hundred pounds, looking at the ten-forged silver patterns on the spear shaft, he was puzzled.

His father also used Ten-forged Weapons, given the size, the spear should at most weigh around six to seven hundred pounds, so how could it be so unexpectedly heavy?

"Ten thousand times, this..."

Recalling Xia Hong’s words, Luo Cheng’s face instantly showed difficulty, but upon seeing Xia Hong already inside, he gritted his teeth and began wielding it arduously.

"Ten thousand times it is, start with chopping..."

Inside the room, Xia Hong sat alone on a chair, watching Luo Cheng chop with the spear through the window, as his posture swiftly transformed from clumsy to adept, a slight smile appeared on his face.

"This kid is indeed most suited for the spear!"

For a period after that, Luo Cheng frequently visited Xia Hong, each time cooking beast meat without a word, eating dinner with Xia Hong, then taking a Beast Essence Pill and starting spear practice.

Luo Cheng’s Basic Strength was just five thousand pounds, trying to wave a fifteen-hundred-pound spear ten thousand times consecutively wasn’t easy initially.

He first practiced with chopping; on the first day, he managed a little over seven hundred times before becoming nearly exhausted. On the second day, he slightly improved, reaching over nine hundred times; the third day more, fourth, fifth...

It’s worth mentioning that Cheng Ping dropped by occasionally during this time.

Luo Cheng was aware of Cheng Ping being controlled by Xia Hong, watching this man who once wielded power at the city gate, often hassling his father, now bowing submissively before Xia Hong, Luo Cheng’s admiration for Xia Hong grew daily, and he became increasingly eager for power.

"I did it, I finally did it, hahaha!"

On February twentieth, or the twenty-fourth day since Xia Hong taught him spear techniques, Luo Cheng once again cut down heavily with the spear, despite his twitching and cramping arms, he lay on the ground laughing heartily.

Ten thousand times!

He really did it.

"Uncle Yu, I did it, hahaha, I did it!"

Xia Hong walked out of the house, observing the ecstatic Luo Cheng. Despite feeling impressed, he feigned indifference and said, "What’s so exciting? You’ve just managed ten thousand times. Keep it up for another month, then you’ll truly master the chopping technique."

Having a bucket of cold water poured over him, Luo Cheng’s spirit immediately dampened but soon rekindled his determination, nodding resolutely, "I understand, Uncle Yu. I will keep pushing until I master it fully."

This kid probably doesn’t even know how much his Basic Strength has increased!

The amazement in Xia Hong’s eyes wasn’t solely because Luo Cheng achieved ten thousand spear chops, but because, by observing his Qi Muscle, he knew Luo Cheng’s Basic Strength had exceeded six thousand pounds.

In just over a month, with the aid of the Beast Essence Pill and salt, Luo Cheng’s innate talent should not be underestimated.

An increase of a thousand pounds in Basic Strength in a month, if he remembered correctly, besides himself, only a few others like Xia Chuan and Yuan Cheng were capable of achieving this.

This kid’s talent is indeed not low!

"Hong Yu, is Hong Yu here?"

Just then, a familiar voice suddenly came from outside the door.

Xia Hong raised an eyebrow slightly, gesturing to Luo Cheng on the ground.

Luo Cheng quickly got up to open the door, welcoming the person outside.

"Sir, there’s a situation!"

The person entering was Cheng Ping, and upon seeing Xia Hong, he immediately saluted with a bow.

"Luo Cheng, return home first, come back tomorrow."

"Yes, Uncle Yu!"

Hearing about the situation, a glimmer of interest appeared in Xia Hong’s eyes, signaling Luo Cheng to leave before leading Cheng Ping inside.

"Sir, our leader just transported hundreds of thousands of pounds of coal stone yesterday, he likely intends to boil Jade Bone. Today I saw a man in black with him, one I’ve never seen before, should be the outsider you mentioned."

"Where’s the place for boiling Jade Bone, can I go there?"

Cheng Ping looked troubled upon hearing this, explaining, "The top floor is where the boiling takes place, and besides the Great Leader, second leader, and Commander Chen, no one else can go up without summons."

Xia Hong pondered for a moment, then said, "Take me to the main building first, I’ll check it out myself."

Despite his hesitation, Cheng Ping had to follow Xia Hong, who was already leading the way.
