Chapter 307: Chapter 167: Seizing the Mirror, He Yuanhun’s Confidence

From the moment he emerged, the Mirror Immortal Camp was no longer a threat.

The reason Xia Hong had this confidence was that after breaking through the Cold Resistance Level, his Basic Strength had increased to an unimaginable extent.

Nine-maned Power!

With a strength of 900,000 jin, his Skin Remodeling was perfect. At the moment of emergence, realizing the terrifying power within himself, Xia Hong was stunned.

According to the earlier description by Gao Yu, that is, He Yuanhun’s explanation.

At the Earth-digging Realm’s limit of 30,000 jin, using Beast Blood to start Reshaping Skin, an ordinary person at this stage would only have a power increase of about 20,000 jin. Those with Battle Body Talent could reach much higher levels, but according to his description of upper, middle, and lower tiers, the power increase would be at most 70,000, 50,000, and 30,000 jin.

Once Skin Remodeling is complete, achieving a Leakless Body, and officially breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, the power could double again.

Even by this standard, the most powerful Superior Battle Body could only reach two manes of power, which is 200,000 jin, after breaking through the Cold Resistance Level.

Of course, Xia Hong didn’t completely believe Gao Yu’s words.

He had roughly guessed that when He Yuanhun came to the camp to gather intelligence, he intentionally said those words to entice him to use an excessive amount of Beast Blood, hoping to affect his breakthrough process or even kill him.

Unfortunately, He Yuanhun didn’t expect that he could use system resources to offset the nerve pain brought by Skin Remodeling.

Before the last batch of Beast Blood was collected, Xia Hong’s skin remodeling progress had already reached 70%, and his Basic Strength was about 120,000 jin at that time.

Based on his estimates, with 50 jin for one progress point, and each point increasing strength by 1250 jin, the subsequent 30 progress points should only require 1500 jin of Beast Blood, with a strength increase of around 37,000 jin.

But the closed-door training process was completely different from his predictions.

As the Skin Remodeling progress increased, the amount of Beast Blood used at once grew larger, and the strength increase was more significant. Fortunately, he had prepared nearly 2000 jin of Beast Blood, using system resources to counteract nerve strain, continually operating at maximum capacity, otherwise, achieving perfect Skin Remodeling would have been questionable.

At the moment Skin Remodeling reached 100% progress, he sensed it, and his Basic Strength had already skyrocketed to 300,000 jin.

According to He Yuanhun, once the body was fully remodeled, achieving a Leakless Body, Basic Strength could double again.

Based on this calculation, indeed, his strength was 600,000 jin.

Six-maned Power, Xia Hong was already quite satisfied.

Gao Yu’s description of a Superior Battle Body would yield only two manes of power after the breakthrough.

He was already three times that, what was there to be dissatisfied with?

Never did he expect, after truly achieving a Leakless Body and breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, his Basic Strength would increase not just double, but a full three times!

From 300,000 jin, it directly soared to 900,000 jin.

Nine-maned Power!

Limited by his knowledge, Xia Hong was unsure if his situation was unique in the Ice Abyss or if there was some special term for it.

But it was certain that his potential far exceeded expectations.

Compared to a normal Cold Resistance Level with only one mane of power, his strength was nothing short of crushing.

Even against a normal Cold Resistance Realm, as long as the numbers were not overwhelming, facing many alone with Xia Hong’s strength posed no problem at all.

This was the real reason behind Xia Hong’s confidence.

...

Poof!

The Yi Blade stabbed into He Yuanhun’s left eye, the Solidified Fire Oil on the blade instantly igniting, Xia Hong’s gaze sharpened, and his body lunged forward, channeling the blade’s power to the extreme.

Swoosh...

A blade of one and a half meters almost entirely stabbed into He Yuanhun’s left eye.

Strangely, He Yuanhun’s pupil seemed connected to some Different Space, Xia Hong’s blade hilt had already pressed against his face, yet the tip did not pierce through the back of his head.

But fortunately, the reaction between that Different Space and the Solidified Fire Oil was still intense.

He Yuanhun’s eyes were already emitting thick smoke, seemingly corresponding to two mirrors in the sky, and after Xia Hong thrust the Yi Blade inside, the smoke intensified several times over.

Simultaneously, He Yuanhun’s screams grew even more intense compared to before.

"Ah... ah..."

After He Yuanhun’s miserable howl, he quickly retreated, trying to escape the Yi Blade.

But Xia Hong wasn’t about to let him have his way.

Swoosh...

Xia Hong suddenly pulled out the Yi Blade from his left eye, his legs stomping fiercely against He Yuanhun’s chest.

Boom...

With Xia Hong fully unleashing his Nine-maned Power, that kick directly stomped He Yuanhun through the bamboo floor, his body bulldozed into the ground like a cannonball.

Before He Yuanhun could react, the Yi Blade Xia Hong had just pulled out shot back toward his right eye like lightning.

Poof...

The Yi Blade accurately pierced the right eye, and a vast amount of thick smoke erupted instantly.

"Ah... ah......"

Unlike the previous sharp, ear-piercing screams, after the Yi Blade pierced his second eye, He Yuanhun’s screams became suddenly fearful and weak.

The black smoke billowing from his two pupils gradually thinned.

Correspondingly, the two ancient mirrors in the sky stopped emitting thick smoke, and the flames on them were shrinking, seemingly on the verge of extinguishing.

Ding...

A crisp sound of a mirror striking came from mid-air.

Xia Hong looked up, his expression slightly shifting.

As the flames extinguished, the two mirrors seemed to lose their essence, gradually shrinking in the air until they became two palm-sized mirrors, one black and one white.

Then, the two mirrors began to slowly fall.
