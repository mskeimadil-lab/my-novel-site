Chapter 912: Chapter 343: 5 Rounds of Bone Tempering, 1000-Strand Bone Burning in the Visible Yang Realm (Part 3)

Above the entire Star-picking Hall, it was as if a nuclear explosion had occurred, directly forming a vacuum.

"More than a thousand strands, no, the punch I delivered carried a scorching high temperature capable of melting everything. This is a completely different nature of power compared to before; describing it with strands isn’t enough, and moreover..."

While Xia Hong was testing his strength, he noticed that his bones had not shattered, and the surface cracks on his bones didn’t even show a trace of fluctuation, his face suddenly full of delight.

"Indeed, these should not be cracks, but a bone form higher than Jade Bone. The strength of my bones is completely fine. With my current rough estimation, the strength is at least twenty times that of normal Iron Bone. The self-healing ability remains to be verified, but my control over the bones..."

Xia Hong suddenly opened his eyes. He was already in a suspended state, with his legs slightly moving in midair, and with a flicker, he appeared over a hundred meters in the sky above the Star-picking Platform.

"As expected, the secret of Xianyang Level sky control is to control the overall weight of bones and flesh in a very short period, then utilize airflow and explosive momentum. In this sense, the greater the control over flesh and bones, the stronger the sky control ability, and the faster the speed."

Just now, it took him the time of one breath to flash.

One breath is more than two seconds, let’s say three seconds, a hundred meters in three seconds, meaning that in full-speed sky control flight, he can reach over thirty meters per second.

This speed can already crush all ground-bound Cold Resistance Levels.

"Indeed, even without the latter four rounds of Bone Tempering, relying solely on the strength of the Iron Bone Stage is enough to crush all Cold Resistance Levels. Even at my Peak of Cold Resistance, facing my Iron Bone Stage self, I would have no power to resist, let alone other Cold Resistance Levels. Xianyang Level, this is Xianyang Level!"

Floating in midair, Xia Hong slightly clenched his fist, his eyes full of joy.

The realm originally expected to take decades to break through took only five years.

Moreover, his breakthrough was exceptional; a normal Xianyang Level probably couldn’t even touch the edge of his.

"Since my bones are fine, it proves that above Jade Bone, there really is a higher-level bone form, dark red in color, with veins like lava, capable of forming fire externally when fully released. I’ll name it Bone Burning, and from now on, let’s see if there are other people in Great Xia who can reach this step!"

Xia Hong took a deep breath, looking at the flames on his body surface, still blazing intensely, and feeling the strong power within his bones that desperately needed release, his expression slightly condensed.

"Having just broken through, the power hasn’t stabilized yet. I need to find a place to fully release it, and also learn how to control it smoothly. Otherwise, being as conspicuous as a furnace wherever I go is unacceptable!"

After a moment of contemplation, Xia Hong turned his head to look north of Xia City.

"The Beast Tide in the Arrow Bamboo Forest is happening right now, and Xia Chuan is there with the Six Armies. It’s perfect to go take a look!"

Whoosh...

The fireball above the Star-picking Platform flew directly north with a whoosh.

Below, Zhao Long, Hong Gang, the Zhao Family descendants, Hong Ye, and many other Great Xia people all stared dumbfounded at the fireball, unable to come back to their senses for a long time.

"How did all the snowflakes in the sky just disappear?"

"Was that shockwave created by the Lord?"

"I saw the cold air above the Inner City completely dissipated!"

"It wasn’t a punch, right? Could a person create that shockwave?"

"Definitely mistaken, the Lord must have used some means."

"The shockwave might not have been created by the Lord, but he really did fly in the sky just now! The Lord can fly?"

"Not only can he fly, but his speed is astonishing!"

"The Lord must have broken through!"

"One hundred percent, to Xianyang Level."

........

Xia Hong had been floating in the sky the entire time.

The younger generation and those with inferior Earth-digging Realm abilities simply couldn’t see him clearly, let alone his punching action. They could only guess, and most people didn’t believe the recent commotion was man-made.

They only determined Xia Hong’s cultivation breakthrough from his final sky control flight posture, their faces full of envy and excitement.

Unlike them, Zhao Long, Hong Gang, and all other Cold Resistance Levels were all silent at this moment.

Clearly seeing everything, their pupils were filled with horror and shock at this moment.
