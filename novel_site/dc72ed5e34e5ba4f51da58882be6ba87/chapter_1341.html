Chapter 1341: Chapter 436: Qingxun Treasure Tree, Black Wind General and Toll, Blood and Rescue (Part 3)

However, this time, the opponent clearly reacted.

A big saber shrouded in black fog suddenly slashed horizontally with the airflow, precisely colliding with Xia Hong’s longsword.

Boom......

With the collision, Xia Hong’s body shot back more than ten meters, while the opponent’s black blade only caught some sparks from the Solidified Fire Oil.

"Humph!"

A cold snort emerged from the black fog, and soon the part touched by fire was stripped away along with the airflow.

"Strength surpasses mine, a hundred jun, probably even more..."

Retreating more than ten meters away, Xia Hong’s face was full of solemnity, especially when he saw the opponent could strip away the Solidified Fire Oil, his pupils contracted slightly.

"What’s your toll?"

A dull voice once again echoed from the black fog, and finally, a Mysterious Armor Giant, six meters tall, emanating black mist and dressed like a general, came into Xia Hong’s view.

It’s not unusual for deceitful monsters to communicate, so hearing the opponent’s question, Xia Hong did not show any surprise, he only furrowed his brow slightly and asked, "I am new here, may I ask what the toll is?"

Whoosh......

The Mysterious Armor Giant was obviously impatient, and hearing Xia Hong’s question, two gusts of air spurted from his face, his body swelled visibly, and then he suddenly raised his big saber and charged at Xia Hong.

Woo woo woo......

As the Black Wind General moved his saber, the entire Bone-Eroding Path’s black airflow also began to move, seemingly hundreds or thousands of black blades simultaneously slashing at Xia Hong from mid-air.

The airflow impact within this Bone-Eroding Path casually carries two or three jun, here being the core area, the force is basically above five jun, this is equivalent to a thousand Xianyang level beings with the power of five jun each, coupled with one person whose strength might exceed a hundred jun, simultaneously slashing at him.

As thousands of blades attacked, Xia Hong’s pupils contracted sharply, his face turned pale, and without a moment’s hesitation, he directly backed away to the north to evade.

"Run, can you escape, little reptile!"

The Black Wind General let out a muffled laugh, and the blades that were five or six meters away from Xia Hong suddenly disappeared, reappearing above Xia Hong’s head the next second, slashing downwards mercilessly.

"Teleportation, no, it can freely change position within the black fog!"

Xia Hong’s expression drastically changed, seeing the Black Wind General’s form gathering behind the blades, he instantly understood its shifting mechanism.

But seeing this now did him no good.

Seeing the blades descending from above, Xia Hong’s face showed a look of determination, he stopped his body, and at the same time, swiftly gripped the sword blade with both hands, stomped his feet fiercely on the ground, straightened his waist, and raised the sword horizontally in front of him as a block.

Clang......

As the black blade pressed down, Xia Hong was nearly driven halfway into the ground, and the Shattered Star Sword also got pressed from its position at the top directly to his chest, breaking through flesh, digging down three or four inches, almost reaching the bone.

Hmm......

Above a hundred jun, definitely above a hundred jun!

Xia Hong groaned, confirming the Black Wind General’s strength, his pupils filled with fear and dread, watching thousands of blades close in with the black blade, he hesitated no longer, with his left eye suddenly emitting a powerful light, which shot rapidly towards the face of the Black Wind Monster General.

The Black Wind General’s intention was clear, to pin Xia Hong’s body and let the thousands of black blades deliver a fatal blow, so even when he saw the strong light shooting from Xia Hong’s pupils, he didn’t move, intending to withstand it.

Shh......

Unfortunately, as the strong light struck his face, he regretted it instantly.

"Ah... ah......."

The Shattering Divine Light shot directly at him, the Black Wind General first let out a scream, then realized something, and his voice became sharply pained, his six-meter-tall burly form, originally converted from black mist, began to quickly scatter after being hit by the Shattering Divine Light.

"Qishan Demon Lord... Qishan Demon Lord’s Yin Yang Mirror, how do you have this thing, how is it possible, ah... ah......"

The Black Wind General retracted his saber, howling incessantly, his voice filled with terror, in a frenzy stripping the black mist trying to sustain his dissipating form, his once six-meter-tall figure quickly shrank to just over five meters.

Whoosh......

And on Xia Hong’s side, once the Black Wind General retracted his saber, he immediately endured the immense pain in his chest, dashing madly towards the north attempting to flee.

"Don’t think you can escape, don’t think you can escape, the Yin Yang Mirror, the Yin Yang Mirror, hahaha..."

Sadly, Xia Hong underestimated the Black Wind General’s power, in less than four or five breaths, he completely stripped away the part of his body affected by the Shattering Divine Light, though his body shrank to just over four meters, with several manic laughs, black mist from all directions gathered once more, soon restoring his body to six meters.

The Black Wind General kept murmuring about the Yin Yang Mirror, his voice filled with greed, even with a hint of madness, his body trembled vigorously, then directly chased after Xia Hong.

"Bring the Yin Yang Mirror to my lady, there will certainly be a great reward!"

..........

"If it weren’t for quickly reporting Cai Qiu’s name, I might not have escaped, why did the Black Wind General directly appear?"

At the northern exit of the Bone-Eroding Path, Zhu Yan, who just emerged, turned to look at the black mist-covered path, his pupils filled with fear and confusion.

"Is the Black Wind General intending to let the Bone-Eroding Path continue expanding north, this can’t happen, I must quickly report back to Lord Governor!"
