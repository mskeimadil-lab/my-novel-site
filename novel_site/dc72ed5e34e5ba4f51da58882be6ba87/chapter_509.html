Chapter 509: Chapter 228: Justified Campaign, The Infiltration Unveiled (Part 3)

But never expected, on the fifth day after he sent out the letter.

That is, on April 20th of the Second Year of Great Xia.

The already dead Lu Sheng actually returned to the Hive with four others!

On the night the letter was sent, Lu Sheng was beheaded by Yue Feng himself. His four subordinates, one died and three were severely injured, all witnessed by Luo Ming. When he saw Lu Sheng come back, he thought it was some trick causing it and was terrified.

Later, when Lu Sheng himself came looking for him, he found out it was Xia Chuan, and among the four people following Xia Chuan were his old subordinates Luo Yuan and Zhao Long.

Luo Yuan and Zhao Long had actually broken through to the Cold Resistance Level!

Luo Ming’s excitement and joy at that time can be put aside for now. It was Xia Chuan’s step-by-step deployment that was the reason for his current inner turmoil.

On April 20th, infiltrating the Hive, Xia Chuan, after figuring out the passage situation, immediately sent Luo Yuan and Zhao Long to control the mining area behind Passage 19, and then ordered Yuan Cheng and Hong Tian to control an exit at the southernmost end of the Hive.

In the subsequent three days, the people of Great Xia, gathered in Jing Valley, poured continuously into the Hive underground through that exit, and they headed straight for the mining area, actually infiltrating completely unnoticed by the Main Zone side.

Originally, according to Xia Chuan’s plan, it was to seize the Main Zone entrance with thunderous means and then control the entire Hive Camp in one fell swoop. But no one expected that on April 23rd, Zhaoyang Camp attacked in advance.

Xia Chuan responded extremely quickly, immediately realizing that this was an excellent opportunity to weaken both the Hive and Zhaoyang camps, and it could allow Great Xia to take the Hive at the lowest cost and speed.

"The layout of the Hive Station foreordains that whenever external forces invade, they will definitely change the passage numbers and then release wolf smoke to smoke people out. Others will surely have to cover their faces to resist, which is our perfect opportunity!"

Xia Chuan’s actions towards the Hive were always spot on with his predictions.

Chen Yingba released the wolf smoke, and both Zhaoyang and Hive personnel began to cover their faces with wet cloths, just perfectly facilitating Great Xia to fish in troubled waters.

"We’ve truly done it; now it’s just how to communicate with Chen Yingba!"

Luo Ming looked back and saw the six people still communicating at the end, a trace of expectation arising in his heart.

If Great Xia could truly take the Hive in one fell swoop, then he and Li Baihe would definitely be greatly credited, and rewards were certainly inevitable, along with assured status upon returning to Great Xia!

"Brothers, Zhaoyang killed so many of us, now that we have brothers from Great Xia here to help, what are we waiting for, charge!"

Suddenly, Chen Yingba spoke up from behind.

As soon as he spoke, it instantly ignited the already tense atmosphere.

"That’s right, with so many helping us, what are we afraid of?"

"They killed so many of us, let Zhaoyang’s bastards pay with blood!"

"Brothers, charge."

"Encircle from front and back, brothers, charge with me......"

.........

The hatred accumulated in the hearts of the Hive people over the past two days exploded completely with Chen Yingba’s order.

Whoosh whoosh whoosh......

A round of dense arrow rain was released from the Hive’s side, and the dark crowd surged up, tidal waves of furious roars and howls filled the entire Main Zone Space in an instant.

"Fire!"

Whoosh whoosh whoosh......

What’s worrying is that over a thousand from Great Xia, under Yuwen Dao’s orders, also released the long-tense bowstrings.

The two volleys of arrow rain instantly overwhelmed the people of Zhaoyang.

Pft...pft......

Two rounds of arrow rain swept through, and Zhaoyang, which only had over eight hundred left, instantly lost another quarter, and seeing that the arrow rain from behind hadn’t stopped yet, Hou Ming’s eyes immediately filled with rage as he roared and rushed into the Main Zone.

"Charge inside, fight them to the death!"

"First kill Chen Yingba, take the Hive."

Wu Tianxing, Hou Bing, Hou Ying, and the injured Lin Ye followed closely behind, the surviving people of Zhaoyang, realizing this was their last chance for survival, also rushed into the Hive people within the Main Zone.

The forces of Great Xia, whether in strength, attire, or spirit, were clearly much stronger than those on the Hive side.

Charging inside was their only choice!

"Keep shooting, wait for my order to advance."

In front of the iron gate, Yuwen Dao, seeing Zhaoyang’s choice, a cold light rose in his eyes, and he whispered to the few people beside him, commands were passed one by one.

The over a thousand people of Great Xia, all motionless, continued to draw bows and shoot at the charging Zhaoyang people.

.........

"Commander Chen indeed is a wise man, rest assured, after exterminating Zhaoyang’s people, Great Xia guarantees, especially will not harm the Hive, only waiting for Leader Chen to return from Long Mountain, you and I will immediately sign a covenant, and henceforth become allied nations, watching over and helping each other!"

At the very end of the Hive, Xia Chuan, ignoring Chen Yingba’s gloomy expression, smilingly proposed to him, and the four surrounding Luo Yuan all revealed knowing smiles.

It’s just that the bright smiles on the faces of the five, contrasting sharply with Chen Yingba’s gloomy face.

No matter how you look at it, it seems odd.
