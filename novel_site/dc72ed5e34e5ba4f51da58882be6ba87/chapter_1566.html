Chapter 1566: Chapter 492: Invisible Assassins, Foolishness, Calamity and Panic

More than a hundred breaths ago, on the east side of Milk Tiger Forest, at the Dragon Forbidden Captain’s army.

"To the north, be careful!"

Cheng Guang’s warning made Meng Ying and Tie Xinchuan, along with other Dragon Forbidden soldiers, all turn their heads to the north, and once they saw the scene on the north side, their pupils contracted abruptly.

The continuous barrage of powerful arrows from thousands of Dragon Forbidden soldiers nearly covered the sky above the caravan for hundreds of meters. You must know that these soldiers all have Basic Strength above 25 Manes; the power of the arrows they shoot simultaneously is quite extraordinary.

At this moment, up in the sky, not only snowflakes, but even the air currents are completely dispersed by the arrow winds.

But there is a small space in the sky tens of meters to the north, where clearly there is nothing at all, yet no matter what, the arrow barrage can’t break through it. The arrows, after being shot up, seem to hit an invisible wall, falling to the ground simultaneously.

"Is it a deceit technique?"

Whoosh......

"Why are you wasting time talking? Light the Yellow Candle!"

Meng Ying reacted the fastest. While Cheng Guang and others were still suspicious, he had already drawn his saber and fiercely struck that area, simultaneously signaling others to quickly light the Yellow Candle.

The Yellow Candle can not only be used for sacrifices, enhancing the divinity of the Spiritual Tablet, but also within its luminous range after being lit, any deceptive techniques will become ineffective.

Cheng Guang, Jiang Ping, the two Xianyang Level, along with Tie Xinchuan and all Dragon Forbidden soldiers suddenly reacted, quickly taking out Yellow Candles from their bodies, intending to light them.

Yellow Candles are essential for every Dragon Forbidden soldier.

To say their movements were already fast enough, in less than half a breath, hundreds of Yellow Candles were lit simultaneously, illuminating the entire area around the caravan for hundreds of meters.

But the luminous range of the Yellow Candle fire is only about three meters in radius, which means that its reach in the skies at most is three meters high.

Clank......

And as Meng Ying charged out, when his saber touched that area blocking the arrow rain, he sensed a massive force ahead of the blade, his expression instantly changed.

Someone used weapons to block him, he could even feel two presences far stronger than him in front, yet he couldn’t see anyone at all.

"Invisibility technique, it’s those Xianyang Levels from the Feudal Town, damn it!"

Bam......

As Meng Ying’s mind reacted, his chest suddenly received a heavy hit, his body fell from mid-air towards the ground, making a loud thud.

"Ugh... Cheng Guang, Jiang Ping, get into the caravan and protect His Highness closely!"

Meng Ying suppressed the blood boiling in his heart, fiercely ordering Cheng Guang and Jiang Ping, and then didn’t dare to delay any further, quickly getting up and rushing towards the caravan.

But at the crucial moment, no matter how fast he was, it was too late.

When Meng Ying was kicked down, the two invisible figures had already dashed towards the caravan, almost in an instant reaching above the caravan.

Just Cheng Guang and Jiang Ping, as they always stuck close to the caravan, had enough speed. Upon hearing Meng Ying’s command, they immediately lifted the carriage curtains ahead of time and rushed inside.

However, the opponents ultimately arrived!

Those two invisible individuals, although close to the caravan top, had also entered the illumination range of the Yellow Candle light, hence their invisibility technique was naturally nullified.

The two figures in black clothing, one carrying a saber, one holding a sword, both relatively tall. The person on the left constantly emitted thin white smoke, enclosing both himself and the person on the right tightly.

Both were currently head down, with only their upper bodies within the Yellow Candle’s luminous range, thus revealing themselves, while the higher lower bodies remained outside the Yellow Candle’s reach, hence still invisible.

Clearly, that white smoke was the crucial point of the invisibility technique!

The two invisible figures, realizing their presence was revealed, now had expressions slightly flustered. Yet, with their target so close, naturally, they couldn’t retreat.

They gritted their teeth, accelerating towards the caravan.

"Fire arrows to drive them back!"

Though Tie Xinchuan’s reaction wasn’t as quick as Meng Ying’s trio, upon seeing the figures attached to the caravan top, he promptly shouted for everyone to fire arrows.

The bows and crossbows of Dragon Forbidden Captain were never stopped, it’s just that many didn’t react as quickly as those two invisible figures, hence their arrows missed.

Upon hearing Tie Xinchuan’s command, they promptly turned their bows, shooting at the now-visible invisible figures.

This was already the last chance; within the caravan sat Xia Yusheng, if those two attached to the caravan, their bows and crossbows would become completely ineffective.

All Dragon Forbidden soldiers naturally understood this, so after firing arrows, they didn’t look at those figures but directly threw down their strong bows, drew their blades, and charged fiercely towards the caravan.

Clank... clank......

Admittedly, this last round of angry arrow barrage still served a purpose; if Dragon Forbidden’s numbers were slightly fewer, those two Xianyang Levels probably wouldn’t fear. Yet, now almost all Dragon Forbidden soldiers were present.

Thousands of silver-made Ten Thousand Forged Arrows shot all at once. The pupils of the two invisible Xianyang Levels contracted abruptly.

"I’ll block, you break into the carriage and catch the target!"

The person on the right shouted angrily, wildly swinging the saber forming a blade curtain more than two meters in diameter, directly shielding the other person, helping to block most of the arrow rain.

Thud thud thud......

Arrows from single or fewer Cold Resistance Levels, even if Ten Thousand Forged and silver-made, cannot pierce a Xianyang Level’s bones, but hundreds and thousands of them, especially fired by Dragon Forbidden soldiers whose Basic Strength all exceed 25 Manes, the result changes completely.
