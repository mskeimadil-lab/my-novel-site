Chapter 875 - 335: Songyang Ridge, Seven Houses Surrender, First Batch of Silver Arrives

Chapter 875: Chapter 335: Songyang Ridge, Seven Houses Surrender, First Batch of Silver Arrives

"It seems you all came prepared today. Brother Ji, speak directly. I have already said that any requests you have, feel free to propose. As long as it’s within my authority and does not violate the current rules and regulations of Great Xia, I can promise on behalf of Great Xia!"

Xu Ning’s straightforward attitude only made Ji Yang more apprehensive. After exchanging glances with the six people beside him, he slowly spoke and proposed a total of five conditions.

"First, all the resources that our seven families have previously stored, we will take them out and distribute them to everyone. Great Xia must ensure that this property remains untouched;

Second, for the iron and coal mines owned by the seven families, we seven wish to sell them to Great Xia in exchange for the Contribution Value that Lord Xu mentioned. Of course, this Contribution Value will still be distributed to our people below, so they can have time to adapt after moving to Great Xia;

Third, for our 14 people at the Cold Resistance Level, each person requires 10 Yangyuan Pills, and for the 129 people at the Earth-digging Realm Limit, each person requires 100 catties of Beast Blood;

Fourth, since the 14 of us have chosen to join, we naturally hope to have some achievements in Great Xia in the future. Therefore, we earnestly request the Lord to give the seven of us some minor positions in the camp. Of course, positions as high and powerful as Lord Xu’s are beyond our imagination; lower positions will suffice;

As for this fifth condition, it is....."

When he got to the fifth point, Ji Yang suddenly stopped.

It wasn’t because the people of Great Xia reacted in any way. Xu Ning and the others had been quietly listening, their expressions showing no fluctuations throughout.

"Ji Yun, Nie Rou, come to the front!"

Only then did the two Earth-digging Realm girls slowly step forward.

The two girls were both about fifteen or sixteen years old, and their looks were indeed exceptional. It was also evident that they were deliberately dressed up. However, the conditions in Songyang Ridge were truly limited, with a light red cotton dress paired with a snow-white Beast Fur jacket. They shone with youthfulness, and besides, whatever was applied to their faces made their skin look less dull than ordinary people’s, indeed making Xu Ning and the others’ eyes light up slightly.

"This is my daughter Ji Yun and Leader Nie’s daughter Nie Rou. I, Ji, boldly wish to propose a marriage alliance for the two of them, allowing them to marry the le..."

"Outrageous!"

Before Ji Yang could finish, he was loudly interrupted by Xu Shicheng to Xu Ning’s left.

Xu Shicheng clearly spoke with anger. With his Late-stage Cold Resistance cultivation, boasting a basic strength of eight 鬃, he fully unleashed his aura, startling all the Cold Resistance Level individuals at Songyang Ridge. The two girls turned pale, their bodies trembling slightly.

Ji Yang couldn’t understand why Xu Shicheng would react with such anger. Seeing that Xu Ning did not speak and realizing that Xu Shicheng’s status was not simple, his heart skipped a beat, feeling a bit of regret.

Yet, he felt more confusion.

The first four conditions were all about asking for something, but this last condition was supposed to be them offering something to Great Xia, a sign of their pledge. Why such a big reaction?

"Lord Xu, our Great Xia’s expansion and annexation outward is certainly not reliant on marriage alliances. For someone like the Lord, in the future, there will undoubtedly be many camps wishing to send women his way. If he takes everyone who comes, the household can hardly remain peaceful, and the lady might not be happy. I think this fifth condition should be omitted!"

..........

This Xu Shicheng, he must be someone from Lady’s side.

Ji Yang instantly realized, but after exchanging a glance with Nie Zichuan beside him, he gritted his teeth and continued to speak to Xu Ning:

"Xu Shouzheng, though Great Xia is powerful, our seven families of Songyang Ridge did not fare poorly here. If the lord is unwilling to agree to these five conditions, our seven families will not merge into Great Xia..."

"Heh!"

Swish...

Xu Shicheng directly let out two cold laughs. The two people standing behind him, along with the more than forty elite members of the Qingmang Hunting Team at the rear, all drew their weapons, glaring at Ji Yang and the other fourteen.

"If you don’t agree, that’s better. We might as well take care of you all together right now, saving a large batch of cultivation resources!"

"Lord Xu, please stay calm!"

With shock and anger across their faces, Xu Ning finally spoke as Ji Yang and the others were full of it.

He first signaled Xu Shicheng to calm down, then turned to glance at the two girls before finally looking at Ji Yang, speaking in a deep voice, "Brother Ji, we have known each other for three months. Songyang Ridge cannot continue to delay. I won’t hide it from you; tonight, no matter what your seven families think, there is only one path to take, and that is to merge into Great Xia!"

Upon hearing this, Ji Yang and the others showed slightly dark expressions.

Upon first seeing the more than three hundred fully armed troops from Great Xia, they had actually already had a premonition. It was just that upon hearing Xu Ning state it so plainly, they couldn’t help but feel a weight in their hearts.

"You just proposed five conditions. I can agree to the first three, but the fourth is definitely not possible. To hold a position in Great Xia, one either needs to make contributions and achieve merits for the camp or be strong enough to earn the respect of the Lord and Minister. Unfortunately, your fourteen from Songyang Ridge do not meet any of these conditions, so granting positions is impossible.

As for the fifth one!"

Xu Ning paused here, turning to look at Xu Shicheng, whose face was still somber. He quickly deliberated in his mind before continuing:

"What Lord Xu just said is true. If anyone can send people to the Lord’s side, and should something go wrong, we would be derelict in our duties. How about this? I can present them to Lady for her consideration. Whether they remain to serve the Lord will entirely depend on Lady’s decision, contingent on your agreement!"
