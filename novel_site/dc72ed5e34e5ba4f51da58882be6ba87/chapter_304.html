Chapter 304: Chapter 166: Broken Defense of Mirror Immortal_2

They all held large blades, their faces expressionless, looking like puppets on strings, but their movements were not slow at all, quickly rushing to the front of Xia Hong and the others.

Swoosh... Swoosh... Swoosh...

Not only did these hundred or so people rush out, but hundreds of silver pupils also lit up from the shadows of the wooden huts, followed by thousands of iron arrows suddenly shooting towards them.

"Find cover, they all have Earth-digging Realm power!"

Xia Hong cut down the first iron arrow and quickly drew his blade to meet the two people in front. Wielding the Yi Blade abruptly, he neatly chopped off their heads, but realizing these people’s basic strength was more than ten thousand pounds, his expression changed sharply, and he quickly warned Xia Chuan and the others behind him.

"They were all just ordinary people in the camp before, and they just committed suicide en masse. They have been resurrected and controlled by He Yuanhun."

The reminder from Yuwen Dao behind was somewhat unnecessary. Noticing the number of people in the wooden buildings, Xia Hong immediately had an answer in his mind.

There were only a little over three hundred Earth-digging Realm individuals in the Mirror Immortal Camp before. Now, there are at least five or six hundred people in the two rows of wooden houses on the left and right, which is clearly abnormal.

"Follow me!"

Xia Hong moved like lightning, his Yi Blade flashing about, sending out cold gleams, not only cutting down dozens of arrows but also simultaneously killing a dozen people in front of him.

When the long blade occasionally slashed the eyes of those people, their heads would ignite immediately, exactly the same as in the previous valley.

Breaking through the blockade of the frontline human wall, Xia Hong led the group directly into a wooden house on the left.

Swoosh...

As soon as he knocked open the front door, dozens of blades greeted them head-on.

Prepared, Xia Hong cleverly kicked the door panel towards the front, sending it flying.

With normal force, Xia Hong’s kick would have surely penetrated the wooden door, but using clever force was different. The wooden door flew out entirely backward, directly slamming dozens of people against the wall of the house behind them.

Bang...

With a loud crash, a large section of the wall was directly knocked down.

The people in the front row who were directly struck by the wooden door were instantly torn apart, leaving no piece intact; even those affected by the aftermath spewed blood all over the ground, losing all ability to act.

"This..."

Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and other Earth-digging Realm individuals of Great Xia, having witnessed Xia Hong’s strength in the valley, were not surprised by this scene.

But Yuwen Dao, Hong Guang, and the forty Earth-digging Realm warriors by his side were different. Everyone couldn’t help but gasp, their eyes at Xia Hong filled with astonishment and shock.

They had seen Xia Hong’s previous battle with Yuwen Hu.

At that time, although Xia Hong took some bold moves that nearly put Yuwen Hu at a disadvantage, it could only be said that their strengths were evenly matched at best.

But the scene before their eyes now was different.

Over a hundred people charging, Xia Hong calmly broke through the encirclement, and without saying, he kicked the door panel flying, knocking dozens of Earth-digging Realm warriors behind, all flying and being killed or seriously injured. This was not something that could be accomplished by high basic strength alone.

After all, it’s just a wooden door, not to mention that even a Logging Realm person wielding a blade could break it directly, let alone the dozens of Earth-digging Realm warriors just behind it.

"Leakless muscles, muscle control, this ability only comes from achieving Perfect Skin and breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level. It’s not surprising that he broke through, but to immediately defeat the three-maned strength of He Yuanhun upon breakthrough, could Xia Hong possibly be a Superior Battle Body?"

Yuwen Dao’s pupils suddenly contracted, recalling his father’s previous words that once a Superior Battle Body breaks through, the basic strength would at least soar to the power of three-maned. Looking at Xia Hong, his eyes suddenly filled with envy.

Not only him, but Hong Guang, Hong Tian, and the other five slightly older individuals understood something when noticing Xia Hong showing strength without any hint of breath or muscle movement, their expressions changed to match Yuwen Dao’s instantly.

"Super-grade Battle Body, extremely rare even in village-level camps, and it unexpectedly appeared in this small Redwood Ridge, it’s simply incredible!"

......

"Go upstairs!"

Whatever Yuwen Dao and the others were thinking, Xia Hong was unaware of it.

In this short moment, he had already led Xia Chuan and the others, clearing the entire wooden building. The hundred or so Earth-digging Realm people hiding inside had all been slaughtered.

Leading the group up to the top floor of the wooden building, they saw people continuously surging from all directions toward this place, and their expressions slightly tightened.

"Brother, look at that bamboo building..."

It seemed Xia Chuan had discovered something more important. At his reminder, everyone turned to look, and instantly, their scalps tingled.

Although this wooden house was at the outermost perimeter of the camp, it was only two to three hundred meters away from the bamboo building, just barely allowing them to see the situation over there clearly.

The flames from the two mirrors above the bamboo building were still present.

Even He Yuanhun’s screams had not stopped.

But likewise, the light emitted by the two mirrors also had not ceased.

The mirror on the right seemed to be controlled deliberately, its black dark light repeatedly illuminating the bodies of the corpses on the ground.

Corpse after corpse, that had just been lying motionless by the bamboo building, twitched upon being hit by the dark light, and as if coming back to life, immediately got up from the ground and turned toward the wooden building where Xia Hong and the others were.

"These people, is this how they come back to life!"

Xia Hong’s heart trembled slightly. They were all Earth-digging Realm.

If all these nine thousand plus people were resurrected, no matter how strong he was, he would probably fall here today.

"Brother, the solidified fire oil can only suppress them, it seems not enough to kill them. The number of revived ones is getting more, we can’t drag on any longer, you go ahead, we will hold them here!"
