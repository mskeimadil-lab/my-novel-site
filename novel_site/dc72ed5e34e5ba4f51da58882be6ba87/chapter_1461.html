Chapter 1461: Chapter 463: Green-eyed Fire Suanni, the Massacre and Ascension, Furious Counterattack, Trial Changes_6

Of course, the actual situation was not like this!

As cracks appeared, Xia Hong felt as if something inside him had awakened. His originally listless aura suddenly recovered, and his momentum continued to rise.

106...116...126...136...

If someone could see through Xia Hong’s current strength, they would find that his basic strength was soaring crazily. From the original 106 Jun, it suddenly surged to nearly 160 Jun.

"You vile beast... you vile beast... you actually forced me to this step. Ten years of Dan poison metabolism, twenty years of cultivation speed decline, you have guts, you have guts, you have guts..."

Xia Hong was actually already naked at this moment, but his body had long been burnt to a crisp, even his hair and eyebrows were gone, making it hard to recognize his original form.

Of course, he didn’t have time to care about his appearance at this moment. After taking the Bone Burning Pill and feeling his strength quickly grow to 160 Jun, he turned his head to look at the Green-eyed Fire Suanni that had already chased him, with no trace of joy on his face, his tone full of anger and hatred, and his pupils filled with a towering killing intent.

He had been fleeing in all directions for so long, ultimately because he didn’t want to use the Bone Burning Pill.

Ten years of Dan poison metabolism, the subsequent twenty years of cultivation speed impact, there’s no need to say more.

If it wasn’t absolutely necessary, he wouldn’t use it even if he were beaten to death.

Coming to the Ice Abyss for over ten years, it’s not like there wasn’t such dangerous times.

The problem is, such a huge loss all at once, it’s his first time!

Xia Hong suddenly turned his head and looked towards the southern sky, at the Beast Emperor with a body similar to the Suanni, covered in blue mane, the three-headed wolf-shaped Beast Emperor, watching it hover in the air with blue wind under its four feet, the killing intent in his eyes had not diminished at all.

Wind belongs to wood, a Five Luminaries Realm Beast Emperor of the Wood Element!

The Green-eyed Fire Suanni is hateful, but the real straw that breaks the camel’s back is still this newly appeared Three-headed Wolf Emperor in front of him.

If it weren’t for this Three-headed Wolf Emperor, he still had the confidence to contend for a moment, wouldn’t choose to take the Bone Burning Pill, waiting for any chance to escape alive.

But now, no matter what is said, it’s too late!

"Die for me..."

Xia Hong shouted violently, his body leaning backward to store strength, the rapidly soaring momentum instantly poured out, the sword edge suddenly tore through the blood sky, drawing a dazzling Star River Sword Qi in mid-air that almost instantly reached the middle of the Three-headed Wolf Emperor’s face.

Heaven-pulling Sword Technique!

The Three-headed Wolf Emperor evidently didn’t expect the Star River Sword Qi to be so fast, its three pupils suddenly contracted, following which, its huge body instantly disappeared in mid-air.

Wind belongs to wood, that speed is naturally what this Three-headed Wolf Emperor is best at.

However, Xia Hong’s current strength is not something this Wolf Emperor can match!

It seems as if he had already expected the Beast Emperor’s action, Xia Hong’s Star River Sword Qi did not stop at all, but continued to fly forward for another mile.

Swoosh...

The Star River Sword Qi crossed the sky for a kilometer, slicing through the blood-colored miasma, penetrating straight through until a mile away before meeting any slight obstruction.

Crunch... crack...

Meat torn apart, bones fractured, crisp sounds echoed continuously, the Three-headed Wolf Emperor’s body stiffened at the end of the Sword Qi, while Xia Hong, holding the Shattered Star Sword, already stood behind the Beast Emperor’s skull.

"240 Jun strength, even ordinary Second Transformation Tribulation Body Realm is no match for me, you mere solitary Five Luminaries Realm Beast Emperor also wants to turn the sky!"

Bang...

Xia Hong’s cold laughter synchronized with the sound of the Three-headed Wolf Emperor’s body crashing to the ground, the Green-eyed Fire Suanni a kilometer away on the southern side, along with dozens of Beast King Suanni it brought, their pupils suddenly contracted, their beast faces instantly froze.

Whoosh...

The Green-eyed Fire Suanni only froze for less than half a breath before quickly turning, fleeing towards the east, while the remaining dozens of Beast Kings hadn’t even reacted yet.

Swoosh...

The result of reacting slower was being hit by the Shattering Divine Light continuously shot from Xia Hong’s left eye, their bodies quickly cracked, life force severed, crashing to the ground.

"Don’t you like chasing? Run, now you want to run, I’ll let you run, there’s only three days, I don’t have much time to waste on you, die!"

Xia Hong’s tone was grim. He didn’t care about the few Beast King level Suanni that managed to escape, his body vigorously advanced, directly pursuing the Green-eyed Fire Suanni, the killing intent in his pupils almost condensing into substance.

The Bone Burning Pill has only a three-day effect, since it’s used, naturally it needs to be maximized, leaving some time to escape Blood Miasma Plain, all remaining time used to hunt the Beast Emperors in the Miasma Area.

Ten years can’t use it again; subsequent twenty years cultivation speed will slow.

Thinking of these two points, Xia Hong’s inner anger was overwhelming, the Green-eyed Fire Suanni fleeing ahead had already become a corpse in his eyes.

.........

Leaving aside Xia Hong’s situation in the Miasma Area for now.

Caiqiu Calendar year 297, November tenth, nighttime

The tenth day since the trial began.

In the snowy forest on the west side of Cai Qiu Trial Area, the cold wind was howling, no living person could be seen in any visible areas, the ground, trees, rocks, mountain bodies, everything seemed to be completely frozen, even the blood-colored miasma in the air seemed more diluted due to the touch of frost color.

On the west side of a mountain body, a dozen young people were frantically fleeing.

"Su Xing’er, faster, the Cold Beast is catching up."

"Su Jing, too slow, at this rate we’ll all die."

"The cold current is getting stronger, if we don’t find a cave to hide in, we’ll lose our lives."

......

Their escape formation was quite interesting, divided into four formations in total, each formation had one girl and three boys holding a Yellow Candle, with other people huddling around them, looking extremely nervous, wanting to speed up but not daring, only continually urging the four holding the Yellow Candles to speed up.

The four holding the Yellow Candles were none other than Su Xing’er and her siblings.

Su Xing’er stood at the front, Cai Shiqin right next to her, and there were three other girls, their eyes occasionally glancing at the Yellow Candle in her hand, their expressions clearly full of greed.

"There’s a cave over there, quickly hide in it!"

Su Xing’er had long noticed the eyes of the three girls but at this moment her expression was extremely solemn, clearly fearing something else, frantically searching for shelter at the base of the mountain, finally seeing a cave entrance blocked by dead branches, she immediately exclaimed, alerting the crowd to hide in the cave.

Upon hearing, the crowd immediately looked towards the direction she pointed, discovering indeed a cave entrance on the flank, pupils filled with ecstasy, quickly huddling the four siblings holding Yellow Candles, rushing towards the cave entrance.
