Chapter 244: Chapter 149: The Ultimate Combat Body, The Dangerous Gao Yu [Please Vote]

The process of reshaping his own skin is likely very different from others.

Xia Hong had long suspected this in his heart.

The power level of humans at the Cold Resistance Level, judging by the current situation, is probably only equivalent to intermediate Cold Beasts.

Just to break through to one Cold Resistance Level, it takes the blood of hundreds of intermediate Cold Beasts.

No matter how you look at it, it seems unreasonable.

After the encounter with Yuwen Hu, this suspicion was confirmed.

The difficulty of reshaping his own skin is indeed different from others.

But Xia Hong never imagined the difference would be so great.

According to Gao Yu, when the basic strength reaches the limit of 30,000 jin, the skin reshaping phase can increase it by at most 20,000 jin, reaching 50,000 jin.

At the limit of 50,000 jin, the whole body achieves the Perfect Skin, reaching the Leakless Realm, and breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, the strength doubles again.

That is, 100,000 jin, known in the Ice Abyss World as the "One-mane Power."

So, the standard for Cold Resistance Level is one-mane power.

Of course, this is the standard Cold Resistance Level!

Just like different types of Cold Beasts have different power limits, there is also a group of humans with extraordinary talent, whose skin can endure pain and the Beast Blood much more than ordinary people.

This group of people takes longer to reshape their skin and can absorb several times more Cold Beast blood than others.

What is called the Battle Body refers to the unique physique of this group.

Though the cost is higher, naturally, those with the Battle Body Talent experience higher limits in strength growth in this phase.

Generally speaking, battle bodies are divided into superior, intermediate, and inferior grades.

The upper limit of strength during the skin reshaping phase serves as the benchmark.

100,000 jin for superior; 80,000 jin for intermediate; 60,000 jin for inferior.

If the strength during the skin phase can reach 100,000 jin, then after achieving the Leakless Body and officially breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, the strength doubles, and basic strength skyrockets to 200,000 jin, which is two-mane power.

Following this logic, even the most inferior battle body, upon breakthrough, has a strength of 120,000 jin, 20% higher than a typical Cold Resistance Level.

This 20% increase in strength is enough to make a big difference in many situations.

"Of course, such prowess is not gained out of nowhere.

In the Ice Abyss World, time equates to life, and this part of the people with battle bodies often exceed the limit in using Beast Blood to save time breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level since more Cold Beast blood is required.

A normal person using two or three jin at a time would nearly reach their limit.

With greater willpower, they might withstand three to five jin at most.

But for them, they start at a minimum of five jin, with some going up to ten jin, enduring pain far beyond ordinary people’s imagination, and many die directly during the use of Beast Blood."

As Gao Yu’s words fell, the entire first-floor hall became dead silent.

The fear in Xia Hong’s eyes arose at this moment.

Of course, the fear only lasted for a while.

More than anything, he felt excitement surging inside.

At that moment, all seventeen members of the Hunting Team turned their heads to stare at Xia Hong in unison, with expressions shifting from initial shock to horror, and then to great excitement.

"Ten jin? Big brother uses more than a hundred jin at a time and even two or three hundred jin!"

"The leader’s dosage is at least over a hundred jin every time!"

...

Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, Yuan Cheng, Yue Feng—all members of the Hunting Team—how could they not be excited at this moment?

According to Gao Yu’s words, the greater the amount used, the more the boost, and correspondingly, the higher the potential. After breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, the strength would certainly be much stronger.

"No wonder when I asked the leader just now if he had broken through, he remained silent!"

Recalling how Xia Hong was silent when asked about his breakthrough after defeating Yuwen Hu, Luo Yuan suddenly realized.

Xia Hong hadn’t even reached the Cold Resistance Level yet.

Not only him, but Xia Chuan and a few others who reacted quickly immediately remembered it.

Before breaking through, his strength was already on par with Yuwen Hu.

When he does break through, how strong will the leader be?

They all turned to Xia Hong, their eyes full of admiration and fervor.

If the others were thinking like this, how much more so for Xia Hong himself?

The superior battle body has a limit of 100,000 jin.

But his current strength was already close to 70,000 jin, just a 31% progress!

Conservatively estimated, once he completes the skin remodeling phase, his basic strength could rise to at least 150,000 jin, doubling upon breakthrough to 300,000 jin.

Three-maned power!

More than one mane above a superior battle body.

So what kind of battle body does he possess?

Is there something beyond the superior, perhaps an ultimate or more powerful physique...

Xia Hong swallowed down his excitement, suppressing his urge to inquire further, and after calming himself, he gave Xia Chuan and the others a look to keep quiet.

Receiving his signal, everyone understood instantly and controlled their excitement, their expressions turning calm.

Even if Gao Yu seemed courteous, ultimately he was still an unknown stranger within the camp.

Moreover, his power clearly surpassed theirs by far.

Though the wooden mannequin was a safety net, a cautious part should still remain.

"This Twin Dragon Mountain, although I’ve never ventured there, I’ve scouted it several times. It spans at least two or three hundred li east to west. Amidst it, not to mention Cold Beasts, even deceitful monsters are numerous. That Gao brother can traverse it alone suggests his strength far exceeds ordinary people, Cold Resistance Level..."

Xia Hong didn’t finish his sentence, leaving the last three words hanging in hesitation, with an obvious look of skepticism as he gazed at Gao Yu.
