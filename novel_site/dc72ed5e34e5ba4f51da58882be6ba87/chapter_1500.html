Chapter 1500: Chapter 471: Frostfire Scorches the Fields, Success and Revenge, the Tragedy at River Mouth, and Another Expert?

Just as he was about to stuff the Spirit Pill into his mouth.

An unexpected scene occurred.

In front, Zhang Long who had rushed out dozens of meters suddenly stopped without reason, and then froze on the snow.

Zhang Yuchuan’s expression froze as well, quietly clutching the Dan Medicine in his hand, then with a suspicious expression walked slowly towards him.

Upon reaching less than ten meters away, he found that Zhang Long’s Qi Muscle showed no change, and immediately he grew vigilant. Moving a little closer, he circled around to the front, keeping a five-meter distance.

As he reached the front of Zhang Long, seeing clearly the other’s situation, he was instantly taken aback.

Zhang Long’s face was full of terror, he wasn’t dead yet, he was deliberately twisting his legs, clearly attempting to escape.

But the problem was that at this moment his knees were pierced by a black tree branch, and obviously, it was through the bone, almost nailing his legs together, making it impossible for him to make any small movements.

It was just an ordinary tree branch, about the thickness of a thumb, more than thirty centimeters long, the end that pierced through wasn’t sharp, clearly just broken off by someone.

"There is an expert!"

Zhang Yuchuan, seeing the appearance of the tree branch, immediately became vigilant, quickly looking around.

"May I ask, which senior has intervened to assist?"

Unfortunately, after looking for a long time, he still couldn’t find a single figure. Helpless, he spoke up but waited for several seconds until the two protectors and Liu Zhang came over here, he finally gave up and slowly walked to Zhang Long’s front.

"Spare... spare..."

Plop!

Zhang Yuchuan wasted no more words, drew his knife, and directly beheaded Zhang Long, then carried his head towards the two protectors and Liu Zhang.

"The Chief has great skill!"

"No wonder Little Dragon Head appreciates him, Steward Zhang, amazing!"

The three approaching from afar saw Zhang Yuchuan carrying Zhang Long’s head, their pupils instantly showing a trace of shock.

No matter Zhang Long’s injuries, he still possessed Five Manes strength, while Zhang Yuchuan only had Sixteen Manes, his strength less than half, but beheading him was alarming regardless of the method used.

"It wasn’t just me who killed, there was an expert who intervened!"

As Zhang Yuchuan spoke, the three instantly changed their expressions, swiftly becoming vigilant of the surroundings, but like Zhang Yuchuan, they found nothing.

"Baiying Village’s uprising was brought forward so much, surely there’s a problem, now an unfamiliar expert appears here, this uprising is likely to have many unexpected changes..."

Upon hearing Zhang Yuchuan’s words, Liu’s protector said solemnly, "During the battle, I saw Yellow Leaf Village in the north launch command arrows, the timing was synchronized. Among the nearby three villages, only Baiying had a problem. Let’s go back and clear the battlefield first, quickly get people to support!"

Liu Zhang nodded and replied, "There are a total of sixteen branches, surely some will have issues, that’s not surprising. The key is the River Mouth Village, it’s only three kilometers from Lu River Camp guarding the water source, once the uprising occurs, the army will quickly react, we need to immediately head south to lead support..."

"Let’s go, arrange things first! It’s 32 kilometers from here to River Mouth Village, going via the Su Field outskirts is fast, should be less than an hour, hope Baiying Village doesn’t have any issues."

Unlike outside, Su Field District has no wind, also with coal stone providing long-term heating, the ground has no snow, barely affecting travel. As long as personnel routes are arranged well, the journey will be quick.

These matters were discussed in the County City months ago, Zhang Yuchuan naturally proceeded without a hitch, taking the three and swiftly heading back to clear the final battlefield.

.........

In the high air south of Qingwu Village, Xia Hong stood in a blue Gai clothes, peering down at the eastern sides of the entire Rice Field, his eyes slightly narrowed.

"The Su Field planting area in Lu River Valley can roughly be seen as a twenty-kilometer radius semicircle. The river passing through the center of Su Field should be Lu River, sixteen villages use Lu River as the boundary, all are distributed along the eastern sides of Su Field, the middle being River Mouth Village, with seven villages north and eight south.

The sixteen branches will surely merge after the uprising to confront the thousands of County Guard Army in Lu River Camp, so once successful, they will all converge in between River Mouth Village, meaning the key to this uprising is River Mouth Village..."

At this moment, Xia Hong internally felt somewhat pleased.

The almost skyfaring Zhang Long, his legs nailed through by a tree branch, was Xia Hong’s handiwork no doubt, the process of Qingwu Village’s uprising was fully witnessed by him.

Such meticulous and comprehensive planning, from uprising to the ultimate success, indeed made Xia Hong wide-eyed, and Qingwu Village was merely a part of a plan.

This Frost Ash Association truly showed some capability.

Has that Little Dragon Head arrived or not?

Qingwu Village’s position slightly north, even though Xia Hong hovered in the air, he could only see the situations of nearby five villages.

"Though Baiying Village had trouble, the three northern villages all succeeded and are nearing completion, should soon reach south for support, the situation at the southern village is similar, traveling via Su Field will be faster, they should gradually head towards the middle at River Mouth Village..."

Since concluding Frost Ash Association’s uprising key is River Mouth Village, Xia Hong naturally had to go over and see. Based on what he witnessed in Qingwu Village as long as it ensured safety and concealed identity, he wouldn’t mind lending Frost Ash Association a hand.
