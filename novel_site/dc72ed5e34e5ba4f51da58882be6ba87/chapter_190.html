Chapter 190: Chapter 135: The Fearless Mirror Immortal [Three in One]_2

With a thundering crash, the massive body collapsed to the ground, making a huge noise.

"Finally dead!"

Xia Hong up in the tree finally breathed a true sigh of relief.

A portion of Snow Mane’s head had been shot off directly by relentless iron arrows. Not only blood, but even brain matter had spilled out quite a lot.

Even if it could play dead, this time it was definitely futile.

"Don’t get close yet, I’ll go check it out alone!"

Although it seemed almost certain, Xia Hong still adhered to caution, deciding to go check it out first by himself.

"No need, leader, I’ll go check it."

But before he could finish speaking, and before he could move, Xia Chuan had already jumped ahead.

"There’s no need for the leader to go personally, I’ll do it."

"Let me do it."

...

Even the others scattered on both sides started vying for the task.

Everyone scrambling to go forward and confirm themselves made Xia Hong’s tense nerves immediately relax a lot, and a hint of a smile appeared on his face.

"Let Xia Chuan go alone, the rest of you wait with me."

Their behavior was indeed heartwarming, but habits still needed to be nurtured; having so many people rush forward at once could lead to regret if any problems arose in the future.

The eleven didn’t wait long, after about ten breaths, Xia Chuan’s slightly excited voice came from beside Snow Mane’s corpse.

"No problem, everyone can come over, it’s completely dead!"

Xia Hong leapt down from the tree, arriving first.

Looking at the large Snow Mane corpse on the ground, Xia Hong’s eyes were filled with joy, and the fatigue on his body seemed to vanish in an instant.

Even the injury on his shoulder seemed to ease quite a lot.

The other ten also arrived one after another.

"Tsk tsk, it’s huge!"

"Hahaha, Intermediate Cold Beast, nothing more than that!"

"This size is about two or three times that of a Low-level Snowman."

"A body length of seven or eight meters, this might yield four or five thousand pounds of meat."

"Probably more than that; not just the length, even the legs and body are a few circles larger than the low-level ones, the meat yield must be astonishing."

"We’ll know when we take it back."

"This is an Intermediate Cold Beast, has anyone ever hunted one in the Redwood Ridge camp before? Leader, did we make history today?"

...

The crowd gathered around the Snow Mane corpse, their words filled with awe and excitement.

Truth be told, during the hunt, aside from Xia Hong’s brief close encounter with Snow Mane, the other fourteen people had all been attacking from tens or even a hundred meters away using bows and arrows.

Just now, they hadn’t realized; now that they were closer, they truly felt how massive this Snow Mane they had hunted was.

"Alright, stop gawking, split into five with Xia Chuan to pull out the arrows from its head; the other five go chop some trees to make long wooden poles so we can carry it back later!"

The ten’s exclamations and shouts only lasted a moment before Xia Hong commanded, splitting them into two groups to start on their tasks.

"You guys can’t withstand its blood now, be careful not to get it on your skin while pulling out the arrows."

Actually, Xia Hong didn’t even need to remind them, as Xia Chuan, who had first confirmed Snow Mane’s death, was already pulling out arrows, and seeing his cautious actions, the others immediately followed suit, not daring to let the blood from Snow Mane touch their skin.

Hiss...

Xia Hong sucked in a sharp breath, touched his stinging cheek, and looked down at his chest and arm, already torn and tattered, immediately guessing that his face was probably just as disfigured now.

It wasn’t for no reason that he reminded everyone; just now, when crazily stabbing Snow Mane’s seven orifices with the Yi Blade in the pit, the splattered blood almost drenched him.

At that time, a strong burning sensation flooded his mind, but with the fierce battle occurring, his attention was all on Snow Mane, not letting it bother him.

Now that he relaxed, the pain instantly became intense.

"Had it not been for elevating my basic strength to the peak of thirty thousand pounds in the last phase, forget about slaying this Snow Mane; its splattered blood alone would have cost me half my life!"

When his basic strength was only over twenty thousand pounds, the Mouse King’s blood temperature could directly burn through his skin, only with the help of the Sun Cold Light could he eliminate the burns and fully recover.

Now at the thirty thousand pound peak, this blood, though still burning him till his skin burst open, didn’t harm the inner flesh and bones, and moreover that pain, was less intense than before.

Though torn and tattered, miserably looking, accompanied by intense pain.

But Xia Hong could clearly feel, that he could endure it, and in enduring, his skin membrane and basic strength had visibly increased.

"Just that the appearance is a bit unsightly, it should recover soon... "

Also luckily, it was nighttime, and given the depth of the location, he also dared not let anyone light a fire, the others could only see him covered in blood, if they saw his current terrifying, tattered appearance, maybe they’d mistake him for a ghoul.

Hoo...

"Quickly tidy up, we can’t stay here long."

With a deep breath, Xia Hong resisted the severe pain on his body, urged everyone to speed up, then climbed a tree himself to observe the surroundings.

This was already four or five kilometers deep into the Redwood Ridge area, where the trees were not only generally much thicker than those on the outskirts, the variety was also noticeably more diverse.

Three or four kilometers north, there was a large Cloud Mist Zone, its estimated range some five or six hundred meters wide; further south, one could see a towering giant tree with an incomparably exaggerated diameter, on which dozens of massive figures were perched, each one not much different from the just slain Snow Mane.
