Chapter 1474: Chapter 466: The Cunning Red Gold Demon Tiger, Chance Encounter and Shocking News

In the southwestern sky, a strong red-gold light was rushing toward the area where they were at breakneck speed.

The visibility in the Miasma Area was very low, only about a hundred meters for the Xianyang Level, and about one mile for the Tribulation Body Realm. By all accounts, that red-gold light was at least ten kilometers away, yet everyone could see it clearly at a glance.

Especially as it got closer, the outline of the light became clear first in Chu Tianxu’s eyes, and the fright in his pupils instantly grew several times stronger.

It was a giant beast over three hundred feet long, with a shoulder height of at least one hundred fifty feet, covered in a layer of flaming red fur densely packed like it was burning, with dazzling golden patterns all over, like a fiery Jin Mountain suspended in mid-air.

It was currently flying northward, like a moving wall, and because of its exaggerated shoulder height, its enormous tiger-like head could almost meet the towering peaks, so great that everyone couldn’t make it out at first sight.

"It’s the Red Gold Demon Tiger, run!"

The first to see clearly was, of course, Chu Tianxu, with his Second Turn Tribulation Body Realm Cultivation.

"County Father..."

"Don’t babble, run quickly, it can’t kill me!"

Interrupted by the roaring command, Chu Xuanfeng and the others dared not delay any longer. Hearing this, they moved, their faces’ panic instantly turning into energy in their legs, madly escaping toward the north side.

"Having taken the Hidden God Pill before coming in and walking the safe route the whole time, and still being targeted, we can’t run directly outward, this is trouble..."

Chu Tianxu drew out his black longsword, his expression sinking to the extreme. However, as the Red Gold Demon Tiger got closer, upon seeing it clearly, the gloom on his face instantly transformed into bewilderment and disbelief.

"Is it... running for its life?"

The Red Gold Demon Tiger’s left eye had been pierced by a sharp weapon, continuously bleeding; there were more than ten deep sword scars on its face and neck. At this moment, its face was full of panic and distress, its limbs charged with Tribulation Qi frantically fleeing, leaving no trace of Emperor-level Cold Beast’s majesty.

Seeing the relentless figure pursuing behind the Red Gold Demon Tiger, the bewilderment on Chu Tianxu’s face quickly disappeared, replaced by profound shock and astonishment.

The Red Gold Demon Tiger was indeed running for its life; behind it, a dark figure was pursuing with a black longsword in hand, the blade densely dotted with star points, its speed too fast, the Sword Qi it drew in mid-air as brilliant and dazzling as the Star River.

Of course, even if the Star River Sword Qi was attractive, it wouldn’t cause such a reaction in Chu Tianxu; his intense reaction was because, behind that black figure, a dark red figure of over seven feet hovered.

Appearing with Bone Appearance phantom during combat signifies the Xianyang Level having pushed the bone power to its extreme; combined with the dark red Bone Appearance over seven feet high and his perception, reaching an astonishing conclusion isn’t difficult:

"Peak of Xianyang Level, this... this... how can it be!"

Chu Tianxu was too shocked inside, along with being too far away, that he didn’t carefully discern the bone appearance’s specific form and directly concluded that person’s cultivation.

In his understanding, it could only be the peak of Xianyang Level.

A peak Xianyang Level chasing the Golden Fire Dual-Path Beast Emperor, Red Gold Demon Tiger!

"Using a sword, could it be the Heshuo Sword God, Li Wuji?"

The suspicion in Chu Tianxu’s eyes didn’t last long. Seeing the Red Gold Demon Tiger being less than three kilometers away, a hint of intention flashed across his face, pulling out the black longsword at his waist, he suddenly flew into the air.

"Little brother, I’ll assist you!"

A loud voice fell, Chu Tianxu’s black longsword pointed horizontally in mid-air, his body burst forth with a blue and red divine light, swelling rapidly to over thirty feet high, instantly transforming into a hundred-meter-tall giant.

In his hand, that black longsword also expanded proportionally with his body. A fierce blue cyclone rose behind him, the longsword suddenly thrusting forward, tearing the air, bringing a trail of starlight.

Boom......

The moment the starlight appeared, the blue cyclone exploded, the sparks instantly igniting, like a roaring fiery dragon that hit the Red Gold Demon Tiger’s head instantly under the force of the gale.

Roar......

Even though the Red Gold Demon Tiger was blinded in the left eye, it didn’t seem surprised at Chu Tianxu’s appearance, or rather, it intentionally ran this way.

When the fiery dragon transformed from Chu Tianxu’s sword edge flew to its face, the Red Gold Demon Tiger let out a roar, golden patterns on its body rapidly spreading, instantly its whole tiger body shone brightly.

Clang......

"Blocked it!"

Chu Tianxu’s expression suddenly tensed, withdrawing his sword to turn and strike again, but an intense blaze, faster than him, surged forth.

Boom......

The blaze erupted from the magical tiger’s surface.

The magic tiger just spread the golden patterns over its body to block Chu Tianxu’s sword, the surface originally shining bright gold. Yet, the instant it successfully blocked, it not only quickly returned to normal but also explosively burst forth a flame, impacting the front Chu Tianxu.

Not only Chu Tianxu, but even the dark figure chasing from behind was also blasted away by this blaze.

"Wretched beast..."

Chu Tianxu was blasted over a hundred meters, seeming to detect something, a hint of anger appeared on his face, and behind him three blue cyclones condensed again, the black longsword in his hand igniting into a flame, instantly transforming into red.

"I am the Chen Cang Yanling County Governor, Chu Tianxu. May I ask, little brother, are you Wei Bo, Li Wuji? This beast is already gravely injured; it’s trying to sow discord between us to fight for a chance to live. Little brother, you mustn’t fall for this beast’s trick and let us together, surely slay this fiend!"
