Chapter 715 - 293: The Lion Hunts the Rabbit – Luring the Enemy and Falling Into a Trap (Part 3)

Chapter 715: Chapter 293: The Lion Hunts the Rabbit – Luring the Enemy and Falling Into a Trap (Part 3)

Li Xuanling remembered very clearly, at that time the scout reported back that the thousand defenders of Hanqiong were all of the ordinary Earth-digging Realm cultivation.

Supposedly, deploying a thousand Earth-digging Realm to Wushuang wouldn’t be of much use, Xia Hong must have known this, which is why she was suspicious of this information, suspecting that Li Xuanping had seen wrong or been deceived.

"Absolutely certain, I even risked getting closer to observe, they are all just ordinary Earth-digging Realm, their war armor and weapons are only at the Ten-forged Level..."

Li Xuanping paused here, then sneered: "Then Xia Hong surely has no one else to use, that’s why she had no choice but to transfer these thousand from Hanqiong, hoping to hold Wushuang to the death, to resist our Long Mountain advance."

What use can a thousand ordinary Earth-digging Realm be?

Li Xuanling frowned tightly, still having some confusion on her face.

"The garrison had two thousand Earth-digging Realm, plus these thousand from Hanqiong, Wushuang now has at most three thousand Earth-digging Realm, plus that one thousand Cloud Dragon Army, Xia Hong couldn’t possibly think that with these four thousand, she could block the three thousand Longyou Army, could she?"

As soon as Li Xuandu spoke, everyone immediately started mockingly teasing.

"Four thousand against three thousand, does she think the Longyou Army soldiers are just ordinary Earth-digging Realm too?"

"Talking nonsense in one’s sleep."

"Unless that Cloud Dragon Army are all Cold Resistance Level."

......

The Earth-digging Realm soldiers who have begun reshaping their skin with beast blood, and those who only have hunting qualifications, are completely different concepts.

To put it directly, it’s the difference between a basic strength of over 30,000 catties and over 10,000 catties. If you factor in skin toughness, weapons, war armor, and other factors, the gap only widens.

There’s a common understanding in Longyou that an army composed of ordinary Earth-digging Realm, wanting to confront regular troops on the battlefield, shouldn’t even think about it without at least ten times the numbers.

Earth-digging Realm, plus 1000 Cloud Dragon Army, four thousand defending against three thousand Longyou Army.

If Xia Hong really intends this, then Great Xia is doomed tonight.

The other Cold Resistance Level are clear, and Li Xuanling is naturally even clearer, she just thinks a bit more than the others, she always felt that Xia Hong wouldn’t be that foolish.

As she pondered, suddenly someone pointed to the east and shouted urgently:

"Xuanqiu has also returned."

Another scout was back.

Li Xuanling perked up, watching her cousin Li Xuanqiu hastily approaching from a distance.

Li Xuanqiu wasted no words, speaking directly upon arrival:

"Reporting to the leader, nearly a thousand Earth-digging Realm arrived at Wushuang, judging by the direction they came from Beehive. Xuan Dong and I discovered an underground passageway beneath the snow southeast of Wushuang, it should be connected to Beehive, it’s expected that they haven’t just sent one group, a large number of people are likely to follow from that passageway."

"A large group of Earth-digging Realm from Beehive are also headed to Wushuang!"

Upon hearing this news, smiles appeared on everyone’s faces.

Even Li Xuanling completely set aside her previous suspicions.

"Xia Hong truly plans to gather all the Earth-digging Realm of Great Xia in Longyou to defend Wushuang to the death. Good, very good, this is perfect."

"Leader, the Great Xia’s passageway is very long, it’s a bit difficult to completely destroy it, but with a few more people and some time, it could still be done. Why don’t I take some people to destroy that passageway and stop Great Xia’s reinforcements from continuing to gather at Wushuang?"

Li Xuanqiu suggested, with some eagerness on everyone’s faces.

But Li Xuanling, after thinking for a moment, immediately shook her head:

"No need, the passageway can be destroyed any time, just not now."

Hearing this, everyone was somewhat puzzled.

"Exactly!"

Li Xuandu was the first to catch on, his eyes slightly brightened. Seeing the continued confusion among the crowd, he promptly explained: "Having reinforcements arrive is what allows Great Xia to dare continue holding Wushuang. If the passage is destroyed and the reinforcements can’t come for a while, Great Xia won’t defend Wushuang, and that’s when we would be in trouble, understand?"

After hearing these words, everyone suddenly came to a realization, nodding in agreement.

"Li Xuanping, Li Xuanqiu, you two should keep an eye on the surroundings of Wushuang, report back with any updates, regardless of how many people Great Xia sends, just watch them go in."

"Understood, leader!"

Li Xuanping and Li Xuanqiu bowed and immediately turned to head east.

Li Xuanling turned to look east, her voice brimming with murderous intent.

"Inform the army to speed up, there’s only 9 kilometers left, strive to arrive in just over an hour, tonight we will show Great Xia who truly commands Longyou land."

"Yes!"

All the Cold Resistance Level responded in unison, their faces filled with killing intent, and then dispersed throughout the army, loudly urging them on.
