Establishing the Pioneer Department opens a new path for advancement;

and then using strength to speak for the thirty spots.

With these four measures, everyone’s enthusiasm has been fully mobilized and ignited, and they have even begun competing to enter the Pioneer Department.

With such enthusiasm, it is foreseeable that, barring any accidents, the subsequent camp expansion effect will undoubtedly be excellent.

"Talents are not born; with dedication in training, they can be found anywhere!"

Even Xia Hong couldn’t help but express such sentiments.

When he first took over the leader position, Xia Chuan wasn’t as impressive as he is now.

Even when Xia Chuan had just become the Camp Supply Officer, there were still many things he needed to consult Xia Hong about, and for a few, Xia Hong even had to personally attend to them.

But now it’s different; Xia Chuan, though not yet fully matured,

at least has more than enough management capability for the current Great Xia Camp of over a thousand people.

"And it’s not just him — Luo Yuan, Yuan Cheng, Yue Feng, Lin Kai, Bai Dongying, Cheng Feng, Mu Dong, the three brothers Zhao Long, Qiu Peng, Lu Yang, Xu Ning... As long as we invest thought into their training, they are all talents!"

Xia Hong lowered his head in contemplation; as the saying goes, ’though the sparrow is small, it has all the vital organs.’ Though the Great Xia Camp only has over a thousand people, internally, there are many miscellaneous affairs to manage, and the needs for departments naturally increase.

If it hadn’t been for Xia Chuan’s reminder, he himself wouldn’t have realized that establishing a new Pioneer Department highlighted that the camp’s development can’t rely solely on him as the leader.

As the strongest warrior of Great Xia, his most important task is cultivation.

And to invest more time in cultivation, he must have someone helping to share these camp management affairs.

"In the future, I will participate in these things less. Even if mistakes are made, we need to nurture these people first; otherwise, when there are more people, I will have to divert my energy to manage these affairs!"

Having made up his mind, Xia Hong’s furrowed brow slightly relaxed, and he quickly packed his gear. After a brief hesitation while looking at the giant axe and Yi Blade on the weapon rack in the room, he decided to take both weapons with him.

With his current strength, neither weapon was suitable, but he couldn’t forge new ones in a short time.

"Ten-forged Weapons are no longer suitable for me; I need to start considering forging Hundred-forged Weapons!"

Recalling the Hundred-forged Yanling Saber in Li Hu’s hand, Xia Hong’s eyes showed some focus. Back then, he couldn’t tell, but thinking back now, Li Hu’s basic strength should not have reached the three-thousand-pound limit.

With such cultivation, he could wield a Hundred-forged Weapon. The resources of Beishuo Town are probably far beyond his imagination.

"In the blink of an eye, more than half a year has passed. Those two have probably forgotten such a small place like Redwood Ridge!"

Of course, Xia Hong didn’t want those two to come back and cause trouble; he just thought about how, after half a year of painstaking effort, he had finally brought Great Xia to its current level.

But even today’s Great Xia, compared to Beishuo Town with a population of over a hundred thousand, is probably an infant compared to a strong man, insignificant.

"Leader, we are all ready and can set off."

"Got it!"

Having packed his things, Xia Hong walked straight out, and upon seeing the seventeen Hunting Team members outside, he revealed a slight smile:

"Tonight, follow me to the north and see if the people from the Mirror Immortal Camp dare to come again!"

Upon hearing this, everyone’s faces immediately revealed a hint of excitement.

After repelling Yuwen Hu last time, there hadn’t been any movement for six consecutive days.

They truly wanted to see if the people from the Mirror Immortal Camp dared to come again.
