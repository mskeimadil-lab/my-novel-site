Chapter 885: Chapter 337: Major Changes in Xia City, the Richest Person in Great Xia

As for the Artifact Refining Hall, they can produce dozens of Thousand Forged Weapons daily. The output of Ten Thousand Forged Weapons is also increasing. I heard that Director Mu is currently leading craftsmen to forge a Ten Thousand Forging Level weapon for the Lord, consuming tens of thousands of pounds of silver alone. Tsk, tsk...

Listening to Hong Yu along the way, Lu Yang had already reached the bottom of the main building.

After learning about the many significant changes in Xia City, Lu Yang’s face was naturally full of undisguised amazement. However, all this amazement slowly dissipated upon seeing the main building.

His eyes clearly showed a hint of excitement, obviously looking forward to what he was about to do next.

Hong Yu noticed the excitement on Lu Yang’s face and curiously asked:

"By the way, Lord Lu, is there something urgent for you to return from Hanqiong this time?"

"It’s of great importance, so I can’t say much. I hope Brother Hong understands!"

"No problem, no problem. In that case, Lord Lu, please hurry to see the Minister!"

Upon hearing this, Hong Yu was so startled that he quickly waved his hands.

If his father, Hong Tian, heard him being called Brother Hong, he would probably scold him.

Although Lu Yang was only five years older than him, in terms of identity, status, and strength, he surpassed him by too much, clearly someone at the same level as his father, Hong Tian. Being able to walk through the city gates with him and chat all the way was already an honor for him.

On the main road just now, Hong Yu clearly saw the envious looks many people were giving him.

Moreover, since Lu Yang said it was something important, it was definitely something he wasn’t qualified to hear. If he rashly inquired and crossed a taboo, it would definitely be more of a curse than a blessing.

Lu Yang smiled, cupped his hands, and quickly strode towards the top floor of the main building.

......

At the residence just behind Star-picking Hall, in Li Xuanling’s room.

Xia Yuyao and Xia Yusheng were each lying in a small rocking bed, sleeping soundly with eyes closed, while Li Xuanling sat in the middle, looking at one then the other, her face full of affection.

She held a Bone Fan in her left hand, gently fanning Xia Yusheng, while occasionally adjusting Xia Yuyao’s little quilt with her right hand, fearing she might catch a cold.

"Madam, you’ve been fanning for so long, let me do it instead. The Jade Dew next to the Second Prince’s bed has been collected, and I have nothing else to do."

Han Shuang, having just finished collecting a batch of Jade Dew, took the fan from Li Xuanling and continued to fan Xia Yusheng. As she looked at the fifteen drops of Cold Apple Jade Dew in her hand, she couldn’t help but exclaim, "I’ve heard that this Cold Apple Jade Dew can be exchanged for 5,000 Contribution Value points per drop at the Camp Supply Department. The First Princess can yield fifteen drops just from sleeping, probably making her the richest person in the entire camp."

Li Xuanling chuckled at her amusing words.

Cold Apple Jade Dew is the main ingredient for refining Yangyuan Pills, with one drop able to make nine pills. Although it requires other herbs as well, it is quite valuable, and the Camp Supply Department’s pricing of 5,000 points is fairly reasonable.

According to Han Shuang’s statement, the eldest daughter, Xia Yuyao, is indeed the wealthiest in the entire camp, perhaps even more than what Xia Hong could earn through hard work.

"The Second Prince is unique too! You don’t feel cold at all standing next to him; he’s like a natural heater. I love holding him the most."

Nong Ying, at the rocking bed, didn’t forget to compliment Xia Yusheng too.

Li Xuanling smiled and nodded, "Indeed, compared to Yao’er, Sheng’er’s physique seems even more suited for survival in the Ice Abyss, and he hasn’t even begun cultivation yet. I believe his physique will undergo further changes once he starts cultivating."

"Once the two Princes begin cultivating, Han Shuang predicts that all the talents in the Great Xia will pale in comparison."

"The entire Great Xia?"

Upon hearing this, Li Xuanling raised her eyebrow slightly and then revealed a proud expression, seemingly about to say something, but restrained herself.

"Ming Yue greets Madam and the two Princes!"

Suddenly, Ming Yue entered quickly from outside, paying respectful homage to Li Xuanling and her children before approaching Han Shuang to say softly:

"Sister Han Shuang, the Minister said there’s something urgent and asked me to notify the Lord. I couldn’t find Sister Han Yue after searching for a long time, so I came to you."

Though Ming Yue was a maid of the Star-picking Hall, she often went to the Political Hall. If someone urgently needed to see the Lord, they would have her report it.

Xia Hong was in seclusion at the Star-picking Platform, and only Han Yue and Han Shuang had access, so evidently, Han Yue was off doing something, which is why Ming Yue came to Han Shuang.

"Understood, I’ll head over now."

"Go notify him, don’t worry about me."

Li Xuanling gestured for Han Shuang to go notify Xia Hong immediately, watching her leave before turning to look at Ming Yue and asking, "Do you know what the matter is?"

"It seems Defender Lu Yang from Hanqiong brought back some important news!"

Li Xuanling slightly furrowed her brows, then gently said, "Go listen to what’s being said at the Political Hall and come back to tell me."

Ming Yue was momentarily stunned but quickly nodded and headed in that direction.

After all, it’s not like she was asked to eavesdrop, and even if she did, she didn’t have the capability for it. With Madam’s orders, even if the Lord found out, he wouldn’t blame her.
