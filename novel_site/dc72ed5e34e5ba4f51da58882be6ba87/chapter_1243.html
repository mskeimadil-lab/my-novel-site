Chapter 1243: Chapter 413: The Truth, Shifting the Blame to Cai Qiu, Hongmen Affairs Concluded

"Everyone, why don’t I give it a try?"

In front of Xia Chuan, the fourth in line, Xu Ning, slowly stepped forward.

All eyes immediately focused on him, full of confusion.

Xu Ning didn’t speak, he just walked slowly toward Yang Ning, Cao Yuanwu, and Long Bohai in the middle of the hall.

"What are you trying to do?"

Although Xu Ning didn’t look like he was about to make a move, Beishuo Institute Chief Yang Rong couldn’t sit still and got up to ask Xu Ning’s intentions.

"Chief Yang, please be patient, this is Xu Ning, the Deputy Director of the Great Xia’s Supervision Department. He happens to have some techniques to verify if there are any issues with Yang Ning and the others!"

As Xia Chuan spoke, Yang Rong and the other institute chiefs from the Seven Towns naturally dared not say anything more, continuing to watch Xu Ning to see how he was planning to conduct the verification.

Xu Ning first approached Yang Ning, made eye contact with him, then suddenly stirred his blood and Qi, and as three Great Xia Sacred Patterns emerged from his brow, his entire aura suddenly surged several times over.

"Lord Xu made a move at the city gate in early September; back then, many people speculated that his strength was probably around 30 zong, but it seems far beyond that now."

"Only 30 zong? His aura is stronger than that of Chief Yang, Shangguan Yuan, and Ye Yizhou, at least over 40 zong!"

"Hehe, stop guessing wildly, watch the faces of those three chiefs instead."

This time, the Eight Towns sent people to Hongmen to cooperate with Great Xia in the investigation, so the number of people that came was originally limited, and those qualified to enter the Government Office building were even fewer, with each town having only five to six people, including the eight institute chiefs.

With so few people and since they were on Great Xia’s territory, they naturally dared not act too presumptuously, so those making the remarks at this moment were all from Great Xia, mostly soldiers from the Cloud Dragon Army.

As everyone speculated about Xu Ning’s strength, someone reminded them to watch the expressions of the three institute chiefs, prompting them to immediately look up.

When the three institute chiefs heard the noise, although they quickly suppressed their facial expressions, the hint of astonishment in their pupils didn’t escape the eyes of Great Xia’s people.

The expressions of all the soldiers from Great Xia suddenly intensified.

Five days ago, when the eight institute chiefs arrived, Commander Liu Yuan of the Cloud Dragon Army disclosed the strengths of the eight to them. The strengths of the eight institute chiefs mirrored the situation in the Eight Towns, with the strongest three being Yang Rong with 43 zong, Shangguan Yuan with 41 zong, and Ye Yizhou with 41 zong; the remaining five were somewhat weaker, mostly between 35 to 40 zong.

The hint of astonishment in Yang Rong and the other two’s pupils clearly indicated that Xu Ning’s current outburst of strength was certainly above theirs, which was undoubtedly a major morale booster for Great Xia’s people.

Hearing the discussions around, a faint smile played at the corners of Xu Ning’s lips.

His basic strength was actually only 36 zong, but with the Great Xia Sacred Pattern, a 30% increase allowed his strength to surge to nearly 47 zong, naturally far exceeding these so-called Eight Towns Institute Chiefs.

If it were to come to a life-and-death fight, his techniques were numerous!

Of course, now was not the time to think about these things.

Xu Ning brushed aside unnecessary thoughts, looking at Yang Ning in front of him, who was struggling for breath under his imposing presence, his eyes suddenly focused.

His eyes began to emit a faint green light, and then the green light quickly spread within his eyes until finally, both pupils transformed completely into a shiny green color.

"Lord Xu, why did your eyes change color?"

"I don’t know, do you?"

"I know, it must be a great treasure bestowed upon Lord Xu by the Lord..."

"Right, I remember, don’t you all recall? Lord Xu previously didn’t have a right eye, and last year he suffered from soul-loss syndrome for over half a year. I heard it was the Lord who used a great treasure to awaken him."

"Not only was he awakened, but the Lord also elevated Lord Xu’s Battle Body Talent to top-grade. It’s said his rapid strength growth was because of that great treasure!"

......

The matter of the three Master’s Organs was known only to the core members of Great Xia, and even though the soldiers of the Cloud Dragon Army present had higher privileges than ordinary people, they only heard a rough idea.

All they knew was that Lord Xu received a great treasure from Lord Xia Hong, curing the soul-loss syndrome, restoring his right eye, and elevating his Battle Body Talent from top-grade to top-grade. Naturally, they were full of envy as they discussed it.

Meanwhile, as people speculated, new changes were happening with Xu Ning.

After his pupils fully turned into shiny green, Yang Ning in Xu Ning’s vision began to become transparent.

No, not transparent!

Instead, he could see through Yang Ning’s pupils and observe a concentrated spiritual light hidden beneath his flesh, specifically in the liver area.

The spiritual light seemed to have noticed Xu Ning’s gaze and trembled suddenly.

"The liver belongs to wood in the five elements, contains the soul body, governs vision. The Lord had me use prisoners in Xia City’s Heavenly Prison for experiments. My eyes can now not only slightly manipulate the soul body stored in the liver but also detect deceitful materials invisible to ordinary people. Let’s see if Yang Ning has indeed encountered something deceitful!"

Recalling Xia Hong’s words, Xu Ning’s expression slightly condensed, his gaze shifting away from the soul body in Yang Ning’s liver and starting to examine his entire body carefully.

As for Yang Ning, his body suddenly froze when the soul body in his liver trembled. He was clearly still conscious, but not knowing why he couldn’t move, he looked at Xu Ning with fear in his eyes.

The eyes!

Soon, Xu Ning’s gaze fixed on Yang Ning’s pupils.
