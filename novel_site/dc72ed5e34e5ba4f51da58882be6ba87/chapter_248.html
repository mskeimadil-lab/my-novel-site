Chapter 248: Chapter 150 Green Bamboo Fence and Marking Stone, Expanding Territory_2

Seeing that there was still more than half of the beast blood left in the wooden basin, Xia Chuan nodded immediately, knowing that Xia Hong was going to continue his closed cultivation, and quickly withdrew.

Xia Hong remained in the room alone, continuing to pour the beast blood over himself ladle by ladle, the intense pain once again surging to his forehead.

This time, he was much bolder than before.

For the past two days, he had been using it to the limit, afraid to pass out, because he was worried about Gao Yu. Now that Gao Yu had left, he could naturally be a bit bolder.

On the third day after Gao Yu left, which was the second day of the third month in the First Year of the Great Xia,

standing in the wooden basin, Xia Hong opened his eyes once more, looked at his increasingly smooth and crystal-clear skin, gently clenched his fist, and felt his strength, a trace of emotion flickering in his eyes.

[Camp: Great Xia (Level 0)]

[Lord: Xia Hong]

[Cultivation: Earth-digging Realm Complete (Skin Remodeling Progress 56%)]

[Territory Population: 1088]

[Resources: Wood 54030 Coal 4740 Iron 13210 Silver 0 Gold 0]

"A total of five hundred and eighty jin, increased progress by 25 points all at once, nearly a quarter. The effects produced by different Cold Beast Blood actually vary in quality!"

A quarter increase all at once, saving so much time, Xia Hong was naturally thrilled, thinking of the four Cold Beasts that Gao Yu had sent over, Xia Hong even felt a bit grateful to this stranger.

Boom...

Clang!

Xia Hong punched forward with great force, a blast of cold air and wind suddenly rushing out, the fire basin more than ten meters away was knocked over by the air current, the flames were extinguished, and coal stones and sparks were scattered all over.

"This is the power of one mane!"

One hundred thousand jin!

Seeing the commotion caused by his casual punch, Xia Hong immediately thought of the frost cold airflow brought by the Snow Mane’s charge, and his expression grew even more invigorated.

"Even if I were to fight the Snow Mane barehanded, I wouldn’t feel afraid at all. More importantly, the power of one mane, my basic strength, can already rival an ordinary Cold Resistance Level expert. If Yuwen Hu were to appear in front of me again..."

Recalling the situation when he faced off against Yuwen Hu five days ago, a hint of coldness flashed in Xia Hong’s eyes.

"Outdoor activities shouldn’t cease, it’s only been five days without going out, and the iron ore increased slightly, but the wood and coal stone decreased, and now even the Cold Beast Blood is used up..."

Looking at the nearly stagnant resources in the system, Xia Hong’s excitement immediately subsided, and he shook his head slightly.

If no collecting or hunting is done outside, it’s just pure sitting around consuming resources.

Fortunately, there’s a ready-made iron mine in the valley, otherwise, even the iron resources wouldn’t have increased in these five days.

"But before going out..."

Xia Hong pondered for a moment and focused on the system.

To be precise, on the buildings behind the system.

[Opened Buildings 4: Iron Refining Furnace (Level 2) Martial Arts Training Ground (Level 2) Medicine Decoction Pot (Level 1) Polishing Stone (Level 1)]

[Unopened Buildings 5: Green Bamboo Fence, Marking Stone, Yellow Earth, small wooden table, Rough Iron Chain]

Marking Stone: Coal 150 Iron 90

Green Bamboo Fence: per meter / Wood 20 Iron 5, upgrade available after exchanging ten thousand meters

Yellow Earth: per square meter / Wood 200 Coal 20, upgrade available after exchanging ten thousand square meters

So far, except for the longsword in Yang Ning’s hand, suspected to be made of silver, Xia Hong still doesn’t know what gold and silver look like in the Ice Abyss World.

small wooden table and Rough Iron Chain are definitely not coming out for now.

The buildings that can be created now are just these three.

The biggest reason these three buildings haven’t been created yet is definitely due to insufficient resources. New house construction, rodent groups, Mirror Immortal Camp, breaking through to Cold Resistance Level, resource shortages keep emerging one after another, Xia Hong wanted to initiate the buildings but didn’t have the chance.

In fact, even now, if it weren’t for the urgent situation, he probably wouldn’t have the mind to launch these three buildings.

First is the Yellow Earth, judging from the vegetation size in this world, even the slimmest Ice Tree, even assuming its radius is three or four meters, at least more than thirty square meters are needed to transplant one tree.

More than thirty square meters means 6000 wood and 600 coal stones.

Ice Tree, Xia Hong certainly won’t transplant it now, after all, Crystal Fruit is not considered an unimportant resource for the Great Xia currently.

"If they are to be transplanted, they must be Blue Jade Trees, but among the few already discovered Blue Jade Trees, the thinnest one has a radius over six meters, at least one hundred and twenty square meters of Yellow Earth is needed, converting this requires resources..."

Forget it, Yellow Earth, too expensive to exchange!

Once the Blue Jade Tree is successfully transplanted, stable Blue Frost Grass can be obtained in the future, thus, Golden Healing Powder can be stably produced.

Despite knowing how beneficial this would be for the camp, Xia Hong immediately gave up the idea of exchanging for Yellow Earth.

No choice, the required resources are too many!

One hundred and twenty square meters of Yellow Earth, just the wood requires twenty thousand, and that’s fine, but the coal stone requires 2400, that’s 2400 units, converted into actual weight, that’s 48000 jin.

Seventeen Earth-digging Realm, all equipped with Chewing Teeth Chisels, can produce at least 13600 jin of coal stones a day, actually only about four or five days of work.

But Xia Hong still found it not very worthwhile.

It’s not so much not worthwhile, ultimately, it’s still a matter of insufficient resources.

"Better to prioritize exchanging for the Green Bamboo Fence!"

Green Bamboo Fence: per meter / Wood 20 Iron 5

The Green Bamboo Fence, as the name suggests, should be a protective material for the camp, looking at the exchange rules, it might even be a one-time consumable.

Previously, it wasn’t exchanged because the camp hadn’t encountered any outside enemy invasion, and with the Iron Refining Furnace protection against deceitful monsters, the demand for the Green Bamboo Fence wasn’t that high.
