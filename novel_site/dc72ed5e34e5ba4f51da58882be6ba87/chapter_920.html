Chapter 920: Chapter 346: Formidable Soldiers and Brave Generals, One Against Three, Reason Behind the Beast Tide

"Subordinate here to receive the order!"

After Hou Quan nodded in agreement, he took over the command and led the Dragon Martial Army to continue fighting towards the west side, obviously planning to first rendezvous with the Cloud Dragon Army.

Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao quickly moved three to four kilometers eastwards, arriving in the area where the person just shouted from.

Upon arrival, Xia Chuan discovered that it was Hong Tian’s Divine Martial Army who was fighting the Cold Beasts in this area. His sharp eyes soon noticed on the eastern outskirts, the Divine Martial Army Commander Hong Tian was standing dumbfounded, looking up at the sky.

"What is Hong Tian looking at?"

Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao both showed a hint of curiosity on their faces, then followed Hong Tian’s gaze to look into the sky on the east side.

Upon looking, they too were stunned just like Hong Tian!

Above the eastern sky, at an altitude of roughly 500 meters, a blazing fireball was entangling with three streams of frosty aura.

The fireball moved incredibly fast, covering tens of meters almost in an instant. With each flash, it caused a fierce sonic boom, causing the snow mist in the area to vaporize instantly, and even seemingly dispersing the clouds with sheer force, creating a vacuum in the entire eastern sky.

"In that... inside that fireball is... the Lord!"

"What are those three streams of frosty aura?"

"Seems like Cold Beasts, a Frost Wolf, a Snow Mane, and the last one, the larger one, seems like a White Bear!"

"Can advanced Cold Beasts fly in the sky?"

"What advanced Cold Beasts? Anyone can tell they’re Beast Kings; all three are Beast King Level, and the Lord is fighting one against three?"

"Gulp... Not just one against three; it seems the Lord is actually overwhelming those three Beast Kings!"

......

Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao finally understood why Hong Tian was stunned.

And it wasn’t just the three of them; as Cold Beasts on the battlefield decreased, many Cold Resistance Level individuals were gradually freeing up as well. They looked up and saw Xia Hong in the sky, along with the three fierce-looking Beast Kings.

After seeing clearly, their pupils were instantly filled with horror and shock.

As the last person just said, Xia Hong was indeed overwhelming the three Beast Kings.

Roar......

The Frost Wolf King on the left opened its blood-red mouth and spewed a thick white frost, the frost seemed to carry a mystical force, stirring up a cold current in the air, forming a fierce and violent hurricane, which fiercely rushed toward Xia Hong’s position.

At that moment, the Snowmane King behind also opened its mouth, but it didn’t spew out white frost, instead, it spat out hundreds of sharp, ice-like long tongues, those sharp tongues resembled transparent pythons, not only were they fast, but also incredibly agile, drawing cold edges in the air, which looked very haunting.

The White Bear in front was covered in long white fur, despite being only two meters tall, its hovering posture in the air inexplicably gave off a heavy oppression like a mountain, making it hard to breathe.

"Cold aura, techniques, strength, it seems Beast Kings of different species each have their own strengths, though about other Beast Kings I can’t say, you three today are doomed for encountering me!"

Staring at the three Beast Kings’ distinctively black and white pupils, Xia Hong could almost be sure they could understand the contempt in his eyes.

Roar......

The largest White Bear was the first to lose its cool.

It let out an angry roar, rushing ahead of the Frost Wolf King’s cold aura, being the first to lunge at Xia Hong, its body suddenly expanding dozens of times, from merely two meters, in a flash, it transformed into thirty-five zhangs, like a snow peak crashing down, mountains covered the top, in mid-air, it directly rushed towards Xia Hong to crush him with force.

"Competing with me in strength?"

At that moment, Xia Hong had the Cold Blood Blade hanging by his waist, but seeing the White Bear King lunging at him, he didn’t draw his blade, but took a deep breath, retracting his right fist, gathering force, aimed at the White Bear King’s chest, and suddenly shot forward.

Bang......

A sound far stronger than before echoed abruptly, the entire Arrow Bamboo Forest east side above, the sky within five miles, actually shook in waves.

Immediately following, an extremely horrific scene appeared.

Behind Xia Hong, an enormous dark red phantom a thousand zhangs tall suddenly appeared.

While the thousand zhang phantom synchronizing with his actions, also struck with a right fist at the White Bear’s abdomen before it, the visual effect it created was incomparably different.

That Beast King Level White Bear showcasing its battle form, was merely thirty-five zhangs long, before Xia Hong’s thousand zhang phantom, it appeared so insignificant.

If that thousand zhang phantom was the comparison, the White Bear being punched through its torso was well comprehensible.

As the phantom dispersed, the shockwave gradually expanded, clouds within several miles were actually forced to scatter away.

With clouds being driven off, the White Bear tanking Xia Hong’s punch, positioned at the center of the shockwave, its fate was imaginable.

Its body did not get blown away but was frozen where it stood, yet its back shot out a torrid blood pillar as hot as lava.

At this moment, the White Bear was staring at Xia Hong before it, its distinctly black and white pupils filled with terror and despair, its previously strong vitality also rapidly drained away, it couldn’t muster a roar, just instinctively wanted to turn and flee.

Plop...

How could Xia Hong let it escape, the Cold Blood Blade at his waist plunged into the wound created by the punch.

"When the Lord threw that punch, I seemed to see a thousand zhang tall phantom behind him!"

"I saw it too, it seemed dark red, resembled a skeleton."
