Chapter 599: Chapter 255: Frenzied Fortune-Making, Dangerous Thoughts, Perfect Timing

"60 drops of Cold Apple Jade Dew, 180 stalks of White Frost Grass, 180 stalks of Zhu Yinhua, 60 Red Sun Fruits, 120 stalks of Cold Beard Grass, 60 Red Sun Fruits, 120 Double-colored Liquid Spirit Fruits, 60 Panxiang Leaves, 180 stalks of Jade Spirit Grass, 60 drops of Ice Soul Liquid, 180 stalks of Yin Yang Flower, 60 Linglong Fruits, all these herbs are enough to make 180 portions of Jade Bone Medicine. Long Mountain and Zhaoyang are still quite rich indeed, tsk tsk..."

Looking at the dazzling array of herbs on the ground and hearing Xia Chuan’s sigh, Xia Hong couldn’t help but feel a bit emotional. In the few days he spent in Ping Valley, Hou Hu had no shortage of complaints about the poverty, always implying how precious Jade Bone and Cold Apple Jade Dew were, and how rare the materials for refining Jade Bone Medicine were.

But now, so many could be taken out all at once.

"Both families have quite a number of Cold Resistance Level individuals in the late stage. How did they manage it? They must have a stable production of Cold Apple Jade Dew, and I suspect they also have stable channels for acquiring the other eleven herbs. They just keep them well hidden, and we don’t know about them."

After Xia Hong finished speaking, his gaze shifted to the back of the herbs.

Behind the herbs were two silver stones, each about the size of a baby’s fist.

The stones were irregular in shape, emitting a holy silver glow, shining brightly in the firelight of the hall, making it hard for one not to take a second glance.

"200 jin of silver, yet it’s just this small piece, not even one-tenth the size of a palm; these two pieces weigh 400 jin, still not enough to make a dagger."

At the moment Xia Hong saw the silver, he immediately understood why the two families didn’t use silver to save the people infected with the Dewdrop Plague.

Look at this volume; even if silver is melted into water at low temperature, it’s probably just a tiny bit. How many can it save?

Xia Hong stepped forward to grasp the silver. With a slight application of force, his mind shifted slightly.

"The hardness, density, and weight all far exceed iron ore. The difficulty of melting and forging is definitely much higher than iron. Just this volume, the weight of a piece of silverware must far exceed imagination. No wonder..."

Recalling the time in Gourd Valley, where only Li Xuanling wielded a silver longsword, while the other ten Cold Resistance Level individuals only held silver daggers, Xia Hong’s eyes suddenly showed a flash of understanding.

Initially, he didn’t understand why, if there were a silver mine, they only made such a small dagger. It turns out the density of silver is so astonishing.

Judging by the volume and weight of these two pieces of silver, the daggers in the hands of those ten Cold Resistance Level individuals must weigh at least a thousand jin each.

"I’ve already handed them the remaining 900 blocks of Solidified Fire Oil. We’ll see if the materials from the two families will be delivered in full."

Xia Chuan spoke cheerfully. In truth, the moment this batch of materials arrived, Great Xia had already profited from the transaction. Of course, the next 3 million jin of beast meat, 20 million jin of iron ore, and 50 million jin of coal stone are also crucial. He certainly didn’t want the two families to renege on the deal.

"Most likely, they won’t. As long as they’re not foolish, they can figure out that we still have Solidified Fire Oil in our hands. If they want more in the future, this batch of materials must be delivered in full. Seeing how exaggerated the volume of silver is, I estimate they will soon come to request a second batch of Solidified Fire Oil. Just wait, the materials will soon..."

"Leader, Yuwen Dao and Yuan Cheng have arrived!"

As Xia Hong was speaking, Luo Yuan’s voice suddenly came from outside the door.

Upon hearing that it was Yuwen Dao and Yuan Cheng, who were recovering in the Beehive, Xia Hong’s face immediately showed a smile and said, "Bring them in!"

The Jade Bone Medicine is about to be ready, these two indeed came at a good time.
