Chapter 291: Chapter 161 Who Told You That a Furnace Needs to Be Lit by Someone?_4

Swish...

"An’er!"

Luo Yuan on the second floor saw the direction the dagger was flying, and his face was immediately filled with terror because the child glaring angrily at He Meng was his son, Luo An.

He instinctively started running frantically upwards, but it was utterly futile.

With He Meng’s prowess, even a casually thrown dagger couldn’t be outrun.

In the Ice Abyss World, death was a constant companion to humanity.

Nearly six-year-old Luo An actually had a concept of death.

Seeing the dagger flying towards him, though afraid, he instinctively shielded a younger child beside him with his body.

Clang...

In the nick of time.

A cold glimmer suddenly flew from directly above the valley.

It was a long sword, a sword all the people of Great Xia were immensely familiar with.

A narrow, slender blade of one and a half meters, descending vertically from the sky, with such exaggerated power and speed that it nearly left a trail of snow-white air current.

The air current descended sharply, directly striking the dagger.

The dagger was actually pinned directly to the floor by the sword tip.

Considering the force with which the long sword descended, how could a mere floor block it?

There was only one explanation - the person who threw the long sword used skill.

He only needed to pin down the dagger.

All the Great Xia people looked up towards the sky, their eyes full of hope.

However, He Meng and his crew’s reaction was different; their faces showed slight tension as they looked up.

"Boom...... Crack......"

After the long sword fell, a massive explosion followed.

Above the valley, two intersecting mountain masses seemed to show signs of breaking, with countless rocks falling, as if on the verge of collapsing.

"Ugh... Ah..."

A piercing wail came from above, and Xia Chuan and others initially looked puzzled, but upon noticing the worried expressions of He Meng and his crew, they quickly reacted, their hearts immediately filled with excitement.

"Your strength... how could... how could..."

After the wail, a trembling voice spoke, clearly having discovered something unbelievable, filled with terror and dread.

"Didn’t you intentionally come to the valley to tell me about the Combat Body? Lower, Intermediate, Upper, take a look, which rank does my Combat Body belong to."

Xia Hong’s voice came from above, and the entire Great Xia Camp below was suddenly filled with wild joy and excitement.

Everyone looked up, eyes glinting with anticipation.

Bam...

Another sound of air exploding rang out.

A white figure, like a cannonball, was smashed down from above the valley.

The person crashed directly into the northern stone wall of the valley, penetrating three or four meters in, the stones blasting out could perforate the ground, evidencing the terrifying force the person just endured.

Though the explosive dust in the air completely obscured the scene above, the Great Xia people below continued to watch with fervent anticipation.

In their line of sight, a right leg first penetrated the dust.

Then, Xia Hong in a black cloak, panting heavily, broke through the dust, falling directly from hundreds of meters in the sky.

Bam...

"Big brother!"

"Leader."

"Leader, are you okay?"

...

The moment everyone clearly saw Xia Hong, their expectations vanished, leaving only worry and horror.

Especially Xia Chuan, who couldn’t help but directly rush towards Xia Hong.

Xia Hong’s black cloak was already tattered beyond recognition, his whole body almost soaked with blood. The exposed left arm was filled with countless fine cracks, resembling shattered pottery or cracked dry earth, with the exposed crimson blood amidst the cracks appearing extraordinarily pronounced, and terribly frightening.

There were two wounds on his left face, and a dozen or so blood holes on his chest, all seeping blood outward.

Even on his thigh, there was a large piece of flesh turned outwards.

"I’m fine!"

Despite his severe injuries, Xia Hong still turned to the crowd, giving Xia Chuan and others an approving look, before forcing himself to suppress his injuries and turning to gaze at the position of the stone wall.

"Hehe haha..., discovered, so what if you’ve discovered, can the fire in your furnace be ignited again, you all must die, must die!"

The man in white flew out from the stone wall, without caring about Xia Hong below, but instead leaped directly to the furnace on the third floor.

"Brothers, kill!"

The moment the man in white flew out, He Meng and He Gang’s worries disappeared, replaced with a ruthless look, commanding the two hundred-odd Mirror Immortal Camp members on the perimeter to charge.

Four Intermediate Cold Beasts, glaring fiercely, began to move at this time.

"Who told you my furnace needs someone to ignite it?"

With Xia Hong’s rhetorical question, both the man in white, He Meng, He Gang, and the more than two hundred Mirror Immortal Camp members, even the four Intermediate Cold Beasts, were all momentarily stunned.

Fizzle... fizzle... fizzle...

The sound of a flame burning, though extremely subtle, caused the bodies of the man in white, He Meng, and He Gang to tremble violently.

The previously extinguished Iron Refining Furnace suddenly reignited out of nowhere.

A warmth rapidly spread over a hundred meters.

The firelight instantly enveloped the man in white, He Meng, He Gang, the four Intermediate Cold Beasts, and the more than two hundred Mirror Immortal Camp members.

"Ah... Ah..."

The more than two hundred Mirror Immortal Camp members on the perimeter were the first to let out terrified screams, their eyes instantly transforming into silvery-white liquid form, and as the exposure to the firelight grew longer, silver liquid began to flow out.

Following that, the four Intermediate Cold Beasts also showed signs of distress.

Then He Meng and He Gang.

Finally, even the expression of the man in white began to twist as his eyes underwent the same transformation.
