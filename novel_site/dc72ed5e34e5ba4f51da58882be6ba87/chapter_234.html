The Collection Team, Logging Team, Craftsmen Workshop, and even the Logging Realm at the Camp Supply Station, all requested to join in.

"There’s no need to mobilize so many for dealing with a bunch of riffraff. No need to say more about it—tonight, it’s enough for the seventeen members of the Hunting Team to follow me!"

After Xia Hong finished speaking, he turned and went straight to the third floor, leaving no chance for others to speak.

The crowd on the second floor immediately began to discuss fervently. Members of the Hunting Team dispersed to prepare, except for Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and Yue Feng, who exchanged glances and nodded before going up to the third floor together.

Upon reaching the third floor and seeing Xia Hong’s room door still open, Xia Chuan led the two inside, bowed, and asked with a probing tone:

"Elder brother, did you break through?"

After asking, not just him, but even Luo Yuan and Yue Feng who stood behind him, gazed at Xia Hong with hopeful eyes.

Unfortunately, their hopes fell flat.

Xia Hong shook his head slightly and said in a deep voice, "Not yet, it will take at least another month. You all..."

Seeing the three hesitate upon hearing he hadn’t broken through, Xia Hong immediately understood.

Apparently, his earlier attitude had led to a misunderstanding among them.

"You think that the Mirror Immortal Camp is too strong, and it’s not wise to split with them right now. Even for revenge, we should wait until I’ve broken through, right?"

The three remained silent for a moment, then nodded lightly.

Anger can cause one to lose reason, but time allows calm reflection.

Two days ago, upon first seeing the eight corpses, Xia Chuan was furious, ready to do anything for revenge against the Mirror Immortal Camp.

But after two days of cooling down, he had to admit that seeking revenge now against the Mirror Immortal Camp was indeed impractical.

The two Cold Resistance Level experts were like towering mountains, insurmountable barriers for Great Xia.

At least until Xia Hong’s breakthrough, this was the case.

As for the Earth-digging Realm, just looking at the three previous encounters, the Mirror Immortal Camp clearly outnumbered Great Xia.

Their high-level forces were weaker, and their core strength fewer.

They did not possess the power necessary for revenge.

"No need to fear!"

Xia Hong stood and waved his hand, continuing in a deep voice:

"Unless the two Cold Resistance Level experts from the Mirror Immortal Camp follow them at all times, which is unlikely.

Of course, even if they do, there’s no problem.

Tonight, each of you will carry five torches, just to be prepared.

We won’t concern ourselves with the outside, only hunt them within Redwood Ridge’s kilometer-deep area, until they’re completely driven out of the northern territory!"

While anger was present, Xia Hong had already considered Xia Chuan’s concerns.

If possible, he would certainly prefer not to tear apart relations with the Mirror Immortal Camp so soon.

But the other parties had pushed things too far, leaving no choice.

Not only did they kill, but sought out Great Xia’s camp location.

The murder was already crossing a line; scouting the camp was like trampling Great Xia’s honor.

In the Ice Abyss World, camp locations were forbidden topics.

From the first encounter, Xia Hong considered himself tolerant towards the Mirror Immortal Camp, extending much goodwill.

Last time, he had the chance to kill Yuwen Dao but refrained, instead conveying a message seeking non-aggression;

Three nights ago, he could have used the Cold Beast to kill He Meng and others but refrained, only snatching three of their prey, allowing He Meng’s escape unimpeded.

Clearly, the other side showed no gratitude.

Instead, He Meng brought people to the southern side of Redwood Ridge, showing they truly disregarded Great Xia, considering it a trivial foe.

Investigating camp locations, did they intend to strike at home too?

Xia Hong’s eyes frosted with chill, seeing the worry on Xia Chuan and the others’ faces, knowing their concerns, pondered for a brief moment and then acted.

"Roar..."

Suddenly, a low growl echoed from above.

Xia Chuan and the others instinctively looked up and were startled, their expressions changing, hands reaching for their swords almost simultaneously.

Five Teng Jiao, sixty to seventy meters long, were entwined around the wooden beams, blood basins open, eyes locked on the trio below.

"Five Intermediate Teng Jiao, in the wooden house!"

"Leader..."

Luo Yuan was the first to cry in alarm, yet upon realizing the Teng Jiao didn’t move, combined with Xia Hong’s calm demeanor, his mood shifted to joy.

Xia Chuan and Yue Feng responded even faster, shifting their gaze from the Teng Jiao to Xia Hong, filled with excitement.

"Leader, is this...a wooden mannequin?"

As Xia Chuan asked, the Teng Jiao on the beams transformed back to their original form.

Despite the upgraded wooden mannequins being vastly different from before, Xia Chuan and the others recognized them at once.

After all, only this object could transform into Cold Beasts at the camp.

"Five figures, five Intermediate Cold Beasts, leader, this..."

"With these, we need not fear, even if Cold Resistance Level experts come, we’ve nothing to fear, hahaha, no wonder, no wonder..."
