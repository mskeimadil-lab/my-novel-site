Chapter 819: Chapter 321: Purging the Yanglu Realm, Long Wu Army Soon to Be Formed (4)

After Xia Chuan walked away, another Cold Resistance Level scout from the Reconnaissance Camp, Zhu Xing, immediately ran up to Wang Yuan, full of curiosity, and asked:

"Old Wang, why did the Minister suddenly come over?"

Xia Chuan hadn’t left Xia City for over two months, suddenly coming to the Beehive surely wasn’t a trivial matter, Zhu Xing’s curiosity was understandable.

Wang Yuan clearly knew a bit, and he laughed, "The Minister just said he had important news to inform Yuwen Sizheng, what else could it be, surely it’s about the army formation!"

Upon hearing this, Zhu Xing’s expression immediately changed slightly, and he nodded.

The Dragon Wu Team was just promoted to an Advanced Hunting Team in November. According to the lord’s original rules, the first hunting team to be promoted to advanced level qualifies to establish the second army.

Originally, last month when the Dragon Wu Team was promoted, the army formation should have happened, but it was postponed because the number of people at the camp’s Earth-digging Realm Limit wasn’t enough.

Xia Chuan hurried over to inform Yuwen Dao now, it’s likely nine out of ten that it’s about this matter.

"The lord is eager to clear the Cold Beasts in the Yanglu Realm, relying on our single army is too slow, and the Minister is definitely aiming for this point, to build the Dragon Martial Army as soon as possible, then the two armies will clear together, efficiency will surely be faster!"

Hearing that the Dragon Martial Army was about to form, Zhu Xing clenched the Big Saber in his hand, disbelief etched on his face, he was also reflecting on the fickle nature of life’s opportunities.

Last April, he was just a leader of an ordinary mid-sized camp at Five Ape Mountain, his basic strength barely over twenty thousand pounds. Who would have thought that today he has already broken through to the Cold Resistance Level and is a member of the Cloud Dragon Army’s Reconnaissance Camp under the Minister.

Moreover, Liu Yuan is likely to succeed the Minister, becoming the Commander of the Cloud Dragon Army, by then Wang Yuan will surely take the position of Colonel of the Reconnaissance Camp, and Zhu Xing is likely to become the second-in-command of the Reconnaissance Camp.

No, Liu Yuan recently intended to promote him to become a Colonel of the Nine Camps, maybe after the Yanglu affair is over, Liu Yuan will apply to the Commander?

Zhu Xing suppressed his excitement and thought of what Wang Yuan just said about the Dragon Martial Army, he laughed and said, "The second army, our Cloud Dragon Army will no longer be the only one, Director and Minister have some shadows of rivalry, now with two army designations coming out, their future competition will surely be more intense!"

Wang Yuan was taken aback, but quickly nodded in agreement with his statement.

The open and covert competition between Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao in Great Xia wasn’t exactly a secret, almost everyone knew it, the Cloud Jiao and Long Wu teams from low-level hunting teams to advanced hunting teams, and now to army formation, it is essentially a process of them chasing each other, anyone slightly clever can see it.

"The Minister and Director are both favored children of heaven, only in Great Xia, with the lord suppressing them, otherwise placed in any other camp, these two could never coexist peacefully."

Zhu Xing nodded, showing complete agreement on his face.

"Alright, inform the soldiers to quickly prepare the prey, heading back to the Beehive, after today’s clearance, we can rest for a few more days."

"Alright, I’ll go notify them now."

"Prepare the prey, ready to return to Beehive Camp!"

Deep within the Yanglu Realm, Xia Chuan walked westward in the White Mist Zone, traveling only three to four kilometers, he heard sounds coming from ahead.

"Tighten the ropes, don’t let them come over!"

"Yue Feng, take eleven people around to the back, block those three Demon Sheep."

"Xuanling, take the remaining people, eliminate those five Striped Tigers, all other Cold Beasts leave to me, go!"

......

"It’s big brother’s voice, already coming deep in here!"

Recognizing Xia Hong’s voice and knowing he was handling the Cold Beasts, Xia Chuan immediately leaped forward, swiftly rushing over.
