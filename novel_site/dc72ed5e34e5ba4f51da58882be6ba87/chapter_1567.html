Chapter 1567 - 492: Invisible Assassin, Stupidity, Crisis and Panic (Part 2)

Chapter 1567: Chapter 492: Invisible Assassin, Stupidity, Crisis and Panic (Part 2)

The Xianyang Level warrior, who actively shielded his companions from arrows, swung his Big Saber swiftly, yet could not block the rain of arrows shot from all directions towards the carriage. In the end, two or three-tenths of the arrows pierced through his blade defense and struck his body.

Two or three-tenths of the arrows amounted to more than 200 arrows.

Without a hint of suspense, his entire body was almost instantly riddled with holes by the arrows. Although the sound showed that the arrows did not penetrate his bones, the terrifying impact of the arrow rain still made his body sway back and forth, and his speed of blade swinging dropped by more than half, causing the blade screen to slowly dissipate.

Not only him, but the person behind him also had a dozen arrows sticking on their body.

Yet Xianyang Level is Xianyang Level after all, even the man who blocked the arrows, despite being shot to look like a sieve, continued to protect his face and crucial areas with his raised hand, indicating that this volley of arrows did not deal a fatal blow to him.

Given his condition, the person behind who only got hit by a dozen arrows was even less affected. He had already leaped onto the roof of the carriage, and his Longsword suddenly pierced down through the roof.

Bang!

The two of them had been lurking invisibly for so long, using wind sounds to confuse, attacking from the north side, braving the intensive arrow rain, finally nearing the carriage; the strength in that man’s strike was imaginable.

He was well aware that surrounded by over a thousand Dragon Forbidden Captains, his chance to strike would be this time, so he had to succeed at once.

Boom...

The sword edge abruptly fell, causing the ground around the carriage to tremble fiercely, the force transmitted to the ground, abruptly raising the surrounding snow, instantly enshrouding the entire carriage in a snow fog tens of meters high, including hundreds of Dragon Forbidden Captains nearby.

"25 Jun strength only managed to break a hole, how is this possible..."

Within the snow fog, the swordsman, Cai Qiu’s invisible assassin, looking at the Longsword in his hand which only managed to create a hole less than half a meter in diameter on the top of the carriage, his pupils suddenly contracted, his voice full of astonishment.

Whizz...

However, his horror did not last long.

Inside the carriage suddenly flew out two green spiritual lights, hitting his sword-wielding right hand and neck position. The assassin looked down to find two thumb-thick green tiny snakes.

"Soaring Snake King, Great Xia has more than twelve Beast Kings..."

As the final thought flashed through the invisible assassin’s mind, the two tiny snakes at his neck and hand positions abruptly twisted their bodies, their lengths expanded almost instantly to hundreds of meters, biting the assassin in their mouths, directly rising into the air, tearing him into pieces right there.

This wasn’t the end. The other person who helped block arrows, though attempted to retreat when the two green tiny snakes flew out from the car roof, the Dragon Forbidden Captains were unwilling to let him escape.

"Protect the Second Prince!"

"Charge to the roof of the carriage, don’t let the assassin escape!"

"Charge up!"

......

Lu Dong, Luo An, Zou Ping, and other young Dragon Forbidden Captains roared furiously, rushing towards the assassin trying to flee.

Their roars made all surrounding soldiers charge altogether, their target was obviously the carriage.

Tie Xinchuan saw countless soldiers surrounding the carriage, his heart inexplicably skipped a beat, then his brows furrowed, angrily shouting: "Fools, all of you back off, what are you doing!"

Not mentioning the two Xianyang Level warriors, Cheng Guang and Jiang Ping, inside the carriage, the two Beast King Level Teng Jiao also indicated that Madam had already set multiple safeguards for the Second Prince’s safety; there was no need for them to worry.

All these Dragon Forbidden Captains surrounded the carriage, while they managed to hold the assassin, they were making the situation completely chaotic.

The second assassin, regardless of run or stay, was bound to die.

However, once the situation around the carriage fell into chaos, things would worsen.

Presently, among the thousand Dragon Forbidden Captains present, half of them had just been on alert near the Milk Tiger Forest and were returning to regroup.

Did any outsiders slip amongst them? Who knows?

"All get back, back off completely, don’t let others exploit this!"

Dragon Forbidden Vice Commander Yuan Bai was the second to react. He almost shouted following Tie Xinchuan.

Even so, the speed was still half a beat too slow for both.

Lu Dong, Luo An, and other Eight Camp Commanders, plus hundreds of Dragon Forbidden soldiers, had already rushed out halfway, either standing on the carriage roof surrounding the assassin or encircling the carriage.

Boom...

Upon hearing Tie Xinchuan and Yuan Bai’s shouts, Lu Dong and others realized the chaos around the carriage, though they quickly reacted, they were late.

A violent gust suddenly exploded among the Dragon Forbidden soldiers, pushing away dozens of soldiers crowded on the carriage roof; the shockwave of the gust even pushed hundreds of soldiers around.

The soldiers’ Yellow Candles were pushed away as well.

Clang... Clang...

Two fierce clashing sounds of metal instantly rang from inside the carriage.

Ding...

An intense white light subsequently burst forth from the large hole on the carriage roof, Tie Xinchuan and Yuan Bai, including Lu Dong, Luo An, and the dispersed Dragon Forbidden soldiers, suddenly turned pale.

"Auntie Han Shuang..."

A shocked voice from Xia Yusheng came from inside the carriage, Tie Xinchuan’s pupils tightened sharply, quickly rushed to the carriage front, worried about what was happening inside, as he got closer, he directly reached to lift the curtain.

Woo...

Just as he touched the curtain, a palm wind came head-on, the palm’s force was terrifying, and the speed was astounding, completely unexpected. He couldn’t avoid it, only barely managed to turn his shoulder to withstand it.
