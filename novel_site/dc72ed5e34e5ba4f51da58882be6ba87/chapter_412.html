Chapter 412: Chapter 197: Cloud Jiao Advances, Beast Blood Dosage_4

Qiu Peng fell silent upon hearing the voice, remembering clearly that initially, Luo Yuan’s Basic Strength was above Xia Chuan’s. But now, not only has his Basic Strength been surpassed by Xia Chuan, even the amount of Beast Blood used can’t compare.

According to the leader’s words, when they break through to the Cold Resistance Level in the future, the gap in strength between Luo Yuan and Xia Chuan will become even larger.

From Qiu Peng’s perspective, this matter is actually normal.

After all, Xia Chuan is the leader’s biological brother.

His talent is naturally absurdly high.

Of course, Qiu Peng wouldn’t say this to provoke Luo Yuan.

"I never thought of surpassing the Minister, but seeing the gap widen like this, I can’t help but feel a bit unwilling!"

Upon hearing Luo Yuan’s words, Qiu Peng didn’t know how to comfort him, so he could only smile and say, "The Minister’s talent is actually manageable, we can at least see some possibility of catching up. Think of the leader, that’s truly despairing..."

After saying this, Qiu Peng walked away.

Left alone, Luo Yuan’s expression changed from odd to relieved. Finally, he clenched his fists and headed home to start his cultivation.

Indeed!

Although the Minister is formidable, he is at least within the realm of understanding.

When Xia Hong initially broke through the Cold Resistance Level, he used over a hundred pounds and even more, which everyone witnessed firsthand.

Thinking of him, Luo Yuan was no longer as upset.

......

The Great Xia Valley’s appearance is now completely different compared to the beginning of the month.

Previously, the entire valley only had the largest wooden building at the center and the Martial Arts Hall, Forge, Medicine Workshop buildings at the back.

Now, along the eastern and western sides of the four buildings, sparse rows of fifty to sixty houses have been built along the edges of three main roads.

Xia Chuan’s home is located closest to the wooden building.

The original allocated land was ten meters by ten meters, so the houses built by the Craftsmen Department are all three-story small buildings, not spacious but comfortable to live in.

On the second floor of a small building, Xiao Ning and Xiao Yu, the sisters, are standing at the door, their eyes fixed on the inner silent room, their faces full of worry and anxiety.

"Heh... Heh..."

From inside the silent room, suppressed painful growls of Xia Chuan incessantly echoed.

"Breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, must it be this painful? Can’t he use a little less Beast Blood at once? Applying ten pounds in one go, could there be any problems?"

Upon hearing Xiao Yu’s words, Xiao Ning gently shook her head and said, "Big brother said before, the more Beast Blood used, the stronger the power when breaking through the Cold Resistance Level, and future potential will also be greater. Husband probably doesn’t want to disappoint big brother and thus is pushing himself so hard. Didn’t husband just say, Yuwen Dao can now use twelve pounds of Beast Blood at once, he cannot lose."

Upon hearing these words, Xiao Yu momentarily froze, understanding deep down, but upon hearing Xia Chuan’s persistent and painful growls, she couldn’t help but feel a bit heartache.

"Pain from using only ten pounds, then husband mentioned his big brother previously used over a hundred pounds at once or even more, what kind of willpower must that be!"

Xiao Yu’s mind conjured this thought, feeling heartache for her husband while also developing a genuine admiration for this big brother’s willpower.

"You guys go out first, I have something to discuss with Xia Chuan."

In midst of worry, a gentle voice suddenly came from the side.

Xiao Ning and Xiao Yu immediately trembled, turning their heads only to find a man in black standing silently behind them.

"Greetings, leader!"

The visitor, was Xia Hong dressed in black.

Hearing the two’s address, Xia Hong jokingly said, "We’re all family, no need to be so formal in private."

The two sisters blushed slightly and corrected themselves, "Greetings, big brother."

"Alright, you all go attend to your own matters, Xia Chuan is fine here."

Xiao Ning nodded, taking Xiao Yu with her as they turned to leave.

Xia Hong turned his head and looked at Xia Chuan inside the silent room, observing his skin for a while, a hint of surprise arose in his eyes before he walked inside.
