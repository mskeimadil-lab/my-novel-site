Chapter 683: Chapter 284: Astounding Training Results, The Voyeur_3

They leaped down from the trees, not splitting into units of twenty like the other nine camps, but instead targeting those final dozen Intermediate Cold Beasts with full force.

Blinding, piercing, grabbing the weaknesses of these dozen Intermediate Cold Beasts, the hundred elite troops acted steadily, accurately, fiercely, and in an instant, pinned these Intermediate Cold Beasts firmly in place.

Though not killing them, compared to the other nine camps, the suppression on the Cold Beasts after these hundred elite troops entered the field was significantly stronger.

And in this interval, the other nine camps had also dealt with the remaining thirty-plus Low-level Cold Beasts.

With their hands free, they didn’t need Xia Chuan to issue further commands; they all charged forward, directly surrounding the dozen Intermediate Cold Beasts.

The people from the nine camps, working with the hundred elite troops, executed a full-scale assault on these final dozen Intermediate Cold Beasts, taking less than fifty breaths to complete.

Bang!

With the last Intermediate Frost Wolf trying to flee collapsing, this small-scale beast tide was completely declared over.

"126 Low-level, 16 Intermediate, totaling 142 Cold Beasts, the smallest three-digit beast tide, took a total of two hundred eighty-nine breaths, thirty breaths less than the last time, not bad!"

Xia Chuan, clad in pale golden armor, turned to look at his thousand troops behind him, a smile appearing on his face.

Upon hearing the results he announced, Liu Yuan, closest to him, and over a thousand people behind him, all showed expressions of great excitement.

"Getting faster and faster, at this rate, perhaps later we won’t even need two hundred breaths to deal with a wave of small beast tide."

"What two hundred breaths, Minister said our goal is within a hundred breaths, I estimate, a few more times should be enough."

"Stop the nonsense, in the army, we should call him Commander."

"Right, right, forgot."

...

Hearing everyone’s joking, Xia Chuan’s eyes also showed a hint of exhilaration.

After the Cloud Dragon Army arrived at Wushuang on the night of June 9, Xia Chuan immediately led the army into Long Mountain the next day to start military training.

In half a month’s time, from dealing with a few beasts at a time at the start, to attracting over ten Cold Beasts with small fire torches, taking three nights;

Then lighting a large bonfire tonight and actively creating a small-scale beast tide, taking four nights;

In the following eight days, Xia Chuan had the Cloud Dragon Army handle two such small-scale beast tides every night.

From initial damage and taking several hours, even up to one or two hours, then a few tens of minutes, shortening to several hundred breaths, the growth of Cloud Dragon Army soldiers has become stronger each day.

By today, they can already compress the time to under three hundred breaths.

The effect of this half-month military training has already far exceeded his expectations.

"This is the power of an elite army. I was previously worried that withdrawing elite personnel from the Hunting Team might affect the camp’s supply of Beast Meat, but looking back, it’s ridiculous. Such an elite army has terrifying hunting abilities. If I could form a few more, the camp in the future would certainly not lack Beast Meat and Beast Blood!"

Xia Chuan sighed slightly, then looked at the soldiers of the ten camps, a glimmer appeared in his eyes.

"Big brother is right, Mo Blade Camp, Horizontal Blade Camp, Elite Scout Camp, focusing on distinguishing between the three troop types and choosing the timing for entering the battlefield is more important than enhancing their individual strength.

Further refinement of troop classification must continue, so that on the real battlefield, their power will be even more terrifying."

"Who, come out!"

Just as Xia Chuan was feeling emotional, Liu Yuan, who was tidying up the battlefield on the other side, seemed to have discovered something. He shouted fiercely and charged towards a large tree on the left side of the battlefield’s periphery.

"Someone is spying!"

Xia Chuan’s brows furrowed sharply, turning towards Liu Yuan’s direction.

The Cloud Dragon Army arrived at Wushuang on June 9, and on the 10th, he urgently led the army into Long Mountain. Military training was, of course, the most important objective.

But there was another layer of purpose; he didn’t want the Cloud Dragon Army to be known too early by Long Mountain and Zhaoyang families.

Being spied upon is a big taboo!

Liu Yuan had already caught a person and brought them over.

Xia Chuan looked at that person, a flash of killing intent appeared in his eyes.
