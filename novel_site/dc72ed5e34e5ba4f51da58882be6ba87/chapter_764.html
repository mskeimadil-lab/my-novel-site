Chapter 764: Chapter 306: Helplessness, Shifting Loyalties, Total Annihilation and New Territory (Part 5)

"The fire is getting bigger, leader."

"Leader..."

.........

"It’s over, it’s over, it’s over..."

Li Xuanling watched the Longyou soldiers being massacred at the tunnel exit, tears constantly rolling down her cheeks, her expression extremely bleak. She seemed unable to hear the shouts of Meng Ying, Cheng Guang, and others, only murmuring softly that it was over.

"Leader, wake up quickly. It’s too late to regret now!"

"Leader, make a decision!"

"Fourth Brother, leave some green..."

Li Xuandu’s call of "Fourth Brother" finally brought Li Xuanling back to reality.

He obviously felt that his following words were not suitable to shout loudly, so he only said three words and immediately stopped, just turning his head to look at Li Xuanling.

Li Xuanling’s face was full of hesitation and struggle. She knew what Li Xuandu’s gaze meant, nothing more than asking her to order all the Cold Resistance Level to escape.

Of course, they could escape, but what about the Longyou soldiers?

"There’s no other way, Fourth Brother. Even if we fight to the death, we can’t stop so many Cold Beasts. Look behind us, don’t hesitate anymore."

Li Xuandu finally couldn’t hold back, directly speaking bluntly.

Li Xuanling turned and looked around. Seeing more and more Cold Beasts surrounding them, she immediately clenched her fist and shouted, "Break through to the west!"

Li Xuanyan, Li Xuandu, Li Xuanhong, and many of the Li Family’s Cold Resistance Level immediately stopped fighting the Cold Beasts and quickly gathered together, breaking through to the west.

Meng Ying, Jiang Ping, Cheng Guang, and Song Kang, the Cold Resistance Level from other families, glanced at the Longyou soldiers near the tunnel with a flash of reluctance, but after hesitating for a moment, they also ran towards the west.

"No, don’t leave us, leader, save me!"

"Sir, you can’t leave us, we’ll all die here."

"Help, leader, leader, save us."

"I am also a descendant of the Li Family, save me, Uncle Three."

"Don’t abandon us, leader."

"You beasts, may you die a bad death."

"Li Xuanling, you coward, do you even deserve to be a leader."

"Bitch, you beasts won’t get away."

.........

When Li Xuanling gave up, the fate of the Longyou soldiers was already predictable. With all the Cold Resistance Level gone, they were like lambs to the slaughter against the Cold Beasts.

In the face of death, some begged desperately, some cursed loudly, some insulted all forty-plus Cold Resistance Level, and some even directly targeted Li Xuanling as the leader.........

No matter how they reacted, they ultimately couldn’t change the impending deadly situation.

More than forty Cold Resistance Level striking simultaneously found it easy to break through. Li Xuanling quickly led everyone out of the Beast Tide circle, running all the way to near the Wushuang Giant Stone Camp, daring to stop only then, looking back toward the southeast.

Bang, bang, bang........

Seeing more and more Cold Beasts rush to where the big fire was, tears continuously flowed down Li Xuanling’s cheeks, her expression becoming more despondent and hopeless.

Li Xuanyan, Li Xuandu, and many surrounding Cold Resistance Level watched the fire direction, their expressions full of shame, guilt, anger, and despair... their emotions entangled, clearly reaching a complex extreme.

"Those four bastards are from Great Xia."

"They clearly came prepared. All four planned it, first drawing our attention, then seizing the chance to ignite the target with fire arrows."

"It’s a pity Xuanping and Xuanqiu are gone; if they were here, maybe..."

"Great Xia, it’s all Great Xia’s fault, forcing us to retreat, knowing we’d use the tunnel. They had planted those Cold-Attracting Beasts there long ago."

"This is Long Mountain’s last capital. We must take revenge, definitely take revenge on the Xia Hong brothers, kill them, destroy Great Xia."

..............

Li Xuanling, with a desolate look, completely ignored the crowd’s arguments. She even ignored Meng Ying mentioning Li Xuanping siblings and the four from Great Xia just doing the burning. Despite knowing what’s happening, she still didn’t pursue it, clearly in the depths of despair.

"Fourth Brother, don’t be like this. The destruction of the Longyou Army is already a fact, don’t dwell on it. We still have Long Mountain, just the forty-plus Cold Resistance Level, let’s go back to the camp and see what’s happening!"

"That’s right, the camp should not have been breached by the Xia Army. Let’s go back first."

..............

Hearing Long Mountain, Li Xuanling finally got a bit of spirit, turning around to look around the crowd, clenching her fist to force herself to perk up.

"Let’s go back and take a look."

After saying that, she led everyone, heading toward Long Mountain in the west.

Including Li Xuanling, no one looked back at the fire behind, only rushing toward Long Mountain.

..............

"This Li Xuanling is really ruthless, abandoning the 2,300-plus Longyou Army, just like that. Judging by the scale of the Cold Beasts, few might survive."

"Cough, cough, cough... It’s not called ruthless. There are indeed too many Advanced Cold Beasts. Even if she fights desperately with those forty-plus Cold Resistance Level, not much could be saved. Reason prevailed, I guess!"

On a snow hill five kilometers east of the fire, hearing Yuwen Dao’s joke, Xia Chuan lightly coughed twice and smiled, speaking up for Li Xuanling.

Yuwen Dao shook his head and said, "This Li Xuanling is smart, but a bit too rational. I remember she’s done this twice already. Such a leader, it’s no surprise if Long Mountain loyalty wanes."

"It’s just a pity for those 2,000-plus Longyou soldiers. They were all good seedlings at the Earth-digging Realm Limit, tsk tsk... cough..."
