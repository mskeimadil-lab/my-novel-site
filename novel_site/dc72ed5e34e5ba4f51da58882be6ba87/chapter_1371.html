Chapter 1371: Chapter 443: White Phoenix Forest of Snow Phoenix Mountain, Five-Colored Demon Sound Rabbit, Trying to Outwit Me?

"Ee..."

Suddenly, Xia Hong sensed something, turning his head toward the depths of Snow Phoenix Mountain, an expression of surprise crossing his face.

Three kilometers to the east, there were two Xianyang Level auras!

And both were going all out, clearly trying to deal with something.

Xia Hong looked at the Beast King Level Snow Phoenix in front of him and the hundreds of Snow Phoenixes that had not yet retreated, then turned again to glance at Cai Mengya on the ground. Noticing her gaze occasionally shifting toward the deeper area, he pondered for a moment before slightly furrowing his brow.

He did not look again at the Beast King Level Snow Phoenix but instead sneered, the flames around his body suddenly expanding into a strong light, heading straight east.

"Screech..."

As Xia Hong suddenly flew toward the east, the Snow Phoenix was obviously caught off guard. However, it quickly sensed something, letting out a long, plaintive cry, and abruptly pursued Xia Hong’s trail.

The hundreds of Advanced Snow Phoenixes in mid-air heard the cry, swiftly changing course to follow to the east.

"He’s been discovered! Uncle Bai, quickly come with me to help!"

Upon seeing Xia Hong and the Snow Phoenix’s movements, Cai Mengya’s expression immediately changed. Ignoring the large army in front of her, she shouted and quickly dashed towards the depths of Snow Phoenix Mountain in the east.

As she finished speaking, a strong light shot out from the forest behind her, rushing ahead of her toward the east.

Buzz...

Three kilometers into the depths of Snow Phoenix Mountain to the east, a strange and unique melody suddenly sounded from within a mud slope. The moment the sound emerged, a sword and a blade, two beams of strong light, abruptly rushed toward the mud slope.

Boom...

The mud slope, barely a hundred meters in diameter and only three to four meters high, exploded violently as both the blade and sword qi struck it simultaneously, flattening it in an instant.

As the slope was blasted open, a bizarre giant rabbit was revealed inside!

This rabbit, stretching five meters long, was truly enormous.

The key thing was, its face had only a nose and mouth, shockingly lacking eyes. Moreover, it was not injured at all, clearly born without eyes rather than blinded later.

Its bizarre features did not end there; this giant rabbit surprisingly had five tails of gold, green, blue, red, and yellow.

Humming...

Its five tails swayed in the wind, constantly colliding to produce a peculiar and unique melody. Despite the chilling wind whistling around, the sound was exceedingly clear and strangely compelling.

"Finally found it. I knew it was hiding here!"

"Don’t waste time. As agreed, whoever gets the Magic Sound Rabbit will marry Miss Mengya."

The voices of the two men who wielded the sword and blade spoke simultaneously.

The two appeared to be in their early thirties, youthful in appearance, both clad in Ten Thousand Forged Silver Armor, with a fierce aura between their brows.

Upon speaking, the man holding the blade did not wait for a response and charged straight at the Magic Sound Rabbit; the man with the sword was not to be outdone, simultaneously dashing toward the Magic Sound Rabbit.

Humming...

Though the Magic Sound Rabbit had no eyes, the urgent melody produced by its five colliding tails seemed to express an angry emotion.

As the sword and blade neared, the Magic Sound Rabbit’s five tails collided sharply, producing a clear, explosive sound like a silver bottle suddenly shattering.

Ding...

The two silver-armored youths, dressed like generals, suddenly froze.

Yet their freeze lasted less than half a moment, quickly recovering as they slashed their sword and blade toward the Magic Sound Rabbit.

Whoosh...

However, a strong light descended from above, beating them to it.

"Playing mind games with me?"

Xia Hong’s sneer rang out, causing the expressions of the two silver-armored youths to suddenly stiffen.
