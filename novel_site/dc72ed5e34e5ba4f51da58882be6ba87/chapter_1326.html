Chapter 1326: Chapter 432: Shattered Star Sword, Yellow Mustard Bag, Departure Preparations, Rankings and Rewards (Part 4)

The Great Xia has always grouped competitors based on their cultivation levels, usually dividing them into Cold Resistance Level, Earth-digging Realm Limit, and Earth-digging Realm. Honestly, this makes the competitions much more exciting, though not as rich in topics.

The young people are absolutely the most watched group at all camps;

Plus, their pursuit of rankings on these lists far exceeds that of ordinary people.

Geniuses are inherently focal points, not to mention that Great Xia provides a platform for all young people to showcase themselves publicly.

Instantly, everyone who came to the Inner City to watch the competition today fell into heated discussions.

"Next comes the rewards for the top ten!"

However, the heated discussions were quickly interrupted.

As Lin Kai began to announce the rewards, everyone, including the twenty young people standing on the stage, couldn’t help but breathe heavily.

"Youth Group Champion: 500,000 taels of silver, 30 Yangyuan Pill, one customized Ten Thousand Forged Weapon, 50 pounds of refined salt, 10 pounds of top-grade spiritual wine, 50 meters of silk, 5 pounds of Beast King Level blood, 20 pounds of beast meat, 1 Suppression Talisman;

Runner-up: 400,000 taels of silver, 30 Yangyuan Pill, one customized Ten Thousand Forged Weapon, 40 pounds of refined salt, 8 pounds of top-grade spiritual wine, 40 meters of silk, 4 pounds of Beast King Level blood, 15 pounds of beast meat, 1 Suppression Talisman.

Third place.........

Teen Group Champion: 300,000 taels of silver, 30 Yangyuan Pill, one customized Ten Thousand Forged Weapon, 50 pounds of refined salt, 10 pounds of top-grade spiritual wine, 50 meters of silk, 5 pounds of Beast King Level blood, 20 pounds of beast meat, 1 Suppression Talisman;

Runner-up: 250,000 taels of silver, 30 Yangyuan Pill, one customized Ten Thousand Forged Weapon, 40 pounds of refined salt, 8 pounds of top-grade spiritual wine, 40 meters of silk, 4 pounds of Beast King Level blood, 15 pounds of beast meat, 1 Suppression Talisman.

Third place......"

The rewards for the top ten in both Youth and Teen Groups are mostly the same, except for the varying amounts of silver. The higher the rank, the richer the rewards, and they gradually decrease, with the tenth place receiving only 100,000 taels of silver, and the other rewards significantly discounted.

Yet even so, after Lin Kai finished reading the rewards, everyone below instantly fell into a dead silence, breathing heavily.

Great Xia strictly controls silver resources, and families are not allowed to hoard silver. Unlike other camps, the Xia people still prefer to measure wealth with Contribution Value.

At the Camp Supply Department, 1 tael of silver can now be exchanged for 10 Contribution Value points, meaning even the tenth place’s silver reward can be directly converted to 1 million Contribution Value points. Converting the other eight items, the total value of those rewards is at least around 2 million Contribution Value points.

The Youth Group Champion, Tie Xinchuan, earns at least 6 million points in one go!

Not just those listening below, even the twenty people standing on the stage clearly hadn’t expected such astonishing rewards; their expressions instantly froze.

After a long time, the twenty finally reacted, simultaneously bowing their heads, paying homage to the Lord on the main hall, and shouted in unison: "Greetings, Lord, may the Lord reign for ten thousand years, may Great Xia endure for ten thousand years!"

"Greetings, Lord, may the Lord reign for ten thousand years, may Great Xia endure for ten thousand years!"

It wouldn’t be surprising if it were just the Xia offspring, but even the four from outside towns, including Xiahou Qingqing, followed suit in bowing down, with no visible sign of anomaly on their faces.

This scene naturally made the smile on Lin Kai’s face grow even broader.

His expression suddenly froze as if hearing something, swiftly looking up toward the main hall, realizing the chair was still empty, and quickly understood something and said with a smile: "You twenty are lucky today, go up the hall to meet the Lord!"

Upon hearing Lin Kai’s words, a group of young people’s expressions turned incredibly excited.

Especially Lu Dong, Luo An, Liu Peng, Zou Ping, Zhao Yuandao, Zhao Yuankong, and so on; those Great Xia young people who’ve idolized Xia Hong since childhood, their faces turned rapidly red with excitement, and without thinking, walked toward the main hall stairs.

Only the last person from the Youth Group had hesitation in his eyes.

But seeing everyone quickly walking toward the main hall, he could only grit his teeth and follow.

"Don’t go, you can’t go up."

"You were the one who wanted me to compete, and now you know fear? Running is impossible, you’ll just die faster, I have no other choice, you better hide well, if discovered, we’re both doomed!"

Fu Tiandu followed behind the Youth Group, looking at the glittering main hall, unlike others full of excitement, his face was full of tension and unease.

However, aside from these, deep within his pupils, there seemed to be a trace of hope.

This trace of hope was deeply concealed by him, almost impossible to see.
