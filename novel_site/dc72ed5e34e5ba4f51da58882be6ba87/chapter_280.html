Chapter 280: Chapter 159: Blood and Flesh Wall, The Leader Hasn’t Broken Through Yet?_2

At this moment, the scene outside the passage finally imprinted itself into the eyes of Xia Chuan and the Earth-digging Realm experts on the third floor.

"Gulp! Three hundred, four hundred... just how many are there?"

Yuan Cheng’s pupils quaked as he swallowed a mouthful of saliva, as if he couldn’t even feel the cold of the outside world. His body’s shivering was abruptly stopped by sheer fright.

Not only him, but also Luo Yuan, the three Zhao Long brothers, Yue Feng, Lin Kai, Xu Ning, and the rest of the Earth-digging Realm individuals on the third floor, all had their faces instantly filled with despair.

At the mouth of the valley, countless Cold Beasts were flooding in relentlessly.

The first to charge in were the larger Snow Manes and Demon Sheep, running side by side, each about three to five meters long, numbering nearly a hundred each.

No wonder, no wonder all the rubble and wood at the mouth of the valley couldn’t hold them back.

Two or three hundred Snow Manes and Demon Sheep, even if they’re only low-level, the destruction they cause by charging simultaneously is enough to shake the heavens and earth.

Even worse, above these two beasts, Frost Wolves are continuously climbing up the northern and southern sides of the valley, clawing at the stone walls and circling to charge toward the wooden house from the sides;

Directly above the valley, at the intersecting stone walls of Twin Dragon Mountain, heads of Teng Jiaos protruded, their eyes blood-red, staring at the wooden house below. They too descended the mountain wall towards the east side of the wooden house, which was the deepest part of the valley.

"What are you all standing there for, waiting to die?"

In Xia Chuan’s trembling shout, one could see that his despair was no less than the others’.

But despite the despair, his actions of drawing the bow and notching the arrow were not slow at all, quickly shooting an iron arrow at a low-level Snow Mane that had already started crashing into the wooden wall.

"Everyone, don’t stand around dazed. No matter what, we can’t just stand here waiting to die!"

"That’s right, let’s fight these damned beasts. I’ve just broken through to the Earth-digging Realm, so I need to take one down with me."

"With the wooden wall blocking them, killing one is a victory."

...

Everyone else snapped out of it at this moment, shouting angrily to bolster their courage, stepping forward quickly to draw their bows and shoot arrows crazily outside the wooden wall.

Swoosh... swoosh... swoosh...

Not only those in the corridor, but in the shadows inside the wooden house, Luo Yuan led another group passing arrows to Xia Chuan’s group while finding openings to shoot arrows at the outside as well.

Bang...

The first Snow Mane struck the wooden wall several times, yet it didn’t budge at all.

In fact, not only did it not move, but the wooden wall seemed to become even more solid under its impacts, with each ensuing strike causing less and less commotion.

Not only that, the part of the Snow Mane’s body that crashed against the wall showed many wounds, from which dark green blood flowed.

Seeing this scene, Xia Chuan and the others’ eyes lit up immediately.

The wooden wall on the west side had already been piled up a hundred meters thick and over ten meters high, laid out in a crisscross pattern with many specially made iron spikes. ree

Roar...

The pain triggered the ferocity of the Snow Mane, and after a furious growl, it didn’t retreat but instead mustered more strength to charge again.

Whoosh!

But Xia Chuan and the others on the upper floor wouldn’t give it a second chance.

Dozens of iron arrows shot through the many gaps in the wooden walls, directly hitting the body of the Snow Mane. It’s worth noting that each of these arrows had an impact force of over ten thousand pounds.

The seventy-centimeter-long pure iron arrows mostly penetrated the flesh of the Snow Mane, deeply embedding into its body. A few arrows even pierced through, with arrowheads protruding from the other side.

The few arrows that pierced through were naturally shot by Xia Chuan, Zhao Long, and a few others.

In the blink of an eye, the Snow Mane was riddled with iron arrows. Despite its increased aggressiveness, its strength clearly waned compared to before.

Seeing the Snow Mane’s weakening strength, Xia Chuan’s eyes shone even brighter.

"The ones at the front are just low-level Cold Beasts. With the wooden walls and our arrows, we can hold them off!"

Xia Chuan and the Hunting Team weren’t truly afraid of low-level Cold Beasts; their earlier distraction and despair were merely due to the overwhelming numbers.

This scene had an even greater impact on those recently breaking through to the Earth-digging Realm.

They had yet to venture out to hunt Cold Beasts, and were not only unfamiliar with them but also had a natural fear. Now, seeing their iron arrows causing harm, and potentially even killing a Snow Mane, their confidence soared.

"I thought they were much stronger. Brothers, we’re hunting now too!"

"Hahaha, thank goodness Lord Xia Chuan prepared the wooden walls in advance."

"It’s not just the wooden walls. If we hadn’t built so many bows and arrows in the past two days, we wouldn’t be this fortunate now."

...

Swoosh... swoosh... swoosh...

The morale-boosting shouts did little to improve Xia Chuan’s complexion.

Because in their voices, he could hear a distinct tone of despair.

Quite normal, as they had only just struck the first one.

Next to that mortally wounded Snow Mane, more were endlessly lining up behind; to its right stood the larger Golden-eyed Demon Sheep, which had already arrived.

The instant impact force of the Snow Mane and the Demon Sheep was no less than ten thousand pounds each.

No matter how solid the wooden structure was, or the number of iron spikes installed, one, maybe two, might be stopped, perhaps even a dozen with Xia Chuan and others’ arrows coordinating could withstand them for a while.

But at this moment, what was charging in from outside the valley were hundreds.

Bam...

The Demon Sheep reached the wooden wall, and with a ferocious push from its six sharp horns, the wooden wall exploded and scattered.
