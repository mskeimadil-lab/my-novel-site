Chapter 519: Chapter 231: Investigating Yang Lu by Day—Whose Side Are We On?

"Wushuang and Hanqiong each have three Cold Resistance Levels. On Wushuang’s side, aside from the leader Wu Xiong and Wu Ying, there is also Duan Pingyu, who is in the late-stage Cold Resistance, with the power of five manes. As for Hanqiong’s side, besides the leader Han Jiuli and Wu Lin, there’s someone called Han Feng, also in the late-stage Cold Resistance. I remember his strength is formidable, with the power of eight manes."

After Wu Tianxing finished speaking, Chen Yingba immediately chimed in: "I heard from my brother a year ago that Wu Xiong has over thirteen hundred in the Earth-digging Realm, and Hanqiong is even stronger, with over two thousand."

Wu Xiong and Wu Ying have brought eight hundred Earth-digging Realm; Han Jiuli and Wu Lin have brought more than a thousand Earth-digging Realm, and all these people are at Yang Lu, which means...

Upon hearing the general situation of the two camps, the expressions of the six from Great Xia instantly became extremely invigorated.

Xia Chuan couldn’t help but stand up directly and spoke:

"No time to lose..."

Boom!

Before Xia Chuan finished speaking, a loud bang echoed from inside the Yangluo Base.

After the loud bang, there was an extremely shrill roar of anger.

"Ah... Wu Xiong, Han Jiuli, you killed my wife, children, and brothers! I, Peng Bo, will have an endless death feud with your two families!"

Inside, the eight people immediately turned pale and all turned their heads to look.

"Peng Bo? The leader of Yang Lu!"

"He should have gone to Long Mountain to attend the alliance meeting. He’s back!"

"It’s not just him; there are four auras running out..."

"My brother is also with them!"

Hearing the last words from Chen Yingba, Xia Chuan and the others quickly walked to the window, directly pushed it open, and looked towards the west side inside.

Inside the Yangluo Base, four figures were frantically fleeing outward.

The first two carried a small child on their backs, the third was holding a baby in their arms, and the last middle-aged man was wielding a Snake Spear, covering the four of them as they fought and retreated, clearly holding the rear.

"That’s, the second son of Long Mountain, Li Xuantiang!"

Wu Tianxing immediately recognized the identity of the last person, while Chen Yingba next to him looked anxiously at the one on the left of the two leading figures at the front.

"The one holding the baby is Peng Bo, the leader on the left is Chen Yingyuan, and on the right is Cold Resistance level, He Tu from Long Mountain. Why are they here?"

"Chen Yingba and Peng Bo must have defected to Long Mountain, and Li Xuantiang, like us, came to investigate Yang Lu’s situation and discovered that Wu Xiong and Han Jiuli have already occupied the nest, attempting to rescue people first!"

Yuwen Dao quickly analyzed the situation in front of them and then looked at Xia Chuan.

"Who do we help?"

Yuan Cheng directly asked aloud, taking two steps forward with a giant ax, his face eager, clearly wanting to have a go with the people outside.

Chen Yingba realized he didn’t have the right to speak, so he could only stand anxiously to the side, waiting for Xia Chuan to make a decision.

"Wait a moment, let me take another look!"

Xia Chuan did not immediately decide but continued to observe the outside with the group.

With Peng Bo, the leader leading the way, the four fleeing soon reached the outer buildings, and the four chasing them quickly appeared in the sight of Xia Chuan and the others.

"Wu Xiong, Wu Ying, Han Jiuli, Wu Lin, it’s those four!"

The short-statured Wu Xiong wielded a long staff fiercely, leaping upward continuously cleaving at Li Xuantiang. The violent force left Li Xuantiang no room to escape, forcing him to turn back and defend.

Han Jiuli on the other side had his sights more on Peng Bo in the front, his eyes cold and chilling, clearly intent on killing Peng Bo.

Meanwhile, Wu Ying and Wu Lin were constantly chasing He Tu and Chen Yingyuan. It was clear that the former’s strength was probably inferior to the latter’s, but the problem was He and Chen both carried a small child on their backs, preventing them not only from turning back to confront them but also having to guard against the two from harming the children.

The speed of the four kept slowing, and as they reached the outer buildings, the situation became perilously visible to the naked eye.

The situation at the Yangluo Base was tight on the outside but loose on the inside. The outer buildings were made of iron, while the inside was wood, so the outer area was the key defense zone, with all the Earth-digging Realm basically concentrated here.

Wu Xiong and Han Jiuli, having taken Yang Lu, obviously inherited this aspect and placed all their Earth-digging Realm on the outer side.

It was daytime, and naturally, the Earth-digging Realm couldn’t come out to intercept them.

But not being able to come out doesn’t mean they can’t do anything.

Whoosh whoosh... whoosh...

The windows of the outer area buildings opened one after another, and countless Iron Arrows were launched from inside, speeding towards the four escaping Li Xuantiang.

The Iron Arrows shot by the Earth-digging Realm couldn’t possibly harm the Cold Resistance Level, but with enough numbers, the situation changed.

Even the strongest skin of the Cold Resistance Level couldn’t cover every part of the body.

Moreover, among the people hiding inside the buildings, there were quite a few Earth-digging Realm Limit individuals, whose arrows could still pose a threat to them.

The four had just rushed to the outer perimeter of the buildings when they were forced back by the rain of arrows, and because they were carrying children, they couldn’t expose themselves to the arrow rain at will. Helplessly, they had to take cover behind the buildings and retreated again.

"You think you can run after coming in? Stay right here!"

"Endless death feud with us, Peng Bo, Yang Lu no longer exists. Surrender, and I’ll give you a quick death!"

The triumphant voices of Wu Xiong and Han Jiuli made Li Xuantiang below instantly turn much gloomier.

He scanned the two children and one baby around him and signaled to Peng Bo with his eyes.

"Second Young Master, this is the only surviving member of my family, and those two are the only children of my brothers Liu He and Qin Gu. I cannot leave them."
