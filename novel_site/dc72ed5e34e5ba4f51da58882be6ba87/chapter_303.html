Chapter 303: Chapter 166: The Defenseless Mirror Immortal

"Xia Hong, my leader, among our three thousand people, none have paid homage to the Mirror Immortal. The remaining nine thousand in the camp have already been bewitched to commit suicide. It seems to be preparing some ritual, be careful!"

Upon hearing Yuwen Dao’s voice, Xia Hong’s pupils sharply contracted.

In fact, he had arrived at the Mirror Immortal’s outpost for a while, but due to the uncertainty about the internal situation, he made some other preparations first to ensure safety, which delayed him a bit.

In this short period, over nine thousand people have all been bewitched to commit suicide!

Of course, shocked as he was, Xia Hong immediately responded to Yuwen Dao’s warning, gratefully saying gravely: "Thank you, Brother Yuwen. You take the three thousand people and escape first. Leave this deceitful monster to me!"

Upon hearing this, a look of joy appeared on Yuwen Dao’s face.

The first thing he said indicated that his three thousand people were unscathed. He was just worried that Xia Hong and others might mistakenly believe they were connected to the deceitful monster and stop them from escaping.

Xia Hong obviously understood his meaning and promptly told them to leave.

The three thousand people had already snapped out of it, with the foremost charging like headless chickens out of the passage. Yuwen Dao glanced at the sky and then at the people from Great Xia on the other side, immediately starting his deployment.

"Uncle Hong, you take these people and run to the outer Arrow Bamboo Forest, find a place to hide. Do not enter the bamboo forest; don’t go too far either. Everyone else stays with me to help the people of Great Xia."

Forty-one people surrounded him at the moment, all with Earth-digging Realm cultivation. Among them, Hong Guang, Hong Tian, Meng Yi, Zhu Ling, and Zhu Yuan were even above his cultivation, all former subordinates of his father, Yuwen Hu.

"Dao, we almost... With this few from Great Xia, can they deal with He Yuanhun? Maybe we should escape together!"

Hong Guang was about to turn away, but glanced at the people from Great Xia, furrowing his brows and immediately reminding Yuwen Dao.

Hong Tian, Meng Yi, and the others did not speak, but seeing the people from Great Xia, they too furrowed their brows, evidently agreeing with Hong Guang’s suggestion.

There were only sixty-seven people from Great Xia in total. Apart from Xia Hong whose strength they couldn’t gauge, the others hadn’t even reached the limits of the Earth-digging Realm.

Not long ago, their over three thousand people almost got bewitched into suicide by He Yuanhun. How could this few from Great Xia fight He Yuanhun?

Upon hearing this, Yuwen Dao hesitated only briefly before solemnly speaking:

"It’s almost dawn. With over three thousand people, we can’t get far. Moreover, if He Yuanhun wins, it’s a dead end for us all. We must stay here to help them.

Have you forgotten who forced He Yuanhun to this point? Don’t underestimate the people of Great Xia. The fact that they dare to pursue means they are confident. Listen to me. Don’t waste time. Uncle Hong, go quickly; the people at the front are almost scattered."

Everyone was instantly awakened by Yuwen Dao’s words, their expressions changing.

"Alright, I’ll go now!"

After speaking, Hong Guang was the first to turn around, heading towards the front of the fleeing crowd.

Those who came to their senses also followed behind Yuwen Dao, heading towards the position of the Great Xia group.

"This is Solidified Fire Oil. Apply it on your weapons, it can harm the deceitful monster."

Just as Yuwen Dao approached without saying a word, Xia Chuan, who was shooting arrows, tossed a leather pouch towards him.

Opening the leather pouch, he saw fist-sized chunks of black oil inside, realizing this might be the divine weapon Great Xia used against the deceitful monster. Yuwen Dao’s pupils slightly contracted, and he immediately distributed it to those around him.

Following the example of the people from Great Xia, Yuwen Dao’s group also applied it to their weapons and Iron Arrows. Once applied, they immediately joined Xia Chuan and the others in continuously shooting arrows at the ancient mirror in the sky.

At the front, Xia Chuan turned to look at Yuwen Dao, showing a hint of appreciation in his eyes.

The big brother was right; he truly is a smart person!

He had also heard Yuwen Dao’s words just now.

"Over nine thousand people, all died by the bamboo tower?"

At the forefront of the crowd, after shooting half a quiver of arrows, Xia Hong turned to ask Yuwen Dao.

Yuwen Dao quickly nodded in response: "Most of them did, a few might have taken their own lives before reaching the bamboo tower..."

After responding, he immediately realized why Xia Hong asked this and quickly added: "He Yuanhun just tried to bewitch our three thousand people into suicide again. He must still be heavily injured, and whatever those nine thousand were used for shouldn’t be ready yet!"

Smart people make communications efficient.

With a shift in his expression, Xia Hong immediately ordered everyone:

"Follow me inside. Leave half a quiver of arrows, and be cautious!"

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan and the others nodded, immediately slinging the Iron Core Bow behind them, drawing the broadsword from their waists, and followed Xia Hong through the passage into the Mirror Immortal’s outpost.

"Ugh...ah..."

Overhead of the bamboo tower, the two mirrors were still ablaze, black smoke billowing up continuously, enveloping the entire basin.

Even though Xia Hong and the others had stopped shooting, He Yuanhun’s wailing screams hadn’t ceased, still ongoing.

"Solidified Fire Oil ignites deceitful monsters upon contact. Those two mirrors must be the true form of the so-called Mirror Immortal, which means He Yuanhun is being controlled!"

Charging first into the outpost, Xia Hong looked up at the two grotesque mirrors above the bamboo tower, a glint of light passing through his pupils. Suddenly he sidestepped, swinging his long blade.

Clang...

An Iron Arrow was struck down by Xia Hong, and immediately several hundred people rushed out of the wooden houses nearby.
