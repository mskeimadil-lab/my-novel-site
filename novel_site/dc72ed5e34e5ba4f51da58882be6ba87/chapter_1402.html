Chapter 1402: Chapter 451: Overwhelmed by Lust, Ignorant and Arrogant, Chui Mountain Changes Hands, Cai Shiqi Breaks Down, Intimidation_7

From start to finish, Mu Qinghe never relied on him, but on Mu Longhe, or rather, the hidden Great Xia behind the scenes.

Of course, this doesn’t mean he played no role, but the role he actually played was nothing like what he had imagined.

The role he, as the Princely Heir, played was to confuse Duan Hong and Beishuo, making them think that Mu Qinghe’s support for this coup was him, to conceal Great Xia as the real mastermind.

In other words, he was being used!

He thought he was the one using Mu Qinghe, but in reality, he was the one being used by Mu Qinghe, or rather by Great Xia behind Mu Qinghe.

"Ignorant barbarians, lowly ants, how dare you insult this Princely Heir like this, such audacity, such audacity..."

No one expected that after Cai Shiqi understood the ins and outs of the situation, he would actually break down!

His tone was furious to the extreme, his whole face turned a liver-red color, pointing at Li Xuanling and others in mid-air, repeatedly saying, "such audacity", as if his emotions were on the verge of losing control.

"This Cai Shiqi, known for his narrow-mindedness, will sure provide some drama to watch!"

Chu Yan’er saw Cai Shiqi’s state, her pupils slightly gleaming with schadenfreude.

However, the schadenfreude on her face quickly froze.

Whoosh......

In mid-air, Li Xuanling swung her sword directly at Cai Shiqi.

The physical power at Xianyang Level is measured in jun, with such an immense impact force, even a sword swing through the air, the power of its sword air is enough to split gold and cut stone.

And at this moment, Cai Shiqi was pointing at Li Xuanling.

Li Xuanling’s swordsmanship follows a gentle path, thus her control over strength is extremely exquisite, this sword swing came down just right, precisely severing the forefinger Cai Shiqi was pointing at her.

"Ah......"

The pampered Cai Shiqi, obviously never suffered such a severe injury, immediately let out a wretched howl.

"Princely Heir!"

"Princely Heir, are you okay?"

"Princely Heir!"

......

Many of his servants and Cai Qiu youths of his age quickly rushed up in fear to support him.

"You’re crazy, you’re crazy, how dare you harm this Princely Heir, Great Xia is finished, your Great Xia is finished, vile..."

"You dare to say that last word, I’ll kill you now!"

Cai Shiqi hysterically spoke, but was shocked silent by Li Xuanling’s incredibly calm voice. Looking at Li Xuanling’s slightly raised longsword, the hysteria in his eyes quickly subsided, replaced by fear.

"Senior, Great Xia has some strength, but at most it only dominates the Nine Towns Territory, in front of my Cai Qiu..."

Among Cai Shiqi’s forty-odd servants, not all were incompetent.

Soon a middle-aged man stepped forward, respectfully addressing Li Xuanling as "senior", and the subsequent words were clearly intended to restore Cai Shiqi’s dignity.

"Does Cai Qiu belong to him?"

Unfortunately, before he could finish, he was impatiently interrupted by Li Xuanling.

Hearing Li Xuanling’s question, the middle-aged man was momentarily stunned, then continued in a deep voice: "Senior might not know the weight of the Princely Heir title, in Feudal Town’s county mansion, there are only five Princely Heirs, my family’s Princely Heir’s grandfather is Prefectural Governor of Bai Shui County, Cai Qiuhong..."

"Then does Cai Qiu belong to his grandfather?"

The middle-aged man was once again interrupted, leaving him speechless.

"If even his grandfather doesn’t count, then who does he count as in Cai Qiu? Just relying on the Princely Heir’s title, thinking the whole world has to yield to him?"

Li Xuanling sneered, turning to signal towards Yuan Cheng on the north side.

Yuan Cheng stood not far from Cai Shiqi, directly throwing the sack in his hand towards Cai Shiqi and others.

Bang......

Five heads fell out, rolling right to Cai Shiqi’s feet.

Those five heads were exactly the ones just killed in the restaurant, Cai Shiqing and others.

Seeing the five heads on the ground, Cai Shiqi’s body jerked violently, the fear in his eyes rapidly grew realizing Great Xia truly dares to kill, he dared not show even the slightest arrogance, his body trembling slightly in fright.

Not just him, even Chu Yan’er, whose solidified expression had a few more touches of fear.

This woman in white, truly dares to kill them!
