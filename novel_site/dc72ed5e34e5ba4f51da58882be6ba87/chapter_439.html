Chapter 439: Chapter 205: The Director Emerges, Treasure Presentation Opportunity (3)

"Leader, this must be the Great Xia Leader..."

Xiang Liang looked at the young man in black, his eyes full of admiration and reverence.

So strong!

A flying intermediate Cold Beast, so many people just now tried shooting arrows to no avail.

But the young man in black, took less than five minutes to resolve it.

Is this the Great Xia Leader?

So strong, unbelievably strong!

However, the next second, what the people of Great Xia said.

Made Xiang Liang’s expression instantly freeze.

"The Lord Chief is mighty!"

"Lord Yuwen has also emerged, hahaha, impressive!"

"Congratulations to the Lord Chief on breaking through."

.........

This person isn’t even the leader!

The Director?

Not even the Minister?

Gulp......

Xiang Liang looked at the man in black, his eyes widened, his face full of disbelief.

The man in black, namely Yuwen Dao, seeing the flying creature had lost its breath, revealed a trace of excitement in his pupils, and after a long time, he laid down the legs of the creature, looked back at the group congratulating him outside the main building, and smiled:

"Hehe, keep at it, you’ll be there soon too..."

Looking at the five or six thousand people in the middle of the front of the main building, and then at Xu Ning and Lin Kai and the others, he immediately realized something and smiled: "Well done, Xu Ning, so many people at once, you deserve another big credit."

Xu Ning immediately stepped forward, chuckled and said: "Thank you for your good words, sir."

"Five or six thousand people, over a hundred Earth-digging Realm captives, just the contribution value is two or three hundred thousand, I remember the squad stationed in Wuyuan had only twelve teams, this time you’ve hit it big!"

The faces of Xu Ning, Lin Kai, and the other four immediately showed even brighter smiles.

"Enough chatter, clean up the ground, Yuwen Dao, Xu Ning, Lin Kai, Lu Yang, come on up!"

At this moment, the clear voice from earlier sounded again from the third floor.

Below, Yuwen Dao immediately stopped the small talk, bringing Lin Kai, Lu Yang, and Xu Ning along, and walked up to the third floor.

Xiang Liang stood in place, looking up at the third floor, his face full of curiosity.

The owner of this clear voice must be in a position above this black-clad Director Yuwen Dao, is it the Minister they mentioned earlier, or the Great Xia Camp’s leader?

"You too, come with me, bring the items!"

As Xiang Liang was pondering, someone beside him suddenly pulled him, he looked up to find Xu Ning at the wooden building front pointing at him.

Realizing that Xu Ning was calling him up, Xiang Liang showed a trace of excitement, hurriedly clutching the wooden box in his hand, dashed out of the crowd and ran over.

"Who is this person?"

As Yuwen Dao climbed the stairs with the four, nearing the third floor, he glanced at Xiang Liang following them and asked curiously.

Xu Ning promptly answered: "His name is Xiang Liang, from one of the four families north of Wuyuan Mountain, he holds something quite valuable, says he wants to present it to the leader, I’ve seen it, it’s indeed a treasure, so I brought him to meet you first."

"To present to the leader?"

In Yuwen Dao’s eyes, a trace of oddity flashed, but he nodded without saying much.

Behind them, Xiang Liang, realizing that he might soon meet the Great Xia Leader, felt his emotions surge with excitement.

Of course, he didn’t forget to cast a grateful look at Xu Ning.

To be given the chance to present the treasure personally, Xu Ning’s favor was not small.

With his head low, Xiang Liang respectfully followed the four up the wooden stairs to the third floor, arriving directly at the porch; walking along it for a while, he reached a grand vermillion door.

The door was open, and with his head bowed, Xiang Liang could only see the elegant wooden floor carved with beast patterns. Realizing there were important figures inside, he didn’t dare to raise his head.

"Wait here for a moment, you’ll be called in shortly."

"Yes, sir!"

Xiang Liang replied respectfully. Only after Xu Ning and the others went inside, did he dare to slightly raise his head, curiously peeking inside the vermillion door.

At a glance, the worldview he once held instantly crumbled.

A solemn and majestic grand hall appeared before him, with a splendid grand ceiling; various vivid statues of Cold Beasts; eight imposing golden pillars; dozens of pure iron beast-shaped braziers; and that central three-legged giant cauldron...

Everything in this immersive view greatly impacted Xiang Liang’s spirit, leaving him somewhat dazed, and his inner humility soared to the extreme.

He couldn’t clearly see the scenes at the back of the grand hall, only that five figures were conversing over there, and he couldn’t make out any voices, he just clutched the wooden box, waiting nervously.

"You can come in now!"

Finally, half an hour later, Xu Ning’s call came from inside the grand hall.

Xiang Liang’s body quivered slightly, tidying his messy beast skin clothing first, then stepped over the threshold with a tense expression, entering the grand hall.

"This is my only chance, I must seize it."

Looking at the wooden box in his hands, Xiang Liang took a deep breath and continued walking in.
