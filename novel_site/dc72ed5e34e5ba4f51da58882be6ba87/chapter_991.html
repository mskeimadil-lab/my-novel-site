When Chen Ying saw He Yuanfeng, his expression changed, and lowering his head to see the few lines written on the beast skin, he leapt directly from his chair.

Great Xia Year Seven, October First

Star-picking Platform, Xia Hong sat cross-legged alone atop the Enlightenment Lotus Platform, holding two golden pills in his hand, with a thoughtful look in his eyes.

Although both pills were golden, the color of the one on the left was significantly lighter, and the pill had only three inscriptions swirling around it, while the right one had six.

"Bone Condensing Pill and Bone Gathering Pill, the difference in medicinal power is thirty times higher, the energy contained in the Bone Gathering Pill is too powerful, likely ordinary Iron Bone Xianyang Level cannot withstand it."

The Bone Condensing Pill is a Dan Medicine used for Xianyang Level cultivation. When Xia Hong first obtained the High-level Jade Bone, it was confirmed by the Alchemy Workshop to synthesize the formula directly. Unfortunately, at the time, he was only at Cold Resistance Level Cultivation, unable to use it.

As he broke through to Xianyang Level, the Alchemy Pavilion naturally began to refine the Bone Condensing Pill.

The formula for this Bone Gathering Pill, however, came from five years ago, when he used the Divine Machine Crossbow to kill the Frost Wolf King at Yanglu Realm, and confirmed its synthesis.

Xia Hong has also killed many beast kings now, their bones generally showing a silver effect, so they are referred to using Silver Bone.

The formulas for the two pills are basically the same, the only difference is the main ingredient, Bone Condensing Pill uses Jade Bone, Bone Gathering Pill uses Silver Bone.

"Since there are six inscriptions, is there also one with nine inscriptions?"

Staring at the six inscriptions on the Bone Gathering Pill, Xia Hong was moved.

If there is one with nine inscriptions, whose medicinal power is also several dozen times that of the Bone Gathering Pill, then his cultivation speed could be faster.

"Xianyang Level cultivation no longer directly enhances power but focuses on the enhancement of bones. Unlike the refining process during breakthroughs, now it is using medicinal power to nourishe the bones, although the speed is very slow, I can feel the color of the bones gradually becoming brighter, it should be similar to the Cold Resistance Level cultivation, once it reaches a critical point, the bones can be fortified once, and the power will greatly surge."

Xia Hong clenched his fist, murmuring slightly: "Lu Yang transmitted a letter back the day before yesterday mentioning that the power measure standard at Nine Towns for Xianyang Level is ’jin,’ one ’jin’ being roughly equivalent to the power of one hundred bristles. My strength now is about eleven ’jin,’ I wonder what this strength compares to at Nine Towns."

As he said this, a hint of pride flashed in Xia Hong’s eyes.

Nine Towns has more than twenty Xianyang Levels in total; although he has only interacted with one Yang Fa, he has gained a lot of insights.

"If I could see Vajra Master and Xiahou Zhang in combat, the strength from both sides of Nine Towns and Dajue Temple would be clear in my mind, unfortunately..."

Xia Hong’s expression moved slightly, suddenly looking up towards the position of the main building.

"Chen Ying has returned, so urgently, there must be news from Lower River Village!"

His eyes slightly brightened, he headed directly towards the main building.

Just after he flew out, a white figure from the dwelling behind the Star-picking Hall also came out, who else could it be but Li Xuanling.

"Are you thinking..."

Li Xuanling clearly thought Xia Hong was heading out for something and opened her mouth with a charmingly angry expression. She stopped speaking as Xia Hong just went into the main building, but her face showed a strong worry, pondering for a moment as she headed towards the main building as well.
