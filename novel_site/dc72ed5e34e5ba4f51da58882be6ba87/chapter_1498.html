Chapter 1498: Chapter 471: Frostfire Scorches the Land, Triumph and Vengeance, River Mouth Devastation, More Experts? (3)

"Second Brother!"

Zhang Long’s voice, tinged with sorrow, roared out, and as Hong Xicheng looked ahead, he saw the scene and his face was suddenly filled with surprise.

It turned out to be the two protectors wearing the terrifying ghost masks with red faces and tusks. Their strength was terrifying; one covered, and the other charged, they had already reached the middle of the crowd, clashing with Zhang Long and his group.

As the formation scattered, Zhang Long and the subordinate bureau officials had long taken their men to fight their own battles, leaving only him, his brother Zhang Hu, and dozens of County Guard Army soldiers in his area.

The two protectors had been besieging the Zhang brothers, but dozens of County Guard Army soldiers fiercely guarded Zhang Long, preventing them from succeeding, so they shifted their target to Zhang Hu.

Zhang Hu’s strength was inherently inferior to his elder brother Zhang Long, and he was not the protection target of the County Guard Army soldiers; as soon as the protectors switched targets, they swiftly accomplished their goal.

Crunch...

The protector who stabbed through Zhang Hu’s neck heard Zhang Long’s hysterical roar. Instead of stopping, he turned to glance at him, then suddenly exerted force with his right hand, brutally cutting off Zhang Hu’s head.

Then he reached out to grab a tuft of loose hair, and with extreme provocation, he threw Zhang Hu’s head towards Zhang Long.

"Ahhh... Beast, I’ll cut you down! All County Guards, follow this manager, kill this fiend!"

Seeing his decapitated brother, Zhang Long instantly lost control of his emotions, roaring as he drew his saber and charged directly at the protector.

Though appearing emotionally overwhelmed, Zhang Long’s call to the County Guard soldiers showed he was not entirely charging recklessly.

He took the Blood Frenzy Powder, suppressing the injuries on his chest for the time being, and with dozens of County Guard Army soldiers, they had a chance against two protectors.

"Heh, so many died, yet I haven’t seen you, this dog of an official, shed a tear. But lose a brother, and this is your reaction?"

"Ah..."

At this moment, Zhang Long could hear nothing else, his big saber directly cutting towards the protector, and dozens of County Guard Army soldiers charged along, immediately surrounding the two protectors.

Whoosh...

The County Guard Army was also equipped with sabers, and under the assault of dozens of Cold Resistance Level soldiers, the two protectors’ movements immediately became restrained. Still, their strength was formidable, even surrounded by so many, they occasionally used brute force to break open a gap; though it was difficult for them to kill anyone, escaping was evidently not hard for them.

"Damn it, damn it! Nearly 40 Zong strength, where did the Frost Ash Association find such powerful experts? It’s over, even if we survive this..."

Zhang Long turned to look at the battle around him, his pupils swiftly filled with despair.

The two protectors deliberately did not escape, clearly helping restrain the others; how could he not see that? And at this moment, the situation around them was indeed becoming increasingly unfavorable.

Blood Frenzy Powder wasn’t some common thing, these Cold Resistance Level soldiers and County Guard had it, but those over a thousand Earth-digging Realm Limit loyalists didn’t have it.

Their numbers were already at a disadvantage, and the rebel army, over a thousand Earth-digging Realm Limit loyalists, had taken Dan Medicine that disregarded pain; the consequences of this collision were imaginable.

Among those over a thousand loyalists, more than twenty percent had already perished, starting a major collapse, with some already kneeling down to beg for mercy.

"Beg for mercy now? Give me back my father’s life!"

"When my sister was sold to the city’s brothel, I cried and begged you while kneeling, you didn’t let her go, now you want me to let you go, dream on!"

"Last month, my son was driven to death by you beasts in the Su Field, I will kill you, kill you..."

"Ah... ah..."

"Spare me, spare me... ah..."

.........

Just now, Zhang Long and his group slaughtered at least four thousand people, the pervasive scent of blood had long made everyone lose their minds, and those kneeling to plead hadn’t opened their mouths for more than a few cries before being drowned by the angry crowd.

No need to watch, from their extremely anguished screams, one could tell how miserable their fate was after being submerged.

"Run, hurry to the Lu River Camp for reinforcements!"

Zhang Long shivered and, seeing the gathering crowd, his lost rationality finally returned, realizing that if he didn’t escape, he’d truly lose his life there, he immediately made a decision.

"Now you want to flee, too late!"

Unfortunately, the two protectors they had just surrounded were not done yet.

Whoosh...

Seeing several County Guard Army soldiers start to retreat and break out towards the sparsest direction on the east, the two protectors leaped high, stepping on the heads of County Guard Army soldiers, quickly rushing to the eastern gap, assisting the remaining Earth-digging Realm to repel the assault.

Slash...

The two protectors, both at 38 Manes strength, stormed to the east, directly killing several of the fastest charging soldiers, and seeing Zhang Long and the others rushing over from behind, they immediately called out loudly to the crowd: "Squad Leader Hong, quickly bring people over, don’t let a single one escape."

"Subordinate obeys the order!"

At this moment, Hong Xicheng had only twenty-five people left beside him, but hearing the protector’s call, he still responded loudly and swiftly rallied the remaining twenty-five Cold Resistance Level, quickly surrounding the east.

However, he suddenly thought of something, turned to the west towards the Rice Field, and seeing over a hundred people outside holding Iron Scythes, their faces full of hesitation, he immediately shouted: "Ladies and gentlemen, just standing still now is useless. Today, when the matter is done, all present will share responsibility. Do you think, when Lu River Camp and the County Governor pursue it, they will let you go?"
