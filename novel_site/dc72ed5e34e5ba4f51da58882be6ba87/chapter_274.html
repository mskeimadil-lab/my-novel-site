Chapter 274: Chapter 157 The Disappeared Cold Beast, Breakthrough Tide_3

After setting the tone, he paused for a moment and continued:

"Whether it’s true or not, we must be prepared first..."

"Hahaha, I’ve broken through, I’ve broken through!"

Before Xia Chuan could finish speaking, an excited voice suddenly came from the third floor.

Being interrupted, Xia Chuan instead showed a smile on his face.

"Isn’t that the voice of Hong Gang’s son, Hong Mu?"

"Even that kid has broken through!"

"This is already the sixth one today."

"Six today?"

Upon hearing Shi Ping say that Hong Mu was the sixth to break through today, the joy on the faces of Xia Chuan and the other two deepened.

"It’s a pity that my big brother has been training lately. If he knew, he’d definitely be happier!"

Xia Chuan glanced at Xia Hong’s room on the third floor, feeling immensely invigorated.

On March 3rd, when the one-kilometer ban was officially implemented, the camp’s activity area decreased, meaning not so many people were needed for logging.

The extra people naturally couldn’t be idle.

Especially now, facing the threat from the Mirror Immortal Camp, enhancing the overall strength of the camp is an urgent matter.

Under Xia Hong’s directive, anyone in the camp whose basic strength approached ten thousand pounds could be prioritized to enter the Martial Arts Training Ground for cultivation.

The Martial Arts Training Ground officially started operating on the twentieth of last month. Initially, to ensure fairness and manage resource consumption, a queuing system was adopted: as long as you could afford 20 contribution points a day, you could get a turn.

Special situations naturally require special treatment.

With the urgency of enhancing the camp’s strength, Xia Hong’s order met no opposition.

Thus, almost starting from the next day, which was March 4th,

the camp experienced a wave of breakthroughs in the Earth-digging Realm.

Bai Dongying, Bai Wuting, Lu Sheng, and Lu Yang all broke through on the first day.

Tong Xinglong, Ying Yi, and Li Longkai on the second day.

After that, almost every day, two to three people broke through.

Today, unexpectedly, there were six at once!

"Most people in the camp don’t know, but those who’ve broken through to the Earth-digging Realm spare some time each day to chop wood. If not for worrying about surpassing others too much, with their logging efficiency, even if those in the Logging Realm worked themselves to death, they likely couldn’t compare with them."

Shi Ping glanced at the Wood List ranking behind him, his expression slightly peculiar.

Xia Chuan also glanced over, seeing that in the top twenty of the Wood List, almost everyone had broken through to the Earth-digging Realm. After pondering for a moment, his brows furrowed.

"You’re saying that those who’ve broken through to the Earth-digging Realm are still sparing some time each day to chop wood, not fully focusing on mining?"

Seeing Xia Chuan’s darkened face as he inquired, Shi Ping’s neck immediately shrank, showing a hint of restraint, as he nodded.

He himself was one of them, ranking seventh on the Wood List.

"Shi Ping, go notify everyone who’s broken through to the Earth-digging Realm to go to the southern mine and mine iron ore. Yuan Cheng, go inform the Hunting Team to come to the Blacksmith Room; I’ll be waiting for them there."

Hearing the coldness in Xia Chuan’s tone, Shi Ping didn’t dare ask more questions and directly turned to inform the others.

Yuan Cheng nodded and also turned to leave.

Luo Yuan stood where he was, showing a bit of confusion, and asked, "We’ve gathered enough iron ore recently, and it’s all been sent to the leader. Why do we need them to focus fully on mining iron?"

"Preparation!"

Xia Chuan only answered with three words. After pondering for a moment, Luo Yuan’s eyes suddenly brightened, understanding his intentions immediately.
