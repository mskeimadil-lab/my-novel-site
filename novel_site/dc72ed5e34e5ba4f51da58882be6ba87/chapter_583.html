Chapter 583: Chapter 250: Rescue, Intimidation, and Surprise (Part 2)

"Those people in light gold armor have been using solidified fire oil to save people, didn’t you see?"

"There’s hope, we don’t have to die!"

......

In fact, the Great Xia soldiers had been using solidified fire oil to rescue people, but because there were too many people, many simply didn’t notice. Plus, the dew exploding on some people made those who saw it even more panicked, causing continuous unrest.

At this moment, since Lu Yang had his hand raised the entire time, the dew on the infant gradually dissipated, eventually turning into ordinary water droplets, which Lu Yang wiped away, visible to everyone.

Realizing that Great Xia indeed had a way to save them, hope instantly ignited in everyone’s hearts. The fear in their eyes when looking at Lu Yang was replaced by hope and longing.

"See? We have a way to save people. Now listen to me, everyone line up in three queues: Long Mountain, Zhaoyang, and Hanqiong. Everyone will receive it. I will also have people patrolling in the middle, and in more urgent situations, they will be prioritized. But let me say this upfront, if any queue causes chaos, I will stop distributing immediately, so don’t blame me then!"

As soon as Lu Yang finished speaking, the more than ten thousand people below fell silent. With the assistance of the Great Xia soldiers, all obediently formed three long queues to receive solidified fire oil one by one.

The scene was finally under control. Once the distribution of solidified fire oil was arranged, Lu Yang breathed a slight sigh of relief. Seeing Lin Kai, Zhao Hu, and others in the crowd giving him a thumbs up, he felt a bit proud.

"Minister was right, these people won’t listen until they see blood!"

Previously, when Wu Xiong caused a rebellion, Xia Chuan’s approach was to stop it with killing, although bloody, it was very effective.

Lu Yang’s handling method was a complete replication of Xia Chuan’s previous approach.

Seeing the effectiveness of this method, Lu Yang naturally agreed with Xia Chuan’s last words even more.

......

"That thing can actually eliminate dew, it’s amazing!"

Among the three groups of people, in Zhaoyang’s queue, Yang Zhong received a small piece of solidified fire oil, and after removing the dew from himself, he walked to the back of the crowd with Li Hu, eyes full of curiosity.

Li Hu, who received solidified fire oil before him and whose legs had already recovered, nodded upon hearing this and said, "I heard at Long Mountain that only silver can handle mystical creatures, but silver is too precious for ordinary people to afford. It’s amazing that Great Xia has such a divine thing!"

Few people knew about silver’s restraining properties on mystical creatures, but Li Hu and Yang Zhong were exceptions, so they were even more aware of the preciousness of the solidified fire oil they had just used.

"The key is that they’re willing to give it to ordinary people like us. Hu Zi, we’ve always wanted to leave Long Mountain, right? This is a great opportunity, I’ll go find those adults later, okay?"

Li Hu immediately nodded, a hint of excitement showing on his face, then thinking of something, he saw Yang Zhong’s eager expression and smiled, "No rush, why do you think Leader Xia risked his life to save us and gave us solidified fire oil?"

"You’re right."

Yang Zhong quickly realized and nodded.

Great Xia’s actions were essentially to recruit this group of people, so he didn’t need to actively find someone; probably soon someone from Great Xia would speak up.

But Yang Zhong looked around at the people, then looked at the queue from Long Mountain, smiling, "Aside from those over ten thousand from Hanqiong, the people from Long Mountain and Zhaoyang truly willing to join Great Xia shouldn’t be many."

Both Long Mountain and Zhaoyang were part of the Earth-digging Realm. Regardless of their loyalty to their own camp, most of these people had families, so they wouldn’t easily want to change allegiances.

Li Hu of course understood what Yang Zhong meant, but he turned to look at Lu Yang and other Cold Resistance Level people on the high platform, thinking of how they had just deterred everyone by killing, he immediately sneered:

"Certainly not many, but I bet they won’t have a choice!"

From Lu Yang’s actions just now, it was clear that Great Xia wasn’t a pushover. With the leader personally rescuing people and using so much solidified fire oil to help them, it was clear they wouldn’t let them leave for free.

"Hearing you say that, my impression of Great Xia isn’t that good, but leaving Long Mountain is good for us in a way. Let’s stay in Great Xia for now, and if it doesn’t work out, we can find somewhere else."

Hearing Yang Zhong’s words, Li Hu nodded.

"Look quickly, Leader Xia is back!"

"That mystical creature, he must have killed it, right?"

"Obviously, can’t you see his joyous smile?"

Hearing voices from the crowd, Yang Zhong and Li Hu turned to look at the high platform.

On the high platform, seven more people had appeared at some point, headed by a man in black clothes, it was Xia Hong, who had just saved them in Gourd Valley.

As the crowd said, Xia Hong being here with a smile proved that the battle over in the valley was likely won by him.

Thinking of that mystical creature that scared away the likes of Li Xuanling and other Cold Resistance Level experts, who ended up defeated by Xia Hong, admiration instantly appeared on Li Hu’s face. Especially seeing Lu Yang and others who just now seemed proud, now humble in front of Xia Hong, admiration rose even higher.
