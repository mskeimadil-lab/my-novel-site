No need for Li Baihe’s reminder, everyone’s eyes were already focused on the Sword Puppet, so at this moment, they clearly saw the Sword Puppet’s performance.

After staring at Xia Chuan for so long, all the soldiers of the Six Armies had already started facing the Beast Tide head-on.

With such a vast area of the military confronting the Beast Tide, it naturally cannot be all tangled together at the first moment, mainly the confrontation in the front row.

Despite the large scale of the Beast Tide, due to the lack of unified command, the overall combat power they exert is far less than the Cold Beast Clan of the same quantity.

For instance, at this moment, the Beast Tide side only knows to recklessly charge forward; many Cold Beasts even directly step on the bodies of their companions toward the army. The Cold Beasts have robust life force, and it’s not enough to cause death by trampling, but the problem is that such headless and chaotic charging greatly reduces efficiency.

Unlike the Cold Beasts, the human side’s response is clearly more organized.

Among the Six Armies, the first batch to rush up are all the strongest from the Reconnaissance Camp, six hundred elite Cold Resistance Level troops holding various weapons instantaneously create hundreds of gaps in the Beast Tide’s front row.

The subsequent five thousand plus army soldiers, in units of camps, rapidly occupied these gaps, slaughtering Cold Beasts while swiftly traversing through, in less than a hundred breaths, they even broke the Beast Tide into dozens of small battlefield segments.

At this moment, Xia Chuan with a Black Blade in his right hand and threads in his left, shuttled through the Beast Tide, while opening breakthrough points for the halted army, controlling the Sword Puppet to continue rushing towards the core of the Beast Tide.

Crack...

The ten-meter-high Sword Puppet, although its size is smaller compared to those Advanced Cold Beasts that have entered combat form, its enormous weight forming tremendous inertia, plus the thread conduction from Xia Chuan, can exert a force that directly crushes all Cold Beasts.

Moreover, because the Sword Puppet is covered with spikes, even if Cold Beasts occasionally crash into it, no matter if they knock the Sword Puppet down, they can’t inflict any substantial damage on it, instead leaving numerous bloody holes on themselves.

Embracing the pain without fear, the force that can crush almost all Cold Beasts, and with the sharpness enhancement of the silver-made Big Saber, the Sword Puppet is unbeatable on the battlefield, slaughtering countless middle and low-level Cold Beasts while being able to repel or even severely wound parts of the Advanced Cold Beasts. As time passes, wherever the Sword Puppet goes, the Cold Beasts scatter and flee, completely unwilling to face its sharpness.

"Press the army forward, the Beast Tide has already started to collapse, scare them, so these vile beasts will never dare to cross the Arrow Bamboo Forest again!"

Xia Chuan had already penetrated deep into the Beast Tide, and with a bellow fueled by fury, quickly spread across the entire battlefield.

"The Divine Martial Army Reconnaissance Camp charges in, Camps One to Five flank and guard the west side, Camps Six to Nine block the east side of the Beast Tide, completely splitting them up."

With the orders issued, all soldiers immediately initiated a more intense charge.

Boom boom boom boom...

The army truly pushed the Beast Tide back into the southern side of the Arrow Bamboo Forest.

No, not just retreated, the ground covered with Cold Beast remains added up to no less than two thousand heads, middle-level accounted for at least thirty percent, and even Advanced Cold Beasts numbered several dozens.
