Chapter 1529: Chapter 479: Brief Encounter, Elusive Dan Medicine, Eldest Son of Jiang Mansion in East City

The development and exploration of the Blood Miasma Plain by Chen Cang and Cai Qiu, two vassal towns, have been ongoing for many years, so Blood Jade Pearls and Blood Jade Crystals are no longer secrets in these towns.

Low-quality Blood Jade Pearls can patch skin membrane defects, with a single pearl valued at five hundred taels; High-quality Blood Jade Pearls can rectify flesh defects, priced at ten thousand taels each; the Blood Jade Crystals can remedy bone defects, with a single block reaching a staggering price of a million.

The problem is, those three items are all top-notch resources for enhancing aptitude post-birth, even if they have a price, they’re not available on the market!

Blood Jade Crystals are not to be mentioned, as they can only be found on the Beast Kings and Iron Bone Corpse Demons in the Blood Miasma Plain, and those capable of obtaining them surely lack no money!

As for the high-quality Blood Jade Pearls, they only exist within the Miasma Area’s Advanced Cold Beasts and the Beasts King on the outskirts; those who can acquire them basically possess Xianyang Level cultivation and, even if they don’t use them, will surely keep them for their younger family members, unlikely to sell them.

Currently, the only circulated on the Chen Cang market are the low-quality Blood Jade Pearls.

Ji Hong is not to be mentioned, as he participated in the trial and is currently striving for 60 Juns, urgently needing to patch flesh defects, lacking mostly high-quality Blood Jade Pearls. Xia Hong casually tossed him a bag containing over 200 high-quality Blood Jade Pearls, not to mention the three Blood Jade Crystals that would greatly assist his future breakthrough to Xianyang Level.

Jiang Xinfan and Luo Ming with the nine others stayed in Chen Cang for half a year, and of course have heard about it. Not only did they receive many low-quality Blood Jade Pearls from Ji Hong, but they even went to the outskirts of the Blood Miasma Plain, hunted Beast Kings and obtained some high-quality Blood Jade Pearls.

But they went three times, and their total haul of high-quality Blood Jade Pearls was only 22, while Xia Hong casually tossed 200 at once, even including the Blood Jade Crystals they had never seen.

"Don’t rush, everyone’s share will be given when we return!"

Xia Hong’s words instantly thrilled Jiang Xinfan and the nine others, quickly getting up excitedly to open the door for him.

Su Xing’er subconsciously wanted to follow out, but quickly remembered Xia Hong’s instructions before entering the city and stopped in disappointment.

Yanling City is not like the outside world; if they mingle together, and if someone with a keen eye notices, problems may arise, so they need to be cautious.

Zhang Yuzhu and Zhang Yuhong, the sisters, have already been arranged by Zhang Yuchuan, so at this moment there are fifteen people in the room, including Xia Hong:

Zhang Yuchuan, Liu Biao, and Wang Feng are Ji Hong’s subordinates and attendants living with him in the Adviser Chen Mansion;

Su Xing’er and the four siblings moved from Cai Qiu to Yanling City last year, only having slight interactions with Ji Hong and are not considered familiar, having bought a home in the west of the city, Su Xing’er and Su Jing returning to their own home;

And Jiang Xinfan and Luo Ming along with nine others from Great Xia, although guests of the Adviser Chen Mansion, lived there for just over a month, then independently purchased a house in the east of the city and moved out of the Chen Mansion.

The three groups deliberately staggered their entrance to the city, only pretending to encounter each other at the Worldly Noble Tavern and using the chance to gather in a private room.

Jiang Xinfan’s alias in Yanling City is Jiang Fan, and Xia Hong’s current fake identity is his Elder Grandson Jiang Hong brought back from the Outer Realm, naturally staying with him at the Jiang Mansion.

Being a family, there must be respect for order between the elders and juniors.

Hong Yu helped open the main door, Jiang Xinfan led the way, Luo Ming, Chen Hua, and five other middle-aged men formed the middle, while Xia Hong followed behind with Meng Ao, Zhu Kang, and finally Hong Yu who carried the door.

Coincidentally, Xia Hong and his ten just exited the private room.

A private room nearby also opened its door at this time.

Three middle-aged men in black led the way, followed by a middle-aged scholar in a blue jade silk robe with a black hat, looking elegant, followed by a young man and woman in green silk attire.

Six people in total walked out of the private room, coincidentally meeting face to face with Xia Hong and his ten, both parties stared at each other from a distance.

Jiang Xinfan first glanced at the three middle-aged men leading the way, eyes slightly constricted, then shifted his gaze to the blue-clad middle-aged man, paused briefly, then bowed and smiled amiably.

The middle-aged scholar was evidently good-tempered, seeing Jiang Xinfan smile, immediately returned the bow, and briefly surveyed the nine behind him, noticing the familial configuration, initially intending to retract his gaze.

But he seemed to have discovered something, suddenly paused, then swiftly turned, accurately locating someone in the rear.

"61 Jun, stronger than Fan Qinghu, considered the strongest Xianyang Level I’ve met within the Moao River realm, and the eyes are merged with Deceitful Object, that’s... seeing through my disguise."

Chen Cang is truly a place of hidden talents!

Xia Hong exchanged glances with the blue-clad scholar, pondering a moment internally, then offered a smile.

Since the Vermilion Wood Mask is ineffective, there’s no need to conceal.

The blue-clad scholar first stared blankly, then smiled and nodded in response, even gesturing to Jiang Xinfan to proceed first.

"Thank you!"

Jiang Xinfan softly expressed gratitude, leading the nine downstairs.

"Uncle, what’s wrong?"

"It’s nothing, let’s go!"

Xue Zijing watched Jiang Xinfan and his group descend, his gaze glued to Xia Hong, until his niece Bai Qingqing called, bringing him back to reality. He waved his hand, leading the five towards the stairs.
