Chapter 543: Chapter 237: Rescue and Wushuang’s Doomsday (Part 4)

Astonishingly, after the clash of two blades, both showed signs of slight curling.

"It’s also a Hundred-tempered Blade, but unfortunately, your strength is not as good as mine!"

The man from Wushuang noticed that Hong Mu was also using a Hundred-tempered Blade and initially looked surprised. But then he snorted coldly, and with a sudden burst of strength, he pushed Hong Mu’s body backward violently.

His strength far exceeded Hong Mu’s, which was his greatest advantage.

As long as he didn’t allow Hong Mu to retreat into the Great Xia crowd, killing him was just a matter of time.

Hong Mu retreated repeatedly under the pressure, moving toward the center of the defeated crowd of Wushuang. Realizing the opponent’s plan, a surge of regret rose in his heart in an instant.

"After all, it is a massive camp with many experts inside, shouldn’t have charged so fiercely. Now it’s finished!"

Whoosh......

An Iron Arrow attacked from behind his head, and Hong Mu swiftly ducked to evade, but with a glance out of the corner of his eye, he saw more arrows flying towards him, causing a chill in his heart.

"Even if I lose, I will take some of you down with me!"

The man from Wushuang, assured of his victory, smirked evilly at the corner of his lips. Taking advantage of Hong Mu’s despair and distraction, he suddenly withdrew his large blade and fiercely slashed down.

Bang......

However, the expected scene did not occur as anticipated.

The man was directly kicked tens of meters away, crashing into the middle of the defeated crowd of Wushuang with a violent impact, knocking over more than ten people.

With such force, survival was impossible!

Hong Mu looked up and saw a burly figure clad in golden Hundred-forged Iron Armor in front of him, his eyes instantly filled with intense joy.

"Father, why are you here?"

The one who kicked the man away was his father, Hong Gang.

Hong Mu turned his head and saw that the battlefield had several more figures appearing at some point, dressed similarly to his father, turning the already defeated crowd of Wushuang into a chaotic mess, and his eyes were even more filled with joy.

"Uncle Yong, Mr. Zhu, Lord Lu... why are all of you here?"

"Nonsense, without us, how did you get in so quickly?"

After finishing, Hong Gang’s expression turned cold, and he scolded, "Just have some strength and don’t know your limit? I told you to stay with the Hunting Team members, look at where you charged off to?"

Hearing this, Hong Mu turned his head and saw the group of Green Wolf Team members fifty or sixty meters away, and immediately shrank his neck, laughing awkwardly.

"Father, I thought this time only Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and Zhao Long were here. With all of you here, capturing Wushuang is a sure thing."

"It was already a sure thing; the adults mainly wanted to train your abilities. Look over there, that Cold Resistance Level of Wushuang is about to lose his life!"

Following his father’s gaze, Hong Mu saw the core area of Wushuang’s camp, where a Cold Resistance Level battle was erupting.

"Duan Pingyu, I want your life!"

"Peng Bo, you dare collude with foreign forces and wreak havoc on Pingxi Plain; I will report to Lord Marquis to have you severely punished!"

"We’ll see if you can survive today first. I can’t take Wu Xiong and Han Jiuli’s lives for now, but I’ll take yours to honor Yang Lu’s departed soul!"

Peng Bo’s angry roar was earth-shattering, carrying enough hatred in his voice that even Hong Mu, from afar, couldn’t help but feel a chill in his heart.

"The Minister and several other adults didn’t make a move throughout?"

Seeing Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, Zhao Long, and several other Cold Resistance Level individuals standing still around the man from Wushuang with most of their gazes fixed on the Earth-digging Realm battlefield, Hong Mu couldn’t help but be stunned.

"I told you it was to train your abilities on purpose. I just watched for a while; your fight was terrible. Even Mr. Minister didn’t look pleased; I guess we all need to be reprimanded!"

Hong Mu was momentarily stunned upon hearing this, looking at the ground and seeing hardly any corpses of Great Xia people, filled with confusion in his heart.

"The fighting was so good, yet we still get reprimanded, how could that be?"
