Chapter 1503: Chapter 471: Frostfire Sweeps the Land, Success and Revenge, The River Mouth Tragedy, and Another Expert?

The idea of the overseer team was his suggestion to his father.

"It’s been over two hours, and still no reinforcements from the village are coming, what are they doing, what are they all doing..."

The overseer team can only serve a temporary role; the battlefield situation cannot be reversed, and ultimately it’s hard to escape death.

Liu Yu, of course, understood this principle. He raised his saber to defend against the charge of the County Guard Army soldiers, turned his head to look north and south, his heart continuously shouting in hopes that reinforcements would arrive quickly.

"You scoundrels, face your death!"

Unfortunately, after incessantly shouting, he didn’t see the reinforcements but first heard the angry shout filled with killing intent from Chu Qingwu above.

Liu Yu looked up, and the despair in his pupils grew even stronger.

In mid-air, that Green-faced Heavenly King who reignited their fighting spirit was now being forced out of the battlefield by Chu Qingwu; he clearly couldn’t withstand Chu Qingwu any longer, his longsword could only defend, unable to make any counterattack.

"Such little strength, rebel..."

Another saber was parried and blocked, Chu Qingwu sneered and gripped the big saber with both hands, using the rebound force to spin around from behind, bone energy within suddenly flared, as a towering bone appearance phantom quickly condensed behind. The big saber raised overhead, suddenly slashed down.

Though he only slashed down once, due to the speed, it brought out three afterimages, successive slashes. By the third, sword Qi had already turned substantial, with a roaring wave of air nearly a hundred meters rippling out, pressing towards the head of the Green-faced Heavenly King.

"Die!"

The Three-layer Demon Saber, Chu Qingwu’s signature skill, is also one of the Chu Family’s inherited martial arts, with nearly a forty percent force amplification, meaning this saber’s force was now as high as 14 Jun.

Chu Qingwu coldly spat a ’die’, proving his confidence in this saber strike.

The reality was much as he had anticipated.

As the big saber slashed down, the pupils beneath the mask of the Green-faced Heavenly King showed a trace of panic, but he didn’t give up on defense.

The Green-faced Heavenly King, fists, legs, and waist combined, suddenly exerted force, rapidly waved the longsword, and in a moment actually swept out over a thousand silver radiances in mid-air, the silver radiance looking just like... just like... silver, majestic feathers...

Clang......

A thousand Feather Light Sword Edges instantly poured out, fiercely clashing with Chu Qingwu’s Three-layer Demon Saber in mid-air.

Boom......

The frenzied commotion seemed to signal that the showdown between the two Xianyang Level fighters had reached the final moment.

The collision of Feather Light Sword Edge and Three-layer Demon Saber abruptly blasted out a vacuum region of a hundred meters in radius mid-air, with terrifying shockwaves spreading to the ground, instantly knocking away over a hundred people, stirring up a dust cloud tens of meters high, instantly engulfing the entire area.

Since the current battle location between the two was behind the rebel forces, those hundreds knocked away were naturally rebel soldiers.

This also added further calamity to the already desperate situation of the rebels.

However, after the dust cloud cleared.

Everyone anticipating the outcome glanced towards mid-air, instantly showing startled expressions, staring blankly, their pupils filled with disbelief and surprise.

Chu Qingwu’s brow, pierced by a dark long stick.

His face was full of astonishment, clearly even he didn’t expect it.

The other end of the long stick was held by a person dressed in blue Gai clothes, head adorned with a red mask. He stood before Chu Qingwu, more accurately, between Chu Qingwu and the Green-faced Heavenly King.

"Chu Qingwu, is dead?"

"The Frost Ash Association has sent other experts!"

"I knew it, how could there only be one person, hahaha..."

"The commander is dead!"

"The commander has been killed!"
