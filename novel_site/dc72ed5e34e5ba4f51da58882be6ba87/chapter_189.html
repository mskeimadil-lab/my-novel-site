Chapter 189: Chapter 135: The Fearless Mirror Immortal [Three in One]

Snow Mane could pretend to be dead, and pretend it did well.

This wasn’t something new.

But the fact that it could shoot out those hundreds of quills on its back, Xia Hong really hadn’t thought of that.

Thud, thud, thud...

The ground trembled continuously under Snow Mane’s frantic escape, and amidst the flying snowflakes in the rear were the figures of Xia Hong and the other eleven pursuing relentlessly.

"It seems the quills on its back are its ultimate move, luckily we mainly used bows today, this counts as a lucky hit!"

In the midst of his quick chase, Xia Hong glanced at the wound on his shoulder, his eyes showing a hint of relief.

If it weren’t for lacking strength, he wouldn’t have let the other fifteen disperse and hide in the surrounding trees, using bows to hunt Snow Mane.

On the other hand, thinking about it, if everyone’s strength were stronger, unafraid of facing Snow Mane head-on, and using over a dozen people to attack closely, Snow Mane wouldn’t have waited until the end to pull such a move.

Even when standing so far away, and even though he had everyone jump down from the trees in advance, his shoulder was still grazed by a quill shot out by Snow Mane, leaving a palm-sized wound.

"Why are three people missing?"

Xia Hong turned his head to see how everyone else’s injuries were and found only eight people following him, immediately asking.

Zhu Shun and Zou Yuankai were injured, he had left Qiu Peng and Ying Xuan to take care of them, now there should be eleven people with him, not just eight.

Xia Chuan, panting slightly, answered, "Zhao Long and the others stayed back to collect arrows, worried we might run out, they should catch up soon."

Hearing this, Xia Hong nodded, his eyes showing a hint of approval.

They were chasing Snow Mane now, indeed only carrying bows without arrows left.

Just in the hunt of Snow Mane, all of them had shot all their arrows, though most of them remained stuck in Snow Mane, quite a few were also on the ground.

Patter, patter, patter, patter, patter...

"Leader, it’s slowing down!"

Hearing the change in Snow Mane’s footsteps, Luo Yuan immediately warned.

Seeing Snow Mane’s staggering figure, no longer in the manic running state, Xia Hong smiled with joy.

"Let’s slow down too, keep our distance and wait for Zhao Long and the others to bring arrows."

Despite being ninety percent sure Snow Mane was at its wits’ end.

Yet, the earlier scene still startled Xia Hong slightly; who knows what other life-saving tricks or moves Snow Mane might have?

It’s best to be cautious!

Even with these thoughts, Xia Hong watched Snow Mane continue on, glancing ahead with a hint of worry on his face.

Snow Mane had fled westward, deeper into Redwood Ridge.

In just a short time, it had brought them about three kilometers in, beyond the periphery.

Counting more than a kilometer before, all nine of them were about four to five kilometers deep into Redwood Ridge by now.

"We can’t go any deeper, attracting other Cold Beasts would be the end!"

At this moment, not only Xia Hong, but the others also noticed the unfamiliar surroundings, the variety of trees increased, and there seemed to be glimpses of some colossal creatures in the distance.

The environment here was evidently much quieter than outside.

Except for the commotion caused by Snow Mane’s escape and their footsteps, no other sounds could be heard.

Every view was shrouded in shadows and darkness.

This deep area of Redwood Ridge, they had never ventured before.

If an accident occurred, the outcome would be hard to predict.

Xia Hong struggled internally, reason told him to stop pursuing, yet the desire for Intermediate Cold Beast Blood made it hard for him to abandon the already gravely injured Snow Mane.

"Leader, we’ve arrived!"

Fortunately, Zhao Long and his brothers had arrived from behind.

Not only did the three arrive, but each held a bundle of Iron Arrows in both hands.

"Move up, keep it pinned down, don’t let it escape further!"

Xia Hong’s face lit up with joy, this was why he nodded in approval when Xia Chuan mentioned Zhao Long and the others going to fetch arrows.

When Snow Mane made its hasty escape, he hadn’t thought they were out of arrows.

He only thought Snow Mane was at death’s door, and catching up would have it in hand.

If it weren’t for Zhao Long and the others being so considerate, they’d either continue following Snow Mane deeper in or risk their lives; either method would be fraught with danger.

Most Iron Arrows were lodged in Snow Mane, and given the limited time, they probably didn’t have time for an extensive search. Zhao Long and the others brought about sixty to seventy Iron Arrows, not a massive number.

But these sixty to seventy arrows were enough to issue the final death knell to the grievously injured Snow Mane.

Whoosh... whoosh... whoosh...

Before shooting, the twelve had already sped up and dispersed to both sides of Snow Mane.

Over sixty Iron Arrows flew rapidly from both sides.

Their targets were all directed at Snow Mane’s head.

Snow Mane, already staggering, got shot left and right by the continuous Iron Arrows from both sides. Its head, already riddled with Iron Arrows and grievously wounded, was shot multiple times more, its gait deforming, and its massive body twitching and stopping.

Finally, after stumbling over twenty meters more, Snow Mane’s vitality began to drain faster, a withering aura spread from its massive body.

Bang...

In the end, its limbs finally went limp.
