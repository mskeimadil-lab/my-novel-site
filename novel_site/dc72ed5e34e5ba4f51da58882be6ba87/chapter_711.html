Chapter 711: Chapter 292: White Dew Gu’s Open Plot, Long Mountain’s Advance

"With the Holy Sun Pill, that White Dew Gu will definitely use Li Xuantian to secretly win over the majority of Cold Resistance Level from both families. Li Xuanling should have calculated this point long ago. To deal with Great Xia, she is willing to spend heavily, hum!"

Xia Hong’s eyes flickered with gloom, the taste of being manipulated by White Dew Gu was naturally unpleasant, but this was not yet severe. The key was Li Xuanling, and also that group from Long Mountain. To maintain their ruling position in Longyou, they were willing to be driven by Deceitful Monsters, which made him very annoyed.

If Deceitful Monsters grow too powerful, the harm to humans is essentially unlimited.

At the beginning of White Dew Gu’s resurgence, just using the spread of plague could massacre the Yang Lu Camp with nearly thirty thousand people, and there was also the subsequent tragedy at Hanqiong Gourd Valley. If not for Great Xia using Solidified Fire Oil to save people, likely none would have survived.

In such a situation, Li Xuanling still dares to let White Dew Gu go unchecked, clearly in this woman’s heart, maintaining Long Mountain’s hegemonic position surpasses everything.

"Wanting to rely on Deceitful Monsters to maintain Long Mountain’s hegemonic position, this woman is also blinded by greed. It’s just a pity that Long Mountain and you are destined to be trampled under my feet!"

Xia Hong’s eyes flickered with disdain, no longer dwelling on Li Xuantian and White Dew Gu’s matters, first raised his head to glance at the sky outside, then turned to the giant map of Longyou behind the main seat, asked in a deep voice:

"Have Xia Chuan and Peng Bo returned yet?"

Yuwen Dao knew Xia Hong was about to start deploying, his expression brightened as he replied: "Minister came back half an hour ago, he is currently leading Chen Yingyuan and others in a three-kilometer vigilance around the camp, strictly preventing Long Mountain from scouting our situation;

Peng Bo just returned, bringing fifteen Cold Resistance Level in total. Also, according to him, Xia City has already been notified, a large number of Earth-digging Realm are starting to pack up, and will head to Jing Valley to Longyou overnight at the fastest speed."

During the day in Ice Abyss, there are only two hours.

Xia Chuan ran separately to Hanqiong and Beehive places, Peng Bo went to Xia City and time basically passed, now it’s less than an hour until dawn.

Xia Hong pondered for a moment, then said: "You go now to notify Zhao Long, isn’t he going to lead the army through Xia Direct Path overnight? Have him make a commotion when approaching Wushuang, definitely let the people from Long Mountain know."

Yuwen Dao only pondered for a moment before quickly understanding, showing a smile.

Yesterday Xia Hong indeed said that Wushuang does not need to be firmly defended, so the reason for having Zhao Long purposely make noise is not hard to deduce.

"Notify everyone on Wushuang, after nightfall, start quietly moving batches through Xia Direct Path to Beehive, strive to transfer everyone before Zhao Long’s army arrives, leaving only 1000 Cloud Jiao Army and all Cold Resistance Level.

The interconnected direct paths of Hanqiong, Beehive, and Wushuang have been completed, Zhao Long’s thousand people will arrive quickly after nightfall. Notify Xia Chuan to relax vigilance at that time, it’s best to let Long Mountain’s spies see we have reinforcements moving toward Wushuang."

"That’s all, go handle it."

"Understood!"

Yuwen Dao acknowledged with a bow, then swiftly turned and exited the hall.

"Three thousand Longyou Army must all be gathered at Song Yuan base, after nightfall will press toward Wushuang. It’s just a pity they don’t have Direct Path, even if they send people ahead to open the way, the marching speed is extremely slow. As long as I give them misinformation, my operational space is large, there’s no way to lose this battle!"

Xia Hong continued to look at the map, pondered for a moment, then showed a confident look in his eyes.

Forty-eighth year of Longyou, June 21, dusk before nightfall

Song Yuan base, main hall

Li Xuanling still sat at the main seat, Li Tiancheng was at the foremost position on the lower left.

After Li Tiancheng’s previous mediation and admonition, the atmosphere among Long Mountain’s Cold Resistance Level was noticeably improved. Everyone sat upright, focusing their eyes on Li Xuanping, Li Xuanqiu, Meng Ying, and Cheng Guang in the center.

All four of them were covered with snowflakes, obviously just returned from outside.

Li Xuanping was the first to stand out, bowing: "I went to the north side of Wushuang, at least sensed five Cold Resistance Level auras, they seemed to sense me too, immediately surrounded me. Although their strength isn’t strong, I’m worried Great Xia might have more reinforcements coming, so I came back first."

Meng Ying was the second to say: "I went to the south side, with six Cold Resistance Level, the situation is similar, also discovered before getting close, could only retreat."

Li Xuanqiu was a rare female among the Cold Resistance Level, standing third to bow: "I went east, also six Cold Resistance Level."

The last was Cheng Guang, with the most solemn expression, bowing: "I went west, Great Xia deployed at least ten Cold Resistance Level in this direction, and the distance ahead is very far, clearly meant to prevent us from scouting the camp situation."

Wow...

"This is more than Zhaoyang’s Cold Resistance Level."
