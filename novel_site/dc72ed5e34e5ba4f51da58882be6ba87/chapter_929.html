Chapter 929: Chapter 348: Zhu Ling in Dire Straits, Eight Camps Martial Contest, Betting Five Years—East Ridge Has Fallen!

If you defend or attack the stage successfully, you can earn one point. The final ranking of the eight villages depends on the total score accumulated by the five competitors from each village.

With these rules, a round-robin battle is unavoidable.

Under normal circumstances, it’s almost impossible for the other seven families to win not even a single match. But with the aid of the Holy Item Blood Qi Pill from Beishuo, and if Zhu Baixu and his team are cautious enough, it might be achievable.

"Alright, it’s almost dark. Zhu Da, go notify the five of Bai Xu to get ready at the stage. Zhu Mai, notify He Wuyang to come over. Everyone else, follow me to the entrance of the base, those seven families should be arriving soon!"

Everyone in the hall stood up and followed Zhu Tong’s instructions.

......

The eight villages of Beining Mountain are actually not far from each other, all under Beishuo’s jurisdiction. Although there is friction, there is no concern of annexation, so the specific locations of each village’s base are known to each other.

After nightfall, Zhu Tong waited at the entrance for less than an hour before Head of Bai Yuan, Bai Bo, and Head of Long River, Nie Ying, each arrived with a small team.

After all, coming into someone else’s base, even if both have a good relationship with Zhu Tong, and both families clearly respect Zhu Ling, they still brought four Cold Resistance Level members each, obviously considering safety.

Zhu Tong didn’t say much, just glanced at the five young people behind them with a smile on his face and extended his hand, "There are still five families not here. Shall we wait here for a while, my two leaders and I?"

"Yanggu and Wanxing are closer than us, and they haven’t arrived first?"

Bai Bo’s words had just landed when he noticed Zhu Tong’s face darkening, realizing he had hit a sore spot, he quickly closed his mouth.

"Wagering future tribute supplies for three years in one go, it seems Brother Zhu is determined to win this time. Indeed, Lu Yang has been too rampant in the past three years, sheltered by Lord He. If not curbed, it might lead to big trouble."

Obviously, Nie Ying was better at this, and after listening, Zhu Tong’s dark expression eased considerably. He was about to speak when his gaze suddenly lifted to the outside of the base, noticing something, and a hint of anger surfaced in his eyes.

"It’s Yang Quan and Wan Tianhe arriving..."

"Not only them, can’t you see there’s a large group?"

"Hong Fan and Zi Jin are all here, with Lu Yang leading!"

"Joining Lu Yang, are Yanggu and Wanxing done pretending?"

......

The words from Nie Ying, Bai Bo and others undoubtedly added to Zhu Tong’s anger, but after some thought, he forcibly suppressed his fury, letting his face gradually calm down.

It was only when Lu Yang approached that he smiled, took one or two steps forward, and with a slightly sarcastic tone said, "Leader Lu has only been in Beining Mountain for a little over three years, and already has such a following, truly enviable to me!"

As he spoke, his gaze was sharply focused on Yang Quan and Wan Tianhe.

Both were well aware that Zhu Tong was mocking them, but being in their fifties or sixties, they naturally wouldn’t lose face over such a trivial taunt and simply ignored Zhu Tong, acting as though they hadn’t heard him at all.

This only made Zhu Tong angrier.

But his anger didn’t stop there.

"Three years and still unable to win Brother Zhu’s respect, even fighting over an iron mine earlier this year, I’m truly ashamed. Only when Brother Zhu is in this entourage of followers will it truly be an accomplishment."

"Lu Yang, you are too presumptuous!"

"Lu Yang, how dare you insult our leader like this."

Too arrogant!

Lu Yang’s utter disregard for Zhu Tong, his domineering attitude, it was just too arrogant. Given his suppression of Zhu Ling over the past three years, even before Zhu Tong, the person in question responded, the group of Cold Resistance Level from Zhu Ling’s side couldn’t hold back and stepped forward to angrily rebuke.

Tshh...

"What, isn’t today meant for the younger generation’s competition? Shall we have a match first?"

Lu Yang hadn’t yet spoken, but Zhao Hu, with his fiery temper, already drew out the knife from his waist. His vigor surged, and just the cold edge of the knife as it was drawn made several Cold Resistance Level from Zhu Ling’s side step back.

Including Zhu Tong, along with the leaders Nie Ying and Bai Bo, the three houses with over twenty Cold Resistance Level members together looked at Zhao Hu, and their faces were filled with dread.

"Zhao Hu!"

"He’s the Zhao Hu from Mountain Yu of East Ridge Village, in charge of the Hunting Team, rumored to be the strongest Cold Resistance level across Beining Mountain."

"The East Ridge Tiger, it refers to him, doesn’t it?"

...

Lu Yang heard several whispers, and a slight smile appeared on his face.

Three years ago, Zhao Hu already had Late-stage Cold Resistance Cultivation, with the power of fourteen. Now, he’s just a step away from the Peak of Cold Resistance, with a basic strength of nineteen. It’s indeed not exaggerated to call him the strongest Cold Resistance level in all of Beining Mountain.

In these three years, East Ridge has mainly been seizing hunting grounds. Zhao Hu, in charge of the Hunting Bureau, naturally attacked frequently, and with so many incidents, a surrounding busybody gave him the title East Ridge Tiger. When they first heard about this, they joked around with Zhao Hu using those four words quite a bit.

"Stay calm, Leader Lu is still as straightforward as ever. Today’s for the younger generation’s competition; us elders won’t join the fun. However, Leader Lu..."

Zhu Tong was evidently also wary of Zhao Hu, first calming his people behind him, intending to change the subject. But as he lowered his head and saw the five young people in black robes behind Lu Yang, his eyebrows furrowed sharply.

From their posture, it was easy to determine they were young, but the issue was he couldn’t sense any hint of Qi Muscle from them.
