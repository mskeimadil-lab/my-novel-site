Chapter 202: Chapter 138 Mirror Immortal Strange, Cold Resistance Level? [3-in-1]_2

"When we just merged, the temperaments of the brothers He Meng and He Gang were not as irritable and violent as they are now. During the last hunt, you personally confirmed it, both were clearly dead, yet mysteriously resurrected. Doesn’t that explain the issue? That Mirror Immortal is definitely related to the Deceitful Monster. I even suspect that the leader now may have already..."

"Be careful with your words, the leader is impossible!"

Yuwen Hu directly interrupted his son, pulled out a silver-edged circular jade pendant from his bosom, and shook his head heavily.

"The leader cannot be associated with the Deceitful Monster, I can be sure. Several times he came near me, and this Deception Detection Jade had no reaction at all.

He Meng, He Gang, and others who have died once indeed experience an enormous change in temperament, but the leader has always remained the same. As you heard today, He Meng proposed to kill that Xia Hong and the leader did not agree, not only promoting you but also acknowledging your way of persuasion, letting you and He Meng compete fairly."

Yuwen Dao was suddenly speechless; he also believed in the Deception Detection Jade.

He Yuanhun’s temperament has indeed not changed, still gentle and understanding like before, not like He Gang and He Meng.

But with the recent events, saying that leader He Yuanhun has no relation to the Deceitful Monster really does not add up.

"In this world, no one is unafraid of death, whether it is you, father, or me, nor even the leader; none is an exception. He Meng, He Gang, people in the camp, and even those from Ningzhou Camp who merged over, all who have worshiped the Mirror Immortal become unafraid of death, which defies logic.

Not to mention He Meng and others resurrecting, and the eight people I saw with my own eyes last night. Even if the leader himself has no relation to the Deceitful Monster, that mirror in his hand cannot be unrelated.

Father, please do not be swayed by the leader to worship that Mirror Immortal!"

Although the Deception Detection Jade could be considered ironclad evidence, Yuwen Dao couldn’t help but analyze it carefully and persuaded his father once more.

Knowing his son was worried about him, a warmth rose in Yuwen Hu’s heart, he shook his head gently and said, "Rest assured, your father was once a leader too, I’m not so muddle-headed yet. Old He talked to me several times, wanting me to worship that Mirror Immortal too, but I have declined several times already, it’s just... "

Speaking of this, Yuwen Hu hesitated slightly and continued:

"More than half of the camp have already worshiped that Mirror Immortal, and more keep joining. If this continues, once the entire camp has worshiped, and only our family hasn’t, by then, whether to worship it or not will no longer be in our control."

Upon hearing this, Yuwen Dao’s face immediately turned troubled.

Outsiders are not tolerated anywhere.

Especially in such a massive camp population like Mirror Immortal’s.

When everyone in the camp truly accepts the Mirror Immortal, even knowing it relates to the Deceitful Monster, they will have to accept it.

"Fortunately, the leader has not yet forced others, currently only adopting a voluntary attitude. I have refused several times, and he hasn’t said much."

Upon hearing this, Yuwen Dao’s eyebrows slightly relaxed, but he couldn’t help but ask with confusion, "The leader is such a wise and strategic person, how can he not see through that the mirror is related to the Deceitful Monster and actually believes in the resurrection?"

Yuwen Hu pondered for a moment after hearing this, then leisurely asked in return:

"Is it possible that you... do not believe it at all?"

This question stunned Yuwen Dao into complete silence.

He Meng, He Gang, and others who have worshiped the Mirror Immortal, anyone who has been resurrected once, their memories, cultivation, actions, and everything else are exactly the same as before, without a single visible difference.

The only change is that their temperament becomes gradually more irritable.

But even that point is actually within an acceptable range.

If others were clearly told that worshiping the Mirror Immortal could grant the Immortal ability, with the only side effect being a more irritable temperament after dying once and everything else remaining normal, who would not be willing?

Who in the perilous Ice Abyss World isn’t afraid of death?

Seeing the indomitable spirit of those people hunting without fear last night, although Yuwen Dao found it strange, he could not deny he was somewhat envious as well.

If merely worshipping the Mirror Immortal could grant the Immortal ability...

"Alright, let’s not talk about this anymore. That leader of the Great Xia Camp almost killed you earlier, but based on what you said, you seem to have a pretty good impression of him?"

Seeing his son becoming more engrossed in thought, Yuwen Hu quickly changed the topic.

"That Xia Hong is quite reasonable, able to understand the implications in my words, a clever man. He handles matters cautiously and has a prudent character. If we could work together, he should be a strong asset. Indeed, I have a fairly good impression of him!"

"Then you must hurry, He Meng has already notified the members of the Hunting Team to head to Redwood Ridge specifically, it seems like he intends to cause trouble for Great Xia. If you could persuade Xia Hong to lead his people to join the Mirror Immortal, it would save the camp a lot of trouble, not to mention that you’d gain a powerful assistant!"

Yuwen Dao nodded first, but soon shook his head and said, "It’s just a good impression; I’m not going to exhaust my efforts to save him. Persuasion is merely my duty, nothing more. From what I can see, he doesn’t seem like someone who would be content with a subordinate position. I’ll let He Meng cause some trouble first, and persuasion should be more effective afterward."

Hearing his son’s mature and steady response, Yuwen Hu nodded slightly in approval, his face showing a bit of a smile.
