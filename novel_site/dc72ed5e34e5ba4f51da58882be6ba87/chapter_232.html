Chapter 232: Chapter 145 Murder, Gruesome_4

"These bastards!"

He Meng gritted his teeth and shouted angrily, then quickly drew back his knife and dodged.

The first arrow targeted the knife, which he could ignore, but the arrows coming at him couldn’t be ignored.

Even though he had begun reshaping his skin, he was still far from ignoring arrows shot from the Earth-digging Realm, otherwise he wouldn’t have lost an ear just now.

Swish... swish... swish...

"Leader, there are many of them coming!"

A continuous rain of arrows hurriedly approached from behind. Not only He Meng, but everyone at the Mirror Immortal Camp turned to look back, feeling their scalps tingle.

On the big trees behind them stood at least a hundred people, frantically shooting arrows at them, more than twenty people.

"Retreat first!"

He Meng was unable to kill Hong Mu. Though angry, seeing the hundreds of Great Xia archers and the Cold Beast rapidly approaching internally, he still retained his reason.

The people from the Mirror Immortal Camp obeyed the order, immediately dispersing and escaping towards the north.

"Hong Mu, throw the torch and come over, quickly!"

Xia Chuan’s voice sounded from afar. Hong Mu immediately threw the torch in his hand and quickly ran east with the person beside him.

Rumble... rumble... rumble...

In the distance, an enormous creature was rapidly approaching, the ground ceaselessly trembling.

"My son, are you alright?"

Hong Gang caught his son from the front of the crowd. Seeing Hong Mu in a sorry state, with multiple arrow wounds on his body, his face was filled with rage.

"Father, I’m fine."

Hong Mu first reassured Hong Gang, then, with a trace of pain on his face, turned to Xia Chuan and said, "My lord, those at the Mirror Immortal Camp came directly over. Initially, they wanted to spy on our base location, so they didn’t go for the kill. But later, when they saw we weren’t cooperating, they started killing directly. Eight out of the ten in my group were killed!"

"To directly cross the border south to kill, it’s outrageous how arrogant He Meng is!"

"These bastards, killing people!"

"When the leader took us to patrol the north, we didn’t go for the kill. Yet, they dared to kill directly."

"Didn’t you hear, they still want to find out our base’s location. This means they plan to fight us to the death!"

......

The others might not be clear, but the Hunting Team, of course, understood why He Meng and his crew came over.

Three pieces of prey were snatched from them, and He Meng and the others were understandably angry.

But such a reaction, they never expected.

To directly kill people and even try to spy on the Great Xia’s base location!

This clearly shows they don’t respect Great Xia, thinking Great Xia and the Mirror Immortal Camp are unequal, coming to the door to find an explanation!

The seventeen members of the Hunting Team, including Xia Chuan, were instantly filled with overwhelming anger.

"Too many people have come out, everyone else go back first, the Hunting Team will follow me north to see if there are any survivors. Other matters, we’ll wait till the leader wakes up!"

Xia Chuan looked north, clenched his fists tightly. Despite the intense killing intent in his eyes, he held back his anger and signaled the others to quickly return.

Though he had a poor impression of the Mirror Immortal Camp, Xia Chuan had to admit that their strength was greater than Great Xia’s.

Especially in the absence of Xia Hong, even more so!

All of the Logging Realm returned to the camp, while Xia Chuan took the sixteen members of the Hunting Team and followed the traces of Hong Mu’s recent escape, searching north.

As one gruesome corpse after another was discovered, the expressions on Xia Chuan and the others’ faces grew increasingly furious.

"The chest was stabbed over ten times."

"The head was sliced off, with an ear still in its mouth, likely tortured by He Meng."

"Nailed to a tree with arrows, then interrogated, with blows to the head and body."

"This is Bai Wuting’s brother, Bai Wufan, who just broke through last month!"

"He Meng and these beasts!"

...

Until they found the headless corpse, the fury in everyone’s hearts surged to its peak.

Everyone looked at the headless body on the ground, their chests heaving, their hands clenched tightly, eyes full of coldness.

"Bring the bodies back, we’ll speak after the leader comes out of seclusion!"

Xia Chuan was now also enraged, and after speaking, directly picked up the corpse on the ground and headed back to the base.

"Losing eight Logging Realm members at once, it’s a first for Great Xia, and when my brother wakes up, he’ll probably be very angry too!"

As Xia Chuan led everyone back to the base, the anger on his face lingered.

Guessing Xia Hong would never have thought He Meng’s temper would be so big.

Only just after the prey was snatched, he immediately crosses the border to the south to kill.

Not only killing, he also wanted to directly find out the location of Great Xia’s base!

If he had known, he certainly wouldn’t have used Beast Blood to go into seclusion.
