Chapter 1250: Chapter 414: Birthday, the Painter Living in Xia City, Population Soars, Disheartened

"That means, there are at least two to three thousand people involved in management, and our camp currently has a total of over eight thousand at Cold Resistance Level. Subtracting those in the army, there are probably just over three thousand left. So, those assigned to hunting at Cold Resistance Level are less than a thousand?"

Upon hearing this question, Xia Chuan immediately lowered his head and fell into thought.

With the migration of the Bashang population, the Cold Resistance Level in Great Xia directly increased by more than 500, bringing the total to over 8,200; the number at the Earth-digging Realm Limit is even more, currently over 31,000.

Those holding official positions go without saying, they are the elites among the Cold Resistance Level;

As for those who can work under the Eight Departments and the Eight Divisions in various jurisdictions, they must at least have a Cold Resistance Level Cultivation.

Of course, it’s not that these people don’t hunt, it’s just that their management duties mean they have less time to spend on hunting compared to others, and each month they only complete the minimum tasks.

So Xia Hong’s calculation is indeed correct, now the number of Cold Resistance Level personnel capable of hunting in the camp is truly less than a thousand.

"The population suddenly increased to nearly 2.6 million, there’s no need to say anything about absorbing more population, even natural growth is happening swiftly, and the Camp Needs Department is already calling for resources because of shortages; we shouldn’t expand jurisdictions for now.

I’ve already discussed with Qiu Peng, asking him to acquire more basic cultivation resources in trade with the Northern Six Towns to fill the gaps in the Camp Needs Department;

There are still not enough Cold Resistance Level personnel in the camp, the Martial Arts Pavilion should continue to supply the Peak of Earth-digging Realm in the next year, fostering more Cold Resistance Level individuals as much as possible."

"Understood!"

"As for persuading the Eight Towns to form an alliance with us, there’s no need to rush; take it slowly. As for Chen Cang and Bashang, we currently know almost nothing about them. Anyway, these two won’t come over for now, deploying more spies can at most stir up relations among the Nine Towns, nothing major can be done, so there’s no need to worry about them."

Chen Cang?

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan was momentarily stunned, then immediately realized something, and asked with a joyful expression, "Brother, has Mu Longhe relented?"

Seeing Xia Hong confirm with a nod, Xia Chuan immediately smiled and shook his head, saying, "It’s not surprising. That fool Duan Hong took just over half a month to seize the position of head of the courtyard from Mu Qingyu, replacing him with Duan Xing. From the news transmitted by several Mu Family younglings, Mu Qinghe’s position as Deputy Army Chief is also soon to be lost. Mu Longhe knowing all this, feeling despondent, would be too foolish not to turn to us!"

"Hahaha..."

Xia Hong laughed heartily for a few moments, saying, "Don’t blame Duan Hong for being foolish. He and Yang Zhun thought Mu Longhe was crippled after losing both arms. Who would have thought you could reconnect severed arms? Mu Longhe now owes three Holy Vein Pills to the Camp Needs Department, and his injuries, like Xiang Fuhai’s, are almost fully healed."

This means that Great Xia has now gained two more individuals at the Xianyang Level!

Xia Chuan’s expression immediately brightened, and he quickly asked, "Did Mu Longhe only tell Brother the words ’Chen Cang’? Did he not say how strong Chen Cang is?"

Of course, not just that!

Xia Hong’s eyes glowed brightly, then he shook his head, saying, "He was informed about Chen Cang’s situation by Yang Zhun, and doesn’t know much.

However, from the fact that Chen Cang was able to casually dispatch three Xianyang Level individuals to assist the Northern Three Towns this April, it can be inferred that a Feudal Town Level camp definitely does not lack those of the Xianyang Level..."

"Brother, does Mu Longhe know the level above Xianyang Level?"

Upon hearing this question, Xia Hong’s face truly became excited, and he nodded emphatically, saying, "After Xianyang, it’s the Tribulation Body Level!"

Tribulation Body Level!

Upon hearing these three words, an indescribable sense of awe surged in Xia Chuan’s heart, his eyes full of longing;

Xia Hong, meanwhile, was recalling last night’s discovery about the cultivation realm after the Xianyang Level from Mu Longhe, shaking his head repeatedly.

[Special Function 2: Main Cauldron can be used for injury diagnosis and healing; any injuries of cultivators below the Venerable Level can be identified by the Main Cauldron and treated accordingly, leading to recovery]

Previously, because of this description from the Alchemy Pavilion, Xia Hong eagerly believed that the level beyond the Xianyang Level was the Venerable Level, but he had only learned from Mu Longhe that after the Xianyang Level comes the Tribulation Body Level, followed by Venerable Appearance Level.

"I’ve been misled by the system, but..."

Xia Hong initially grumbled, but then his heart grew more fervent.

If based on his earlier understanding, the Main Cauldron of the Alchemy Pavilion could at most be used to heal and save Xianyang Level individuals;

But now, having confirmed that after Tribulation Body comes Venerable Level, it means the Main Cauldron can be used to heal Tribulation Body Level strong individuals.

"Utilizing this function well is a powerful weapon indeed!"
