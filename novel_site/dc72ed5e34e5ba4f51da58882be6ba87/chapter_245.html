Chapter 245: Chapter 149: Supreme Combat Body, The Dangerous Gao Yu【Please Vote】_2

Considering Great Xia’s current activity area, a Cold Resistance Level being able to cross more than two hundred miles of Twin Dragon Mountain from the east side to the west is clearly impossible.

This Gao Yu, is either hiding his strength or not telling the truth!

Xia Hong’s expression changed, and Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and the others immediately reacted, all turning to stare at Gao Yu, their expressions gradually becoming vigilant.

Facing everyone’s suspicious looks, Gao Yu was silent for a moment, then gave a bitter smile and said, "Alright, alright, it seems I can’t hide it. I might as well be honest."

As he spoke, Gao Yu paused, then turned to face the crowd and directly unbuttoned his shirt, revealing his chest.

"Hiss..."

Xia Hong, along with everyone else, couldn’t help but gasp.

Gao Yu’s exposed chest was covered with countless fine cracks.

It looked like broken pottery, or like parched earth after a drought, with the exposed red flesh between the cracks very apparent, the sight truly horrifying.

It was hard to believe that Gao Yu, who had just been smiling gently and communicating warmly with them, was carrying such terrifying and horrifying injuries.

"Was this done by a Deceitful Monster?"

Xia Hong frowned slightly and asked.

Such injuries clearly couldn’t have been caused by a Cold Beast or a human.

Gao Yu turned around, facing away from the others towards the Iron Refining Furnace, put his clothes back on first, and then turned around to sit down, nodding lightly to Xia Hong:

"Yes, it was an Intermediate Strange Monster..."

After speaking, he paused before continuing to explain:

"Ten days ago, I left the village to hunt in Twin Dragon Mountain. Usually, the area I hunted was at most a little over ten miles in the outskirts of Twin Dragon Mountain, but that day I was unlucky, and soon after entering, I encountered a Deceitful Monster.

The Deceitful Monster could not only control many Cold Beasts at once but also shoot an evil light that fragmented human bodies. I was hit by it, chased all the way, panicked, and unintentionally crossed Twin Dragon Mountain to this side."

As Gao Yu finished his words, including Xia Hong, everyone stood up with a whoosh, glanced at the sky above the valley, and then looked back at Gao Yu, their eyes filled with fury.

"Rest assured, rest assured, I had already shaken off that Deceitful Monster midway. The four Cold Beasts it controlled have also been killed by me. It won’t follow me here, please rest easy!"

Even though Gao Yu explained quickly, Xia Chuan and the others’ expressions didn’t relax, they just all turned to look at Xia Hong.

Xia Hong looked at Gao Yu, brows tightly knit, his heart filled with anger.

As expected, strangers hardly bring any good news!

This person’s fleeing was still being hunted by an Intermediate Strange Monster.

Gao Yu said nicely that the Deceitful Monster wouldn’t come after him, but can he guarantee that?

A Wooden Puppet Monster that hadn’t even advanced to Intermediate level almost slaughtered all humans in Redwood Ridge.

What if a genuine Intermediate Strange Monster is brought here?

Being able to control multiple Cold Beasts at once and shoot light that fragments human bodies, just these two abilities alone would bring Great Xia enormous trouble, how could Xia Hong not be angry.

If it weren’t for Gao Yu being so strong, and having been polite from the start, without intentionally hiding anything, Xia Hong would have probably already scolded him out of anger and driven him away.

Seeing the expressions on everyone’s faces, Gao Yu clearly realized that he was already unwelcome, thought for a long time, and finally showed a bit of helplessness, cupping his hands toward Xia Hong and saying:

"Brother Xia, in this matter, it is indeed Gao who is wrong. But right now, I’m injured and unfamiliar with the surroundings, I only ask to stay in Great Xia for five days. Once my injuries have sufficiently healed, I will leave immediately, how about it?"

Even though Gao Yu’s tone carried a hint of pleading, Xia Chuan and the others shook their heads immediately, showing clear signs of refusal on their faces.

However, Xia Hong remained silent.

Seeing this, Gao Yu knew there was still a chance, and immediately continued to add:

"Of course, if during this time the Deceitful Monster really comes, Gao will not just leave, and will definitely stay and face it together with Brother Xia!"

Xia Hong’s brows furrowed slightly, looking at Gao Yu, he became even more dubious.

This person’s appearance was really too inexplicable;

To be honest, until now, he hadn’t fully believed Gao Yu’s words.

But the problem was, this person didn’t seem bad, and his attitude was okay.

His story had some holes, but it could barely make sense.

Chased by a Deceitful Monster, fleeing in panic to arrive here, with such serious injuries on him, it could explain why he was hesitant and reluctant to clarify things earlier.

After all, Gao Yu was a stranger to Great Xia, and Great Xia was also unfamiliar to him; they were cautious, and naturally, he was cautious too.

The main issue is, five days is a bit long...

Remembering that there might be a big trouble following this person, and that this big trouble might be eyeing the Great Xia Station from above the valley, Xia Hong thought for a long time, and finally took a deep breath, saying:

"Two days, we’ll give you a maximum of two days, then you must leave. Though our Daxia Camp is small, we have over a thousand people, hope Brother Gao will understand!"

Xia Hong considered himself not a very warm-hearted person, but he also couldn’t really leave people to die.

Looking at Gao Yu’s chest injuries, don’t mention five days, without special treatment, probably wouldn’t recover in ten days or half a month, he probably didn’t even plan to stay in the camp for treatment, just needed a place to rest.

In that case, two days’ time will be enough!
