Chapter 287: Chapter 160 The night has fallen, they should arrive_4

Not to mention, the Intermediate Thunder Kui transformed from the wooden mannequins isn’t afraid of pain.

In such a situation, it’s expected that the four Thunder Kui transformed from the wooden mannequins, even if unable to kill the four Intermediate Cold Beasts outside the wooden house, should at least have the upper hand.

But at this moment, the reality is, the four Thunder Kui and the four Cold Beasts outside are evenly matched, with neither side having the advantage.

On the eastern side, a Thunder Kui collided, crushing Teng Jiao’s chest directly.

Yet Teng Jiao acted as if nothing had happened, standing half-upright, with eyes gleaming like a mirror, and leapt back toward the Thunder Kui.

Not just on the eastern side, but the Snow Mane on the western side, the Demon Sheep on the southern side, and the Frost Wolf on the northern side, were the same.

These four Intermediate Cold Beasts are not only unafraid of pain, but also seem fearless of death, or rather, they do not seem to feel their injuries at all.

Especially seeing the Snow Mane on the western side, whose front limb was snapped by a blow from the Thunder Kui, and yet in just four or five breaths, it stood back up as if nothing had happened, Xia Chuan’s pupils instantly constricted.

Fearless of death, isn’t that how the people from the Mirror Immortal Camp are!

No, it’s not just fearlessness of death, these four Cold Beasts can also ignore their injuries.

Bang...

Although the four Intermediate Cold Beasts were restrained by the Thunder Kui transformed from the wooden mannequins, the large iron gate on the western side finally collapsed under the relentless assault of the two to three hundred Snow Manes and Demon Sheep outside.

"Yuan Cheng, pull back the people, quickly!"

"Everyone listen to my command, when I count to three, release together."

The moment the gate collapsed, Xia Chuan turned to glance at the sky, and immediately shouted twice.

The first shout was directed at Yuan Cheng and the over hundred people at the gate;

The second shout was toward Mu Dong and the more than four hundred people in the main hall.

"One,"

Xia Chuan finished speaking and began counting down.

As the gate collapsed, Yuan Cheng led over a hundred people to retreat, and outside the western gate, a dark mass of Snow Mane Demon Sheep poured in like a tide.

"Two,"

By the second count, the beast horde from the west was less than a hundred meters away from everyone.

"Three!"

As soon as the count reached three, Mu Dong was the first to release, followed by over four hundred people, though some were faster and some slower, but the interval was less than a breath, almost simultaneously releasing the chains in their hands.

Clang... clang... clang...

Without the strength of the people pulling the chains, the iron materials at the crucial points of the wooden house rapidly reset, the speed was so fast that it even dragged out four long lines of fire on the ground.

Boom...

Without the reinforcement of those iron materials, the collision from the Cold Beasts outside made an immediate impact, and the four walls of the wooden house began their first wave of collapse.

Now, not only the Snow Mane Demon Sheep at the western main gate, but also the Teng Jiao on the eastern side, and the Frost Wolves on both the southern and northern sides, all rushed into the first floor of the wooden house following the collapsing wooden walls.

The overwhelming surge of Cold Beasts caused a terrifying tremor, and Xia Chuan and others, standing unsteadily in the center of the main hall, had the most to say about it.

Everyone’s faces were filled with fear, looking up at the Cold Beasts charging from all directions, some desperate people even closed their eyes directly.

However, just as the wooden house collapsed to its most central position, coincidentally when the Cold Beasts were only a hundred meters away from everyone.

Clang...

The chains that had fully reset suddenly seemed to be caught by something.

Half of the outer layer of the wooden house collapsed, revealing a four-sided fifty-meter long bluish bamboo fence, blocking the Cold Beasts in front.

Those bamboo fences were diagonal and crisscrossed, with a layer of more than twenty centimeters of sharp dense spikes on the surface, and when the Cold Beasts attempted to charge through, those spikes shot out like raindrops at them.

Whoosh... whoosh... whoosh...

Splat...

The power of those spikes was terrifying to the extreme.

Watching the Cold Beasts fall one after another across the bamboo fence, Xia Chuan, Yue Feng, and the others, the despair on their faces disappeared instantly.

Only Xia Chuan, looking up at the already completely dark sky, clenched the fist under his sleeve tightly.

"It’s dark now, those beasts should be coming any time!"
