Chapter 1337: Chapter 435: Yinhe Valley, Bone-Eroding Path, Han Peng’s Decision, At Last Awaited

Han Li placed 10 Dragon Carp Pills in front of Han Peng, and then continued to introduce, "5 Yangyuan Pills, 15 pounds of refined salt, 5 pounds of mid-grade Cold Blood Wine..."

As he watched his grandson present one precious resource after another, and heard him talk about the prosperity of Xia City and the abundance of cultivation resources, Han Peng’s expression grew increasingly shocked, while his pupils also showed a trace of determination, clearly having made some important decision.

"Li’er, tell Grandpa, how has Lu Yang treated you this past year?"

Upon hearing this question, Han Li was briefly stunned, then his face filled with gratitude as he replied, "Grandpa, my master has treated me very well, personally taught me martial arts, imparted many hunting skills, and a lot of cultivation essentials. I’ve also gotten along well with my fellow apprentices, and additionally..."

In truth, having only been an apprentice for a year, Han Li was not qualified to participate in the Martial Arts Competition in the Great Xia, especially when taking the East Ridge Secret Passage to Hanqiong to reach the Great Xia. Han Li, now 24, was not foolish; the moment he learned his master Lu Yang was from the Great Xia people, he had figured out many things.

After figuring it out, his first response was naturally gratitude for his master’s trust. Reflecting on the past year, he had almost no resistance to his master’s teachings and care, immediately accepting the fact of becoming a member of the Great Xia.

Not to mention, having seen so much in Xia City, making him clearly realize that to go further on the path of cultivation, joining the Great Xia was undoubtedly the best choice.

"Grandpa, I’ve seen so much in the Great Xia, let me tell you, even Jinshan Town can’t compare to it. We shouldn’t stay here in Jinyang Village anymore, not only do we have to pay annual tributes to Jinshan Town, but we also have to constantly worry about those bandits from the north. Grandpa, let’s take the people from the village and join the Great Xia directly. I’ll speak with my master, we’ll go to East Ridge first, then to the Great Xia later. I’m sure my master will agree."

Hearing his grandson continuously praise Lu Yang and the Great Xia, and even suggesting to bring the whole village into the Great Xia, Han Peng’s expression was initially complicated. Then, looking at the 10 Dragon Carp Pills in his hand, he showed a trace of relief, speaking in a deep voice, "Li’er, you don’t need to worry about these matters. These cultivation resources, Grandpa doesn’t need them. Keep them for your own use. In a few days, you should return to East Ridge to continue your cultivation with your master. For now, you don’t need to concern yourself with the village’s affairs."

Han Li was stunned hearing this and quickly said, "Grandpa, these Dragon Carp Pills are specifically for you, what are you going to exchange them for if you don’t take them..."

"Enough, Grandpa is of such an age, even with more Dragon Carp Pills, at most, I’d only be able to enhance to above 25 Zong. But you’re different, you have a Superior Battle Body, with your aptitude, you can naturally reach above 16 Zong. With enough Dragon Carp Pills, you could even surpass 40 Zong in the future. Besides, you’ve joined the Great Xia with your master’s support. Who knows, you might even reach Xianyang in the future. Listen to Grandpa and keep the Dan Medicine!"

Han Peng, with a tone that allowed no refusal, pushed the Dan Medicine back to him. Seeing Han Li’s face full of confusion, he smiled and changed the subject, "By the way, that young man named Hong Yu who came with you this time, I saw both Li Hu and Yang Zhong were so respectful towards him. He should be an important figure from the Great Xia, right?"

Hearing this question, Han Li’s face immediately showed some hesitation.

Xia Hong had instructed him on the way to not reveal his identity.

While he was thinking about what lie to make up, Han Peng had already noticed his grandson’s difficulty, smiled, and waved his hand, "Alright, if it shouldn’t be said, don’t say it. You haven’t been back for a year, go see your parents, as well as Feng’er and Ling’er. Let Grandpa be alone for a while."

Hearing this, Han Li had no choice but to nod and went to see his parents and younger siblings.

Han Peng watched his grandson walk away, and after a period of calm, he sighed slightly.

"Six or seventy thousand people, moving them all at once, how difficult. If Cai Qiu finds out I leaked information about them, it would be a disaster. Moreover, the Great Xia has specifically sent people here, clearly wanting me to continue monitoring Cai Qiu’s movements. Even if I wanted to leave, I probably couldn’t..."

Han Peng pondered for a moment, his expression slightly revitalized, and he murmured resolutely, "But danger also comes with opportunity. The Great Xia willing to invest so many resources in Li’er, values Jinyang Village’s position as well. People like Cai Qiu, though strong, are hot-tempered and stingy, not treating us as people. Rather than working for them, it would be better to serve the Great Xia!

The Feudal Towns may be powerful, but not so much that the Great Xia cannot resist. The fact that they sent two talented young people like Li Hu and Yang Zhong shows the Great Xia’s importance on my Jinyang. If I have to choose a side, it’ll be the Great Xia!"

After making his decision, Han Peng exhaled a long breath, finally easing his mind, softly saying, "That Li Hu and Yang Zhong are just scouts. However, the young man named Hong Yu who came with Li’er should be a high-ranking figure in the Great Xia. I don’t know how long he plans to stay in Jinyang Village, better find him soon to express my loyalty!"

.........

Xia Hong, under the alias Hong Yu, settled down in Jinyang Village.

On the third night, Han Li returned to East Ridge alone, so only Li Hu and Yang Zhong knew his identity in the entire Jinyang Village.

Li Hu’s Zhaoyuan Squad was already an Advanced Hunting Team with a full capacity of 100 people. He only brought 10 people to operate in Jinyang Village, while the remaining 88 people were dispersed among other village-level camps in Yinhe Valley.
