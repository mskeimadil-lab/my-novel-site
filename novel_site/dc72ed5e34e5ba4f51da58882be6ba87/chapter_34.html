Chapter 34: Chapter 34 Golden-eyed Demon Sheep

"Keep pulling back!"

The seven of Xia Chuan pulled the Cold Beast away, allowing Xia Hong to free himself from the wall. However, he didn’t turn to flee; instead, he gestured for Xia Chuan and the others to keep pulling. Then, with the second stone axe he had just received, he bent forward, straightened his waist, and executed a fierce, upward diagonal chop.

The head of this Cold Beast had six horns that were incredibly large and hard, making it impossible to cut through.

The layer of white mane covering its body, each strand half a meter long, almost coated its entire frame. During the brief contact moments ago, Xia Hong had already sensed the hardness of those manes.

Undoubtedly, this Cold Beast’s defense was even more terrifying than that of Snow Mane.

Attempting to breach its body under normal circumstances was out of the question.

Its lower jaw seemed to be the only weak point Xia Hong could think of.

"My strength is already nine thousand seven hundred pounds. Combined with the Taizhan Boxing technique and a weapon, I can exert more than ten thousand pounds of force. I refuse to believe your bones are this hard!"

The stone axe Xia Hong swung upward carried a fierce gust, rubbing swiftly against the cold air seeping in from outside, even sparking slightly in mid-air.

The power of this axe chop was beyond imagination.

Feeling threatened, the sheep-shaped Cold Beast’s struggles became increasingly frenzied, causing the seven holding the ropes to sway left and right.

Xu Ning, who was the last of the seven to break through, was even pulled to the ground, yet he clung to the rope with gritted teeth, not letting go. His legs braced against a raised stone on the ground, were scraped continuously until they bled profusely.

Bang......

Desperately holding onto the rope, the seven finally bought Xia Hong the opportunity for this axe swing. The stone axe hit the lower jaw, producing a huge clashing sound of stone against bone, as if the air itself vibrated.

However, Xia Hong’s face changed dramatically after the swing.

The stone axe failed to break through the beast’s defense; instead, it was shattered by its jawbone, just like the axe that had struck its horn earlier.

This vile creature’s jaw was as hard as its horns.

Roar......

Though the stone axe was shattered, the Cold Beast was evidently pierced with intense pain by this blow. It howled in fury, unleashing power far surpassing before, and almost instantly, broke all the ropes, sending the seven nearby tumbling head over heels.

But it didn’t stop there; after breaking free from the ropes, its first target wasn’t Xia Hong who had just chopped it with the stone axe, but Xu Ning instead.

Among the seven who were pulling, Xu Ning was at the head position, pulled directly to the side of the Cold Beast when the ropes snapped, with the nearest proximity.

"Xu Ning, get out of the way!"

Xia Hong rushed to the front of the Cold Beast, standing in front of Xu Ning.

This time, not opposing it was not an option.

Gritting his teeth, Xia Hong braced his legs on the ground, seizing the moment to catch the charging horn of the Cold Beast.

Xia Hong’s intent was to withstand the Cold Beast’s impact. Even if he couldn’t, he’d at least save Xu Ning; for himself, retreating or getting slightly injured was the worst that could happen.

However, his thinking was too simplistic.

After the Cold Beast’s enraged impact, it was on an entirely different level than before.

Bam...

This time, the Cold Beast not only sent him flying, hitting the cave’s top wall but almost severed all his ten fingers at the roots.

"Don’t touch its horns; you’ll get cut!"

Xia Hong, falling down from the mountain-top wall, looked at the cuts on his hands. Without time to tend to the blood streaming from his hands, he casually wiped them off and shouted a warning immediately to everyone.

Unfortunately, his warning came too late. Right after knocking him away, the Cold Beast rushed towards the crowd.

Perhaps the posture of tugging at the rope was too fierce, making the Cold Beast remember it, and its target still remained Xu Ning.

Xu Ning tried to retreat behind the Cold Beast, but he was too slow. He hadn’t taken more than two steps before the Cold Beast’s sharp horn had already pressed against his waist.

If pierced, it was certain death.

Standing now, Xia Hong dashed quickly towards the Cold Beast, but he was too far away to help in any meaningful way. The other six also attempted to stop the Cold Beast, but their strength was too weak, bouncing off before even getting close.

His fate seemed already sealed.

Detecting the perilous signal from his waist, cold sweat dripped down Xu Ning’s forehead. Glancing sideways at the half-meter-long manes on the Cold Beast, he gritted his teeth, sidestepped, and leaped, grabbing the mane. Ascending into the air, he astonishingly vaulted, seating himself on the beast’s back.

Thud...

Of course, it wasn’t smooth. Though avoiding impalement on his waist, his shoulder was stabbed by the Cold Beast’s horn. If not for the intense survival instinct prompting him to draw his shoulder from the horn, a casual flick from the Cold Beast could have torn his body in two.

The stab through his shoulder seemed to ignite a brutal streak in Xu Ning. Sitting on the Cold Beast’s back, he seized a stone axe and hacked fiercely at its back.

Indeed, as Xia Hong had surmised, the Cold Beast’s defense was far more fearsome than Snow Mane. Xu Ning chopped dozens of times, only to sever some of the manes, leaving the skin underneath unscathed.

Yet even so, Xu Ning did not halt the axe swings. Blood from the wound on his shoulder stained half his body, driven by sheer toughness, fearing once he stopped, he might be immobile.

Roar......

Suddenly, for reasons unknown, the Cold Beast let out a heartrending scream.

Seated on its back, Xu Ning was taken aback. The Cold Beast had little reaction to his relentless chops; why this sudden wail now?

Xu Ning reacted quickly, halting his hand and raising the axe to examine the spot he had just been hacking.

Although much of the Cold Beast’s back hair had been chopped off, the manes were still long and covered a large area. Xu Ning used the axe to part the area he had been chopping, and his expression froze.

Underneath the mane, concealed remarkably, was a palm-sized eye, with a pure golden pupil in the center, staring at him unblinkingly, devoid of emotion.

"This is the weakness!"

Xu Ning did not spend much time examining the eye; noticing the axe mark beside it, he immediately realized this eye was the Cold Beast’s weak point.

"There’s a golden eye on its back; it should be the weakness!"

Xu Ning shouted to alert everyone, then with a grim expression, raised the stone axe toward the golden eye and swung down fiercely.

"Don’t act recklessly, Xu Ning!"

Entangled with the Cold Beast, Xia Hong, perceiving what Xu Ning intended to do, immediately yelled a warning, but unfortunately, it was too late.

Snow Mane’s weakness was the long tongue in its mouth, but its most dangerous part was also that tongue; Frost Wolf’s weakness was its small size, yet because of its size, its speed was incredibly fast.

No creature evolves a weakness without reason.

A weakness often signifies greater danger.

Thinking he was about to succeed, Xu Ning’s pupils dilated, axe almost about to strike the fist-sized golden pupil.

But at this moment, the golden pupil suddenly emitted a dazzling light, blinding Xu Ning’s eyes.

Yet, Xu Ning was reluctant to let this opportunity slip away, closed his left eye, used his right eye and the axe to continue aiming at the pupil without stopping.

Thud......
