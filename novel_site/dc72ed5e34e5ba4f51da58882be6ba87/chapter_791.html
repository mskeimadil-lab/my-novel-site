Chapter 791: Chapter 315: New Policies for Long Mountain, Cold Apple Tree and the Silver Mine

"Regarding the issue of the nobility system, there is still a long time for you to consider it. Now let’s talk about the eight new deployment points in Longyou?"

Xia Hong directly interrupted Xia Chuan. Seeing that everyone was looking over, he continued:

"Longyou has a total area of 13,000 square kilometers. Currently, except for the Yanglu Realm, the rest of the area is considered part of our Great Xia. According to the usual practice, each of the eight deployment points should have a Defense Envoy in charge. However, the situation at the Long Mountain deployment point is clearly somewhat special. What do you think should be done?"

Everyone lowered their heads in thought for a moment, and soon understood Xia Hong’s meaning.

The Long Mountain deployment point is not only large enough to accommodate tens of thousands of people, but also, because of the presence of the Iron Stake Wall, its city defenses are extremely strong. It also backs onto Long Mountain, the largest resource hub, with astonishing amounts of coal and iron mines and being the most important Spiritual Medicine production area in all of Longyou. From any angle, its importance far exceeds all other deployment points in Longyou.

The key is, such an important strategic deployment point is still on the very edge of Great Xia’s territory.

Xia City is the main base and certainly cannot be moved lightly.

But a vast place like Long Mountain also cannot be relocated.

Thus, some thought must be given to the Long Mountain deployment point.

Simply appointing a Defense Envoy like at other deployment points is definitely not sufficient.

"All the entrances to the main ridge of Long Mountain are here. In the future, a large number of people from the camp will come to cut down trees and hunt. This place will inevitably become an important resource transit hub for our Great Xia. For such an important position, I propose to send Deputy Director Shi Ping from the Garrison Department to garrison here, and also establish subordinate institutions of the Six Ministries here, so as to cover all the people at the seven deployment points in Xiling, White Abyss, Zhaoyang, Changning, Song Yuan, Wushuang, and Yucheng."

Xia Chuan reacted quickly and immediately offered a solution.

Upon hearing this, everyone showed approval on their faces.

However, Xia Hong did not immediately nod in agreement but first raised an eyebrow and asked, "Has Shi Ping reached the Cold Resistance Level?"

Although Shi Ping is the Deputy Director of the Garrison Department, Xia Hong remembered very clearly that when he set off from Xia City at the end of last month, Shi Ping had only reached the Earth-digging Realm Limit cultivation.

At this moment, Xia Chuan confidently recommended Shi Ping, which should not be surprising if Shi Ping had broken through.

Sure enough, Xia Chuan nodded and smiled, "Shi Ping has successfully broken through during the recent Hive Guard battle."

"Not bad!"

Xia Hong first expressed approval of Shi Ping but still did not agree with Xia Chuan’s proposal.

Sending a heavyweight to garrison and establishing subordinate institutions of the Six Ministries to strengthen ties with Xia City is certainly the right idea. The problem is...

"Your proposal is good, but I have a question. If I appoint Shi Ping, the Deputy Director, to preside over Long Mountain and set up subordinate institutions of the Six Ministries, then who will have the final say on the operations of the entire Long Mountain?"

"Of course, Deputy Director Shi..."

Xia Chuan reflexively answered, but before he could finish saying the word "Ping," he already keenly perceived the deeper meaning of Xia Hong’s question. His face slightly tightened, and he lowered his head in thought.

The original intention of setting up deployment points was for Great Xia to govern a region, or to put it more broadly, for Xia City to continuously extract local resources.

Who has the final say?

Of course, it is Xia Hong, the leader, or rather, Xia City has the final say.

However, according to my recent proposal, Long Mountain has both people and land, as well as strong city defenses, plus fully equipped institutions of the Six Ministries, which almost have all the key elements to form an independent camp. If the power is then handed over to one person, the consequences would be unimaginable if this person harbors malicious intent.

Of course, Xia Chuan trusts Shi Ping, but this is about system design, unrelated to Shi Ping personally. In the future, as Great Xia’s territory receives more key deployment points like Long Mountain, we cannot hope that everyone sent to guard them will be like Shi Ping.

So, the system is key!

Not only Xia Chuan, but all the Cold Resistance Level cultivators present clearly realized this as well.

Everyone was thinking about how to address Xia Hong’s concern.

"Leader, the Six Ministries already include the Garrison Department. Currently, the Garrison Department only has the position of defender. I propose to add a Defender position, specifically targeting key deployment points like Long Mountain. The Defender would nominally be the head of the deployment point but would be equal to the heads of the other five subordinate institutions. During peacetime, each ministry would manage their own affairs, and only during wartime would the Defender have authority over the other five ministries!"

Yue Feng spoke up, and immediately, Xia Chuan and the others, including Xia Hong above, their eyes lit up.

"The Garrison Department’s responsibility is the defense of deployment points. In other words, the Defender would nominally be the head of the deployment point, but during peacetime, they could only manage the defense of the deployment point and would be on the same level as the other five ministries, each reporting to their superiors, namely the Six Ministries of Xia City. Only during wartime would they have overarching authority. Very good, very good, Yue Feng’s proposal is excellent!"

Xia Chuan instantly grasped the cleverness of Yue Feng’s suggestion.

The Defender being equal to the chiefs of the other five ministries, and each only needing to report to their respective superiors, means that the chiefs of the six ministries in Long Mountain would have a form of mutual supervision, effectively sharing the power within the deployment point, inevitably forming checks and balances among them.

"Indeed excellent, Yue Feng, you have been the Garrison of Mirror Valley for quite a while, right?"

Yue Feng was already delighted when his proposal was accepted. When Xia Hong suddenly mentioned his current position, he was momentarily confused, then immediately realized something, and his expression instantly turned excited.

"Reporting to the leader, subordinates have served as the garrison of Mirror Valley since March of the first year, and it has been one year and four months so far."
