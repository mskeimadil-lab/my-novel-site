Chapter 299: Chapter 164: The World in the Mirror, Immortal_2

"That Xia Hong’s strength is actually so formidable, it’s actually so formidable, how is it possible, he used so much Beast Blood and didn’t die, didn’t die!"

Not sure if he was afraid of people outside hearing, He Yuanhun was full of unwillingness, yet he still tried hard to suppress his roar.

His two silvery pupils, like two mirrors, slowly revealed a person after he let out a low growl.

That person was actually He Yuanhun.

However, the He Yuanhun in the pupils had a gentle face and appeared much calmer than the one outside.

After appearing, he chuckled lightly and slowly spoke:

"You deliberately used my mouth to tell him about the Combat Body, luring him to reshape his skin with a large amount of Beast Blood in an instant, hoping to kill him, even willing to expose your own matters. Do you think I don’t know?"

Oddly, once the He Yuanhun in the pupils spoke, the He Yuanhun outside moved his lips as well.

Yet, the voices of the two were completely different.

The former was deep, resentful, and sinister, full of evil;

The latter was gentle like jade, speaking leisurely, with a hint of calculated shrewdness, as if everything was under his control.

"Heh heh heh heh heh, isn’t this something we tacitly understand? I want to kill him, you want to use him against me, otherwise, why would I let you gain control of the body so smoothly."

"Now it seems, I’ve won, he’s broken through to the Cold Resistance Level, and directly gained the Six-maned Power, his raw strength is already much stronger than yours, plus that black oily substance, you won’t be able to fight him, Mirror Immortal!"

"Shut up, heh heh heh, who wins or loses, it’s not decided yet!"

"Stop dreaming, that Xia Hong obviously let you return on purpose to kill you in front of the whole camp, to subdue the rest of the camp, do you really think it was He Meng and the others who got you the chance to escape?"

"Heh heh heh heh heh..."

Hearing He Yuanhun’s words, Mirror Immortal laughed a few times mockingly, his face still in pain, but his low voice carried bits of arrogance and pride:

"You think I don’t know, you humans have always been scheming, even at this point, you’re still thinking about annexing the camp, he let me go on purpose, and wasn’t I returning to the camp on purpose too?"

He Yuanhun’s gentle voice finally turned low at this moment:

"We had a contract before, you can’t force others to become your believers, you ghastly things, violating the contract has serious consequences, doesn’t it?"

"Heh heh heh heh heh, He Yuanhun, you don’t even know, the allure of immortality to you humans is immense, just watch, just watch..."

Mirror Immortal let out a twisted laugh, suddenly raising his hand sharply towards a corner of the room, a person was instantly drawn into his hand.

It was none other than the Second Leader of the Mirror Immortal Camp, Yuwen Hu!

However, at this moment, Yuwen Hu’s whole body was covered with cracks, his eyes were dull and lifeless, and he didn’t respond at all when strangled by the Mirror Immortal, like a puppet without a soul.

The moment Yuwen Hu appeared, the He Yuanhun in the pupils of Mirror Immortal’s eyes immediately looked down, a trace of guilt appearing on his face.

"What’s wrong, are you feeling embarrassed?

He Yuanhun, all these years, to control me, although you’ve handed the body over to me, you’ve actually kept part of the control, some things seem like it’s my doing, but in essence, you’re involved too.

Your sworn brother no longer trusts you, I helped you by killing him, you should be grateful to me!"

The Mirror Immortal’s eyes suddenly shone brightly, illuminating Yuwen Hu’s whole body.

The cracks on Yuwen Hu’s body began to heal rapidly, moreover, the dullness in his eyes quickly disappeared, returning to normal.

"Big brother, are you okay?"

The restored Yuwen Hu saw the seriously injured He Yuanhun in front of him, immediately showed a worried look, and stepped forward to support him.

Apparently, he no longer remembered being killed by He Yuanhun with his own hands back at Redwood Ridge.

"Second brother, the Great Xia’s strength is too strong, they will attack soon, I can’t stop that Xia Hong, the people in the camp must..."

The moment Yuwen Hu was restored, the Mirror Immortal’s expression returned to normal, and then he spoke slowly, explaining one thing to Yuwen Hu.

A matter that, to any ordinary person, would sound bizarre and terrifying.

Yet, Yuwen Hu showed no abnormal expression, even nodding continuously in agreement.

...

"Everyone, quiet down!"

Outside the bamboo building, all the people from Mirror Immortal Camp were waiting anxiously when a voice suddenly sounded from the upper floor, and everyone immediately turned to look up.

Seeing it was the Second Leader Yuwen Hu, all the panic on everyone’s faces immediately subsided a lot, and they became quiet.

Amid the crowd, only Yuwen Dao showed a continuously changing expression, from initial joy gradually turning to horror, even ending with a hint of sorrow.

Not only Yuwen Dao, but Hong Guang and four others of the same age as Yuwen Hu, as well as forty-one Earth-digging Realm members from Yuanzhou Camp, seemed to realize something, their bodies suddenly shuddering.

"Dao’er, the Second Leader he..."

"Don’t speak yet, let’s see what he has to say."

Yuwen Dao took a deep breath, suppressed the turmoil in his heart, and looked up at Yuwen Hu on the third floor, with a glimmer of hope still flickering in his eyes.

"Tonight’s action outcome, everyone must have guessed it..."

Yuwen Hu continued with a trace of sorrow on his face: "It was a defeat. He Meng, He Gang, and two hundred seventy-one Earth-digging Realm were all killed by the Great Xia, even the Leader was severely injured by Xia Hong and is now in grave danger!"
