Chapter 1073 - 376: Primordial Soul Pill and Rebirth Mantra, Red Blood Demon Dragon, Sudden Upheaval 4

Chapter 1073: Chapter 376: Primordial Soul Pill and Rebirth Mantra, Red Blood Demon Dragon, Sudden Upheaval 4

The temperature of the fire pillar was exceptionally intense. It engulfed the old man instantly, and like the alternation of heat and cold, it emitted black smoke, suddenly transforming into a massive black stone, floating mid-air.

Whoosh......

The white-robed middle-aged man and the old man were successively submerged by molten lava. Another middle-aged man, dressed in black and holding a silver longsword, suddenly charged from the sky towards the face of the demon dragon. His longsword was as swift as a meteor, trailing a hundred-zhang-long silver sword edge in the air, slashing ferociously towards the demon dragon’s left eye.

As the sword edge flew out, a thousand-zhang-tall golden bone shadow also materialized behind the black-robed middle-aged man, holding a longsword like him, thrusting synchronously, targeting the demon dragon’s left eye.

"Yang Zhun, Shangguan Yang, stop pretending. If we don’t join forces to trap the demon dragon, we’re all dead!"

Before the black-robed middle-aged man’s sword edge reached its target, he realized it was an excellent opportunity. Seeing the demon dragon about to maneuver its tail to dodge, he immediately roared at the two black stones suspended in mid-air.

Boom... Boom...

The two boulders exploded suddenly, and the white-robed middle-aged man and the green-robed old man flew out simultaneously from inside. Although their clothes were scorched by molten lava and they looked a bit disheveled, there wasn’t much panic on their faces.

Bone shadows appeared behind both men, each towering a thousand zhang high.

The bone shadow of the white-robed middle-aged man, Yang Zhun, was golden.

The bone shadow of the green-robed old man, Shangguan Yang, was silver.

"Everyone, join forces to trap the demon dragon!"

Yang Zhun shouted to the crowd, and his long spear flew out abruptly, leaving a silver streak in the sky. The bone shadow synchronized, thrusting the thousand-zhang-long spear directly into the demon dragon’s left abdomen.

Although Shangguan Yang didn’t utter a word, his movements were swift. With twin green blades held by his bone shadow, he slashed down fiercely from the right side, cutting into the demon dragon’s right back.

Meanwhile, twelve figures launched simultaneous attacks from all directions. The bone shadows behind them were much smaller than those of the three, the smallest over three hundred zhang, the largest over eight hundred zhang.

The twelve joined the assault from various angles, attacking almost all parts of the demon dragon’s body.

Boom... Boom... Boom......

"Roar..."

Attacked simultaneously by fourteen people, the demon dragon seemed to be in pain, and its massive body struggled and tumbled in the air.

However, after just a few rolls, it stopped.

Because among the fourteen, none withdrew their weapons after striking the demon dragon. Everyone unleashed their strongest power, tightly suppressing the demon dragon, rendering it immobile.

Whoosh......

Meanwhile, the sword edge finally arrived.

"Demon Dragon, meet your end!"

At the front end of the several-hundred-meter-long sword edge, Qin Feng held his longsword, staring at the demon dragon’s perpetually rippleless emerald pupils, his face full of ferocity.

This demon dragon’s strength was indeed terrifying, but at this moment, its body was suppressed by the joint force of fourteen Xianyang Level fighters, unable to move, facing inevitable doom.

Roar......

Yet, just as the sword edge was less than half an inch from the demon dragon’s left eye, the demon dragon suddenly raised its head and let out a furious roar.

Qin Feng’s expression instantly froze.

He held the longsword, maintaining his advancing posture, and even the bone shadow behind him remained undissipated, consistent with his body’s integrity. However, neither his body nor the bone shadow could advance any further.

"Qin Feng, what are you dawdling for?"

"Such a hard-earned opportunity!"

"Go on, now!"

......

Seeing Qin Feng standing still before the demon dragon’s pupils, Yang Zhun, Shangguan Yang, and other Xianyang Level fighters shouted in fury, urging him to strike swiftly.

Gulp......

Without answering, Qin Feng just swallowed hard.

It wasn’t that he didn’t want to advance; he simply couldn’t.

The demon dragon’s recent roar was distinctly different from the previous ones, immobilizing him completely.

Moreover, the demon dragon’s perpetually rippleless emerald pupils changed after the roar.

Its left pupil suddenly became cloudy, faint gold emanating from within, gradually spreading until it turned entirely gold.

The enormous golden eye blinked, but inside lay a hint of confusion and perplexity. It first examined Qin Feng before turning to the fourteen Xianyang Level fighters behind it, seemingly pondering something.

Soon, as if realizing something, the demon dragon’s left eye widened in rage, with its body flaring up, violently shaking off Qin Feng and the fifteen Xianyang Level fighters.

The next moment, the demon dragon’s actions floored the fifteen Xianyang Level fighters, including Qin Feng.

"How dare you manipulate my body!"

The demon dragon actually opened its mouth and spoke human words.

The demon dragon’s voice was incredibly loud, rife with domination and strength. It immediately turned to gaze at the southeast direction, its left eye ablaze with fury.

"What do you mean? This demon dragon is being controlled?"

"I sensed it earlier. Its perpetually unemotional emerald pupils were likely under the control of Dajue Temple."

"How did it awaken again?"

"Probably that thrust from Qin Feng’s sword threatened the demon dragon, so it forcibly rid itself of the master’s control and awakened."

.........

"I’m just borrowing your dragon body, why so angry?"

Leaving aside the discussions of Yang Zhun and others, the demon dragon continued speaking after its previous declaration. This time, its voice was old and peaceful, carrying a hint of serenity, unlike the previous domineering tone.

Within the Red Blood Demon Dragon, two consciousnesses were clearly present. Once the old voice faded, the demon dragon’s original consciousness quickly regained control, angrily saying, "You little brat from Lingyin Mountain, even when your master Five Treasures Tathagata sees me, he has to bow. You dare to covet my dragon body, get out of my body now!"

"The Dragon Shedding Period lasts at least a century. You have decades left to slumber, don’t fool me, just give up your dragon body willingly!"

"How dare you threaten me, roar......"

The Red Blood Demon Dragon seemed genuinely angered by the old man, with intense flames suddenly rising in its left eye. It roared at the sky, with already flickering thunder now wrapped in black flames, appearing more terrifying than before.

Nevertheless, the demon dragon’s rage couldn’t alter the fact that the golden glow in its left eye was gradually retreating, with the emerald glow inside re-surfacing. Moreover, as it continued to roar, the emerald spread grew larger.

This was about to be controlled again!

The expressions of Yang Zhun and the fifteen other fighters became considerably solemn.

"Ah......"

As the demon dragon’s left eye was nearly reverting to entirely emerald.

Something unknown occurred, causing it to emit a fierce scream.

It was the old man’s voice!

The expressions of the fifteen jerked tight, hearing the pain and disbelief in the old man’s voice, their faces immediately filled with trepidation and uncertainty.
