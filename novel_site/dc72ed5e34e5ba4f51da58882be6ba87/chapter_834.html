Chapter 834: Chapter 326: Divide and Annihilate, Shattering Divine Light, Trump Card Activated! (Part 2)

Xia Chuan controlled the Sword Puppet, slashing open a Demon Sheep beside him, then sternly commanded the crowd, signaling them to divert several Cold Beasts charging at Xia Hong.

Rumble... Rumble...

This Snow Hill was merely about a mile in circumference, with the highest point only twenty meters. When over a hundred Advanced Cold Beasts charged over, it took just a moment to flatten the Snow Hill completely.

Moreover, with those of Great Xia’s Cold Resistance Level dispersed evenly into seven squads engaging in battle with them, the violent power from both sides continued to pour onto the ground through weapons and limbs, causing the originally flattened Snow Hill to begin sinking slowly, gradually forming a depression.

"This spot was a wasted choice, the power is too great, the terrain’s changed!"

Xia Hong dodged a golden light shot from the surface of a Demon Sheep, plunged the Cold Blood Blade violently into its back, and turned to see the Snow Hill already sunken by three or four meters, a grave look flashed in his eyes.

The Snow Hill he had carefully chosen evidently wasn’t going to serve much purpose.

"Lord, no way! There are too many Cold Beasts, there are more coming, it seems all the Cold Beasts around Yangyuan Peak are here, we should extinguish the Cold Ruins Tripod, or we can’t hold them off!" ree

Peng Bo’s loud shout from the flank attracted Xia Hong’s attention, he turned to look at the dense fog area to the south and indeed saw Cold Beasts continuously emerging from it. He took a deep breath, spoke in a low voice:

"Don’t panic, they can’t reach the Cold Ruins Tripod."

After saying this, he immediately drew the Cold Blood Blade, slashing open the Striped Tiger rushing at him from the flank.

Roar...

The Striped Tiger, having waited a long time for this moment, was immediately sliced open by Xia Hong, used its tail to balance its form, roared angrily, turned and leaped high, once again pouncing at Xia Hong.

Moreover, the black spots on its body instantly began to pulse, the muscles knotted inside swelled significantly, and the entire tiger’s body expanded by three or four tenths.

"Trying to compete with me in strength, you must be mad!"

Striped Tigers are a unique species of Long Mountain, Xia Hong, having spent ample time in Longyou, was naturally familiar with them. The layer of black spots on the Striped Tiger could boost its power by fifty percent, generally only used in times of desperation.

This Striped Tiger’s newfound impact strength was roughly equivalent to eighteen bristles, and after activating its black spots, its strength could increase to about twenty-seven bristles, which would spell certain defeat for other Cold Resistance levels.

But it encountered me.

"It’s useless, even joining forces won’t help!"

Xia Hong glanced sidelong and quickly noticed to his left another Thunder Kui charging at him with its horn on its forehead flickering with electricity, clearly having stored power for a long time nearby, planning to coordinate with the Striped Tiger to attack him.

Bang!

Xia Hong suddenly stomped the ground, flash-like, drawing the Dragon Sparrow Saber from his waist with his left hand, first intercepting the horn of the Thunder Kui to his left.

The Thunder Kui’s nearly thirty-meter-long body was just held down by Xia Hong with one hand, and the arcs of electricity flickering from the horn on its forehead suddenly exploded, splattering starry particles all across the sky.

Roar...

The Thunder Kui maintained its forward charging position, roaring as it increased its force, but no matter how hard it tried, it couldn’t move the Dragon Sparrow Saber an inch.

Meanwhile, the Striped Tiger’s Blood Basin mouth arrived.

Bang!

Xia Hong didn’t even look at the Striped Tiger directly, just raised a leg and kicked it viciously against its jaw, then raised the Cold Blood Blade high with his right hand, stabbing directly into the back of the Golden Armored Thunder Kui to his left.

Spurt!

Roar... Roar...

The Golden Armored Thunder Kui had planned to dodge initially, but it had been in a perpetual forward dive position all along, and to wrestle with the Dragon Sparrow Saber, it had exerted nearly all its strength. Attempting to suddenly rein in its power to evade was naturally not fast enough.

As the Cold Blood Blade plunged into its nape, the Thunder Kui’s blood instantly started to boil. It could distinctly sense the abnormality in its body, with horror and shock filling its pupils, wildly shaking its head trying to rid itself of the Cold Blood Blade.

But Xia Hong certainly wasn’t going to give it the opportunity.

He gripped the Cold Blood Blade tightly, and under the mighty force, the Thunder Kui’s head shaking barely spanned even half a meter, being forcibly drained of its blood by the Cold Blood Blade until its pupils completely lost color and it collapsed heavily.

Bang...

"The ninth one, no, still a bit slow, need to speed it up!"

Xia Hong frowned deeply, turning to see the number of Cold Beasts around him still increasing, a glint of light flashing in his left eye as he once again charged at the Striped Tiger he had just kicked away.

The Striped Tiger, after being kicked away, clearly realized it couldn’t possibly defeat the person before it, so in a straightforwardly honest manner, it turned to flee toward the top of the Snow Hill.

However, it hadn’t even run a few steps.

Swoosh...

A fierce white light suddenly shot from Xia Hong’s left eye, striking directly upon the back of the Striped Tiger.

Crack...

The Striped Tiger, hit by the white light, stiffened violently, then stood uncontrollably still, with an intense heat emanating from its body surface. Its body began to slowly crack.

Fear and horror filled the Striped Tiger’s pupil, it frantically twisted its head trying to roar but its throat felt mute, unable to make any sound.

Not only could it not make a sound, it couldn’t even move its body.

Spurt!

Xia Hong chased from behind, leapt high, and beheaded it from mid-air.

Bang...

The massive tiger’s head thudded to the ground with a loud crash, and then it, together with its body, returned to normal size.
