Regarding cultivation, it doesn’t allow him any carelessness.

"So, I need to find an opportunity to go out and learn about the cultivation situation after the Cold Resistance Level from others!"

Upon hearing the last sentence, Xia Chuan naturally understood.

In the canyon camp here, there must be someone at the Cold Resistance Level holding the fort, and if he and Yuwen Dao don’t break through, Xia Hong definitely can’t go out at ease.

Although worried about Xia Hong going out, Xia Chuan also knew how important cultivation matters were, and after pondering for a moment, he said in a deep voice: "Big brother, rest assured, I will definitely break through as soon as possible."

Xia Hong shook his head slightly, waving his hand, he said: "No rush, since you have the qualification for a Superior Battle Body, just follow the steps one at a time. Once the foundation is solid, you will be stronger in the future, and be able to help me better."

Not everyone is like me with a system.

An amount of ten catties at a time is already astounding.

Seeing the persistent expression on Xia Chuan’s face, knowing he hadn’t really listened to his words, Xia Hong smiled and said: "Your dosage is almost catching up to Yuwen Dao’s, and he has already reached the Earth-digging Realm Limit, now at most, he uses twelve catties at a time."

Hearing that the gap between himself and Yuwen Dao was only two catties, Xia Chuan’s expression suddenly became a bit more excited, as Yuwen Dao had already reached the Earth-digging Realm Limit before coming to Great Xia and started using Beast Blood.

By this calculation, his improvement was undoubtedly slightly larger than Yuwen Dao’s.

"By the way, big brother, is Yuwen Dao also a Superior Battle Body?"

Xia Hong turned his head at the words, seeing the curiosity on Xia Chuan’s face, and chuckled lightly.

He was aware of the silent contest between these two.

Yuwen Dao was younger, and his cultivation was above Xia Chuan’s, from this point, his talent should be stronger, but Xia Hong didn’t think so, with the three inheritances of the Wood Puppet Monster, Xia Chuan’s future potential was not low either.

"With a forty percent progress, his Basic Strength is already fifty-nine thousand, it should be a Superior Battle Body without any surprise."

Hearing these words, Xia Chuan’s expression was both joyful and heavy, being rather complicated.

Joyful, because he thought Yuwen Dao’s strength would be more beneficial to the Great Xia Camp; and heavy, because in terms of qualifications, there was still some gap between himself and Yuwen Dao.

This was why his reaction was so complicated!

Xia Hong could naturally see it, he smiled slightly, and didn’t say anything more.

Competition is a good thing.

"By the way, how much beast blood reserve does the camp have and how long will it last?"

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan’s expression immediately turned serious, and he said: "There are 1300 catties, and currently, there are already more than a dozen people using beast blood. As long as people are in the camp, they basically change every day, with a daily consumption of about 100 catties. The beast blood reserve is not ample, it’s a bit tight."

Xia Chuan, Yuwen Dao, Hong Guang, Hong Tian, Zhu Ling, Zhu Yuan, Meng Yi, Luo Yuan, Yuan Cheng, Zhao Long, Zhao Hu, Zhao Bao, Huang Yong...

Currently, those who have reached the Earth-digging Realm Limit that Xia Hong is aware of, number as many as thirteen people, and surely there are some others he doesn’t know about.

Moreover, the number of people breaking through subsequently will continue to increase.

The daily consumption of a hundred catties is sure to climb steadily higher.

catties won’t last even half a month!

Xia Hong shook his head slightly, just about to speak, he suddenly noticed that the token hanging at Xia Chuan’s waist was different from before, and his eyes lit up immediately as he asked: "Has your Hunting Team already been promoted to Intermediate?"

Xia Chuan grinned with a laugh, nodding: "Three days ago, I led the team and hunted a Golden Armored Thunder Kui, now the five kinds of Intermediate Cold Beasts are all in place. Just today, I went to Luo Yuan for promotion, and big brother, you just came back."

This could be considered an unexpected surprise!

There was a bit more joy in Xia Hong’s eyes.

He knew that Xia Chuan had been leading the team in hunting Intermediate Cold Beasts, but being able to hunt five kinds in such a short period was a bit of a surprise to him.

Xia Hong suddenly thought of something and said in realization:

"I see, I was wondering why Yuwen Dao was in such a hurry to go north to find Intermediate Cold Beasts these days in Jing Valley, it’s because your two teams were recently competing to advance to Intermediate, right?"

The Beast Tide in the Arrow Bamboo Forest came from north of Redwood Ridge, the canyon side could still hunt on the western outskirts, and Jing Valley is just north of Redwood Ridge, naturally, it’s not accessible during the Beast Tide.

In the previous five days trapped in Jing Valley, Yuwen Dao often led the Dragon Wu Team to the Pingxi Plain area ten miles north, to find Intermediate Cold Beasts, and Xia Hong didn’t understand those days, but now he understood the reason.

"Heh, it’s also luck, if Yuwen Dao hadn’t been trapped in Jing Valley for so many days, the Dragon Wu Team should have been promoted ahead of me, but now, they can only take second place!"

Hearing the smugness in Xia Chuan’s tone, Xia Hong couldn’t help but smile faintly.

Apparently, the competition between these two wasn’t just in terms of strength, it had even elevated to the speed of their respective Hunting Teams’ advancements.

"Don’t get too complacent, the day before yesterday, I saw they had already hunted an Intermediate Green Thunder Roar, when Yuwen Dao comes back this time, the Dragon Wu Team should also be able to advance to Intermediate."

Though he was cautioning Xia Chuan, realizing that Great Xia was about to have two Intermediate Hunting Teams, there was still uncontrollable joy in Xia Hong’s tone.

Successfully hunting five different kinds of Intermediate Cold Beasts, the standard for Great Xia Intermediate Hunting Team was actually set very high, a point that Yuwen Dao had made clear at the outset when drafting the promotion rules.

This is tantacity a solid foundation being laid.

Like the Cloud Jiao Team, having successfully hunted five types of Intermediate Cold Beasts, when they enter Redwood Ridge in the future, the range of targets they could choose to hunt will be so broad; even if they encounter unfamiliar Intermediate Cold Beasts, from their extensive experience of hunting five types, their success rate would surely be very high.
