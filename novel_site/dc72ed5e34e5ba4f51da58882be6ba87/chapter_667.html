Chapter 667: Chapter 278: Thousand-eyed Demon Sheep, Terrifying Power, Spiritual Medicine 3

The biggest problem with this unexpected disaster was obviously that Chen Peng and his companion chose the wrong target. They picked a Cold Beast whose strength was far superior to theirs; it’s no wonder they got beaten up so badly.

The issue is that both of these men are leaders of their respective groups. Given their age, their hunting experience should be quite extensive. Making such a serious mistake is indeed inexcusable, which is why Xia Hongcai asked this question.

Hearing the implication in Xia Hong’s words, the two men’s faces flushed red with embarrassment.

Finally, Chen Yingyuan spoke up to explain, "Leader, you might not know, but we usually enter Redwood Ridge from the north and typically go no deeper than ten kilometers. Normally, the advanced Demon Sheep encountered within this range are all at the Hundred Eyes Level. We never expected to come across a Thousand-eyed Demon Sheep just seven kilometers in..."

With this explanation, Xia Hong finally understood.

The Beehive and Yang Lu are both to the north of Redwood Ridge. To Chen Peng and his companion, the east side of Redwood Ridge was indeed completely unfamiliar. Coupled with the fact that Yuwen Dao and others were ignorant about advanced Cold Beasts, it’s understandable why such an oversight occurred.

"The hunting area for advanced Cold Beasts can only be slowly explored by you on both sides, eventually connecting the northern and eastern sides. This way, future hunting areas will be more expansive.

In addition, the hunting area information from Yang Lu, Beehive, and Wushuang’s families should be promptly consolidated to Great Xia to prevent such mistakes from happening again!"

Yuwen Dao immediately nodded and said, "Leader, rest assured, I’ve already handled this last month. The current exploration depth on the north side is 10 kilometers; on the east side, it’s only 5 kilometers. The Hunting Department’s current focus for exploration is on the northeast side. It should take just a few months to completely link the two areas."

Xia Hong then showed a satisfied expression and nodded.

At this moment, Peng Bo thought of something and quickly walked to the large tree where the Demon Sheep had been perched, dragging his injured body with him. After rummaging for a while, he let out a cry of surprise.

"Leader, come quickly!"

The Demon Sheep had already knocked down the large trees within a mile radius, and the tree it perched on was no exception.

Xia Hong led the group to the top of the fallen tree and looked in the direction Peng Bo pointed, immediately showing a shocked expression.

On some of the main branches of the big tree, there were many tightly clustered, spiral-shaped, dark green leaves. In the middle of some of the leaves were a few fist-sized, double-colored fruits, totaling more than ten.

The fruit was half blue and half red, with translucent flesh like liquid, quite ornamental.

"Double-colored Liquid Spirit Fruit and Panxiang Leaf!"

Xia Hong sniffed the leaves and fruit and immediately displayed great joy.

"Leader, you have a good eye. These are indeed the two essential spiritual medicines for brewing Jade Bone Medicine. These Panxiang Leaves are the symbiotic spirit plants of the Demon Sheep and are also their favorite food. Wherever the Demon Sheep perch, there’s a high probability of finding them. This Demon Sheep must have perched here for quite some time. There are many Panxiang Leaves; if we came earlier, there might have been more spirit fruits."

Essential spiritual medicines for brewing Jade Bone Medicine!

Xia Hong and Peng Bo got excited for different reasons.

Or rather, Xia Hong was excited about more than just this.

These two spiritual medicines can not only be used to refine Yangyuan Pills;

Crucially, they are also the main materials for making Blood Essence Pills.

Blood Essence Pills are auxiliary cultivation elixirs for the Earth-digging Realm Limit. From a certain perspective, they can accelerate the increase in Cold Resistance Level numbers at the camp.

Suddenly realizing something, Xia Hong’s eyes lit up. He looked at Chen Yingyuan and Peng Bo and asked, "Are there any other spirit plant varieties that are symbiotic with advanced Cold Beasts? You’ve hunted many advanced Cold Beasts before, tell me everything you know."

These are critical pieces of information that can be obtained without risking life and limb.

Chen Yingyuan and Peng Bo have been at the camp for so long, and he’s only now asking.

Xia Hong patted his forehead and secretly cursed his own foolishness.
