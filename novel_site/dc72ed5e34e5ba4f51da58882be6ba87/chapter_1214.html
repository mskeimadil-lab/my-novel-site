Of course, Xia Hong did not forget about the aftermath. He first instructed Xia Chuan and Qiu Peng, and after the two responded, he suddenly turned around and looked at the lords of the Six Towns who were about to leave, asking them with a playful tone, "Gentlemen, you don’t have any objections, do you?"

"Lord Xia Hong, rest assured."

"Once Great Xia’s decree arrives, Wuchuan will wholeheartedly offer its silver."

"Yangqu has no objections."

"Dragon Valley awaits Great Xia’s decree."

"Yang has no objections."

"Chui Mountain will wait for Great Xia’s decree."

At this moment, all the lords of the Six Towns only wanted to leave quickly. Who would dare have objections? After leaving a sentence, they quickly flew away.

"By the way, don’t forget, gentlemen, I just said that within ten days, all the bodies of the victims must be sent to Hongmen. This investigation will be led by Great Xia, and the Six Towns must send people to jointly participate!"

The lords of the Six Towns hadn’t flown far. Xia Hong made sure his words clearly reached their ears, and without looking at them anymore, he immediately withdrew the twelve Cold Beast Stakes in the sky. A trace of pain flashed in his eyes before he smiled and looked down at the more than thirty thousand soldiers around the Ministry Building.

"Why are you all looking at me like that?"

The moment Yang Zhun and the other eleven left, the over thirty thousand soldiers around Great Xia truly relaxed completely.

Once everyone relaxed, without exception, they all raised their heads to look at Xia Hong in the sky.

The over ten thousand Cold Resistance Level soldiers from South City, after all, came from different towns, and their feelings were mixed at the moment. Hence, their gazes toward Xia Hong were filled with various emotions—shock, reverence, admiration, respect, fear... almost every kind was present.

But the reaction of the more than thirty thousand soldiers from Xia City was different.

At this moment, they were extremely excited, looking at Xia Hong with faces full of reverence. The fervor in their eyes was almost overflowing.

It should be known that these more than thirty thousand people were gathered from various stations of Xia City and the territory of Great Xia. Before coming to Hongmen, they already knew their opponents were from Six Towns, so they were mentally prepared to face a fierce battle.

Who would have thought, except for some minor movements with the Divine Machine Crossbows by the soldiers of the Cloud Jiao and Long Wu, the others simply showed up throughout the event?

Defeating the strongest of the Nine Towns, Yang Zhun, cutting off Mu Longhe’s arms with the Bloodletting Jade Fox, summoning the twelve Beast Kings to intimidate the Six Towns, and making all lords obediently bow their heads...

In the previous two hours, what Xia Hong did was just secondary to the excitement it caused; the crucial point was that every single act paved the way for Great Xia to become the number one town in Moao.

Xia Hong’s strength was a mystery to the people of Great Xia.

Everyone knew he was strong, but no one could say exactly how strong, not even Li Xuanling, who was with him day and night.

At this moment, these more than thirty thousand people finally understood just how powerful their lord was!

Defeating Yang Zhun was nothing compared to this!

Having twelve Beast Kings by one’s side, everyone knew what this meant.

From now on, Great Xia should be able to dominate throughout the entire Moao South Slope.

No, what South Slope? Is there anyone in the entire Moao Mountain territory stronger than Xia Hong? Is there any camp more powerful than Great Xia?

These two questions arose in everyone’s mind.

Instinctively, they found the answers in their hearts, and then their gazes toward Xia Hong grew increasingly fervent, their faces slowly turning red.

"Long live the lord, long live Great Xia!"

Fervor is an emotion that easily causes one to lose rationality, especially given the bewildering display Xia Hong put on today.

So, someone finally couldn’t suppress the fervor in their heart, raised their weapon, and shouted out loud to Xia Hong in the sky.

That shout completely ignited the flame within everyone’s heart.

"Long live the lord, long live Great Xia!"

"Long live the lord, long live Great Xia!"

"Long live the lord, long live Great Xia!"

......

One loud and fervent cheer after another echoed continuously in Hongmen City. Even the slowly descending night couldn’t suppress the explosive fervor among the crowd at that moment. From Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao to some new recruits with Earth-digging Realm Limit Cultivation who had only joined the camp for one or two years, they all looked up to Xia Hong in the sky, as if gazing at a deity.

In their cheers, there was not just reverence and fervor for Xia Hong, but also a deep sense of pride in being a member of Great Xia.

This emotion quickly spread to the more than ten thousand Cold Resistance Level troops in South City and those of the Earth-digging Realm who gradually emerged from their houses with the coming of the night.

As they heard wave after wave of chanting, a hint of envy appeared on their faces, which soon turned into a firm resolve as if they understood something.

"To join Great Xia, I must find a way to join Great Xia!"

This thought emerged simultaneously in the minds of the vast majority of people.
