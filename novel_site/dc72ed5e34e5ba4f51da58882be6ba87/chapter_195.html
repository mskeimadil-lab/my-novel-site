Chapter 195: Chapter 136 He Yuanhun, Yuwen Hu, Yuwen Dao (Three in One)_3

Adding to the subtle expression on Yuwen Dao’s face, it was evident he was hinting that within the Mirror Immortal Camp, not everything was under He Yuanhun’s control.

Of course, the deeper implication was that if they killed him, his father, Yuwen Hu, would undoubtedly change his stance. He Meng’s proposal would be smoothly adopted by He Yuanhun, ultimately leading to a situation where the Great Xia and Mirror Immortal Camps would be locked in an endless feud.

Such a short statement, yet it cleverly blended softness and firmness.

Not only did it clearly outline the immense benefits of Xia Hong and the others refraining from action;

It also subtly threatened them with the consequences Great Xia would face if they chose to kill, leaving them without a hint of escape.

Is this Yuwen Dao really a young man?

Although Xia Hong’s face showed no emotion, his heart was quite shaken by Yuwen Dao’s mature and steady demeanor.

No matter if Yuwen Dao’s previous words were true or false, at least his final remarks were valid. It’s better to resolve animosities than to create them. In the future, the Great Xia will definitely be neighbors with the Mirror Immortal Camp, and forming too deep an enmity will be detrimental to both sides.

And killing the son of a Cold Resistance Level expert clearly leads to an irreconcilable situation, which is unnecessary.

Sneakily kill them without being discovered?

Xia Hong shook his head. This is the Ice Abyss, not the normal world. If someone dies on your territory, you cannot evade responsibility; evidence is not required.

At this point, Xia Hong no longer harbored the thought of killing Yuwen Dao and his group of ten. Even Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan, standing by his side, had considerably diminished their intent to kill.

At this moment, there was no choice but to hope that what Yuwen Dao said about Yuwen Hu and He Yuanhun, the two Cold Resistance Level experts, was true.

The Mirror Immortal Camp’s strength, whether or not it was as Yuwen Dao claimed, was surely stronger than Great Xia before Xia Hong’s breakthrough to the Cold Resistance Level. At least this point is undeniable.

Currently, Xia Hong dared to confront the Mirror Immortal Camp because his greatest support was that the other party was unaware of Great Xia’s camp location, only encountering them in the Redwood Ridge.

However, this is not foolproof.

If those in the Mirror Immortal Camp are truly determined, finding the valley is only a matter of time.

The current conflict between the two sides hasn’t escalated too much, so the Mirror Immortal Camp shouldn’t expend great effort to locate Great Xia’s camp.

But in the future?

The two neighboring camps will inevitably encounter each other, and judging by He Meng’s group’s stance, future conflicts will be unavoidable.

Xia Hong has been urgently trying to break through to the Cold Resistance Level during this period for this very reason.

Before more intense conflicts erupt with the Mirror Immortal Camp, if Xia Hong doesn’t break through to the Cold Resistance Level, the outcome for the Great Xia Camp cannot be good.

Tonight, he could surely kill Yuwen Dao and his group of ten, but what then?

If he couldn’t break through to the Cold Resistance Level in a short time?

Xia Hong needs time, a lot of time!

If it is indeed as Yuwen Dao hinted, that there are factional struggles within the Mirror Immortal Camp, then the time Xia Hong can gain will be even greater.

Moreover, Yuwen Dao deliberately mentioned his father Yuwen Hu’s philosophy, which was clearly suggestive. It’s highly likely that the camp leader He Yuanhun’s philosophy differs from his father’s.

This could also explain why his father would stop Leader He Yuanhun from adopting He Meng’s proposal to cause trouble for Great Xia.

Yuwen Dao’s gaze remained fixed on Xia Hong, his demeanor tense, his hands never leaving the broadsword, evidently waiting for his decision.

"Let the blood flow!"

After a long silence, upon hearing Xia Hong utter these three words, Yuwen Dao immediately relaxed, his tension dissipating in an instant. He turned to instruct the others, his face breaking into a smile.

"Thank you, Leader Xia!"

Xia Hong only slightly cupped his fists, not showing any pleasant expression.

While he admired Yuwen Dao, the relations between the two camps could not yet be considered good. Sparing them tonight was a temporary measure; if he later discovered Yuwen Dao had deceived him, any positive expression he showed now would be a colossal joke.

Knowing Xia Hong’s concerns, Yuwen Dao didn’t take it to heart. With an awkward chuckle, he glanced at Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan behind Xia Hong, showing a hint of curiosity before nodding to them with a smile.

It was at this moment that he finally showed some signs of youth...

This slightly childish behavior of Yuwen Dao lightened Xia Hong’s heart, significantly reducing the hostility in his eyes.

Naturally, besides focusing on Yuwen Dao, he didn’t forget to observe how the other members of the Mirror Immortal Camp were letting the blood from the Demon Sheep.

The group walked to the chest area of the Demon Sheep, slicing open the outer skin with a knife, and near its heart, they found a red blood vessel with a diameter of three to four centimeters.

One person stepped forward to cut open the blood vessel, and the crimson blood gushed out immediately. The others held large leather bags to collect the blood, soon filling one bag entirely.

Strangely, during the bleeding process, the Demon Sheep’s originally enormous body, nearly nine meters long, began to shrink slowly. After half of its blood was drained, its body had already shrunk by one or two-tenths, leaving only a length of over seven meters.

Although this scene piqued Xia Hong’s curiosity, he didn’t ask any questions, nor did his face show any unusual expression, the same with Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan.

The three even deliberately turned their heads to conceal their curiosity.

No way around it, if they asked, it would confirm it was their first time witnessing an Intermediate Cold Beast being bled. It would not only expose Great Xia’s strength but also instantly lower their prestige.
