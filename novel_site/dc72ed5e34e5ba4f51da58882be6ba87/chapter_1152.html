Chapter 1152: Chapter 393: Bountiful Harvest at the Jiao Long Nest, Rise of the New Generation (Part 2)

Perhaps it might even be used as medicinal materials!

Xia Hong suppressed the excitement in his heart and looked at the giant Jiao Shed in front of him. Suddenly, he thought of something, his expression pensive.

"So not all Beast Kings are small in size. For example, the Red Carp Monster in Wuyuan Waters is over two hundred meters long; just now, the Black Flood Dragon was also over a thousand meters. This Jiao Shed should be from its previous stage, meaning its previous stage was over six hundred meters long!"

Last time when battling that Red Carp Monster, Xia Hong was puzzled.

The Beast Kings he had fought against were generally divided into two forms: one was the true form, very small in size; the other was the battle form, which could instantly enlarge hundreds of times.

But that Red Carp Monster, from appearing, fighting, to eventually fleeing, its length remained over two hundred meters, noticeably different from other Beast Kings.

Of course, today’s Black Flood Dragon is the same.

"The Red Blood Demon Dragon in Qinghepu exceeded a thousand meters, but it’s surely above the Beast King level, essentially worthless for reference, so the conclusions I can draw are twofold.

One is that Water Cold Beasts and Land Cold Beasts are completely different species, so their sizes at the Beast King level are also entirely different;

The other is a matter of bloodline, the higher the bloodline of the Cold Beast, the larger its size at the Beast King level!"

The Red Carp, Black Flood Dragon, counting the Red Blood Demon Dragon in Qinghepu, in terms of bloodline, these three are clearly more advanced than any Beast King I’ve previously battled, hence Xia Hong came to the second conclusion.

"Too bad I haven’t succeeded in slaying a Beast King from the waters yet. If I could kill one and see if their bones are different, I could basically come to a conclusion!"

Xia Hong frowned slightly. In terms of sheer power, both last year’s Red Carp Monster and today’s Black Flood Dragon were not his match.

The problem is, these water-dwelling Beast Kings are very intelligent. As soon as they realize they can’t win, they immediately flee, and they have many escape methods. Plus, the water realm is their home ground, making it incredibly hard to kill them.

I can only take it slow, first take these things, like the Heavenly Yang Fruit and other mature Spirit Plants, including small ores and Soft Shedding—these can be taken away first, but the others...

Looking at the scales on the Jiao Shed and the twenty or so pieces of giant ores with a diameter over half a meter on the ground, Xia Hong was instantly stumped.

"I’ve been in the Ice Abyss for almost ten years, and I still haven’t gotten a storage ring. Am I the most unsuccessful transmigrator?"

Xia Hong suddenly mocked himself and then shook his head with a low laugh, musing that this world might or might not have storage rings—he was overthinking.

This place is closest to He Yin, so I can only ask Xiao Kangcheng to send some people over to transport the items back to Bashang first!

He Yin used to be a village-level camp at the southernmost end of Baisong Camp, right next to Moao Mountain. In July, Jiangxia Town had assigned it to Great Xia, hence it became a post for Great Xia.

Xiao Kangcheng did well in Zhaoyang for the past six months. Using the matter of He Yin in July, Xia Chuan promoted him to Bashang Hunting Bureau Mountain Yu, and additionally appointed him as He Yin Guard Commander.

Xia Hong stayed in Xia City during this time, so he was quite clear about the personnel shifts.

Having made up his mind, he didn’t waste time and began to tidy up.

The eighth year of Da Xia, the tenth day of September, first half of the night

Southeastern end of Moao Mountain, about twenty kilometers deep into Snow Forest.

"Roar..."

The originally silent Snow Forest was suddenly awakened by a roar.

The ice crystals on the surrounding tall trees fell to the ground, making crisp sounds.

A Rock Armor Bear, standing upright, about seven to eight meters long, covered in yellow mane, was standing beside a large tree, roaring non-stop.

The Rock Armor Bear’s left leg was caught in an animal trap with a diameter of over half a meter, and obviously, the roar originated from the intense pain the trap caused it.

"Luo An, Luo Cheng, Zou Ping, pull the ropes, the rest shoot arrows!"

The Rock Armor Bear’s roar had barely stopped when a command was suddenly heard.

From a nearby large tree, three young men wearing golden armor jumped down. Each holding a rope, they heard the command, stuck their weapons into the ground, and then pulled the ropes back fiercely all at once.

Swoosh...

The three ropes were all connected to the animal trap.

The three young men pulled the ropes forcefully, and the animal trap immediately began to move south, naturally dragging the left leg of the Rock Armor Bear along with it.

The Rock Armor Bear was not only seven to eight meters tall but also extremely bulky, obviously weighing quite a bit. The key is its strength was apparently not weak; its left leg was only moved half a meter before it reacted, stamping its foot down forcefully, fixing the trap in place. No matter how the three young men tried, it did not budge.

"Roar..."

Not only unmovable, with a roar, the Rock Armor Bear began retracting its left leg, pulling the three young men back.

Whoosh whoosh whoosh whoosh......

Unfortunately, it wasn’t only dealing with these three young men.

From a dozen big trees nearby, suddenly a dozen Silver Sharp Arrows flew out, straight towards the Rock Armor Bear’s eyes, underarms, mouth, throat, and other weak spots.

Bang!

The Rock Armor Bear certainly wouldn’t just sit still waiting to die. It first swatted away the arrows flying towards it, then dropped its forelimbs to the ground, using its back to block the arrows, and right after, it lunged fiercely, directly charging at the trio who were pulling the ropes.
