With the building upgrades completed, Xia Hong began to toast and drink with the people in the hall, simultaneously contemplating how to utilize the remaining resources.

Whether for his future cultivation or to gather information about gold and silver mines, this expedition is imperative.

Therefore, preparations before setting out naturally must be thorough.

Although the Cold Element Festival lasts fifteen days, the grand feast cannot go on.

This grand feast consumed various resources, causing Qiu Peng, the Camp Supply Department Director, to frequently complain to Xia Chuan, if it continues for two more feasts, he’d probably curse.

However, even though the grand feast ended, the festive atmosphere in Great Xia Valley continued for several days.

After the entire camp celebrated, it was time for each household’s small gatherings.

Once the outposts in Wuyuan and Jing Valley were established, many Hunting Team and Harvesting Department personnel went to these outposts, returning only every few months, catching this good festival, they naturally gathered well.

This celebratory atmosphere continued until the tenth of January.

On the night of January tenth, people of Great Xia discovered that the valley’s iron gate, originally supposed to be sealed until the fifteenth of January, had surprisingly opened early.

Starting from that night, for the following three nights, Xia Hong would lead both the Hunting and Craftsman Departments, totaling over two thousand people, out of the valley iron gate, busy with unknown activities.

Until five days later, the first day after the Cold Element Festival, on January 16th of the Second Year of Great Xia, people who exited the valley iron gate after half a month, were amazed to find a giant black soil wall standing on the camp’s west side.

"Is this what the leader has been busy with these days?"

"It’s built along the mountain wall, almost reaching Redwood Ridge."

"It’s true, it has entered Redwood Ridge, I heard that once the valley’s land is all sold out, building houses in the future will have to be outside the valley."

"This wall is so thick, come quickly and see."

"Follow me, the city gate is on the west side here, come on."

"Let’s go, to the west side, the wall is there."

"The leader seems to be here today as well."
