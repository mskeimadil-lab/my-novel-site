Chapter 1239: Chapter 412: Outer Realm People, Demon Dragon’s Strength, Confident Chu Yan’er, Hou Ming’s Skills_3

Heavenly Phenomenon Realm!

Bang......

Before Chu Yan’er finished speaking, Chen Yimou sprang up from his chair.

"At least Venerable Level, doesn’t that mean even Lord Landgrave..."

"I haven’t finished speaking, why are you in such a rush? Sit down!"

"Subordinate was disrespectful, please forgive me, Miss."

Seeing Chen Yimou’s panicked face, Chu Yan’er quickly chastised him, and only after he sat back down did she continue: "There’s another possibility, it could be a very powerful Calamity Level Demon Monster."

Swoosh......

Chen Yimou, who had just sat down, along with Chu Dongning and the other four, all shuddered violently and looked up at Chu Yan’er in shock.

You might as well not have finished speaking!

The six of them had only this one thought in their minds at the moment.

Heavenly Phenomenon Realm, which is the Venerable Level Fierce Beast, Chen Cang would already find it difficult to deal with, or basically wouldn’t be able to deal with at all.

Calamity Level Demon Monster, what kind of concept is that?

Among Crafty, Nightmare, Fierce, Divine, and Demon, Chen Cang currently only has four levels: low, intermediate, high, and above that, Calamity Level.

Because Deceitful Monsters have all sorts of bizarre abilities, it’s very hard for humans to rate them before they are completely slain. Luckily, Chen Cang has killed enough monsters to come up with a set of differentiation methods.

No matter how mysterious the methods of low-, intermediate-, and high-level Deceitful Monsters are, when it comes to the final stage of killing, it ultimately relies on strength.

According to Chen Cang’s years of accumulated experience, if it can only exert below Cold Resistance Level strength, it’s a low-level Deceitful Monster; if it can exert Cold Resistance Level strength, it’s intermediate; if it can exert Xianyang Level strength, then it’s high-level, like the news about the Five Great Masters at Dajue Temple this May. When it reached Chen Cang, the Night Patrol Office immediately classified them as advanced Deceitful Monsters.

As for Calamity Level, just from the name alone, you understand.

The strength a Calamity Level Deceitful Monster can exert is beyond comprehension for most people. Chen Cang Feudal Town has been recorded over its 200-year development, only twice.

However, fortunately, both times happened to other feudal towns.

"The Chen Cang records mention two occurrences of Calamity Level Deceitful Monsters, one was over eighty years ago at Hezang Feudal Town, where they encountered a Calamity Level Nightmare Monster. That Nightmare Monster caused an earthquake, with half of Hezang Four Prefectures’ land collapsing, and over seven million people vanished overnight without a trace, am I right?"

When Zhu Dong started speaking, Chu Dongning beside him immediately nodded heavily, and then continued: "Correct, the second time was over thirty years ago, in the east of Cai Qiu’s Wei Bo Feudal Town, which was invaded by a Calamity Level Crafty Monster. I remember the books call it the beekeeper, for it raised countless giant monstrous bees. The smaller the bees, the more toxic they were, even masters at the Xianyang Level couldn’t resist them."

Wei Bo sacrificed over five million people and used an unknown method to drive that beekeeper away, but they have yet to recover their vitality."

In the Ice Abyss World, information is very important, so any camp with some resources pays great attention to recording various news and secrets, especially those concerning Deceitful Monsters. Not only the ones their own camps encounter but also those encountered by other camps are recorded in detail for future warnings.

Chen Cang is no exception. All four present, including Chu Yan’er, have naturally read the Chen Cang records, so they’re familiar with what Zhu Dong and Chu Dongning talked about.

After hearing it, their expressions all froze.

At this moment, they have only one thought: beneath Chilong Lake, there’s a high possibility that a Calamity Level Demon Monster is lying dormant. Even if it’s not, at least a Venerable Level Cold Beast is.

"This Great Xia is going to have a huge misfortune!"

"Not just Great Xia, it’s this entire Moao South Slope that’s going to have misfortune."

"According to Yuanbai Uncle’s description, that Red Blood Demon Dragon could awaken at any time in decades. Whether it’s a Demon Monster or a Venerable Level Cold Beast, the Moao South Slope Nine Towns can’t handle them, they..."

......

For a moment, a look of sympathy appeared on the faces of all six, including Chu Yan’er.

The rise of Great Xia is truly legendary, and even Chu Yan’er feels inferior compared to the talent of Lord Xia Hong of Great Xia. She can even be certain that there might not be anyone in the entire Chen Cang who can compare with Xia Hong in terms of aptitude.

But regardless of which of her two speculations is correct, Great Xia growing strong enough to deal with that Red Blood Demon Dragon is impossible in just a few decades, not even in a hundred years.

"The Moao Nine Towns have a total population of over 15 million, but their strength is a little weak. With such a large ticking time bomb buried in Chilong Lake, once it explodes someday, it will certainly devastate countless lives. It’s a pity..."

"Even if the Nine Towns unite, no, integrating them won’t help."

"The strength levels are not even in the same league, integration won’t help."

"Whether it’s a Venerable Level Cold Beast or a Calamity Level Demon Monster, it would be a devastating blow to this region. No one can save them, so we shouldn’t move toward Moao South Slope from Chen Cang either..."

"Who said no one can save them?"

The last speaker, He Yang, was interrupted by Chu Yan’er before he finished. He and the other five looked up in confusion.

With a grave face, Chu Yan’er showed a trace of confidence and said, "Humans struggling to survive together in the Ice Abyss, how can we from Chen Cang stand by and watch the people of Moao South Slope die in vain? Does Lord Landgrave’s teachings not remind us that with greater strength comes greater responsibility?"

When the six heard Chu Yaner’s words, their expressions were slightly invigorated.
