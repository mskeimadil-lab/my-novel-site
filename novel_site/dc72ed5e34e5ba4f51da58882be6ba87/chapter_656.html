Chapter 656 - 274: Weakness and Strength, the Final Battle Begins (Part 3)

Chapter 656: Chapter 274: Weakness and Strength, the Final Battle Begins (Part 3)

Bang......

The broad sword slammed into the great saber, Peng Bo, who was already in a forward charging state, was suddenly catapulted like an iron arrow from a bow by Yuwen Dao’s forceful strike, flying out over a hundred meters and crashing to the ground with a resounding boom, shaking the surroundings for hundreds of meters.

Even after all that, he still skidded on the ground for over ten meters before finally coming to a halt.

Whoosh......

How did I get knocked flying?

On the ground, Peng Bo didn’t have time to figure out this question when a sharp sword whistle reached his ears.

His reaction was not slow, as he inserted his two fingers into the ghost head, twisting his wrist, and the great saber rose directly from the ground, blocking the direction from which the sword whistle came.

Clang......

Blocked!

The moment this thought flashed in Peng Bo’s mind, a heavy punch suddenly struck his face.

Bang!

Yuwen Dao hadn’t come wielding a sword; he had directly thrown the broad sword to lure him into blocking with his great saber. The real fatal strike was his fist.

In that split second, even though Peng Bo realized it, it was already too late.

His body was directly knocked from the ground, soaring at least twenty meters into the air.

The most troublesome part was that his ghost head great saber also slipped from his grasp!

And as he soared, Yuwen Dao not only retrieved the broad sword but also kicked the ghost head great saber into the air, using the force to leap up.

Whoosh......

The broad sword suddenly ascended, and just as Peng Bo was turning his body, before he landed, he felt the great saber graze over his forehead.

Landing on the ground, Peng Bo caught a handful of his black hair, his face full of astonishment. After a pause of over ten breaths, he looked up at the youthful face of Yuwen Dao and sighed heavily, revealing an aged demeanor.

"No wonder Lord Yuwen can command the Hunting Department; Peng truly admires you!"

When Peng Bo’s voice fell, the whole area fell into silence.

Those in the Earth-digging Realm probably didn’t understand the significance of Yuwen Dao’s victory.

But others like Xia Chuan, Chen Yingyuan, Chen Yingba, Wu Tianxing, including those of Cold Resistance Level from Yuan Cheng, all had faces full of shock and disbelief.

Especially Xia Chuan and Chen Yingyuan, their eyes on Yuwen Dao carried not only shock but also a hint of seriousness.

"Yuwen Dao not bad, Peng Bo was defeated in 381 breaths, currently ranking fourth."

Xia Hong smiled slightly, giving the first compliment of the night to Yuwen Dao.

Yuwen Dao was indeed impressive.

In the absence of superior strength, victory could only be achieved through combat experience and combat talent, or otherwise by giving it all.

In internal competitions, neither side needed to give it their all.

And in terms of combat experience, Yuwen Dao was obviously not as seasoned as Peng Bo.

Therefore, Yuwen Dao’s victory relied entirely on his extraordinary combat talent, something that couldn’t be taught.

"Third match, continue, Chen Yingba against Chen Yingyuan!"

This match was even less suspenseful.

Though Chen Yingba had the basic strength of six manes, in terms of combat experience and talent, he wasn’t on the same level as his elder brother.

Key point was that Xia Hong had sparred with both of them, so he clearly knew Chen Yingba should have been taught by his elder brother hand on hand.

Thus, there was nothing much else to say about it.

It seemed to Chen Yingyuan entertained a little crooked thought, intending to prolong Chen Yingba’s defeat, but after a light cough from Xia Hong as a prompt, he immediately realized he couldn’t fool anyone and straightforwardly defeated his younger brother.

"Third match, Chen Yingyuan wins, Chen Yingba directly ranked sixth, fourth round does not need drawing lots, you will get a bye, and after Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao decide victory, you can compete with the loser for second place."

Fourth round, no need for a draw?

Chen Yingyuan directly gets a bye, and can only be second or third.

Among the hundred Earth-digging Realm present, most appeared puzzled, only a few smarter ones picked up clues from the laughter of those of Cold Resistance Level and Chen Yingyuan’s embarrassed smile.

To be sly or cunning naturally requires payment of a price.

Actually, it wasn’t much of a price.

Chen Yingyuan’s strength was not as good as Peng Bo’s, Xia Hong could confirm.

Rather than as punishment, it was more a time-saver.

After an embarrassed smile, Chen Yingyuan nodded without any objection, clearly understanding in his heart.

"Come on, the last match, Yuwen Dao versus Xia Chuan!"

The winner would be able to form the first army of the Great Xia.

Having rested for one match, Yuwen Dao returned to the arena.

On the other side, Xia Chuan also slowly walked into the arena.

However, following behind him was a giant figure three meters tall.

Seeing that imposing figure, Yuwen Dao’s eyes were full of seriousness, and his grip on his saber tightened.

"That is......"

"The Minister’s... Sword Puppet!"

"Wasn’t the Minister’s Sword Puppet five meters tall, how did it shrink?"

"It must be something the Minister recently developed in Longyou, I feel it’s even more terrifying than the previous five-meter one."
