Chapter 951: Chapter 351: Lu Yang’s Plan, Xia City Shaken, Sumeru Nightmare, and His Highness’s Golden Words_5

Xia Hong darted to dodge the approaching Cold Beasts, constantly searching for weaknesses with his left eye.

Sumeru Space is not a normal world.

Where is the exit, where is the exit!

As the number of Cold Beasts increased, Xia Hong’s anxiety grew. He searched for a long time but couldn’t find the exit. Finally, by instinct, he focused his gaze on the black cloud in mid-air.

Sure enough, the moment he lifted his gaze, the black cloud immediately fled backward.

"There’s a gap, the exit is on its body!"

Xia Hong instantly noticed the gap in the middle of the black cloud, with small flames burning on both sides—clearly caused by Solidified Fire Oil.

Without hesitation, he flew directly toward the black cloud.

The Sumeru Space was full of clouds inside, surrounded by chaos, and was not very large. That black cloud couldn’t escape for long before it began to circle around.

Xia Hong moved quickly, and while dodging the pursuit of Cold Beasts, he soon caught up with the black cloud, seizing the opportunity to thrust his Dragon Sparrow Saber, coated with Solidified Fire Oil, directly into it.

But unlike last time, the moment the Dragon Sparrow Saber pierced into the black cloud, it immediately dispersed to the sides, retreating over a hundred meters before regrouping.

"Jiājiājiājiā, I fell for it once, but now you want to hurt me? Dream on..."

"Is that so!"

Phew......

The regrouped black cloud had just started laughing when suddenly, a divine light as bright as daylight shot out from Xia Hong’s left eye, striking it directly.

Ding......

The moment the divine light hit the black cloud, like before when Xia Hong used it against an enemy, the targeted part of the black cloud shattered like glass, making a cracking sound, and quickly began to fissure, forming a web of cracks.

"What is this... what is this... Yin Yang Mirror, how could you have a Yin Yang Mirror, ah... ah..."

Another Deceitful Monster that recognized the Yin Yang Mirror appeared!

Xia Hong’s gaze focused slightly, watching as the black cloud directly split its body, abandoning the fissured part and retreating backward.

After the cracked part of the black cloud completely dissipated, it left behind a twisted space rift.

Xia Hong, ignoring whether this was the exit, seeing more Cold Beasts approaching from behind, gritted his teeth and charged directly into the rift.

......

Arrow Bamboo Forest, East cliff section.

A disheveled figure suddenly fell from the clouds.

"This is... Arrow Bamboo Forest!"

Xia Hong turned his head to look at the vast bamboo forest to the west, his expression slightly stunned.

But quickly, remembering the massive Cold Beasts in the Sumeru Space, he turned his gaze further west of the Arrow Bamboo Forest and immediately realized.

"Every year, the Cold Beasts that invade Arrow Bamboo Forest during the Beast Tide are all collected by that black cloud monster just now. Sumeru Space, the entrance and exit of Sumeru Space, is this cloud layer above?"

Xia Hong looked up at the white clouds above the cliff, and many previously incomprehensible questions in his mind were instantly answered.

"Sumeru Space, I was caught while falling, mistakenly entering another world, so that black cloud should also count as a Sumeru Nightmare. Let’s temporarily call it Sumeru Monster!"

No wonder the Nine Towns couldn’t cross the Double Dragon Mountain Wall to reach the world below the valley.

He had previously been puzzled that the Nine Towns, stronger than the Great Xia, couldn’t be stopped by a mere two-thousand-meter wall.

Xia Hong had always suspected that the Nine Towns already knew about the Great Xia’s situation and had been secretly observing for a long time.

But from Zhu Yang and Yang Fa’s reaction upon seeing him, he could basically infer that his suspicion was unnecessary.

Once they confirmed he wasn’t from the other Eight Towns, their subsequent reaction was certain—he was from the north.

In other words, regarding the world below the western side of the Double Dragon Mountain Wall, they truly knew nothing.

Even Lu Yang’s three years of subtle inquiry in Beishuo matched this point; the entire Moao Nine Towns were fundamentally ignorant of the world below the western Double Dragon Mountain Wall.

If the Sumeru Monster blocked the way, then this makes sense.

The Yin Yang Mirror is not something everyone has. Even he almost fell for it just now; if it were anyone else, even at the Xianyang level, they would probably die without a doubt.

"Flying along the mountain wall is fine, using Hanqiong’s tunnels through the mountain’s interior is also fine, but jumping down from the clouds results in a trap. The saying ’easy to climb, hard to descend’ is truly accurate; this Sumeru Monster is practically a natural isolation Divine Artifact!"

In an instant, Xia Hong’s eyes immediately lit up.

Not to mention the isolation effect of the Sumeru Monster, just that vast Sumeru Space alone is enticing—a completely isolated Different Space!

Imagine if this space could be his own; wouldn’t the Great Xia then have a completely isolated safe zone from the outside world?

"Calm down, calm down. Hundreds of Beast Kings—just this alone is beyond what I can handle. That Sumeru Monster misjudged my strength this time; if it had initially surrounded me with all the Cold Beasts, the outcome would have been uncertain. Without the strength, I can’t make it my target."

This Sumeru Monster has not actively sought trouble with the Great Xia. Let’s let it stay above the mountain wall; even if it only isolates the Nine Towns’ prying, it immensely benefits the Great Xia, and there’s no need to resolve it urgently.
