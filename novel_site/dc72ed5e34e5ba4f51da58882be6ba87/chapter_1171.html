[Resources: Wood 8,726,100,000 Coal 9,087,200,000 Iron 9,862,500,000 Silver 21,820,000]

"September 10, according to Eight Towns...no, it’s Seven Towns, the first round of trade should be completed. According to what Qiu Peng said the day before yesterday, the first batch of trade profits totaled 420 million taels of silver, which converts into 8.4 million system resource points. Coupled with what has been self-mined over the past year, silver is definitely not lacking now!"

Xia Hong, flying towards Hongmen City over the outskirts of Moao Mountain, started calculating the total account of the system’s silver resources.

The turmoil caused by Yang Ning and Li Hu in Hongmen City on September 1 was known to him before he headed to White Tiger Pond, and he was also aware of Qiu Peng’s refusal to trade with Beishuo Town.

Truth be told, Xia Hong was most wary of Beishuo among the Northern Three Towns, so he rather supported Qiu Peng’s decision.

Great Xia’s core objectives in foreign trade are twofold:

One is to change the lifestyle habits of the Eight Towns’ people with Great Xia’s goods, whether it’s called wooing or transforming, the essence being infiltration under the guise of trade;

The other is to earn silver from the Eight Towns.

Regardless of Great Xia’s intentions, selling cultivation resources to the Eight Towns will inevitably provide some support to enemies, and Xia Hong is most wary of Beishuo Town, thus avoiding trade with them is indeed a good thing.

The items Qiu Peng sells externally are meticulously planned, treating all Seven Towns Chamber of Commerce the same, with a fixed number of precious cultivation resources paired with ample everyday materials. If they want to buy, they must buy them all in bundled packages, and the Seven Towns could only agree for those cultivation resources.

The trade volume of Seven Towns is uniformly 60 million taels of silver, hence Xia Hong could immediately come up with the figure of 420 million taels.

Things that Great Xia buys from Seven Towns are generally negligible, given Lu Yang has thoroughly explored the produce of the Nine Towns over the past four years, excluding very few special resources like Blood Ginseng Pill from Muyin Town, Great Xia basically has no need to buy anything from Seven Towns.

"The trade volume from the Seven Towns Chamber of Commerce is definitely substantial, while the amount of silver brought forth by those village-level camps in the Eight Towns should be quite limited. Still, even the smallest mosquito has meat, earning a bit is still something, and the core purpose is to cultivate dependency on Great Xia’s goods among these village-level camp dwellers within the Eight Towns, so it must continue!"

Looking at the 21,820,000 silver resource points on him, Xia Hong finally felt much more at ease. From when he first came into contact with silver until now, not having to worry about silver resources essentially took him about seven years, it truly wasn’t easy.

These over 2,000,000 silver resource points are what he mined himself last year after exiting seclusion from those silver mines opened in Long Mountain, not including the silver yet earned from the Seven Towns.

"After the Seven Towns tasted the sweetness, unless something unexpected happens, the trade shouldn’t be interrupted. Going forward, annually we should earn nearly a billion taels of silver from the Seven Towns Chamber of Commerce, which converts into approximately 20,000,000 silver resource points. It’s truly sufficient, the Martial Arts Pavilion, Artifact Refining Pavilion, and Alchemy Pavilion can continue planning upgrades, while Iron Wall and Black Land can accumulate a few years without much issue."
