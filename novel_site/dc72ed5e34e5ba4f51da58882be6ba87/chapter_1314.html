Chapter 1314: Chapter 430: Emergence from Seclusion, Hairpin Ceremony and Blunders, Great Xia’s Splendor and the Eve of an Explosion

Star-picking Platform, Enlightenment Lotus Platform

Xia Hong was sitting cross-legged with his eyes closed, his entire body emitting flames. Behind him floated a dark red bone appearance nearly six zhang high, with a layer of molten gold-like patterns on the surface, appearing quite mysterious.

As his breathing rhythm intensified, the light emitted by those golden patterns became even more dazzling, seemingly assisting in the constant growth and expansion of the dark red bone appearance.

Indeed, the phantom bone appearance behind him was still growing.

Although the speed was astonishingly slow, almost inch by inch, it was indeed gradually enlarging and stretching, and the momentum emanating from the expanding dark red bone appearance was increasingly terrifying.

The height of the bone appearance was already nearly six zhang.

With this final wave of enlargement and stretching, it was infinitely close to six zhang.

But it was this last inch of discrepancy that made Xia Hong’s breathing increasingly heavy, the pain on his face growing more intense.

"Huh... huh......"

As the medicinal power rushed into his body faster, he even let out a suppressed low hiss, a ball of white light suddenly emerged from his brow, then split into a small part, and crazily surged into his body along with the medicinal power.

Buzz!

A rumbling sound exploded inside Xia Hong’s heart, the dark red bone appearance behind him once again erupted with strong light, and with that strong light, finally crossed the last inch at the critical point.

"Six zhang bone appearance, achieved!"

Xia Hong’s tightly closed eyes suddenly opened, the flames surrounding him soared upwards, doubling in height, and the warming range that originally radiated only 500 meters quickly expanded outward.

meters... 600 meters... 700 meters... 900 meters... 1100 meters... 1300 meters... 1500 meters.

The full depth of the expanded Inner City of Great Xia was only slightly over five kilometers, with a radius of three kilometers within the radiating range, meaning that, at this moment, any movement within the Inner City was almost within Xia Hong’s perception.

"The warming range is three times what it was before, so what about the strength..."

Xia Hong slightly clenched his fists, looked at the exposed sky above the Star-picking Platform, eyes flashing with brilliance, raised his hand directly towards the sky, and threw a punch.

The fist wind erupted, almost directly turning into a flame, like a flaming dragon punching into the sky, a violent sonic boom that resounded across the sky a moment later.

Boom......

Centered on the Flame Dragon Fist Wind, within a radius of five li above the Inner City, not to mention the snowflakes, even the surrounding cold air was instantly dispelled, forming a vacuum, cleansing everything like a nuclear explosion.

Buzz......

Whirr whirr whirr......

Shockwaves spread out over a dozen li, and as they spread, the previously dispelled air currents gathered again, directly forming a fierce and dominant air vortex.

Leaving aside the commotion in the Inner City for now, Xia Hong’s eyes were scorching as he looked at the clouds high above, shaken by his fist wind, his pupils full of excitement and thrill.

"40 Jun, 3 times, no, before the breakthrough at the critical point, my basic strength was only 12 Jun, so it’s more than 3 times, close to a 3.5 times increase, that’s too exaggerated, too exaggerated..."

At this moment, Xia Hong’s pupils were full of fervor.

Jun equals 100,000 jin, and 100 Jun equals 1 Jun, meaning that 1 Jun is approximately equal to 10 million jin, tens of millions of jin, which is almost a kind of strength that cannot be directly visualized with data; it belongs to another level of power.

This is the Xianyang Level, where without breaking through the existing cultivation level, the strength cannot continue to rise upwards.

Because, at the level of Jun, the difficulty is too great to further enhance!

Xia Hong previously envisioned a twofold increase, from 12 Jun to 24 Jun, which was already his boldest idea.

3.5 times, it really gave him a big surprise!

Bear in mind, this is just the change in basic strength.

"Earlier, I also speculated that Yang Zhun, Shangguan Yang, Qin Feng, all three individuals were late-stage Xianyang, but now it seems that the three are only at mid-stage cultivation, but when compared to me, the gold content of their mid-stage cultivation is woefully low."

Xia Hong moved slightly, closed his eyes to sense the change in his bone appearance.

The biggest change in bone appearance was certainly the size, growing from the initial three zhang to the current six zhang; next was the color of the bone appearance, which was increasingly polished compared to before, and the lines attached to it were turning into an extreme gold color; and finally, on the outer perimeter of the bone appearance, an additional layer of transparent meridian tissue.

Last September, during the battle at Hongmen, Yang Zhun’s bone appearance perimeter also had such a layer of meridian tissue, and Xia Hong had previously thought that was a sign of late-stage Xianyang cultivation, now that he has broken through himself, he naturally understands.

"Mid-stage exhibiting meridians, so late-stage is likely to grow flesh, then at Peak, with the emergence of skin membranes, the bone appearance battle body can be fully materialized, so it should be like this then?"

Xia Hong looked at the change in bone appearance, with a thoughtful expression on his face.

Although he does not have ancestral experience, the cultivation progresses layer by layer, everything has traces to follow, and he can roughly guess through speculation.

Battle Body Talent is divided when breaking through the Cold Resistance Level, that is the phase of reshaping skin membranes, and subsequent power growth at the Cold Resistance Level is closely related to it;

The War Body Bone Appearance starts to be divided upon breaking through the Xianyang Level, and the subsequent strength growth at the Xianyang Level is noticeably also greatly related to it.

Iron Bone 3 Jun, Silver Bone 4 Jun, Gold Bone 5 Jun, Jade Bone 7 Jun, to the final Bone Burning is 10 Jun, these five stages, he remembers clearly his own strength at each stage.
