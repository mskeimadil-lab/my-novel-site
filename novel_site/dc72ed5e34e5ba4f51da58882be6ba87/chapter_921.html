Chapter 921: Chapter 346: Formidable Soldiers and Brave Generals, One Against Three, Reason Behind the Beast Tide

"That shadow moves just like the Lord, throwing a right punch."

"A Beast King level White Bear couldn’t withstand even a single punch from the Lord?"

"The snow within five miles has stopped, this can change the weather itself."

"Look at the clouds in the sky! With this punch, do you think our Divine Martial Army with a thousand men standing together can withstand it?"

"This..."

"He broke through, the Lord must have broken through to the Xianyang Level."

......

"The shadow just now, was formed after I unleashed the full power of my bones and skin membrane, what was that?"

The Cold Resistance Levels below all saw that towering dark red skeletal shadow, how could Xia Hong not see it himself?

But this was his first all-out attack after breaking through, so he himself couldn’t figure out what exactly that shadow was.

"In the entire Great Xia, I’m the only one at Xianyang Level, without comparison, I simply don’t know what it is, it seems I need to hurry and face opponents of the same level!"

Xia Hong contemplated for just a moment, the speed of the Cold Blood Blade extracting blood from the Beast King level Cold Beast noticeably slowed, and Xia Hong had no patience to wait, he directly kicked the White Bear in front of him flying, then leapt towards the Snow Mane.

"Roar..."

The fate of the White Bear King was right before them; how could the remaining two Beast Kings have any courage to confront Xia Hong? They had originally escaped from Redwood Ridge and were intercepted by Xia Hong midway, having no choice but to join forces to deal with him.

Realizing Xia Hong’s terrifying power, the two Beast Kings didn’t hesitate, let out a low howl, and turned to flee towards the east side of Arrow Bamboo Forest.

The Frost Wolf managed to escape smoothly, but the Snow Mane was not so lucky.

It had just flown east less than a hundred meters when it was forcefully stopped by the heatwave from Xia Hong’s blade, followed by even more violent blade strikes.

Xia Hong didn’t randomly choose the Snow Mane; he wanted to deal with two of them but found it somewhat unrealistic. The Frost Wolf was too fast, so choosing the Snow Mane was clearly the best decision.

The Snow Mane’s long tongue kept entwining around Xia Hong, and at the same time, it never gave up the thought of escaping, constantly shifting its body eastward.

Shh... Shh...

Unfortunately, its long tongue couldn’t even break Xia Hong’s skin, instead getting too close to the Cold Blood Blade, and soon dozens were chopped off.

"Roar..."

The Snow Mane howled in pain, trying to retract all its tongues into its mouth, but it was too late by then.

Xia Hong had already entangled its remaining tongues with the Cold Blood Blade, and then with a fierce tug in front of him, the Snow Mane’s body that was fleeing eastward was forcefully pulled back, flying like a cannonball towards Xia Hong’s face.

"Move aside!"

On the ground, the reassembled six-thousand-strong army, including Xia Chuan and Yuwen Dao, the six Commanders, froze for a moment at Xia Hong’s voice, then immediately noticed that the army was directly beneath the Snow Mane and quickly reacted.

"Everyone scatter around, quick!"

The soldiers of the Six Armies, who had long been trained to follow orders without question, swiftly scattered around at Xia Chuan’s command, quickly clearing an empty area with a radius of five to six hundred meters.

Bang...

The soldiers had just cleared the space, without having time to look up at the sky, when a loud bang exploded in the previously empty area.

It was the Snow Mane, kicked down from the sky by Xia Hong.

The violent shockwave spread outwards, and the soldiers who hadn’t yet regrouped were instantly forced to retreat, the front line of hundreds of soldiers even staggering to the ground, notably embarrassed.

It should be noted that the soldiers standing at the front were mostly from the elite scout camps of the Six Armies, which means they all had Cold Resistance Level cultivation!

"Gulp..."

"From five hundred meters high, the shockwave of a Beast King being kicked to the ground can knock us all down?"

"If we were any closer, we’d probably get injured, right?"

"Injured? We could be shaken to death!"

"Getting knocked down is nothing? That was three Beast Kings just now!"

"The Lord fought three and killed two."

"If the Frost Wolf King had been any slower, it would certainly be dead too."

"This is the Xianyang Level, the Lord’s strength is on a completely different level from us, right?"

"Not just another level, it’s entirely another world."

......

Even if Xia Hong didn’t intentionally look, he understood the reactions of the people on the ground, but he had no mind for that now. Instead, he turned to look at the fleeing silhouette of the Frost Wolf King towards the east, with a thoughtful look.

"It seems my previous thinking was wrong all along; the reason for the Beast Tide isn’t something in the Arrow Bamboo Forest attracting them, but rather..."

Xia Hong turned his head towards the west side, Redwood Ridge, showing a cautious expression.

"They’re afraid, of something in Redwood Ridge!"

When the army began charging into Arrow Bamboo Forest, Xia Hong was already there, but he wanted to see how much the Six Armies had grown over the years, so he didn’t intervene in the battle but observed closely both the army and the Beast Tide situation.

Seeing the three Beast Kings rushing out, he immediately moved to stop them.

When battling the three Beast Kings, Xia Hong distinctly felt their urgent desire to escape, and in the end, the Frost Wolf King chose to continue fleeing east rather than turn back towards the closer Redwood Ridge, confirming his earlier assumption.

Deep in the Redwood Ridge, something happens every four months that scares these Cold Beasts, prompting them to run east towards Arrow Bamboo Forest in groups.

There aren’t many things that can frighten Cold Beasts.

And even fewer things that would make a Beast King flee in fear.

Xia Hong turned his gaze toward the depths of Redwood Ridge, and his expression gradually became more serious.

"The exploration of Redwood Ridge is only halfway done; it’s not the time to resolve this issue now. Since the Beast King is no longer a threat, the next step should prioritize solving the Silver Mine problem, and of course, the eastern advance can also be put on the agenda!"

Xia Hong’s expression steadied, retracting his gaze, looking down at the army below, a smile on his face. As he withdrew the flames from his body, he slowly descended to the ground.

Xia Chuan, Yuwen Dao, Yuan Cheng, Luo Yuan, Hong Guang, Hong Tian, the Six Army Commanders, and all soldiers, now all focused their eyes on him, their expressions extremely excited, with a fervor in their eyes almost overflowing.

"Congratulations to the Lord for achieving great success at the Xianyang Level, may the Lord live forever, may the Great Xia live forever!"

Xia Chuan was the first to bow and salute Xia Hong, congratulating him for breaking through to the Xianyang Realm.

Yuwen Dao, Yuan Cheng, and the rest of the six thousand soldiers then realized and all saluted and bowed in unison toward Xia Hong, shouting in one voice.

"Congratulations to the Lord for achieving great success at the Xianyang Level, may the Lord live forever, may the Great Xia live forever!"

After their extremely excited shouts, they looked up at Xia Hong, the fire and reverence in their eyes showed no signs of diminishing.

This emotion naturally infected Xia Hong as well.

"Hahaha, thank you for your kind words, with my great success at the Xianyang Level, and the progress you Six Armies have made over the past five years, as long as we stand united, there will surely be a day when the phrase ’Great Xia lives forever’ becomes a reality!"

As long as we stand united, there will surely be a day when the phrase ’Great Xia lives forever’ becomes a reality!

Everyone stood solemnly, engraving this sentence firmly in their hearts.
