Chapter 402 - 194: A Tragic Scene, Ice Skin Jade Bone, Xia Hong’s True Strength

Chapter 402: Chapter 194: A Tragic Scene, Ice Skin Jade Bone, Xia Hong’s True Strength

"Gulp..."

...

The people who had just scattered and fled didn’t get far before turning back, including Yuwen Dao. They all looked back at the gigantic figure towering over the wooden hut, eyes filled with shock and terror.

"Whoosh..."

While these people were busy being shocked, Xia Hong had no such leisure!

The moment Snow Mane’s body enlarged, Xia Hong was also slightly startled. But realizing that there was no change in the spot where the broken blade was inserted, he swiftly reacted by withdrawing the blade and stabbing at the other eye.

Bang...

This time though, Snow Mane didn’t give him the chance.

It swung its head fiercely, flinging Xia Hong into the air, its two sharp, long tusks coming towards Xia Hong’s body with sudden ferocity.

Whoosh...

The fierce wind generated by Snow Mane’s enormous body tightened Xia Hong’s expression mid-air. He forcibly stabilized his fall, aimed at Snow Mane’s tusks, swung his broken blade against them for leverage, and flipped his body, gripping onto Snow Mane’s right front limb with one hand.

If it can’t be broken, then use brute force!

A fierce determination flashed in Xia Hong’s eyes. He bit the broken blade in his mouth, stomped his legs onto the ground, held his breath, and suddenly exerted strength with both hands.

"Haa... Ah..."

With a loud roar, Xia Hong’s eyes were bloodshot, veins bulging all over his body, as he pulled Snow Mane’s enormous body forward.

"Gulp... The leader... the leader’s... strength..."

"This Snow Mane must weigh fifty to sixty thousand pounds, right?"

"Dead or alive, none of us could move it, much less when it’s alive."

"Look at that Snow Mane, even with its other three legs struggling on the ground, it’s useless."

"The leader’s prowess..."

......

Leaving everyone’s astonishment aside for the moment.

The other three limbs of Snow Mane were continuously struggling and flailing on the ground, yet no matter what, it couldn’t break free from Xia Hong’s grasp.

After more than ten seconds, realizing it was futile, panic and fear finally crawled onto Snow Mane’s face.

On Xia Hong’s side, he began his assault.

He suddenly exerted force, flipping Snow Mane’s gigantic body and slamming it directly downwards.

Bang...

After an earth-shattering crash, Snow Mane’s body flipped and fell to the ground, lying on its back with all four legs in the air. Xia Hong’s hands still firmly clamped its right front limb, refusing to let go and even using the ground’s force to tug at it.

"Roar... Roar..."

Snow Mane, of course, wouldn’t surrender. Its body thrashed wildly on the ground, trying to roll over, while the other three limbs constantly attacked Xia Hong, attempting to escape.

Yet Xia Hong swayed left and right, evading its grasp.

And while dodging, Xia Hong could free a hand to continuously punch at Snow Mane’s arm joint.

Bang... Bang... Bang...

You didn’t need to hear to see how terrifying Xia Hong’s punches were.

The terrifying shockwaves from the punches not only made the thirty-odd houses in Jing Valley start to shake left and right, but even the people more than a hundred meters away could clearly feel the ground tremble.

"Who was it who said that in front of the leader, we’re no different from five or six-year-olds? I didn’t believe it then."

"More like no different from babies."

"The leader’s full-force punch could probably kill us from a distance."

"The leader is too strong."

......

"So this is the leader’s true strength!"

If those in the Logging Realm and Earth-digging Realm felt only reverence and excitement upon seeing Xia Hong’s current divine might;

Then Yuwen Dao felt shock and awe at this moment.

The weaker one’s cultivation, the less clear their perception of power.

Others might not be clear about the terrifying power Xia Hong was displaying at this moment, but Yuwen Dao knew.

"He Yuanhun once pushed back Chen Yingyuan and the others, but he didn’t have the ability to fight High-level Cold Beasts alone. The leader’s strength..."

For the first time, witnessing Xia Hong’s true strength directly, Yuwen Dao’s pupils were filled not only with shock and awe but also with deep admiration.

He even, like those in the Daxia Camp, felt for the first time a hint of fanaticism towards Xia Hong...
