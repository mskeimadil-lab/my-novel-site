Chapter 665: Chapter 278: Thousand-eyed Demon Sheep, Terrifying Power, and Spiritual Medicine

"Roar!"

After the muscles all over the Demon Sheep’s body stopped trembling, it let out an earth-shattering roar.

Immediately after, the seven people including Luo Yuan and Xu Ning, who were not yet fully grounded, as well as Chen Yingyuan and Peng Bo who were struggling to dodge under the Demon Sheep’s belly, felt something simultaneously, and glanced out of the corners of their eyes.

At that glance, the nine individuals immediately felt their scalps go numb and their bodies stiffen with shock.

What did they see!

The surface of the Demon Sheep was covered with snow-white bristles, and hundreds of eyes suddenly opened on its body.

The eyes were veined with blood, appearing extremely menacing from a distance with a scarlet hue, and inside were vertical golden pupils. The eyes on different parts were staring at the people in their vicinity, following them as they moved, sinister and mysterious to the extreme.

"It’s not Hundred Eyes Level, it’s a Thousand-eyed Demon Sheep! We’re done for!"

On the ground, Peng Bo raised his head to see countless pupils on the Demon Sheep’s belly, and then exchanged a glance with Chen Yingyuan not far away. They saw despair on each other’s faces.

The Demon Sheep had just shattered the trees within a mile radius to drive Luo Yuan and the seven down. With them falling from the trees, there was naturally no opportunity to draw their bows.

And it took this opportunity to open all its eyes on its body.

The purpose was obvious!

Luo Yuan and the seven were further away and still had a chance to escape.

The two of them were right under the Demon Sheep’s belly.

They had no chance to escape at all.

Whoosh whoosh whoosh......

In the next moment, just as Chen Peng and his companion thought, the thousand golden pupils on the Demon Sheep’s body suddenly shot out sharp golden beams.

Countless beams pierced through the snowy night, illuminating the surrounding area for miles in an instant.

Five people fell from the trees, Yuan Cheng, Xu Ning, Luo Yuan, and Yue Feng could not dodge and were at least pierced by four or five beams each, falling to the ground with nearly no ability to move;

Among the five, Yuwen Dao, who was slightly stronger, blocked a critical hit with his broad sword, but his shoulder was still struck by a beam, slashing off a large chunk of flesh;

Chen Yingba and Wu Tianxing, being the closest, met the worst fate, with their shoulders, chests, abdomens, and lower limbs each hit by two or three beams on average, their bodies bore over a dozen bloody holes. Even before hitting the ground, they were unconscious, and upon landing, blood gushed from their wounds, staining their clothes red;

The seven more distant individuals were already like this, so Chen Peng and his companion, who were just under the Demon Sheep’s belly, were surely doomed without an unexpected turn of events.

However, surprisingly, not only were the two of them not dead, but their injuries were the lightest.

"Leader!"

"Leader."

A shadow stood in front of them, blocking all the golden beams shot from the Demon Sheep’s belly, and that shadow even grabbed them and threw them directly beside Yuwen Dao.

The two, who had narrowly escaped death, saw that it was Xia Hong who saved them, and they were overjoyed and shouted in excitement.

But the next second, realizing the Thousand-eyed Demon Sheep’s power was even more terrifying than Xia Hong, they immediately prepared to issue a warning.

Unfortunately, before they could speak, Xia Hong spoke first.

"Didn’t you say it was no problem? A Demon Sheep, and you ended up like this?"

Hearing Xia Hong’s displeased tone, Chen Yingyuan and Peng Bo quickly explained.

"Leader, we misjudged, it’s a Thousand-eyed Demon Sheep."

Earlier, Peng Bo thought this Demon Sheep was just about to break through to Thousand Eyes Level, but having witnessed the scene of it braking through the trees within a mile radius, it was clear they had been deceived by this Demon Sheep.

"The strength of a High-level Demon Sheep is measured by the number of eyes it has. Hundred Eyes Level is most common, with power between fifteen to eighteen bristles. This Demon Sheep’s eyes exceed a thousand, its strength is definitely above twenty bristles..."

He quickly spoke out the Demon Sheep’s strength, trying to persuade Xia Hong to flee, but he was interrupted even before he could finish.

Bam!

He was interrupted by a high kick from Xia Hong.

After throwing them, Xia Hong remained under the Demon Sheep’s belly. He hadn’t even drawn the knife from his waist, merely struck it with an upward kick into the belly.

Then, a breathtaking scene unfolded.

The massive body of the Demon Sheep, nearly thirty meters long, was kicked into the sky by Xia Hong, ascending twenty to thirty meters high.

It wasn’t over yet, Xia Hong leaped off the ground, flew over the Demon Sheep’s head, with a blood jade-colored horizontal blade appearing in his hand.

Slash!

With a reverse grip on the horizontal blade, Xia Hong fiercely plunged down, piercing straight through the Demon Sheep’s neck, the terrifying force driving its body into the ground with an earth-shattering crash.

Bam!

The Demon Sheep had just shattered the trees within a mile, but the shockwave from Xia Hong’s blow was even more terrifying, spreading over half a mile outward.

Crack...

"Roar........"

The sound of bones cracking at the Demon Sheep’s neck was incredibly crisp, and this time its roar was even more furious than before, carrying a hint of pain, bordering on frenzy.

Stamp stamp stamp......

It was being firmly pressed into the ground by Xia Hong, thrashing its limbs dozens of times, and realizing its strength could not break free from Xia Hong, it let out a sudden roar, the golden eyes all over its body opened once more.

The golden pupils in the eyes without exception were fixated on Xia Hong.

It was determined to fight to the death!

Whoosh whoosh whoosh whoosh......

Hundreds of beams shot from the eyes on the Demon Sheep’s back, all aiming for Xia Hong.

Hiss...

Xia Hong sneered, gripping the horizontal blade with both hands, dodging the beams while encircling the Demon Sheep’s neck, abruptly pulling down from the left.
