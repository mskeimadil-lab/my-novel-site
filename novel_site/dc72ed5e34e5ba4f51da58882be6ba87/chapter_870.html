Chapter 870: Chapter 334: Hanqiong’s Seven Divisions, Moao Mountain, Hearing Beishuo Again

Lu Dong and the other six stood up with a rush, their faces full of delight.

These four consecutive pieces of good news were truly abundant in content.

However, it was clear that Xiao Yuan’s good news was not over yet.

He then pulled out a green porcelain bottle from his pocket, excitedly saying, "The Lord is thrilled, directly rewarding every Cold Resistance Level in the camp with four Yangyuan Pills each. There are a total of eighteen people here in Hanqiong, and the Camp Supply Department gave me seventy-two pills to pass on to you."

After speaking, he poured out four pills and then directly handed the porcelain bottle to Zhu Shun.

Zhu Shun was the Camp Supply and Military Equipment officer, so it was naturally his job to distribute the Yangyuan Pills.

Lu Dong and the others looked at the Yangyuan Pills, their eyes filled with even more delight.

The Yangyuan Pill was considered extremely precious cultivation material for them.

Moreover, each person received four pills...

"A thousand years for the Lord, a thousand years for His Highness!"

Lu Dong couldn’t help but bow toward Xia City’s southern direction, and the other six also solemnly bowed toward the south, following his lead.

"A thousand years for the Lord, a thousand years for His Highness!"

After everyone sat back down, Zhu Shun quickly distributed the Yangyuan Pills.

Lu Dong held his four Yangyuan Pills, struggling to suppress the excitement in his heart.

"Xiao Yuan, did you see the teams from Wuyuan and Long Mountain this time?"

Xiao Yuan clearly understood what Lu Dong wanted to know. His face first showed a hint of shame, then he nodded and said, "I saw them, and I personally witnessed the announcement of all station Profit Creation Values by Director Qiu Sizheng. In July, Long Mountain ranked first with 3,620,000, Wuyuan second with 1,920,000, Zhaoyang third with 820,000, Xiling fourth with 690,000, and Hanqiong was only fifth..., with Beiyuan in last place having only 150,000."

Not only him, but Lu Dong and the others also showed expressions of shame on their faces.

Let’s not talk about Long Mountain; Wuyuan was almost four times Hanqiong’s.

The key was that the stations without Seven Departments’ jurisdiction, Zhaoyang and Xiling, were also higher than them!

This was simply too outrageous.

As if the mood of Lu Dong and the others wasn’t low enough, Xiao Yuan continued with a bitter smile, saying, "I heard from the Deputy Martial Xu An of Wuyuan Camp Supply Department that Xu Shouzheng discovered seven giant camps north of Songyang Ridge at the beginning of this month, and they have already started negotiations for merging. In the next few months, Wuyuan’s Profit Creation Value will surely reach new heights and might even compete with Long Mountain!"

Pa...

Lu Dong finally couldn’t sit still, and he stood up directly, speaking solemnly:

"No, no, we can’t drag on like this. If we continue to waste time, not to mention comparing with Wuyuan, all the lower stations will surpass Hanqiong. When we return to Xia City, how will I face the Lord and the Minister?"

After speaking, he turned to He Xing and said, "He Xing, allocate manpower, gather all Earth-digging Realm personnel in Hanqiong first, we must prioritize clearing that..."

"Sir, the tunnel is open, the tunnel is open!"

Lu Dong’s words were interrupted again by the joyful voice from outside the door.

A person covered in mud, looking somewhat disheveled, rushed in from outside, saluted everyone, and then looked at Lu Dong, saying, "Master Fan instructed me to inform you all that the tunnel has been opened, and the end has been reached."

This... this... is indeed quite a coincidence.

Just as he said to allocate manpower for excavation, the tunnel had already been opened!

Lu Dong’s expression was a bit surprised, but it quickly turned into joy.

With the tunnel opened, exploration of the area above Twin Dragon Mountain could now take place.

New resource points could be developed soon!

Lu Yang was overjoyed, standing up directly and commanding, "Zhao Hu, Zhu Shun, Wang Tao, take the elite team with me."

"Alright!"

The three responded with faces full of joy, and then left the hall to assemble the team.

Lu Yang then looked up and called out to the outside, "Qingshan, inform Team One that they don’t need to go out hunting tonight, have them gather at the main building."

"Understood!"

A response came immediately from outside the door, followed by the sound of quickly descending the stairs.

"Two Advanced Teams and two Intermediate Teams that are about to be promoted, that should be enough. It’s just a reconnaissance, shouldn’t be too dangerous!"

The resources at the Hanqiong station were not favorable, and the Hunting Teams willing to come here were already few, and their strength was not great. The whole station only had two Advanced Hunting Teams, one was his East Sun team, and the other was Zhao Hu’s Steel Mane team.

Zhu Shun’s Zhao Long team and Wang Tao’s Zhuhuan team were both Intermediate, but each had three Cold Resistance Levels. They were actually not far from being promoted to Advanced.

"Last year, Han Feng took more than a thousand people through the secret passage, there’s no reason we can’t. So let’s first explore the situation then report back to the leader in Xia City!"

Lu Dong pondered for a moment, made up his mind, and quickly walked out of the hall.

The entrance to the secret passage was right beneath the main building.

It was already just after nightfall, and people at the station hadn’t had a chance to go out yet.

When Lu Dong reached downstairs, all the elites from the four hunting teams were already gathered.

"Sir, Team One with thirty people, all are present."

Deputy Captain Hong Qingshan came forward directly, indicating that everyone was present.

The East Sun team was already an Advanced Hunting Team, with a full complement of 100 people. Lu Dong emulated other Advanced Hunting Teams in the camp and divided the members into three teams.

Team One consisted of the most elite thirty members, all with cultivation above the Earth-digging Realm Limit, responsible for hunting Advanced Cold Beasts; Team Two had thirty members with lower cultivation, all with basic strength over 20,000 pounds, responsible for hunting mid and low-level Cold Beasts; Team Three was the weakest, solely responsible for fulfilling the team’s mining quota.

Thirty elite members from Zhao Hu’s Steel Mane team also came, and Zhu Shun’s and Wang Tao’s teams each brought twenty people, all clad in Hundred Forged Level Lei Kui Golden Armor, similar to his own team members, all with cultivation above the Earth-digging Realm Limit.
