Chapter 677 - 282: Humiliation and the Formation of a New Army (Part 2)

Chapter 677: Chapter 282: Humiliation and the Formation of a New Army (Part 2)

"Old Third, you’ve recently improved your basic strength quite a bit. Be honest with big brother, how did you, Li Hu, manage to do it?"

Yang Zhong, with a temperament even fiercer than Li Hu, was furious at such humiliation. Even knowing he wasn’t as strong as the other party, he roared in anger, "Yang Gan, if you have the guts, kill me. As long as I’m alive, there will be a day when I kill you."

It wasn’t the first time hearing Yang Zhong utter such words. A flash of murderous intent crossed Yang Gan’s eyes, but he quickly restrained himself, sneering, "A waste who hasn’t broken the thirty-thousand jin limit by the age of twenty-two. If it weren’t for the camp rules, you would’ve been dead countless times by now. Even with another ten years, you will only be beneath my feet, understand, you waste?"

Bang!

"Waste, you, the waste!"

Yang Gan’s temper was extremely volatile, and probably feeling unsatisfied, he cursed "waste" while ruthlessly stepping on various parts of Yang Zhong’s body.

Yet, it could be seen that Yang Gan didn’t really intend to kill; otherwise, with the kick that just sent Yang Zhong flying, if he’d used his full strength, Yang Zhong wouldn’t have survived.

Not killing, but the humiliation reached the extreme.

"If you have the guts, kill me, kill me."

"You bastard, kill me, kill me!"

"Beast, I will definitely kill you, kill your mother."

......

Yang Zhong, with his head buried in the ground, continued to roar, refusing to yield even in pain, seemingly provoking Yang Gan on purpose, eventually voicing what’s deep in his heart.

Hearing the threat to kill his mother, Yang Gan genuinely got angry, a murderous gleam in his eye, and he lifted his foot high, clearly intending to deliver a fatal blow.

"Li Yuanqing, you’ve broken through to Cold Resistance Level already?"

But at this moment, Li Hu from the other side suddenly spoke.

This inquiry seemed to provoke something in Yang Gan, who recalled something, and although the foot went down, he retracted seventy to eighty percent of his strength.

Yang Zhong’s roars also stopped, turning to look at Li Yuanqing, his lying body slightly shivered, a trace of despair flashing on his face.

Evidently, Li Hu’s question just now was due to taking Li Yuanqing’s punch, sensing Li Yuanqing’s strength.

Two years ago, Li Yuanqing already broke through the Earth-digging Realm Limit and started using Beast Blood to reshape his skin. Li Hu’s question implied something obvious.

"You’ve got some insight!"

Li Yuanqing’s voice was full of pride as he slowly walked up to Li Hu, using his foot to kick him out of the ground, then squatting in front of him to continue:

"Li Hu, you and Yang Zhong have been cultivating unusually fast recently. You must’ve hidden something. If you’re wise, tell me quickly, otherwise, if I really use some means, you and Yang Zhong won’t even have the chance to hunt in Long Mountain in the future, believe it?"

Seeing Li Hu remain silent, he seemed to recall something, a cold gleam flashing in his eyes as he said, "Right, you’ve broken through the Earth-digging Realm Limit, and so has Yang Zhong. Next, you need Beast Blood..."

Yang Gan immediately picked up on Li Yuanqing’s words, mocking, "Two little wildlings dreaming of exchanging Beast Blood!"

Lying on the ground, Li Hu and Yang Zhong’s expressions darkened significantly upon hearing Yang Gan’s words.

Yang Gan’s father, Yang Fengping, not only the strongest Divine Archer of the camp but also in charge of all elite Hunting Teams in Long Mountain, and the Beast Blood Bank was managed by his mother, Li Xuanqiu.

Li Xuanqiu, who was the enemy that killed Yang Zhong’s mother, naturally wouldn’t allow them to exchange Beast Blood like other normal Earth-digging Realm Limits.

So Yang Gan was right. Their desire to exchange for Beast Blood was indeed a dream.

"How about this, as long as you tell me the secret to speeding up cultivation, in the future, I won’t stop you from going to the warehouse to exchange Beast Blood, how about that?"

Li Yuanqing pretended to deliberate and offered conditions, also giving Yang Gan a glance, clearly signaling him to cooperate.

Yang Gan immediately responded, a flicker in his eyes as he said, "Exactly, Old Third, we are still family, after all. As long as you tell me the secret, I won’t trouble you anymore, how about that?"

Hehe......

Li Hu and Yang Zhong couldn’t help but smirk inwardly.

Growing up under Zhu Rou and Li Xuanqiu’s noses, how could they be easily deceived? Of course, they wouldn’t be fooled by Li Yuanqing and Yang Gan’s petty tricks.

The two remained silent, and the atmosphere suddenly turned cold.

Li Yuanqing and Yang Gan waited for a long time without a response, realizing that their tricks had been seen through by the two, their faces turned to anger.

"Fine, if you’re not going to talk, then don’t. Anyway, you two wastes have never been in my eyes, but..."

Li Yuanqing’s tone gradually grew sinister, continuing, "If you thought joining the Longyou Army would solve everything, you’d be dead wrong. Last night, the camp council decided to establish a new Longyou Army. I’ve already been appointed as one of the two Commanding Officers of the new army. Even if you join the new army, you won’t escape my grasp."

Yang Gan couldn’t help but speak now, grinning at Yang Zhong on the ground:

"I’m also close to breaking through. You won’t escape my grasp either!"

Upon hearing this, Li Hu and Yang Zhong’s expressions turned grim.

Today is the first of June, and yesterday was the last day of May, indeed the monthly meeting day of Long Mountain. Li Yuanqing likely wasn’t lying.

The Longyou Army, a thousand to form an army, establishing a new army, means that future Earth-digging Realm Limits will all be incorporated into the new army.
