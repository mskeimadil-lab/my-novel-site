Chapter 418 - 199: Good News Keeps Coming for the Hong Gang Family (Part 2)

Chapter 418: Chapter 199: Good News Keeps Coming for the Hong Gang Family (Part 2)

Husband Hong Gang is the captain of the Green Wolf Team of the Hunting Department. He earns over a thousand contribution points every month and bought plot number eight early on, building their house there. When they moved out of the wooden building, the envious looks from their neighbors lingered in Cheng Fang’s memory.

Eldest son Hong Gang also broke through to the Earth-digging Realm last month, smoothly entering his father’s hunting team, ensuring future contribution points would be no problem.

Even though the younger son is only ten years this year, his basic strength already exceeds three thousand pounds. With a bit of effort, he might break the Logging Realm record. His talent is higher than his father and older brother, though he is a bit mischievous, fearless, and prone to causing minor troubles.

Though her cultivation is only at the Logging Realm, her husband and eldest son could bring home a few trees from their outings, which was enough to submit to the Camp Supply Station, so she had no need to go out.

Food, Crystal Fruit, clothes, household items, housing, and even fearing she’d tire from chopping trees, Hong Gang and the eldest son recently got a wooden cart for the family...

They lack nothing in their daily life, something Cheng Fang could scarcely dream of in the past. This family of four is the envy of all in the camp.

Cheng Fang finds joy in these fulfilled days.

"Dad, I want to go cultivate at the Martial Arts Hall."

As soon as Hong Ye spoke, the three who were quietly eating immediately stopped, all eyes focused on him.

Cheng Fang quickly noticed her husband Hong Gang’s face darken, and hurriedly knocked the younger son’s head with her chopsticks, slightly scolding, "You rascal, you just promised me you wouldn’t anger your dad, and now it’s only been a short time..."

Hong Gang waved his hand to stop his wife and looked at his younger son, saying, "Previously, I told you to go, but you refused, claiming that even without going, your cultivation would be faster than others. What changed your mind?"

Cheng Fang remembered too, at the beginning of last month, when Hong Gang formed his team and frequently needed to hunt outside, he offered the Martial Arts Hall spot to Hong Ye. However, Hong Ye boasted that his talent surpassed others, declaring that even without going, he’d be faster.

As a mother, she knew her younger son just wanted to play. Every night, he’d gather a group of children, fooling around behind the valley. Cheng Fang understood that the chance to cultivate at the Martial Arts Hall was rare, and tried to persuade him earnestly several times, but Hong Ye wouldn’t listen.

Last month, Hong Gang even lost his temper over this, kicking Hong Ye a few times. Yet, this stubborn child resisted, refusing to go, leaving no choice but for Cheng Fang to go herself.

Only after going for a day did Cheng Fang refuse to return.

Inside the Martial Arts Hall, apart from prominent figures, were young men and women, and even children around ten years old. Middle-aged women like her were nonexistent.

In the end, Hong Gang’s spot got wasted.

This matter angered Hong Gang for several days.

So when the younger son proposed going to the Martial Arts Hall, Hong Gang naturally showed a sour face.

Meanwhile, Hong Ye realized his predicament, heard his father’s stern inquiry, shrank his neck, and said, "Lu Dong’s record has been broken. The youngest Logging Realm now is named Zhao Yuanwu, ten years and nine months old, just three months older than me."

Ten years and nine months?

Hong Gang and his son Hong Mu hadn’t focused on the Logging Realm situation at the camp for a long time, but hearing this news from Hong Ye, they were instantly shocked.

Cheng Fang was different; with ample free time, she often gathered with a few women, not discussing husbands, but comparing children, eagerly learning about such matters.

"That Zhao Yuanwu, I’ve heard of him. He’s Zhao Long’s eldest son."

Zhao Long’s eldest son...

Upon hearing this, Hong Gang and Hong Mu’s expressions softened, nodding slightly, instantly less surprised.

"I’ve heard the Falcon Hunting Team has started seeking intermediate Snowmanes. The Falcon and Zhao Long Teams are the leaders of the third tier!"

Upon hearing the eldest son Hong Mu’s words, Hong Gang nodded; he naturally knew these circumstances.

Currently, the Hunting Department consists of over fifty teams, divided into roughly four tiers.

The first tier undoubtedly includes the Cloud Jiao and Long Wu teams led by the Minister and Director respectively, the top intermediate teams;

The second tier consists of six teams, including the Deputy Director Luo Yuan’s Tiger Leopard Team, along with Divine Martial, Vibrant Martial, Heroic Martial, Mighty Martial, and Yuan Cheng’s Dragon Slayer Team.

These six teams have successfully hunted intermediate Cold Beasts, waiting to gather five types to upgrade to intermediate.

The third tier includes ten teams, apart from Zhao Long’s Falcon Hunting Team, his brothers Zhao Hu’s Steel Mane, Zhao Bao’s Golden Eagle Team are also part of it, alongside Huang Yong’s Zhao Long Team, Yue Feng’s Mountain River Team, etc.

Zhao Long and Huang Yong are attempting to hunt intermediate Snowmanes recently, which Hong Gang heard about. Falcon and Zhao Long are indeed third-tier leaders.

The remaining thirty-odd teams are basically categorized in the fourth tier.

Hong Gang’s own Green Wolf Team belongs to this tier.

With Zhao Long’s strength, he has ample contribution value, naturally offering better cultivation conditions for his son Zhao Yuanwu who breaks through the Logging Realm earlier than other children, which is understandable.

Generally, the meat quota is the same for everyone, and the exchange price for contribution values is very low. All children should have no disparity.
