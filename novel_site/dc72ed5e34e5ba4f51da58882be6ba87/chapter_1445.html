Chapter 1445: Chapter 460: Golden Bone Melting Furnace, Reasons for Slow Progress, Army Departure and Arrival_3

"Perhaps it’s some other formidable deceitful object, merely employing methods similar to the Nightmare Monster. First, we should go in to understand what the Blood Cold Beast and Copper Bone Corpse Demon are all about before drawing any conclusions."

Xia Hong slightly shook his head. Ahead, Fan Qinghu had already begun assigning tasks to the people below, and he listened attentively.

Fan Qinghu’s arrangements were not complicated. A total of a hundred thousand troops came out this time, plus a hundred Xianyang Level warriors. The troops remained stationary, with fifty Xianyang Level warriors assigned to guard the area. Only the remaining fifty people were called forward by Fan Qinghu.

These fifty were clearly the main force responsible for entering the Blood Miasma Zone this time.

Xia Hong was naturally among these fifty.

"The Blood Miasma Zone we need to clear this time is numbered 21, which is the area of four kilometers wide and five kilometers deep in front of us, lasting a total of two months. As per previous rules, each entry allows a maximum stay of five days. The emergency supplies, the Golden Bone Flute, Clear Jade Pill, Advanced Deceit Detection Stone, Qingxun Treasure Bow, and its matching fifty Silver Dragon Arrows, were distributed to you last night. Unexpected situations abound once inside..."

Listening to Fan Qinghu’s extensive briefing on various precautions, Xia Hong’s expression became slightly peculiar. This pre-departure orientation instantly brought back memories of his early days leading expeditions into Redwood Ridge.

Ultimately, Xianyang Level warriors are just somewhat stronger humans. Facing the perilous Blood Miasma Plain is quite similar to when he confronted Redwood Ridge at the Earth-digging Realm.

The five items Fan Qinghu mentioned were given to him by Cai Yunlan last night.

Emergency supplies refer to things used in unforeseen circumstances.

The Golden Bone Flute, literally made of Beast King Golden Bone, is evidently for summoning assistance and communication;

Clear Jade Pill is an advanced version of Heart Cleansing Pill. Xianyang Level warriors can stay in the Blood Miasma Zone for a maximum of five days, so if an unexpected situation exceeds this time limit, one Clear Jade Pill can extend the time for half a month. Each person was given ten by Fan Qinghu;

The Advanced Deceit Detection Stone is self-explanatory, a cyan stone more advanced than the ordinary red Deceit Detection Stone, with greater sensitivity to Deceitful Monsters;

As for the Qingxun Treasure Bow and Silver Dragon Arrow, they are quite intriguing!

Xia Hong took down a cyan-colored Strong Bow from his back, his eyes brightening.

"The Qingxun Treasure Tree in the Bone-Eroding Path is truly for bow making. The cyan vine sinew connecting the tree body is perfect for making bowstrings. Unfortunately, Cai Qiu doesn’t seem to emphasize refining materials; these cyan vine sinews were only simply processed to make bows, with a pull strength of only 3 Jun, suitable only for emergencies..."

Jun pull strength can easily overpower those at the Cold Resistance Level.

But now they are entering the Blood Miasma Plain.

Judging by the stance of Fan Qinghu and others, whether it’s Cold Beasts or Deceitful Monsters, the strength within is evidently formidable. To expect miraculous results from a mere 3 Jun pull strength Strong Bow is wishful thinking.

"Yesterday, the 148 Xianyang Level warriors seen in the hall, the weakest had a strength exceeding 9 Jun. The fifty people present today are among the stronger batch, all with strength exceeding 15 Jun. From this, it can be deduced that the previous estimation of only 300 Xianyang Level warriors by Cai Qiu is likely incorrect. The truly weak Xianyang Level warriors don’t even have the qualification to come here..."

Xia Hong took a slight breath, gazing at the Blood Miasma Plain, just within reach on the southern side, and his eyes gradually grew solemn.

"In any normal area, a 20-square-kilometer region, let alone fifty Xianyang Level warriors, even a single Xianyang Level warrior entering alone could wreak havoc in no time. But here, it’s the Blood Miasma Plain..."

After just now experiencing the deceptive methods of the Nightmare Monster, Xia Hong naturally won’t view the Blood Miasma Plain through the lens of common sense.

"The Nightmare Monster’s methods cannot be divorced from spatial aspects. If the Blood Miasma Plain possesses such abilities, what is seen from the outside with the naked eye will be entirely different from the experiences felt upon truly entering!"

Blood Cold Beast, Copper Bone Corpse Demon, what exactly are these things...

Xia Hong pondered slightly, his expression solemn, yet internally, a strange sense of excitement arose.

This feeling of danger, he hadn’t experienced in a long time!
