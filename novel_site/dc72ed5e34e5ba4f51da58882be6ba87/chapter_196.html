Chapter 196: Chapter 136 He Yuanhun, Yuwen Hu, Yuwen Dao (Three in One)_4

"Leader Xia Hong!"

Soon, Yuwen Dao tossed over the sealed bag of Cold Beast blood.

Xia Hong reached out and caught it steadily, estimating it to be about thirty or so pounds.

Considering the previous handling of low-level Cold Beasts, and estimating from the size of an Intermediate Cold Beast, the blood volume seemed about right.

After receiving the blood, Xia Hong did not leave immediately. He looked at Yuwen Dao, his expression gradually becoming solemn, and spoke in a deep voice:

"Since the misunderstanding tonight has been resolved, I shouldn’t say much. However, Brother Yuwen, as you yourself just mentioned, you also believe that our two camps should maintain good neighborly relations, then please, help me convey a few words back.

The previous Rodent swarm incident was entirely a misunderstanding, and any discerning person can see clearly that we, of the Great Xia, have no intention of bearing a grudge against the Mirror Immortal Camp.

Our two camps being neighbors is a fact, and though we may not speak of living in harmony, we should at least avoid unnecessary disputes and conflicts.

In the future, I will also ensure that the people of our camp do not venture into any area of the Arrow Bamboo Forest, and I ask that the people of the Mirror Immortal Camp do not cross the boundary to intrude upon our Redwood Ridge.

What happened tonight should happen only once.

If it happens again, the Great Xia Camp will show no mercy; do not say I did not warn you!"

"Alright, Leader Xia Hong’s words will definitely be passed on."

Yuwen Dao quickly nodded in response, and when he looked up again, he found that Xia Hong and his three companions had already retreated into the southern dense forest.

At the same time, rustling noises came from several surrounding large trees, clearly from the archers Xia Hong had brought.

"1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9..."

Yuwen Dao closed his eyes, counting silently, and only after the noises from the trees completely disappeared did he open his eyes, looking in the direction where the people of the Great Xia had vanished, and heaved a deep sigh of relief.

"Nine Earth-digging Realm archers, thankfully there was no conflict!"

He glanced at the wound on his shoulder, a flash of relief appearing on his face.

The change in Xia Hong’s expression just now had been closely monitored by him, and if the other party had changed his mind, the outcome tonight would have been completely different.

The other nine people also relaxed a lot at this moment, but most of them still had a hint of discontent on their faces, clearly still brooding over the half of the Cold Beast blood.

"Alright, your lives are saved, what are you sulking for?"

As soon as Yuwen Dao finished speaking, the nine of them immediately burst into laughter.

One of them even ran to the back of a large tree on the north side and slowly dragged out a huge object.

It turned out to be a dead Snow Mane corpse.

"Hahaha, this Xia Hong would never have expected that we actually had another prey hidden here."

The person dragged the Snow Mane next to the Demon Sheep, unable to help but laugh out loud.

Upon hearing this, Yuwen Dao turned his head to look in the direction Xia Hong and his companions had left, his face also gradually revealing a smug smile.

"Hahahaha, if I weren’t afraid this Snow Mane would also be discovered, I wouldn’t have handed over half of the Cold Beast blood so easily. But this Leader of the Great Xia is very smart and must not be underestimated..."

Yuwen Dao turned back to look at the nine people, a deep glint flashing in the depths of his eyes.

"Alright, pack up and leave quickly. Let’s head back to report to the Great Leader and Second Leader; from now on we will have to engage with the Great Xia Camp."

The group quickly packed up the two pieces of prey and also carried away those who were unconscious or had lost their lives, and only then did they follow Yuwen Dao towards the outskirts of the Redwood Ridge on the east side.

......

On the other side, Xia Hong, who had obtained half a bag of Cold Beast blood, led Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and eleven others to find Zhu Shun, Qiu Peng, and four others, happily returning to the valley base.
