Chapter 1071: Chapter 376: Primordial Soul Pill and Rebirth Mantra, Red Blood Demon Dragon, Tremendous Upheaval (Part 2)

They originally intended to continue reciting the final segment of the scripture, but that Bhikkhu was directly killed by Li Xuanling. Naturally, they would not know the final content of the scripture after that.

They were all ordinary people, with the highest cultivation only at the Logging Realm. They could clearly distinguish that Li Xuanling on the high platform was far beyond their imagination in strength, so they could only stand there dumbfounded, wide-eyed and unsure what to do.

"This green pill is not the same as the Fire Melting Pills, Zen Gold Powder, or Cold Clearing Beads previously taken out by Dajue Temple; this is something that can kill!"

Xia Chuan, along with the six people from Yuan Cheng, jumped onto the high platform of the main building and looked at the pupil of the Bhikkhu’s corpse on the ground, which was still leaking spiritual light, the pupils trembling incessantly.

Xiang Liang and Bai Shanqiu took out the green pill that Dajue Temple had distributed to them before the battle, and their expressions were equally filled with panic.

"Is it just the Baiping Village like this, or are the more than sixty village-level camps controlled by Dajue Temple all experiencing this now?"

Upon hearing Xia Chuan’s words, Xiang Liang immediately reacted and said solemnly, "For the past six months, the combination of the Fire Melting Pills, Zen Gold Powder, and Cold Clearing Beads with the power of the scriptures has spread throughout the Nine Towns. Everyone knows about these items in the village-level camps controlled by Dajue Temple. If they handed out these green pills to everyone in the village-level camps before the battle in Bashang and then sent out Bhikkhus to lead them in reciting the scriptures..."

Everyone present instantly had their expressions frozen.

"The Lord’s guess just now was right! Dajue Temple doesn’t just want Bashang’s 1.5 million people; they don’t intend to spare the people in these sixty-plus village-level camps either."

Whoosh.....

As Yue Feng finished speaking, a fierce wind sound came from above.

Everyone immediately looked up at the sky, their pupils suddenly contracting.

Countless trails of blue spiritual light were flying swiftly from the sky in the north, rapidly gathering towards the south, which is where the Bashang Town they left a while ago is.

The spiritual light from the eyes of the gray Bhikkhu who just died on the ground hovered in the air for a moment, as if sensing something, then followed the rest of the spiritual light, hastening towards Bashang Town.

Li Xuanling’s face changed rapidly with worry and finally couldn’t help but be concerned about Xia Hong. He ordered everyone in a deep voice, "I’ll go back to Bashang to notify the Lord. Xia Chuan, you rush back to Lower River Village at full speed. Bai Shanqiu stays here to watch. The rest of you disperse and check the other villages. Save as many as you can."

"Yes, madam!"

Xia Chuan and the others, knowing the urgency of the matter, quickly nodded in agreement.

Li Xuanling headed back alone to Bashang City in the south, while Xia Chuan continued north. Besides Bai Shanqiu, who stayed to guard Baiping Village, the other five also parted ways after a brief discussion.

.........

As the sky filled with azure light in the sixty plus villages occupied by Dajue Temple in Jiangxia and Bashang, the eighteen households in the two villages of Qinghepu were not idle either.

"What is that azure light?"

In Lower River Village, Liu Yuan, donned in Lei Kui Golden Armor, stood on top of the main building, gazing toward the continuously rising azure light in the north. He observed the azure light gathering toward the southeast with a puzzled expression.

Boom!

The rooftop of the main building suddenly shuddered as two individuals clad in Thousand Forged Thunder Kui Golden Armor leaped onto the rooftop, walking quickly toward Liu Yuan and bowing:

"Scout camp Colonel Wang Yuan, reporting to the Vice Commander!"

"First Camp Colonel Zhu Xing, reporting to the Vice Commander!"

Upon hearing their voices, Liu Yuan immediately turned and urgently asked, "What is the situation over at Qinghe Village, and how many people from Dajue Temple are engaged in battle?"

Only after speaking did he notice that Wang Yuan and Zhu Xing’s pupils were filled with deep horror, and their expressions were even more bewildered than his.

Wang Yuan spoke first, "Sir, everyone in Qinghe Village just took the green pill and then recited the scriptures along with a group of Zen Temple Bhikkhus. After finishing the scripture, it seemed like everyone had a blue spiritual light extracted from within; these lights flew out from their eyes, hovered in the sky for a moment, and then all flew toward the southeast.

After the blue lights were extracted from their bodies, they immediately died. Everyone, including the Zen Temple Bhikkhus, perished; not a single person survived!"

All dead...

Upon hearing Wang Yuan’s words, Liu Yuan’s pupils contracted abruptly. He quickly took a cloth bag from his waist, opened it, and seeing the tens of thousands of green pills inside, his face instantly filled with shock.

"So the more than twenty Zen Temple Bhikkhus who came here earlier planned to distribute these green pills to the people of Lower River Village and then lead them in chanting scriptures. If we weren’t here, the more than fifty thousand people in Lower River Village..."

"Would all be dead, not a single one spared!"

Wang Yuan took over Liu Yuan’s statement, his voice incredibly somber.

Recalling the scene he glimpsed earlier in Qinghe Village, he still felt unable to recover, with his pupil filled with lingering terror.

Suddenly remembering something, he turned and signaled Zhu Xing.

Beside him, Zhu Xing, unable to hold back, seeing Wang Yuan’s prompt, hurriedly saluted Liu Yuan, "Sir, an hour ago, a Xianyang Level from the Eight Towns directly destroyed the Great Awakening Hall. Fearing that those people would notice, I didn’t dare go close, so I couldn’t clearly see the situation. I only vaguely saw fifteen people acting simultaneously.

After the Great Awakening Hall was destroyed and submerged into Chilong Lake, then Chilong Lake..."

Zhu Xing paused, exchanged a glance with the nearby Wang Yuan, and, with deep fear reflected in his pupils, continued, "Chilong Lake suddenly erupted with billowing black smoke, and a Demon Dragon, about a thousand feet long, covered in blood-colored scales with four claws and green eyes, flew out from the lake.
