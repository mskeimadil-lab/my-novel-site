Chapter 823: Chapter 322: The Cold Beast Clans Joining Forces for Treasure, White Dew Gu’s Panic (Part 4)

"I’ve already started deploying the people from Longyou, and the Lord might not be aware, but in the past two months, quite a few have broken through to the Cold Resistance Level in the camp!"

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong’s eyes immediately brightened. He realized he hadn’t been paying attention to the number of people at the Cold Resistance Level in the camp and asked immediately, "How many?"

With an excited tone, Xia Chuan said, "The entire camp has a total of seventeen. The Cloud Dragon Army alone accounts for four. Another nine come from the five hunting teams: Long Wu, Tulong, Steel Mane, Tiger Leopard, and Hunting Eagle. The remaining four are from the people merged from Longyou this time."

Seventeen have broken through, and with the eighty-nine from early October, the number of people at the Cold Resistance Level in Great Xia has already exceeded three digits, reaching 106!

After arriving at this number, everyone’s expressions brightened significantly.

Xia Hong was no exception, his eyes lit up as he said, "Not bad, if this breakthrough speed can be maintained, it might not be long before we can completely clear out the Yanglu Realm!"

The most difficult issue with the Yanglu Realm currently is the advanced Cold Beasts banding together. With enough Cold Resistance Levels, plus the support of the Cloud Dragon and Long Wu armies, the hope is considerable.

"Clean up here, drag these ten prey back to the Beehive, and Yuwen Dao, take them back to Xia City. There are still twenty-five days left in the month; before the first of January, improve the supply of Beast Blood and High-level Beast Blood in the Camp Supply Department. Also, allocate more spots from the Martial Arts Academy and do our best to assist in the Earth-digging Realm Limit breakthroughs in the camp to see how many can break through. With enough manpower, we might resolve the troubles here before next year."

Upon hearing this, Xia Chuan and the others immediately bowed and replied, "Understood!"

Currently, Great Xia does not lack Earth-digging Realm Limit people. The Cloud Dragon Army and the soon-to-be-formed Dragon Martial Army soldiers number two thousand, plus the members of each Advanced Hunting Team department below, there are at least two thousand five hundred.

With sufficient Beast Blood supply, supported by the Martial Arts Academy spots, more and more people are breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level—it happens almost naturally.

As long as there’s enough manpower, resolving the Cold Beasts in the Yanglu Realm is not a problem at all!

"The only concern is whether the White Dew Gu will cause any disturbances during this process. I will remain stationed at the Beehive and check in periodically. Leave the other matters to you. On December 29th, just tell me the total number of Cold Resistance Levels in Xia City."

"Understood."

"Alright, let’s head back!"

After Xia Hong finished speaking, he directly headed east. The others picked up the ten advanced Cold Beasts on the ground and followed him back to the Beehive.

.........

Seven kilometers to the west, within the dense fog of Yangyuan Peak, a fist-sized orb emitting blue flames continuously released thick white smoke. The orb seemed alive, eagerly awaiting the east.

"Splitting off a third of the Holy Sun Body, and still unable to kill him. He’s about to come with that great cauldron. The weaklings I’ve drawn in are useless, they can’t stop them at all. What to do, what to do..."

From inside the orb came a sharp female voice. The voice carried a strain of hysterical madness and a sense of impending doom and unease.

"He possesses the Yin Yang Mirror, no matter how I disguise myself, I can’t escape, absolutely can’t escape. I won’t be captured, not after such a hard-fought revival. I’d rather die than become another’s slave!"

"Got it, got it, hehehahaha..."

Roar...

After the sinister, frenzied female voice vanished, a panicked beast’s roar suddenly echoed beside Yangyuan Peak.
