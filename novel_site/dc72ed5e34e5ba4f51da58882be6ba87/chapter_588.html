Chapter 588: Chapter 252: A Thousand Gold for a Steed’s Bones, Yang Li Surrenders

At dawn, on the first floor of Wushuang Main Building.

It’s supposed to be sleeping time now, but Li Hu sat at the edge of the bed, his eyes occasionally glancing at the door, his face full of anxiety.

"They wouldn’t do anything to Ah Zhong, would they? Could it be that our identities have been exposed? No, no, even if they were exposed, both Ah Zhong and I have just expressed our willingness to defect to Great Xia, they have even less reason to act against us..."

Before daylight, more than 1600 Earth-digging Realm members from the two families of Long Mountain and Zhaoyang were all arranged to rest on the first floor of Wushuang’s main building. His luck was good enough to be assigned a room with Yang Zhong.

But just now, someone from Great Xia came to take Yang Zhong away.

No matter how much he asked, the other party wouldn’t say anything, only told him to rest assured. But now, over ten minutes had passed, and Yang Zhong hadn’t returned yet. While worrying about his brother’s safety, Li Hu couldn’t help but let his thoughts run wild.

His basic strength and Yang Zhong’s hadn’t reached the limit of thirty thousand yet, so their strength wasn’t considered very strong. He thought about it carefully and felt that the only place where the two of them held any value for Great Xia was perhaps their somewhat unique lineage.

The problem is, being illegitimate children doesn’t have any actual impact, does it?

Neither his father Li Xuantiang nor Yang Zhong’s father Yang Fengping took them seriously; this was almost common knowledge in Long Mountain.

"Great Xia probably doesn’t know about this, and they think controlling us will allow them to threaten Long Mountain. Could it be like this?"

The more Li Hu thought, the more anxious he became, and he grew increasingly worried about Yang Zhong.

But soon, his worry was abruptly interrupted by the sound of footsteps.

The door opened from the outside, and Yang Zhong with a complex expression, walked in carrying a heavy package, followed by two Great Xia soldiers.

"Ah Zhong, are you alright?"

Li Hu sprang up from the bed, instinctively asking Yang Zhong what was happening, but before Yang Zhong could reply, the two Great Xia soldiers interrupted him directly.

"Don’t speak, Li Hu, come with me!"

Li Hu looked slightly bewildered, noticing Yang Zhong wasn’t saying anything and only made a complex expression, then gestured a mouth shape to him as if he understood something. After a slight nod, he followed the two soldiers out of the room.

In the main building hallway, apart from the Great Xia soldiers stationed every five steps and a post every ten steps, there was no one else.

Li Hu silently followed behind them, and soon reached the top floor, arriving at the side room in the central hall.

Upon reaching the doorway, a rich aroma of meat wafted into his nose, causing Li Hu to freeze momentarily. After sniffing carefully, his face immediately showed an expression of delight and curiosity.

"It seems like Intermediate Beast Meat, why does it smell so good?"

Having led a hunting team in Long Mountain, he quickly recognized the aroma source from Intermediate Beast Meat, yet this scent contained something he had never smelled before, sparking his curiosity.

"You’re in for a treat, hurry inside!"

The two Great Xia soldiers who brought him, upon smelling the aroma, also inhaled a few times before prompting him in with a hint of envy.

"Thank you both for leading the way!"

Li Hu bowed in gratitude and then slowly stepped into the side room.

Inside, two people were seated, surrounded by a small iron pot, and the fragrance smelled earlier was clearly wafting from the pot.

"Li Hu, greetings to Minister Sir, greetings to Lord Qiu Peng!"

Li Hu quickly stepped forward, bowing in respect to each of them.

He had seen both of them before, the black-clothed young man on the left looked about seventy percent similar to the leader Xia Hong he had seen before. This should be that Minister Sir Xia Chuan, as called by those from Great Xia; the right one was Qiu Peng, who announced the distribution of Solidified Fire Oil and their departure.

"You and your good brother Yang Zhong, both are quite clever."

Without any concealment, Xia Chuan displayed a look of admiration on his face because when Yang Zhong had entered earlier, he also first paid respect to him and then to Qiu Peng.

"These two names are quite interesting!"

Having just learned the names of Li Hu and Yang Zhong, Xia Chuan also understood what Xia Hong’s words truly meant.

Three years ago, among the two people who wreaked havoc in Redwood Ridge and Beishuo Town, one was named Li Hu, and the other surnamed Yang, but his name was Yang Ning.

Recalling Li Hu and Yang Ning, a gleam of cold light flashed in Xia Chuan’s eyes. He hadn’t forgotten these two, especially Li Hu’s foot, which he still remembered vividly.

However, now wasn’t the time to think about these things.

Xia Chuan slightly moved his attention, giving Qiu Peng across him a look.

Understanding, Qiu Peng nodded and invited, "Li Hu, we should be around the same age, feel free, come sit, and eat together!"

Li Hu’s face was a bit nervous, but remembering the mouth shape Yang Zhong had made before he came, he pondered for a moment and gathered the courage to sit beside Xia Chuan and Qiu Peng.

The two brothers had enough tacit understanding and enough travelers, and the mouth shape Yang Zhong had made to him was "be honest." Since that’s the case, he would be honest.

Li Hu went to the table, and without waiting for Xia Chuan and Qiu Peng to speak, he directly grabbed a piece of Cold Beast Meat and stuffed it into his mouth.

Last night they first attacked Hanqiong and then encountered Deceitful Monster, not only him but everyone from Long Mountain, Zhaoyang, and Hanqiong—none had eaten for over twenty hours. They were all terribly hungry, and since Qiu Peng had personally invited him, Li Hu wouldn’t hold back and began to feast.
