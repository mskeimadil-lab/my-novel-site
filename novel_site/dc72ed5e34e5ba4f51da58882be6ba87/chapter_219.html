Previously, Xia Hong was unwilling to use the wooden mannequin to improve the cultivation of others in the camp for this reason, but now things are different.

"The improvement in cultivation speed has been distinctly quantified with a doubled metric. In this way, as long as they are members of the Great Xia, breaking through the Logging Realm is no longer a problem, and the speed of breakthrough to the Earth-digging Realm will also rapidly increase. Therefore, the subsequent resource extraction rate will become higher and higher, making such consumption negligible!"

Xia Hong, with an excited expression, walked into the martial arts training area, looked around, and then, with a slight movement of his mind, a smile appeared on his face.

"It can be moved freely, one hundred square meters, just right to place in the main hall on the third floor. Daily, it can be used for those in the Logging Realm and Earth-digging Realm for cultivation!"

Of course, it cannot be free.

A glimmer of light flashed in Xia Hong’s eyes. This martial arts training ground can at most accommodate a hundred people at a time, and the consumption of coal stone is so high that it cannot be used for free by the camp members.

To use it, one must exchange contribution points!

The specific pricing will be researched by Xia Chuan and others, as Xia Hong would not bother with these matters. His gaze finally stopped on the five wooden mannequins, gradually showing a strong passion.

"Five cold-resistance-level intermediate Cold Beasts that follow commands without fear of pain. Now the camp should be considered thoroughly secure. Even if those two cold-resistance-level experts from the Mirror Immortal Camp come together, it should not be a big problem..."

The security of the camp is finally assured!

Looking at the five wooden mannequins, Xia Hong couldn’t help but let out a long sigh of relief.

With the current progress of his cultivation, the basic strength of a cold-resistance-level expert is at most around 150,000 jin, and one or two intermediate Cold Beasts would certainly pose no threat to them.

But what about five?

Moreover, these five completely disregard pain, their material is tougher than real Cold Beasts, and most importantly, they completely obey his commands, even to their deaths if necessary.

"It’s a pity they can’t be taken outside for hunting, if only they could be taken out..."

The thought crossed his mind, and Xia Hong immediately chuckled and shook his head.

Taking the Iron Refining Furnace and all the camp members around, who knows what might happen, he really cannot take on that risk.

It’s better to play it safe.

"Of course, since the camp’s security is assured, it’s time to look to the north to see if the people from the Mirror Immortal Camp have crossed the line!"

Outside, it was daytime, and as Xia Hong pushed open the window, the cold wind swept in with frost and snow, continuously rushing into the room.

Through the swirling frost and snow, he cast a distant glance toward the northern side of Redwood Ridge, his gaze flickering with a hint of chill.

...

On February 20, the first year of the Great Xia

Xia Chuan received a command, went up to the third floor, and paid a visit to Xia Hong’s room.

After coming downstairs, Xia Chuan immediately gathered everyone in the camp and excitedly announced that the Camp Supply Station had added a new item that could be exchanged.

"The cultivation time of the martial arts training ground on the third floor can be exchanged for 20 contribution points a day. Once fifty people exchange, the martial arts training ground will open for a day."

Initially, when Xia Chuan announced it, everyone was a bit puzzled.

What is the martial arts training ground?

Why does it cost 20 contribution points for a day, and what effect does cultivation have?

Faced with everyone’s confusion, Xia Chuan didn’t explain but directly led the entire hunting team and selected the thirty-five people with the highest basic strength, plus himself, to make up fifty people to stay on the third floor for a day.

No one knew what happened on the third floor that day.

Everyone only knew that when the people, including Xia Chuan, came down from the third floor, their faces were all filled with excitement and agitation.

Once the news spread, everyone in the camp instantly boiled over...

"The leader has built a new martial arts training ground on the third floor. As long as you cultivate in the training ground, the efficiency of absorbing Cold Beast Meat will double!"

"Double, it really is double. The hunting team members have confirmed, just go up, and you can double the speed."

"Cheng Feng, Lin Kai, and Xu Ning, the three of them, broke through to the Earth-digging Realm on the very day they went to the third floor. It must be real, I must go as well!"

"You can exchange 20 contribution points for one day. I must go too. I am not far from the Earth-digging Realm. As long as I stay up there for a few days, I’m sure I can break through too."

...

Do not underestimate those who have practiced in their obsession with strength enhancement.

Even now, with wooden products, beast skin products, iron tools, weapons... the camp already has many items that can be exchanged, but the hottest-selling resource in the Great Xia undoubtedly remains Cold Beast Meat.

Because everyone knows that no matter how good, useful, or beautiful other things are, they can’t compare to the real benefits of enhancing one’s strength.

Strength is the foundation of everything, without which any treasure is just a castle in the air, ready to crumble at a touch.

And now, the martial arts training ground can help them double their cultivation speed.

The collective frenzy among the people is imaginable.

The next day, February 21.

As soon as night fell, a long line had already formed at the Camp Supply Station.

There was no need to gather fifty people; most who were led up by Xia Chuan the day before had already secured the front spots, with only a few perceptive ones seizing the top fifty places.

Those who came later, realizing they wouldn’t get a chance this time, had looks of regret on their faces and secretly resolved to come early next time to queue.

After Shi Ping completed the registration, all fifty people followed Xia Chuan up to the third floor.
