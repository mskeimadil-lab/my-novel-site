Chapter 953: Chapter 351: Lu Yang’s Plan, Unexpected Changes in Xia City, Sumeru Nightmare, His Highness’s Golden Words

The little girl was quite mischievous. As soon as she jumped out, she covered her face and pretended she was only just discovered by Xia Chuan, shaking her head and making silly sounds.

"Hahaha, it’s broad daylight and you’re sneaking out from Star-picking Hall in a Black Feather Cover. If your mom finds out, you’re going to get punished again."

Seeing his niece being so quirky, Xia Chuan couldn’t help but laugh and stepped forward to pick her up.

Noticing the small black cloak casually tossed on the ground behind the seat, Xia Chuan shook his head slightly, bent down, and picked it up.

"This cloak is something many people in the camp yearn for, yet you just nonchalantly throw it on the ground. You’ll regret it when your mom confiscates it."

Hearing her mother mentioned twice, Xia Yuyao was obviously not thrilled, turned her head away with a huff, then her eyes darted around, and she turned back and said:

"Second Uncle, I just miss Daddy, I wanted to see if he’s here. If not, I’ll go back. You can’t tell my mom."

Xia Chuan snorted with a smile, deliberately hesitated for a moment before Xia Yuyao, who looked at him with anticipation, until he couldn’t help but smile and nod in agreement.

"Hee hee hee, I knew it, Second Uncle is the best."

Xia Yuyao immediately laughed, then turned her head to look at the two people below in the hall who had just been arguing, her face showing a hint of curiosity.

She had lived in Star-picking Hall for a long time. Although she was able to sneak over to the main building, her range of activities was mostly limited to the Political Hall here, so she didn’t encounter many strangers.

People like Hou Jing, around his age, she had a grandfather as a template, and someone like Meng Yi, who was middle-aged, she was seeing for the first time.

"Hou Jing pays respects to Your Highness!"

"Meng Yi pays respects to Your Highness!"

Seeing Xia Yuyao’s gaze, the two below hurriedly bowed in greeting.

Witnessing this scene, Xia Chuan was suddenly intrigued and, laughingly, asked the niece in his arms, "After eavesdropping for so long, Yu Yao, who do you think should get the position of the Tenth Army?"

Upon hearing this, Hou Jing and Meng Yi immediately felt nervous.

In fact, both of them knew that because their advantages were similar, Xia Chuan found it difficult to decide. At this moment, Xia Chuan was clearly planning to leave it up to chance by passing the question to Xia Yuyao.

Since Xia Yuyao’s decision would be random, there was no question of fairness or unfairness, and naturally, neither would blame Xia Yuyao.

With this in mind, both hurriedly lifted their heads, each squeezing out what they considered their most amiable smile towards Xia Yuyao.

"Eee..."

Hearing Xia Yuyao’s disdainful sound, Xia Chuan almost couldn’t hold back his laughter but forcibly restrained himself and waited for his niece’s answer.

"Second Uncle, since they both want to form an army so much, then just set up an Eleventh Army, so they both get one and won’t argue."

Given Xia Yuyao’s current scope of activities and understanding, Xia Chuan strongly suspected that she might not understand what the Tenth Army really meant.

Despite her lack of understanding, she actually suggested forming a new army.

Xia Chuan’s expression slightly tensed as he recalled the words Xia Hong said three days ago in Mirror Valley, and he immediately made a decision in his heart.

"Alright, then I’ll listen to Yu Yao and set up an Eleventh Army!"

Upon hearing these words, Meng Yi and Hou Jing below immediately beamed with joy.

The words of the Imperial Highness carry weight indeed!

With just one sentence, the Minister was instructed to add another army.

Both couldn’t help but sigh inwardly and cast grateful glances towards Xia Yuyao.

Of course, Xia Yuyao couldn’t see their expressions.

Because she was already being swiftly escorted back to Star-picking Hall by the hurriedly arriving Ming Yue and other maids, who helped her put on the small cloak.
