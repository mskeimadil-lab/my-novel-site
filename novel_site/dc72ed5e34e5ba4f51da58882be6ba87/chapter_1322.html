Chapter 1322: Chapter 431: Deceitful Object Pricing, Skill Transcendence, Martial Arts Competition Finale (Part 4)

Extraordinary skill transformation...

According to Xia Hong’s understanding, it’s like how Chu Yan’er paints, where personal specialties can be transformed into extraordinary means.

The problem is, he really doesn’t know what his specialty is?

Painting, brewing wine, forging, mining, playing the zither, playing chess, calligraphy...

He can do a little of these, but to call them specialties, he’s not quite there.

"My real specialty that I can surpass others at should be cultivation. It’s impossible to make my cultivation extraordinary again, making my cultivation speed faster, right?"

Xia Hong felt the white light emitted by the Enlightenment Lotus Platform continuously pouring into his sea of consciousness, and couldn’t help but show a hint of anticipation on his face.

If he could really make his cultivation specialty extraordinary, perhaps...

Buzz!

Soon, the white light completely flooded Xia Hong’s sea of consciousness.

And as a sound emerged from his sea of consciousness, Xia Hong was initially stunned, then his pupils burst with intense joy.

"Succeeded, the sixth time, surprisingly succeeded!"

[Law-level Martial Arts Imitation and Deconstruction]

These words suddenly appeared in Xia Hong’s mind, leaving him full of shock.

"Oh right! My specialty is the martial arts talent brought from my previous life, I actually forgot about this..."

Xia Hong realized suddenly and let out a silent laugh, shaking his head incessantly.

[Law-level Martial Arts Imitation: Upon seeing any martial arts technique, what appears in the eyes are not the movements, but the trajectory of qi and blood, the principle of exerting force, and the rules of bodily meridian evolution. One glance can see through the core of the technique and all variations, achieving perfect replication while instantly pointing out the bottlenecks and flaws in the founder’s initial creation of the technique.]

[Law-level Martial Arts Deconstruction: Not only can perfectly replicate, but can also automatically optimize to perfect form based on the understanding of underlying rules, instantly deducing a seamless "perfect version," unleashing more powerful force.]

But soon, after reviewing his extraordinary martial arts specialty, Xia Hong’s expression instantly became extremely excited.

"This... what is this? Invincible regardless of the same strength, and even against opponents stronger than me, as long as there’s no overwhelming superiority, I am invincible!"

Perfect replication, even replicating more perfect martial arts techniques, is actually nothing; the problem is seeing through the core of an opponent’s technique at a glance, this martial arts ability is simply heaven-defying!

It’s equivalent to, as soon as someone makes a move, Xia Hong knows exactly what part of him the opponent intends to attack, and the follow-up moves are entirely under his control.

"Extraordinary, this extraordinary transformation, is truly heaven-defying!"

Xia Hong’s face was full of joy, he suddenly noticed something, lifting his head to look at the entrance of the Star-picking Platform, his expression slightly halted.

Li Xuanling, dressed in court attire, was looking at him with a worried expression, slowly speaking, "I want to go over to the Feudal Town side to have a look."

Xia Hong sighed softly, his body moved slightly, and in the next moment, appeared in front of Li Xuanling, gently holding her, he whispered, "Don’t worry, I planned to go to the Feudal Town this time after this retreat. You just broke through, stay home and consolidate your cultivation first. Xia Chuan hasn’t broken through yet, he definitely can’t handle it alone, Xia City still needs your watch..."

Saying this, seeing that Li Xuanling didn’t show any reaction, he changed his tone and continued, "Yu Yao and Yu Sheng are just six years old, can you really leave them in Xia City to cultivate on their own?"

Li Xuanling’s expression finally softened a bit, but she was still unwilling to give up and continued, "But Uncle can’t wait much longer, I..."

"Don’t worry, I’ll set off in two days, at the latest. If there are Dan medicines that can extend life at Chen Cang and Cai Qiu, I will risk everything to get them for you, alright?"

Upon hearing this, Li Xuanling bit her lip, glaring at Xia Hong, "Are you serious about that? I don’t want you to risk anything! Get it if you can, if it’s too difficult forget it, I guess Uncle can’t hold on much longer..."

Xia Hong thought of Li Tiancheng’s condition, sighed softly, looking down at Li Xuanling’s beautiful eyes already filled with tears, he couldn’t help but use a little more force as he hugged her.

"Rest assured, as long as it’s there at Feudal Town, I’ll get it for you."

In front of the Inner City Main Hall, the two platforms were intense with battles.

As each contestant landed and was eliminated, the two groups’ advancement slots and subsequent rankings were announced one after another.

"The top ten list for the youth group is out, Luo An, Luo Cheng, Lu Dong, Liu Peng, Zhao Yuanwu, Murong Yan, Zou Ping, Xiao Yan, Chen Ping, Jiang Yichen, next we’ll see them compete for the top ten ranks."

"The top ten list for the young adult group is also out, Zhao Yuandao, Zhao Yuankong, Yuan Bai, Tie Xinchuan, Zhou Yalong, Xiahou Qingqing, Xiao Hen, Fu Tiandu, Zhou Ying, Meng Qing."

"There are only four people from Outer Town, right?"

"Only four, just Murong Yan from Muyin; Jiangxia has Xiahou Qingqing and Jiang Yichen; the last one, Fu Tiandu, is from the Outer Realm, I heard he only came from a village-level camp, amazing! He managed to make it to the top ten."

"How does the final compete?"

"Both groups adopt a gain-challenge point system, first draw lots to select three battlers, three battlers have two challenge opportunities, the other seven, each have three challenge opportunities, 1 point for successful defense or attack, no points for failure, after finishing, rank by points."

"Isn’t this unfair? What about marathon battles?"

"Do you think you’re smart, others couldn’t think of it? Healing and qi-blood replenishing Dan medicines are placed on the side of the platform, whoever needs it can use it, no one gets any advantages."

"I see."

"The champion of the youth group, I guess it’s Luo An or Lu Dong, these two have too strong cultivation and strength, others will have a hard time."

"What about the young adult group?"

"The young adult group is less clear, it seems hopeful for everyone; Tie Xinchuan and Zhou Yalong have age advantage, but Zhao Yuandao and Zhao Yuankong as siblings are extraordinarily strong, plus Xiao Hen is also good, I estimate these five could all win."

"Those four from Outer Town, do they have no chance at the championship?"

"Joking, this is a competition organized by Great Xia, letting Outer Town people win the championship, how would that look?"

"True, the lord and all the grown-ups might be watching, letting Outer Town take the championship wouldn’t make sense."

"Hurry, watch, it’s starting, it’s starting!"
