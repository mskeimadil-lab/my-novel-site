Chapter 582: Chapter 250: Rescue, Intimidation, and Surprise

"My child is dying, help, help!"

"I’m burning up, save me, save me..."

"Help, help, waaaah..."

......

At the Wushuang Giant Stone Camp, on the largest open space, a throng of people gathered, filling the area with desperate cries for help. From time to time, there were bursts of flames, followed by intense screams and wails of pain.

From their clothing, it was clear these people had fled from the Hanqiong side.

They were divided into three groups, with the largest covering the right side, occupying about ninety percent of the total, while the left and middle groups were smaller, totaling about sixteen or seventeen hundred people.

Though the scene was extremely chaotic, upon closer inspection, one could see that the people in these three groups were not randomly jumbled together, maintaining a distance of over one meter from each other.

Furthermore, people in pale golden armor were moving back and forth, maintaining order while using the black oily substance in their hands to save people.

"Captain, it’s not good, there are too many people, and it’s noisy and chaotic. We have only about fourteen hundred people, and we need to assign some to guard those from the Earth-digging Realm. The speed at which we are saving people can’t keep up; we’re running out of time!"

"And disturbances keep breaking out; we need to send people to suppress them, it’s not working."

"The droplets are still spreading, our people are getting infected too, we can’t continue like this, we have to think of a solution."

"Many people don’t know about Solidified Fire Oil and have started to give up, inciting others to disturb the peace."

......

On the high platform at the forefront of the open space, upon hearing the reports from four subordinates, Lu Yang’s eyes filled with agitation, and seeing another disturbance breaking out on the left side, his eyes turned sharp as he drew the horizontal blade from his waist and walked over.

"Brothers, we’re trapped here with no way out, better to follow me and fight our way out to return to Zhaoyang to find the leader, there might still be hope!"

"That’s right, follow Hou Jing and fight our way out, brothers."

"Kill!"

......

The eight hundred people on the left were from Zhaoyang’s Earth-digging Realm. As Lu Yang approached and heard the words of the one inciting disturbance, he immediately frowned, especially seeing seven or eight people being stirred by him and standing up to resist. He raised his horizontal blade and rushed over quickly.

Thud... thud... thud......

Lu Yang, with swift movements, cut down the eight who were about to cause a commotion, causing several nearby from the Great Xia Earth-digging Realm to be startled.

If even they were like this, needless to say, the nearby crowd from Zhaoyang’s Earth-digging Realm was instantly terrified into silence, not daring to make any sound. A few people who initially intended to move also returned to their places quietly, with downcast eyes.

Lu Yang turned his head to survey the square, and seeing another disturbance at the other end, he raised his voice and shouted:

"Lin Kai, Zhao Hu, Zhao Bao, and all others in charge, listen up, anyone who dares to move will be killed immediately, if anything happens, I will take responsibility!"

Thud... thud......

Almost as soon as he finished speaking, in the middle and right side areas, the sounds of cutting and killing erupted one after another.

After three or four waves of such sounds, the crowd in the square immediately quieted down, everyone shut their mouths, even small children covered their mouths tightly, with only a few infants who hadn’t been covered in time managing to let out a few cries.

Once the square quieted down, Lu Yang returned to the high platform immediately.

Lu Yang’s voice had been so loud just now that everyone knew it was him who ordered the killings, and naturally, they focused their attention on him.

Knowing that everyone was watching him, Lu Yang dared not waste time. He pointed to a young woman in her twenties holding a baby on the right side and ordered:

"Someone, bring this mother and child up here."

"Spare us, sir, I didn’t move, I didn’t move."

"Please, sir, spare me, spare me!"

......

Seeing two Great Xia soldiers come to drag her, the woman thought Lu Yang intended to kill her child, and her face showed fear as she continually pleaded with Lu Yang.

But the two Great Xia soldiers paid her no heed, dragging her to the high platform and bringing her beside Lu Yang.

Lu Yang ignored the woman’s pleas and directly snatched the bawling baby from her arms.

The woman thought he was going to kill her child and immediately knelt down, begging:

"Don’t kill my child, don’t kill my child, please, sir..."

The people from Hanqiong Station on the right, witnessing this scene, noticeably drew heavier breaths, and some from the Earth-digging Realm even stepped forward, obviously wanting to help the woman and save the child.

But the next moment, the woman’s cries abruptly stopped, and those from the Earth-digging Realm gazed at the high platform with a look of bewilderment.

Lu Yang didn’t kill; instead, he opened the baby’s clothing, showcasing the droplets appearing on its body to everyone, then took out a small piece of Solidified Fire Oil, crushed it, and evenly applied it to the infant’s body.

In just a moment, the droplets on the baby emitted a sizzling sound, after which Lu Yang wiped, and the droplets vanished completely.

The baby, who had been crying due to the rising body temperature, immediately stopped crying.

Even let out a crisp laugh.

"That black oil block can counteract the droplets?"

"It really works, I just saw it with my own eyes, and you didn’t believe me."
