Chapter 539: Chapter 236: White Dew Gu, Despairing Yuwen Dao, Yang Lu

Now that things have come to this, the destruction of Yanglu Camp is already a foregone conclusion, and at his age, how could he not see it clearly?

But as the leader, having worked tirelessly for the camp for so many years, it’s not easy to make him give up the Yanglu label.

Not only himself, but also Liu Ming, Qin Gu, and the Peng Zu in his arms, they are considered the core of Yanglu, and he is even more reluctant to let these three forget their identity as Yanglu people.

Peng Bo’s thoughts moved slightly, and he spoke, "Xiao Ming, do you know why our camp is called Yanglu?"

Liu Ming immediately nodded and said, "I know, grandpa told me..."

Liu Ming’s grandpa, Liu He, is Peng Bo’s sworn brother and also one of the three Cold Resistance Levels of Yanglu, so naturally he knows a lot.

"I remember grandpa said that the legend says, more than ninety years ago, in the early morning, a huge dew drop suddenly fell from the sky. The gorges surrounding our base were created when the dew drop smashed down. The dew drop was extremely hot, solidifying rapidly upon hitting the ground, forming a raised mound. Our base, Yangyuan Peak, was formed like this!"

Qin Feng next to him obviously also heard this story from his grandpa, and quickly chimed in, "I heard grandpa say it too. He also said that ever since we chose the base on the mound, we’ve never been disturbed by Cold Beasts. That dew drop must be some kind of treasure, but unfortunately, our camp’s strength is insufficient to utilize it."

Hearing this, Peng Bo nodded lightly. The history of Yanglu Camp is not long, the rumors from more than ninety years ago are unclear whether true or not. However, this story counts as the origin of Yanglu Camp, and naturally he hopes the three grandsons won’t forget it.

"That’s right, grandpa told you..."

"Leader Peng, the Minister from my home has arrived."

Yuan Cheng suddenly walked in from outside, followed by a young man in black. Seeing Yuan Cheng’s respectful attitude towards the young man, Peng Bo immediately composed himself and quickly stood up.

Six-maned Power, the strength of this Great Xia Minister is not strong.

But he is terrifyingly young, seemingly around Yuan Cheng’s age.

It seems he just hurried back from somewhere, looking quite rushed!

"Great Xia Minister, Xia Chuan, Leader Peng, I’m quite interested in the story you just told. If you don’t mind, could you tell me more?"

Xia Chuan, who just rushed back from Jing Valley, was still panting, but quickly opened his mouth to ask Peng Bo about the Yanglu Base.

The legend of the dew drop was intriguing enough, but mainly it was Qin Feng just said that ever since choosing Yangyuan Peak as the base, Yanglu Camp has never been disturbed by Cold Beasts, which piqued his interest.

There was also a vague sense that something was not quite right!

Aside from something creepy, he couldn’t think of anything else that could scare away Cold Beasts. His suspicions made him want to ask more.

Peng Bo, after all, was beneath others’ roofs and Yuan Cheng had been kind to him. Moreover, when he first entered the Hive Station, he had seen a glimpse of the strength of Great Xia, so he naturally did not dare to put on airs towards Xia Chuan and quickly recounted the matters of Yanglu Base.

The more Xia Chuan listened, the more something seemed off. Eventually, he furrowed his brow, whispered a few words to Yuan Cheng beside him, and upon hearing them, Yuan Cheng nodded and turned to leave.

After Yuan Cheng left, Xia Chuan turned back to look at Peng Bo, glanced at the two children behind him, and slowly revealed a smile on his face.

"Leader Peng, I also know about the affairs of Yanglu Camp. Wu Ying and Han Jiuli have been so heinous. Leader Peng must hate those two families to the core, right?"

Upon hearing these two names, Peng Bo gritted his teeth and said, "Of course!"

"Now, I can offer Leader Peng an opportunity for revenge. I wonder if Leader Peng would be interested?"

Peng Bo raised his eyebrows and looked at Xia Chuan, asking, "What opportunity?"

As Xia Chuan slowly revealed the plan, Peng Bo’s expression first showed shock, then hesitation. Eventually, Xia Chuan said something that made him extremely intrigued, and afterwards, he nodded heavily.

"Alright, just as you said, if Great Xia can bring out so many people, Peng Bo will accompany you on this trip."

"Alright, Leader Peng is straightforward. I will inform you shortly."
