Impressive!

After Xia Hong sorted out the structure of the top thirteen households of the Worldly Noble Tavern, his eyes suddenly brightened, and he couldn’t help but feel a touch of admiration.

The person who thought of this method is simply a genius!

"In the coming time at Chen Cang, continue to figure out the situation of this Worldly Noble Tavern, and see which seven surnames remain. This Worldly Noble Tavern is too interesting; it’s almost like the fifth vassal outside of the Four Vassals."

"Subordinate obeys!"

After instructing Jiang Xinfan, Xia Hong put away the Dan Medicine, his gaze flickering slightly, and continued: "In this way, the loophole of this Frost Ash Association is too significant. However, as you mentioned, it’s hard for the subordinates in Frost Ash to know the real identities of those above them, but on the contrary..."

"The lord is spot-on, I admire you!"

A trace of helplessness appeared on Jiang Xinfan’s face, and he continued: "This is the biggest problem. Not many may know Ji Hong’s real identity, but the Six Dragon Heads who contacted him directly, that is, the Yanling General Leader, must have an idea if they are inclined. As for me, the situation is comparatively better. I was brought in by Ji Hong, ranking last among the twelve Heavenly Kings of the Yanling Headquarters, and my current superior is Ji Hong.

But on Ji Hong’s side, it seems there’s no need to worry too much..."

Upon hearing Jiang Xinfan’s last sentence, Xia Hong immediately reacted, and his expression became incredibly peculiar.

The real identity of Ji Hong mentioned here refers to his identity in Chen Cang, which is the closed-door disciple of the Adviser Chen Yuanhong of Yanling County.

Chen Yuanhong is someone of the Chen Family!

And the Six Dragon Heads who could contact him directly are also highly likely to be from the Chen Family, and is it even possible...

"Hahaha, not likely, not likely!"

Thinking of this, Xia Hong couldn’t help but laugh, as Chen Yuanhong is one of the three advisers of Yanling County. If he were in the Frost Ash Association, the Chen Family would truly disregard the Landgrave too much.

"This issue isn’t too big; there’s no need for us to guard against it now. If Ji Hong really feels in danger, I, the lord, can just step in and settle that Six Dragon Head for him!"

Xia Hong spoke with a smile, his eyes flashing with a hint of coldness, and continued: "Let him secure the identity of Little Dragon Head. Whether he needs money or people, he can say it. This good shell of the Frost Ash Association is of great use to us. In the future, even the Four Vassals might find it usable..."

"Rest assured, lord, I understand!"

Jiang Xinfan, being a wise person, quickly caught on to Xia Hong’s meaning and immediately nodded heavily in response.

"By the way, lord, about that middle-aged scholar we encountered at the Worldly Noble Tavern on the fifth day of the month, I’ve looked into his identity as you asked, and there’s now a lead."

"Who is it?"

"Grand Master Chen Cang, Xue Zijing!"

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong’s eyes instantly lit up, and then he thought of something else, displaying a hint of a different expression on his face: "It’s him, no wonder. My identity must have been exposed, and the presence that’s been spying on me around here these days should be people sent by him."

Jiang Xinfan was taken aback by these words, his expression becoming instantly solemn, and he hurriedly bowed to Xia Hong to apologize: "Being spied upon, lord, I..."

"It’s not your fault; that person’s strength is much greater than yours!"

Xia Hong interrupted him with a wave of his hand and then looked at the Spirit Pill in his hand, his eyes flashing slightly as he said: "There’s nothing wrong with being discovered; it’s actually a good opportunity to use it as part of our strategy, allowing you and Ji Hong to live more stably in Chen Cang from now on..."

Saying this, he paused for a moment and then quietly instructed Jiang Xinfan.

As Jiang Xinfan listened to his instructions, his expression gradually became peculiar, and by the end, it turned quite extraordinary. After Xia Hong finished speaking, he hurriedly bowed and said:

"Lord, your plan is brilliant; I admire you! I will take care of it right away."
