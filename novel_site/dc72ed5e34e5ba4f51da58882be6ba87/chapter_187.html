Chapter 187: Chapter 134 Difficult Hunt [Warm Sun Giant Adds More 3/10]_5

Moreover, Xia Hong didn’t just pull it out.

Taking advantage of the fact that Snow Mane’s struggles were not too violent at first, he reversed his grip on the Yi Blade with both hands and frantically stabbed at its eyes, nose, mouth, and even ears dozens of times.

"Roar... Roar... Roar..."

After these dozens of stabs, Xia Hong instantly became a man covered in blood.

And Snow Mane exploded with even greater motion than before.

The fifteen people on the outside had already grabbed the ropes attached to the animal trap.

Worried that the ropes might snap, Xia Chuan also added on the White Thread.

But even so, the fifteen people holding the ropes couldn’t last beyond three breaths.

Snow Mane’s body twisted directly in the trench, deforming the animal trap on its right leg. Then it went berserk, extending its straightened tongue around, slashing indiscriminately.

Whirr... Whirr... Whoosh... Whoosh...

The long tongue swung like a sharp sword, the sword edge howling relentlessly.

The fifteen people on the south side kept changing positions, evading while not loosening the ropes in their hands at all.

Xia Hong atop the pit was also adjusting his position, but because he was the closest, he was actually stuck in Snow Mane’s blind spot.

Unless Snow Mane softened its tongue, it couldn’t attack him at all.

"Exhausted, throw me a section of rope, Zhao Long, you three continue firing from the trees, the rest follow me, don’t let it get up."

Xia Chuan on the south side tossed over a section of rope, Xia Hong grasped the blade in one hand and caught the rope with the other, looping it around the struggling neck of Snow Mane.

In the gap of looping the rope, the out-of-control Snow Mane suddenly had a strange gleam in its pupils, its straightened tongue instantly softened and coiled towards Xia Hong.

Thud...

However, just as Snow Mane softened its tongue.

A swift blade light sliced directly through the air.

Almost without suspense, Snow Mane’s long tongue was severed.

"Just waiting for you to do that, I thought you could hold it in!"

At this moment, Xia Hong with single-handedly clasping the blade, his whole body blood-red, smiled hauntingly like a fierce ghost.

The rope he had been gripping tightly, he had long tossed aside.

That rope was merely bait to lure Snow Mane into making a move; being this close, how could he not guard against Snow Mane’s long tongue.

"Roar..."

With its tongue severed, Snow Mane roared madly, struggling even more fiercely.

The twelve people on the south side with Xia Chuan found themselves unable to tug it, the rope continuously slipping from their hands, but they knew it was a critical moment. No matter how the rope slipped, they immediately picked it up again, restricting it for even one more second counted.

Whoosh... Whoosh... Whoosh...

The three on the tree, Zhao Long among them, took advantage of the open-mouthed Snow Mane, drew their bows, and shot arrows continuously, several Iron Arrows penetrating through Snow Mane’s lips and tongue, straight to the throat.

"Almost there, hook the ropes to the trees, everyone, move away immediately!"

At Xia Hong’s command, Xia Chuan and the others pulling on the ropes immediately scattered, each holding onto a segment of rope.

The three in the trees, including Zhao Long, also stopped shooting arrows, leapt down, and received a section of rope from Xia Chuan, then circled around several nearby large trees.

"Is it ready?"

"Ready."

"Ready, leader!"

"Ready on my side too."

"Ready."

...

"Disperse!"

After confirming that everyone responded, Xia Hong gave the order.

And at this point, the Snow Mane on the ground was already standing up, panting heavily.

Not to mention the eyes, Snow Mane’s entire face couldn’t find a good piece of flesh.

Its head was almost dyed in blood red, its emotions discernible only through its heavily weighty breathing sounds.

"Roar..."

Snow Mane roared in angry fury, kicked off with its limbs, and charged fiercely toward the last place Xia Hong’s voice came from.

Crack...

However, its charge did not work as before.

To be precise, it didn’t rush out at all.

Countless ropes mixed with White Thread entangled not only around its body but also linked and wrapped around the dozens of large trees surrounding it.

When it moved, it simultaneously tugged those dozen or so large trees.

The trees within this area were at least a dozen meters in diameter.

The dozen or so large trees selected by Xia Hong and the others, as soon as they were pulled.

Virtually all the surrounding large trees moved along.

With Snow Mane’s strength, breaking one big tree would be a breeze.

Breaking two trees together wouldn’t be a big problem; five trees in front might be a bit strenuous; if it were ten trees, it would probably need to gather strength.

With over a dozen trees, it would face some pressure.

Dozens, or even hundreds of trees.

Not to mention one Snow Mane, even three more.

Wouldn’t be able to break them all at once.

That was the problem this Snow Mane was facing now.

Crack...

Snow Mane strained to the point where its limbs were taut, but only then did the outermost trees start to fracture; when it strained until its body flushed red, only three or four of the outermost trees had collapsed.

Pant... Pant...

Snow Mane stopped straining and gasped twice, looking around at the ropes with blood-red pupils, finally showing a trace of fear.

Realizing its movement was restricted, even escape was a luxury.

Snow Mane finally, in hindsight, started to feel fear.

It stretched out its long tongue, beginning to quickly cut the ropes entangling it.

"You wish! Its movements are already restricted, archers, get ready!"

After devising for so long, now successfully, Xia Hong wouldn’t give it a chance.

Sixteen people spread around the ten or so large trees surrounding Snow Mane, drew their bows and arrows, and shot frantically, with almost no gaps in between.

The countless ropes combined with surrounding trees’ strength rendered Snow Mane’s mobility down to a mere fraction. Facing these Iron Arrows, it couldn’t even flee, only able to avoid them to the greatest extent by turning its head.

Thud... Thud...
