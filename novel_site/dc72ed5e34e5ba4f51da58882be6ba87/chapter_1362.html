Chapter 1362: Chapter 441: Three Surprises, Four Vassal Class Structures, Lesson 1

Xia Hong took out a total of three small porcelain bottles from the bundle.

The first porcelain bottle contained a type of pill that was crimson like blood.

"The Washing Marrow Pill is considered an advanced version of the Blood Ginseng Pill. It doesn’t need to be used with any cultivation technique; just consuming it alone can flush out impurities in the meridians and enhance the flow of qi and blood!"

The Blood Ginseng Pill is a product of Muyin Town. It’s not a normal cultivation elixir, but an essential auxiliary pill for cultivating the Murong Family’s secret Twelve Blood Scripture Skills.

After the Hongmen Banquet ended in the eighth year of Great Xia, Xia Hong went to Muyin Town and got hold of this cultivation technique. At the time, he was puzzled because, even if Muyin had sought something from Great Xia, Murong Chui wasn’t likely to agree so readily and hand over their secret technique.

Later, after learning that the cultivation technique had to be used in conjunction with the Blood Ginseng Pill, and that Muyin was selling it to him at 180,000 silver taels per pill, he suddenly realized and laughed at himself, knowing he’d fallen into Murong Chui’s trap.

Of course, Muyin Town only profited from this for a little over a year. After Cheng Feng and a pharmacist studied the Blood Ginseng Pill’s recipe last year, Great Xia stopped being this big fool.

Flushing out impurities in the meridians; enhancing the efficiency of qi and blood flow.

The former can purify and unblock the meridians, releasing the body’s potential, thereby improving strength slightly; while the latter can directly enhance the cultivation speed.

Currently, the importance of the Twelve Blood Scripture Skills paired with the Blood Ginseng Pill is most evident in Li Xuanling’s cultivation speed. Without these two, Li Xuanling would never have broken through to Xianyang Level so quickly.

And right now, the Washing Marrow Pill in his hand can directly replace these two.

"Not only replace them, but I just tested in the quiet room of the Treasure Pavilion, this Blood Marrow Pill not only has mild properties and can automatically penetrate all meridians, but its medicinal power is more than ten times that of the Blood Ginseng Pill. This means I can also use it to assist in cultivating the Twelve Blood Scripture Skills, and the effect may be several times stronger than taking it alone..."

Xia Hong’s expression was full of excitement. Whether it’s enhancing strength or cultivation speed, these are the things he’s most concerned about and urgently needs right now. The Washing Marrow Pill is indeed the first big surprise the Treasure Pavilion brought him.

"Next, the Bone Burning Pill!"

Following that, Xia Hong opened the second porcelain bottle. This porcelain bottle contained only one pill. The pill was milky white inside, with a layer of magma-like patterns on the outside, emitting intense heat.

"It can allow the bones of the Xianyang Level to increase their strength by fifty percent under extreme conditions, and maintain it for about three days. Even if you don’t consider the two side effects... no, even considering the two side effects, it’s still undoubtedly a Divine Pill!"

Enhancing strength by fifty percent under extreme conditions – even if not defeating formidable enemies, the probability of escaping alive would increase significantly. This kind of pill, which allows Xianyang Level cultivators to save their lives, is aptly named a Divine Pill.

The side effects of the Bone Burning Pill are, one, it contains very strong Dan Poison, which affects the bones and cannot be cured with medicine, only metabolized through normal cultivation, a process that is extremely long, generally taking more than ten years;

The second is, after consumption, it will consume a large amount of bone marrow. Bone marrow is also a core substance for Xianyang Level cultivation, so once consumed, it severely affects the speed of subsequent cultivation, which lasts twice as long as the Dan Poison’s metabolism, about twenty years.

"The effect on cultivation speed means it can’t be used casually, only in extreme circumstances to save life. Bone Burning Pill... Bone Burning, it seems quite a coincidence..."

Thinking about the two side effects of the Bone Burning Pill, Xia Hong muttered slightly, then put the pill back, and continued to open the third porcelain bottle, pouring out a golden pill.

The pill emitted a golden spiritual light throughout, with nine black magma patterns spiraling on the surface. Once it was taken out, the temperature in the main hall rose rapidly.

"Just as I guessed from the beginning, it indeed has nine patterns, although the name is different. Cai Qiu calls it the Bone Shedding Pill, in low grade with three patterns, middle grade with six patterns, and top-grade with nine patterns - a cultivation elixir for Xianyang Level!"

Xia Hong looked at the Top-grade Bone Shedding Pill in his hand, his eyes burning with intense heat.

The current Great Xia has only two types of elixirs for Xianyang Level cultivation, namely the Bone Condensing Pill and the Bone Gathering Pill. The main material for the former is Jade Bone, the bones of an Advanced Cold Beast; while the latter’s main material is Silver Bone, which is from Beast King Level Cold Beast.

When the Bone Gathering Pill was first produced, he guessed that above it might be something with nine patterns – this Top-grade Bone Shedding Pill proved his hypothesis.

"Its main material is Golden Bone, also from Beast King Level Cold Beast. According to this speculation, those Beast Kings can evolve through cultivation, turning their bones from silver to gold..."

Beast King Level bones not only have silver but also a more advanced gold.

Previously, in the Nine Towns Territory, due to the fact that the hunted Beast Kings all had silver bones, Xia Hong didn’t actually know this. It was during the four months in Evergreen Valley, after he hunted five Beast Kings, and found out that the two strongest ones had golden bones, that he confirmed it.

After putting the Bone Shedding Pill into his bundle, Xia Hong furrowed his brows slightly and sighed softly:

"Assuming the identity of the Daqin Prince and borrowing a tiger skin brings many benefits, but also a lot of disadvantages. I can’t casually buy things and have to maintain appearances everywhere for fear of being seen through. As for inquiring about the situations of Tribulation Body Realm and Venerable Realm experts, that’s even out of the question..."
