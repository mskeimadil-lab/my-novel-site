Chapter 878: Chapter 335: Songyang Ridge, Seven Houses Surrender, First Batch of Silver Arrives

"Everyone, move to the sides and make way for the army to pass. Don’t just stand there!"

"Brother Ji, quickly lead them away, the army will be here soon."

Li Longkai and Xu Ning, hearing the voice, swiftly addressed the group at Songyang Ridge.

Ji Yang and the others were somewhat at a loss, but they quickly led more than five hundred people to the sides of the street, then curiously looked towards the direction of the west city gate.

At this sight, Ji Yang and the five hundred others were instantly shocked to their cores.

Thud, thud, thud......

A fully armored army of a thousand troops marched with heavy, synchronized steps from the direction of the west city gate.

Just like the soldiers Ji Yang and the others had seen earlier at the city gate, these thousand men were clad in light golden Hundred Forged Battle Armor. However, they differed significantly from those soldiers, as each exuded a thick aura of murderous intent, converging together almost to a palpable presence.

Their armor and weapons were stained with dark green or bright red blood, clearly from within the Cold Beasts.

"All of them... all have Earth-digging Realm Limit Cultivation!"

"What Earth-digging Realm Limit, look at the front one hundred, those holding different weapons, one, two, three... thirty-nine, forty, gulp..."

"Forty at the Cold Resistance Level, this... this... how is this possible!"

"Almost everyone has started Reshaping Skin, this..."

"Quick, look at what they’re carrying at the back!"

"Rodents, they’re Rodents, high-level Rodents, as many as eighteen!"

"There’s also a lot of intermediate Rodents, almost over a hundred."

"Look at what is loaded on that big cart in the middle, such a bright silver glow, it’s not iron ore, that’s..."

"Silver, it’s silver, that whole big cart, it’s all silver!"

.................

The first batch to migrate to Xia City tonight were mostly the core members of the seven families from Songyang Ridge. Compared to ordinary people, they were clearly more knowledgeable, not only recognizing the high-level Rodents but also realizing that the carts escorted by the Cloud Dragon Army were filled with silver.

At this moment, Ji Yang and the other Cold Resistance Levels were all speaking in trembling voices.

Especially following, Xu Ning and Li Longkai’s question and answer made their bodies shiver intensely.

"Silver, did the Minister lead the army to find a silver mine and start excavation?"

"No, it was seven days ago, Chen Ying found it on the mountain wall’s north slope. He was lucky; that silver mine was only occupied by one Rodent tribe. The Lord was exceedingly pleased upon learning this and immediately ordered Yuwen Sizheng’s Dragon Martial Army to return from Long Mountain, joined by the Minister’s Cloud Dragon Army and Yuan Sizheng’s Dragon Slayer Army. By the sixth of the month, the three armies went to clear out that group of Rodents. It’s estimated that the cleansing was completed a few days ago, and this should be the first batch of silver extracted, which the Cloud Dragon Army brought back. The Dragon Martial Army and the Dragon Slayer Army are still there."

Such an army, Great Xia actually has two more...

"Alright, Brother Ji, you follow Mr. Li inside. I need to report back to the Minister now. Mr. Li, see you later."

"Sure, see you later!"

Even after the army walked past, Ji Yang and the more than five hundred others took a long time to recover their senses. It was only when Xu Ning bid farewell that their thoughts were pulled back to the present.

"Leader Ji, right? Come in with me."

"Mr. Li, please don’t call me that, just call me Ji Yang."

Ji Yang finally let go of the conflict in his heart. Hearing Li Longkai’s address, he was so startled he repeatedly waved his hands, lowering his stance to the extreme.

Nie Xinchuan and the Cold Resistance Levels from the other five camps were even more so.
