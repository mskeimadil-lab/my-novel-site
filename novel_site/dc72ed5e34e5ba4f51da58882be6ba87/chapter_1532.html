Chapter 1532: Chapter 479: Brief Encounter, Dan Medicine Hard to Find, Eldest Young Master of Jiang Mansion in East City (Part 4)

Looks exactly like Cai Qiu!

Hearing Jiang Xinfan say his share of the Washing Marrow Pill was used up, and not much remained of the Bone Shedding Pill, Xia Hong’s brows furrowed even deeper.

He naturally couldn’t use Jiang Xinfan’s Cultivation Resources, so if he wanted to buy Dan Medicine, did he have to publicly handle Chen Cang’s identity?

This Feudal Town really is vigilant against outsiders!

Thinking about the encounter with the blue-robed scholar at the Worldly Noble Tavern, Xia Hong’s expression subtly changed, starting to seriously consider whether he should move as Hong Yu in Chen Cang.

It’s a bit worthless just for some Cultivation Elixirs!

It’s almost time to return, would this be creating complications at this juncture?

"By the way, Lord, there’s another way..."

Just as Xia Hong hesitated, Jiang Xinfan suddenly thought of something and quickly continued: "Frost Ash Association also has channels to purchase these Dan Medicine, but the price would overflow about twenty percent more than normal, and the purchase process is somewhat cumbersome, needing Ji Hong to accompany me to complete."

"Let’s do it this way! Go find the Frost Ash Association to buy them, Washing Marrow Pill, Bone Burning Pill, Top-grade Bone Shedding Pill, buy as many as you can. If you lack money or need help, you can tell me."

Almost forgot about the Frost Ash Association!

As for the twenty percent overflow price, it’s not even within Xia Hong’s calculation range, silver can be exchanged for power, no matter how expensive, it’s worth it.

Jiang Xinfan nodded, gathered all the silver ingots, and directly turned to leave.

This central high building in Jiang Mansion previously was where he lived alone, now naturally Xia Hong must stay here as well. Fortunately, with Xia Hong’s false identity as Elder Grandson, living here is reasonable. The servants in the mansion have already been instructed and arranged.

Xia Hong watched Jiang Xinfan leave, drew out the invitation from Congyang City from his Yellow Mustard Bag, and upon seeing the date of December 25th on it, his expression slightly heightened.

"It’s best if Dan Medicine can be bought, if not, no big deal. I’ll wait until the 25th of next month to enter Yang City. Today is November 5th, fifty days of time, better seize the moment to cultivate. Once out of Congyang City, I’ll return directly, no need to linger here and waste time..."

After pondering briefly, Xia Hong waved his hand to summon the Enlightenment Lotus Platform, sat on it cross-legged, and took out the last fifteen Bone Gathering Pills, ready to cultivate.

He had already asked Jiang Xinfan to buy Bone Burning Pills anyways, purchasing as many as he could, regardless of quantity.

Simply because after the final period of cultivation in Qingwu Village, he had completely confirmed that Bone Burning Pills not only had no negative impact on his body but also did not hinder his cultivation speed.

"Moreover, the magma vein patterns on my bones became deeper due to taking Bone Burning Pills, and the overall structure seems more solid. Following this, I need to test if the enhancement effect of Bone Burning Pills on my strength has any periodic limit; others affected by Dan Poison may use it only once every ten years, but my usage cycle couldn’t be that long. If there’s no periodic limit, that would be quite..."

Xia Hong slightly suppressed his inner excitement, calming his emotions, then slowly closed his eyes, officially beginning his cultivation.

The Jiang Family only opened their mansion in the east of the city in July, so their days here weren’t long, hence their reputation wasn’t big in the east of the city. However, neighbors still dwelled around, and for better integration into Chen Cang, Jiang Xinfan, along with Luo Ming and eight others, would occasionally interact with them, naturally leading to mutual understanding.

A new grand prince has arrived at Jiang Mansion named Jiang Hong, talented and highly favored by the Jiang Family Head Jiang Fan, being the only descendant among the Jiang Family who could live with the Family Head in the main residence.

As time passed, these news traveled swiftly and soon spread around.
