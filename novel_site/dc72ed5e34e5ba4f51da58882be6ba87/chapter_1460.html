Chapter 1460 - 463: Green-eyed Fire Suanni, Battle Royale and Advancement, Furious Counterattack, Trial’s Transformation (Part 5)

Chapter 1460: Chapter 463: Green-eyed Fire Suanni, Battle Royale and Advancement, Furious Counterattack, Trial’s Transformation (Part 5)

By relying on the Fire Suanni’s Fire Tribulation Qi, the Blood Jade Pearl and Blood Jade Crystal he swallowed were constantly being refined. His flesh and bone defects were quickly being repaired. His clothes were long gone, leaving him completely naked; although he appeared like a charred figure, his expression was increasingly invigorated, and the turmoil in his pupils was gradually diminishing.

"Roar..."

Yet, unlike him, the Green-eyed Fire Suanni, having chased him for a long time without success, was becoming increasingly frenzied. It evidently noticed Xia Hong constantly leading it in circles, and its roars grew more frequent.

Bang, bang, bang, bang...

Xia Hong fled forward again for quite some time. Suddenly, the sound of a landslide came from all directions. He looked around and was instantly stunned, his pupils filled with intense dread.

Dozens of Beast King Level Suanni, each measuring forty to fifty meters in length, were rushing in from the west and north, along with the Green-eyed Suanni pursuing fiercely from the east, leaving him with only one route to escape.

Swoosh...

Without hesitation, Xia Hong plunged into the depths towards the south.

No matter how dangerous the depths are, they cannot outweigh the importance of survival!

Given the number of Suannis, being surrounded would mean certain death.

"Roar..."

After summoning the tribe, the Green-eyed Fire Suanni ceased randomly releasing molten fire pillars, instead commanding with a few roars. The dozens of Beast King Level Suanni immediately spread out from east and west to continue chasing Xia Hong.

The Fire Suanni also accelerated, pushing Xia Hong farther into the depths, while coldness rose in its golden-green eyes.

The speed of the Beast King Level Suanni was still quite inferior to Xia Hong’s. Surrounding him naturally wasn’t easy.

Xia Hong ran south for five or six kilometers, realizing this, he immediately changed direction, turning westward in an attempt to escape the encirclement.

Bang...

However, the Green-eyed Fire Suanni didn’t give him a chance.

Whenever Xia Hong was about to break out of the encirclement, a pillar of molten fire shot up to the sky from the front, forcing him to change direction and continue south.

"52...53...54...55..."

It’s unclear how long it lasted. Xia Hong felt his body was about to burst from the Blood Jade Pearl and Blood Jade Crystal. He hadn’t engaged the Fire Suanni for a long time, only relying on feeling to estimate his power increase.

"56...57...58...59...60...61..."

Relying on the potent Medicinal Power of the Holy Vein Pill, Xia Hong’s injuries remained at the flesh and skin level. His veins continuously broke and healed, repeating this cycle endlessly.

But Dan Medicine will eventually be exhausted!

His Holy Vein Pills were down to nine when entering Blood Miasma Plain. During the pursuit through the Romantic Flower Sea, two were used, leaving a total of seven. He didn’t know how long these seven lasted, only that he sensed injury couldn’t be sustained and used them until they were completely finished.

"71 Jun, the bones are still a bit short of being perfect..."

Once more reaching into the Yellow Mustard Bag for the pill bottle containing Holy Vein Pills, he shook it twice softly, hearing no sound produced. Xia Hong’s brows furrowed deeply, and he threw the pill bottle away.

His flesh had long reached a perfect state, Blood Jade Pearls had no longer been needed for a while now, currently only the bones still had defects, so he exclusively took Blood Jade Crystal.

He could feel his bones were just one step away from perfect.

Precisely at this moment, the Holy Vein Pill ran out!

"71 Jun, under normal circumstances, even if I can’t say I’d kill it, at least I wouldn’t be forced to this extent. But now..."

Xia Hong glanced at the relentlessly pursuing Green-eyed Fire Suanni, clenching his fist, his face filled with fury.

The strength had increased, yet his current state was too poor.

After continuously consuming so many Holy Vein Pills, his meridians were already strained beyond capacity. When the Holy Vein Pill stopped, the side effects immediately appeared, moreover his injuries had long ago extended beyond just skin and flesh. Cracks appeared on bones in the back, chest abdomen, and side waist areas.

How poor was his state?

So poor that he couldn’t even perform martial arts properly!

Adding the Holy Pattern, at most he could display 106 Jun of strength, still unable to fight it. At this time, turning back for a direct confrontation, falling behind would mean certain death...

Xia Hong gritted his teeth and continued to sprint, catching a glimpse of the still relentless pursuit of the Green-eyed Fire Suanni, and those dozens of Beast King Level Suanni in the distance, inner anger rising continually.

"I’ve been running for almost two months, at the very least, this damned beast just insists on killing me. Are all these Five Luminaries Realm Beast Emperors so stubborn?"

Though he had completely lost concept of time, Xia Hong roughly sensed that this great escape had lasted nearly two months, enough that Cai Qiu’s disciples’ trials were nearing completion.

That Green-eyed Fire Suanni persisted so unwaveringly against him.

Truly defying all odds!

Swoosh...

While Xia Hong relentlessly cursed the Fire Suanni internally, a fierce azure wind suddenly flew towards his face. His thoughts were interrupted, causing him to tremor immensely, then instinctively without hesitation, he reached into the Yellow Mustard Bag quickly taking out a Dan Medicine.

The Dan Medicine was milky white inside, enveloped by a layer that appeared as molten fiery red stripes, exuding intense heat.

Without any hesitation, Xia Hong directly swallowed the Dan Medicine.

The moment the Dan Medicine entered his stomach, his previously charred black complexion instantly flushed crimson, and the molten patterns on his bones erupted with brilliant light.

The light was so intense, it shined through flesh and skin, the surface of his charred black body suddenly seemed to have countless strong light cracks. From a distance, it looked as though his body was on the verge of shattering.
