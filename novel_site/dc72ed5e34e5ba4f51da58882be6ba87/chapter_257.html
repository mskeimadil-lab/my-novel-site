Chapter 257: Chapter 152: Mirror Immortal’s Killing Intent, Who Has Been Framed?_3

"They plan to wipe us out in one fell swoop!"

Xia Hong turned back to look at the hundreds of people on the eastern side, a distance of fifty meters—enough for him to clearly discern their figures.

Seeing Yuwen Hu at the forefront, the murderous intent in Xia Hong’s eyes had already risen to its peak, yet he forcibly suppressed it. Raising his long blade, he transformed into a gust of wind, sweeping past Xia Chuan and the others, rushing to the front.

"Watch out for the arrows shot from behind, keep dispersing, run southward!"

Xia Chuan and the others understood Xia Hong’s intention the moment they saw his action, continuing to spread out laterally while maintaining their southern escape route.

In less than ten breaths, Xia Hong had traversed dozens of meters, rushing to the side of a large tree from where arrows were flying from the south.

The Yi Blade turned into a gust of wind. Without any hesitation, Xia Hong glanced at the direction from which the arrows came, leapt up, and swung his blade forcefully.

Squish...

A person dressed in black, holding a giant bow, didn’t even have the chance to scream before their body was slashed in two, falling straight down from the tree.

"Split up and run first..."

The death of the first person made the few from the Mirror Immortal Camp ambushed in the south realize the extent of Xia Hong’s power. Immediately after someone shouted, more than a dozen leapt down from the trees, scattering into different directions to escape.

"You want to run? Die, all of you!"

Xia Hong was not about to let them have their way. Although he came to the south primarily to aid Xia Chuan and the others in breaking through, it would be even better if he could slaughter a few more Earth-digging Realm members of the Mirror Immortal Camp.

He moved like lightning, and in almost an instant, he caught up with the nearest person. As he swung his Yi Blade with his right hand, he drew the Double-edged Giant Axe from his back with his left, tilting his head slightly, and hurled it at another person.

Crack... Boom...

As the Yi Blade cut down one person, the giant axe, which had been thrown, also smashed another into the snow, the crimson blood instantly staining the ground—the two lives extinguished in an instant.

Whoosh...

Glancing back, Xia Hong saw that out of the hunting team of seventeen, two had already fallen. His heart was uneasy, and his murderous intent grew even stronger. He directly flung his Yi Blade at the third person.

The slender Yi Blade, measuring 1.7 meters, pierced straight through the person’s heart like a spear. In less than ten breaths, three Earth-digging Realm members of the Mirror Immortal Camp were already dead by his hand.

The exact deployment of the Mirror Immortal Camp’s ambush was unclear, but the southern side had the fewest people, no more than ten. In the time that Xia Hong was here, he had already torn a breach on this side.

As if on cue, Xia Chuan and the others from the rear also charged over just in time.

"Keep running, don’t worry about me!"

Hearing Xia Hong’s words, Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan hesitated briefly, but seeing the unyielding look in Xia Hong’s eyes, they gritted their teeth and left.

"Don’t let them escape, brothers, hold them off! Once the Second Leader arrives with reinforcements, they’ll all perish!"

Amazingly, the nine people who had just fled from the south dared to rush back again when they saw the hunting team break through the encirclement.

As he watched the approaching nine, the murderous intent in Xia Hong’s eyes was chilling.

It wasn’t that he couldn’t catch up with those nine earlier; he stopped because he feared more casualties among the hunting team and wanted to shield Xia Chuan and the others.

Those nine people dared to come back just to try and hold them off.

"Not afraid of dying, then I’ll send you to your deaths!"

Xia Hong had no intention of escaping. With the Yi Blade thrown, he charged bare-handedly toward the one closest to Xia Chuan and the others among the nine.

The man, middle-aged in appearance, felt a flash of fear cross his pupils instinctively when he saw Xia Hong rushing over, but it quickly turned into ferocity and frenzy.

He discarded his longbow, raised his blade, and struck at Xia Hong vehemently.

"Over twenty thousand jin!"

At this moment, Xia Hong had no mind to admire the man’s fearless fighting spirit. Sensing his strength through his Qi Muscle, Xia Hong didn’t even bother to dodge, directly kicking diagonally at the incoming large blade.

Squish...

As the blade clashed with Xia Hong’s leg, the violent force caused the man’s grip to shatter. The blade followed its hacking route back.

The difference was that the returning force of the blade was greater, and its speed was faster.

Though its back was unsharpened, about two to three inches thick.

Yet, despite this, the back of the blade cleaved open the man’s face, hacking straight into his skull.

"The fourth one!"

After dispatching this person, Xia Hong immediately shifted his target, rushing to where the Yi Blade had been thrown, and after punching another person to death, he retrieved the Yi Blade and charged toward the remaining seven.

Though these twelve from the south were valiant, holding off the whole hunting team against Xia Hong was just a pipe dream.

The bodies lying on the ground should be Li Yun and Li Pingkai.

Xia Hong turned to the east side, recognizing the identities of the two corpses, and his murderous intent intensified, especially as he saw Xia Chuan and the others had successfully broken through to the south. Realizing he was now unburdened, his movements quickened.

Swoosh...

The seven knew they couldn’t match Xia Hong, yet they still tried pursuing Xia Chuan and the others ahead. Xia Hong ignored the arrows flying from behind, concentrating solely on killing these seven.

In less than five or six breaths, three of the slower ones among the seven had been killed by him.

Whoosh...

Just as he was rushing toward the fourth one, another swift sound flew into his ear. Xia Hong’s brow twitched sharply. This time, he chose to dodge rather than take it head-on.

Squish...

Seeing the iron arrow pierce through the tree, Xia Hong’s eyes first showed surprise, then as he turned to look behind him, his eyes filled with coldness at the sight of Yuwen Hu standing less than twenty meters away, holding a strong bow.
