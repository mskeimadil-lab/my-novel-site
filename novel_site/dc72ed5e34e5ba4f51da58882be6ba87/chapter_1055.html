Chapter 1055: Chapter 372: Chen Hua and Kong Xiu, Muyin’s Chaotic Troubles, Bashang’s Rout (Part 3)

Now, Jin Mountain wants to annex Muyin. Bashang can barely protect itself, and the vitality of Jiangxia is greatly damaged. The other Three Towns in the Central South are guarding against Dajue Temple, so it is estimated that they have neither the intention nor the energy to manage this side. But Beishuo and Wuchuan are different; they have strength and fear Jin Mountain growing too big, definitely won’t sit idly by."

"So it seems all Northern Three Towns are focusing on Muyin now. Should this information be sent back tonight?"

"Of course!"

The two deliberated for a while, integrating all the information they’ve gathered over the past two days, and waited quietly for the night to come.

Great Xia placed four clandestine agents in each of the eight town cities, with two specifically responsible for gathering, and the other two for transmitting. The ones responsible for transmitting, one south and one north, link the entire Eight Towns in a straight line, summarize information through Lu Yang at East Ridge, and finally pass it to Xia City.

The method of contact was set before departing from East Ridge last year. Unless special circumstances occur, even days are for receiving southward information, and odd days for sending northward. The specific way of connecting is to meet once at the agreed location during the first hour after nightfall, thus ensuring the information is delivered to Xia City at the first opportunity.

Today is the first day, precisely the day for sending information northward.

Kong Xiu has already infiltrated the Defensive Army and naturally cannot leave the town casually.

Thus, after nightfall, Chen Hua immediately armed himself with weapons and a bow, pretending to go hunting, and left the town.

Leaving the town heading east towards Moao Mountain, walking three to five kilometers, ensuring no one was following, Chen Hua then changed course, speeding northwest, covering over twenty kilometers quickly until he reached the agreed meeting spot.

The meeting time is the first hour after nightfall. If an hour hasn’t passed, it means the other party couldn’t come, and he needn’t wait, coming next time instead.

There have been many such special situations in the past, so Chen Hua was very patient, sitting beside a large tree, slowly watching the time pass.

But today, he didn’t wait long; ten minutes later, someone arrived from the north.

As he looked north, his expression changed instantly, jumping onto the large tree to hide swiftly.

Because more than one person came from the north, there were over twenty of them.

"Zheng Qing got caught? How come so many people!"

Muyin is responsible for transmitting information, specifically Zhang Fan and Zheng Qing, with the north one being Zheng Qing. The two have cooperated for half a year, very familiar with each other.

Previously, connections were always one-on-one; with so many people suddenly appearing, Chen Hua naturally became vigilant, thinking Zheng Qing might have been caught.

If he got caught, it would be a major problem.

Chen Hua’s face became much more somber, initially planning to flee, but gritting his teeth, he stayed, slowly drawing out the big saber from his back, trying to spot Zheng Qing among the twenty.

Fortunately, Zheng Qing was there.

Not only that, he was leading, apparently giving directions to the twenty behind him, constantly pointing to his location.

"Traitor!"

Chen Hua cursed these two words in his heart, observing Zheng Qing’s approach with intense killing intent.

But quickly, seeing the appearance of several people behind Zheng Qing, his killing intent vanished instantly, replaced by astonishment and joy.

He swiftly returned the big saber to its sheath, then, without hesitation, jumped from the tree and rushed toward Zheng Qing and the others.

"Subordinate Chen Hua, pays respect to the Director!"

Among the twenty behind Zheng Qing, the leader was none other than Director Yuwen Dao, and Chen Hua happened to know the two behind Yuwen Dao, the Vice Commander of Dragon Martial Army Hou Quan, and Dragon Martial Army Reconnaissance Camp Colonel Yuwen Yong.

Yuwen Dao and the nineteen behind him, though dressed in casual clothes, carried large packs, which by their shapes were easily discernible as war armor.

Dragon Martial Army, taking action?

In Chen Hua’s mind, countless thoughts flashed instantly, but ultimately, he could only reach this conclusion, still filled with confusion.

"No need for such formalities, Chen Hua, I’ve heard you’ve infiltrated Muyin Town, well done. What’s the current situation in Muyin Town, tell me."

Yuwen Dao’s compliment was not without reason; he had seen Chen Hua from afar, including all his subsequent changes in expression.

He could immediately guess Chen Hua’s thoughts, hence offering this positive evaluation. Yet, his temperament being naturally cold, being able to say these four words was quite a feat, and he directly started asking about military affairs afterward.

Chen Hua’s slightly excited reaction upon hearing the compliment indicated the difficulty of receiving praise from Yuwen Dao.

Chen Hua quickly recounted the latest in Muyin Town, naturally highlighting the Murong Family’s internal conflicts and Kong Xiu’s recent infiltration into the Defensive Army.

Though Yuwen Dao had little reaction to the Murong Family’s internal strife, given prior intelligence, upon hearing Chen Hua mention many with Cold Resistance Level infiltrating Muyin Town daily and partner Kong Xiu becoming a Defensive Army member, his face revealed a deep joy.

"... Recently infiltrating Cold Resistance Level, mainly from Jinshan Town, with many also from Beishuo and Wuchuan, while the nearby Dragon Valley, Yangqu, and Chui Mountain Three Towns remain unclear. Overall, the current town is roughly divided into two factions, one led by Lord Murong Yan backed by Jinshan Town, the other led by Town Chief Murong Chui, both holding equal numbers of Cold Resistance Level."

Murong Chui hasn’t surrendered to Beishuo yet!
