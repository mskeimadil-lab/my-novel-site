Chapter 194: Chapter 136 He Yuanhun, Yuwen Hu, Yuwen Dao (Three in One)_2

Yuwen Dao, along with the nine behind him, looked at the iron arrow on the ground, and then suddenly looked up at Xia Hong with a changed expression.

From such a distance, to pierce the ground so deeply, the archer’s skill definitely reaches the Earth-digging Realm, three arrows might not be much, but the problem is, who knows how many people and how many arrows are behind Xia Hong and his two companions?

Yuwen Dao’s originally smiling face immediately became much more solemn, and his mood was no longer as relaxed as before. He took two steps forward and spoke in a deep voice:

"Leader Xia, when Lu He joined our Mirror Immortal Camp, he had already transferred all the original territory of Lu Shang Camp at Redwood Ridge to us..."

"Didn’t they tell you how Lu Shang Camp’s territory came about?"

Before Yuwen Dao could finish speaking, Xia Hong interrupted him directly.

"No need for so much nonsense, I’m not interested in explaining the affairs of Lu Shang Camp to you. Let’s be straightforward, tonight, considering it’s your first offense, I won’t pursue it further. Leave the Demon Sheep, and never step into Redwood Ridge again, I can let you go!"

Xia Hong was also disinterested in explaining, directly stating the conditions for letting them go.

As soon as he proposed these conditions, the nine people beside Yuwen Dao reacted like their tails had been stepped on, each shouting angrily.

"You’re dreaming!"

"Boss, ignore him, we’re not afraid to die, let’s fight him."

"We’ve been fighting all day, and you think you can take advantage for free? Dream on."

"Boss, absolutely don’t listen to him. If he dares to kill us, when the Leader brings people over, his entire Great Xia Camp will have to pay for this..."

...

At this moment, it wasn’t just Xia Hong; even Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan beside him, upon hearing the last person’s words, their expressions immediately turned cold as well, and their gaze towards the ten people became icy.

Clap...

"Shut up!"

Before the last person could finish saying "bury," Yuwen Dao angrily scolded him to stop, even turning around to slap him hard.

The person was already injured, and this slap sent him flying two or three meters back, unable to even stand, falling directly to the ground.

"Leader Xia, my subordinate was out of line, speaking nonsense, please forgive us!"

After hitting him, Yuwen Dao quickly turned around, bowing deeply towards Xia Hong and the others, while internally cursing those nine as complete fools.

Hearing no response from Xia Hong and his companions for a long time, Yuwen Dao grew even more anxious and threw a glance at the large corpse of the Demon Sheep behind him, a look of pain flitting across his face, before gritting his teeth, lifting his head and saying:

"Leader Xia, we suffered significant losses to hunt this Demon Sheep. If we give away the entire prey, not to mention what my brothers would think, even I wouldn’t be willing. How about this, I’ll offer half of the blood from this Demon Sheep, would Leader Xia agree to this?"

In response to these words, the otherwise silent Xia Hong showed a slight change in expression.

Evidently, Yuwen Dao had noticed the unusual condition of Xia Hong’s skin and realized that Xia Hong was at a critical moment of breaking through to the Cold Resistance Level, desperately in need of Intermediate Cold Beast Blood.

Was it better to accept half of this blood, leaving behind a group of potential enemies?

Or refuse, and with Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and others, strike and eradicate Yuwen Dao and his group of ten right here.

"Big Brother!"

"Leader..."

Seeing Xia Hong in hesitation, Xia Chuan and Luo Yuan spoke up one after another. Xia Chuan’s eyes were cold, while Luo Yuan was more direct, gesturing lightly across his neck.

Understanding that both were urging him to give the order to attack, Xia Hong was somewhat moved.

Although they had just fought and were not in peak condition.

The ten before them were in a much worse state!

The severely injured Yuwen Dao, and the nine Earth-digging Realm members barely standing.

On their side, there were still nine archers hidden behind the trees!

"Leader Xia Hong, if I’m not mistaken, the previous incident with the Rodents, He Meng should have had quite a conflict with your camp. When he returned last time, he proposed to my leader He Yuanhun to lead people to the Great Xia Station to demand an explanation, but fortunately, my father dissuaded him, so Leader He did not agree..."

Yuwen Dao’s expression was slightly tense, clearly seeing the hesitation on Xia Hong’s face, with cold sweat forming on his forehead. After saying the aforementioned words, he didn’t forget to add:

"Oh, and I haven’t mentioned yet to Leader Xia, our Mirror Immortal population exceeds ten thousand, with hundreds at the Earth-digging Realm, and our camp is guarded by Leader He Yuanhun and my father Yuwen Hu, two Cold Resistance Level experts!

Leader Xia, my father has always believed that humans in the Ice Abyss World should support each other, thinking that enmities should be resolved rather than deepened. Mirror Immortal occupies Arrow Bamboo Forest, and will be neighbors with Great Xia going forward. Even if we are not cordial, it’s unwise to form deep grudges.

Leader Xia, don’t you think so?"

...

He Yuanhun, Yuwen Hu, He Meng, two Cold Resistance Level experts guarding...

The once indecisive Xia Hong, upon hearing Yuwen Dao’s words, had a subtle change in his expression.

Particularly when he met Yuwen Dao’s gaze, a hint of subtlety flickered, and his heart wavered.

Yuwen Dao was superficially saying that his father, Yuwen Hu, had persuaded He Yuanhun, preventing He Meng from carrying out his suggestion to retaliate against Great Xia, implying that his father had done Great Xia a favor.

Yet this was obviously not enough for Xia Hong to let them go, as words without evidence hold no weight. Who knows whether his claim is true or not.

The underlying implication in the middle part, however, truly moved Xia Hong.

The Mirror Immortal Camp, with a population exceeding ten thousand, hundreds of Earth-digging Realm experts, this information, Xia Hong could already roughly estimate earlier, but Yuwen Dao’s final statement about the two Cold Resistance Level experts guarding it caught Xia Hong’s genuine focus.
