Chapter 284: Chapter 160: Night Falls, It Has Arrived

Second floor corridor.

A Frost Wolf’s limbs were entangled by white silk threads, its body already flipped over, belly up, trying to turn around with teeth baring and claws waving.

Pfft...

A large knife suddenly slashed from the side, and the Frost Wolf’s belly was immediately sliced into two, spraying out dark green blood, with intestines and organs spilling all over the ground.

After Xia Chuan killed the enemy with a single stroke, the bulging veins on his hands gradually calmed down. He pulled out the knife, quickly turned around, and looked towards the northern corridor.

Yuan Cheng, Yue Feng, Zou Yuankai and over ten others were divided into three groups, each facing a Frost Wolf. All three groups were handling it with ease. On Yue Feng’s side, they even managed to corner a Frost Wolf, seemingly ready to kill it.

"Yue Feng, take your men to block the hole, leave this Frost Wolf to me!"

Xia Chuan leaped forward, slashing the Frost Wolf with a transverse knife, taking over the battlefield while shouting orders at Yue Feng and his team.

"Sir, we can’t block it; over a dozen have already come up!"

Just as Yue Feng was about to lead his men to the end of the corridor, Xu Ning’s warning voice came from not far away.

Xia Chuan swung his transverse knife to sweep the Frost Wolf aside, glancing back with a chill in his heart.

The two Frost Wolves that climbed up first had already been killed by him and the others. The four current Frost Wolves were the second wave that climbed up.

Four Frost Wolves, for over a dozen Earth-digging Realm level fighters, were more than manageable.

But the problem was, these four Frost Wolves caused them to miss the best opportunity to block the hole.

There were already more than ten Frost Wolves rushing into the corridor.

At the end of the corridor, Frost Wolves were continuously squeezing through the hole.

The moment those Frost Wolves’ heads peeked in, their eyes quickly focused on the furnace on the third floor, filled with excitement and madness.

"Roar..."

The Frost Wolves that hadn’t fully squeezed in were already this crazy; as for those inside the corridor, and the one fighting him now, there’s no need to mention their reaction.

Swish... swish... swish...

If Xu Ning hadn’t led a few men to madly shoot arrows to suppress them, the Frost Wolves in the corridor would have rushed into the main hall long ago.

In the main hall on the second floor, all the women and children in the camp were gathered.

"Take all the children to the third floor!"

Having finally resolved the Frost Wolf at hand, Xia Chuan pulled out his large knife, casually wiped the green blood off his face, and urgently spoke towards the center of the main hall.

"Yes, sir!"

Lin Wan, Shi Lu, and the other women in charge of looking after the children immediately sprang into action upon hearing the command.

"Children, hurry upstairs, quickly..."

"Alright!"

In the Ice Abyss World, the average lifespan of a weak camp is just over forty years; hence there are no elderly people, and Great Xia is no exception.

Among the 1088 people in Great Xia, excluding sixty-seven Earth-digging Realm fighters and over five hundred Logging Realm fighters, the remaining over four hundred children are mostly under the age of fifteen.

Under fifteen, there are differences as well. Older children have already begun training. Even if they haven’t reached the Earth-digging Realm, their strength is often three to four thousand pounds. The youngest are still infants who need to be carried.

Typically, such a large-scale Cold Beast invasion would surely make these never-before-out children panic-stricken, but in reality, aside from the occasional crying of a few infants, none of these kids made any noise.

The older children even helped hold infants. Even five or six-year-olds simply pursed their lips and silently followed the team upstairs in an orderly manner, without the need for Lin Wan and Shi Lu to worry.

When they saw the ferocious Cold Beasts, of course, there was fear in their eyes. Some, like Luo An and Zou Ping, had fathers fighting the Cold Beasts on the second floor. Apart from fear, they had worry for their fathers. Some women carrying infants were also concerned about their husbands...

Despite the myriad emotions in their eyes, knowing they couldn’t do much to help, their only option was to obediently listen to Xia Chuan and others, not to cause any more trouble.

So, even if they were terrified and flustered, they gritted their teeth and made no sound, not wanting to disturb those fighting the Cold Beasts.

"Sir, more Cold Beasts have entered the wooden house, and there should be a lot intruding through the partitions too. They’re all rushing towards the stove, and we can’t hold them back with just our people. If we don’t extinguish the fire now, it will be too late. I’ve already lit a regular bonfire with wood on the third floor, temperature won’t be a problem!"

Xia Chuan was blocking a Frost Wolf charging towards the main hall in the northern corridor when he heard Shi Ping’s loud shout from the staircase, and a trace of struggle immediately arose in his eyes.

Shi Ping’s voice was so loud that all the Earth-digging Realm fighters trying to block the Cold Beasts in the four corridors on the second floor turned to look at Xia Chuan.

Noticing the struggle on Xia Chuan’s face, everyone showed a hint of confusion.

Bam!

Coincidentally, at that moment, a room on the edge of the second floor’s main hall was suddenly broken from the inside. Flying out was the corpse of a Demon Sheep with its head bitten off.

Many people’s faces were stunned as they looked toward that room.

"Roar..."

A Golden Armored Thunder Kui, over nine meters long, slowly walked out of the room. All who saw the Kui felt a jolt in their pupils, filled with terror.

Subsequently, a few people noticed the Thunder Kui’s eyes, clearly recalling something, and the terror on their faces instantly disappeared, replaced with hope.

"This is an Intermediate Thunder Kui transformed from a wooden mannequin; it’s the leader’s wooden mannequin. Hahaha, we’re saved, we’re saved!"

Yuan Cheng’s words immediately cleared the confusion for those who didn’t understand.

Recalling the five wooden mannequins in the Martial Arts Training Ground, all Earth-digging Realm fighters, including those not fully on the third floor yet, instantly had hope on their faces.
