Chapter 651: Chapter 273: Top 100 Revealed, Entering the Hall, Team Numbers as Team Names

June 3rd, nightfall

The battle for the top hundred places in the Logging Realm groups began on time.

It is worth mentioning that Qiu Peng and others handed over the entire finals to Liu Yuan and fourteen other newly appointed Cold Resistance Level members, as they needed to prepare for the Cold Resistance Level group match later that night.

"Yuan Bai, keep it up, win another match, and you’ll have a chance to be in the top ten."

"Yuan Kong, go for it!"

"Third sister, make sure you get into the top three."

"Ah Hen, keep going! Try to surpass Father in rank."

.........

The ground floor of the main building was bustling with voices.

By the ten arenas, the air was filled with cheers from relatives.

Apart from family members, many from the Earth-digging Realm and participants who hadn’t made it to the top 128 were present as well. Listening to the cheers by the arena, while envious, they secretly vowed that next year they would take the stage themselves and make their families proud.

By the staircase on the second floor, Liu Yuan watched the arena with a group of Cold Resistance Level members.

Although they were said to be fully responsible, their actual role was merely to prevent unexpected incidents in the arena, as the Camp Supply Department people were in charge of managing the arena matches.

"Few new faces, most of the top 128 are still from Xia City, with only 14 from the three Longyou outposts, these youngsters have done well."

Hearing Liu Yuan’s words, everyone smiled and nodded.

The people from Wuyuan and Mirror Valley were originally from Xia City and were recently integrated into the camp along with the three Longyou outposts, which is indeed a bit different.

Though now everyone belongs to Great Xia, naturally, from a closeness perspective, they still hope to see Xia City names on the top hundred list.

Li Yuankun smiled and said, "I checked, most of the top hundred should be from Wuyuan, Ning Ge is really shrewd, knowing to let the Logging Realm from Wuyuan go out with the Hunting Team to acclimate to the environment in advance, they have 16 teams that advanced past the qualifiers and took up as many as 98 spots in the top 128."

All were originally from the early days on the dirt slope, and Li Yuankun’s close relationship with Xu An, Xu Ning’s brother, led him to call Xu Ning Ning Ge.

Everyone looked a bit surprised hearing Li Yuankun’s last words.

With 98 spots in the top 128 taken, the final top hundred list might see Wuyuan occupying at least seventy or eighty percent.

Only Liu Yuan appeared unfazed, clearly already aware of the situation, smiling at Li Yuankun, he said, "You might not know, Ning Ge came back from Wuyuan during the day, I heard he received three Yangyuan Pills from the leader, for him to share his experience with Qiu Peng, it’s expected that Xia City will also organize the Logging Realm to go out with the Hunting Team in the future."

Three Yangyuan Pills!

Li Yuankun and the others showed envious expressions upon hearing this.

"The leader’s commendation for Xu Ning seems to be aimed at fostering a competitive relationship between the outposts, annual matches henceforth should become livelier."

Ying Xuan sharply perceived Xia Hong’s intention behind the commendation.

Xiao Kangcheng also realized, nodding in agreement, "Certainly, and with the increase in personnel at each outpost, there’ll likely be a competitive relationship among Logging Realm, Earth-digging Realm, and even Cold Resistance Level groups."

"Heh, Brother Xiao sure dreams big, if even Cold Resistance Level groups compete, our Great Xia’s Cold Resistance Level numbers might be overwhelming."

"Hahaha..."

Xiao Kangcheng and the others laughed at Zhu Shun’s jest.

Yet, amidst the laughter, there was a distinct sense of anticipation.

Everyone understood the implication behind Zhu Shun’s words.

This year’s inter-outpost competition is limited to the Logging Realm group for the reason that the total number of Earth-digging Realm in Great Xia isn’t high. Apart from the Wushuang and Hanqiong outposts having a thousand each, qualifying them to host their preliminaries independently, the Earth-digging Realm numbers in the other three outposts are too few, necessitating their participation in Xia City.

The issue with Earth-digging Realm isn’t significant, given the current wave of breakthroughs observed in this year’s competition. By June next year, the number of Earth-digging Realm in Great Xia will certainly be sufficient to support independent preliminaries at each outpost, naturally leading to a comparison of Earth-digging Realm advancements at each outpost.

But if Zhu Shun’s suggestion about Cold Resistance Level group competition comes to fruition, the difficulty level would dramatically rise. At a minimum, each outpost would need enough Cold Resistance Level members to first manage an internal elimination round, right?

"I estimate that day won’t be far off; we already have 37 Cold Resistance Levels in Great Xia, and the numbers are only going to grow. Perhaps in three to five years, it’s entirely possible."

Li Yuankun’s bold statement slightly perked everyone up.

Even though they were somewhat skeptical, lifting their eyes to the top floor brought an unexplainable sense of hope.

They had a leader who excelled at creating miracles.

Perhaps, it really is possible!
