Chapter 1074: Chapter 377: Five-Headed Monster Face Maitreya, Five Viscera and Five Elements Evil-Breaking Method, Great Xia’s Eastward March Begins

"Ah......"

Moao Calendar Year 132, May 2

The sky was already fully bright.

The cold light scattered by the freezing sun illuminated Bashang Town completely.

But unlike before dawn, when the air was filled with the sounds of fighting, at this moment, there was no sound other than the biting cold wind. The entire town had fallen into a death-like silence.

The entire Bashang City seemed to have turned into a ghost town.

A scream suddenly pierced the heavens and the earth, breaking the eerie silence.

Over the town, the sky was full of azure spiritual light, which had already condensed into the body of a child clad in a yellow robe. However, this child was three hundred zhang long, essentially a thousandfold enlarged eerie giant child.

Strange, not merely due to its enormous size.

The giant child emitted a blue light all over, appearing ethereal rather than solid, and remarkably, it had five heads on its neck.

The faces of the five heads were basically the same, with skin delicate as that of a newborn infant, paired with long snow-white brows and massive earlobes hanging down. Their expressions were also extraordinarily consistent, all staring with open mouths in a laughing pose, unbelievably eerie.

Strangely enough, though the five faces clearly shared the same expression, the impression they gave was completely different.

The face in the center had a pair of glowing green eyes, a smile tinged with madness, and conveyed an extreme sense of anger.

The face on the left had golden pupils, seemingly smiling but with deep sorrow in its expression, giving a feeling of immense sadness.

The third face further left had bright yellow pupils, lips shining with a black luster, and a smile full of worry, as if pondering something.

The fourth face had water-blue pupils, with fear in its smile.

The fifth face with crimson pupils differed from the other four; it seemed genuinely joyous, yet paired with its exceedingly bizarre visage, the more genuine the smile, the more unsettling it became.

The giant child stood in the northern part of Bashang City, and the central head with glowing green pupils was lowering its gaze over the entire Bashang Town, opening its massive mouth to inhale violently. With each breath, it drew tens of thousands of azure spiritual light strands from the city.

However, following that scream, the giant child’s inhaling motion abruptly halted, seemingly sensing some intense agony, its pupils filled with bewilderment and rage.

"How dare you!"

"Why is there a Xianyang level coming?"

"It’s you, you’re not Xianyang level from the Nine Towns!"

........

Above the other four heads of the giant child, the Four Great Masters: Vajra, Hei Ming, Ju Yuan, and Yan Long were all staring above the central head, their pupils quivering, continuously exclaiming in shock.

On the top of the central head, a bright silver narrow long blade was inserted in the lower right chest of Master Wusheng. The tip had already pierced out a small half-meter from his back, with billowing black smoke emanating from the ends of the wound, Master Wusheng’s expression was the epitome of agony, clearly struck at a vital spot.

"You..."

Master Wusheng withdrew his consciousness placed on the Demon Dragon, lowered his gaze upon the black-robed man holding the long blade before him, and the colossal bone shadow silhouette behind the black-robed man. His pupils were full of suspicion and confusion, but he quickly realized that now was not the time for perplexity, his glowing green pupils suddenly condensed, locking eyes with those of the black-robed man.

The black-robed man was of course Xia Hong. The instant Master Wusheng’s eyes intersected with his own, he sensed something stirring restlessly in his lower abdomen, daring not to hesitate even a second, his left eye’s pupil condensed rapidly, instantaneously shooting out a violent beam directly at Master Wusheng’s left eye.

Ding......

Just how strong the Shattering Divine Light was, Xia Hong was unclear, but until now, whenever he had used it in battle, failing was generally not an option.

This time, he believed it wouldn’t let him down either.

However, he clearly underestimated the situation.

Master Wusheng’s left eye was indeed momentarily frozen after being hit by the Shattering Divine Light, and the reaction in Xia Hong’s liver area vanished too.

But it merely amounted to this.

"The Yin Yang Mirror of the Dark Realm Qishan Demon Lord, it’s heading for Ice Abyss too!"

Master Wusheng’s pupils quickly regained clarity, staring at Xia Hong’s left eye, his expression abruptly turning wary.

Recognized everywhere he went.

It seems the reputation of the Yin Yang Mirror is genuinely significant...

Hearing Master Wusheng’s comment and observing his eyes, Xia Hong couldn’t help but sigh inwardly.

Yet sighs aside, sensing the giant’s hands positioning upwards, reaching for him, with the other Four Great Masters simultaneously rushing toward him, he dared not delay and immediately grasped the Dragon Sparrow Saber, launching forward forcefully and colliding with Master Wusheng’s body.

Bang......

The three vertical stripes of blood-red Holy Pattern on Xia Hong’s brow had surfaced, precisely speaking, since the very moment he attacked Master Wusheng suddenly, he had unleashed every available method he could.

With brow Holy Pattern, bone shadow silhouette, Solidified Fire Oil, Mu Dong’s remade Ten Thousand Forging Level Silver Dragon Sparrow Blade, Yangxu Tripod fervently burning within him, even employing the Long Fist Form for striking from mid-air, along with his own devised Rapid Lightning Spear Technique.

All Five Great Masters present, creating such a terrifying Five-headed Mysterious-faced Maitreya, once deciding to act, it had to be with complete commitment.

The reason he chose to act was precisely because of this Five-headed Mysterious-faced Maitreya.

In the last moment before dawn, Xia Hong had infiltrated Bashang City, realizing all Five Great Masters were there, the thought of retreat had indeed crossed his mind.
