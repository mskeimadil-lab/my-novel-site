Chapter 785 - 312: Regaining Focus, Killing, Shock, and Submission (Part 3)

Chapter 785: Chapter 312: Regaining Focus, Killing, Shock, and Submission (Part 3)

"Those seem to be the families of Meng Ying, Jiang Ping, and several other nobles. Weren’t they supposed to switch allegiance to Great Xia? Why are they coming to the dungeon?"

"I don’t know, let’s hurry, the back is urging us more and more."

............

More than 120,000 people, took over two hours to arrive in the North District.

The space outside the dungeon in the North District was originally not large, so naturally, it was impossible to accommodate 120,000 people smoothly.

Thanks to Xia Chuan who specifically ordered people to demolish all the surrounding buildings, leaving a huge square of a kilometer square, and then having ten thousand-man teams led by Yuwen Dao maintain order while dividing the crowd into ten sections, all the people were successfully gathered.

Everyone faced the direction of the dungeon, and near the dungeon entrance, a temporary platform five meters high was built with wood, clearly to allow everyone to see.

Tens of thousands of people gathering together would be chaotic, with every cough making a scene, so naturally, the entire square was buzzing with noise and utterly disorderly.

On the high platform, Yuwen Dao frowned slightly due to the excessive noise below and cupped his hands towards Xia Chuan beside him, saying, "Minister, everyone is here, shouldn’t we make them quiet down?"

Hearing Yuwen Dao’s words, Xia Chuan shook his head and directly nodded towards Peng Bo beside him.

Peng Bo cupped his hands in understanding and walked to the back of the high platform.

"Mmm... mmm..."

"Mmm..."

..........

Soon, from the back of the high platform, soldiers of the Cloud Dragon Army wearing light gold armor, each leading a prisoner appeared. All the prisoners had iron hooks piercing through their collarbones and wood stuffed in their mouths, rendering them unable to speak, only able to make muffled sounds.

"Isn’t that Council member Xuanping?"

"His son is also there."

"Lord Xuanqiu is also present."

"They’re all from the Li Family!"

"Not just them, Lord Xuanqiu’s husband, Yang Fengping, is also there."

"Their son, Yang Gan, is in the front row too."

"They’re all the main and extended family of the Li Clan!"

..........

The first batch pulled out consisted of twenty-nine people in total. Besides the twenty-seven who attacked the first floor last night, two were seriously injured during the assault on the Beehive earlier. All were at the Cold Resistance level of cultivation, and their identities were either core members of the Li Clan or related by marriage.

The identities of those prisoners on the high platform quickly spread by word of mouth to everyone below.

For all those below, these twenty-nine Cold Resistance level figures were untouchable bigwigs in daily life, but now they were kneeling on the high platform, in front of them, under the guard of Great Xia soldiers. The psychological impact was imaginable.

Every single person had their eyes wide open, staring at the high platform.

The noise in the square quickly diminished.

As Yue Feng and the other ten Cold Resistance levels of Great Xia, walked behind the prisoners, raising the butcher knives in their hands, everyone’s expressions below became incredulous.

Could it be?

Click...

Clang...

With a slight wave of Xia Chuan’s hand, Yue Feng and the others simultaneously raised their butcher knives, and the heads of Li Xuanping and the ten in the front row were instantly severed.

The sound of the knives and the rolling of heads on the ground were almost synchronized.

The last bit of noise in the square vanished completely as ten heads rolled, with everyone’s already wide eyes now filled with shock and disbelief.

Of course, hidden deep within their eyes was a hint of... joy!

Click...

Click...

Yue Feng and the others went through two more rounds, and after all twenty-nine people on the high platform were executed, dozens of Golden Armored Soldiers immediately rushed out to collect the bodies.

Once the high platform was cleared, Xia Chuan waved his hand again.

Mmmmmm......

Another batch of over a hundred people was brought up by the Golden Armored Soldiers, almost filling the entire high platform. The executioners were now the Cloud Dragon Army soldiers who escorted them up.

Click...

The Cloud Dragon Army soldiers raised their knives and heads rolled continuously on the high platform.

Xia Chuan still did not speak, merely kept waving his hand amid everyone’s bewildered gazes.

This cycle repeated for six rounds.

After at least seven or eight hundred people were killed on the high platform, Xia Chuan finally stopped waving his hand.

Clatter......

The volume of blood splattered when someone was beheaded was already shockingly great, and with nearly eight hundred people being continuously killed on the platform, the entire platform was almost dyed red with blood, with some blood even dripping down from the eaves of the platform.

Such a scene of rivers of blood left even the executing Cloud Dragon Army soldiers, and those Cold Resistance level individuals like Yuwen Dao, Yue Feng, and Hou Jing standing on the high platform, feeling somewhat uneasy.

As for the tens of thousands below, they didn’t dare move, not even a little. Some infants, still eagerly awaiting food, had their mouths covered by adults, not daring to make a sound.

"Everyone!"

Xia Chuan slowly walked to the front of the high platform, and gathering his energy into his throat, at the quietest moment in the square, he projected his voice to reach everyone.

"The Longshan Li Family has been exploiting the people, oppressing the good folk, and has enslaved over four hundred thousand citizens of Longyou for decades, inciting public outrage and the wrath of the heavens. Now that our leader Xia Hong has taken control of Long Mountain, how can we allow it to continue?"

Xia Chuan’s last rhetorical question was shouted out angrily, pointing towards the last wave of uncollected corpses on the high platform.

Almost 60-70% of people in the square were startled with a shiver.

"There were a total of 788 executed just now, of which 438 were severely resisting during the siege last night, the rest were all members of the Li Family..."
