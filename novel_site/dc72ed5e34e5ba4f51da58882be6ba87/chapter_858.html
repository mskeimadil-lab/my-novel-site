Great Xia, Year Three, Fifth Day of the Eighth Month

As soon as August arrived, there was a noticeable change in the atmosphere of Xia City.

Especially in the Inner City.

Today’s Inner City Main Building has already become the area dedicated to handling official business for the Seven Departments of Great Xia. Except for the official staff of the Seven Departments, the Advanced Hunting Team under the Hunting Department, and the soldiers of the daily rotating garrison, no one else is casually allowed inside.

Lately, anyone returning from outside would inevitably glance towards the Star-picking Hall before entering the main building to inquire about the news.

The reason for this is that Lady Li Xuanling is about to give birth!

Those who can enter the main building now hold significant status in Great Xia and have all participated in last year’s Beast Tide battle in the Yanglu Realm.

No one is foolish here; at that time, Li Tiancheng had already mentioned that Li Xuanling’s fetus was two months old, so calculating the days, the delivery should be in this August.

Some had already leaked the news, and now everyone in Xia City knew that Lord Xia Hong’s child would be born this month.

If the birth of Xia Chuan’s first son, Xia Yuzong, last year only moved the Cold Resistance Level elite, then the two children in Li Xuanling’s womb have truly become the focus of the fifty thousand residents of Xia City.

In the Star-picking Hall, the maid deputy Han Shuang held a basin of hot water as she entered Li Xuanling’s room. Seeing that Li Xuanling was already awake and half-sitting in bed, she quickly walked over and draped a blanket over her.

"The lady is about to give birth, so be careful not to catch a chill!"

Seeing Han Shuang’s slightly scolding tone, Li Xuanling gently shook her head.

Despite being in the late stage of Cold Resistance, close to breaking through to the peak, even while pregnant, she is by no means fragile. Moreover, with the Cold Ruins Tripod always in her room, no wind could possibly make her catch a chill.

However, after living with Han Shuang day and night for the past six months, Li Xuanling had come to understand her temperament. If she were to argue now, Han Shuang would immediately become anxious, fearing she did something wrong.

So, Li Xuanling changed the subject and asked, "Has Lord returned from seclusion yet?"

Upon hearing this, Li Xuanling lightly furrowed her brows, clearly displeased.
