Chapter 859: Chapter 332: Chen Ying’s New Discovery, Xia Yuning, Good News Abounds_4

This Blood Agate Jade is a unique treasure of Beiyuan, nourished by a large amount of beast blood. Even those at the Cold Resistance Level can use it to restore their vitality, and for ordinary people, it enhances the skin membrane’s tolerance. I heard that in the Camp Supply Department, one tael costs 500 contribution points, and madam’s piece weighs over four pounds; it’s truly an extraordinary treasure, showing the lord’s favor towards the madam."

Upon hearing Han Shuang’s eager defense of Xia Hong, Li Xuanling looked up at her, her expression suddenly turning a bit peculiar, quickly forgetting her displeasure.

She realized that not only Han Shuang, but also those responsible for serving her, Nong Ying, Qiu Ru, Ming Yue, Hua Ling, including Han Yue, all the maids were almost fanatically obsessed with Xia Hong, the lord. They not only defended his image everywhere publicly, but also would not allow anyone to defame him privately.

As long as she showed any disapproval towards Xia Hong, Han Shuang and the others would immediately speak for Xia Hong, partly to maintain his image, and apparently also fearing disharmony between the two.

"Han Shuang, I remember you and your sister Han Yue both came from Hanqiong to Great Xia, not too long ago, right?"

Han Shuang first nodded and then remembered something, her face full of reverence, saying, "Back then, when Hanqiong was in trouble, if not for the lord’s rescue, my sister and I would not be alive, nor could we live the life we have now."

Li Xuanling’s expression paused, and she immediately recalled the events of Hanqiong Gourd Valley, instantly understanding why the two sisters were so loyal to Xia Hong.

She suddenly felt a bit of mischievousness and quietly asked Han Shuang:

"Your basic strength is over twenty thousand pounds now, meaning you could live a normal life. Are you really willing to stay at the Star-picking Hall serving me?"

Sure enough, upon hearing her question, Han Shuang’s complexion immediately turned pale, her eyes reddened, and she knelt down with a thump.

"Madam, did Han Shuang do something wrong? Please, madam, don’t send me away. I willingly choose to stay by your side forever. Han Shuang is truly willing, please, madam..."

"No, no, no, what are you so nervous about? I was just teasing you!"

Li Xuanling, of course, didn’t let her kneel; she reached out to help her, soothing her gently upon seeing her tense, fearful demeanor, feeling a bit guilty.

It’s understandable, not to mention Hanqiong Camp, but compared to life in Long Mountain, life in Great Xia is undoubtedly superior.

Moreover, Han Shuang could live in the Star-picking Hall; not to mention anything else, living with Xia Hong means what Han Shuang wears, eats, and uses is beyond imagination for most people in Great Xia.

Her status is also special; having served Xia Hong long-term, she can even speak with Xia Chuan from time to time. Just this privilege alone is unimaginable for many in Great Xia.

Even those at the Cold Resistance Level in the camp greet Han Shuang proactively, let alone ordinary people.

To live a normal life is almost a punishment for maids like Han Yue and Han Shuang.

"Alright, enough of that. What’s happening in the city lately?"

Seeing Han Shuang still looking anxious and worried she might overthink, Li Xuanling hurriedly changed the subject, asking about happenings in Xia City.

Han Shuang stood up and wiped her teary cheeks, replying with a sobbing tone, "The most lively event recently, of course, is the two His Highnesses in madam’s belly. Everyone in Xia City is guessing when the two His Highnesses will be born. People who come to the main building inquire about it every day, and when Nong Ying and I go to the Camp Supply Department to get things, we’re asked several times."

Li Xuanling couldn’t help but smile when hearing her speak with a crying tone, then continued asking, "Besides this matter?"

She knew the attention her unborn children received well. Her second uncle, Li Tiancheng, nearly inquired every few days, making her quite weary.

Han Shuang thought for a moment, then her eyes brightened, saying, "Oh, there’s another thing I forgot to tell madam. Minister Xiao Yu, the Second Madam of Xiao Ning’s family, seems to be due this month. I heard from my sister that the lord informed the Camp Supply Department recently to prepare gifts, presumably for the young master."

Xiao Yu is due to give birth!

Li Xuanling immediately recalled Xiao Yu, the slightly younger second wife of Xiao Ning she met in early Xia City last year.

"Also this month..."

Li Xuanling pondered for a moment, then glanced at the Blood Agate Jade on the table, gently breaking off a small piece, handing it to Han Shuang, saying, "You take this piece of Blood Agate Jade to the Craftsmen Department later, have them carve it into two jade rings for me. When Madam Xiao gives birth, send them over together as a gift from me, the aunt, to the two young masters."

"Han Shuang obeys!"

Han Shuang took the Blood Agate Jade, smiling, "My sister and I guessed today whether the two His Highnesses will be older or younger siblings. Would madam like them to be born later or earlier?"

Li Xuanling smiled upon hearing this, gently stroking her belly with a maternal glow, saying, "Certainly, being younger is better. I hope Yu’er gives birth before me so the two children can have an older brother or sister."

Compared to others’ cautiousness, Li Xuanling was much more casual; she thought it was the same whether the baby was a boy or a girl, so she directly mentioned having a brother or sister.

Han Shuang naturally didn’t correct anything, smiling, "Then Han Shuang also hopes the two His Highnesses will be born a few days later; madam will surely have her wish come true."
