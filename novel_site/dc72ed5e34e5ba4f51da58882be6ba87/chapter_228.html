"Don’t touch that Teng Jiao, it’s poisonous!"

"The Hunting Team’s strength is really getting stronger and stronger."

......

"Hahaha, they’ll never guess in their dreams that we got these two prey for free, oh, and there’s also the blood of a Demon Sheep, haha!"

In the main hall on the second floor of the wooden house, hearing the enthusiastic discussions downstairs, Yuan Cheng looked down at the prey below and couldn’t help but laugh out loud, his face full of pride.

The others, hearing this, also laughed heartily.

"Hahaha, the Mirror Immortal Camp has worked for us for free tonight!"

"Weren’t they arrogant? This is how we should punish them."

"They were the ones who ignored the leader’s warning and hunted recklessly beyond the boundary, they deserved it!"

"If they come to hunt in Redwood Ridge, isn’t it just right for them to work for us?"

"If we encounter them again, we have to continue to deal with them like this."

...

Hearing everyone’s joyous laughter, Xia Hong’s expression also relaxed a bit.

Doing things that harm others for personal gain is not in line with his values.

But that, of course, depends on the specific situation.

Since the Mirror Immortal Camp ignored his warning and recklessly invaded Great Xia territory, hunting beyond the boundary, then he couldn’t be blamed.

"Leader, in the future, let’s hunt north every three days, if we encounter them once, let them go back empty-handed once, keep doing this until they don’t dare to venture into Redwood Ridge anymore!"

Upon hearing Yuan Cheng’s proposal, the others nodded in approval immediately.

Xia Hong showed a hint of temptation on his face, but after pondering for a moment, he still shook his head and said deeply: "No surprises, He Meng must be fuming right now, let’s see how the Mirror Immortal Camp reacts after he returns, we’ll continue with our regular hunting, just go north occasionally to check!"

At the mention of the Mirror Immortal Camp, everyone immediately calmed down.

Indeed, He Meng alone is nothing, what truly needs to be worried about is the Mirror Immortal Camp behind him.

With numbers far surpassing Great Xia in the Earth-digging Realm, and two Cold Resistance Level powerhouses akin to cornerstones, from any perspective, it’s not wise for Great Xia to fall out completely with the Mirror Immortal Camp right now.

Even knowing full well that the other side is invading their territory, they can only endure.

Just like tonight, judging from Xia Hong’s strength displayed while he killed the Teng Jiao at the end, they could easily have killed He Meng and his group directly.

But Xia Hong chose not to do so, and that’s precisely the reason.

Great Xia’s current strength is not enough to support them in falling out completely with the Mirror Immortal Camp. If those two Cold Resistance Level powerhouses were to take action, the outcome could be disastrous.

"For now, let’s leave it at that, don’t completely anger them, but we also can’t ignore their transgressions, let’s check the north occasionally in the future!"

Checking, of course, doesn’t just mean checking. If they can, like tonight, reap three heads of prey without doing anything, they certainly wouldn’t miss such a good opportunity.

The entire Hunting Team showed smiles of mutual understanding on their faces.

"Harming others and benefiting self does feel good, it’s much easier than dragging a team to fight with those Cold Beasts!"

Xia Hong’s eyes flickered slightly; if he weren’t worried about thoroughly offending the Mirror Immortal Camp and provoking those two Cold Resistance Level powerhouses, he’d really want to mess with He Meng a few more times like this.

The feeling of getting something for nothing is indeed delightful.

But enhancing one’s strength should still be the priority!

"Draw the blood and bring it to my room."

"Yes, Leader."

Xia Hong turned to instruct Xia Chuan, then went upstairs directly.

Last time there were still three Snow Mane’s blood unused. Adding the Teng Jiao, Thunder Kui, and Demon Sheep recovered this time, there are six in total, at least three or four hundred pounds.

At a progress of fifty pounds per point, this will immediately boost the progress by 7 to 8 points, Basic Strength, increasing by about ten thousand pounds.

"More importantly, this time we also figured out the hunting method for Teng Jiao. Last time we also saw Yuwen Dao hunt a Demon Sheep, with the Demon Sheep and Teng Jiao, we can start to try hunting them in the future. In this way, the hunting efficiency can be improved further, and my skin membrane progress can be faster..."

......

Inside Redwood Ridge, the group of He Meng who had scattered and fled gradually returned to the previous location of the Teng Jiao hunt.

Looking at the empty ground and the large areas of blood seeping from the Teng Jiao near the swamp, He Meng and the others instantly looked livid.

The ground was so clean, it was obviously not the work of Cold Beast cannibalism, meaning that those two prey were taken away by the people of Great Xia.

"Damn bastards, not only did they screw me over, but they also took the Thunder Kui and Teng Jiao away!"

He Meng’s chest heaved with anger, his eyes fixed on the southern side of Redwood Ridge, as if weighing something.

"Boss, something’s wrong!"

Suddenly, a call came from the east side.

Everyone turned to look.

The newcomer, also filled with rage, approached He Meng and said:

"Boss, the blood of that Demon Sheep has been completely drained!"

Upon hearing this, He Meng’s face first turned stiff, then fury instantly rushed to his head, and he drew the large knife from his waist.

"Everyone goes to the south to find, find Great Xia’s camp, we must find Great Xia’s camp, if I don’t slaughter that gang of dogs, then my surname isn’t He!"
