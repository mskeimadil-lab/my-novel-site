Chapter 472 - 216: Great Medicine, Going Out, Turmoil in Longyou (Part 2)

Chapter 472: Chapter 216: Great Medicine, Going Out, Turmoil in Longyou (Part 2)

Looking at the person directly across from the big pot, still talking to himself, Xia Hong, although not responding, lowered his head and a spark of brilliance instantly erupted in his eyes.

This could also be considered a breakthrough in adversity.

Zhu Yinhua, White Frost Grass, Cold Apple Jade Dew!

He never expected that the person opposite would tell him the names of three medicinal ingredients all at once.

Seeing that person’s clothes clearly better than others’, Xia Hong was slightly moved, worried about being detected, remained silent, stirred a few spoonfuls, and then quietly sneaked down.

"Pharmacist Liu, it seems the fire isn’t hot enough!"

"Go out and ask someone to bring more coal rocks. One hundred and fifty thousand catties is not enough, we’ll need over two hundred thousand catties for this batch of Jade Bone."

"Okay, I’ll inform someone to go right away."

......

At this moment, someone below the big pot spoke, letting Xia Hong know the identity of the person who had just spoken to him.

A pharmacist?

From the Hive Camp specializing in alchemy, so that’s it.

One hundred and fifty thousand catties, over two hundred thousand catties, when stirring earlier, Xia Hong saw with his own eyes that the Jade Bone in the pot was just over three pounds, deducing from the last harvest of the Advanced Snow Mane Broken Arm, it should be the bones of a whole Advanced Cold Beast.

Just over three pounds, requiring over two hundred thousand catties of coal stone to brew.

This level of consumption...

While clicking his tongue mentally, Xia Hong leaned against the windowsill, seized a gap, directly slipped out of the windowsill, and silently descended along the outer wall of the wooden building to the fourth floor.

Just as he returned to the windowsill of Cheng Ping’s room, Xia Hong suddenly looked up, glanced towards the top floor, his eyes revealing a glint.

"One, two... three... four, all four people have gone out, what are they going out for, hunting, or is there something else?"

Xia Hong pondered for a moment, then turned back, mouthed silently towards the fourth-floor room, then leapt towards the direction of the door.

......

"You go back first, I have some matters to attend to. Every ten days from now, come to me for the antidote."

Cheng Ping, waiting anxiously on the fourth floor, suddenly heard Xia Hong’s voice, his body jerked in surprise, glanced around once or twice, found no trace of Xia Hong in the room, and immediately nodded respectfully, "Yes, sir!"

That Hong Yu, wasn’t discovered after all!

Cheng Ping was sure that the Great Leader’s three were on the top floor today.

But Xia Hong just snuck up there but wasn’t found.

What does this mean?

Gulp...

Cheng Ping swallowed a mouthful of saliva, his pupils instantly filled with fear.

"This Hong Yu’s ability... no, I must be more obedient from now on!"

......

It was already dawn.

The sky above Hive Camp was bright.

Due to being located in a plain area with sparse vegetation, the tracks of Chen Yingyuan’s group of four walking were exceptionally clear, Xia Hong emerged from underground, came to the surface, and it didn’t take much effort to find them.

"Heading north, it shouldn’t be hunting, I’ll follow and take a look."

Redwood Ridge is south of the Hive, and Chen Yingyuan’s group of four heading north, naturally wasn’t hunting, but what else could make them move out all at once?

Besides wondering why four people moved out at once, the identity of Hive’s fourth Cold Resistance Level person also intrigued Xia Hong. After a simple disguise, Xia Hong quietly followed their tracks.

Xia Hong walked north along the path for five or six kilometers, finally seeing four figures from afar on a dirt slope.

Despite being five or six hundred meters away, Xia Hong immediately recognized that three of them were Chen Yingyuan, Wu Tianxing, and Chen Yingba.

Meanwhile, he also saw clearly the true visage of the fourth Cold Resistance Level figure.

It was a purple-faced, broad-faced middle-aged man, his outfit a fine black, standing over two meters tall, with a robust physique like a towering iron tower, carrying a silver eight-sided giant hammer on his back, arms crossed before his chest, exuding a domineering presence.

Xia Hong gauged the surrounding environment, soon selecting a large tree just over two hundred meters from the dirt slope, quietly climbed up the tree, and listened silently to the four’s conversation.

"Leader Chen, is there a need for such excess?"

The purple-faced middle-aged man’s voice boomed like a bell, matching his domineering aura, although now looking at Chen Yingyuan, his expression was clearly a bit impatient.

"It’s about the alliance, I hope Brother Hou will understand!"

Chen Yingyuan was very patient, and after speaking, he immediately fell silent; Chen Yingba and Wu Tianxing, standing beside him, also remained silent, standing quietly.

Seeing this, the man with the surname Hou slightly shook his head and also fell silent.

Regarding the alliance...

Hiding on the tree, Xia Hong’s eyes suddenly shone brightly.

Today is already the end of February, and with just over a month to go until mid-April’s alliance, these four came to discuss the matters of the alliance!

However, what the middle-aged man with the surname Hou meant by saying unnecessary, Xia Hong still didn’t understand.

But soon, with the approach of two more people from the north.

Xia Hong immediately understood what it was about.

The two people coming from the north, both middle-aged in appearance.

The person on the left wore a yellow outfit, small and lean, with rugged features, and strangely large fists, a black stick roughly a yard long slung diagonally across his back;

The person on the right was just the opposite, tall, handsome, with a slim mustache on his chin, a longsword at his waist.

"Wu Xiong, pay respects to Lord Hou!"

"Han Jiuli, pay respects to Lord Hou!"

The two, upon reaching the dirt slope, first saluted the middle-aged man with the surname Hou, then turned to look at Chen Yingyuan, with a slight smile, and spoke.

"Old Chen, long time no see."
