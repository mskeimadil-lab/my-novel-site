Chapter 1403: Chapter 452: Gathering from All Sides, Chui Mountain Concluded, Lord of Nine Towns, Alliance Finalized

"All of you, surrender immediately, lay down your weapons, and your lives will be spared!"

The display of five heads by Yuan Cheng not only shocked the groups from the feudal towns led by Cai Shiqi and Chu Yan’er but also made the brows of the brothers Yang Zhun and Yang Fa twitch violently.

The entire Inner City, already plunged into a deathly silence due to the sudden emergence of a large number of Xia Army, was instantly filled with Yuan Cheng’s powerful shout, which reached everyone’s ears. Except for those at the Xianyang Level and a few individuals with strong Cold Resistance abilities, everyone shuddered with fear.

Especially the two thousand Beishuo Army soldiers and the over one thousand Duan Family members who had retreated to the Duan Clan Land; not only did they shudder, but their faces involuntarily turned pale.

They understood clearly that Yuan Cheng’s target for this shouted command was them.

"All of you, surrender immediately, lay down your weapons, and your lives will be spared!"

"All of you, surrender immediately, lay down your weapons, and your lives will be spared!"

......

However, Yuan Cheng’s shout was only the beginning.

Qiu Peng, Xu Ning, Liu Yuan, and Hou Quan quickly followed suit, shouting with full force, while Yue Feng, Zhao Long, Meng Yi, Hou Tong, Hong Guang, Hong Tian, Zhou Yuan, Zhu Qing, Li Longkai, and Hou Ming — ten commanders or vice commanders — not only joined in but also gestured to the soldiers of the army behind them.

"All of you, surrender immediately, lay down your weapons, and your lives will be spared!"

......

A formidable army composed of fifteen thousand soldiers at the Cold Resistance Level, even if they did nothing but stand under the cold sun, exuded an immense pressure.

Moreover, at this moment, their imposing air was unleashed, as they shouted powerfully toward the Inner City.

The swirling energy blood soared, the fierce air currents dispersed the clouds above the Inner City in an instant, and it seemed that the sound had shaken the entire Chui Mountain Town, surpassing the mere five-kilometer diameter area of the Inner City.

The earth trembled ceaselessly, even the faces of brothers Yang Zhun and Yang Fa changed as they quietly ascended into the air; not to mention the over three thousand battered soldiers shrinking in the Duan Clan Land, their expressions turned bleak, faces swamped by despair.

Clang... Clang...

A few scattered soldiers even dropped the weapons from their trembling hands.

At this point, resistance was utterly futile.

The moment the fifteen thousand Cold Resistance Level Xia Army appeared already sealed the fate of the Beishuo and Duan Family rebels, not considering their earlier engagement in fierce battle against Mu Family’s elite troops for over an hour, leading to exhaustion; even in their prime, they’d stand no chance.

If they wanted to survive, disarming was the only option!

Clang... Clang........

The Duan Family acted fastest, immediately discarding their weapons, emerging from the Duan Clan Land to kneel and surrender to the nearest Cloud Jiao Five Armies;

The two thousand Beishuo soldiers seemed to cling to hope, turning to look at Lord Yang Zhun, realizing Yang Zhun and Yang Fa had ascended to the sky, despair rose in their eyes, leading them to gradually drop their weapons.

"Confiscate their weapons, strip off their war armor, and imprison them all!"

The joyous tone in Yuan Cheng’s voice was evident, for though the Xia Army was unafraid of war, achieving victory without any casualties was naturally most favorable.

As the Cloud Jiao Five Armies soldiers began detaining the defeated forces from the Duan Clan Land, the nearly two-hour-long mutiny finally reached its conclusion.

Duan Hong was slain, and the Duan Family along with Beishuo’s defeated forces entirely surrendered, as the Mu Family faction achieved a decisive victory under the support of Great Xia.

From this day forth, Chui Mountain has completely changed hands!

Yang Zhun clenched his fists beneath his sleeves, looked first at Mu Longhe, then quickly shifted his gaze toward Li Xuanling standing in the air.

Naturally, not only him, Yang Fa, Cai Shiqi, Chu Yan’er and all other defeated troops, even the victorious Mu Family army, focused their gaze on Li Xuanling at this moment.

Since Li Xuanling had just ignored Chu Yan’er’s inquiry, as of now, besides Great Xia and a few others, the rest were still unaware of her exact identity.

When did Great Xia produce such a formidable Xianyang Level figure?

Who is she?

Very soon, everyone received an answer.

Mu Longhe mid-air suddenly bowed towards Li Xuanling: "Great Xia’s righteous intervention in battle, Chui Mountain is eternally grateful. On behalf of everyone in Chui Mountain, Mu Longhe expresses gratitude for Madam’s assistance!"

Below, Mu Longhe, Zhu Ziying, Helian Shan, Qiu Sanming, and other Mu Family members saw their father bowing to Li Xuanling, and hurriedly followed suit, bowing: "Thank you, Madam, for your help!"

"Thank you, Madam, for your help!"

Among the Mu Family participants in the mutiny, totaling over ten thousand people, only about six thousand remained standing, all bowing in unison.

Madam, who else in Great Xia bears this respectful title?

Li Xuanling!

Upon hearing Mu Longhe’s address, Yang Zhun, Yang Fa, Chu Yan’er, Cai Shiqi, and others immediately recalled the name Li Xuanling, faces flushing with shock and amazement.

Great Xia had integrated into the Nine Towns Territory nearly two years ago, though Xia City remained mysterious to outsiders, with the Hongmen trade outlet present, plenty of internal information from Great Xia inevitably spread.

For instance, Xia Hong has married, his wife named Li Xuanling, possessing notable strength, this piece of news was among them.

However, the phrase ’notable strength’ leaves much room for imagination!

It’s undeniable, women in the Nine Towns Territory still hold relatively low status, perhaps the entire Ice Abyss region shares this characteristic.

A woman described with ’notable strength’ already signifies an impressive feat; many speculate she’s at most a powerful Cold Resistance Level, either 30 Zong... 40 Zong, at best possessing a formidable strength of 50 Zong.
