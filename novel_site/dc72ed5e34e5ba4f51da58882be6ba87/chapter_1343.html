Chapter 1343: Chapter 436: Qingxun Treasure Tree, Black Wind General and the Toll, Courage and Rescue (Part 5)

"29."

"That many? I only got 12."

"Don’t just capture them. Break their limbs first and then throw them in."

"Hahaha, makes sense."

......

As the soldiers kept laughing sadistically, the remaining over two hundred young people had long lost their courage, their eyes filled with panic and fear, and there were hardly any resisting anymore.

"Xing’er, I’ll charge there with Second and Third Brother, and you run!"

On the west side of the crowd, a soldier in armor punched fiercely, and before Su Xing’er could react, she was knocked away by her big brother, Su Jing.

Crack...

"Big Brother!"

Su Xing’er saw her big brother, Su Jing, lose his left arm, cut off by the soldier’s blade as he saved her. She let out a wail, tears streaming down her face.

Bam...

However, with only a basic strength of over 60,000 pounds, Su Jing was no match for the Cold Resistance Level soldier. Losing his left arm was just the beginning. The soldier swiftly retracted his big saber, and with his right hand, he grabbed Su Jing’s throat, tossing him towards the Bone-Eroding Path.

At the critical moment, Second Brother Su Xu and Third Brother Su Zhi rushed over. One grabbed the soldier’s right arm, and the other locked around his neck, fighting desperately to prevent him from throwing Su Jing.

"You little brats... ah!"

The soldier, pinned down by the two brothers, didn’t panic at all. After letting out a vicious laugh, he jolted his body, attempting to shake them off. But just as he moved, he let out a scream.

It was Third Brother Su Zhi, who had somehow pulled out a dagger and stabbed it into the soldier’s left eye.

Though the soldier possessed the strength of eight cavalrymen, the eye was still a vulnerable spot. Caught off guard by the stab, he quickly channeled his energy to protect his eye, stopping the dagger from penetrating further, but his eyeball was ruined.

"You bastards, you bastards, you bastards..."

With one eye blinded, the soldier’s rage reached its peak, dooming his future. The fact that it was four young men at the Earth-digging Realm Limit who ruined him added to his fury.

Bang...

In a frenzied rage, he shook off the two clinging to him. Looking at the beautiful Su Xing’er not far off to the side, countless ways to exact revenge flashed through his mind.

With his right hand still gripping Su Jing’s throat, he thought of killing him directly but remembered the captain’s orders. With a roar of rage, he hurled Su Jing toward the Bone-Eroding Path.

"You dare ruin my eye? I’ll make you watch as I play with this bitch until she’s barely alive, ahhh!"

Rip...

The soldier’s shouts didn’t cease. Instead of targeting Su Xu and Su Zhi on the ground, he dashed towards Su Xing’er, lifted her, and tore her clothes with one swift motion, clearly intending to violate her.

"Help me, help me, Big Brother, Second Brother, Third Brother, save Xing’er!"

"Hehehe, save you? With what? I’m saving them to watch as I toy with you, wretched girl, wretched girl!"

It wasn’t so much lust driving him as pure anger, seeking vent through this means to alleviate the pain of losing his eye.

"No!"

"Xing’er..."

Seeing the soldier already strip Su Xing’er of nearly half her clothing, and hearing their sister’s helpless cries for help, Su Xu and Su Zhi were filled with despair and unwillingness, biting their lips almost to the point of drawing blood.

Thud...

But just as the soldier was about to strip Su Xing’er of her last bit of modesty.

A stone suddenly flew out from the Bone-Eroding Path, piercing straight through the soldier’s brow, freezing him in place.

"Who is it!"

"Wasn’t that guy thrown in?"

"How did he get out?"

......

He Qing was the first to notice the stone. He jerked his head towards the Bone-Eroding Path, a look of shock on his face.

Seeing Su Jing, who had been thrown in, walk out of the Black Wind Zone unharmed, the soldiers were all stunned.

Bang...

The next moment, a young man in black suddenly flew out from the Bone-Eroding Path.

No, it was more like he was burst out.

The young man in black, holding a Starlight Long Sword, with a face pale from blood staining half his body, was violently ejected and flung northward dozens of meters, crashing into the snow.

Puh...

The young man’s breath was chaotic, and after spitting out a mouthful of blood on the ground, he turned to look towards the Bone-Eroding Path, eyes full of fear.

"It can’t come out?"

But soon, he realized something, and the fear in his eyes vanished, replaced by a look of intense joy.

However, the joy didn’t last long.

The young man turned to He Qing and the soldiers, his expression suddenly becoming incredibly cold.

"Toll road, so that’s what it meant!"
