Chapter 800: Chapter 316: Blood Jade Fox and Teasing, Boiling Xia City, Migration Begins (Part 3)

Li Xuanling and Li Tiancheng both walked over from behind at this moment.

The two of them looked at Xia Hong, the shock on their faces still hadn’t faded. They were clearly amazed by how easily and thoroughly he had slain a Blood Jade Fox.

"You managed to kill a Blood Jade Fox on your own?"

Li Xuanling couldn’t help but exclaim, and after shouting, she looked at the Blood Jade Fox in Xia Hong’s hand, with a noticeable expression of desire flashing in her eyes.

Li Tiancheng’s mouth was agape, and after a long silence, he slowly spoke:

"Leader Xia, your strength is exceptional, and I admire you!"

Xia Hong didn’t take their praise to heart, waved his hand, and suddenly turned to Li Xuanling, asking softly, "You’re not thinking of wanting this Blood Jade Fox, are you?"

Li Xuanling’s cheeks reddened upon hearing this, surprisingly, she didn’t refute this time.

"Then keep thinking about it, let’s go back."

After Xia Hong finished speaking, he carried the Blood Jade Fox and turned to leave.

Li Xuanling was vexed by being teased like this, her already slightly red cheeks flushed even further, and she stomped her foot, angrily shouting:

"Pah, who said I wanted it? Xia Hong, you are insufferable!"

Li Tiancheng watched this scene from the side, shook his head repeatedly, and finally advised Li Xuanling a few words, pulling her along to follow behind Xia Hong, heading toward the station.

Li Xuanling couldn’t tolerate such teasing easily, and she was angry thus didn’t speak to Xia Hong again the entire way. Upon nearly reaching the station, after Li Tiancheng had gone, she suddenly recalled something, took out a Yangyuan Pill from her arms, thought for a moment, then directly chased after Xia Hong ahead.

"Xia Hong, how much contribution value is needed to exchange for a Yangyuan Pill in Great Xia?"

The new policy was discussed and finalized on July 8th, today is the 12th, Xia Chuan with Yuwen Dao and others had already set up the six departments on this side of Long Mountain.

Of course, Long Mountain is a subordinate organ, so it’s not called a department but referred to as the Six Ministries. Additionally, Xia Chuan had also confirmed the titles for the positions within the Six Ministries.

Guardian Department is called Defender, Camp Supply Department is Military Preparation, Artisan Department is named Artisan, Civil Affairs Department is Chief, Hunting Bureau is Mountain Yu, Logging Department is Medicine Lord.

With the establishment of the Six Ministries, naturally, the individuals above the Logging Realm among the tens of thousands in Long Mountain have started registering in the Camp Supply Department, with exclusive contribution value records.

As these measures unfolded, the resource distribution rules and contribution value system of Great Xia became clear to everyone.

Li Xuanling asking about the price of Yangyuan Pill proved she had quietly been paying attention to the matters regarding the Six Ministries and the contribution value system these days.

Xia Hong slightly raised his eyebrows, replied:

"3000 points for one pill, only those at Cold Resistance Level can exchange as needed, why?"

"It’s really overpriced, from what I saw being publicly stated by the Camp Supply Department; submitting one Advanced Snow Mane to them only yields 15,000 contribution points. Based on the output of three catties, you can refine nine Yangyuan Pills. Calculating at 3000 points each, that’s 27,000 contribution points. If you include the advanced Beast Skin, Beast Blood, and Beast Meat, which are of good value, the camp profits half the amount..."

"What are you trying to say, just say it straightforwardly without rambling!"

Xia Hong couldn’t be bothered to debate this with Li Xuanling, besides, the account isn’t calculated like she said. The formula for Yangyuan Pill, the requirement of 18 precious medicinal ingredients for refining, and the massive amount of coal stone and labor consumed during the process...

The camp indeed profits, but definitely not as exaggerated as Li Xuanling claimed.

Recalling Great Xia’s meat allocation to reserves and children under six years old, including various initiatives in Long Mountain over the past four days, Li Xuanling evidently realized her position wasn’t justified. She didn’t lose her temper upon being interrupted. Instead, she softly spoke to Xia Hong: "My second uncle is getting old, it’s too dangerous for him to go out hunting Advanced Cold Beasts with others. Can’t the Yangyuan Pills be allotted to him monthly?"

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong raised his eyebrow, looked at Li Xuanling, and said, "You’re saying that he wouldn’t do anything, but I should directly allot him a few pills every month?"

Li Xuanling’s expression was naturally nodding at this.

Clearly, she still thought Great Xia was like the previous Long Mountain, where high-level individuals have special privileges, someone like Li Tiancheng with considerable strength and seniority acquiring several Yangyuan Pills monthly wouldn’t be considered extraordinary.

"Do you know, since the establishment of the contribution value rule in Great Xia, who doesn’t need to use contribution value to exchange for resources?"

Li Xuanling looked stunned for a moment, instinctively responded: "You, your brother Xia Chuan, and those who fought to the death among the Cold Resistance rank in Beehive probably all..."

"One person, throughout Great Xia, only one person!"

Li Xuanling was taken aback by the interruption, realized the only possible person Xia Hong was referring to is himself, immediately showing an expression of disbelief.

"How’s that possible?"

"Why not? Otherwise, do you think, why are so many people in Great Xia willing to voluntarily submit the Cold Beasts they hunted, including collected medicinal ingredients, mined iron ore, and coal stone... It’s because this rule treats everyone equally, everyone knows what the camp earns will ultimately return to them through the Camp Supply Department..."

Xia Hong paused here, then continued: "Your second uncle has twenty-seven Seven-maned Basic Strength, bringing along a few people at Cold Resistance Level to go out and hunt Advanced Cold Beasts shouldn’t be too difficult. If you’re genuinely afraid of danger, even hunting Intermediate Cold Beasts, with a tenth of the contribution value rate, exchanging for the Yangyuan Pills needed for cultivation is very easy."

With the Cold Resistance Level among the camp greatly increasing, to prevent anyone from deliberately hunting Intermediate Cold Beasts excessively for contribution points, Xia Chuan and Qiu Peng already promulgated a new regulation in mid-June, Cold Resistance Level hunting Intermediate Cold Beasts can only exchange for one-tenth of the original value.
