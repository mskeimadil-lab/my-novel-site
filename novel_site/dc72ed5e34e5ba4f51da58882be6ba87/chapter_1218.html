Chapter 1218: Chapter 408: The Aged Li Tiancheng, a Powerful Team, the Real Intention Behind the Move, Merger of Three Towns (Part 2)

After speaking, he bent down, touched Xia Yuyao and Xia Yusheng’s foreheads, a face full of affection.

"Yes, yes, yes, Ah Yao loves Grandpa the most, Grandpa should spend more time with me."

"Sheng’er also loves Grandpa the most, I also want to spend more time with Grandpa."

The two little ones were only five years old after all, they didn’t even understand what longevity was, naturally they couldn’t grasp the deeper meaning behind Li Tiancheng’s words, they only knew that Grandpa often brought them delicious treats and fun things, hearing Li Tiancheng say he would spend more time with them, they were overjoyed.

"Hahaha, yes, Grandpa loves you too!"

The childish voices of the two children made Li Tiancheng laugh heartily. He squatted down, picked them up one in each arm, and ran around in circles with them, making the two children laugh with joy.

Seeing the heartwarming scene of grandfather and grandchildren playing, Xia Hong first smiled slightly, then quietly reached out his hand to gently pat the back of Li Xuanling, whose eyes were red with tears, and whispered close to her ear to comfort her: "The Heavenly Yang Fruit from Jiao Long Nest should soon be refined into a Spirit Pill, your second uncle still has hope for a breakthrough, and I will make a trip to Cai Qiu Feudal Town in a while to see if there is any Spirit Pill to increase longevity."

Upon hearing this, Li Xuanling’s eyes immediately filled with hope, she turned to look at Xia Hong and nodded vigorously, saying, "Okay!"

Having been married for more than five years, the couple’s relationship was already deep, and words of thanks naturally didn’t come out.

Seeing Li Xuanling’s hopeful expression and realizing how much she anticipated it, Xia Hong felt a bit overwhelmed, quickly turning his gaze aside and saying:

"Alright, come with me outside! Let’s not keep the people in the main hall waiting too long."

"Almost forgot the important matter, Lord, I shall take my leave first!"

Hearing Xia Hong’s words, Li Tiancheng immediately put the two kids down, clasped his hands to say goodbye to the two and left the side hall first.

"You go, I’ll stay here with the two kids."

"No need, take the two kids with me, everyone is here today, it’s good to let them meet people."

Hearing Xia Hong’s words, Li Xuanling was stunned for a moment and asked in return: "Aren’t there people from other towns too? Take both of them out?"

Xia Hong waved his hand, his eyes revealing a dominance: "Do you think that after today’s events, in all of the Eight Towns of Moao South Slope, anyone would dare to lay a hand on the two of them?"

Not just the Eight Towns, even Cai Qiu Feudal Town would have to think twice.

Although Xia Hong did not speak this sentence aloud, the tacit understanding between the couple quickly let Li Xuanling see it in his eyes, she couldn’t help but roll her eyes at him first, then after contemplating for a moment, she quickly nodded in agreement.

There are only two outsiders in the main hall, and given their status and their current relations with the Great Xia, they wouldn’t spread rumors indiscriminately, there shouldn’t be much of an issue.

"Yao’er, Sheng’er, when you go in later, just sit next to mother, don’t speak randomly, and don’t move around, understand?"

"Understood, mother!"

Hearing that Xia Hong was taking them to the main hall and they could meet many people they’ve never seen before, Xia Yuyao and Xia Yusheng could barely contain their excitement. They were worried that their mother, Li Xuanling, would not agree, so they immediately agreed in unison when Li Xuanling stated her requirements.

"Hahaha, then let’s go!"

Xia Hong directly picked up the two of them, and with Li Xuanling, headed toward the adjacent part of the side hall.

The side hall they were in was located at the back of the main hall, with an interior corridor connected to the back of the main seat of the main hall, so before they even took a few steps, they could hear the sounds inside the main hall.

"Twelve Beast Kings, the Lord has such means."

"That Six Towns Lord’s face turned pale with fright, hahaha!"

"I say, the twelve Beast Kings only win in numbers, our Lord’s strength is truly unfathomable, had it not been for Yang Zhun’s strange movement technique, today’s battle wouldn’t have been simple defeat."

"What strange movement technique, if it weren’t for Shangguan Yang and Qin Feng stepping in to interfere, Yang Zhun would surely be dead today!"

"Right, right, I forgot about that, the Lord’s strength clearly crushes Yang Zhun!"

"Those two Town Lords have truly lost their minds, they dare to attack the Minister and Yuwen Director, the Lord’s roar was so grand for our Great Xia!"

"Hahahaha, the way that Six Towns Lord slunk away in the end, I fear I’ll never forget it."

"Dream on to think any dirt would stick to our Great Xia."

......

It was just after nightfall, the events of the day were indeed invigorating, and within the main hall, the people of Great Xia were still fervently discussing it.

Step... step... step...

Xia Hong did not intentionally make his step louder, but the moment he entered the main hall carrying the two children, the fervent discussions came to an abrupt halt.

As silence engulfed the entire hall, plunging it into a complete stillness, the footsteps of Xia Hong and Li Xuanling naturally became strikingly profound.

As Xia Hong carried the two children, leading Li Xuanling, slowly coming out from behind the main seat, over six hundred Cold Resistance Level soldiers of Great Xia, adorned in Thousand Forged Thunder Kui Golden Armor, each holding various weapons, with an incomparably solemn demeanor, standing in neat order, their expressions froze for a moment.

But they quickly understood, their gazes instantly focused on the two children in Xia Hong’s arms, faces full of curiosity.

Soon, the curiosity on everyone’s face solidified, followed by disbelief, after carefully inspecting, seemingly confirming something, their faces filled with shock and awe, finally turning into deep joy.
