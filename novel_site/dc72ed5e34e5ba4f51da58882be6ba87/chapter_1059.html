Chapter 1059: Chapter 373: Muyin’s Dead End, Only We Can Help You, Qinghuang Elixir Pills, Battle of Bashang (Part 2)

As die-hard supporters of Murong Chui, they naturally tried persuading him countless times, but Murong Chui remained indifferent. Even at this critical juncture, Murong Chui was still defending Murong Yan, leaving them somewhat disheartened.

"Zhang Hai pays respects to the Town Chief!"

Just as the entire room fell silent, someone suddenly rushed in from outside the hall.

Zhang Hai entered first to salute Murong Chui, then glanced around to confirm that everyone present was on his side, and immediately faced upwards to speak: "Town Chief, Bashang still hasn’t started fighting, but according to the scouts below reporting, Dajue Temple’s side is continuously increasing their troops, all village-level camps within their territory in the Earth-digging Realm have been recruited, Bashang has kept the city gates tightly shut for half a month, refusing to take the initiative in anything, it seems they are determined to defend the Town."

Upon hearing this, everyone inside the hall immediately appeared sullen.

Muyin’s only hope to avoid being annexed by the Northern Three Towns is to gain the joint support of the Central South Four Towns, yet how easy is it to gain their support?

While the battle at Bashang is still undecided, if Dajue Temple truly annihilates Bashang, their next target will undoubtedly be the Central South Four Towns; should they support Muyin at this time, they will offend the Northern Three Towns, and when Dajue Temple comes knocking, no one will help them.

Conversely, if Bashang can withstand Dajue Temple, letting the Four Towns realize that Dajue Temple isn’t as frightening, they won’t fear offending the Northern Three Towns, then Muyin Town could certainly usher in a turnaround.

In summary, their fate hinges on the outcome of the battle at Bashang.

Either Bashang falls, and they honestly surrender to Beishuo;

Or Bashang holds out, and they seek aid or form an alliance with the Four Towns.

The worst scenario is this current stalemate without a fight.

With Murong Yan and Shangguan Hong cooperating, Jin Mountain’s infiltration into Muyin has been deepening continuously, and the longer the delay, the more unfavorable it becomes for them.

"Why not seek Beishuo..."

Murong Ping finally couldn’t help but speak, but only got halfway through.

Because at this time, another person burst in from outside the hall.

A young girl, roughly eighteen or nineteen years old, wearing red.

With a touch of naïveté on her face, the girl’s expression seemed pure, and her bold intrusion into the hall was taken without regard; despite being watched by dozens of people, she appeared completely unfazed and directly bowed to Murong Chui at the top: "Yan’er pays respects to Grandfather, Grandfather, Father, uncles and cousins, today Yan’er wants to introduce you all to a gifted talent!"

Upon seeing Murong Yan enter, everyone in the hall, including Murong Chui, suddenly relaxed, expressions softening.

The Murong Family had two branches, if the eighteen-year-old who broke through the Cold Resistance Level, Murong Yan, was the face of the first branch; then the young girl standing in the center of the hall, Murong Yan, was the face of the second branch.

Murong Yan’s talent, compared not only to Murong Yan, was notable throughout the Nine Towns; at seventeen she broke through Cold Resistance Level using her Superior Battle Body, upon breaking through immediately possessing power greater than the four-talented, surpassing both brothers Murong Chui and Murong Ding in their youth.

When Murong Yan was born nineteen years ago, Murong Chui was already 108 years old, considered a late adoring grandson, and was moreover the child of his beloved youngest son Murong Yuan, so Murong Chui cherished her greatly from youth, and with her showing astounding talent, things only became even more extraordinary.

Especially two years ago, when Murong Yan broke through the Cold Resistance Level with Superior Battle Body Talent, realizing she might become the Murong Family’s third Xianyang Level after him, Murong Chui indulged her enormously, catering to her virtually every whim.

With her grandfather’s affection, Murong Yan naturally acted without reservation in the Town; whatever she wanted to do, she did, like barging into the hall at the moment, something nobody else dared do unless something urgent compelled them, lacking the courage to otherwise.

Murong Yan not only dared but even raised no temperament among everyone.

After all, Murong Chui at the top was full of smiles.

Who among them dared object?

Of course, some still dared speak.

Murong Yuan heard his daughter say she wanted to introduce a talented individual to her grandfather and immediately shook his head, scolding softly: "Young girls ought to behave properly, can’t you see your grandfather is discussing important matters with us? Go play with others."

Being scolded by her father in front of so many people, Murong Yan instantly became indignant, saying to her father: "Dad, don’t underestimate people. The talented individual I’m referring to might not be weaker than you."

"So Yan’er, tell us, where did this talent you’re introducing to your grandfather come from? What made you think of introducing them to him?"

Murong Yuan, after hearing, just shook his head and was about to continue speaking, but his father Murong Chui above interrupted, about to pull his daughter away, stopped by his fourth brother Murong Ying.

His expression turned slightly perplexed, seeing his fourth brother Murong Ying pointing outside the hall, he hurriedly looked out, concentrating for a moment, his expression suddenly changed.

Cold Resistance Level’s sensing ability can roughly cover about two hundred meters.

Standing outside the hall how many people there were, they were crystal clear.

Just now there were evidently only twenty guards, but now one more appeared.

Moreover, he couldn’t perceive that person’s aura.

So the daughter’s statement about strength perhaps not being weaker than his was true!

Murong Yuan’s mind swiftly raced with countless thoughts, his gaze becoming gloomier, focusing intently.

Meanwhile, Murong Yan was still cheerfully answering grandfather’s question.

"Grandfather, wasn’t Brother Yan organizing a very powerful Imperial Guard? Yan’er recently, having nothing to do, wanted to check out that Imperial Guard’s situation, didn’t expect to witness something amusing; Commander of the Imperial Guard Shangguan Qing, Deputy Commander Shangguan Xing were both reprimanded by a minor soldier, one against two; Shangguan Xing even lost an arm, causing the Lady to become enraged, now sending that Imperial Guard all over the city searching for them!"
