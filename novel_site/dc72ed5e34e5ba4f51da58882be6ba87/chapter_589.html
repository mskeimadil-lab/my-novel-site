Chapter 589: Chapter 252: A Thousand Gold for a Steed’s Bones, Yang Li Surrenders

But as soon as the first bite of Cold Beast Meat entered his mouth, Li Hu was instantly stunned!

An unprecedented taste exploded in his mouth, his tongue and nasal cavity instantly filled with that unique flavor. An incredibly refreshing experience made him feel as though the pores all over his body had opened up.

"How can it be this delicious? Could it be high-level Beast Meat?"

This question, Li Hu ultimately didn’t dare to ask out loud. He ate three large pieces of Beast Meat in one go, and only when he looked up did he notice Xia Chuan and Qiu Peng’s amused expressions, quickly wiping his mouth with an embarrassed look.

"Don’t be shy, keep eating, use chopsticks, there’s more if it’s not enough..."

Qiu Peng smiled and handed him a pair of chopsticks, gesturing for him to continue eating. After pondering for a moment, he continued, "There are nine people in Long Mountain in total, expressing their desire to join Great Xia. Among them, seven are from Changning, Song Yuan, and Yucheng. They’ve only just joined Long Mountain, so it makes sense for them to want to switch sides. But only you and Yang Zhong have grown up in Long Mountain and are considered purebred scions. Li Hu, do you think our Great Xia would take you and Yang Zhong in?"

Snap.....

Hearing the last question, Li Hu directly put down his chopsticks, his face showing a hint of anger, "Since you know, why use the words ’purebred scion’ to mock us?"

The identity of being an illegitimate child had always been a sore spot for Li Hu.

He didn’t envy the bloodline of the Longshan Li Family; he only hated this identity that caused the death of his mother, Li Yuning, and deprived him of a normal family, unable to grow up like other kids.

This identity as an illegitimate child brought nothing but pain and disgrace, never any tangible benefits. He never considered himself part of the Longshan Li Family; his surname had always followed his mother, Li Yuning.

Now, even Great Xia refused to take them in because of this identity.

A miserable look suddenly appeared on Li Hu’s face. After clenching his fists, he stood up directly and said, "Since Great Xia doesn’t want to take us in, Yang Zhong and I will leave at night, gentlemen..."

"No need to rush!"

At this moment, Qiu Peng suddenly interrupted Li Hu, reaching out to press him back into his seat.

Li Hu, whose limit was just under thirty thousand pounds, naturally couldn’t resist, only looking at Qiu Peng with dissatisfaction, his eyes still filled with anger.

At this moment, Xia Chuan from the other side spoke up.

"Zhu Rou’s father Zhu Xing is Cold Resistance Level. Her son Li Wuqing is only one year older than you, but he started using Beast Blood two years ago. Whether you return to Long Mountain or go elsewhere, unless something extraordinary happens, your revenge for your mother will remain forever out of reach..."

Upon hearing this, Li Hu’s expression was momentarily stunned, his anger flaring further, but before he had any reaction, Xia Chuan continued.

"Yang Zhong, even more so, his mother was forced to death by Li Xuanqiu. Li Xuanqiu himself is Cold Resistance Level, and his father Li Tiancheng is currently the strongest in Long Mountain. His difficulty in seeking revenge is even greater than yours!"

Not only himself, but even Yang Zhong’s matter was thoroughly investigated by them.

The anger on Li Hu’s face gradually subsided. After completely calming down, he looked at Xia Chuan again, and a glimmer of hope gradually rose in his eyes, speaking directly:

"Please speak plainly, gentlemen."

Spending such effort to investigate both him and Yang Zhong proved Great Xia indeed had designs on them.

Seeing the delicious Beast Meat he had never tasted before on the table, and Xia Chuan sitting before him with a status only slightly below the leader Xia Hong, Li Hu already started to guess.

Truly smart!

Seeing Li Hu’s reaction, Xia Chuan couldn’t help but sigh inwardly once more.

If Li Hu’s earlier entry and greeting only showed he and Yang Zhong were perceptive, his reaction now proved his alertness far exceeded the average person.

"Alright, since you’re smart, I won’t beat around the bush!"

Xia Chuan nodded to Qiu Peng, who immediately retrieved a large package from behind and then took out the items one by one, placing them on the table.

First, there was a tin can ten centimeters in diameter, containing a white crystal. Smelling the scent wafting from the can, Li Hu immediately realized what it was, looking at the Beast Meat on the table.

"This is salt. The Beast Meat you just ate became so delicious because of it. Additionally, after eating it, your body should feel something; its function isn’t just to make food tasty."

Next were two wooden boxes. Xia Chuan opened the two boxes, each containing Dan Medicine; the left one with fifteen emerald green pills, and the right one with only five golden pills:

"The green ones are Beast Essence Pills, which can enhance your cultivation speed by thirty to fifty percent; the golden ones are called Blood Sore Pills, Healing Pills for Cold Resistance Level, lifesaving Holy Items for you."

The third item was a large pack of deep blue Beast Meat, although the meat featured some red streaks, clearly indicating it was Intermediate Beast Meat.

"Five hundred pounds of Intermediate Beast Meat, your Basic Strength is twenty-eight thousand. With this batch of Beast Meat, plus that can of salt and bottle of Beast Essence Pills, I estimate when you’re done, you could almost push to the thirty thousand pound limit."

These three items alone already made Li Hu breathe heavily.

But Qiu Peng wasn’t done, he then took out a leather bag.

Xia Chuan took the bag, opened it, and placed it in front of Li Hu.
