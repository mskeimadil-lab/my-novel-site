Chapter 712: Chapter 292: White Dew Gu’s Open Plot, Long Mountain Advances

......

Over an hour ago, Li Tiancheng described to them everything he had seen of Xia City.

A total of ten kilometers of black stone walls on three sides;

Xia people dressed in various attires, freely coming and going at the outskirts of Redwood Ridge;

A densely packed Hunting Team, emerging en masse after nightfall;

Countless iron ores and coal stones being transported into Xia City from various places;

......

In fact, Li Tiancheng had not successfully infiltrated Xia City; he had only glimpsed some scenes from the perimeter, yet even the partial imagery he depicted astonished the Cold Resistance Level members present.

The Long Mountain people initially thought that the Great Xia was similar to those other giant camps in Longyou. Even if they deployed the Cloud Dragon Army this time, they believed that Great Xia’s strength was at most comparable to Zhaoyang and still had a considerable gap with Long Mountain.

But after hearing about Li Tiancheng’s observations, they realized that wasn’t the case.

The scene at the Great Xia Station was visibly much better than Long Mountain, with a population greater than Long Mountain, and possibly even more members of the Hunting Team and Earth-digging Realm than Long Mountain...

They were already somewhat anxious, and upon hearing about the Wushuang situation that Li Xuanping and the others had scouted, their expressions became a bit tense.

The strength of Great Xia clearly exceeded their imagination.

"It can be confirmed that within three kilometers surrounding Wushuang, Great Xia has at least stationed over twenty-seven Cold Resistance Level guards. Our people couldn’t even get close; trying to spy on the situation inside the Wushuang Camp is even more impossible."

Li Xuanling’s expression was also very serious, evidently taken aback by the number of Cold Resistance Level members in Great Xia. Seeing that some people below were starting to appear uneasy, she furrowed her brow and said in a deep voice: "No matter how large their cities are or how strong they are, they’re still over by Redwood Ridge. This is Longyou, our home ground in Long Mountain. Even if they’re a dragon, they have to coil obediently here..."

She paused at this point, then changed her tone and continued: "Moreover, Great Xia only has one Cloud Dragon Army in Longyou. Just with this alone, Great Xia could never defeat our Long Mountain!"

The people below, upon hearing Li Xuanling’s words, contemplated briefly and then were suddenly invigorated, their eyes lighting up.

That’s right!

At Changbai Gully and Ning Yuan Hill, the two battles of the Cloud Dragon Army last night were indeed well executed, but they adopted ambush tactics, and midway, they used other ordinary Earth-digging Realm members as lures and distractions, with Xia Hong personally covering.

This basically confirmed that besides the Cloud Dragon Army, Great Xia had no second formal army, or even if they did, they couldn’t mobilize them to Longyou.

In that case, Long Mountain’s chances of victory were quite high.

"The inability to spy on the situation inside the Wushuang Camp doesn’t affect our advance. After receiving the news last night, we immediately sent people to investigate. There are about less than two thousand Earth-digging Realm members inside. Counting the 1,000 Cloud Dragon Army, the total number does not exceed three thousand. With so few people, there’s no way they can block three thousand Longyou Army!"

Li Xuandu spoke first, and everyone slightly nodded.

Li Xuanling also nodded. After the news from Changbai Gully returned last night, she was the first to order people to explore the Wushuang Camp situation, so she knew the number of people well inside Wushuang also.

"No matter what plan Xia Hong has, our army will directly press forward. With brute force, he either evades or fights; those are his only two options. Placing so many Cold Resistance Level members for perimeter vigilance might just be a bluff to make us think he has some ambush preparations, discouraging us from advancing easily."

After Li Xuanling finished speaking, she glanced at the already darkened sky and directly ordered: "It’s night now, gather the army and set off. Order people to clear the snow path ahead, all Cold Resistance Levels group explore the route conditions along the way, and be sure to arrive within four hours, giving enough time for battle. Tonight, we will completely expel Great Xia from the Wushuang Realm..."

Song Yuan is to the west of Wushuang, with a straight-line distance of eighteen kilometers between both camps. However, the path requires some detours, bringing the total distance to about twenty-five kilometers. With the march speed of the Longyou Army, four hours should suffice.

Li Xuanling thought of something else and said to the four in the center, Li Xuanping included:

"You four, each take a Cold Resistance Level member and continue watching the four directions of Wushuang, keep yourselves safe, and report any changes immediately in turns."

"Yes, ma’am!"

Li Xuanping, who tried to topple Li Xuanling from the leader position yesterday, seemed much calmer at this moment. He nodded in agreement with the other three and then turned and walked out of the hall.

On the snowy night, a group from the Song Yuan post began to clear the snow path along the way. Three thousand heavily armored Longyou Army members majestically and swiftly advanced towards the east, toward the Wushuang Realm.

The Cold Resistance Levels, following Li Xuanling’s assigned tasks, went ahead to their respective positions.

This war, deciding the attribution of Longyou, commenced as Long Mountain took the initiative to advance.
