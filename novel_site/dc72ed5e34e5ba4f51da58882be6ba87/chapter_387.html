This Xia Li currently has only four parts, namely Camp, Ritual, Hunt, and Cultivation.

The Camp section standardizes all the titles of positions within the Six Ministries of the Daxia Camp, and clearly delineates the various responsibilities unique to each role.

The Ritual section encompasses topics like marriage, funeral, sacrifice, and custom, used to regulate our Daxia people’s conduct and daily activities. Today’s dowry offering, future bride welcoming, and the act of marriage itself are all contents of the marriage section, consider today as me giving you a template.

The Hunting section, as the name implies, relates to the regulation of hunting behavior, including who can hunt, where to hunt, preparatory work and things to be aware of before and after a hunt, all explained in detail.

The Cultivation section regulates our practices in areas such as logging, digging, cold resistance, and even the yet unknown Xianyang level, exploring methods of cultivation across different stages, power levels, and corresponding titles, providing a basic framework.

Xia Chuan introduced the four parts of Xia Li with a brief explanation all at once, observing the confused looks in people’s eyes, he remembered what his elder brother had said and sighed inwardly.

"The civilization of the Ice Abyss World has fallen for too long, so long that apart from some rules passed down through generations, everything else has been forgotten.
