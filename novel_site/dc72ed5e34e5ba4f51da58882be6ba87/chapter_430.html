Chapter 430: Chapter 202: Worthless Rabble, Four Camps Bow Down (Part 3)

"Tomorrow night I can finally go back, I’ve been staying at Wuyuan for more than a month."

"This time, I’ve gathered quite a lot of Cheng Guo and Xiang Han Fruit. When I go back, I should be able to exchange for plenty of Contribution Value, buy a new outfit for my son, add some new furniture at home, and for my wife..."

"I’m not far from the Earth-digging Realm. This time, I can go back, and it’s my turn for the Martial Arts Hall. If I stay for a day, I should be able to break through smoothly."

"Impressive, once you break through, joining the Hunting Department will be comfortable from now on."

"Just now, the Lord of Guard was talking to Captain Lin. I overheard it. Apparently, the leader will come out of seclusion tomorrow night. We haven’t seen the leader for several months, right?"

"The leader coming out of seclusion? No wonder the Lord of Guard is in such a hurry. I guess he wants to relocate everyone from the four camps of the Northern Mountain to please the leader."

....

Contribution Value, Martial Arts Hall, Hunting Department... The people’s dialogue was filled with a bunch of terms Xiang Liang couldn’t understand, but there were some he could comprehend.

Great Xia Camp, just like them, also calls their leader similarly.

The Lord of Guard should be the one-eyed Xu Ning.

Combining with Xu Ning’s recent attitude during the lecture to the people, Wuyuan Guard, presumably means the top official of the Great Xia Camp on Five Ape Mountain.

....

Xiang Liang inferred various situations about the Great Xia camp by overhearing others’ conversations, while constantly observing the movement of the wooden door.

With only half an hour left until dawn, finally...

"The winds and rains from all directions can’t compare to the rain of our Redwood Ridge."

A voice came from outside the wooden door, immediately someone walked over from the side.

"Dragon Gate Mountain is raining, the Tiger of the Snow Plains descends."

After exchanging the secret codes, the person directly opened the door.

"Everyone come over, escort the captives inside first, the rest go back and help, there are more than five thousand people, first settle them outside the wooden house, quick quick quick..."

Xu An’s urgent voice sounded outside the wooden door, everyone inside the wooden house began to act immediately, and with someone notifying, people constantly came from the northern cave to help.

"Get inside, quickly!"

"Don’t dawdle, get inside fast."

....

Xiang Liang wanted to help, but a few stern shouts came from outside the wooden door.

After the stern shouts subsided, a disheveled person, hands bound, covered in wounds, walked in first.

"Wang... Wang Lie..."

Seeing that person’s face, Xiang Liang’s expression changed instantly.

Soon after, two more people walked in.

"He Feng, Liu Qing."

The four camps of the Northern Mountain often trade with each other, which is why Wang Lie took the opportunity to coax three other camps to attack Great Xia together.

Xiang Liang had seen these three camp leaders before, so he could recognize them.

Seeing these once high and mighty leaders, now covered in wounds and sullenly escorted in by Great Xia people, Xiang Liang felt an inexplicable thrill.

Of course, he didn’t forget to quickly find his family among the crowd.

"Father!"

Finally, among the captives at the forefront, he saw his father, Xiang Ping.

He shouted loudly, Xiang Ping immediately froze, then turned to see Xiang Liang inside the wooden house, his face lighted up with surprise and joy.

"Don’t stop, keep moving forward, it’s almost dawn!"

Xiang Ping was about to speak, but hearing the urging sound from behind, he closed his mouth and continued to follow the guidance of the crowd.

Father was captured too!

Not only his father Xiang Ping, seeing a number of Earth-digging Realm from Hefeng Camp among the captives ahead, Xiang Liang’s expression turned grim.

Father also led people to confront those from Great Xia.

He was coerced by Wang Lie and had no choice but to agree.

He had just told Xu Ning about these situations, Xu Ning should know it by now, so why were father and the others still tied up, this...

Xiang Liang saw the outside Xu Ning still directing the crowd, knowing now wasn’t the time to approach him, so he walked to his father’s side first.

"Father, didn’t you explain to Lord Xu Ning that our Hefeng Camp was coerced by Wang Lie? Why were you still tied up?"

Hearing his son’s words, Xiang Ping showed a hint of helplessness as he walked along and said:

"While coming over, I requested several times to see the Lord, but couldn’t even say a word, they kept urging us to move quickly."

Xiang Liang glanced at the sky outside, immediately understanding.

"Don’t worry, father, I’ve already informed Lord Xu Ning, he knows the details. He’ll definitely let you all go later."

Xiang Ping nodded and continued chatting with his son.

Xiang Ping learned a lot about Great Xia from his son’s words;

Xiang Liang also heard from his father about the recent skirmish between Great Xia’s small teams and their group.

Their conversation immediately sparked their eyes with joy.
