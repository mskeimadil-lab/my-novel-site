Chapter 1459: Chapter 463: Green-eyed Fire Suanni, Battle Royale and Advancement, Furious Counterattack, Trial Transformation (Part 4)

Whoosh...

Just after committing this act twice, the Fire Suanni behind him immediately became enraged. Evidently, it understood Xia Hong’s actions, leaping into the air with its hind legs, targeting the third area Xia Hong was about to descend, transforming into a giant blue and red fireball, arriving swiftly.

However, Xia Hong never intended to succeed so easily on the third attempt. He had already swallowed a Blood Jade Crystal he just acquired, and before the blazing fire reached him, he had already raised the Shattered Star Sword.

Slash...

He retreated his body to evade, then flipped in midair to gather strength, sending the Shattered Star Sword straight back along the original path, extending a sword edge that was several hundred meters long.

The sword edge was densely filled with countless starlight, instantly cutting through the Blood Miasma, directly heading toward the pupils of the Green-eyed Fire Suanni.

Heaven-pulling Sword Technique!

After arriving at Cai Qiu, Xia Hong secretly learned this martial art from the City Lord of Qinghua City, Cai Yunzhou. However, unlike Cai Yunzhou, he was performing the most perfect version, increased by fifty percent with his Long Fist routine.

Jun basic strength, each Holy Pattern and martial art increasing by fifty percent, Xia Hong had now displayed his strongest power, reaching a staggering 98 Jun. Not considering the War Body Golden Body, solely in terms of strength, he was barely 2 Jun away from the weakest Tribulation Body Realm expert.

"Roar..."

However, facing such a sword, the Green-eyed Fire Suanni not only showed no fear but Xia Hong’s unexpected resistance seemed to incite more rage.

Without dodging or evading, it raised its head and let out a roar, among the path of Xia Hong’s hundreds of sword edges, suddenly surging forth blue lava fire pillars, attempting to defuse the sword edge’s power midair or directly block it.

Boom...

Unfortunately, the Heaven-pulling Sword Technique is inherently a fast sword. Although the Suanni’s strength is formidable, trying to block at such a critical moment is nothing short of wishful thinking.

"Huff..."

The Beast Emperor still couldn’t speak human language, but its emotional expression was no different from humans. Realizing it couldn’t block Xia Hong’s sword, the Suanni’s nostrils spewed two streams of blue fire, its face even revealed a trace of a sneer, then took a deep breath, opening its enormous Blood Basin mouth toward the approaching sword edge.

"Roar..."

A giant blue lava fire pillar with a diameter of about five zhang suddenly erupted from the depths of the Suanni’s throat. As the lava came out of its mouth, fierce green flames directly vaporized the miasma along the way, creating billowing thick smoke.

The speed of that lava fire pillar even exceeded Xia Hong’s sword edge!

If the blue lava fire pillars erupting from the ground just now were still within Xia Hong’s defense range, then at this moment, the fire pillar spewed from the Suanni’s mouth completely left him with no thought of resistance.

Moreover, it’s not just the lava fire pillar!

Slash...

The moment the sword edge and fire pillar collided, Xia Hong’s eyes flashed with chaos, seeing the fire quickly envelop his entire body, without a trace of hesitation, he directly retrieved the sword and turned to escape.

"Green flame, lava, strength, not only these. This Suanni’s roar can also disturb the mind. It’s unmanageable, utterly unmanageable. Fortunately, before coming to Cai Qiu last year, Mu Dong forged this Shattered Star Sword. Otherwise, with a Ten Thousand Forging Level weapon, it would have been melted by the green flame on the first contact..."

Xia Hong used Instant Body to dash westward for dozens of meters, noticing the sword tip of the Shattered Star Sword showing slight melting, his pupils filled with horror, completely extinguishing the thought of continuing to fight the Suanni.

"Nearly all the skin membranes are gone, flesh can barely withstand three to four more hits. If my bones are injured, I won’t have any chance!"

While running, Xia Hong reached into his Yellow Mustard Bag, his left eye flickered with light, but his face showed hesitation. Soon he sensed something, clenched his fist to feel the strength, and his pupils revealed joy.

"The Suanni’s fire helps accelerate my absorption of Blood Jade Pearls and Blood Jade Crystals. No, no, it’s not the Suanni’s fire, it’s the Fire Tribulation Qi helping me absorb."

Can’t kill me, but helps me absorb Blood Jade Pearls and Blood Jade Crystals!

Xia Hong completely ignored the charring on his body, realizing his bones hadn’t suffered any damage yet, most injuries concentrated on flesh and skin membranes, his pupils revealed madness, pulling his hand from the Yellow Mustard Bag, continuing his escape westward.

"Hold on a little longer, unless absolutely necessary!"

He withdrew his hand from the Yellow Mustard Bag, grasping a golden round Elixir Pill, swallowing it directly, roughly sensing the surrounding area, his gaze coldly heading towards the Cold Beast’s densest area.

"Six Holy Vein Pills remaining, might as well use them all inside, raising strength further, possibly giving a hope for escape!"

The visibility in the Blood Miasma Plain wasn’t high, but the sensing range was still over three hundred meters. The Green-eyed Fire Suanni wasn’t adept at speed. Though Xia Hong couldn’t entirely shake it off, he could barely manage to escape for his life.

The Holy Vein Pills continuously repaired his injuries. Once feeling sufficiently recovered, he changed his course to land, risking a hit from the Suanni to grab handfuls of Blood Jade Pearls and Blood Jade Crystals, directly swallowing them.

One man and one beast, one fleeing and one chasing, thus entangled in the peripheral region of the Blood Miasma Plain. Xia Hong didn’t persistently flee westward; occasionally deviating to grab Blood Jade Pearls and Blood Jade Crystals, safely leading the Fire Suanni in circles.

"48.5... 49... 49.3... 49.7... 50..."

The Blood Miasma Plain inherently divided not by day nor night. Xia Hong, despite all his injuries, eventually lost track of time, grinding his teeth through sheer force, repeatedly carrying out the entire process.
