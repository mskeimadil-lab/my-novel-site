Chapter 479 - 218: Departure, Sending a Letter, Killing (Part 3)

Chapter 479: Chapter 218: Departure, Sending a Letter, Killing (Part 3)

"It’s Lu Sheng!"

"Someone actually followed us, this is bad."

Whoosh......

Before the two could rush over, a corpse was thrust out from the slope below by Yue Feng’s sword.

Yue Feng wasn’t just randomly thrusting; he had aimed the corpse at the slowest of the four who had just escaped.

Bang......

Luo Ming and Li Baihe had just reached the edge of the slope when they saw the corpse Yue Feng had thrust fly and hit that person, causing them to spew blood and collapse, their eyes frozen in astonishment.

"Yue Feng, what is his strength?"

"Two months ago, his strength was almost the same as mine..."

The shock in Luo Ming and Li Baihe’s hearts hadn’t settled when Yue Feng moved again.

Having already killed two people, he leapt towards the third, which was the fastest runner, Lu Sheng. While pursuing Lu Sheng, he even took the longbow from his back, nocked an arrow, and shot six arrows in succession at the other two.

Not even checking the results after shooting, Yue Feng continued his pursuit as there was less than ten meters left between him and Lu Sheng.

Thunk... thunk......

The sound of six consecutive Iron Arrows piercing through flesh echoed, and the two who had fled the furthest fell directly upon the ground.

Seeing six Iron Arrows pin those two bodies to the large tree at the back, the expressions of Luo Ming and Li Baihe turned numb.

"What kind of bow is that? The arrows seem stronger than my own power."

"It’s not just the bow; Yue Feng’s accuracy is terrifying!"

......

"Lu Sheng, I remember, last time it was you, this dog, who went back to inform on us. This time, you’re done for my hands!"

Having just heard Luo Ming’s voice, Yue Feng paused for a moment, then upon getting closer saw the face clearly and recognized him as Lu Sheng, the one who had previously trespassed into Jing Valley to steal iron ore. A savage smile appeared on his face, and his longsword accelerated drastically.

Yue Feng’s spear moved like the wind, slicing through falling snowflakes, aiming straight for Lu Sheng’s neck;

Realizing he couldn’t dodge, Lu Sheng could only grit his teeth, turn back, and raise his longblade to block.

Clang......

The blade and sword clashed violently, creating a mini shockwave that dispersed the snow on the ground and in the air.

Yet to recover from Yue Feng’s terrifying might, Lu Sheng looked down at his chipped blade and immediately leveraged himself to retreat swiftly.

Not a moment to linger...

This Yue Feng’s Basic Strength far exceeds my thousand pounds!

As this thought flashed through Lu Sheng’s mind, he retreated over ten meters and quickly turned to flee northwards to the garrison.

Whoosh......

However, just having fled a few meters, an Iron Arrow swiftly shot out from his flank, blocking his path.

Though the Iron Arrow was swift, Lu Sheng’s survival instincts responded even faster.

In the air, he abruptly shifted his body, avoiding the Arrow, but realizing the situation was even more perilous, he increased his fleeing speed.

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh......

Unfortunately, within the next second, he was completely dumbfounded!

Iron Arrows came rushing from both sides and even from right in front of him simultaneously, totaling over ten arrows altogether.

The sound of arrows tearing through the air echoed loudly, rendering Lu Sheng’s quick actions useless this time.

Thunk......

Ultimately, three Iron Arrows penetrated his cheek, abdomen, and calf, respectively, followed by a scream as he fell to the ground.

Seeing Yue Feng crazily closing in from behind.

Lu Sheng’s face filled with dread, and he raised his head to plead for mercy.

Thunk......

But alas, Yue Feng gave him no chance.

With a single slash, Lu Sheng’s head separated from his body, dying miserably on the spot.

Yue Feng looked at Lu Sheng’s body, his eyes showing a trace of ruthlessness.

Last time, because this person sounded the alert, it led to Chen Yingba arriving at Jing Valley.

If it weren’t for Xia Hong arriving timely afterward, he undoubtedly would have died then.

This time, having encountered him, there would be no mercy!

Yue Feng sheathed his large blade, turned to look at the ten Mountain River Team members on either side, and smiled as he waved his hand:

"Take the three wounded and bring them back, then clean up here."

"Yes, my lord!"

Without prior notification, Luo Ming suddenly signaled for a meeting, which made Yue Feng vigilant, so he brought ten Hunting Team members beforehand, ordering them to set up an ambush nearby.

.........

Not far away, Luo Ming, Li Baihe, and Luo Cheng, standing on the slope, were already dumbfounded, especially at the appearance of the ten Mountain River Team members clad in faint gold armor, causing their faces to turn pale with shock, deeply moved.

"Are they all Yue Feng’s subordinates?"

"What are they wearing? It looks like iron, but seems more advanced!"

"Seems like... it’s made together with Thunder Kui’s hardest skull and iron."

"How strong is Great Xia’s power now?"

.........

Luo Ming and Li Baihe could still discern the origin of the equipment despite how eye-catching the gear was on the ten Mountain River Team members under Yue Feng.

But not the young Luo Cheng.

Seeing the pale-gold armor, his eyes couldn’t look away.

Moreover, each of those ten not only carried longbows but also various primary weapons in hand and even shortswords and short knives at their waists.

This lavish, excellently crafted, and well-rounded weapon setup instantly deepened his longing and admiration for Great Xia.

"Luo Ming, the two of you head back first, Luo Cheng, come with me!"

Li Baihe nodded respectfully, while Luo Ming walked over to instruct his son with a few words before leaving northward alongside Li Baihe.

Yue Feng, on the other hand, took Luo Cheng and the ten members with him back to Jing Valley.

"I still have some impression of the valley. Only two years on, the changes shouldn’t be too big, or rather, there should be changes; with Great Xia’s current strength, the garrison must have improved significantly......."

Following behind Yue Feng and his team, Luo Cheng realized he would soon see Great Xia’s garrison, and his heart gradually began to swell with excitement.
