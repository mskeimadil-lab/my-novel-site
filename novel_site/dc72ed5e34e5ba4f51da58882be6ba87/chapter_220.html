And those coming for the first time began to cultivate by imitation, and when they noticed their basic strength was growing at an unusually rapid pace, their pupils suddenly contracted, and expressions of ecstatic joy appeared on their faces.

......

"Twenty points of contribution value, which equates to digging 400 pounds of coal, and with fifty people together, that’s 20,000 pounds. Isn’t your pricing a bit high?"

Xia Hong, sitting in a chair next to the wall of the Martial Arts Training Ground, looked through the wooden window facing the hall at those fifty people, with a somewhat peculiar expression.

According to the current rules, the contribution points obtained from coal and iron mining have the same conversion unit with the system, 20 pounds of coal for one point, 10 pounds of iron for one point.

The Martial Arts Training Ground runs a day and consumes 9,600 pounds of coal, converting to just 480 points of coal resources, evenly divided among fifty people, the day’s training consumption should be less than 10 points per person.

But Xia Chuan set it at 20 points, doubling it.

Even Xia Hong couldn’t help but think it’s a bit greedy...

Hearing the teasing in Xia Hong’s tone, Xia Chuan chuckled and then straightened his expression, shaking his head, "It’s not greedy, among these people, aside from the Hunting Team members, the rest don’t have mining abilities. They rely on logging collection to earn contribution points, and there is inherently some water in the contribution points in their hands.

Moreover, the opportunity to double the cultivation speed, which can be obtained just by going out to log, makes even 20 points seem low to me.

Even if it’s higher, I bet they’ll fight over it."

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong pondered for a moment and chuckled softly.

Indeed, it’s definitely not greedy. Doubling the cultivation speed, a miracle-like ability with no competition, they could only wish for it. Thinking this way, 20 points of contribution value truly isn’t much.

"But it can’t hinder the resource collection of the camp. I’m planning to open it once every three days in the future. If more people come, we’ll have to raise the price or even bid for it. We definitely can’t spare fifty people for training every day; our Great Xia isn’t equipped for that right now..."

Clearly, Xia Chuan’s considerations are even more far-reaching than his own; he’s already thinking about the detailed arrangements for the Martial Arts Training Ground in the future.

"Go ahead and take care of it..."

Seeing that he had arranged everything so thoroughly, Xia Hong didn’t say more but turned his thoughts to another matter, "Are the two sets of weapons ready?"

"Luo Yuan and Yuan Cheng didn’t come today; they stayed in the Blacksmith Room forging. It should only take one more day to finish them up."

Xia Hong nodded, feeling a slight excitement in his heart.

Yesterday, when the Martial Arts Training Ground opened for the first time, it brought a huge surprise.

Cheng Feng, Lin Kai, and Xu Ning all broke through to the Earth-digging Realm in one day.

Cheng Feng’s breakthrough wasn’t unexpected for Xia Hong, as he was among the oldest of the Rogel Camp, already ranking high in basic strength; it was Xu Ning and Lin Kai who truly surprised him.

Especially Xu Ning; evidently, after losing his right eye, he didn’t let himself go but became even more diligent. Otherwise, he wouldn’t have broken through to the Earth-digging Realm so quickly.

Cheng Feng, being the Collection Team captain, doesn’t currently have a suitable replacement, so it’s not convenient for Xia Hong to recruit him into the Hunting Team; as for Lin Kai and Xu Ning, there’s nothing more to say.

New members naturally need new weapons.

The current standard allocation for the Hunting Team is a large saber, a ten-stone Iron Bow complemented with fifty seven-pound specially made Iron Arrows.

The benefits of having more in the Earth-digging Realm are slowly becoming evident.

Now, unless there’s a special situation, mining, forging, and even hunting low-level Cold Beasts no longer require his personal effort.

"Brother, will we go hunting as usual tomorrow?"

The last hunt was on the nineteenth; it was set to occur every three days. Tomorrow would be the twenty-second, and with Xia Hong specifically asking about weapons for Lin Kai and Xu Ning, Xia Chuan naturally guessed his intention easily.

But evidently, he guessed wrong.

Xia Hong shook his head, speaking in a deep tone, "Not necessarily tomorrow; first, let’s head to the north of Redwood Ridge and assess the situation. If the opportunity is right, we’ll discuss it then!"

To check the north of Redwood Ridge.

Xia Chuan immediately understood the implication, gently nodding, a glint of coldness appearing in his eyes.

During this period, when Xia Hong led them to hunt, he deliberately avoided the northern area, and they all knew the reason.

"Hopefully, those in the Mirror Immortal Camp will adhere to the rules a bit!"

Xia Hong murmured in a low, deep tone, with a hint of severity flashing in his eyes.
