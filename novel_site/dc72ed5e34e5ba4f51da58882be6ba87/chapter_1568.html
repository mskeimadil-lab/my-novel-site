Chapter 1568: Chapter 492: Invisible Assassin, Foolishness, Peril and Panic (Part 3)

Bang......

The palm wind hit, and Tie Xinchuan was directly sent flying dozens of meters, forcefully breaking two big trees. He was thrown far away and spewed out a mouthful of reverse blood, looking at the shoulder part of his Profound Scale Silver Armor already shattered, his face full of fear.

The Profound Scale Silver Armor is currently the most top-level armor in Great Xia, second to none. It has been tested countless times before, able to withstand the impact force of up to three jun, yet it couldn’t even block a single palm from the opponent.

He could feel that the flesh of his shoulder had all been dissolved by the massive force of the opponent’s palm, and the bones beneath the flesh were crushed.

If he wasn’t wearing armor, he would undoubtedly be dead!

Despite his inner fear, Tie Xinchuan still didn’t forget to look at the carriage. Holding his broken arm, he looked up and discovered that the one who just sent him flying was a person also wearing the Profound Scale Silver Armor.

More miserable than the previous two invisible assassins, this person’s left and right hands were respectively being bitten by a Beast King Level Teng Jiao and a Beast King Level Blood Jade Fox, and he was crazily soaring into the sky escaping westward.

This person’s strength was obviously extremely astonishing. Despite being bitten on both arms by two Beast Kings, and both Beast Kings having unleashed their battle forms, his speed was still undiminished, instantly flying to the edge of the Milk Tiger Forest west side.

"Pff... It’s impossible, run!"

That person was wholeheartedly fleeing, heroically severing his own arms, allowing the two Beast Kings to bite them off. When he reached the sky above the west side of Milk Tiger Forest, he spat out a mouthful of reverse blood, then shouted to signal others to flee. He didn’t stop at all and instantly flew out of Milk Tiger Forest, escaped towards the depths of Redwood Ridge.

Tie Xinchuan held his broken arm and quickly rushed to the side of the carriage, lifted the curtain to look inside, only to find Cheng Guang and Jiang Ping both desperately protecting Xia Yusheng, while Han Yue, who was just with Xia Yusheng in the carriage, was nowhere to be seen.

Xia Yusheng’s face was full of panic at the moment. Seeing it was Tie Xinchuan who came in, he instinctively wanted to rush out, but Cheng Guang and Jiang Ping naturally wouldn’t allow him to run away casually and directly grabbed him.

"Uncle Tie, Auntie Han Yue is missing. She was just slapped by that person’s hand, and then she disappeared..."

Xia Yusheng realized he couldn’t get out, so he could only speak with a slight sobbing tone.

Hearing his words, Tie Xinchuan’s expression suddenly shook greatly, but thinking about his responsibilities, he still clenched his fist tightly and said in a low voice, "Your Highness, don’t be afraid, there are only a hundred meters left to the north gate, it’ll be fine once we enter the city!"

"Uncle Tie, please go save Auntie Han Yue, please go save her. I will never leave the city again in the future, never leave the city again..."

Xia Yusheng was obviously very flustered, his voice full of fear and panic, even promising he would never leave the city again in the future, continuously calling out Auntie Han Yue and urging Tie Xinchuan to hurry and rescue her.

If we talk about who suffered the greatest psychological blow tonight, it would definitely be him.

At this moment, Xia Yusheng’s heart was completely blank. He couldn’t understand why he was just coming to Milk Tiger Forest for a trial, and such a major incident happened.

From just now being hugged into the carriage by Tie Xinchuan and others, to the sudden attack by two invisible assassins, breaking the top of the carriage, he was held in Han Yue’s arms throughout the whole process. Not only Han Yue, but Jiang Ping and Cheng Guang also sandwiched him front and back, tightly protecting him in the middle.

Although he didn’t know what exactly happened outside, he could roughly perceive it from the commotion and the voices of the Dragon Forbidden Captain and others. The target was obviously him.

After the top of the carriage broke open, numerous Dragon Forbidden Captains rushed over, surrounded the carriage, and then he heard a loud boom, all the Dragon Forbidden Captains around the carriage were blasted away, and the Yellow Candle inside and around the carriage was instantly extinguished.

Finally, a Dragon Forbidden Captain took advantage of the chaos and rushed in, wearing Profound Scale Silver Armor, but his face was old, his hair and beard were white, clearly disguised as an assassin.

That person rushed in and directly lifted Cheng Guang and Jiang Ping with his right hand Longsword, then his left hand glowed intensely, and directly slapped towards him.

Xia Yusheng remembers very clearly, at that time, facing that palm, he was very afraid, and directly closed his eyes. It was Han Yue who had been hugging him tightly, who suddenly twisted and turned her body to block that palm with her back.

When Auntie Han Yue helped him block that palm, she whispered a sentence in his ear.

"Your Highness, don’t be afraid. Auntie will protect you!"

Years of companionship made Han Yue treat Xia Yusheng like her own; Xia Yusheng had deep feelings for this maid who had accompanied him growing up since childhood.

Li Xuanling, his mother, was extremely strict in managing him, and as he grew older, the degree of strictness was continuously increasing; Han Yue was different, having taken care of his daily needs closely for many years, always trying to make him happy, never asking him for anything, except for some principle-based requests like not being allowed to leave Xia Palace, she would almost always comply with his wishes.

Sometimes when he made a mistake and was subject to punishment by his mother, Han Yue would even help to persuade Li Xuanling. It was precisely because of this reason that Li Xuanling once teases Han Yue, saying she is more like a real mother than herself, thus having Xia Yusheng and Xia Yuyao change and address both Han Yue and Han Shuang as aunties.

After so many years of interaction, Han Yue had long occupied a significant portion in Xia Yusheng’s young heart, this relationship had already transcended master and servant. It wasn’t mother and child, yet it surpassed mother and child.

Xia Yusheng watched Han Yue disappear before him with his own eyes, and it happened in such a crisis. No matter how young he was, he could vaguely feel that the incident tonight was caused by him.
