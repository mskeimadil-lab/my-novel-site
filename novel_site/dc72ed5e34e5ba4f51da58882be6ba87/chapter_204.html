Chapter 204: Chapter 139: Resource Gap, Changes in the Camp [Three-in-One]

"Leader, is it difficult to break through to the Cold Resistance Level?"

"Leader, is it difficult to break through to the Cold Resistance Level?"

Xia Chuan asked twice, Xia Hongcai came back to his senses, saw the curious eyes of everyone, and without holding back, directly told them the method to break through to the Cold Resistance Level.

Of course, it was the normal method of using a small amount of Cold Beast Blood, not like him who could use a hundred pounds directly, as these people didn’t have the system like him.

"Use a small amount, but Leader, you..."

After hearing Xia Hong’s method, Luo Yuan immediately looked confused and pointed to the large wooden tub in the room.

The others also reacted, their expressions the same as his.

Xia Hong had just said that you can only use a small amount of Cold Beast Blood at a time, and if the amount is too large, the pain is something a normal person absolutely cannot endure.

But he used over a hundred pounds this time, what’s up with that?

Xia Hong didn’t know how to explain to everyone, couldn’t exactly reveal the system, after thinking for a moment, said: "My physique is different from ordinary people, when you reach the limit of thirty thousand pounds, try the Cold Beast Blood, then you’ll understand."

Though there was full of confusion in their eyes, seeing that Xia Hong was obviously unwilling to say more, they didn’t ask further.

Xia Hong at the moment was still preoccupied with the five thousand pounds of Beast Blood and the massive resource gap that came with it, in the end, he could only chuckle bitterly twice, sighing helplessly.

The blood content in other Intermediate Cold Beasts, unclear.

But based on the Snow Mane hunted last night, one Snow Mane gives only about sixty pounds, for five thousand pounds of Beast Blood, at least eighty Intermediate Cold Beasts are needed.

Eighty Intermediate Cold Beasts?

Using over a hundred pounds of Beast Blood at one time requires consuming ten thousand units of wood, more than five thousand iron, more than eight thousand coal, adding it all up, if the resource consumption remains unchanged in the future, it means, to break through to the Cold Resistance Level, such resource consumption altogether requires...

Fifty times?

"I thought once the breakthrough started, things would get easier, but instead, even bigger resource gaps appeared, this is just..."

Xia Hong didn’t know what to say, looking at the cold, piercing sunlight outside the window, shaking his head helplessly.

The breakthrough to the Cold Resistance Level was much more challenging than anticipated, obviously requiring much more time as well.

However...

Xia Hong tightly clenched his fist, a tremor in his heart, looking at his own fists, his eyes gradually showing a spark of excitement.

"Correspondingly, the increase in Basic Strength is also extremely terrifying!"

He slightly turned his head, saw a group of Xia Chuan still not leaving, all curiously looking at him, suddenly stirred in his heart, walked to one side of the room, and lifted the Double-edged Giant Axe leaning against the wall with one hand.

Lifting the giant axe with one hand was nothing remarkable; Xia Chuan and the others had Basic Strengths exceeding ten thousand pounds, lifting it with one hand was not difficult.

But the problem was, the two-thousand-eight-hundred-pound Double-edged Giant Axe seemed weightless in Xia Hong’s hand; not only did he lift it with one hand, but he also wielded the axe handle wantonly around the room.

Whoosh... whoosh... whoosh...

The sound of the axe blades slicing through the air instantly filled the entire room, the powerful momentum created by swinging the giant axe caused the wooden floor to shake violently.

Several times, when the axe blade was about to touch the floor, everyone couldn’t help but hold their breath, fearing that if this axe were swung down, the floor on the third level would crack open.

Despite wielding the axe with one hand, every swing of Xia Hong unleashed such tremendous force that it set the airflow in the room into a frenzy.

"The leader swings the axe so casually, I’m afraid I can’t even withstand it..."

Xia Chuan, Luo Yuan, and all the others, facing the gusts kicked up by the giant axe against their cheeks, couldn’t help but think this.

After a long while, Xia Hong finally put down the giant axe and stopped.

After laying down the giant axe, he hadn’t even taken a single breath.

"The amplification should be two thousand five hundred pounds; this is only two percent progress. If I complete the Skin Remodeling stage, to what level will my Basic Strength rise..."

No need for a force measure, Xia Hong slightly clenched his fist, accurately knowing his Basic Strength through sensing the changes in his Qi Muscle.

"Xia Chuan fifteen thousand, Luo Yuan fourteen thousand, Zhao Long thirteen thousand..."

It wasn’t just himself; Xia Hong turned back to look at Xia Chuan and others, observing their Qi Muscle dynamics, he could immediately perceive their Basic Strengths.

"After Reshaping Skin, the perception ability also greatly improved, especially in sensing changes in biological Qi Muscle."

Discovering this further advantage of Skin Remodeling, Xia Hong was suddenly overjoyed.

This wasn’t an insignificant function; previously during hunts, he had to engage to confirm the prey’s strength, now there’s no need to engage, just observation suffices, it not only saves effort but also avoids many risks.

Suddenly, remembering something, Xia Hong turned to Xia Chuan, asking:

"By the way, the Essence Blood from that Snow Mane..."

The moment Xia Hong mentioned Essence Blood, Xia Chuan knew what he was going to ask, he immediately responded before Xia Hong could finish speaking.

"Rest assured, Leader, when processing the prey three days ago, we already extracted the Essence Blood and had the wooden mannequin record it; I had previously instructed Tong Xinglong and the others that in the future, all new species brought back should be handled this way."

Upon hearing this, Xia Hong nodded, feeling a surge of relief in his heart.

No, not just relief.

Xia Hong’s eyes even showed a hint of excitement.

When they brought back that Snow Mane three days ago, he was eager to try Cold Beast Blood and forgot about the Essence Blood recording with the wooden mannequin. Fortunately, Xia Chuan was now familiar with the camp’s buildings’ functions, managing these matters well without his instructions.
