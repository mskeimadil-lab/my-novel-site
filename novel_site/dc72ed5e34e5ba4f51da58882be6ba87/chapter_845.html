Chapter 845: Chapter 328: An Unexplainable Unease, Jade Dew Stone, Li Xuanling’s Panic_5

After shouting, he immediately dropped the rope, kicked against the mountain wall with both legs, and shot upwards, chasing after the Jade Dew Stone.

The Cold Resistance Level members on the north side of the big pit turned pale when they heard Xia Hong’s voice. The Jade Dew Pearl had already flown out, and they quickly drew their weapons and rushed toward it.

"It’s not fast, use the Solidified Fire Oil to block it!"

Li Xuanling, the strongest and quickest to react, jumped to the top of the Jade Dew Stone, threw a piece of Solidified Fire Oil forward, drew the Heavenly Transformation Sword, and struck the Jade Dew Stone with a single blow.

Clang......

"Ah......"

Hit again by the Solidified Fire Oil, the White Dew Gu’s scream was now extremely weak, clearly indicating that its remaining consciousness was minimal.

"Good!"

At the same time, Xia Hong had just leapt out of the pit. Seeing the Jade Dew Stone struck by Li Xuanling and flying towards him, he reached out directly to grab it.

"You can’t kill me, you can’t kill me, hehehehehe..."

But just as Xia Hong’s hand was about to grasp the Jade Dew Stone, the White Dew Gu emitted a frenzied laugh, repeatedly claiming Xia Hong couldn’t kill it, and then paused in mid-air before transforming into a stream of light, flying straight back towards Li Xuanling.

"With your current skills, I can’t kill you?"

With the suppression of the Cold Ruins Tripod, the White Dew Gu was obviously not fast enough. With only a thumb-sized stone true form, it couldn’t do anything but await its fate.

Even without me, Li Xuanling alone could capture it.

Xia Hong didn’t take its words to heart, assuming it was just a last hysterical act. He jumped to the edge of the pit, kicked off the ground, and shot upwards, flanking it with Li Xuanling to corner it.

However, just as Li Xuanling was about to grab it.

The Jade Dew Stone suddenly changed direction, flying towards Li Xuanling’s lower abdomen.

Whoosh......

Li Xuanling was caught off guard but realizing the Jade Dew Stone was aiming at her lower abdomen, her face turned extremely anxious, and she retreated frantically while shielding with her sword.

This scene made Xia Hong’s brow furrow sharply.

"What are you doing, don’t dodge, this creature’s power is nearly exhausted, it can’t break through your Skin Membrane!"

"I......"

Li Xuanling only managed to say "I" before stopping, seemingly having something she couldn’t say.

The Jade Dew Stone first touched the Heavenly Transformation Sword, then, like an eel, transformed into a stream of light, bypassed the sword, and went directly into Li Xuanling’s belly.

"This......"

This scene stunned all the Cold Resistance Level members below.

Even Xia Hong was dumbfounded.

The Jade Dew Stone had gone into Li Xuanling’s belly?

"I have a child in my belly, I have a child in my belly, it can’t go in, can’t go in, please save the child, Xia Hong!"

Yet, Li Xuanling landed and held her belly, her face full of panic, her words left everyone in shock.

"What did you say?"

Xia Hong’s expression was beyond stiff; he rushed to Li Xuanling’s side and grabbed her arm, questioning angrily.

Li Tiancheng, Meng Ying, Yue Qian, Cheng Guang, and other members of the Long Mountain Cold Resistance Level also realized what was happening and rushed to Li Xuanling’s side.

Especially the elder Li Tiancheng, who pulled Li Xuanling’s arm from Xia Hong, felt her pulse for a moment, and his expression turned extremely grim.

"At least two months, and it’s twins, Xuanling, how could you hide something this important from us? Being pregnant, why did you come out with us, you... this... ah!"

Two months, meaning it was before the October migration.

Li Xuanling, tears streaming down her face, couldn’t say anything, just held her belly, looking at Xia Hong, clearly extremely worried about the child inside.

"To the Cold Ruins Tripod, quickly!"

Xia Hong couldn’t utter any reproaches at this point. He grabbed Li Xuanling and rushed near the Cold Ruins Tripod, placing her hands on the tripod wall.

"It’s working, it’s working, it’s afraid, it’s afraid, the reaction inside my belly is getting smaller, getting smaller."

Feeling the change inside her, Li Xuanling was overjoyed and, after a moment, couldn’t resist looking at Xia Hong, asking, "With the Cold Ruins Tripod, it shouldn’t harm my child, right?"

Looking at the once-proud daughter of Longyou now showing such a typical woman’s stance, Xia Hong’s expression softened as he nodded gently, reassuring, "It’ll be fine, let’s go back first and see if there are other solutions."

Finishing saying this, he didn’t bother with Xia Chuan and the others, collected the Cold Ruins Tripod, and took Li Xuanling back towards Xia City.

"The lady is pregnant?"

"The White Dew Gu, seeing the lady’s pregnancy, deliberately went into her belly, trying to hold the Lord back!"

"Why did the lady hide such an important matter?"

"After all, Long Mountain was newly conquered; the lady should still be upset with the Lord, right?"

"Twins! If something happens..."

"A dog’s mouth can’t spit ivory, with the Lord’s ability, how could anything happen to the lady? The two young masters will definitely be born safely."

"Slap... right, right, it was thoughtless of me. The two young masters will surely be fine; heaven helps those with good fortune."

.........

"Alright, no more chatter, pack up, head back to Xia City!"

Xia Chuan interrupted everyone’s discussions, looked back at the departing Xia Hong and Li Xuanling with a face full of concern.
