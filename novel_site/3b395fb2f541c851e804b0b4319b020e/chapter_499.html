Chapter 499: Chapter 499: The Dragon Qi (3)

"Well then, I’ll be taking my leave."

Li Feng said softly and turned his body.

Seeing this, Fen Ling’s mind quickly turned as she thought of ways to make him agree.

’If I directly pressure him, it would be unfit of my status as a senior...’

A subtle glint flashed in her crimson eyes.

’Then... perhaps I can use a softer approach.’

She took a small step forward and spoke in a gentle tone.

"Envoy Li, please wait a moment!"

Her voice was soft, carrying a trace of sincerity.

"I understand your concerns. As a senior, it is indeed improper for me to force a junior into a spar. However..."

She paused, her beautiful mature face showing a faint, helpless smile.

"Then how about this? Just spar with me, and if you manage to land even a single hit on me, then I will owe you a favor?"

At her words, Li Feng paused.

Lan Suyao, who was standing quietly at the side, blinked as she felt a strong sense of déjà vu.

She instinctively turned to look at Li Feng’s face...

Sure enough, a familiar, slightly shameless smile was slowly appearing on his lips.

Li Feng turned around, rubbing his chin as if seriously considering the offer.

"A favor from the Regent Empress herself..." he murmured, his eyes gleaming with interest.

He looked at Fen Ling with a faint smile.

"That is indeed a very tempting offer, Your Majesty."

Then he looked at her directly and asked with a serious tone.

"But how far exactly does this favor go? I don’t want to cause any trouble for you later."

Empress Fen Ling smiled gently, her eyes curving beautifully.

"You don’t have to worry. As long as it is within my power and does not harm the Fen Dynasty or my bottom line, I will do my best to fulfill it."

Lan Suyao’s mouth twitched slightly as she finally understood why this felt so familiar.

She could already tell what this shameless guy was thinking.

’Here we go again...’

Empress Fen Ling maintained her gentle smile on the surface, but inside her heart, a trace of satisfaction flashed.

’Got him.’

She took another small step closer, her voice soft and sincere. "I am serious, Envoy Li. If you agree to this light spar, and if you manage to land a hit, I will owe you one favor. You can ask me for anything within my power."

She looked at him with calm eyes.

"So... what do you say?"

Li Feng pretended to think for a moment before speaking with a serious expression.

"Since Your Majesty is so sincere... then I have one more small condition."

He looked at her directly.

"If I win this spar, besides owing me a favor... you must also agree to grant me one personal request."

Empress Fen Ling was momentarily stunned, but she quickly recovered with a faint smile, though a trace of curiosity flashed in her eyes.

"...A private request?"

She looked at Li Feng deeply for a moment before nodding slowly and agreeing without any hesitation.

"Fine. I accept your condition."

After all, no matter what, she was confident in her own strength.

To her, this felt less like a real spar and more like a senior gauging the capability of a promising junior.

Hearing her promise, Li Feng who was smiling like a helpless junior almost broke into a strange grin, but he held himself back.

’Heh... got her.’

Inside, Li Feng’s thoughts were already running wild.

The first thing that came to his mind was using the favor.

Since Fen Ling would prove her innocence after this, she could be trusted.

And with her authority as Regent Empress, asking her to secretly help search for Fen Ziyan would be much more effective than doing it alone.

She must had deep connections inside the capital and the entire Fen Dynasty.

As for the second personal request...

Li Feng’s gaze unconsciously drifted for a split second toward Fen Ling’s full, mature breasts that were accentuated by her combat attire.

Those pair of jade peaks had been tempting him the entire time.

’Hmph! I, Li Feng, will never suffer any loss!’

Since this woman wanted to experience his strength, then she had better prepare to pay a proper price.

He quickly suppressed the lewd thought and maintained his helpless junior expression on the surface.

"Then... this junior will respectfully accompany Your Majesty," Li Feng said with a respectful bow.

Without hesitation, he reached up and slowly removed his outer envoy robe, revealing the simpler inner clothes underneath.

The white robe with feather fur lining was handed gently to Yue Lan, who took it silently with a soft nod, her golden eyes calm and encouraging.

Li Feng then walked forward toward Fen Ling with steady steps, his expression turning serious.

The atmosphere in the vast training ground grew heavier as the two faced each other.

Empress Fen Ling smiled faintly, her crimson eyes shining with a hint of anticipation.

"Please."

Li Feng was still thinking about how to properly handle this spar when—

WHOOSH!

Fen Ling suddenly disappeared from her spot.

"...!"

In the next instant, Li Feng suddenly felt his balance change as his vision spun.

Thud.

Li Feng blinked as he was suddenly lying on the floor.

He lay there, staring blankly at the ceiling, completely dumbfounded.

"...Eh?"

The entire training ground fell silent for a split second.

Yue Lan and Lan Suyao both widened their eyes slightly in surprise at the sight.

Then, a soft, teasing voice echoed in the quiet training ground.

"Fufu~ It seems this Empress was a little too impatient."

Empress Fen Ling stood gracefully next to Li Feng as she looked down.

A faint dark-golden dragon qi still lingered around her as she looked at Li Feng on the ground with a gentle yet slightly playful smile.

"I thought you were ready, Envoy Li. My apologies."

She tilted her head slightly, her mature and beautiful face carrying a hint of amusement.

"Shall we try again? This time I will wait for you to make the first move."
