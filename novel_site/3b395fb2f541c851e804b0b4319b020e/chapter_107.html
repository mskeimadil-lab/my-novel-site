The early morning mist filled the air as Li Feng followed Goldie in a single direction.

After moving through thick trees and bushes for a long while , Li Feng stopped his step.

As Goldie had also halted in front of him , hiding behind a tree.

"...." Goldie turned its head toward him , signaling for him to come forward.

He nodded and slowly moved to Goldie’s side , then peeked his head out to look in the direction Goldie was pointing.

"...A swamp ?"

Before him lay a huge swamp.

He can sense a faint smell of rot drifted from its depths.

With mud bubbles popped quietly, and the surface was covered with patches of black moss.

Li Feng crouched slightly , scanning the area. " So this is where that thing’s hiding...." he muttered.

Goldie remained still beside him , its golden-lit eyes fixed on the murky water ahead.

At first , there was no movement , no sound except for the occasional ripple spreading across the surface.

But Li Feng’s instincts told him something was off.

Still, he didn’t act immediately and he turned his head toward Goldie.

"Goldie , go around the swamp and wait. I’ll go try to kill it, but if it tries to escape, make sure to stop it. "

Goldie gave a small nod and despite its large frame , silently made its way toward another direction.

Then Li Feng began setting up another trapping formation at his current location.

After a while , he gave a satisfied smile , looking at his perfectly laid encroachment plan.

If that monster tried to escape again , it would either have to face Goldie or be bound by his formation.

And regardless of the outcome , it would buy him enough time to finish it off.

Finally , he turned his attention toward the swamp.

"Hehe... let’s give it a blast wake-up call."

Li Feng then crouched low and leapt high into the air.

"Rise and shine !.... Falling Star Fist !"
