Now he finally understood why Ning Jianli seemed like she was ready to sacrifice herself.

Dual cultivation wasn’t just about sticking his little brother in and activating the technique....it was far more complex and.... delicate than he had ever imagined.

To put it simply.

For a proper, mutual dual cultivation session, both partners had to know the same technique perfectly.

Every subtle pulse, every circulation path, every energy lock had to be executed in near-perfect harmony.

Timing was everything.

Qi would become unstable, meridians could twist, and instead of nourishing each other, the energy might rebound violently....crushing circulation and damaging both foundations.

True harmonious, mutually beneficial dual cultivation was like a delicate dance.... a duet of energies, a synchronization of body and spirit.

One false step, and the gains would turn into wounds.

Ning Jianli herself didn’t know any dual cultivation techniques, which was why she had been thinking only of one-sided dual cultivation when she realized Li Feng wanted to cultivate with her.

That act alone could destabilize her foundation, or even cause her realm to collapse ....and yet she still chose to do it.

Even though they were easier than the true harmonious techniques, it was only because the practitioner didn’t need their partner to know the same technique as they could simply steal their target’s Yin and Yang.

Even then, the process of stealing required precision.

One misstep could reduce the quality of the stolen Yin and Yang or even harm the target...like trying to squeeze a golden goose for its egg, only to crack the egg and kill the goose if you didn’t know the proper method.

Li Feng’s eyes finally brightened as he realized just how powerful the Sovereign Harmonious Guiding Art truly was.

Even incomplete, the technique was shockingly advanced.

And yet.....his Sovereign Harmonious Guiding Art bypassed all of that.

Now, he alone was enough.

Even without his partner knowing the dual cultivation technique, he could handle all the work himself.
