Chapter 487: Chapter 487: 440 Stars

The sudden surge of power shook the entire surrounding grounds.

Everyone present , friend or foe turned their heads in shock.

The guards who were desperately fighting the monsters momentarily froze, their mouths agape as they stared at Li Feng.

The monstrous pressure radiating from him made their legs tremble.

One of the Golden Core experts, Jade Scholar, who was protecting Prime Minister Mu, widened his eyes in disbelief.

"Is this guy really only a Foundation Establishment cultivator...?"

And Prime Minister Mu, who had been maintaining a calm facade the entire time, finally had his expression crack.

His old eyes narrowed sharply as he stared at the dazzling figure wrapped in silvery light.

He couldn’t help but feel that... something was going terribly wrong.

Then a deep, complicated look flashed across his face.

’This young man... is far more terrifying than the Divine Envoy suggested...’

The grotesque monsters themselves seemed to sense the overwhelming threat.

Many of them instinctively slowed down or even retreated slightly, as if their instincts were screaming at them to stay away from the monster that had just awakened.

Li Feng stood at the center of it all, his hair and robes fluttering wildly in the storm of his own power.

’This power... it really is overwhelming,’ he murmured with a hint of shock.

It had been a long time since he last used [Star Ignition].

The feeling of 440 Stars burning inside his body made his blood boil with excitement and strength.

And at this moment, everything around him seemed to move slower in his eyes... the swinging swords, the lunging monsters, even the flickering dark mist in the air.

All of it felt strangely sluggish and insignificant.

A cold, arrogant smile tugged at the corner of his lips.

’So this is what true power feels like.’

He no longer cared about schemes, masterminds, or traps.

Right now, only one thing mattered...

...Destroy everything standing in his way.

Lan Suyao stared at Li Feng’s back with her mouth slightly agape.

She didn’t expect him to take her words this literally... or for the result to be so ridiculous.

’...Just how deep is he hiding?’

Even someone as strong and experienced as her felt a chill run down her spine at the sheer gap in power Li Feng had just displayed.

’No wonder he got Yue Lan as a dao companion...’

Lan Suyao finally understood this point as she glanced at Yue Lan, who was smiling proudly and quietly at Li Feng’s display of strength.

There was no jealousy in Yue Lan’s eyes, only pure admiration and trust.

Li Feng didn’t pay attention to their reactions as [Star Ignition] had a time limit, so he had to end everything fast.

In the next instant—

Li Feng suddenly vanished from his spot like a ghost.

A beautiful flash of silvery starlight filled the area and—

Puff!

Puff!

Puff!

Every single monster within thirty meters of him disintegrated into black mist on the spot.

Their grotesque bodies couldn’t withstand the pressure for even a single breath.

Hundreds of abominations turned into drifting dark smoke that scattered helplessly in the wind.

....Then the battlefield suddenly fell into a brief, eerie silence.

Everyone looked up simultaneously at the figure floating high above them.

There...

Li Feng stood with his arms crossed, surrounded by flashes of brilliant silvery starlight.

His cloak fluttered wildly in the wind, giving him the appearance of a true war god descending upon the mortal world.

His cold, sharp eyes swept across the remaining black walls that still sealed off large parts of the Prime Minister’s courtyard.

A terrifying killing intent surged from his body as he uncrossed his arms, and locked onto the largest black wall blocking the path toward the imperial palace.

"You think these toys can stop me?"

Without any further words, his figure shot forward like a blazing comet toward one of the dark walls.

He flew like a human cannonball, leaving a long, brilliant trail of light across the darkened sky.

Sensing Li Feng approaching, the wall suddenly rippled as those strange faces resurfaced, but before it could do anything—

Li Feng had already arrived at extreme speed with his fist clenched.

"Break!!"

BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM!!!

His fist slammed into the massive black wall with unstoppable force.

The entire structure trembled violently before a huge section of it exploded into countless fragments.

Those grotesque faces screamed in agony as they disintegrated into black mist.

But Li Feng didn’t stop there.

"All of you... disappear."

With a single thought, his figure blurred again.

FLASH!

He moved like a silver phantom, appearing instantly in front of each remaining black wall in rapid succession.

BOOM!!

One punch.

BOOM!!

Another punch.

Each strike was clean, decisive, and carried overwhelming power.

In the blink of an eye—

BOOM!!

BOOM!!

BOOM!!

The black walls that had sealed the entire mansion shattered one after another like glass under a hammer.

And in just a few breaths of time, all the oppressive black walls that had trapped everyone were completely demolished.

Hundreds of monsters that were still crawling out were erased into nothingness the moment they appeared.

The entire Prime Minister’s courtyard grounds were now wide open under the dark sky, as the oppressive aura that had weighed down on everyone vanished like smoke.

Li Feng hovered in the air for a brief moment, his cloak still fluttering as silvery starlight wrapped around his body.

All the people present were too stunned to even say anything.

Their eyes were glued to the tall and powerful figure floating high in the sky.

Just then, Li Feng glanced toward the direction where he had sensed Shadow and the Divine Brick.

His eyes narrowed with cold killing intent.

Without wasting any time—

BOOOOOOM!!!

His figure suddenly disappeared from the sky, shooting toward the imperial palace like a silver blazing comet.

A powerful shockwave exploded outward from where he had been standing, rattling the ground and sending dust and debris flying in all directions as everyone below covered their faces.

The wind howled violently in his wake.

On the ground, everyone could only stare speechlessly at the long trail of silver fading into the distance.

And Prime Minister Mu’s expression was extremely ugly.

His hands were clenched tightly behind his back as he muttered under his breath.

"...Just what kind of monster have we provoked?"

_

At the same time,

"Futile."

Just as the cloaked figure was about to release the string—

The figure paused slightly and narrowed their eyes.

Their glowing amber eyes glanced sharply toward the sky.

And at that moment,

CRACK...

The sky above suddenly cracked like glass and—

CRACK!!

The sky itself shattered.

Then a figure descended like a falling star, wrapped in a faint yet overwhelming ethereal silvery light.

The distant stars in the sky behind his figure seemed to flicker in resonance with his power.

It was Li Feng.

The cloaked figure’s amber eyes flashed with genuine surprise for the first time tonight.

And a low voice escaped from beneath the hood, carrying a trace of disbelief.

"...So soon?"
