But she hesitated.

With the cunning and shrewd Prime Minister Mu backing them, perhaps he might even be able to make these two aloof fairies suffer under these brutes... if given enough time and opportunity.

And the difference in their builds was striking.

Mad Bull looked like a ferocious bear, while the muscular Li Feng appeared almost slim in comparison.

His voice was low, but every word carried a bone-chilling coldness.
