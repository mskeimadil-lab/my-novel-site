Chapter 481: Mad Bull (2)

Chapter 481: Chapter 481: Mad Bull (2)

Prime Minister Mu watched the entire scene with clear amusement in his eyes.

A faint, almost imperceptible smile hung on his old face as he stroked his beard.

His sharp eyes glinted with unknown thoughts while staring intently at Li Feng.

’In the end, you can only blame your own lack of strength... such is the merciless truth of this world-’

BAM!

’...Hmm?’

Prime Minister Mu suddenly blinked in surprise as Mad Bull’s massive body suddenly leaned forward, temporarily blocking his view of Li Feng.

_

_

_

Meanwhile...

Mad Bull was coughing up large mouthfuls of blood, his face twisted in agony.

He looked down in confusion and horror, only to see Li Feng’s elbow deeply embedded into his muscular chest.

The area around the impact was grotesquely caved in, ribs completely shattered, and blood poured freely from the wound.

"Cough! What... happened...?"

Mad Bull hadn’t even sensed the strike coming.

It was so fast and ruthless that his instincts couldn’t react in time.

All he knew was that his vision blurred as intense pain assaulted him.

"You... you..."

Mad Bull looked up at Li Feng, who was staring calmly at his face.

But then Li Feng frowned slightly and murmured in a low voice.

"I don’t like looking up."

Before Mad Bull could react, Li Feng’s elbow shifted as he drove his fist upward with terrifying force, striking directly under the Golden Core expert’s chin.

BOOOM!!

The impact was brutal.

The air exploded outward from the force of the strike, forcing nearby guards to shield their faces.

"GAHH!!" Mad Bull’s head snapped back violently.

Several teeth flew out along with a thick spray of blood.

His massive body was even lifted slightly off the ground before crashing down hard onto both knees.

Thud!

Mad Bull knelt heavily in front of Li Feng, blood flowing from his mouth and chest.

His vision spun wildly, and he could barely stay conscious.

The once arrogant and lecherous Golden Core expert was now forced into a humiliating kneeling position, completely suppressed by a Foundation Establishment youth.

But the humiliation only ignited his fury.

"YOU BASTARD—!!"

Mad Bull roared, his eyes turning bloodshot.

A violent, blood-red aura exploded from his body as Golden Core spiritual energy surged wildly around him.

He forced himself back to his feet.

"I’LL TEAR YOU APART!!"

He charged at Li Feng like a frenzied bull, swinging a massive fist filled with destructive power that could shatter mountains.

However, Li Feng remained expressionless.

As the heavy punch came, Li Feng stepped forward and threw a simple, straight punch.

BAM!

His fist met Mad Bull’s head-on.

CRUNCH!!

The sound of breaking bones rang out.

Mad Bull’s fist deformed visibly as his fingers and wrist shattered.

The force traveled up his arm, causing him to stagger.

"Wha—"

Before he could even recover, Li Feng grabbed the back of Mad Bull’s head with one hand and brutally drove his knee upward into the man’s face.

BOOM!

SPLURT!

Mad Bull’s nose exploded in a spray of blood.

His head whipped back, but Li Feng didn’t let go.

He pulled him forward and delivered another devastating elbow strike to the side of his head.

THUD!

Mad Bull’s ear ruptured, and he stumbled sideways, dizzy and disoriented.

Li Feng followed relentlessly.

He stepped in close and unleashed a brutal series of body blows.

BAM!

BAM!

BAM!

Without any fancy techniques or spiritual skills, Li Feng brutally beat him down using pure physical dominance.

A heavy punch to the face.

An elbow to the temple.

A brutal knee to the stomach.

Each strike was simple, direct, and devastating.

Mad Bull’s wild attacks were completely dismantled.

His Golden Core strength was suppressed again and again as Li Feng’s raw power overwhelmed him.

CRACK!

CRACK!

More bones broke, and blood splattered everywhere.

...At the same time, the entire area had fallen into complete, dumbfounded silence.

The surrounding guards stared with wide eyes and open mouths, unable to believe what they were witnessing.

A Foundation Establishment youth had just brutally beaten a Golden Core expert into a bloody mess using nothing but raw physical power.

The scene was so surreal that no one dared to speak.

The only sounds left were Mad Bull’s painful groans, the occasional crack of breaking bones, and the dripping of blood onto the rubble.

Yue Lan smiled proudly as she looked at Li Feng’s back, her golden eyes shining with pride and excitement.

Lan Suyao, on the other hand, stood completely dumbfounded.

She knew Li Feng was strong, but this... was simply too freakish.

Now she finally understood why she could never win their spars no matter how hard she tried.

Her mouth twitched slightly as she realized Li Feng had clearly been holding back during their spar... It seem he purposely playing with her and taking advantage of her body the entire time.

Then,

She turned her face slightly and saw that Prime Minister Mu’s usually composed expression had finally cracked.

The old man’s eyes were wide and his mouth slightly agape.

She couldn’t help but smile inwardly as it seem she wasn’t the only one shocked.

But the silence was suddenly shattered.

"AAAAAHHHH—!! I’LL KILL YOU!!"

Mad Bull let out a hysterical, rage-filled roar.

Even though his body was broken and covered in blood, his survival instinct and humiliation pushed him to the limit.

BOOOOMMM!

His blood-red aura then exploded violently, becoming even thicker and more frenzied.

"Blood Rage Berserk!!"

A terrifying blood-colored light erupted from his body.

His muscles swelled grotesquely, his veins bulged like earthworms, and his broken bones temporarily knit together with sheer madness.

His aura skyrocketed, reaching the peak of the middle-stage Golden Core realm in an instant.

Prime Minister Mu who was standing nearby, was taken aback at the sight.

He couldn’t help but curse inwardly at this idiot.

’That idiot! What do they think will happen if they reveal traces of demonic qi in front of a monster from a righteous sect? Especially in front of this freak...’

If it had been before this, Mad Bull might have only suffered humiliation and still had a chance to live with his help.

But now...
