Chapter 482: Li Feng’s Decisiveness

Chapter 482: Chapter 482: Li Feng’s Decisiveness

’That idiot! What do they think will happen if they reveal traces of demonic qi in front of a monster from a righteous sect? Especially in front of this freak...’

If it had been before this, Mad Bull might have only suffered humiliation and still had a chance to live with his help.

But now...

Before he could finish his thought, Prime Minister Mu saw that Mad Bull suddenly move.

With bloodshot eyes filled with nothing but killing intent, Mad Bull charged at Li Feng once more.

His speed and power were far greater than before as he threw a devastating, blood-red fist that tore through the air.

But as Prime Minister Mu expected, Li Feng... was smiling.

Li Feng’s eyes flashed with a dangerous glint as he looked at the incoming Mad Bull, who was now emitting faint traces of demonic qi.

"Demonic cultivator...?" Li Feng murmured softly.

He had only planned to teach this bastard a lesson and vent some frustration, but now... as a ’righteous’ disciple, he had another duty to fulfill.

"Dieeeeee!!"

Mad Bull threw a desperate, all-out punch that carried the full force of his secret technique.

The air screeched as the blood red fist tore through space toward Li Feng’s head.

The entire area suddenly filled with intense blood-colored killing intent, turning the sky above the crater a faint shade of red.

Everyone present felt a chill run down their spines.

Yue Lan and Lan Suyao’s expressions changed drastically at this oppressive pressure.

Even they could feel the terrifying power behind this final strike.

Prime Minister Mu’s face turned solemn and Concubine Bai’s face turned pale at the confrontation between the two monsters.

She instinctively took a step back, her heart pounding for some reason.

The guards held their breath, many of them thinking that even if the young Envoy was monstrously strong, taking this head-on strike would be extremely dangerous.

However, Li Feng remained standing in place, that cold smile still on his face.

Just as the blood-red fist was about to smash into his head—

BOOOOOOM!!

Li Feng’s right fist shot out like lightning.

There was no technique, no spiritual light... only pure, overwhelming physical power.

Then his fist collided directly with Mad Bull’s blood-red punch.

CRACKKKK!!!

The sound of shattering bones and exploding flesh echoed across the area.

"Eh...?"

Mad Bull’s eyes widened in horror as he saw that... his entire right arm disintegrated on impact... flesh, bones, and blood turning into a gruesome red mist that sprayed everywhere.

The terrifying force continued traveling through what remained of his shoulder and into his body.

But Li Feng didn’t stop there.

With a single step, he arrived in front of Mad Bull’s towering figure.

He clenched his fist as a faint silvery light flickered across his knuckles.

"Stop!"

A loud shout rang out, but Li Feng ignored it.

A cold, ruthless smile appeared on his face as he threw a devastating punch straight toward Mad Bull’s head.

BOOOOOOM—!!!

The punch landed with merciless precision.

SPLURT!

Mad Bull’s head exploded like a watermelon hit by a hammer.

Blood, brain matter, and bone fragments scattered in all directions.

The massive body of the Golden Core expert stood frozen for a brief moment as blood spurted outward like a fountain, dyeing the surrounding area in crimson red.

Soon the tall body finally collapsed heavily onto the ground with a loud thud, and become completely lifeless.

What remained was nothing more than a headless, one-armed bloody corpse.

The entire area fell into dead silence once again.

No one dared to breathe loudly as they stared at Li Feng, who was now covered in blood.

Li Feng slowly exhaled.

The cold, ruthless smile on his face gradually faded and was replaced by a bright, almost refreshing smile... as if he had just finished a light morning exercise.

He casually raised his hand and ran it through his wet, blood-soaked hair, pushing it back neatly.

Drip...

Drip...

Drip...

Drops of blood dripped from his fingers as he did so.

Li Feng then turned his gaze toward Prime Minister Mu.

His bright smile remained as he spoke in a calm and polite tone, as if nothing big had happened.

" Prime Minister Mu, this Envoy has discovered that one of your guests was actually a demonic cultivator hiding in our midst. I have already eliminated him on the spot in accordance with the righteous path. I hope the Prime Minister does not mind."

But then, he suddenly shook his head and sighed in regret.

"Ah, maybe I shouldn’t have killed him so soon. This guy might have been the culprit behind the chaos in the palace. But as a righteous disciple, my righteous heart couldn’t bear the grief and anger that this bastard has caused, so I just couldn’t hold myself back! I hope everyone forgives me for being so impulsive."

He even cupped his hands slightly in a respectful gesture to everyone, though his body was still drenched in Mad Bull’s blood, making the entire scene both polite and extremely chilling.

Concubine Bai, along with the other two Golden Core experts beside her, had already turned deathly pale.

They knew just how powerful Mad Bull had been... which was exactly why they were even more terrified now.

Subconsciously, all three of them took a step back, as if they didn’t want to get anywhere near this terrifying monster wearing human skin.

Meanwhile...

Prime Minister Mu stared at Li Feng for a long moment.

In that brief silence, both men understood something clearly.

Despite never having truly confronted each other before... Li Feng and Prime Minister Mu had already realized that they were now openly targeting one another.

And as the two continued gazing at each other, the atmosphere grew incredibly tense.

But just then, Li Feng’s next words made the air even heavier.

"Oh, by the way... I think I heard Prime Minister Mu shout for me to stop earlier..."

Li Feng looked at the old man, his blood-stained face still smiling as he politely dropped a bomb.

"I may be too impolite to ask this, but... could it be that Prime Minister Mu is also connected to these demonic cultivators?"
