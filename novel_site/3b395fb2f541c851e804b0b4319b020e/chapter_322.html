’...Is this a dream?’

The thought surfaced naturally, as if the scene itself was too difficult to believe was real.

With a quiet exhale, she began to walk forward.

Each step sent gentle ripples across the black ocean beneath her feet, the surface bending softly before returning to stillness.

The world remained silent and peaceful and even almost... strangely comforting.

For a rare moment, Fen Ziyan’s usually sharp expression softened.

But then her eyes brightened.

In the distance, she spotte... a figure, someone who appeared to be kneeling.

’...Hmm?’

Her pace slowed slightly as she focused, curiosity replacing her earlier calm.

From afar, she could make out long white hair cascading down his shoulders, flowing like silk and spilling into the black water below.

The strands partially obscured his face, leaving his expression hidden as his head remained lowered.

But even with his face hidden, even from such a distance...

There was something about him...

...Something familiar.

Fen Ziyan blinked curiously at this feeling and walked forward.

When she arrived up close, she finally realized how massive the figure was.

Even kneeling, his presence towered over her.

She had to lift her gaze just to take him in and even then, she felt as though her head would barely reach his waist if he were to stand.

Slowly, her eyes traced over his form.

Cracks of silvery light ran across his body like fractured glass, woven with strands of starlight.

And tiny, star-like veins pulsed within those cracks, faintly illuminating the darkness around him.

There was something... majestic about him.

Something ancient.

Something far beyond her understanding.

And yet... he was kneeling.

Almost as if he were trapped... and helpless.

Fen Ziyan couldn’t help but feel a faint sense of awe as she looked at the figure.

She couldn’t understand why someone so majestic would appear here in such a state.

Curiously, she reached out to touch him.

’...Um... hello?’

But her hand passed straight through him.

Just as she was wondering what kind of strange dream this was—

The black ocean beneath her feet began to stir, as if something was approaching.

’W-What...?’

Fen Ziyan panicked, even though she believed it was just a dream.

The sudden change felt too real.

Then, as if guided by instinct... she slowly turned her head.

And what she saw made her breath stop.

There....

A massive black wave loomed in the distance, rising higher than the heavens themselves.

It surged into the sky, blotting out the stars above as though it sought to devour them whole.

It wasn’t just large,... it felt as though the heavens themselves were collapsing.

"...!"

Fen Ziyan’s pupils shrank as the towering wave began to fall, its overwhelming force crashing down toward her.

Or rather...

...Toward the kneeling figure beside her.
