It won’t bring us back, instead, it will make me and your father very sad, son.”

This time Mama Wyatt’s message was a bit lengthy but necessary, it was no normal recorded message but a conditioned recorded message which would only be delivered when young Wyatt can upgrade the grimoire to golden grade before he turns 19 years old. No parent is going to leave their child without enough prior preparation and insurance in case of something tragic.
